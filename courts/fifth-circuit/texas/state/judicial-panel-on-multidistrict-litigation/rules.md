# Texas Judicial Panel on Multidistrict Litigation: motions, responses, replies and amicus letter briefs

Alias `texjpml`. Scope: papers filed in the Texas MDL Panel's docket (Supreme Court of Texas, MDL Panel Clerk): joint motions to transfer, responses, replies, and amicus letter briefs. The MDL Panel is a Texas state panel (Tex. Gov't Code 74.161; Tex. R. Jud. Admin. 13), not the federal Judicial Panel on Multidistrict Litigation: the federal JPML's rules and the federal caption rules are out of scope for this folder. The exemplars are Texas filings (In re Texas Opioid Litigation, No. 18-0358). Verified against the Texas Rules of Appellate Procedure (updated with amendments effective February 6, 2026), the MDL Panel page on txcourts.gov (Wayback copy, snapshot 2026-09-11), and Tex. Gov't Code 33.034 on 2026-10-21.

Which rules govern: Tex. R. Jud. Admin. 13 governs the Panel's procedure, but its text was not retrieved (see the Local rules row). TRAP 9 is the Texas appellate form standard; TRAP 1.1 limits TRAP to "appellate courts," so TRAP 9.4 does not by its own terms bind this Panel. The TRAP 9.4 values below are applied by convention. The typeface is the one point where the exemplars disagree: the reply (18 pages) certifies under TRAP 9.4(i)(3) and Tex. R. Jud. Admin. 13.3(e) that it uses "14-point typeface for all text," and TRAP 9.4(e) sets 14 points; the response and the motion use 12-point Times New Roman. The templates follow the reply's certification and TRAP 9.4(e) (14 points). Open question for David: whether the Panel requires 14-point type for every paper (TEXJPML-FORM-006); if it does not, the 12-point setting is a one-line change in each template.
