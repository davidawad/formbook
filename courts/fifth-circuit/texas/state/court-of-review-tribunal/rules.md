# Texas Court of Review Tribunal

Alias `texrevtrib`. Scope: the review tribunal that reviews a State Commission on Judicial Conduct recommendation for removal or retirement of a judge (Tex. Const. art. V, s. 1-a(9); Tex. Gov't Code s. 33.001(a)(9)). Not governed by Tax Code chapter 111 (titled "Collection Procedures"), and not the special court of review under Tex. Gov't Code s. 33.034 (three justices, sanctions and censures). No exemplar: no public filed brief to this tribunal was found (see `usage/SOURCE.md`); the rows below are the statutory and constitutional requirements only.
