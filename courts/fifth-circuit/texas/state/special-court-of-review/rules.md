# Texas Special Court of Review: answering briefs, motions and reply briefs

Alias `texreview`. Scope: papers filed in a review of a sanction or censure of a judge by the State Commission on Judicial Conduct, under Tex. Gov't Code 33.034: the examiners' answering (principal) brief, the judge's motion and supporting memorandum, and reply briefs. Verified against the Texas Rules of Appellate Procedure (text dated 02/06/2026, as posted on txcourts.gov) and Tex. Gov't Code 33.034, on 2026-10-21.

Scope note (read before the form rows): the Special Court of Review is not named in the Texas Rules of Appellate Procedure, and no Special Court rule of procedure or form was retrieved. TRAP 9.4 governs "a document filed with an appellate court." Section 33.034(h) calls the review an "appeal," and the court decides it on a record (33.034(e)(1)) or de novo (33.034(e)(2)). The template therefore applies TRAP 9.4 as the state's form rule. Its 14-point face is stricter than the 12-point face of the three filed exemplars, and a smaller size is allowed only where a rule expressly permits it. The exemplars are evidence of practice, not of a rule. If David rules that TRAP 9.4 does not reach the Special Court, the body size drops to 12 point (the default) and this note changes; that is a decision for David.

Local rules not retrieved: the Special Court of Review's own procedural rules are not on the Judicial Branch site. https://www.txcourts.gov/rules-forms/rules-standards/ returned HTTP 403 to live requests on 2026-10-21 (three attempts, two of them after a wait); its Wayback copy (HTTP 200) lists the Rules of Appellate Procedure and no Special Court rule. https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1463134/texas-rules-of-appellate-procedure-02062026.pdf returned HTTP 403 live; the same file was read from its Wayback copy (HTTP 200). https://www.txcourts.gov/rules-forms/ returned HTTP 403 live.
