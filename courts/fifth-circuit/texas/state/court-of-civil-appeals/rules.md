# Court of Appeals of Texas (civil appeals): briefs, motions, petitions for writ, amicus briefs

Alias `texapp`. Scope: papers filed in the fourteen Courts of Appeals of Texas in civil matters: appellant's and appellee's briefs, reply briefs, motions and their supporting memoranda, petitions and responses in original proceedings (mandamus, prohibition, habeas), and amicus curiae briefs. Verified against the Texas Rules of Appellate Procedure, updated with amendments effective February 6, 2026, on 2026-10-21. Rule numbers below are TRAP rule numbers.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.txcourts.gov/rules-forms/ , https://www.txcourts.gov/rules-forms/rules-standards/ and https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1463134/texas-rules-of-appellate-procedure-02062026.pdf returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21. The TRAP text below is the Wayback Machine copy of the official txcourts.gov PDF, which the 2026-10-21 fetch returned HTTP 200 for; confirm against the court-posted PDF before relying on a number. Each of the fourteen courts of appeals may adopt local rules under TRAP 9.4 and 38; those local rules were not retrieved and are not reflected here. The TRAP lines cited by number are what this court's papers must meet.

## Length (TRAP 9.4(i))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEXAPP-LEN-001 | Every word and part is counted, including headings, footnotes, and quotations, except the caption, identity of parties and counsel, statement regarding oral argument, table of contents, index of authorities, statement of the case, statement of issues presented, statement of jurisdiction, statement of procedural history, signature, proof of service, certification, certificate of compliance, and appendix. | TRAP 9.4(i)(1). | M |
| TEXAPP-LEN-002 | A brief or response in an appellate court (including a response in an original proceeding in the court of appeals) is limited to 15,000 words if computer-generated, and 50 pages if not. | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(B). | M |
| TEXAPP-LEN-003 | In a civil case in the court of appeals, the aggregate of all briefs filed by a party does not exceed 27,000 words if computer-generated, and 90 pages if not. | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(B). | M |
| TEXAPP-LEN-004 | A reply brief in an appellate court, and a reply to a response to a petition in an original proceeding in the court of appeals, is limited to 7,500 words if computer-generated, and 25 pages if not. | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(C). | M |
| TEXAPP-LEN-005 | Where a petition in the Supreme Court carries an introduction, its word count is included in the overall word count and page limit certification. The certification form for courts of appeals briefs was not extracted. | TRAP 52.3(a) (Supreme Court petitions). | C |

## Appellant's brief (opening brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEXAPP-OPEN-001 | The appellant's brief contains, under appropriate headings and in this order: (a) identity of parties and counsel; (b) table of contents; (c) index of authorities; (d) statement of the case; (e) statement regarding oral argument; (f) issues presented; (g) statement of facts; (h) summary of the argument; (i) argument; (j) prayer; (k) appendix in civil cases. | TRAP 38.1(a)-(k). | M |
| TEXAPP-OPEN-002 | The identity of parties and counsel lists all parties to the trial court's judgment or order, and all counsel, with firm or office name and, for counsel currently appearing, mailing address, telephone, and email, except as Rule 9.8 provides. | TRAP 38.1(a). | M |
| TEXAPP-OPEN-003 | The statement of facts is concise, stated without argument, and supported by record references. In a civil case the court accepts the facts as true unless another party contradicts them. | TRAP 38.1(g). | M |
| TEXAPP-OPEN-004 | The issues presented state all issues or points concisely. | TRAP 38.1(f). | M |
| TEXAPP-OPEN-005 | The table of contents and the index of authorities give the pages where each item appears. | TRAP 38.1(b), (c). | M |
