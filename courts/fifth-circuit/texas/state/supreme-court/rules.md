# Texas Supreme Court: petitions, briefs, motions and rehearing

Alias `tex`. Scope: documents filed in the Supreme Court of Texas (petition for review, brief on the merits, reply, motion, motion for rehearing, original-proceeding petition, amicus brief). Verified against the Supreme Court of Texas orders Misc. Docket No. 25-9092 (proposed, October 24, 2025) and No. 25-9104 (final, effective January 1, 2026), as published in the Texas Register, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.txcourts.gov/rules-forms/rules-standards/ returned 403 (checked three times this session, also https://www.txcourts.gov/ and https://www.txcourts.gov/media/ 403). The full text of Texas Rules of Appellate Procedure Rule 9.4(a)-(h) (paper size, margins, typeface, spacing, footnotes) is not in either order, and no reproduction of it returned 200 this session. Those rows are kind A (assumed from the audited template and exemplars) until the primary text is read.

The orders for 25-9104 amend only the subdivisions they print. Rule 9.4(i) (length) and Rules 52, 53, 55, 56 and 64 as printed are current for filings on or after January 1, 2026. A filing whose petition for review, original proceeding or certified question was filed before January 1, 2026 is governed by the former rules (25-9104 para. 4).

|---|---|---|---|
