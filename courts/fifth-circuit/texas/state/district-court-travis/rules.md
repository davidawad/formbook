# District Courts of Travis County, Texas (Austin): original answer, motion with incorporated brief, notice of hearing, notice of appearance, proposed order, Rule 11 agreement

Alias `txdistrict-travis`. Scope: papers filed in the civil district courts of Travis County, Texas (the 53rd, 98th, 126th, 200th, 201st, 261st, 345th, 353rd, 419th and other civil district courts that sit as the Travis County Civil and Family District Courts). Doc types: `original-answer` (an original answer, with any special exceptions, verified denials, affirmative defenses and counterclaim) and `motion-brief` (a motion with its argument incorporated, with certificate of conference and of service; the same folder renders a proposed order as its own paper; with `signatures` it renders a joint motion signed by more than one firm), `proposed-order` (also an agreed order, with "Agreed to:" blocks) and `rule-11-agreement` (a signed letter agreement between counsel, filed with the clerk; added 2026-10-26).

Verified against the TRCP (official txcourts.gov PDF), the Travis County Local Rules effective November 15, 2024, the Technology Standards v11.0 and Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 132.001 on 2026-10-24. Rule numbers below are the rule numbers of the sources in the table. Where the sources are silent the project rule applies and is marked T (house style from the filed exemplars; each document folder's `usage/SOURCE.md`).

TXTRAVIS-MOT-002 (TRCP 21(b)) and TXTRAVIS-MOT-002 (TRCP 21(b)) applies to this paper. TXTRAVIS-MOT-004 and -005 (Local Rules 2.2, 2.3) apply to the motion being set, not to this notice: the notice does not satisfy them, and the motion must itself carry the certifications.

|---|---|---|---|
| TXTRAVIS-ORD-001 | A draft order must not be typed on the same page with a pleading, motion, certificate of service or any part thereof; each draft has a heading showing the cause number, the style of the case and the court. The template renders the order as its own paper; the filer attaches it to the motion, never filing it alone (LR 11.1, TXTRAVIS-FORM-004). | LR 11.1, 11.4 | M |
| TXTRAVIS-ORD-002 | The signature line for the judge must not be on a separate page but on a page with other text. The template keeps the last paragraph with the signature line. | LR 11.4 | M |
| TXTRAVIS-ORD-003 | The word "entered" is not used to show the date an order is signed; the template prints "SIGNED on ___, 20__." | LR 11.5 | M |
| TXTRAVIS-ORD-004 | A show-cause or other order setting a case that is presented to a judge for signature "must be on a separate page and not combined with a pleading." Judges are not asked to sign orders setting cases except when a show-cause order is necessary or a rule of law requires it; the setting is made with the Court Administrator's Office. | LR 2.5 | A |
| TXTRAVIS-ORD-005 | "So far as practicable, every draft of a judgment, decree, or order to be signed by a judge or associate judge must be signed by all parties evidencing approval as to form before it is presented to the judge. Each attorney's signature block on these documents must identify the parties represented by that attorney." An agreed order carries one signature block per attorney (or firm) that names the party represented; the filed exemplar heads them "AGREED TO:" (`approved_heading`). Agreed orders in pending cases may be submitted without a hearing under the Standing Order for Submission Procedures (LR 7.1; TXTRAVIS-NOH-005). | LR 11.2, 7.1 | M |
| TXTRAVIS-ORD-006 | Agreed order of dismissal: the order is the paper that ends the suit. An order without a conventional trial on the merits "is not final for purposes of appeal unless it actually disposes of every pending claim and party or unless it clearly and unequivocally states that it finally disposes of all claims and all parties" (*Lehmann*, 39 S.W.3d at 205); the Court's own sample is "This judgment finally disposes of all parties and all claims and is appealable." A nonsuit or dismissal under Rule 162 does not prejudice an adverse party's right to be heard on a pending claim for affirmative relief (a counterclaim), so an order that ends the suit names the counterclaims as well. Costs follow Rule 131 unless "otherwise ordered by the court" (Rule 162): "taxed against the party incurring same" is the exemplar's form. The template prints what the author types; the author supplies the finality sentence. | TRCP 131, 162; *Lehmann* | A |
| TXTRAVIS-ORD-007 | The date line may read "Signed this ___ day of ___, 20__." (`date_line: signed-this`, the agreed-order exemplar) as well as "SIGNED on this ..." and "SO ORDERED on this ..."; never "entered". | LR 11.5 | M |

## Rule 11 agreement

Read 2026-10-26 from the TRCP file (HTTP 200). The filed exemplar is a signed letter agreement between counsel, filed with the clerk (`rule-11-agreement/usage/SOURCE.md`).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXTRAVIS-R11-001 | "Unless otherwise provided in these rules, no agreement between attorneys or parties touching any suit pending will be enforced unless it be in writing, signed and filed with the papers as part of the record, or unless it be made in open court and entered of record." The template produces the written, signed paper; the filer signs it (each side's counsel, and any party that is to be bound) and files it with the clerk. | [TRCP](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1463426/texas-rules-of-civil-procedure.pdf) 11 | M |
| TXTRAVIS-R11-002 | The letter form (letterhead, date, addressee, "RE:" line naming the case, cause number, court and county, numbered terms, "If this letter accurately reflects ... please sign below", the sender's signature, "AGREED:" block of the other side with a date) is the filed practice of the exemplar; no rule prescribes it. | `rule-11-agreement/usage/SOURCE.md` | T |
| TXTRAVIS-R11-003 | A Rule 11 agreement is not an agreed judgment, and a court whose plenary power has expired cannot enforce it: *In re Vaishangi, Inc.*, No. 13-0169 (Tex. June 6, 2014). An agreement that promises a later dismissal should be paired with the dismissal order (TXTRAVIS-ORD-006) and, if the parties want the court to enforce it, say so before plenary power ends. Author's decision; the template does not decide it. | *Vaishangi* | A |
| TXTRAVIS-R11-004 | An agreement is filed with the papers as part of the record; Local Rule 11.1 (no stand-alone proposed orders) does not apply to it, but the filing is a document filed under TRCP 21 and is served under Rule 21a on every other party. The template prints no certificate of service (the exemplar has none); the filer's transmittal or the e-filing manager's service covers it. | TRCP 21, 21a | A |
