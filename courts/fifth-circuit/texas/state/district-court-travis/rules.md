# District Courts of Travis County, Texas (Austin): original answer, motion with incorporated brief, notice of hearing, notice of appearance, proposed order

Alias `txdistrict-travis`. Scope: papers filed in the civil district courts of Travis County, Texas (the 53rd, 98th, 126th, 200th, 201st, 261st, 345th, 353rd, 419th and other civil district courts that sit as the Travis County Civil and Family District Courts). Doc types: `original-answer` (an original answer, with any special exceptions, verified denials, affirmative defenses and counterclaim) and `motion-brief` (a motion with its argument incorporated, with certificate of conference and of service; the same folder renders a proposed order as its own paper).

Verified against the TRCP (official txcourts.gov PDF), the Travis County Local Rules effective November 15, 2024, the Technology Standards v11.0 and Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 132.001 on 2026-10-24. Rule numbers below are the rule numbers of the sources in the table. Where the sources are silent the project rule applies and is marked T (house style from the filed exemplars; `usage/SOURCE.md`).

## Motion with incorporated brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXTRAVIS-MOT-001 | Every motion states its grounds and sets out the relief or order sought, is filed with the clerk, and is served on all other parties at the same time. | TRCP 21(a) | A |
| TXTRAVIS-MOT-002 | An application for an order and notice of a court proceeding not presented during a proceeding must be served not less than three days before the time specified for the proceeding, unless these rules say otherwise; notice of a court proceeding must contain the information participants need (location or joining instructions, the court's designated contact, instructions for submitting evidence). Author responsibility: a notice of hearing is its own paper or a part of the motion. | TRCP 21(b) | A |
| TXTRAVIS-MOT-003 | A Rule 91a motion to dismiss states that it is made under the rule, identifies each cause of action it addresses, and states specifically the reasons the cause of action has no basis in law or fact; it is filed within 60 days after the first pleading containing the challenged cause of action is served on the movant, and at least 21 days before the hearing; the respondent gets at least 14 days' notice of the hearing and responds no later than 7 days before it. Author responsibility; the template does not compute dates. | TRCP 91a.1-91a.6 | A |
| TXTRAVIS-MOT-004 | Before a hearing is set the parties make reasonable efforts to speak to each other. Every motion includes a certification that counsel "has conferred with (or made reasonable efforts to confer with) all parties about the date, time, and format of the setting." The template prints the chosen variant (`conference.setting`: `conferred` or `efforts`). | LR 2.2 | M |
| TXTRAVIS-MOT-005 | Every motion other than a dispositive motion also certifies either (a) "Counsel has conferred with all parties who may be affected by the relief sought in this motion in a good faith effort to resolve or narrow the issues raised." or (b) "Counsel has made reasonable efforts to confer with all parties who may be affected by the relief sought in this motion but has been unable to do so." The template prints the chosen variant (`conference.issues`: `conferred` or `efforts`) unless `conference.dispositive: true`. The court may strike a setting where the motion lacks these. A party must not request a setting unless the motion has been filed with the LR 2.2 certifications or will file it immediately, and no later than 24 hours after obtaining the setting. | LR 2.2, 2.3 | M |
| TXTRAVIS-MOT-006 | A certificate of service on the filed paper: the party or attorney of record certifies compliance with Rule 21a in writing over signature, stating the date and manner. A document e-filed must be served electronically through the electronic filing manager when the recipient's email is on file; it may otherwise be served in person, by mail, commercial delivery, fax or email. A certificate is prima facie evidence of service. | TRCP 21(d), 21a(a)(1)-(2), 21a(e) | M |

## Notice of hearing

Read 2026-10-25 from the same TRCP and Local Rules files as in the Sources table (both URLs returned HTTP 200 that day). TXTRAVIS-MOT-002 (TRCP 21(b)) and TXTRAVIS-MOT-004 and -005 (Local Rules 2.2, 2.3) apply to this paper as well.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXTRAVIS-NOH-001 | "Notice of any court proceeding must contain the information needed for participants ... to participate in the proceeding, including the location of the proceeding or instructions for joining the proceeding electronically, the court's designated contact information, and instructions for submitting evidence." Served "not less than three days before the time specified for the court proceeding, unless otherwise provided by these rules or shortened by the court." | [TRCP](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1463426/texas-rules-of-civil-procedure.pdf) 21(b) | A |
| TXTRAVIS-NOH-002 | "Neither the court nor the Court Administrator sends notices of settings. The party who obtains the setting ... must give notice to all parties of the time and date of a setting and the total time needed for the entire hearing for all participants." The notice "must be sent the same day the party obtains the setting, and any delay in sending notice may be argued by opposing counsel as a basis for continuance." Sample notices are posted on the Travis County Civil District Courts' website. | [LR](https://www.traviscountytx.gov/images/courts/Docs/20241120-Amended_LOCAL_RULES_as_of_Nov_15_2024.pdf) 2.4 | A |
| TXTRAVIS-NOH-003 | "Notices of hearings and trials provided by parties pursuant to Local Rule 2.4 must not direct the parties to the court listed in the case style (i.e., the number randomly assigned when the case was filed ...)." Assignments to judges and courtrooms are posted at the Civil and Family Courts Facility and on the courts' website before each hearing or trial; questions about assignments in cases not specially assigned go to the Court Administrator. | [LR](https://www.traviscountytx.gov/images/courts/Docs/20241120-Amended_LOCAL_RULES_as_of_Nov_15_2024.pdf) 1.4 | A |
| TXTRAVIS-NOH-004 | "All hearings and trials (proceedings) will be in person unless (a) all parties agree and the proceeding is set and announced as remote with the Court Administrator's Office, or (b) a judge finds good cause to determine that a remote proceeding is appropriate, after notice and opportunity for objections to be heard." A notice of a remote hearing carries the joining instructions (TXTRAVIS-NOH-001). | [LR](https://www.traviscountytx.gov/images/courts/Docs/20241120-Amended_LOCAL_RULES_as_of_Nov_15_2024.pdf) 9.1 | A |
| TXTRAVIS-NOH-005 | A matter submitted without a hearing (uncontested divorces and name changes, agreed orders, orders to appear, "notices of hearing for temporary orders prior to answer day", and other uncontested matters) follows the Standing Order for Submission Procedures, and the motion must be filed with the District Clerk before it is submitted. A notice of hearing is not used for those matters. Emergency matters are not submitted but presented under the rest of chapter 7. | [LR](https://www.traviscountytx.gov/images/courts/Docs/20241120-Amended_LOCAL_RULES_as_of_Nov_15_2024.pdf) 7.1 | A |
| TXTRAVIS-NOH-006 | The signature block and the certificate of service of a notice follow TXTRAVIS-FORM-007, -008 and MOT-006. The Local Rule 2.2 certifications belong on the motion, not on the notice. | TRCP 57, 21(f)(2), 21a; LR 2.2 | M |

## Notice of appearance

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXTRAVIS-NOA-001 | "On the occasion of a party's first appearance through counsel, the attorney whose signature first appears on the initial pleadings for any party shall be the attorney in charge, unless another attorney is specifically designated therein. Thereafter, until such designation is changed by written notice to the court and all other parties in accordance with Rule 21a, said attorney in charge shall be responsible for the suit as to such party. All communications from the court or other counsel with respect to a suit shall be sent to the attorney in charge." A notice of appearance or change of attorney in charge is that written notice; it is served under Rule 21a. | [TRCP](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1463426/texas-rules-of-civil-procedure.pdf) 8 | A |
| TXTRAVIS-NOA-002 | A notice of appearance asks the court for nothing; the Local Rule 2.2 certifications do not apply. It carries the signature block of TXTRAVIS-FORM-007, -008 (name, State Bar number, address, telephone, email, fax if any) and a certificate of service (TXTRAVIS-MOT-006). | TRCP 57, 21(f)(2), 21a; LR 2.2 | M |
