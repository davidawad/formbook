Plaintiff Larkspur Dairy Supply, Inc. asks the Court to compel Defendant Northgate Bakeries, LLC to produce the documents described below, and shows the Court as follows.

# Background

Plaintiff served its First Requests for Production on October 1, 2026. Defendant's responses were due thirty days later. Defendant has produced none of the documents requested in Requests 3, 4, and 7.[^1]

# Argument

Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 192.3(a) provides the scope of discovery in these terms:

> Parties may obtain discovery regarding any matter that is not privileged and is relevant to the subject matter of the pending action, whether it relates to the claim or defense of the party seeking discovery or the claim or defense of any other party.

## A. The requests are relevant

The requests seek the following:

- the delivery records for the contract period;
- the invoices issued under the supply agreement; and
- the written communications about the late deliveries.

Each category bears on whether Defendant breached the supply agreement.

## B. Defendant has not objected

Defendant served no objection to any of the three requests. An objection not timely made is waived.

# Prayer

For these reasons, Plaintiff asks the Court to order Defendant to produce the documents responsive to Requests 3, 4, and 7 within seven days, and to grant Plaintiff all other relief to which it is entitled.

[^1]: The requests are attached as Exhibit A.
