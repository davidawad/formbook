# Exemplars: rule-11-agreement (txdistrict-travis)

A Rule 11 agreement filed in a Travis County district court. Retrieved 2026-10-26. Only the agreement's own pages (1-3 of a 17-page PDF; the rest are its Exhibits A and B, two earlier injunction orders) are stored. **The only Travis example found is not a settlement or dismissal agreement.** It is an agreement on an informal stay; it is kept as a FORMAT exemplar (Rule 11 agreement as a signed letter between counsel, filed with the clerk). No filed Travis Rule 11 settlement agreement was found after a search (below); an agreement that settles a suit and promises a later dismissal would use this form with settlement terms.
