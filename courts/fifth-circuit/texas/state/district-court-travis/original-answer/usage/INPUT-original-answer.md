COMES NOW, Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff, Catastrophic Solutions LLC d/b/a Hail Specialists, hereinafter "Defendant." Defendant herein files its original answer and counterclaim in the above entitled and numbered cause of action and would respectfully show the Court as follows:

# General Denial

As authorized by Rule 92 of the Texas Rules of Civil Procedure, Defendant denies each and every allegation contained in Plaintiff's Original Petition and all amendments or supplements thereto, and demands strict proof thereof by a preponderance of the evidence of all Plaintiff's allegations.

# Affirmative Defenses

Pleading further, Defendant avers that to the extent Plaintiff failed to mitigate its damages as required by law, any award must be reduced by Plaintiff's failure in this regard.

# Counterclaim: Quantum Meruit

Pleading further, Defendant provided services or materials to Plaintiff, who accepted the services or materials, under such circumstances as would reasonably provide notice to Plaintiff that Defendant expected to be paid.

# Right to Amend

Defendant respectfully reserves the right to amend this Answer and Counter-Claims to Plaintiff's allegations after it has had an opportunity to more closely investigate the claims, as is its right and privilege under the Texas Rules of Civil Procedure and the laws of the State of Texas.
