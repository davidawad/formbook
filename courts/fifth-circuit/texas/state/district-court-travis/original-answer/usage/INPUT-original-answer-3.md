Defendant Cedar Mill Holdings, LLC files this answer to Plaintiff's Original Petition and shows the Court as follows.

# Special Exceptions

Defendant specially excepts to the paragraph of Plaintiff's Original Petition that pleads damages, because it does not state the maximum amount claimed, as Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 47 requires. Defendant asks the Court to order Plaintiff to replead within a time the Court sets, and to strike the paragraph if Plaintiff does not.

# General Denial

Under Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 92, Defendant generally denies each allegation of Plaintiff's Original Petition and asks that Plaintiff be required to prove its allegations by a preponderance of the evidence.

# Verified Denials

Defendant denies that it is liable in the capacity in which Plaintiff has sued it. The Plaintiff sued Defendant as the owner of the warehouse, but Defendant is its tenant. This denial is verified below under Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 93(2).

# Affirmative Defenses

Pleading further, and in the alternative, Defendant asserts the following affirmative defenses under Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 94:

- limitations;
- waiver; and
- failure of consideration.

# Counterclaim

Defendant brings this counterclaim under Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 97 against Plaintiff.

## A. Rule 47 statements

Defendant's damages are within the jurisdictional limits of the Court. Defendant seeks only monetary relief of $325,000 or less, excluding interest, statutory or punitive damages and penalties, and attorney's fees and costs.

## B. Claim for breach of contract

Plaintiff agreed to pay Defendant for storage space, and Plaintiff has not paid. Defendant has performed its obligations.

# Jury Demand

Defendant demands a trial by jury and will pay the jury fee.

# Prayer

For these reasons, Defendant asks that Plaintiff take nothing, that Defendant recover on its counterclaim, and that the Court award Defendant all other relief to which it is entitled.
