Different filers, different offices (Houston plaintiff-side insurance defense firm; an Austin firm for a government defendant). They differ in tier 2 conventions the template therefore keeps as options: the first numbers every paragraph and puts the numeral of each part above its title; the second numbers nothing and runs the numeral in line with the title; the first puts the cause number above the caption, the second does not.
