# Court of Criminal Appeals of Texas: briefs, petitions, motions, and rehearing motions

Alias `texcrimapp`. Scope: papers filed in the Court of Criminal Appeals (CCA): briefs on the merits and amicus briefs, petitions for discretionary review, motion papers, and motions for rehearing. Verified against the Texas Rules of Appellate Procedure, updated with amendments effective February 6, 2026, on 2026-10-21.

## Form of documents (all papers)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEXCRIMAPP-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ by 11 inches, white or nearly white, and opaque. | TRAP 9.4(b). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FORM-002 | Margins are at least one inch on both sides and at the top and bottom. | TRAP 9.4(c). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FORM-003 | Text is double-spaced. Footnotes, block quotations, short lists, and issues or points may be single-spaced. | TRAP 9.4(d). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FORM-004 | A document produced on a computer is printed in a conventional typeface no smaller than 14-point, except footnotes, which are no smaller than 12-point. | TRAP 9.4(e). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FORM-005 | A typewritten document is printed in standard 10-character-per-inch monospaced type. | TRAP 9.4(e). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FORM-006 | The typeface is not named by the rule. The template uses Times New Roman, the project default for a court that names no face. | TRAP 9.4(e) (no face named). | T |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FORM-007 | A paper document is bound so it will not lose its cover; a paper brief or petition has durable front and back covers that are not plastic and are not red, black, or dark blue. | TRAP 9.4(f). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-001 | The front cover states the case style, case number, title of the document, the name of the party filing it, and the name, mailing address, telephone number, fax number (if any), email address, and State Bar of Texas identification number of the lead counsel. | TRAP 9.4(g). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CERT-001 | A computer-generated document subject to a word limit includes a certificate of compliance by counsel or the unrepresented party stating the number of words. The certifier may rely on the word count of the word-processing program. | TRAP 9.4(i)(3). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CERT-002 | A document presented for filing contains a proof of service. In a criminal case, the certificate is signed by the person who made service and states the date and manner of service, the name and address of each person served, and, for an attorney, the name of the party represented. | TRAP 9.5(d), (e). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-PRIV-001 | Unless a statute or court order requires otherwise, a filing does not contain sensitive data in a criminal case: a driver's license, passport, social security, or tax identification number; a bank, credit card, or other financial account number; a birth date; a home address; or the name of a person who was a minor when the offense was committed. The filer certifies compliance by filing. | TRAP 9.10(a), (b), (e). | M |

## Briefs on the merits and amicus briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEXCRIMAPP-LEN-001 | A brief and response in an appellate court (other than a death-penalty direct appeal) is limited to 15,000 words if computer-generated (50 pages if not). | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(B). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-LEN-002 | A brief and response in a direct appeal in a case in which the death penalty was assessed is limited to 37,500 words if computer-generated (125 pages if not). | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(A). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-LEN-003 | A reply brief in an appellate court is limited to 7,500 words if computer-generated (25 pages if not). | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(C). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-LEN-004 | Every word counts toward the limit, including headings, footnotes, and quotations, except the caption, identity of parties and counsel, statement regarding oral argument, table of contents, index of authorities, statement of the case, statement of issues, statement of jurisdiction, statement of procedural history, signature, proof of service, certification, certificate of compliance, and appendix. | TRAP 9.4(i)(1). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-002 | A brief contains, under headings and in this order: identity of parties and counsel; table of contents; index of authorities; statement of the case; statement regarding oral argument (optional in 38.1, one page at most; required in a brief on the merits); statement of issues presented; statement of facts; summary of the argument; argument; and prayer. A brief in a direct appeal need not contain an appendix. | TRAP 38.1(a)-(j), (k); 70.1. | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-003 | A brief on the merits (direct appeal or review granted on a petition) includes a short statement of why oral argument would be helpful, or that oral argument is waived. A party requesting oral argument notes the request on the front cover. | TRAP 70.1; 38.1(e); 39.7. | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-004 | The statement of the case seldom exceeds one-half page and does not discuss the facts. The statement regarding oral argument does not exceed one page. | TRAP 38.1(d), (e). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-005 | An amicus curiae brief complies with the briefing rules for parties, identifies the person or entity on whose behalf it is tendered, discloses the source of any fee paid for preparing it, and certifies that copies were served on all parties. | TRAP 11(a)-(d). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FILE-001 | A respondent's brief on the merits is filed within 30 days after the petitioner's brief. The petitioner's brief is filed within 30 days after review is granted. | TRAP 70.1, 70.2. | M |

## Petitions for discretionary review

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-006 | The petition is addressed to the "Court of Criminal Appeals of Texas" and contains, in this order: (a) identity of the trial judge, parties, and counsel; (b) table of contents; (c) index of authorities; (d) statement regarding oral argument; (e) statement of the case, seldom exceeding half a page; (f) statement of procedural history (dates of the court of appeals opinion or order, of any motion for rehearing, and of its disposition); (g) grounds for review, separately numbered and stated without argument, with record page references where available; (h) argument with supporting authorities; (i) prayer; and (j) appendix containing a copy of any court of appeals opinion. | TRAP 68.4(a)-(j); 68.3(a). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-LEN-005 | A petition for discretionary review is limited to 4,500 words if computer-generated (15 pages if not). | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(E). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-LEN-006 | A reply to a petition for discretionary review is limited to 2,400 words if computer-generated (8 pages if not). | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(H). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FILE-002 | A petition for discretionary review is filed with the clerk of the Court of Criminal Appeals, not the court of appeals. | TRAP 68.3(a), (b). | M |

## Motions and motions for rehearing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-007 | A motion states with particularity the grounds on which it is based, sets out the order or relief sought, and is served and filed with any brief, affidavit, or other paper supporting it. | TRAP 10.1(a)(2)-(4). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-LEN-007 | A motion for rehearing and the response to it are limited to 4,500 words if computer-generated (15 pages if not). The CCA sets no word limit in 9.4(i)(2) for other motions (9.4(i)(2)(G) covers motions in the Supreme Court only). | TRAP 9.4(i)(2)(E), (G). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-CONT-008 | A motion for rehearing briefly and distinctly states the grounds and arguments relied on. A motion for rehearing of an order refusing a petition for discretionary review is grounded only on substantial intervening or other specified significant circumstances, and counsel certifies that it is made in good faith and not for delay. | TRAP 79.2(a), (c). | M |
| TEXCRIMAPP-FILE-003 | A motion for rehearing is filed within 15 days from the date of the judgment or order. A motion for rehearing an order that grants discretionary review may not be filed. | TRAP 79.1. | M |
