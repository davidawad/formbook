# U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Northern District of Texas: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `txnb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Bankruptcy Rules (Revised as of December 1, 2025), TXEB Appendix 5005 (Administrative Procedures for Texas Bankruptcy Courts, adopted December 1, 2004, rev. August 2022), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXNB-FORM-001 | No Local Rule sets the typeface, point size, margins, line spacing, or paper size for an adversary complaint. FRBP 9004(a) requires only that the document be clearly legible, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR 5005-1(d). | A |
| TXNB-FORM-002 | Layout of every pleading: attorney name, state bar number, mailing address, telephone, and email, plus the party represented, in the upper-left corner of the first page; the heading and style below the attorney block; the case number (with the presiding judge's initials) on the right, with the adversary number below it. | LBR 5005-1(c)-(d)(1)-(2). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-001 | Caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010. | M |
| TXNB-CONT-002 | The adversary complaint is filed in the division where the chapter case is pending, unless 28 U.S.C. section 1409 requires otherwise. | LBR 7001-1. | M |
| TXNB-CONT-003 | An adversary proceeding cover sheet accompanies every adversary complaint. | LBR 7003-1. | M |
| TXNB-CONT-004 | The jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the bankruptcy case, and to the district and division. The complaint states whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| TXNB-CONT-005 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| TXNB-CERT-001 | Every pleading's signature block gives the name, state bar number (if applicable), address, telephone number, and email address (if applicable) of the filer, with the firm name and the represented party for an attorney. The attorney signs in an individual name, with address and telephone number. | LBR 5005-1(b); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| TXNB-ELEC-001 | Filed electronically under Appendix 5005. Personal identifiers are omitted or limited to the last four digits of a SSN or financial account number, the initials of a minor, and the year of birth. The rule covers all pleadings and exhibits. | LBR 1007-1(d)(1)-(2); FRBP 9037(a); APP5005 section I. | M |
| TXNB-ELEC-002 | Each pleading attachment is a PDF docketed with the main document. No attachment over 40 pages is filed without an accurate summary, unless authorized by separate order. | APP5005 section III(A)(1)-(2). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXNB-FORM-003 | No Local Rule sets the typeface, point size, margins, line spacing, or paper size for a motion or brief. FRBP 9004(a) requires legibility only, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR 7007-2(a). | A |
| TXNB-FORM-004 | Layout is the same as TXNB-FORM-002 (attorney block upper left on the first page, except on proposed orders; style and caption below it; case and adversary numbers on the right; hearing nature, date, and time below them). | LBR 5005-1(c)-(d). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-006 | A motion conferred on before filing carries a certificate of conference stating that the motion is opposed or unopposed, the date and attorneys of any conference, and why agreement failed. A conference is not required for motions to dismiss, motions for judgment on the pleadings, summary judgment motions, motions for new trial, or when a conference is impracticable. | LBR 7007-1(a)-(b); LBR 9014-1(b). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-007 | Each motion is accompanied by a proposed order set out separately as an exhibit to the motion. An agreed order is signed by the attorneys or parties to the agreement. | LBR 7007-1(c); LBR 9013-1(a). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-008 | An opposed motion is accompanied by a brief containing the contentions of fact or law and the argument and authorities. For motions listed in the table in LBR 7007-1(f) (for example, a motion to dismiss), the brief is required. A brief under 10 pages may appear in the motion document. | LBR 7007-1(d), (f); LBR 9013-1(a). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-009 | A response and brief to an opposed motion are filed within 21 days from the date the motion is filed. | LBR 7007-1(e). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-010 | Motions to which negative notice applies carry the negative notice language, and the movant serves the minimum parties set out in LBR 9007-1(b). | LBR 9007-1(a)-(b). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-011 | A certificate of service is attached to the motion, showing the date and mode of service and the names and addresses served. | LBR 9014-1(b). | M |
| TXNB-CONT-012 | Proposed orders filed with the motion use only the fonts Appendix 5005 permits (Arial, Courier, or Times New Roman for Acrobat Writer 5). A Northern District proposed order has a first-page top margin of 4 inches; all other pages have a 1-inch top margin. | APP5005 section IV.A.2, IV.B. | M |
| TXNB-CERT-002 | The motion's signature block gives the signer's name, state bar number, address, telephone number, and email address. | LBR 5005-1(b); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| TXNB-LEN-001 | Brief: no more than 25 pages, excluding the table of contents and table of authorities; reply brief no more than 10 pages. A brief more than 10 pages includes a table of contents with page references and a table of authorities. | LBR 7007-2(c)-(d). | M |
| TXNB-LEN-002 | Summary judgment: principal brief no more than 50 pages and reply no more than 25 pages, excluding the table of contents and table of authorities. A motion includes a summary on its first page and page citations to any appendix. | LBR 7056-1(c)(1)(A), (e)(2). | M |
| TXNB-LEN-003 | Summary judgment appendix: self-contained; each page 8½ × 11 inches; each page numbered in the lower right. | LBR 7056-1(f)(2)(A)-(C). | M |
| TXNB-LEN-004 | A brief citing an appendix cites each appendix page that supports each assertion about the evidence. | LBR 7007-2(e). | M |
| TXNB-ELEC-003 | A complete paper copy of the filing, with attachments and related briefs, should be delivered to the Bankruptcy Clerk within 24 hours of e-filing for the documents listed in LBR 9013-1(b), including summary judgment motions and motions to dismiss under FRBP 7012. | LBR 9013-1(b)(1)-(5). | M |
| TXNB-ELEC-004 | Motions to file under seal require a motion and the presiding judge's permission unless a statute or rule permits sealed filing. | LBR 9077-1(b). | M |
| TXNB-ELEC-005 | Judge-specific paper copy requirements are in the Attorney Desk Reference (not reviewed). | APP5005 section III(A)(5)(a). | A |
