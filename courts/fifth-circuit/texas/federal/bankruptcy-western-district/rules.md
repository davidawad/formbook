# U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Western District of Texas: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `txwb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Court Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Western District of Texas (Order Adopting Local Rules with Attachment, signed December 17, 2024, effective February 3, 2025), the ECF Procedures for Texas Bankruptcy Courts (Amended January 12, 2017), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXWB-FORM-001 | The Local Court Rules set no typeface, point size, margins, line spacing, or paper size for a complaint. FRBP 9004(a) requires only that the document be clearly legible, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a); LR (no form rule for complaints). | A |
| TXWB-CONT-001 | Caption identifies the district and division (Austin, El Paso, Midland/Odessa, San Antonio, or Waco); the name of the debtor; the bankruptcy case number with the initials of the assigned judge; and the chapter. | LR 9004-1(a)(1)-(4); FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010. | M |
| TXWB-CONT-002 | Title of the document designates the relief sought in the document and in the proposed order. | LR 9004-1(b). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-003 | The jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the bankruptcy case, and to the district and division. The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| TXWB-CONT-004 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| TXWB-CERT-001 | Every pleading is signed and shows the signer's name, mailing address, email address, and telephone number, with the attorney's State Bar Number (and the issuing state if not Texas). An electronic signature is a signature for FRBP 9011. | LR 9011-1; FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| TXWB-ELEC-001 | Filed under the ECF Procedures. For the Western District, a single PDF file (document or attachment) may not exceed 25 MB; larger files are divided into multiple PDFs and accurately described. | LR 5005-1; ECF section III(A)(1), (6). | M |
| TXWB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are redacted or omitted under FRBP 9037(a). | FRBP 9037(a). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXWB-FORM-002 | The Local Court Rules set no typeface, point size (other than the negative notice font in TXWB-CONT-006), margins, line spacing, or paper size for a motion or brief. FRBP 9004(a) requires legibility only, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a); LR (no general form rule). | A |
| TXWB-CONT-005 | Caption and title are the same as TXWB-CONT-001 and TXWB-CONT-002 for the bankruptcy case or adversary proceeding. | LR 9004-1(a)-(b). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-006 | Negative notice: the form language in LR 9014-1(a) is in bold face type of at least 12 point, placed immediately below the caption and before the body of the pleading. | LR 9014-1(a). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-007 | A form of proposed order is submitted at filing, attached as an exhibit to the motion and separately uploaded for the Court's consideration. Its title designates the relief sought. | LR 9013-1(b); LR 9004-1(b); ECF section IV.E.1 (Order Upload). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-008 | Proposed orders use the fonts Appendix 5005 permits (ECF section IV.A.2), and a Western District order has a first-page top margin of 4 inches with a 1-inch top margin on all other pages (ECF section IV.E.3(a)). | ECF section IV.A.2, IV.E.3(a). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-009 | Certificate of service is signed by an attorney or a party, certifies the manner and date of service and the parties served, and lists each entity served by means other than the Court's electronic transmission facilities, with addresses. | LR 9013-1(c)(1)-(2). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-010 | Entities to be served at a minimum: in chapter 7, the debtor, trustee, court-approved committees, and their counsel; in chapter 11, the debtor, committee, trustee, counsel, twenty largest unsecured creditors, parties who filed notices of appearance, U.S. Trustee; in chapter 12 or 13, the debtor, debtor's counsel, trustee. In an adversary proceeding, service is on all counsel and parties without counsel. | LR 9013-1(d)(1)(A)-(C), (d)(2). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-011 | In an adversary proceeding, motions are governed by LR 9013-1 and 9014-1. Responses to a dispositive motion are due 21 days after service; to a non-dispositive motion, 14 days; a reply to a motion is due 7 days after the response. | LR 7007-1 (intro); LR 7007-1(b)(2), (c)(2). | M |
| TXWB-CONT-012 | Sealing: documents are sealed only by court order, on motion with notice to parties in interest; the form of order is submitted with the sealing motion. | LR 9018-1(a)(1)-(3). | M |
| TXWB-CERT-002 | The signature block gives the signer's name, mailing address, email address, telephone number, and State Bar Number. | LR 9011-1. | M |
| TXWB-LEN-001 | Adversary proceeding motions: a dispositive motion (FRBP 7012 or 7056) is limited to 20 pages, a non-dispositive motion to 10 pages; a response is limited to 20 or 10 pages; a reply to a dispositive motion is limited to 10 pages, and to 5 pages for a non-dispositive motion. Caption, signature block, certificate, and accompanying documents are excluded, unless the Court authorizes more. | LR 7007-1(a)(2), (b)(3), (c)(3). | M |
| TXWB-LEN-002 | Non-adversary bankruptcy motions: no page limit is stated in the Local Court Rules. | LR (none stated). | A |
| TXWB-ELEC-003 | Motions and responses are e-filed as PDFs under the ECF Procedures with the 25 MB limit in TXWB-ELEC-001. | ECF section III(A)(1), (6). | M |
