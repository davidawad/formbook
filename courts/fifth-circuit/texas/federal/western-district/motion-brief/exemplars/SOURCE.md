## Why this one

It is a Rule 12 motion with its brief in one document, the form CV-7(c)(1) describes ("legal authority ... must be cited in the motion, and not in a separate brief"). It has the full motion-brief furniture: cover, TOC, TOA, a certificate of service. It is a single filer, so the tier-2 second-filer check is not met for this doc type; see README.md.
