# Western District of Texas: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `txwd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Western District of Texas Local Civil Rules CV-3, CV-5, CV-5.2, CV-7 and CV-10 as posted on the court's civil local rules page (the individual rule PDFs carry no edition date); the Administrative Policies and Procedures for Electronic Filing in Civil and Criminal Cases (adopted January 20, 2006, revised December 11, 2008, effective January 15, 2009); and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition (printed on the rules PDF cover), on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXWD-FORM-001 | Pleadings are typed or printed in 12-point or larger font, including footnotes, double-spaced, on 8½ × 11 inch paper, with one-inch margins on all sides. Headings, footnotes, and quotations more than two lines long may be single-spaced. Matches the default eval (1 in margins, 12 pt body). | LCR-CV-10(a) | M |
| TXWD-FORM-002 | Every electronically filed document keeps a minimum one-inch top margin for the file stamp. Matches the default eval. | ECF § 12(b) | M |
| TXWD-FORM-003 | A PDF converted from a word-processing program is created in 12-point font and double-spaced, and is converted with a fully licensed PDF program (not scanned). | ECF § 12(a), (c) | M |
| TXWD-FORM-004 | Each PDF submitted for electronic filing is no larger than 50 megabytes. | ECF § 12(g) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-001 | Caption is endorsed with the style of the case and the descriptive name of the document. | LCR-CV-10(a); FRCP 10(a) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-003 | A claim for relief states the grounds for jurisdiction, a short and plain statement of the claim, and a demand for relief. | FRCP 8(a)(1)-(3) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-004 | A complete, executed AO Form JS 44 civil cover sheet accompanies each civil case; parties advise the Court of related cases through it or otherwise. | LCR-CV-3(a) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-005 | A nongovernmental corporate party files its Rule 7.1 disclosure statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request to the court. | FRCP 7.1(a)(1), (b)(1) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-006 | A jury demand is made in a pleading or by a separate written demand served within 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue, and filed under Rule 5(d). | FRCP 38(b) | M |
| TXWD-CERT-001 | Signature block of a represented party's filing gives the attorney's mailing address, e-mail address, signature, state bar card number, and telephone and fax numbers with area codes. | LCR-CV-10(b) | M |
| TXWD-CERT-002 | An electronically filed document includes a signature block; the Filing User's login and password serve as the signature, and a typed "/s/" name is acceptable where a signature would otherwise appear. | ECF § 13(a) | M |
| TXWD-ELEC-001 | Documents filed electronically comply with the Administrative Policies and Procedures for Electronic Filing. | LCR-CV-5(a); ECF | M |
| TXWD-ELEC-002 | Filings containing an individual's social-security or taxpayer-identification number, birth date, minor's name, or financial-account number include only the permitted partial identifiers. | FRCP 5.2(a) | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXWD-FORM-005 | Motions meet the Rule CV-10 form: 12-point or larger font including footnotes, double-spaced, 8½ × 11 inch, one-inch margins on all sides. Matches the default eval. | LCR-CV-7(a); LCR-CV-10(a) | M |
| TXWD-FORM-006 | One-inch minimum top margin and 12-point double-spaced PDF conversion as for pleadings. | ECF § 12(b), (c) | M |
| TXWD-LEN-001 | A motion is limited to 20 pages, or 10 pages for a discovery or case management motion, unless the court authorizes otherwise. The page limit excludes the caption, signature block, any certificate, and accompanying documents. | LCR-CV-7(c)(2) | M |
| TXWD-LEN-002 | A response is limited to 20 pages (10 pages for a discovery or case management motion); a reply is limited to 10 pages (5 pages for a discovery or case management motion). Both exclude the caption, signature block, certificate, and accompanying documents. | LCR-CV-7(d)(3), (e)(3) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-007 | Legal authority supporting a motion is cited in the motion itself, not in a separate brief. An appendix may be filed specifying the factual basis; it must include the affidavits, transcripts, or documents supporting the facts relied on. | LCR-CV-7(c)(1) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-008 | A motion states its grounds and cites the applicable rule, statute, or other authority justifying the relief sought. | LCR-CV-7(c)(1) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-009 | A proposed order is filed with each discovery and case management motion; proposed orders are filed through the clerk's office, not sent to the judge. | LCR-CV-7(f); LCR-CV-5(b) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-010 | A nondispositive motion states within its body that counsel conferred in a good-faith attempt to resolve the matter, and certifies the specific reason no agreement could be made. A motion is "unopposed" only after an actual conference. | LCR-CV-7(g) | M |
| TXWD-CONT-011 | A motion for leave to file a pleading or other submission attaches the executed proposed filing as an exhibit. | LCR-CV-7(b) | M |
| TXWD-SEAL-001 | A sealed document is not attached to the public motion; it is filed as an exhibit to a sealing motion, which states the factual basis and complies with CV-7 and CV-10. Sealing motions are disfavored; ECF § 10 requires the sealing motion to be filed electronically with the proposed document as a PDF attachment. | LCR-CV-5.2(a)-(c); ECF § 10(a) | M |
| TXWD-CERT-003 | Signature block and attorney information as for pleadings. | LCR-CV-10(b); ECF § 13(a) | M |
| TXWD-ELEC-003 | Electronic filing complies with the Administrative Policies and Procedures. | LCR-CV-5(a); ECF | M |
| TXWD-ELEC-004 | Redaction of personal identifiers under Rule 5.2(a) in the brief and in exhibits. | FRCP 5.2(a) | M |
