# U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Eastern District of Texas: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `txeb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (Revised: August 22, 2022), TXEB Appendix 5005 (rev. August 2022), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXEB-FORM-001 | No Local Rule or Appendix sets the typeface, point size, margins, line spacing, or paper size for an adversary complaint. FRBP 9004(a) requires only that the document be clearly legible, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR 7003-1(c). | A |
| TXEB-CONT-001 | Caption identifies the district and division; the debtor's name, address, and last four digits of the taxpayer identification number; the bankruptcy case number and chapter; the plaintiffs and defendants; a space for the adversary case number; and a descriptive title of the relief sought. | LBR 7010-1(1)-(5); LBR 7003-1(c); FRBP 7010 (FRCP 10(a) and Form 416 caption). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-002 | The jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the bankruptcy case the adversary relates to, and the district and division where it is pending. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| TXEB-CONT-003 | The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| TXEB-CONT-004 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-005 | The complaint is filed with a completed adversary proceeding cover sheet and a summons for each defendant. | LBR 7003-1(a)(1)-(3). | M |
| TXEB-CERT-001 | Every pleading states the signer's name, address, and telephone number, and the attorney's state bar number. | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 9011-1. | M |
| TXEB-ELEC-001 | Filed under Appendix 5005. Personal identifiers are omitted or limited to the last four digits of a SSN or TIN, the initials of a minor, the year of birth, and the last four digits of a financial account number; driver's license numbers are redacted when a copy is attached. | LBR 5005-1; LBR 9013-1(d); APP5005 section I; FRBP 9037(a). | M |
| TXEB-ELEC-002 | Each pleading attachment is a PDF docketed with the main document. No attachment over 40 pages is filed without an accurate summary, unless authorized by separate order. | APP5005 section III(A)(1)-(2). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXEB-FORM-002 | No Local Rule or Appendix sets the typeface, point size, margins, line spacing, or paper size for a motion or brief. FRBP 9004(a) requires legibility only, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a). | A |
| TXEB-CONT-010 | Caption is the same as TXEB-CONT-001 for the bankruptcy case: district and division; debtor name, address, and last four TIN digits; case number; chapter. | LBR 9004-1(a); FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-011 | The title designates the relief sought in both the motion and the proposed order. One motion is filed per form of relief, except for the combinations listed in the rule. | LBR 9004-1(b)-(c). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-012 | Each motion, application, or objection is accompanied by a proposed order whose title describes the relief and refers to the pleading it accompanies. The order must clearly show granted or denied (or sustained or denied). A missing order may result in dismissal of the pleading. | LBR 9013-1(b)(1)-(3). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-013 | Each motion includes any negative notice language required for its type of relief by the Local Rules. | LBR 9013-1(a). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-014 | Authorities and argument may be briefed within the motion; a separate brief is not required. A separate brief may be filed no later than three business days before the hearing. | LBR 9013-1(g). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-015 | A motion or other document that requires notice to interested parties, or may adversely affect one, contains a certificate of service stating the parties served (with addresses), the method, and the date, signed by an attorney or an agent. | LBR 9013-1(e)(1)-(3). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-016 | A motion that can be granted only after notice and hearing (including stay relief) is served, at a minimum, on the parties listed for the chapter in LBR 9013-1(f)(1)(A)-(C). Any summary served must state the relevant facts, the negative notice language, and that a full copy is available free on request. | LBR 9013-1(f)(1)-(2). | M |
| TXEB-CONT-017 | Proposed orders filed with the motion use only the fonts Appendix 5005 permits (Arial, Courier, or Times New Roman for Acrobat Writer 5). In the Eastern District, the order ends with an open space of at least 1.5 inches for the judge's signature. | APP5005 section IV.A.2, IV.D.4. | M |
| TXEB-CERT-002 | The motion's signature block gives the signer's name, address, telephone number, and state bar number. | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 9011-1. | M |
| TXEB-LEN-001 | Summary judgment motion and brief: no more than 30 pages, excluding attachments. Response to a summary judgment motion: no more than 30 pages. Reply: no more than 10 pages. | LBR 7056-1(a)-(b). | M |
| TXEB-LEN-002 | No page limit is stated for motions other than summary judgment motions. | LBR 7056-1. | A |
| TXEB-ELEC-003 | Evidence submissions for a summary judgment motion and any response in opposition are delivered in paper to chambers within 24 hours of e-filing, unless otherwise ordered. | APP5005 section III(A)(5)(c)(3). | M |
