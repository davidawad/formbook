- Why this filing: it is a real E.D. Tex. defense motion to dismiss under Rule 12(b)(6) (with 12(b)(1)), filed in a division of this district. It is long (the brief runs 46 pages plus front matter); the sample uses the first ten brief pages and the end.
- What the filing does not have: a "Statement of the Issue(s)" heading. CV-7(a)(1) requires dispositive motions to "contain a statement of the issues to be decided by the court" (content, not a heading). This filing's introduction states its grounds but has no separate statement. This is a point for David to decide (see `../README.md`, known gaps).
