# Eastern District of Texas: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `txed`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas (as of February 9, 2026) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXED-FORM-001 | Every document, including a complaint, is double-spaced and in a font no smaller than 12-point type. When filed on paper, it is on 8½ × 11 inch white paper. Preexisting documentary exhibits and attachments are excluded from this rule. The Local Rules set no margin rule for a complaint. | LR CV-10(a)(4)-(5). | M |
| TXED-FORM-002 | Each exhibit or attachment is submitted and described with specificity as a separate PDF document. | LR CV-5(a)(6); CV-10(a). | M |
| TXED-FORM-003 | Attachments and exhibits are in searchable PDF form whenever possible. | LR CV-7(a). | A |
| TXED-CONT-001 | Each document is endorsed with the style and number of the action, and its caption gives the name and party designation of the filer and a statement of the document's character. | LR CV-10(a)(1)-(2). | M |
| TXED-CONT-002 | Caption contains the court's name, a title naming all the parties, a file number, and a Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a). | M |
| TXED-CONT-003 | Jurisdiction, claims, and relief are stated under FRCP 8(a). Claims are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 8(a); FRCP 10(b). | M |
| TXED-CONT-004 | The civil cover sheet, Form JS 44, is filed with the complaint. | LR CV-3(a). | M |
| TXED-CONT-005 | A non-governmental corporate party files its disclosure statement under FRCP 7.1(a) with its first appearance. In a diversity case, FRCP 7.1(a)(2) also applies. | FRCP 7.1(a)(1)-(2). | M |
| TXED-CONT-006 | Jury demand: a separate document titled as a jury demand is encouraged. If the demand is in a pleading, the word "jury" appears at the top, immediately below the case number. | LR CV-38(a); FRCP 38(b)(1). | M |
| TXED-CERT-001 | Signed by the lead attorney, or by an attorney with the lead attorney's permission. Under the signature: the attorney's individual name, state bar number, office address with zip code, telephone and facsimile numbers, and email address. The signature is an image or "/s/" typed in the signature space. | LR CV-10(a)(3); CV-11(a)-(b); CV-5(a)(5); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| TXED-LEN-001 | Civil rights complaint: no more than 20 pages, excluding attachments, absent leave. A document over 10 pages includes a table of contents and table of authorities with page references. | LR CV-3(d). | M |
| TXED-ELEC-001 | Filed electronically through the Electronic Filing System (pro se exceptions per CV-5(a)(1)(B)). No single electronic file may exceed 50 MB; larger files are divided and described. | LR CV-5(a)(1), (4). | M |
| TXED-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are omitted or redacted under FRCP 5.2(a): last four digits of a SSN or TIN, year of birth, minor's initials, last four digits of a financial account number. (LR CV-5.2 covers transcripts only.) | FRCP 5.2(a); LR CV-5.2. | M |
| TXED-ELEC-003 | Hyperlinks may not replace the standard citation format; complete citations appear in the text. | LR CV-10(e). | M |
| TXED-ELEC-004 | If the e-filed document exceeds 10 pages, including attachments, a paper copy goes to the presiding judge's chambers with the Notice of Electronic Filing attached, unless the judge has opted out. | LR CV-5(a)(9); CV-10(b). | M |
| TXED-ELEC-005 | A sealed complaint is filed with the clerk on paper with a motion to seal (CV-5(a)(1)(D)). A sealed document states "Filed Under Seal" at the top and requires a statement by counsel. | LR CV-5(a)(1)(D), (a)(7)(A)-(B). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXED-FORM-004 | The motion and its briefing are one document. A response and its briefing are one document. Each motion is a separate document, except motions for alternative relief. | LR CV-7(a), (c), (d). | M |
| TXED-FORM-005 | Motions, responses, replies, and proposed orders are searchable PDFs that do not restrict copying, highlighting, or commenting. | LR CV-7(a). | M |
| TXED-CONT-007 | The motion's caption names the filer's party designation and the character of the motion, for example "Defendant John Doe's Motion to Dismiss under Rule 12(b)(6)". | LR CV-10(a)(2). | M |
| TXED-CONT-008 | A proposed order accompanies each motion and each response: a separate searchable and editable PDF, endorsed with the style and number of the action, with no date or signature block. | LR CV-7(a), (d). | M |
| TXED-CONT-009 | A case-dispositive motion states the issues the court must decide; a response answers the movant's statement of issues. | LR CV-7(a)(1). | M |
| TXED-CONT-010 | A certificate of conference appears at the end of the motion, after the certificate of service. An opposed motion's certificate states the personal conference, its date and manner, its participants, and why agreement could not be reached. | LR CV-7(h)-(i). | M |
| TXED-CONT-011 | Summary judgment motion: a statement of the issues to be decided and a "Statement of Undisputed Material Facts." A response answers both. | LR CV-56(a)-(b). | M |
| TXED-CONT-012 | Summary judgment evidence is attached as excerpts cited by page and, if possible, by line. Cited portions are highlighted or underlined unless the citation covers the whole page. Only relevant cited excerpts are attached. | LR CV-56(d); CV-7(b). | A |
| TXED-CONT-013 | A motion to reconsider names the action and the docket number to be reconsidered in its title. | LR CV-7(a)(4). | M |
| TXED-CERT-002 | Signature block as in TXED-CERT-001, signed by the lead attorney. | LR CV-11(b); CV-5(a)(5). | M |
| TXED-LEN-002 | Case-dispositive motion and response: no more than 30 pages, excluding attachments, absent leave. Reply or sur-reply: no more than 10 pages, excluding attachments. | LR CV-7(a)(1). | M |
| TXED-LEN-003 | Non-dispositive motion and response: no more than 15 pages, excluding attachments, absent leave. Reply or sur-reply: no more than 5 pages, excluding attachments. | LR CV-7(a)(2). | M |
| TXED-LEN-004 | Summary judgment motions collectively: 60 pages per party for motions, 60 pages for responses, 20 pages for replies, and 20 pages for sur-replies, excluding attachments. | LR CV-7(a)(3). | M |
| TXED-LEN-005 | A response is due 14 days after service of the motion (21 days for a summary judgment motion). A reply is due 7 days after service of the response; a sur-reply, 7 days after the reply. | LR CV-7(e)-(f). | M |
| TXED-AI-001 | Use of generative AI does not change the litigant's responsibility under FRCP 11; the litigant must review and verify all content. No disclosure requirement is stated. | LR CV-11(g). | A |
