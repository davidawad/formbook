# Southern District of Texas: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `txsd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, effective May 1, 2000, reprinted March 2026 (cover: "Amended LR 16.4, effective March 27, 2026"), and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition (printed on the rules PDF cover), on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXSD-FORM-001 | Papers are on 8½ × 11 inch paper and double spaced, and are paginated. | LR 10.2 | M |
| TXSD-FORM-002 | LR 10.2 also says papers are stapled at the top only and punched with two holes, and may not be in covers. These instructions are written for paper filing; they are not a template layout rule. | LR 10.2 | A |
| TXSD-FORM-003 | No local typeface, point-size, or margin rule was found in the S.D. Tex. Local Rules; the template's font and margins are not mandated by a retrieved local rule. | LR (no rule found) | A |
| TXSD-CONT-001 | Caption includes the name and party designation of the party filing, and a statement of the document's character. | LR 10.1; FRCP 10(a) | M |
| TXSD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| TXSD-CONT-003 | A claim for relief states the grounds for jurisdiction, a short and plain statement of the claim, and a demand for relief. | FRCP 8(a)(1)-(3) | M |
| TXSD-CONT-004 | A civil cover sheet (Form JS44c) accompanies all original pleadings. | LR 3 | M |
| TXSD-CONT-005 | Parties advise the Court of related current or recent litigation and of directly affected non-parties. | LR 5.2 | M |
| TXSD-CONT-006 | A nongovernmental corporate party files its Rule 7.1 disclosure statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request to the court. | FRCP 7.1(a)(1), (b)(1) | M |
| TXSD-CONT-007 | A jury demand is made in a pleading or by a separate written demand served within 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue, and filed under Rule 5(d). | FRCP 38(b) | M |
| TXSD-CERT-001 | Signature block gives the attorney's individual name, the designation "Attorney-in-Charge," state bar number, S.D. Tex. bar number, office address with ZIP code, telephone with area code, and e-mail address. | LR 11.3(A) | M |
| TXSD-CERT-002 | A certificate of service appears at the end, reflecting how and when service was made or why service is not required. | LR 5.3; FRCP 5(b) | M |
| TXSD-ELEC-001 | Documents are filed by electronic means that comply with the Court's procedures; the notice of electronic filing constitutes service on registered users. | LR 5.1 | M |
| TXSD-ELEC-002 | Filings containing an individual's social-security or taxpayer-identification number, birth date, minor's name, or financial-account number include only the permitted partial identifiers. | FRCP 5.2(a) | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXSD-FORM-004 | Papers are on 8½ × 11 inch paper, double spaced, and paginated. | LR 10.2 | M |
| TXSD-FORM-005 | No local page limit, typeface, point-size, or margin rule was found for motions or briefs in the S.D. Tex. Local Rules. | LR (no rule found) | A |
| TXSD-CONT-008 | An opposed motion is in writing, includes or is accompanied by authority, is accompanied by a separate proposed order, and (except for motions under Rule 12(b), (c), (e), (f), and 56) contains an averment that the movant conferred with the respondent and that counsel cannot agree. | LR 7.1(A)-(D) | M |
| TXSD-CONT-009 | An unopposed motion and its proposed order bear "unopposed" in the caption. | LR 7.2 | M |
| TXSD-CONT-010 | Evidence not of record is filed with the motion or response as proof by affidavit or other documentary evidence. | LR 7.7 | M |
| TXSD-CONT-011 | A response is in writing, includes or is accompanied by authority, and is accompanied by a separate form order denying the relief sought. A reply may be filed within 7 days after the response, unless the presiding judge directs otherwise. | LR 7.4(A)-(E) | M |
| TXSD-CAP-001 | The caption names the filing party and the document's character, for example "Defendant John Doe's Motion for Partial Summary Judgment." | LR 10.1; FRCP 10(a) | M |
| TXSD-CERT-003 | Signature block and certificate of service as for complaints. | LR 11.3(A); LR 5.3 | M |
| TXSD-ELEC-003 | Electronic filing complies with the Court's procedures; notice of electronic filing constitutes service. | LR 5.1 | M |
| TXSD-ELEC-004 | Redaction of personal identifiers under Rule 5.2(a) in the brief and in exhibits. | FRCP 5.2(a) | M |
