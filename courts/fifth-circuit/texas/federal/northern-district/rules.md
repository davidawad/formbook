## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXND-FORM-001 | No local typeface, point size, line spacing, or margin rule was retrieved for N.D. Tex., and FRCP 10 sets none. The template's page form is therefore not mandated by any retrieved rule. | FRCP 10 | A |
| TXND-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court's name, a title naming all parties, a file number, and a Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| TXND-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances; each claim founded on a separate transaction is stated in a separate count where that promotes clarity. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| TXND-CONT-003 | A claim for relief states a short and plain statement of the grounds for jurisdiction (unless already established), a short and plain statement of the claim, and a demand for the relief sought. | FRCP 8(a)(1)-(3) | M |
| TXND-CONT-004 | A nongovernmental corporate party files a Rule 7.1(a)(1) disclosure statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request to the court. In a diversity action, a Rule 7.1(a)(2) citizenship statement is also required. | FRCP 7.1(a)(1)-(2), (b)(1) | M |
| TXND-CONT-005 | A jury demand is made in a pleading or by a separate written demand served within 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue, and filed under Rule 5(d). | FRCP 38(b) | M |
| TXND-CERT-001 | The signed complaint states the signer's address, e-mail address, and telephone number. | FRCP 11(a) | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXND-FORM-002 | No local page-size, typeface, point-size, spacing, margin, or page-limit rule was retrieved for N.D. Tex. FRCP 7(b)(2) applies the caption and form rules for pleadings to motions and other papers, but sets no size or spacing requirement. | FRCP 7(b)(2) | A |
| TXND-CONT-006 | A request for a court order is made by motion that is in writing, states with particularity the grounds for seeking the order, and states the relief sought. | FRCP 7(b)(1)(A)-(C) | M |
| TXND-CAP-001 | The motion or brief carries the Rule 10(a) caption: court name, title, file number, and designation of the document. | FRCP 7(b)(2), 10(a) | M |
| TXND-CERT-002 | A signed motion or brief states the signer's address, e-mail address, and telephone number. | FRCP 11(a) | M |
| TXND-ELEC-002 | Filings containing protected personal identifiers are redacted under Rule 5.2(a) in the brief and in any exhibit. | FRCP 5.2(a) | M |
