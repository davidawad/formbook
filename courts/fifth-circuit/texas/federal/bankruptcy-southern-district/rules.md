# U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Southern District of Texas: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `txsb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Bankruptcy Local Rules (Effective March 3, 2026; footer dated September 22, 2025), the ECF Administrative Procedures for Texas Bankruptcy Courts (Amended January 12, 2017), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXSB-FORM-001 | Neither the Local Rules nor the ECF Procedures set the typeface, point size, margins, line spacing, or paper size for a pleading. FRBP 9004(a) requires only that the document be clearly legible, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR (no form rule). | A |
| TXSB-CONT-001 | Caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document; the caption substantially conforms to the Official Form for an adversary caption. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010. | M |
| TXSB-CONT-002 | Title of the pleading identifies the filing party and briefly describes the pleading. | LBR 9013-1(a). | M |
| TXSB-CONT-003 | The jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the bankruptcy case, and to the district and division. The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. A local statement of consent is required when the complaint says the proceeding is core. | FRBP 7008; LBR 7008-1. | M |
| TXSB-CONT-004 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| TXSB-CERT-001 | The signature block states the signer's address and telephone number. The signature is a scanned image of a wet signature or an electronic signature. | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 5005-1(b); ECF section III(B). | M |
| TXSB-ELEC-001 | Filed electronically as a PDF under the ECF Procedures. Each PDF file is at most 5 MB (60 pages) for the Southern District; larger documents are divided into multiple PDFs and accurately described. | LBR 5005-1(a); ECF section III(A)(1), (6). | M |
| TXSB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are redacted or omitted under FRBP 9037(a). A party may file a redacted document in the public record, and the unredacted version under seal if needed. | FRBP 9037(a); LBR 9037-1(b), (d). | M |
| TXSB-ELEC-003 | Pleading attachments are PDF attachments docketed with the main document under one docket entry. | ECF section III(A)(2), III(C)(1). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TXSB-FORM-002 | Neither the Local Rules nor the ECF Procedures set the typeface, point size, margins, line spacing, or paper size for a motion or brief. FRBP 9004(a) requires legibility only, so the default eval governs these points. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR (no form rule). | A |
| TXSB-CONT-005 | Caption is the same as TXSB-CONT-001 for the bankruptcy case, and the title identifies the filing party and describes the motion (LBR 9013-1(a)). | FRBP 9004(b); LBR 9013-1(a). | M |
| TXSB-CONT-006 | Negative notice: the form language in LBR 9013-1(b) appears immediately below the title, before the body. Movants who self-calendar add the notice in LBR 9013-1(c); an emergency motion has "Emergency" in its title and the additional paragraph in LBR 9013-1(i). | LBR 9013-1(b), (c), (i). | M |
| TXSB-CONT-007 | Every motion, application, objection, and response is accompanied by a proposed order. | LBR 9013-1(h); ECF section IV. | M |
| TXSB-CONT-008 | Proposed orders use only the fonts that the ECF Procedures permit for Bankruptcy Noticing Center processing (Courier, Helvetica, or Times New Roman in Acrobat Writer 3 or 4; Arial, Courier, or Times New Roman in Acrobat Writer 5). A proposed order is submitted as an attachment to the main document at filing. | ECF section IV.A.2, IV.C.1; LBR 9013-1(h). | M |
| TXSB-CONT-009 | Service on the listed main-case parties (debtor, debtor's counsel, trustee, examiner, committees, parties who filed notices of appearance, the twenty largest unsecured creditors, lienholders on affected property, the U.S. Trustee, and parties the court ordered noticed), and on all parties to an adversary proceeding. Certificate of service lists those served. | LBR 9013-1(d), (f). | M |
| TXSB-CONT-010 | A response to a motion (other than a motion for stay relief) includes a certificate stating either that a conference was held and the parties could not resolve the matter, or the dates the respondent tried to contact the movant and why no conference was held. | LBR 9013-1(g)(1). | M |
| TXSB-CONT-011 | Motions in adversary proceedings are governed by LBR 9013-1 (and 9014-1 for contested matters). | LBR 7007-1. | M |
| TXSB-CONT-012 | Exhibit exchange for contested matters and adversary proceedings with a response: exhibits, exhibit lists, and witness lists are exchanged by noon on the Day of Exchange in Table 1; exhibits are tabbed and bound; each page of an exhibit longer than 10 pages is numbered or Bates labeled. | LBR 9013-2(a)-(c), (e)-(f). | M |
| TXSB-CERT-002 | The signature block gives the signer's address and telephone number, and the signature meets FRBP 9011 and LBR 5005-1(b). | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 5005-1(b). | M |
| TXSB-LEN-001 | No page limit for a motion or brief is stated in the Local Rules. | LBR (none stated). | A |
| TXSB-ELEC-004 | Motions and responses are e-filed as PDFs under the ECF Procedures, with the 5 MB (60-page) limit in TXSB-ELEC-001. Certificates of service may be filed separately from the main document. | ECF section III(A)(3), (6). | M |
