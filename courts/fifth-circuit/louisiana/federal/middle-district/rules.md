# Middle District of Louisiana: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `lamd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda in the Middle District of Louisiana. Verified on 2026-10-17 against the court's posted Local Rules (effective August 18, 2022) and the cited Federal Rules. Court-wide requirements apply to both doc types; differences appear below.

## Doc-type differences

- **Complaint:** satisfy FRCP 8(a), 10(a)-(b), and file the separate civil cover sheet and interested-persons certificate required by LR 10(a)(3) and 7.1.
- **Motion memorandum:** contents follow the motion and the applicable LR 7 procedures. Where a motion is required to have a memorandum, use a separate filing as required by the Local Rules and any judge-specific order.
