# Eastern District of Louisiana: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `laed`. Scope: civil complaints and supporting/opposing motion memoranda in the Eastern District of Louisiana. Verified against the current court-posted Local Civil Rules (revised February 4, 2025) and the cited Federal Rules on 2026-10-17. The same court-wide requirements apply to both doc types; the last section records their differences.

## Length and motion practice

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAED-LEN-001 | A trial brief or motion memorandum supporting or opposing a motion may not exceed 25 pages, excluding exhibits, without prior leave. A reply memorandum may not exceed 10 pages, excluding exhibits. | LR 7.7, p. 13. | M |
| LAED-CONT-006 | A contested motion is accompanied by a separate memorandum giving concise reasons and authorities. A motion relying on facts outside the record is accompanied by the supporting evidence. | LR 7.4, p. 12. | M |
| LAED-CONT-007 | An opposed motion's memorandum is due no later than eight days before the noticed submission date; a reply is due by 4:00 p.m. two working days before that date. | LR 7.5, p. 12. | M |
| LAED-CERT-001 | No certificate of service is required for a post-complaint filing served electronically when all parties are electronic filers receiving CM/ECF notice. Otherwise state the parties served and the non-electronic means of service. | LR 5.4, p. 8; FRCP 5(d)(1)(B). | M |

## Doc-type differences

- **Complaint:** include the civil cover sheet at initial filing; the complaint must substantially follow a clerk form. Include applicable Rule 8(a) allegations, Rule 10(a) caption and Rule 10(b) numbered paragraphs. Separate summons and service filings are governed by the applicable rules.
- **Motion memorandum:** the motion itself and its memorandum are separate filings for contested motions; the memo is subject to the 25-page cap. A reply memorandum is subject to the 10-page cap. The underlying motion, proposed order, and evidence are separate filing components, not template text.
