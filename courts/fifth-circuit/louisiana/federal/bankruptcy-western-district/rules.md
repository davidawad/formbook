Alias `lawb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs.

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAWB-FORM-001 | No LBR sets a typeface, point size, line spacing, margin, or page limit for an adversary complaint. The only form rule is that the document be clearly legible. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR 7007-2(a) (briefs only) | A |
| LAWB-CONT-001 | Caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document; it must substantially conform to Official Form 416. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010 | M |
| LAWB-CONT-002 | Every adversary proceeding is accompanied by an adversary proceeding cover sheet (Official Form 104 is the national version; the LBR does not name a form). | LBR 7003-1; OF104 | M |
| LAWB-CONT-003 | Jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the main case and the district and division where the adversary is pending. The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008 | M |
| LAWB-CONT-004 | A nongovernmental corporate party other than the debtor files a corporate ownership statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b) | M |
| LAWB-CERT-001 | The signature block of every pleading shows the name, state bar number (if applicable), address, telephone number, and e-mail address (if applicable) of the party or attorney filing it. | LBR 5005-1(b); FRBP 9011(a) | M |
| LAWB-ELEC-001 | Documents are signed and filed under the Administrative Procedures; the Clerk may specify electronic practices consistent with the Judicial Conference's technical standards. | LBR 9001-1(a); LBR 5005-2 | M |
| LAWB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are limited to the forms in FRBP 9037(a). A party that files personal information moves ex parte to restrict the unredacted document, files an amended redacted document at the same time, and tenders a proposed order separately. | LBR 9037-1(a); FRBP 9037(a) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAWB-FORM-002 | A brief is printed, typewritten, or presented in some other legible form. | LBR 7007-2(a) | M |
| LAWB-FORM-003 | No LBR sets a typeface, point size, line spacing, or margin for a motion or brief. | LBR 7007-2 (silent on these points) | A |
| LAWB-FORM-004 | Each page of an appendix or exhibit is numbered legibly in the lower right-hand corner. An appendix is a separate document from the motion or brief. For a summary judgment appendix, each page measures 8½ by 11 inches. | LBR 7007-1(f)(2)-(4); LBR 7056-1(f)(2)(i)-(iii) | M |
| LAWB-LEN-001 | A principal brief may not exceed 30 pages or 13,000 words of non-excluded text. A response brief may not exceed 15 pages or 6,500 words of non-excluded text. The excluded text is the cover page, tables of contents and authorities, addendum, certificates of counsel, signature block, and certificate of service. Only the Presiding Judge may grant more for extraordinary and compelling reasons. | LBR 7007-2(c) | M |
| LAWB-CONT-005 | A brief of more than ten pages contains a table of contents with page references and an alphabetical table of authorities with page references. | LBR 7007-2(d) | M |
| LAWB-CONT-006 | A motion sets out the specific relief sought, the party against whom it is sought, and the rule or statute relied on. | LBR 7007-1(g); FRBP 9013(b) | M |
| LAWB-CONT-007 | A motion seeking an extension of time, authority to file an untimely response, or a discovery motion is preceded by a conference and includes a certificate of conference stating whether it is opposed or unopposed, with the details LBR 7007-1(b) requires. | LBR 7007-1(a)-(b) | M |
| LAWB-CONT-008 | When no objection is timely filed, the moving party uploads a proposed order granting the relief to CM/ECF within seven days before the hearing date. | LBR 7007-1(c) | M |
| LAWB-CERT-002 | A certificate of service is filed by the moving party, covers both CM/ECF registered and non-registered users, and states the manner of service or notice on each party. | LBR 9013-1(d) | M |
| LAWB-CERT-003 | Counsel's signature block follows the adversary complaint rule. | LBR 5005-1(b); FRBP 9011(a) | M |
| LAWB-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers are redacted as in the adversary complaint rule. | LBR 9037-1(a); FRBP 9037(a) | M |
