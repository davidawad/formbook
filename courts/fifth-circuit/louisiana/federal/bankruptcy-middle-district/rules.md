# United States Bankruptcy Court, M.D. Louisiana: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `lamb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs.

Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, amended January 1, 2022 (the only edition the court posts), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.

Not retrieved: the court's Administrative Procedures Manual, which LR 5005-1, 9013-5 and 9037-1 incorporate. Nothing in it is reflected in this file.

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAMB-FORM-001 | All documents, including attachments, are electronically sized to 8½ by 11 inches. | LR 5005-2(a) | M |
| LAMB-FORM-002 | All documents, except those filed by pro se parties, use 12-point font and are double-spaced. | LR 5005-2(b) | M |
| LAMB-FORM-003 | No LR sets a typeface family, margin, or page limit for an adversary complaint. | LR 5005-2 (silent on these points) | A |
| LAMB-CONT-001 | Caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document; it must substantially conform to Official Form 416. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010 | M |
| LAMB-CONT-002 | Jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the main case and the district and division where the adversary is pending. The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008 | M |
| LAMB-CONT-003 | A nongovernmental corporate party other than the debtor files a corporate ownership statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b) | M |
| LAMB-CONT-004 | Plaintiff requests issuance of the summons when the complaint is filed. Plaintiff serves the summons, the notice of pretrial conference, and the complaint within the FRBP 7004(e) period, and files proof of service at least seven days before the scheduling and preliminary pretrial conference. | LR 7004-1(a)-(b); FRBP 7004(e) | M |
| LAMB-CONT-005 | No LR requires an adversary proceeding cover sheet. | LR (text searched; no cover-sheet rule) | A |
| LAMB-CERT-001 | Signed filing gives the filer's address and telephone number. A filing through an electronic filing account is the attorney's signature. | FRBP 9011(a); LR 9011-1(a) | M |
| LAMB-ELEC-001 | All documents are filed electronically. The Clerk does not accept documents by e-mail or fax. Filers register an electronic filing account under the Administrative Procedures Manual. | LR 5005-1(a), (a)(3), (d) | M |
| LAMB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are limited to the forms in FRBP 9037(a). A filing that already contains personal information is redacted by motion; the redacted version is attached to the motion and the order is submitted under LR 9013-5. | LR 9037-1; FRBP 9037(a) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAMB-FORM-004 | Motion and all attachments are electronically sized to 8½ by 11 inches, use 12-point font, and are double-spaced, except filings by pro se parties. | LR 5005-2(a)-(b) | M |
| LAMB-FORM-005 | A memorandum is double-spaced and paginated. | LR 9013-2 | M |
| LAMB-FORM-006 | Proposed orders are submitted in Microsoft Word format with 12-point font. The caption of a proposed order shows only the case and adversary numbers. | LR 9013-5(b), (c)(1) | M |
| LAMB-LEN-001 | A memorandum filed with a motion may not exceed 15 pages, excluding attachments, without leave of court. | LR 9013-2 | M |
| LAMB-LEN-002 | An objection is filed by 11:59 p.m. on the eighth day before the hearing. Without leave of court, an objection may not exceed 15 pages, excluding attachments. | LR 9014-1(a)(1), (a)(3) | M |
| LAMB-CONT-006 | Every motion, application, and proposed order includes the filer's current direct e-mail address, a working telephone number, and physical address. | LR 9013-1(a)(1)-(3) | M |
| LAMB-CERT-002 | A certificate of service is filed as a separate CM/ECF document contemporaneously with the motion. It identifies the documents served, the persons served by name and address, and the date and method of service. A certificate that reads "all interested parties" or a similar collective designation does not comply. | LR 9013-4(a)(1)-(3) | M |
| LAMB-CERT-003 | In addition to other required recipients, the filer serves the trustee, the debtor, debtor's counsel, and the United States Trustee. | LR 9013-4(b) | M |
| LAMB-CERT-004 | Counsel's signature block follows the adversary complaint rule. | FRBP 9011(a); LR 9011-1(a) | M |
| LAMB-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers are redacted as in the adversary complaint rule. | LR 9037-1; FRBP 9037(a) | M |
