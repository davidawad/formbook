# United States Bankruptcy Court, E.D. Louisiana: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `laeb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs.

Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, adopted May 1, 2013 (the only edition the court posts), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.

Not retrieved: the court's Administrative Procedures Manual, which LR 5005-1 and LR 9037-1 incorporate for filing format. Nothing in it is reflected in this file.

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAEB-FORM-001 | No LR sets a typeface, point size, line spacing, margin, or page limit for an adversary complaint. The only form rule is that the document be clearly legible. | FRBP 9004(a); LR 1001-1(G) (format rule covers Local Forms only) | A |
| LAEB-CONT-001 | Caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document; it must substantially conform to Official Form 416. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010 | M |
| LAEB-CONT-002 | Jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the main case and the district and division where the adversary is pending. The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008 | M |
| LAEB-CONT-003 | A nongovernmental corporate party other than the debtor files a corporate ownership statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b) | M |
| LAEB-CONT-004 | No LR requires an adversary proceeding cover sheet. | LR (text searched; no cover-sheet rule) | A |
| LAEB-CERT-001 | Counsel signs in individual name. Under the signature, the name, mailing and street addresses, telephone number, and attorney identification number are typed or printed. A party without counsel gives name, mailing and street address, and telephone number. | LR 9011-1(A); FRBP 9011(a) | M |
| LAEB-ELEC-001 | Filing and electronic signing follow the Administrative Procedures Manual. An electronically filed document that requires a signature shows the signer as "/s/ Name". | LR 5005-1; LR 9011-1(B)(1) | M |
| LAEB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are limited to the forms in FRBP 9037(a): last four digits of a social-security or taxpayer-identification number, the year of birth, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number. | LR 9037-1; FRBP 9037(a) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAEB-FORM-002 | No LR sets a typeface, point size, line spacing, margin, or page or word limit for a motion or memorandum. | LR 9013-2 (silent on format); FRBP 9004(a) | A |
| LAEB-CONT-005 | A motion states its grounds with particularity and the relief requested. Authorities support the motion either within the pleading or in a separate memorandum filed and served with it. | FRBP 9013(b); LR 9013-2 | M |
| LAEB-CONT-006 | A motion is made in writing and filed under the Administrative Procedures Manual. Motions are not accepted by telephone or facsimile. | LR 9013-1(A) | M |
| LAEB-CONT-007 | A proposed order submitted with a motion goes through the Administrative Procedures Manual. Its relief is specific. The granting portion refers to another document only where necessary to the relief, and then the document is attached. It uses defined terms only if it defines them. It does not waive appeal delays unless the court so authorized. | LR 9013-3(A)-(B) | M |
| LAEB-CERT-002 | Where a certificate of service is filed, it states the names and addresses of the parties served and the date and form of service. | LR 2002-1(B)(1)(d) | M |
| LAEB-LEN-001 | Paper service copies of a pleading may contain the image of no more than two 8x11 pages per side. This applies only to paper service copies, not to the filed document. | LR 2002-1(B)(2) | M |
| LAEB-CERT-003 | Counsel's signature block follows the adversary complaint rule. | LR 9011-1(A); FRBP 9011(a) | M |
| LAEB-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers are redacted as in the adversary complaint rule. | LR 9037-1; FRBP 9037(a) | M |
