# Louisiana Courts of Appeal (five circuits): appeal briefs, motion memoranda, writ applications

Alias `lactapp`. Scope: briefs on appeal (appellant, appellee, reply), briefs or supporting memoranda filed with motions, and applications for supervisory writs (the petition-for-writ and petition-for-discretionary-review folders). The Uniform Rules of Louisiana Courts of Appeal are the same for all five circuits; the court-level rules here apply to each of them. Verified against the Uniform Rules of Louisiana Courts of Appeal (lasc.org PDF, Rules 1 to 4; latest amendments shown in the rule text: Rule 2-12.2 effective Jan. 1, 2014, Rule 2-12.4 effective May 1, 2014) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the five circuits' own local rules and e-filing procedures. The Fifth Circuit site https://www.la5thcir.org/ refused the connection from this box (HTTP 000 on 2026-10-21); the other circuits' local rule pages were not located. Rows that depend on them are absent, not guessed.

## Form of briefs and memoranda (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LACTAPP-FORM-001 | Briefs are typewritten or produced by a process giving a clear black image on white, unglazed, opaque paper, on one side of each page, on letter or legal size. Illegible copies and photocopies from wet copiers are not acceptable. | ULCA 2-12.2(B) | M |
| LACTAPP-FORM-002 | Type is Roman or Times New Roman, 14-point or larger computer font, normal spacing, for all text; footnotes may be single-spaced but may not be used to circumvent the rule. (The alternative of no more than 10 characters per inch typewriter print is not used by the template.) | ULCA 2-12.2(D)(2) | M |
| LACTAPP-FORM-003 | Margins are 1 inch on each side; at least 1 inch at the top and bottom of each page. | ULCA 2-12.2(B), (D)(2) | M |
| LACTAPP-FORM-004 | Text is double-spaced except for matter customarily single-spaced (block quotations, headings, footnotes). | ULCA 2-12.2(B), (D)(2) | M |
| LACTAPP-FORM-005 | Pages are numbered consecutively. | ULCA 2-12.2(B) | M |
| LACTAPP-FORM-006 | Language is courteous and free from insulting, abusive or irrelevant criticism of any person, class, institution, court or judge; violation may lead to contempt and return of the brief. | ULCA 2-12.2(C) | M |
| LACTAPP-LEN-001 | An original brief (or supporting memorandum) on 8½ × 11 inch paper may not exceed 41 pages; a reply brief on that paper may not exceed 18 pages. The limits exclude the pages of the table of contents and the table of authorities. (On 8½ × 14 paper: 31 and 13 pages.) | ULCA 2-12.2(A), (D)(1) | M |
| LACTAPP-LEN-002 | A motion to exceed the page limit must be filed at least ten days before the brief is due and is granted only for extraordinary and compelling reasons. | ULCA 2-12.2(D)(3) | M |
| LACTAPP-LEN-003 | Briefs may not be extended in time without a written motion filed on or before the due date; the appellee's brief is due 45 days after the record is filed and the appellant's reply 10 days after the appellee's brief. | ULCA 2-12.7, 2-12.8 | A |

## Cover and title page (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LACTAPP-COV-001 | The cover or title page states, in the rule's order: (a) the title of the court to which the brief is directed; (b) the docket number; (c) the case title as on the docket; (d) the name or title of the court and the parish from which the case came; (e) the name of the judge who rendered the judgment or ruling; (f) whether the case is on appeal or in response to a writ; (g) the party on whose behalf the brief is filed and that party's status; (h) the nature of the brief (original, reply or supplemental); (i) counsel's name, address and telephone number, the parties represented, and the designation "appeal counsel"; (j) whether the case is civil, criminal, juvenile or a special proceeding (stating the type). | ULCA 2-12.3(a)-(j) | M |

## Appellant's brief, appellee's brief and reply brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LACTAPP-SEC-001 | The appellant's brief contains, under appropriate headings and in order: (1) a table of contents with page references; (2) a table of authorities (cases alphabetically, statutes and other authorities) with page references; (3) a jurisdictional statement, with the dates of the judgment and of the motion and order for appeal and the basis of appealability; (4) a concise statement of the case; (5) assignments of alleged error; (6) a listing of issues presented for review; (7) a statement of facts with record page references; (8) a short summary of the argument; (9) the argument, with contentions, record and authority references, a standard of review for each assignment or issue, and a statement that any required objection or proffer was made, with record page references; (10) a short conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | ULCA 2-12.4(A)(1)-(10) | M |
| LACTAPP-SEC-002 | The appellant's brief appends a copy of the judgment, order or ruling complained of and of the trial court's written reasons, transcribed oral reasons or minute entry of reasons; if no reasons were given, the brief says so. | ULCA 2-12.4(B)(1) | M |
| LACTAPP-SEC-003 | Citations of Louisiana cases conform to Section VIII of the Louisiana Supreme Court General Administrative Rules; citations of other cases give the volume and page of the official reports and, when possible, the unofficial reporter. | ULCA 2-12.4(B)(2) | M |
| LACTAPP-SEC-004 | Every assignment of error and issue must be briefed; the court may treat an unbriefed one as abandoned; record references are required for the court to consider an argument. | ULCA 2-12.4(B)(3)-(4) | M |
| LACTAPP-SEC-005 | The appellee's brief follows the appellant's order and content, except that it may omit the jurisdictional statement, the statement of the case, the assignments of error, the listing of issues, the statement of facts, the standard of review, the objection statement and the copy of the judgment and reasons, unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statements. | ULCA 2-12.5(1)-(8) | M |
| LACTAPP-SEC-006 | The reply brief is strictly confined to rebuttal of points in the appellee's brief; no further brief may be filed without leave of court. | ULCA 2-12.6 | M |
| LACTAPP-SEC-007 | A supplemental-authority letter is limited to the citation, the issue and a page reference; no attachments; body no longer than two letter-size pages; no argument. | ULCA 2-12.6.1(A)-(C) | A |

## Writ applications (supervisory writ, discretionary review)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LACTAPP-WRIT-001 | The original application for writs, with all documents and exhibits, is filed in an original and three duplicate copies with the clerk. | ULCA 4-1 | M |
| LACTAPP-WRIT-002 | The submission contains, in order: (1) an index of all items; (2) a concise statement of the grounds on which jurisdiction is invoked; (3) a concise statement of the case, including its status and any pending trial or hearing dates; (4) the issues and questions of law presented; (5) the assignments or specifications of error and a memorandum in support, under the brief rules, and a prayer for relief; (6) a copy of the judgment, order or ruling complained of; (7) a copy of the judge's reasons, if written; (8) a copy of each pleading on which the ruling was founded; (9) a copy of any opposition and attachments, or a statement that none was filed; (10) a copy of pertinent court minutes; (11) the notice of intent and return-date order required by Rules 4-2 and 4-3; (12) a separate page entitled "REQUEST FOR EXPEDITED CONSIDERATION" if expedited relief or a stay is sought, with the affidavit required by Rule 4-4(C). | ULCA 4-5(C)(1)-(12); 2-12.2(A) | M |
| LACTAPP-WRIT-003 | The original application is signed by the applicant or counsel of record and contains an affidavit verifying its allegations and certifying that a copy was delivered or mailed to the respondent judge and to opposing counsel and unrepresented parties. The affidavit lists all parties and counsel, the parties each represents, and the addresses and telephone numbers of the respondent judge, opposing counsel and any unrepresented party. | ULCA 4-5(A) | M |
| LACTAPP-WRIT-004 | Briefs in support of an application are filed with the application; opposition briefs are filed before decision or as ordered. | ULCA 2-12.10 | M |
| LACTAPP-WRIT-005 | The application is bound in two places along the top margin without tabs or staples in sections of no more than 250 pages, with consecutive page numbering of the application and attachments. | ULCA 4-5(B) | A |
