# Supreme Court of Louisiana: briefs, writ applications, rehearing applications, motions and amicus briefs

Alias `la`. Scope: briefs in the Supreme Court of Louisiana (answering brief, reply brief, brief on the merits after a writ is granted, amicus curiae brief), motions for leave and their supporting memoranda, writ applications (supervisory and remedial writs, review of court-of-appeal judgments, writs of certiorari), and applications for rehearing. Verified against the Rules of the Supreme Court of Louisiana, Part A, Rules VII, IX and X, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the live Rules pages on lasc.org returned HTTP 521 on every attempt (2026-10-21, with retries). The rule texts below were read from the Internet Archive captures listed in Sources, which carry the rule text and amendment dates. The Court's e-filing standards, the clerk's document-format orders and the writ application filing sheet and priority filing sheet forms (Rule X, Sections 2(d)-(e)) were not retrieved; rows that depend on them are absent, not guessed. lacourt.org returned HTTP 200 but serves a script-only portal shell with no rule text.

Only paper, typeface, margins and spacing that a court rule states are mandatory here. Rule VII (briefs) lets a brief be on letter or legal paper; Rule X (writ applications) prefers legal paper "to the extent practicable." The template uses legal paper (8 1/2 x 14 in) for every doc type, which satisfies both rules. The filed exemplars are mixed: most are legal, the amicus and motion exemplars are letter.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|

| LA-LEN-002 | Criminal briefs do not exceed 35 legal-size pages (50 letter), and capital criminal briefs 85 legal-size pages (115 letter), each excluding the cover and index. | LA VII 2 | M |
| LA-LEN-003 | An amicus curiae brief does not exceed 15 legal-size pages or 20 letter-size pages. | LA VII 12 | M |
| LA-LEN-004 | A motion for leave to file an amicus curiae brief does not exceed 5 pages. | LA VII 12 | M |

## Writ applications (Rule X)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-WRT-001 | The application states why the case is appropriate for review under one or more considerations in Rule X 1(a) (conflicting decisions; significant unresolved issues of law; overruling or modification of precedent; erroneous interpretation or application of law; gross departure from proper judicial proceedings), addressed concisely in the application. | LA X 1(a)-(b) | M |
| LA-WRT-002 | A civil writ application contains, in order: an index of all items contained in it; a statement of which Rule X 1(a) considerations are present; the memorandum (LA-WRT-003); the verification (LA-COV-003); and an appendix containing the trial court's judgment, order or ruling with its reasons, and the court of appeal's order and opinion, including rulings on rehearing. | LA X 3(1)-(5) | M |
| LA-WRT-003 | The memorandum contains (a) a concise statement of the case summarizing its nature and prior proceedings; (b) an assignment of errors in the opinion, judgment, ruling or order complained of; (c) a summary of the argument, a condensation of the argument and not a repetition of the headings; and (d) an argument of each assignment of error on the facts and law, addressing why review is appropriate under Rule X 1(a). | LA X 3(3)(a)-(d) | M |
| LA-WRT-004 | A criminal writ application contains an index, a statement of the Rule X 1(a) considerations, a memorandum in the form of Rule VII 2 (typeface and margins), the verification, a copy of the judgment and opinions of the court of appeal, and a separately bound appendix (charging document, minutes, court-of-appeal briefs, trial-court ruling and pleadings, order fixing time, transcripts). | LA X 4(1)-(6) | M |
| LA-WRT-005 | Briefs filed in the court of appeal are not attached to a civil writ application. Excessive and irrelevant pleadings and exhibits are discouraged. | LA X 3(8); Official Comments | M |
| LA-WRT-006 | A writ application is filed within 30 days of the mailing of notice of the court of appeal's judgment (or of the denial of rehearing), with no extension; the time is not a form rule and is noted here for filing. | LA X 5(a)(1) | A |
| LA-WRT-007 | A copy of the application is mailed or delivered to all counsel and unrepresented parties, to the respondent judge in a remedial writ, and, where the court of appeal has acted, a copy is filed with the court of appeal clerk, containing the memorandum to this Court. | LA X 2(c) | M |
| LA-WRT-008 | The application is signed by the applicant or counsel of record, who verifies the allegations and certifies the copies (LA-COV-003). | LA X 2(d) | M |
| LA-WRT-009 | A priority application (stay order or other priority) certifies that all counsel and unrepresented parties were notified by telephone or equally prompt means, and that the application was served forthwith by a means equal to the filing means. | LA X 2(e) | M |
| LA-WRT-010 | Pages of the application are consecutively numbered (LA-FORM-007). Filing is in duplicate with the Clerk (original and duplicate). | LA X 2(a)-(b) | M |
