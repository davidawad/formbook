# Exemplar source

Two filed exemplars (different filers). Both are public court records from the Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Mississippi, fetched from CourtListener RECAP. The first is the one the template and sample are transcribed from; the second is used for the tier-2 comparison.

## 1. Griffin (primary; sample source)

- File: `FILED-EXAMPLE-adversary-complaint.pdf`
- Court: United States Bankruptcy Court, Southern District of Mississippi
- Case: *Stephen Smith, as Chapter 7 Trustee for the Bankruptcy Estate of Bobby Joe Griffin v. Global Client Solutions, LLC, et al.*
- Bankruptcy case: No. 18-00602-NPO (Chapter 7, Debtor Bobby Joe Griffin). Adversary No. 18-00028-NPO.
- Document: Complaint (Dkt 1), 16 pages. Filed as the adversary complaint.
- Filer: Plaintiff Stephen Smith, Chapter 7 Trustee, by counsel Jason Graeber (Biloxi, Miss.; MSB No. 101267)
- Date filed: 2018-05-01 (stamped "Dkt 1 Filed 05/01/18" on every page)
- Fetched from: https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.mssb.286204/gov.uscourts.mssb.286204.1.0.pdf (CourtListener docket: https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/6878853/bobby-joe-griffin-adversary-proceeding/)
- Date fetched: 2026-10-14
- Text layer: yes (`pdftotext` yields the full text).
- Note: the filed complaint contains no FRBP 7008(a) / Miss. Bankr. L.R. 7008-1 consent statement. See `../README.md`.
