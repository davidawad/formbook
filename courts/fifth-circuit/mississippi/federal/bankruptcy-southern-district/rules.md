# United States Bankruptcy Court, S.D. Mississippi: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `mssb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs.

Verified against the Uniform Local Bankruptcy Rules for the Northern and Southern Districts of Mississippi, as amended March 1, 2026 (the joint rule set the court posts, identical text to the Northern District's posting), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.

Not retrieved: the court's Administrative Procedures for Electronic Case Filing, which ULR 5005-1(F) refers to for exceptions and procedure. Nothing in it is reflected in this file.

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSSB-FORM-001 | All papers filed with the clerk are electronically sized to 8½ by 11 inches. Attachments are sized the same way to the extent possible. | ULR 5005-1(K) | M |
| MSSB-FORM-002 | All papers are clearly legible in a font no smaller than 12 point and are double-spaced. Quotations, footnotes, and legal descriptions may be single-spaced. | ULR 5005-1(K) | M |
| MSSB-FORM-003 | No ULR sets a typeface family, margin, or page limit for a complaint. | ULR 5005-1(K) (silent on these points) | A |
| MSSB-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court's name, the case title (with the adversary style), the case and adversary numbers, and a brief designation of the document; it substantially conforms to Official Form 416. Every matter contained in the document is named in the caption on the first page. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010; ULR 5005-1(M) | M |
| MSSB-CONT-002 | Jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the main case and the district and division where the adversary is pending. The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008; ULR 7008-1 | M |
| MSSB-CONT-003 | A nongovernmental corporate party other than the debtor files a corporate ownership statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b); ULR 7007.1-1(c) (the same statement applies to a general partnership, limited partnership, joint venture, or LLC party) | M |
| MSSB-CONT-004 | No ULR mentions an adversary proceeding cover sheet. | ULR (text searched; no cover-sheet rule) | A |
| MSSB-CERT-001 | Every pleading and every proposed order carries counsel's name, complete address, telephone number, e-mail address, and Mississippi Bar number (or the bar number of the state where counsel practices). An unrepresented party gives name, address, telephone number, and e-mail address if available. | ULR 5005-1(L)(i)-(ii); ULR 9011-1(a)(1); FRBP 9011(a) | M |
| MSSB-CERT-002 | A document filed through a registered ECF user's login is signed by that user. The user's name is shown as an image of a signature or as "/s/ Name" in the signature space. | ULR 9011-1(a)(2); FRBP 9011(a) | M |
| MSSB-ELEC-001 | Filing is through CM/ECF; registered attorneys file electronically. An unrepresented individual may file pleadings and documents on paper. | ULR 5005-1(E)-(F) | M |
| MSSB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are limited to the forms in FRBP 9037(a). A motion to redact a previously filed document is not served by the notice of electronic filing; public access to it is restricted on filing. | FRBP 9037(a); ULR 9037-1(h)(1) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSSB-FORM-004 | Motions, responses, and memorandum briefs meet the same paper size, 12-point minimum font, and double-spacing as the complaint rule (quotations, footnotes, and legal descriptions may be single-spaced). | ULR 5005-1(K) | M |
| MSSB-FORM-005 | No ULR sets a page or word limit for a motion, response, or memorandum brief. | ULR (text searched; no page or word limit) | A |
| MSSB-CONT-005 | A motion specifies the rules and statutory provisions on which it is predicated and states its grounds with particularity and the relief requested. | ULR 9013-1(b)(1); FRBP 9013(b) | M |
| MSSB-CONT-006 | Every motion has a proposed order attached. A responsive pleading identifies the underlying motion and its docket number as "(Dkt. # __)". | ULR 5005-1(H)(i)-(ii); ULR 9004-1(b) | M |
| MSSB-CONT-007 | A summary judgment motion is accompanied by a memorandum brief. The respondent files its response and memorandum brief within 21 days of service. | ULR 7056-1(3)(A)-(B) | M |
| MSSB-CERT-003 | A document that requires notice includes, or is accompanied by, a certificate of service signed by the serving party. It identifies each person served, the date of service, and the manner of service. | ULR 9004-1(c) | M |
| MSSB-CERT-004 | A motion or response is served on at least the persons listed for the chapter of the case (chapter 7, 11, or 12-13 lists, and the United States Trustee), and on any other entity affected by the relief requested. | ULR 9013-1(b)(2), (b)(3)(A)-(D) | M |
| MSSB-CERT-005 | Counsel's identification block follows the adversary complaint rule. | ULR 5005-1(L)(i); ULR 9011-1(a)(1) | M |
| MSSB-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers are redacted as in the adversary complaint rule. | FRBP 9037(a); ULR 9037-1(h) | M |
