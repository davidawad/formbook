# Factual Background

On a date stated in the loan file, the Plaintiff made a loan to the Defendant in the principal amount of $50,000, secured by no collateral (the "Loan").

In applying for the Loan, the Defendant submitted a written financial statement that listed a monthly income figure, a list of existing debts and a statement that no judgments were pending against the Defendant.

The financial statement was materially false. The Defendant's actual monthly income was less than half the figure listed, and the Defendant had at least two judgments pending at the time of signing.

The Plaintiff relied on the financial statement in deciding to make the Loan and would not have made the Loan on the terms given had it known the true figures.

Within ninety days before the petition date, the Defendant made a transfer of funds from the Loan proceeds to a relative, without consideration and with the intent to hinder the Plaintiff's collection.

The Defendant filed a voluntary petition under Chapter 7 on the petition date. The Defendant listed the Loan as an unsecured debt on the schedules.

The Plaintiff timely filed a proof of claim in the Debtor's case. The Plaintiff now seeks a determination that the Loan is excepted from discharge.

# Count I: Nondischargeability under 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(2)(A)

Plaintiff incorporates the preceding paragraphs as if fully set forth.

The Defendant obtained money from the Plaintiff by false pretenses, a false representation, or actual fraud, namely the false financial statement described above.

The Defendant made the representations knowing they were false, or with reckless disregard for their truth, and intended the Plaintiff to rely on them.

The Plaintiff justifiably relied on the representations in making the Loan.

The Plaintiff was damaged in the amount of the unpaid principal and accrued interest on the Loan.

The debt arising from the Loan is therefore excepted from discharge under 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(2)(A).

# Count II: Nondischargeability under 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(6)

Plaintiff incorporates the preceding paragraphs as if fully set forth.

The transfer described above was made by the Defendant to a relative without consideration and with the intent to hinder, delay or defraud the Plaintiff.

The injury was willful and malicious within the meaning of 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(6).
