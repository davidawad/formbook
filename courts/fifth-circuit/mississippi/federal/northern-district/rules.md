# Northern District of Mississippi: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `msnd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the court-posted Local Uniform Civil Rules current December 1, 2024, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure on 2026-10-17.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSND-FORM-001 | The complaint's page form is governed by FRCP 10 and applicable administrative e-filing procedures; the Local Uniform Civil Rules set no general typeface, point size, spacing, or margin rule for complaints. Do not mistake the memorandum-brief rule below for a complaint rule. | FRCP 10; L.U. Civ. R. 5(c), 7(b)(5). | M |
| MSND-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court, action title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation. Plead jurisdiction, claim, and relief under FRCP 8(a); claims appear in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 8(a), 10(a)-(b). | M |
| MSND-CONT-002 | File JS-44 Civil Cover Sheet with each original complaint. A non-governmental corporate party files its corporate disclosure statement separately with its first pleading. | L.U. Civ. R. 5(a)(1), 7(c). | M |
| MSND-CERT-001 | Counsel's signed filing gives name, address, telephone, fax, e-mail, and bar membership number. | L.U. Civ. R. 11(a); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| MSND-ELEC-001 | File under the court's Administrative Procedures for Electronic Case Filing; protect personal identifiers under FRCP 5.2 and L.U. Civ. R. 5.2. | L.U. Civ. R. 5(c), 5.2; FRCP 5.2. | M |
| MSND-CONT-003 | A jury demand is made in the pleading; a demand on the civil cover sheet alone is insufficient. | L.U. Civ. R. 38(a). | M |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSND-FORM-002 | Memorandum briefs use 8½ × 11 inch pages, double-spaced text (quotations may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced), and margins of at least one inch on all sides. Page numbers may be in the margins, but no other text may be. | L.U. Civ. R. 7(b)(5)(A). | M |
| MSND-FORM-003 | A memorandum brief may use proportionally spaced or monospaced type. A proportional face must have serifs; sans-serif is permitted in headings and captions. Text is at least 12 point; footnotes at least 11 point. Use plain roman type; case names are italicized or underlined. | L.U. Civ. R. 7(b)(5)(B)-(C). | M |
| MSND-LEN-001 | Movant's original and rebuttal memorandum briefs together may not exceed 35 pages; respondent's memorandum may not exceed 35 pages. | L.U. Civ. R. 7(b)(5). | M |
| MSND-CONT-004 | The memorandum brief is a separate docket item from the motion or response, not an exhibit to it. A motion (other than a Rule 37 discovery motion) is four pages or fewer, excluding exhibits, states grounds only, and contains no legal argument or secondary-authority citations. | L.U. Civ. R. 7(b)(2)(B). | M |
| MSND-CONT-005 | File motion, response, rebuttal and supporting exhibits. File the memorandum as a separate docket item; supporting evidence is filed as exhibits associated with the motion or response. | L.U. Civ. R. 7(b)(2). | M |
| MSND-CERT-002 | Signed papers include counsel's name, address, telephone, fax, email and bar membership number. | L.U. Civ. R. 11(a). | M |
