# Court of Appeals of Mississippi: briefs, motions and amicus briefs

Alias `missctapp`. Scope: the appellant's and appellee's principal briefs, reply briefs, amicus curiae briefs (Rule 29), motions and supporting memoranda (Rule 27), and motions for rehearing (Rule 40) filed in the Court of Appeals. Petitions for permission to appeal an interlocutory order (Rule 5), petitions for certiorari (Rule 16) and petitions for extraordinary writs (Rule 21) are filed in the Supreme Court and are not in this file. Verified against the Mississippi Rules of Appellate Procedure, "Rules of Appellate Procedure Current" (Rules 25, 27, 28, 29, 32 and 40), and the Appellate E-Filing Administrative Procedures. The rule text was read on 2026-10-21 from the Internet Archive copies listed under Sources, which were re-checked with HTTP 200 on 2026-10-22; verified on 2026-10-22.

Local rules not retrieved: the official courts.ms.gov copies returned HTTP 500 on 2026-10-22 (re-checked) (`https://courts.ms.gov/research/rules/msrulesofcourt/Rules%20of%20Appellate%20Procedure%20Current.pdf`, HTTP 500; `https://courts.ms.gov/research/rules/msrulesofcourt/Appellate_Efiling_Procedures_w_Hyperlinks.pdf`, HTTP 500; `https://courts.ms.gov/rules/msrulesofcourt/rules_of_appellate_procedure.pdf`, HTTP 500; `https://courts.ms.gov/`, HTTP 500). The rule texts in the Sources table were read from the Internet Archive copies of the same official PDFs, which returned HTTP 200. Re-check the official copies when courts.ms.gov is back up. ## Briefs (principal, appellee's, reply, brief on the merits)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MISSCTAPP-FORM-001 | Briefs are produced on pages not exceeding 8 1/2 by 11 inches; each page is numbered; text is typed on one side of the page. | MRAP 32(a) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-FORM-002 | The body text of a brief is at least 12-point type; footnote text is at least 11-point type. | MRAP 32(a) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-FORM-003 | Margins: 1 1/2 inches on the left, 1 inch on the top, 3/4 inch on the bottom, and 1/2 inch on the right. | MRAP 32(a) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-FORM-004 | Double spacing between each line of text, excluding quotations and footnotes. | MRAP 32(a) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-FORM-005 | The rule names no typeface. Only the sizes in MISSCTAPP-FORM-002 are mandated; the template chooses a proportional serif face (Liberation Serif, metric twin of Times New Roman, under docs/FONTS.md). | MRAP 32(a) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-FORM-006 | Electronically filed documents meet the same format and page-limit requirements as conventionally filed documents; the title of an e-filed brief should be in Times New Roman 30-point type. | EFILE 3(A)(1) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-FORM-007 | Cover colors (appellant blue, appellee red, amicus or intervenor green, reply gray) apply to conventional filings only; color requirements for brief covers do not apply to electronically filed documents. | MRAP 32(a); EFILE 3(A)(1) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-COV-001 | The front cover shows, in this order: the caption, the name of the court and the number of the case; the style (title) of the case; the nature of the proceeding and the court or commission below; the title of the document (for example, "Brief for Appellant"); the names, bar numbers, addresses and business telephone numbers of counsel for the filing party; and a statement that oral argument is or is not requested. | MRAP 32(a)(1)-(6); MRAP 34(b) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-COV-002 | The cover states whether oral argument is requested, in the statement required by MRAP 34(b). | MRAP 32(a)(6); MRAP 34(b) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-SEC-001 | The brief of the appellant contains, under appropriate headings and in this order: certificate of interested persons; table of contents with page references; table of cases, statutes and other authorities with page references; statement of issues; statement of assignment; statement of the case (nature, course of proceedings, disposition below, and facts with record references); summary of the argument; argument; and conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | MRAP 28(a)(1)-(8) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-SEC-002 | The certificate of interested persons is in the form MRAP 28(a)(1) prescribes, with the case number and style at its head; a governmental party need not supply it. | MRAP 28(a)(1) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-SEC-003 | The brief of the appellee follows MRAP 28(a) except that a statement of issues, of assignment or of the case need not be made unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement. | MRAP 28(c) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-SEC-004 | The summary of the argument seldom exceeds two pages and never exceeds five pages. | MRAP 28(a)(6) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-SEC-005 | Any brief exceeding 10 pages contains a table of contents and a table of authorities that meet MRAP 28(a)(2). | MRAP 28(m) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-SEC-006 | Statutes, rules and regulations whose study the issues require are reproduced in the brief or in an addendum at its end. | MRAP 28(g) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-SEC-007 | The rules set no separate form for a brief on the merits. The template follows the principal-brief order of MRAP 28(a) and the length limit of MISSCTAPP-LEN-001 as a choice, not a mandate. | MRAP 28(a), 28(h), 28(m) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-LEN-001 | A principal brief does not exceed 50 pages, and a reply brief does not exceed 25 pages, except by permission of the court. The count excludes the oral-argument statement, certificates of counsel, the table of contents, tables of citations and any addendum of statutes, rules or regulations. | MRAP 28(h) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-LEN-002 | A party may move the clerk for an increase in the brief page limit up to 75 pages (or 125 pages in a death-penalty appeal). | MRAP 27(b)(5) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-REPLY-001 | A reply brief contains a table of contents with page references and a table of cases, statutes and other authorities with page references; no further brief is filed without leave of the Court. | MRAP 28(d) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-CERT-001 | Papers presented for filing contain an acknowledgment of service by the person served, or proof of service stating the date and manner of service. | MRAP 25(d) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-CERT-002 | Counsel's signature block in an e-filed document reads "s/(attorney name)" followed by the attorney's name, Mississippi Bar number, firm name, address, telephone number, fax number and e-mail address. | EFILE 3(B) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-CITE-001 | Mississippi cases are cited by Southern Reporter citation or by Clerk's case number (for example, "95-KA-01234-SCT"); quotations carry a page or paragraph reference in the form MRAP 28(f)(3) describes. | MRAP 28(f)(2)-(3) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-STY-001 | Counsel keep references to parties to a minimum by designations such as "appellant" and "appellee," or by the parties' names or the lower-court designations. | MRAP 28(e) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-REF-001 | Briefs are keyed by page to the record excerpts (MRAP 30) and to the record. | MRAP 28(f) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-REM-001 | The brief of an appellant or petitioner that is filed with the Clerk in conventional form includes an electronic copy in PDF (MRAP 28(n)) when the brief is on the merits. | MRAP 28(n) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-ELEC-001 | Electronic filings are uploaded in PDF; the PDF must be searchable (a text PDF), not an image of the document. | EFILE 1(C); EFILE 3(A) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-PRIV-001 | Counsel refrain from including, or partially redact, personal data identifiers: Social Security numbers and financial-account numbers to the last four digits; the initials of a minor child; and dates of birth to the year. | EFILE 6(A)(1)-(4) | M |

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MISSCTAPP-AMI-001 | An amicus brief may be filed only by leave of the court, except that leave is not required for a state brief sponsored by the Attorney General or a guardian ad litem not otherwise a party. | MRAP 29(a) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-AMI-002 | The motion for leave shows one of the four grounds in MRAP 29(a)(1)-(4) and is filed no later than seven days after the initial brief of the party whose position the amicus brief supports. | MRAP 29(a), (b) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-AMI-003 | The proposed amicus brief accompanying the motion is a concise statement not exceeding 15 pages. | MRAP 29(b) | M |

## Motions and memoranda (Rule 27)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MISSCTAPP-MOT-001 | A motion states with particularity the grounds on which it is based and the order or relief sought, and is served with proof of service on all other parties; supporting briefs, affidavits or papers are served and filed with it. | MRAP 27(a) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-MOT-002 | A motion or other paper addressed to the Court of Appeals contains a caption setting forth the name of the court, the style of the case, the docket number and a brief descriptive title. | MRAP 32(b) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-MOT-003 | A motion or other paper is typed on opaque, unglazed paper of 8 1/2 by 11 inches, with double-spaced lines except quotations and footnotes; consecutive sheets are attached at the top left corner when filed conventionally. | MRAP 32(b) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-MOT-004 | A motion has no page limit in MRAP 27 or 32; a motion for rehearing is limited in MISSCTAPP-LEN-003. | MRAP 27, 32(b) | A |
| MISSCTAPP-MOT-005 | A response to a motion is filed within seven days after service of the motion, except for a motion for a procedural order. | MRAP 27(a) | A |

## Motions for rehearing (Rule 40)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MISSCTAPP-REH-001 | A motion for rehearing is filed within 14 days after a decision on the merits; it states with particularity the points of law or fact the court has overlooked or misapprehended, and it does not repeat argument already considered. | MRAP 40(a) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-LEN-003 | A motion for rehearing is in the form MRAP 32 prescribes and does not exceed 25 pages except by permission of the court. | MRAP 40(b) | M |
| MISSCTAPP-REH-002 | Oral argument is not permitted on a motion for rehearing. | MRAP 40(a) | M |
