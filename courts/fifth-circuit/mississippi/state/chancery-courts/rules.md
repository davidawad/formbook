# Mississippi chancery courts: motion memoranda

Alias `misschanceryct`. Scope: motion memoranda filed in the Mississippi chancery courts (trial level; the exemplar is a memorandum in support of a summary-judgment motion, Chancery Court of Hinds County, First Judicial District). Verified against the Mississippi Rules of Civil Procedure (2016 edition), the Uniform Chancery Court Rules (2012 edition) and the Mississippi Electronic Courts Administrative Procedures (November 2016, effective December 1, 2016), each read from the archived copy listed below, on 2026-10-21. The current official editions could not be retrieved (courts.ms.gov returned HTTP 500, "Website Maintenance", on 2026-10-21), so every row below carries the edition it was read from.

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MISSCHANCERYCT-FORM-001 | All pleadings, motions and other papers, including the memorandum, are made on 8 1/2 by 11 inch paper. | MRCP 7(c) | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-FORM-002 | The caption gives the name of the court, the title of the action, the file number, and a designation as in Rule 7(a). The caption rules apply to motions and other papers. | MRCP 10(a), 7(b)(2) | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-FORM-003 | No margin, line-spacing, typeface, point-size, or page-limit rule for a memorandum appears in the retrieved MRCP, UCCR or MEC text. The template's 12-point text, one-inch margins and double-spaced body follow the exemplar, not a rule. Judged as tier 2. | MRCP 7(c); UCCR (2011 print) (no memorandum rule); MEC 3(A)(2) | A |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-LEN-001 | Electronically filed papers must meet the same format and page limits as conventionally filed papers under the MRCP and the Uniform Chancery Court Rules. No page limit appears in the retrieved MRCP or UCCR text; any local limit is not retrieved (see LOCAL). | MEC 3(A)(2); MRCP 7; UCCR (2011 print) | A |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-FILE-001 | A memorandum in support is filed electronically as its own document, shown as a related document to the motion, not combined with the motion under one document number. | MEC 3(A)(7)-(8) | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-ELEC-001 | Documents filed in the MEC system are PDF files. | MEC 3(A)(1), note 1 | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-ELEC-002 | A text (searchable) PDF is preferred over an image PDF where possible. | MEC 1(D) | A |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-ELEC-003 | Each attachment (an exhibit, scanned or otherwise) is under 10 MB; a larger exhibit is divided into bundles, each listed in the attachment description (for example, "Exhibit 1 - Pages 1 - 43"). | MEC 3(A)(9) | A |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-SIG-001 | Every motion or paper of a represented party is signed by at least one attorney of record in the attorney's individual name, and the attorney's address is stated. A party without counsel signs and states an address. | MRCP 11(a) | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-SIG-002 | An electronically filed attorney signature reads "s/" followed by the attorney's name, then the name, Mississippi bar number, firm name, firm address, telephone number, fax number, and e-mail address. | MEC 3(B)(2) | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-SERV-001 | Every written motion, and every paper relating to it, is served on each party; service on a represented party is made on the attorney of record. Service by electronic means is complete on acknowledgment by the recipient's equipment. | MRCP 5(a), 5(b)(1) | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-CERT-001 | A certificate of service is still required when the memorandum is filed electronically (MEC Form 1 is the sample). The template generates it from metadata. | MEC 3(G) | M |
| MISSCHANCERYCT-PRIV-001 | Personal identifiers are omitted or partially redacted from pleadings, papers and exhibits: Social Security numbers and financial account numbers to the last four digits, and the other identifiers listed in MEC 9(A). | MRCP 5.1; MEC 9(A) | M |
