# Supreme Court of New Hampshire: appellate filings

Alias `nh`. Scope: briefs and memoranda on the merits (opening, answering, reply, amicus, brief on the merits), motions (motion-brief), petitions (petition for review, petition for original jurisdiction (writ), petition for discretionary review, motion for rehearing). Verified against the Rules of the Supreme Court of the State of New Hampshire (Procedural Rules 1 to 34, as posted on the New Hampshire Judicial Branch website) and the Supplemental Rules for Electronic Filing in the Supreme Court on 2026-10-22. Amicus briefs and briefs on the merits have no separate rule text in SCR; they follow SCR 16 as written.

|---|---|---|---|
| NH-MOT-001 | Motions relating to substance use good quality, nonclinging 8½ by 11 paper, standard typewriter characters or size 13 font, one side of each leaf, 1.5 line spacing, left-aligned text, and sequentially numbered pages beginning with the cover page as page 1. | SCR 21(1). | M |

## Petitions and motions for rehearing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NH-PET-001 | A petition for original jurisdiction is granted only for special and important reasons. A petition contains, as far as possible and in order: a copy of the decision sought to be reviewed, annexed; the questions presented; the provisions involved, verbatim or cited, with a table of contents when documents are annexed; and a concise statement of the case with references to the appendix. | SCR 11(1), 11(2)(a)-(e). | M |
| NH-PET-002 | A motion for rehearing or reconsideration is filed within 10 days of the opinion, dismissal or summary decision, states with particularity the points overlooked or misapprehended, and may not exceed 3,000 words. | SCR 22(2). | M |
| NH-PET-003 | A motion for rehearing is filed with the original and 7 copies and a certificate of mailing or delivery to opposing counsel and the clerk of the court below (and the attorney general in an agency appeal). | SCR 22(1). | M |
