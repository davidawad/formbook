# First Circuit Bankruptcy Appellate Panel briefs: rules

Alias `bap1`. Scope: the appellant's principal brief (`opening-brief`), the appellee's brief (`answering-brief`) and the appellant's reply brief (`reply-brief`) in an appeal to the United States Bankruptcy Appellate Panel for the First Circuit, filed by counsel through the BAP's CM/ECF system. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (FRBP amendment date) and whenever the BAP posts amended local rules or general orders at https://www.bap1.uscourts.gov/rules-procedures and https://www.bap1.uscourts.gov/general-orders.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (FRBP or 1st Cir. BAP L.R. / General Order). **C** = convention (the rules leave it open; seen across filers). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

Findings:

1. **FRBP 8015 governs form, not FRAP 32.** It mirrors FRAP 32 but has no cover colors and adds counsel's e-mail address to the cover (FRBP 8015(a)(2)(F)). An electronically filed brief must comply with 8015(a) except (a)(1), (a)(3) and the paper requirement of (a)(4) (FRBP 8015(b)).
2. **The Guide conflicts with the rule on type size.** GUIDE p. 8 says "Text must be no smaller than 11-point font"; FRBP 8015(a)(5)(A) says "A proportionally spaced face must be 14-point or larger." L.R. 8015-1(a) only lets the BAP exercise discretion to accept nonconforming documents; it does not set a different size. The rule governs: 14-point (BAP1-FORM-004). The Guide's 11-point figure is not used.
3. **Statement Regarding Interested Parties replaces the FRBP 8012 disclosure statement** (L.R. 8012-1(a)); it and the Statement Regarding Related Cases are attached to the last page of every brief, substantially in the form of BAP Official Forms 1 and 2, and excluded from the length limits (L.R. 8014-1(a)(2)-(3)). Governmental parties do not file the interested-parties statement.
4. **Statement Regarding Oral Argument is mandatory** in the opening or answering brief ("The parties shall include"), at most one-half page, captioned "Statement Regarding Oral Argument", after the TOC and TOA and before the first page of the brief (L.R. 8019-1(a)).
5. **The Guide's reference to "Official Forms 3 and 4"** for the related-cases and interested-parties statements (GUIDE p. 7) does not match the local rules, which name Official Forms 1 and 2; the rules govern.

## Court-wide requirements (all three doc types)

### Page form (FRBP 8015(a)(4)-(6), (b))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP1-FORM-001 | 8 1/2 by 11 inch pages. | FRBP 8015(a)(4), (b) | M |
| BAP1-FORM-002 | Margins at least one inch on all four sides; page numbers may be placed in the margins, but no text may appear there. | FRBP 8015(a)(4); GUIDE p. 8 | M |
| BAP1-FORM-003 | Text double-spaced; quotations more than two lines long may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | FRBP 8015(a)(4) | M |
| BAP1-FORM-004 | Typeface: "Either a proportionally spaced or monospaced face may be used." "A proportionally spaced face must include serifs, but sans-serif type may be used in headings and captions. A proportionally spaced face must be 14-point or larger." "A monospaced face may not contain more than 10 1/2 characters per inch." No footnote exemption is stated; the checks apply 14 pt to footnotes. (GUIDE p. 8's "no smaller than 11-point" is not followed; see Finding 2.) | FRBP 8015(a)(5)(A)-(B) | M |
| BAP1-FORM-005 | Plain, roman style; italics or boldface for emphasis; case names italicized or underlined. | FRBP 8015(a)(6) | M |
| BAP1-FORM-006 | Every font used is embedded and is the template's declared family. | project | T |
| BAP1-FORM-007 | Face: no rule names one. Exemplars: Blumsack (Law Offices of D. Lev) body in Courier New (monospaced) with Times New Roman front matter; U.S. Trustee (DOJ EOUST) in Garamond 14 pt. No shared choice across filers; any 14-point serif face. | EX | C (open) |
| BAP1-FORM-008 | Page numbering: the U.S. Trustee numbers front matter in roman and the body from Arabic 1; Blumsack numbers continuously in Arabic from the cover. No convention across filers; centered footer numbers are common to both. | EX | C (open) |

| BAP1-CONT-010 | Contents, under appropriate headings and in order: disclosure statement if required by Rule 8012 (satisfied by the interested-parties statement, L.R. 8012-1); TOC; TOA; jurisdictional statement (bankruptcy court's subject-matter jurisdiction; BAP jurisdiction; filing dates establishing timeliness; finality assertion or other basis); statement of the issues and, for each, a concise statement of the applicable standard of appellate review; statement of the case with record references; summary of the argument; argument; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; certificate of compliance.
