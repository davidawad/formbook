# Argument

## The Answering Brief Concedes the Recording Issue

Appellees do not dispute that the financing statement identified the collateral by type. They argue only that the Debtor's relocation of the equipment ended perfection. That argument has no support in the statute. A relocation within the same jurisdiction does not require a new filing, as [*Gamma Trust v. Delta Estate*, 300 F.3d 30, 38 (1st Cir. 2002)](toa:gamma) explains.

## The Valuation Question Turns on the Excluded Exhibit

Appellees say the court relied on both witnesses. The order says otherwise: it adopts a figure between the two witnesses' numbers without explaining how it reached that figure. App. at 27. Under [11 U.S.C. § 506(a)](toa:sample-stat), the court must value the collateral, and an unexplained midpoint does not permit review.

# Conclusion

The order should be vacated and the matter remanded for valuation.
