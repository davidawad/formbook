# Supreme Court of Rhode Island: briefs on appeal, reply briefs, briefs on the merits and amicus briefs

Alias `ri`. Scope: filings in the Supreme Court of Rhode Island: the appellant's and appellee's briefs (Rule 16(a)-(b)), reply briefs (Rule 16(c)), briefs on the merits after a writ of certiorari is granted (Rules 12A, 13(e), 16), and amicus curiae briefs (Rule 16(h)). Verified against Rhode Island Supreme Court Rules, Article I (revised February 17, 2026) and Article X, Electronic Filing (revised September 2023), read from the court's PDFs on 2026-10-21.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |

| RI-TIME-001 | The appellant or other moving party may file a reply brief within 20 days after the appellee's brief is filed. | SCt Art. I R. 16(c) | T |

## Appellant's and appellee's briefs (Rule 16(a)-(b))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RI-ORDER-002 | The appellant's brief contains, in order: a brief and concise statement of the facts and prior proceedings with record citations; a specification of the errors claimed with record citations; the specific questions raised, numbered, each with its standard of review; the points made with authority; a conclusion stating the relief sought; and an index of authorities arranged alphabetically with the page on which each is cited. | SCt Art. I R. 16(a)(1)-(6) | M |
| RI-ORDER-003 | The appellee's brief conforms to Rule 16(a) except that no specification of errors is necessary and no statement of the case beyond what corrects the appellant's statement. | SCt Art. I R. 16(b) | M |
| RI-ORDER-004 | Errors not claimed, questions not raised and points not made are treated as waived. | SCt Art. I R. 16(a) | A |
| RI-TIME-002 | The appellant's brief is due 40 days after the Clerk notifies the appellant that the case is assigned to the regular calendar for full briefing; the appellee's brief is due 40 days after the appellant's brief is filed. | SCt Art. I R. 16(a)-(b) | T |

## Briefs on the merits after certiorari

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RI-ORDER-005 | After a writ of certiorari is granted, the parties proceed under the rules for appeals: the Rule 12A statement of the case, then briefs under Rule 16. A brief on the merits of a certiorari case follows the Rule 16(a) or (b) order of contents above. | SCt Art. I R. 12A(1), 13(e), 16(a)-(b) | M |
| RI-TIME-003 | The Rule 12A statement of the case is filed within 20 days after the record is received; it is concise and does not exceed 3,000 words without leave. | SCt Art. I R. 12A(1) | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs (Rule 16(h))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RI-AMICUS-001 | An amicus brief may be filed only with the written consent of all parties, or by leave of the Court on motion. Consent or leave is not needed for a brief presented by the United States or by the State of Rhode Island or one of its officers or agencies. | SCt Art. I R. 16(h) | M |
| RI-AMICUS-002 | The brief may be conditionally filed with a motion for leave, which identifies the applicant's interest and states why the brief is desirable. | SCt Art. I R. 16(h) | M |
| RI-AMICUS-003 | Unless all parties consent, an amicus brief is filed within the time allowed the party whose position it supports. | SCt Art. I R. 16(h) | M |
| RI-AMICUS-004 | An amicus brief is subject to the form, certification and word limits of Rule 16(f) (all briefs filed under Rule 16). | SCt Art. I R. 16(f), 16(h) | M |
