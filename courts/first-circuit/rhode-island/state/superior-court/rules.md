# Rhode Island Superior Court: civil pleadings, motions and papers

Alias `risuperct`. Scope: pleadings, motions and other papers filed in civil actions in the Superior Court, and the electronic-filing requirements that govern them. Verified against the Superior Court Rules of Civil Procedure (February 2024 edition) and the Rhode Island Supreme Court Rules Governing Electronic Filing, Article X (revised September 2023), on 2026-10-21.

## Sources

| Tag | Source | URL |
|---|---|---|
| SCR | Superior Court Rules of Civil Procedure (courts.ri.gov, February 2024) | https://courts.ri.gov/Courts/superiorcourt/Documents/SuperiorCourtRulesOfCivilProcedure.pdf |
| ART10 | Supreme Court Rules Governing Electronic Filing, Article X (revised September 2023) | https://courts.ri.gov/Courts/SupremeCourt/Documents/Supreme-Rules-Article10.pdf |

Neither source states a paper size, margin width, typeface, or point size for trial-court pleadings. The Judiciary User Guide for Electronic Filing, which Article X Rule 4(a) incorporates for technical document requirements, was not located in this pass; no PDF technical requirement is modeled beyond the searchable-format rule.

## Form of pleadings and papers

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RISUPERCT-CAP-001 | Every pleading has a caption setting forth the name of the court, the county, the title of the action, the file number, and a designation as in Rule 7(a). In the complaint the title lists all parties; in other pleadings the first party on each side with an indication of others. | SCR Rule 10(a) | M |
| RISUPERCT-CONT-001 | Averments of claim or defense are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances; each claim or defense (other than denials) is stated in a separate count or defense where separation aids presentation. | SCR Rule 10(b) | M |
| RISUPERCT-CONT-002 | A copy of any written instrument that is an exhibit to a pleading is part of the pleading for all purposes. | SCR Rule 10(c) | M |
| RISUPERCT-FORM-001 | Rules on captions, signing and other matters of form of pleadings apply to all motions and other papers. | SCR Rule 7(b)(2) | M |
| RISUPERCT-CERT-001 | Every pleading, motion and paper is signed in accordance with Rule 11 (signing of pleadings, motions and other papers; sanctions). | SCR Rules 7(b)(5), 11 | M |
| RISUPERCT-CERT-002 | The electronic signature block shows the typed name, address, e-mail address, telephone number and attorney registration number of the signing attorney, in the form "/s/ NAME" or a facsimile signature with the typed name. A self-represented litigant's signature is the typographical form in Article X Rule 7(c). | ART10 Rule 7(b)-(c) | M |

## Electronic filing and format

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RISUPERCT-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing is mandatory for cases in the Superior Court through the Judiciary's Electronic Filing System (EFS), subject to the exceptions in Article X Rule 3(c) and self-represented litigants' election under Rule 3(b). | SCR Rule 1(b); ART10 Rule 3 | M |
| RISUPERCT-ELEC-002 | Documents filed in the EFS are in a searchable format, and conform to the technical requirements in the Judiciary User Guide for Electronic Filing. | ART10 Rule 4(a) | M |
| RISUPERCT-ELEC-003 | Documents in fillable portable document format (PDF), documents scanned in the wrong orientation, unreadable or illegible scans, and documents with a wrong or incomplete case caption are grounds for rejection. | SCR Rule 1 (rejection grounds list, pages 2-3) | M |
| RISUPERCT-ELEC-004 | A filing with a party or document name exceeding the EFS character allotment is rejected. | SCR Rule 1 (rejection grounds list) | M |
| RISUPERCT-ELEC-005 | Documents requiring the signatures of opposing parties are first signed on paper and then scanned and filed in a form that accurately reproduces the signatures. | ART10 Rule 7(e) | M |
| RISUPERCT-PRIV-001 | Non-public and personal identifying information is redacted before filing, with a separate non-public version where required. | ART10 Rule 8(b) | M |
| RISUPERCT-TYPE-001 | No typeface, point size, paper size or margin width is set by the Superior Court Rules of Civil Procedure or Article X for pleadings and motions. | SCR; ART10 (none stated) | A |
