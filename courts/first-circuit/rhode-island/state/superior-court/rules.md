# Rhode Island Superior Court: pleadings and memoranda

Alias `risuperct`. Scope: pleadings and memoranda of law filed in the Superior Court. No public filed exemplar was obtained (no court-root usage folder), so no doc-type folder is built yet. Verified against the Superior Court Rules of Civil Procedure (Rule 10; February 2024 copy on courts.ri.gov), the Superior Court Rules of Practice (February 2024; Rule 1.6), and the Superior Court Electronic Filing System Guidelines on 2026-10-22.

Local rules not retrieved: the typeface, point size, margin and page-size rules for Superior Court papers were not found in the three documents above. A search result claimed an 8½ × 11 inch, one-inch-margin rule in the Rules of Practice; the February 2024 Rules of Practice (9 pages) and the Civil Procedure Rules do not contain it, so it is not recorded here.

## Pleadings

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RISUPERCT-CONT-001 | Every pleading contains a caption setting forth the name of the court, the county, the title of the action, the file number, and a designation as in Rule 7(a). | SCRCP 10(a) | M |
| RISUPERCT-CONT-002 | All averments of claim or defense are made in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | SCRCP 10(b) | M |
| RISUPERCT-FILE-001 | Every document is submitted in searchable PDF or PDF-A, completed in full; fillable PDFs are not accepted. | EFS 4.1, 5.3.7 | M |

## Memoranda of law

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RISUPERCT-MEMO-001 | A memorandum of law is printed or typewritten, distinctly legible, and signed by the presenting attorney under the Supreme Court's electronic filing rules. | SCRP 1.6 | M |
| RISUPERCT-MEMO-002 | A memorandum contains: a brief and concise statement of the case; the specific questions raised, duly numbered; and the points made with the authorities relied on. | SCRP 1.6(1)-(3) | M |
| RISUPERCT-MEMO-003 | Where the court must examine record evidence, the brief states the leading facts the party deems established, with a reference to the pages where the evidence is found. | SCRP 1.6 | M |
| RISUPERCT-MEMO-004 | Memorandum format follows the Superior Court Electronic Filing System Guidelines. | SCRP 1.6; EFS | M |
