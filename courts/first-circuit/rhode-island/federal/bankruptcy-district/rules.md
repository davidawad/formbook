# Bankruptcy Court, District of Rhode Island: rules

Alias `rib`. Scope: filings by counsel through CM/ECF in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Rhode Island; doc types `adversary-complaint` (complaint commencing an adversary proceeding, FRBP 7001, 7003) and `motion-brief` (a motion with its points and authorities, or a supporting memorandum filed as an attachment to the motion). Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (national rule amendments and this court's usual local-rule effective date) and whenever the court posts amended Local Bankruptcy Rules or a Notice of Adoption at https://www.rib.uscourts.gov/local-rules-0 (amendments were adopted December 1, 2024, June 3, 2025, July 21, 2025 and December 1, 2025).

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a court rule (FRBP, LBR, or the District Court rules the LBR adopt for gaps). **C** = convention (seen across exemplars of different filers; the rules leave it open). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

Findings:

1. **11-point floor, no face.** LBR 1005-1(b) (Amended 12/1/2025): "All text ... must appear in at least 11 point type, except for footnotes which shall not be less than 10 point"; LBR 9013-1(b): "the type set (font size) in the body shall not be less than 11 point". No typeface and no margin is named anywhere in the LBR (searched "font", "typeface", "margin", "point").
2. **20-page limit for motions and memoranda, 10 for replies** (LBR 9013-1(b)), with footnotes barred from carrying body text.
3. **Response-time paragraph on every motion** (LBR 1005-1(d)(1)), verbatim text in the rule, with a list of excepted papers and different periods in 1005-1(d)(2).
4. **Fax number is required** in the identifying information (LBR 1005-1(c)), in addition to bar number, firm and client.
5. **Memorandum is an attachment, not its own entry** (LBR 5005-4(d)(2), Amended 7/21/2025).
6. **Page numbering**: the LBR are silent; LBR 1001-1(b) applies the District Court's Local Rules to matters the LBR do not cover, and DRI LR Cv 5(a)(3) requires numbering "at the bottom center of each page". Recorded as M by that reference (an inference from the two texts; no bankruptcy rule says it directly).
7. **FRBP 7008 consent statement** is required in every adversary complaint; neither exemplar (2018, 2019) has it.
8. The 2024 compilation's earlier LBR 1005-1 text (cited in the doc-type READMEs) was compared with the December 1, 2025 amended text: (a)-(c) unchanged in substance; (d)(2) adds item (Q) (FRBP 3002.1(f), (g)(4) motions, 28 days).

## Doc type: motion-brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| RIB-CONT-020 | A request for an order is made by written motion that states its grounds with particularity and sets forth the relief or order requested. | FRBP 9013(a)-(b) (FRBP) | M |
| RIB-CONT-021 | The party filing a motion "shall include with or within the paper points and authorities in support of said party's position", with "Specific reference to the applicable provisions of the Bankruptcy Code, the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, the Rhode Island General Laws or other controlling authorities". Excepted motions are listed in 9013-1(d). | LBR 9013-1(a), (d) (LBR) | M |
| RIB-LEN-001 | "Except with leave of Court, initial motions, responses, briefs and memoranda of law shall be limited to twenty (20) pages, and reply briefs shall be limited to ten (10) pages." Footnotes may not be used to circumvent the limit. | LBR 9013-1(b) (LBR) | M |
| RIB-CONT-022 | Every motion (except the papers in 1005-1(d)(2)) contains language "substantially similar to" the 14-day response paragraph quoted in the rule ("Within fourteen (14) days after service, ..."), single or double spaced, at least 11 point; excepted papers substitute the period listed in 1005-1(d)(2) (e.g. 21 days for sales and compromises; none for emergency motions). | LBR 1005-1(d)(1)-(2) (A25-12); LBR 9013-1(c) (LBR) | M |
| RIB-CONT-023 | A memorandum filed apart from the motion is an attachment to the motion (RIB-ELEC-004). | LBR 5005-4(d)(2) (A25-07) | M |
| RIB-CONT-024 | Motion title in capitals centered under the caption; memorandum title "MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT OF ... MOTION TO DISMISS" in capitals centered (EX-M1, EX-M2; two filers). Neither has a table of contents (9 and 13 pages). | EX-M1, EX-M2 | C |
