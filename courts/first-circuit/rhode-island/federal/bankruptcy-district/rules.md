# Bankruptcy Court, District of Rhode Island: rules

Alias `rib`. Scope: papers filed by counsel through CM/ECF in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Rhode Island; doc types `adversary-complaint` (complaint commencing an adversary proceeding, FRBP 7001, 7003) and `motion-brief` (a motion with its points and authorities "with or within the paper", LBR 9013-1(a), or a memorandum filed with it). Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (national rule amendments and this court's usual local-rule effective date) and whenever the court posts a Notice of Adoption of amended Local Rules on its Local Rules page (on 2026-10-17 the latest is the notice effective December 1, 2025, amending LBR 1005-1, 3002-1, 3016-1 and Local Forms 3016-1.1 and 7026-1).

Exemplar filers checked 2026-10-17 from the signature blocks: four different parties and four different counsel (Furness; Donahue for the U.S. Trustee; Iannotti for the U.S. Attorney; Hogberg for a creditor).

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a court rule (FRBP, R.I. LBR, or the D.R.I. local rule the LBR incorporates). **C** = convention (seen across exemplars of different filers; the rules leave it open). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

Findings:

1. **11-point floor, not 12.** LBR 1005-1(b) (Dec. 1, 2025) and LBR 9013-1(b): text "must appear in at least 11 point type, except for footnotes which shall not be less than 10 point". The D.R.I. district rule's 12-point floor (LR Cv 5(a)(3)) does not apply, because the LBRs cover type size. All four exemplars use Times New Roman 12 pt.
2. **Page limits are the bankruptcy court's own**: 20 pages for initial motions, responses, briefs and memoranda; 10 for replies, "Except with leave of Court" (LBR 9013-1(b)). Footnotes "may not be used to circumvent the page limit".
3. **Response-time language on every motion** (LBR 1005-1(d)(1)), at least 11 point, with the Clerk's Office address; different periods for the papers in (d)(2). EX-M2 (2017) carries it; EX-M1 (2012) does not.
4. **Certificate of service, then by the next business day.** LBR 9013-3(d)-(e): the certificate states how and when service was made, the names and addresses served and the certifier; for an electronic filing it "must be filed by the next business day", and if served on creditors the "Mailing Matrix by Case" ECF list is attached. Late filing means automatic denial.
5. **Signature block.** LBR 1005-1(c) requires the facsimile number, the law firm, the "state bar identification number" and "the name of the client"; LBR 5005-4(b)(2)(C)(ii) requires "/s/" before the electronic filer's name. LBR 1005-1(a) (Dec. 1, 2025) adds that the case name, number and chapter "must also appear on the signature page".
6. **Page numbering** is not covered by the LBRs; D.R.I. LR Cv 5(a)(3) ("the pages shall be numbered at the bottom center of each page") applies through LBR 1001-1(b). Recorded as M by that reading.
7. **FRBP 7008 consent statement.** Neither complaint exemplar (2018, 2019) contains the statement of consent or non-consent to final orders; FRBP 7008 requires it, so the exemplars are non-conforming on this point and the template follows the rule.
