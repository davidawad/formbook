# Supreme Court of Puerto Rico: alegatos, amicus briefs and motions for reconsideration

Alias `prsupreme`. Scope: filings in the Tribunal Supremo de Puerto Rico that the court's exemplars show: alegatos (answering brief, brief on the merits in certification and certiorari), amicus curiae briefs, and motions for reconsideration. Filings may be in Spanish; templates transcribe the filing as filed. Verified against the Reglamento del Tribunal Supremo de Puerto Rico (consolidated text, amended by ER-2023-0001 of June 14, 2023, 2023 TSPR 74), read from the PDF on 2026-10-21. Rule numbers below are the Reglamento's; the quoted text is in Spanish.

## Alegatos (answering brief, brief on the merits)

Rule 33 governs the form and content of every alegato. Rule 19(b) sets the appellee's alegato; Rule 21(c) and Rule 26(a) apply Rule 33 to certiorari and certification alegatos.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRSUPREME-COVER-001 | The cover contains only the caption of the case, the identification of the party filing (for the appellee, the recurrido's alegato), the name, address, telephone, fax, e-mail and Tribunal Supremo bar number of counsel, and for an unrepresented party the name, address and telephone with a statement that the party appears pro se. | TSPR R 33(b) | M |
| PRSUPREME-COVER-002 | The cover shows the Tribunal Supremo case number, and beneath it the lower-court case number and the panel that decided it, where the case came from the Tribunal de Apelaciones (in the exemplars also the lower-court name and case number). | TSPR R 17(d)(1)(C) for appeals; exemplar convention for certification | C |
| PRSUPREME-INDEX-001 | Immediately after the cover comes a detailed Índice de Materias giving the page where each part begins, with the full text of the issues raised in the index. | TSPR R 33(c), 38(a) | M |
| PRSUPREME-INDEX-002 | After the Índice de Materias comes a detailed Índice Legal of all cases, statutes and other authorities cited, with the pages where each appears, grouped as the rule orders (Puerto Rico cases, federal cases, other jurisdictions; then legislation; then commentators). | TSPR R 38(b) | M |
| PRSUPREME-BODY-001 | The body of an answering brief to an appeal (recurrido) answers the appellant's numbered parts; a separate discussion of each issue raised; a separate discussion of the Tribunal's authority to grant the remedy, if challenged; and an appendix only if the party adds one. A statement of facts is included where the party disagrees with the appellant's. | TSPR R 33(i)-(j) | M |
| PRSUPREME-BODY-002 | The body of a brief on the merits follows the numbered parts of Rule 33(e) for appeals, certiorari and certification (jurisdiction, the decision under review, facts, issues, discussion of each separately), and the discussion of each issue states the applicable law and authority. | TSPR R 33(e), 21(c), 26(a) | M |
| PRSUPREME-LEN-001 | An alegato of an appellee, a certiorari party or a certification party is at most 50 pages, excluding the índice and the appendix. | TSPR R 33(f) | M |
| PRSUPREME-CERT-001 | Every alegato carries the certification of notice (how and to whom it was notified), which Rule 39 requires in the filing itself. | TSPR R 39(a) | M |
| PRSUPREME-CONT-003 | Authorities are discussed in the body; no separate memorando de autoridades is accepted for an alegato. | TSPR R 40(g) | M |
| PRSUPREME-CONV-001 | Headings are bold and centred for the parts of the filing; the body is indented at the first line. | exemplar convention (answering brief, brief on the merits) | C |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRSUPREME-AMICUS-001 | An amicus brief is filed only with the consent of all parties, or with leave granted on a motion that states the interest of the amicus, why the brief is desirable and why the questions are relevant. The Government of Puerto Rico needs neither. | TSPR R 43(a)-(d) | A |
| PRSUPREME-AMICUS-002 | The amicus brief is at most 20 pages, excluding the índice and the appendix; original plus ten copies. | TSPR R 43(e), 40(d) | M |
| PRSUPREME-AMICUS-003 | The amicus brief has no reply unless the Court grants leave. The brief is notified under Rule 39. | TSPR R 43(f), 43 (last paragraph) | M |
| PRSUPREME-AMICUS-004 | The amicus brief carries an índice in the form of Rule 38 (the detailed índice and índice legal) and the caption and cover of Rule 33(b). | TSPR R 43(e), 38 | M |

The eval page limit for amicus briefs is not set: the 20-page limit excludes the índice and appendix, which the default check (which counts every page) cannot exclude. The limit is recorded here as `M` and checked by the Gate 2 review.

## Not included: motions (Rule 31)

Rule 31(e) caps every motion at ten pages and bars a separate memorando. The court's own motion exemplar (a 22-page moción with índice) exceeds that cap on its face, so a motion folder cannot be made to pass the page limit with the exemplar as the INPUT. No motion-brief folder is built for this court.
