# Tribunal de Apelaciones de Puerto Rico: appellate filings

Alias `prapp`. Scope: answering brief (alegato de la parte apelada, or memorando en oposición to a certiorari), petition for review (recurso de revisión), amicus curiae brief (escrito de comparecencia), and motion (moción; not built, see MOT). Verified against the Reglamento del Tribunal de Apelaciones (Resolución ER-2004-0010, as amended through Resolución ER-2025-0003 of 19 December 2025), consolidated text published by the Rama Judicial, on 2026-10-21. Filings are in Spanish; the rule text is quoted in English paraphrase here.

| PRAPP-FORM-001 | Paper is letter, 8½ × 11 in, for filings made through SUMAC. Legal paper, 8½ × 14 in, printed one side, applies only where the record is physical. The template uses letter. | RTA R. 70(A) | M |
| PRAPP-FORM-002 | Text is double-spaced. Direct quotations and footnotes (notas al calce) are excepted, and this template single-spaces them. | RTA R. 70(A) | M |
| PRAPP-FORM-003 | Type is pica or larger, which is 12 pt minimum. Reading a serif Times face as "similar" to Courier is an interpretation for David to confirm. | RTA R. 70(A) | M |
| PRAPP-FORM-004 | Left margin is not less than 1.5 in; right margin is not less than 0.5 in. The rule sets no top or bottom margin. The template uses 1.5 in left and 1 in right, top and bottom, all at or above the minimums. | RTA R. 70(A) | M |
| PRAPP-FORM-005 | Pages may not exceed the maximum the rules set unless a separate motion shows good cause and is granted. | RTA R. 70(D) | M |
| PRAPP-FORM-006 | No separate memorandum of authorities is accepted; authorities are cited and discussed in the body. | RTA R. 70(E); R. 16(C)(2), 59(C)(2), 68(C) | M |
| PRAPP-FORM-007 | A reference to an electronic-record entry is cited "Ent. #n" with the page or pages of that document. | RTA R. 70(G) | M |

| PRAPP-CERT-001 | Each counsel's block gives name, postal address, telephone, e-mail and the Tribunal Supremo number (RUA). A self-represented party gives name, postal address, telephone and e-mail if any. | RTA R. 16(A)(2), 59(A)(2), 73(B) | M |
| PRAPP-CERT-002 | A certificación de notificación states the method by which each party was served and that the term was met. Service between counsel is by e-mail to the RUA address. The certificate is excluded from the page limit. | RTA R. 15, 36 | M |
| PRAPP-COVER-001 | The first page is the cover. Its heading reads "Estado Libre Asociado de Puerto Rico", "Tribunal de Apelaciones" and the Región Judicial of origin. The cover holds only the caption, counsel information, and case information. | RTA R. 16(A), 59(A), 73(B) | M |
| PRAPP-COVER-002 | The caption gives the parties in the order they appeared in the lower tribunal, with the designation the rule uses for the filing (apelante/apelada; recurrente/recurrida; parte recurrida). | RTA R. 16(A)(1), 59(A)(1) | M |
| PRAPP-COVER-003 | The cover gives the lower-court case number and Sala (case information) on paper filings; the electronic cover may show the same. | RTA R. 16(A)(3), 59(A)(3) | C |

## Answering brief (alegato de la parte apelada; memorando en oposición)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRAPP-ANS-001 | The body follows this order: (1) citations of the provisions that establish the court's competence; (2) reference to the decision appealed or opposed; (3) a brief statement of the facts where applicable; (4) a separate discussion of the errors, the law and the case law; (5) the súplica. | RTA R. 73(D) | M |
| PRAPP-ANS-002 | Opposition to a certiorari petition (memorando en oposición) follows the same length limit and is filed within ten days of notice of the petition. | RTA R. 37(A) | M |
| PRAPP-ANS-003 | Length: 35 pages when the record is electronic (25 pages when physical), excluding the índice, the apéndice and the certificación de notificación. The template sets page_limit 35 over all pages, including the cover, which is stricter. | RTA R. 73(E), 37(A) | M |

## Petition for review (recurso de revisión)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRAPP-PET-001 | The body follows this order: (a) appearance naming the recurrentes; (b) citations of the provisions establishing jurisdiction and competence; (c) reference to the decision, rule or administrative order (agency, case number, Región Judicial, date of decision and of filing of the notice, and any interrupting motion); (d) concise statement of procedural and material facts; (e) brief statement of the errors; (f) discussion of the errors with law and case law; (g) súplica. The index follows the cover. | RTA R. 59(C)(1)(a)-(g), 59(B) | M |
| PRAPP-PET-002 | Length: 35 pages when the record is electronic (25 pages when physical), excluding the certificación de notificación, the índice and the apéndice. The template sets page_limit 35 over all pages, including the cover, which is stricter. | RTA R. 59(D) | M |
| PRAPP-PET-003 | The cover names the parties as in the administrative proceeding, identified as "parte recurrente" and "parte recurrida". | RTA R. 59(A)(1) | M |

## Amicus curiae brief (escrito de comparecencia)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRAPP-AMI-001 | The brief is filed with the written consent of all parties, or with a motion of appearance that states the nature of the amicus's interest and its relevance and is no longer than five pages. | RTA R. 81(B), 81(C) | M |
| PRAPP-AMI-002 | Filed electronically through SUMAC. | RTA R. 81 (comment, 2025 amendment); R. 2.1 | M |
| PRAPP-AMI-003 | The rule sets no page limit for the amicus brief itself; the five-page limit applies to the motion only. The template sets no page limit. | RTA R. 81(C) | A |

## Motion (moción; not built)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRAPP-MOT-001 | Every motion is served on the other parties the same day it is filed, and the filing states that service. The filing states the name of the party presenting it in the appearance. | RTA R. 68(A) | M |
| PRAPP-MOT-002 | A motion states its legal grounds and includes the argument, the citation of authorities and the plea. No separate memorandum. | RTA R. 68(C) | M |
| PRAPP-MOT-003 | No motion exceeds ten pages. | RTA R. 68(D) | M |
