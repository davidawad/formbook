# Bankruptcy Court, District of Puerto Rico: rules

Alias `prb`. Scope: papers filed by counsel through CM/ECF in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Puerto Rico; doc types `adversary-complaint` (complaint commencing an adversary proceeding, FRBP 7001, 7003) and `motion-brief` (a motion with its supporting memorandum of law, which LBR 9013-2(a) allows in one document). Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (national rule amendments) and whenever the court posts an amended Local Bankruptcy Rule or a general order on its "Local Rules" or "Administrative & General Orders" pages (the latest general order listed on 2026-10-17 is GO 26-02; GO 26-01 adopted LBR 9019-1 and 9019-2, which are mediation rules and do not touch form).

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a court rule (FRBP, LBR, or the District Court local rule the LBR incorporates). **C** = convention (seen across exemplars of different filers; the rules leave it open). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

Findings:

1. **The LBRs set no font, size, spacing or margin.** Searched the LBR text for "point", "font", "typeface", "double", "margin", "pages": the only type-size clause is LBR 9013-1(c)(1) (the response-time notice "must be in at least 11 point type"). LBR 1005-1(b) fixes only the page format (8 1/2" x 11" pdf).
2. **District Court L.Cv.R. 7(e) fills the gap.** LBR 1001-1(b): "The local rules of the United States District Court for the District of Puerto Rico will apply to the extent that a procedural matter is not covered by these LBRs or the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure." L.Cv.R. 7(e) (and L.Cv.R. 10(e) for pleadings) sets the form of papers: 8 1/2 x 11, a proportionally spaced font "no less than twelve (12) points", double spacing with listed exceptions, consecutive page numbers "preferably at the bottom center", and page limits (25 pages dispositive, 15 non-dispositive, 10 reply). This file records those as M through LBR 1001-1(b). This is a reading of LBR 1001-1(b); the court has not issued a statement that L.Cv.R. 7(e) governs bankruptcy papers. L.Cv.R. 7(e) names no margin, so margins are a convention.
3. **No typeface is named** in either set of local rules. The four exemplars use four different faces (Courier New, Leelawadee UI Semilight, Garamond, Times New Roman), all at 12 pt; the face is each filer's style, not a convention.
4. **Response-time notice.** Every initial motion (other than those listed in LBR 9013-1(c)(3)) must carry the LBR 9013-1(c)(1) notice. EX-A2 also puts one on its complaint; that is that filer's practice, not a rule for complaints.
5. **Signature block is longer than FRBP 9011(a).** LBR 1005-1(c) adds e-mail, facsimile, firm, client and the USDC-PR bar number. EX-A1, EX-A2 and EX-M1 omit the facsimile number (EX-M2 gives one in one of its two blocks), and EX-A1, EX-M1 and EX-M2 omit the client's name: the filed exemplars do not fully comply on this point.
