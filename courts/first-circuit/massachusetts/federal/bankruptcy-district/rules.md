# Bankruptcy Court, District of Massachusetts: rules

Alias `mab`. Scope: filings by counsel through CM/ECF in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Massachusetts; doc types `adversary-complaint` (complaint commencing an adversary proceeding, FRBP 7001, 7003) and `motion-brief` (a motion with its argument, or a supporting memorandum filed with the motion). Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (national rule amendments) and whenever the court posts amended Local Bankruptcy Rules, appendices, ECF Administrative Procedures or standing orders (the current rules took effect May 1, 2025).

**Access note.** On 2026-10-17 the court's site (www.mab.uscourts.gov) answered every request from this box, including `/`, with an F5 "Request Rejected" page. The court documents below were therefore read from the Internet Archive's stored copies of the court's own files (the URL of the court file and the archive capture actually read are both given). The capture dates are 2025-05-25 to 2026-01-06; an amendment posted after the capture date would not be reflected. Re-check against the live site when it is reachable.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a court rule (FRBP, MLBR, Appendix 8, or the EAP the MLBR incorporates). **C** = convention (seen across exemplars of different filers; the rules leave it open). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

Findings:

1. **Size floor, no face.** APP8(c): body "not less than 12-point type" and footnotes "10-point"; "a plain, Roman Type". No typeface is named.
2. **First page need not be numbered** (APP8(b)); every other page is numbered consecutively. Page numbers may sit in the margins, but no other text (APP8(a)).
3. **"/s/" signatures are allowed.** SO24-4 (May 2024) required an imaged signature under the then "MLBR Appendix 8, Rule 7". The May 1, 2025 Appendix 8 is "General Requirements of Form" with only subsections (a) to (c), and EAP 1(a) states the EAP "supersede all prior electronic filing rules"; EAP 5(d) allows "a handwritten signature, imaged signature or, "/s/ [name]"". The current rule is EAP 5(d). (Inference from the texts; no order expressly withdrawing SO24-4 was found.)
4. **No page limit for motions or memoranda** in the MLBR (searched for "pages", "page limit", "exceed").
5. **Certificate of service is mandatory for motions** (MLBR 9013-3(a)), even with ECF service, unlike FRCP 5(d)(1)(B) in the district court.
6. **FRBP 7008 consent statement** is required in every adversary complaint; EX-A2 has it, EX-A1 does not (EX-A1 is non-conforming on this point).
