# Supreme Judicial Court of Massachusetts: briefs, amicus briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `mass`. Scope: filings in the Supreme Judicial Court (direct and further appellate review, and motions): principal briefs, reply briefs, amicus curiae briefs, applications for and responses to direct or further appellate review, and motions and other documents. Verified against the Massachusetts Rules of Appellate Procedure as posted by the Commonwealth on mass.gov (rule notes run through 2024) on 2026-10-22.

## Form, typeface, spacing and margins (all documents)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASS-FORM-001 | Paper is 8.5 by 11 inches; pages are firmly bound at the left, with a cover or front page. | MRAP 20(a)(1)(A) | M |
| MASS-FORM-002 | Text is produced in a clear black image on white paper. Visual aids (images, charts, tables) must be supported by citation and meet the legibility and color rules of MRAP 18(a)(1)(F). | MRAP 20(a)(1)(A)-(B) | M |
| MASS-FONT-001 | A proportionally spaced typeface, such as Times New Roman, is set at 14 point or larger, including footnotes. A monospaced face is the alternative: Courier New or equivalent, 12 point or larger, not exceeding 10.5 characters per inch. | MRAP 20(a)(4)(B) | M |
| MASS-FONT-002 | Motions and other documents are set in 12 point or larger, double-spaced. | MRAP 20(b)(2)(A) | M |
| MASS-MARGIN-001 | With a proportionally spaced font, top, bottom, left and right margins are at least 1 inch. With a monospaced font, top and bottom margins are at least 1 inch and left and right at least 1.5 inches. | MRAP 20(a)(4)(A) | M |
| MASS-SPACE-001 | Text is double-spaced: no more than 3 lines per vertical inch and no more than 27 double-spaced lines per page. Argument headings, footnotes, visual aids and indented quotations may be single-spaced (no more than 6 lines per vertical inch). | MRAP 20(a)(4)(C) | M |
| MASS-PAGE-001 | Page numbers appear in the margin. The cover is paginated as page 1, and later pages are numbered consecutively through the last page, including any addendum. | MRAP 20(a)(4)(A) | M |
| MASS-FORM-003 | The text may appear on both sides of the page. | MRAP 20(a)(4)(D) | M |
| MASS-FORM-004 | Briefs not in substantial compliance with these rules are not docketed unless the court orders otherwise. | MRAP 20(a)(7) | M |

## Principal briefs (appellant's brief, appellee's brief, brief on the merits)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASS-ORDER-001 | The brief sections appear in this order: cover; corporate disclosure statement (S.J.C. Rule 1:21, if required); table of contents; table of authorities; statement of issues; statement of the case; statement of facts; summary of argument (required above 20 pages of argument or 4,500 words); argument with standard of review for each issue; request for appellate attorney's fees and costs (if any); conclusion; signature block; addendum; certificate of compliance; certificate of service. | MRAP 16(a)(1)-(15) | M |
| MASS-LEN-001 | A principal brief is at most 50 pages if monospaced, or at most 11,000 words if proportionally spaced. | MRAP 20(a)(2)(A) | M |
| MASS-LEN-002 | In a cross appeal, the appellant's principal brief is limited as in MASS-LEN-001; the appellee's principal and response brief is at most 60 pages or 13,000 words (proportional); the appellant's response and reply brief is at most 50 pages or 11,000 words. | MRAP 20(a)(3)(A)-(C) | M |
| MASS-LEN-003 | Only the parts required by MRAP 16(a)(5)-(11), including headings, footnotes, visual aids and quotations, count toward length limits. | MRAP 20(a)(2)(D), 20(a)(3)(F) | M |
| MASS-CERT-001 | The last page of each brief carries a certification of compliance with the filing rules (including MRAP 16, 18, 20 and 21), stating how the length limit was ascertained: font name, size and characters per inch with the non-excluded page count, or font name and size with the non-excluded word count and the word-processing program name and version. | MRAP 16(k); MRAP 20(a)(2)(F) | M |
| MASS-SIG-001 | The signature block gives the printed and signed name(s), personal pronouns (if elected), BBO number(s), mailing and electronic addresses, telephone number(s), office name, and the date of signing. | MRAP 16(a)(12) | M |
| MASS-STAND-001 | Each issue's argument states the applicable standard of review. | MRAP 16(a)(9)(B) | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASS-TIME-002 | An amicus brief may be filed only by leave of the court or a single justice on motion, when solicited by the court, or when the Commonwealth or its officer or agency files it. | MRAP 17(a) | M |
| MASS-TIME-001 | An amicus brief is filed no later than 21 days before oral argument unless the court or a single justice grants leave for later filing. | MRAP 17(b) | M |
| MASS-COVER-005 | The amicus cover is green, identifies the party or parties supported, and states whether the brief supports affirmance, reversal or neither. | MRAP 20(a)(6)(A); MRAP 17(c) | M |
| MASS-LEN-005 | An amicus brief is at most 35 pages if monospaced, or at most 7,500 words if proportionally spaced (cross appeals as in MASS-LEN-002). | MRAP 20(a)(2)(C), 20(a)(3)(E) | M |
| MASS-CONT-001 | An amicus brief need not carry every Rule 16 part, but includes: a corporate disclosure statement if the amicus is a corporation; a table of contents; a table of authorities; a concise statement of the amicus's identity and interest; the authorship and funding declaration; disclosure of any representation of a party in a related proceeding; a summary of argument if the argument exceeds 20 pages or 4,500 words; the argument; the signature block; the compliance certificate (with the length method, as in MASS-LEN-005); and the certificate of service. | MRAP 17(c)(1)-(10) | M |
