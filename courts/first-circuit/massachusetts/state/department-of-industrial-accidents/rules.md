# Massachusetts Department of Industrial Accidents: Reviewing Board appeals and briefs

Alias `maworkcompcom`. Scope: notices of appeal to the Industrial Accident Reviewing Board and the briefs filed on appeal (appellant, appellee, cross-appellant, amicus, reply). Verified against 452 CMR 1.00 (Adjudicatory Rules of the Industrial Accident Board, mass.gov copy dated 1/27/17, section 1.15 Reviewing Board) on 2026-10-21.

## Notice of appeal

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-001 | A notice of appeal from an administrative judge's decision is filed with the Department on the form the Department prescribes, within 30 days from the filing date of the decision, unless the Director permits a late appeal under M.G.L. c. 152, § 11C. "Filed" under 452 CMR 1.15(1) means placed in the mail, postmarked, or sent electronically no later than midnight on the 14th day when appealing a conference order under M.G.L. c. 152, § 10A. | 452 CMR 1.15(1); 452 CMR 1.02 (definition of "filed") | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-002 | A copy of the notice of appeal is served by mail or in hand on counsel for each party, or on each unrepresented party. | 452 CMR 1.15(1) | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-003 | The filing fee (30% of the average weekly wage in the Commonwealth at the time of payment) or a request for waiver on the Department form accompanies the notice of appeal to the Reviewing Board. | 452 CMR 1.15(2) | M |

## Appellant's brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-001 | Unless the Reviewing Board orders otherwise, the appellant files a brief, with the following parts under headings and in this order: (1) a statement of the issues presented, stated with particularity (a bare statement that the decision is beyond the judge's authority, arbitrary or capricious, or contrary to law does not suffice); (2) a statement of the case, with the nature of the case, the course of proceedings and disposition, and a brief statement of relevant facts with record references; (3) the argument, with authorities and record citations; (4) a short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; (5) names, addresses and telephone numbers of counsel and their firms. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(a)1-5 | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-002 | Statutory provisions relied on are reproduced in full, including all amendments and any applicability and effective-date provisions. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(c) | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-003 | Citations to decisions and other authorities give, besides the first page, a pinpoint page for the material relied on and the year of the decision or authority. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(d) | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-004 | Parties are designated as the administrative judge designated them (for example "the employee", "the employer", "the insurer"); references to "appellant" and "appellee" are kept to a minimum. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(g) | M |

## Length and form of briefs and appendices

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-LEN-001 | Briefs do not exceed 30 pages, excluding pages with a table of contents, tables of citations, and any addendum of statutes, rules or regulations. An administrative judge may permit more only on a motion that names the issues and why they merit additional pages. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(b)1 | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-004 | Briefs and appendices are produced by a duplicating or copying process giving a clear black image on white paper, 8½ inches wide by 11 inches high. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(b)2 | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-005 | Top, bottom, left and right margins are at least one inch. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(b)2 | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-006 | Typeface is 12 point Courier or larger, and not exceeding 10.5 characters per inch. This is the court's mandated face: a template for this court uses Courier, not the standard Times New Roman, and rules.md records the override here with its cite. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(b)2 | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-007 | Text is double-spaced. Argument headings, footnotes and indented quotations are single-spaced. Text may appear on both sides of a page, but the numbered pages of text may not exceed the page limit. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(b)2 | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-008 | The Reviewing Board may accept a brief that does not substantially comply with 452 CMR 1.00, at an administrative judge's discretion on the Board's behalf. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(b)3 | A |

## Appellee, cross-appellant, amicus and reply briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-005 | Response briefs (appellee, cross-appellant, amicus) follow MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-001 and MA-FORM rules, except that a statement of issues is omitted unless the responding party is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(f) | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-006 | An amicus curiae first notifies the Reviewing Board of its intention to file a brief. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(e) | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-007 | Time to file: appellant 30 days after notice from the Reviewing Board that the brief is due; appellee, cross-appellant or amicus within 20 days of the appellant's brief; appellant reply 20 days after that. No further briefs without leave. For cross-appeals, the party that files first is the appellant. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(h) | M |
| MAWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-008 | An extension of time to file a brief is requested in writing, stating the length of extension and the specific reason. | 452 CMR 1.15(4)(i) | M |
