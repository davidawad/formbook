# Massachusetts Appellate Tax Board: petitions, statements, motions and briefs

Alias `masstaxbd`. Scope: petitions under formal and small claims procedure, statements under informal procedure, motions and post-hearing briefs. Verified against 831 CMR 1.00 (Appellate Tax Board Rules of Practice and Procedure, effective 1/5/24, corrected 2/2/24) on 2026-10-21.

## Form of all filed documents

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-001 | Hard-copy pleadings, motions and other documents are printed or typewritten on one side only of plain white paper measuring 8½ × 11 inches, with adequate margins, clearly legible. | ATB Rule 1.03(1) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-002 | The document is signed by the party or the person appearing for the party. An electronic signature may be a scan of a handwritten signature, a stylus or mouse signature, an inserted signature image, or a "/s/ name of signatory" block. | ATB Rule 1.03(1), (3) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-003 | Filing by email to the Clerk is encouraged for pleadings other than the formal petition (Rule 1.05), the small claims petition (Rule 1.10), and the informal statement (Rule 1.07). | ATB Rule 1.03(2) | C |
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-004 | Filing and service rules: the appellant files an original and two copies of the formal petition (Rule 1.06) and of the small claims petition (Rule 1.10(6)), delivered in hand, by first-class mail, or by an alternative delivery service designated in Rule 11. | ATB Rules 1.06(1), 1.10(6), 1.11 | M |
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-005 | The appellant files a signed acknowledgment or certificate of service with the Clerk. The Clerk may receive it by email. | ATB Rule 1.06(4) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-LEN-001 | No page or word limit for pleadings or briefs appears in 831 CMR 1.00. | ATB Rules 1.05 to 1.31 (none stated) | A |

## Motions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSTAXBD-MOT-001 | Motions are in writing, served on the opposing party or its counsel (email service permitted). | ATB Rule 1.16(1) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-MOT-002 | A discovery-related motion includes a certificate that the pre-motion conference was held, with its date, time and the names of all participating parties, or the efforts made to hold it. Without the certificate the motion is not scheduled. | ATB Rule 1.16(4) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-MOT-003 | A discovery-related motion carries a certificate of service stating how the motion and notice of hearing were served on the opposing party. | ATB Rule 1.16(4) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-MOT-004 | Motion hearing days and times follow the Board's Standing Order governing motions as posted on the Board website. | ATB Rule 1.16(2) | A |

## Briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSTAXBD-BRF-001 | Briefs are filed when the presiding Member orders them or a party requests them, and on the schedule the Member sets. Parties may also file briefs before or at the hearing. | ATB Rule 1.31(1)-(2) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-BRF-002 | Briefs are filed with the Board and served on the opposing party by email unless the Board orders otherwise. | ATB Rule 1.31(3) | M |
