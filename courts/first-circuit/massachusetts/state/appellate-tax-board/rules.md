# Massachusetts Appellate Tax Board: pleadings, motions and briefs

Alias `masstaxbd`. Scope: pleadings, motions and briefs filed with the Appellate Tax Board. No public filed exemplar was obtained (see the court's README), so no doc-type folder is built yet. Verified against 831 CMR 1.00, Appellate Tax Board Rules of Practice and Procedure (PDF on mass.gov; footer shows "2/2/24 (Effective 1/5/24) - corrected") on 2026-10-22.

## Form of documents

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-001 | Hard copies of pleadings, motions and other documents are printed or typewritten on one side only of plain white paper measuring 8½ by 11 inches, with adequate margins, clearly legible. | 831 CMR 1.03(1) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-002 | Each pleading, motion and other document is signed by the party or the person appearing for the party. Electronic signatures are permitted (scan, stylus, mouse or similar, per 1.03(3)). | 831 CMR 1.03(1), (3) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-003 | The Board encourages filing by email to the Clerk, except for the petitions under Rules 5 and 10 and the statement under the informal procedure under Rule 7. | 831 CMR 1.03(2) | C |
| MASSTAXBD-FORM-004 | The rules set no typeface, point size, line spacing, page limit or cover format for pleadings, motions or briefs. Those are tier-2 conventions (clerk expectation), not tier-1 requirements. | 831 CMR 1.03 (silence) | A |

## Motions and briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSTAXBD-MOT-001 | Motions are in writing with a copy served on the opposing party or its appearing person; motions may be filed and served by email. | 831 CMR 1.16(1) | M |
| MASSTAXBD-BRIEF-001 | Briefs are filed with the Board and served on the opposing party by email, unless the Board orders otherwise; the presiding Member sets the briefing schedule after a hearing or the parties may file before or at the hearing. | 831 CMR 1.31(1)-(3) | M |
