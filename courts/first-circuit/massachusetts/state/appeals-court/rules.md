# Massachusetts Appeals Court: briefs, motions and amicus briefs

Alias `massappct`. Scope: appellant's principal (opening) brief, appellee's principal and response (answering) brief, reply brief, motion with supporting memorandum, and amicus curiae brief. Verified against the Massachusetts Rules of Appellate Procedure as posted on mass.gov on 2026-10-21 (Rule 20 amended and effective May 7, 2024). No Appeals Court form order beyond these rules was retrieved.

## Format of every brief (opening, answering, reply, amicus)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-001 | Pages are 8.5 inches wide and 11 inches high. | MRAP 20(a)(1)(A) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-002 | Typeface of all text, including footnotes, is a proportionally spaced font (Times New Roman is the example the rule gives) of 14 point or larger. | MRAP 20(a)(4)(B)(ii) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-003 | With a proportionally spaced font, the top, bottom, left and right margins are each at least 1 inch. | MRAP 20(a)(4)(A) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-004 | Text is double-spaced (not more than 3 lines of text per vertical inch and not more than 27 double-spaced lines on a page). Argument headings, footnotes, visual aids and indented quotations may be single-spaced (not more than 6 lines per vertical inch). | MRAP 20(a)(4)(C) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-005 | Page numbers appear in the margin. The cover is paginated as page 1 and pages run consecutively through the last page, including any addendum. | MRAP 20(a)(4)(A), 20(a)(6)(B)(vii), 16(a)(13) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-006 | Cover contents: name of the court and the case number; title of the case (Rule 10(a)); nature of the proceeding and the lower court; title of the document; name(s), personal pronouns if elected, Board of Bar Overseers number(s), mailing and electronic addresses and telephone number(s) of the filer, and the office name if any; cover paginated as page 1. | MRAP 20(a)(6)(B)(i)-(v), (vii), 16(a)(1) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-007 | Where impounded material is included, the cover carries the notification required by Rule 16(m). | MRAP 20(a)(6)(B)(vi) | M (conditional) |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-008 | Table of contents lists each section and heading with the page on which it begins. Table of authorities lists each case, statute, rule and other authority with each page where it is cited, alphabetically or numerically. | MRAP 16(a)(3), 16(a)(4), 16(c), 16(b) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-009 | Each factual statement is supported by a record citation to appendix (or transcript or exhibit) volume and page, in the form RAII/55 or TRIII/231-232. | MRAP 16(e), 16(a)(7) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-010 | Certificate of compliance on the last page of the brief. For a proportionally spaced font it states the font name and size, the number of non-excluded words, and the name and version of the word-processing program. | MRAP 16(k), 16(a)(14) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-011 | Certificate of service appears within the brief and states the court and case number, the case title, the brief title, the party served, the printed and signed name and contact details of the person who made service, the name and mailing address (and electronic address if known) of each person served, and the date and manner of service. | MRAP 13(e)(1)-(2), 16(a)(15) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-012 | Signature block: printed and signed name(s), personal pronouns if elected, Board of Bar Overseers number(s), mailing and electronic addresses, telephone number(s), office name if any, and the date of signing. | MRAP 16(a)(12) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-013 | Redaction: personal identifying information is protected as Rule 21 requires (cross-referenced to S.J.C. Rule 1:24). | MRAP 21, 16(k) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-014 | Color of cover for paper filings: blue (appellant), red (appellee), yellow (intervenor), green (amicus), gray (reply). Color is not required for electronically filed briefs. | MRAP 20(a)(6)(A) | A |
| MASSAPPCT-FORM-015 | A brief not in substantial compliance with these rules may not be docketed unless the court or a single justice orders otherwise. | MRAP 20(a)(7) | A |

## Opening brief (appellant's principal brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSAPPCT-OPEN-001 | Order of parts: cover; corporate disclosure statement if required (S.J.C. Rule 1:21); table of contents; table of authorities; statement of issues; statement of the case; statement of facts; summary of argument (required when the argument exceeds 20 pages or 4,500 words); argument, with a standard of review for each issue; request for appellate attorney's fees and costs, if any; conclusion; signature block; addendum; certificate of compliance; certificate of service. | MRAP 16(a)(1)-(15) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-OPEN-002 | Length of a principal brief in a case other than a cross appeal: not more than 11,000 words if proportionally spaced (or 50 pages if monospaced). | MRAP 20(a)(2), 16(h) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-OPEN-003 | Statement of issues describes each issue presented for review concisely and with particularity. | MRAP 16(a)(5) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-OPEN-004 | Statement of the case gives the nature of the appeal, the procedural history, the record references and the disposition below. | MRAP 16(a)(6) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-OPEN-005 | Each issue states the applicable standard of review. | MRAP 16(a)(9)(B) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-OPEN-006 | Addendum, inside the brief, begins with its own table of contents and includes the appealed judgment or order (with any written decision or findings and a typed copy of any handwritten endorsement), the constitutional provisions, statutes, rules and regulations whose consideration is required, and a copy of each unpublished decision cited. An outline plan is included where geographical facts matter. | MRAP 16(a)(13)(A)-(E) | M (conditional) |

## Answering brief (appellee's principal and response brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSAPPCT-ANS-001 | Contents follow Rule 16(a) and the appellant's brief with these exceptions: statements of the issues, case, facts and standard(s) of review need not be made unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's; the argument states the appellee's contentions; the addendum includes the items pertinent to the appellee's argument. | MRAP 16(b)(1)-(3) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-ANS-002 | Length in a case other than a cross appeal: a principal brief is not more than 11,000 words if proportionally spaced (or 50 pages if monospaced). | MRAP 20(a)(2), 16(h) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-ANS-003 | In a cross appeal, the appellee's principal and response brief is not more than 13,000 words if proportionally spaced (or 60 pages), and contains the issues and argument of the appellee's own appeal as well as the answer to the appellant's brief. | MRAP 20(a)(3), 16(i)(1)-(2) | M (conditional) |

## Reply brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSAPPCT-REPLY-001 | The reply brief responds to the appellee's argument and raises no new issues. It carries the cover, table of contents, table of authorities, argument, conclusion, signature block, addendum, certificate of compliance and certificate of service required by Rule 16(a). | MRAP 16(c), 16(a)(1), (3), (4), (9), (11)-(15) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-REPLY-002 | Length: a reply brief is not more than 4,500 words if proportionally spaced (or 20 pages if monospaced); an appellee's reply brief in a cross appeal has the same limit. | MRAP 20(a)(2)-(3) | M |

## Motion and supporting memorandum (Rule 20(b))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSAPPCT-MOT-001 | A motion or other document addressed to the court has a caption on the first page giving the name of the court, the title of the case, the docket number, and a brief descriptive title of the document's purpose. | MRAP 20(b)(2)(A) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-MOT-002 | Lines of text are double-spaced and in 12 point or larger font, with side and top margins of at least 1 inch. | MRAP 20(b)(2)(A) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-MOT-003 | The document is no longer than reasonably necessary. A motion to exceed a brief's length limit states the issues and why they merit more pages or words, and is not granted except for extraordinary reasons. | MRAP 20(b)(2)(A), 20(a)(2) | M |

## Amicus curiae brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MASSAPPCT-AMI-001 | An amicus brief is filed only by leave of the court or a single justice on motion, when the court solicits it, or when the Commonwealth or its officer or agency is the amicus. The motion identifies the applicant's interest and why the brief is desirable; the brief may be conditionally filed with the motion. | MRAP 17(a) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-AMI-002 | The brief is filed no later than 21 days before oral argument unless leave for later filing is granted for cause. | MRAP 17(b) | A |
| MASSAPPCT-AMI-003 | The cover follows Rule 20(a)(6)(B) and also identifies the party or parties supported and states whether the brief supports affirmance, reversal or neither. | MRAP 17(c), 20(a)(6)(B) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-AMI-004 | The brief contains a table of contents and a table of authorities, a corporate disclosure statement if the amicus is a corporation, a concise statement of the amicus's identity and interest, and the declaration on authorship, funding and prior representation that Rule 17(c) requires. | MRAP 17(c) | M |
| MASSAPPCT-AMI-005 | Length: not more than 35 pages if monospaced, or 7,500 words if proportionally spaced. | MRAP 20(a)(2) | M |
