# United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit: principal and reply briefs

Alias `ca11`. Scope: appellant's opening brief, appellee's answering brief, and reply brief (`opening-brief`, `answering-brief`, `reply-brief`). Verified against the Rules of the United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit (Clerk's bookmarked edition, memorandum dated August 1, 2026) and the Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure (as bundled in that PDF) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Typeface note: FRAP 32(a)(5)(A) requires a proportionally spaced face of 14-point or larger. That is stricter than the repo default (12 pt), so the court's rule governs and the eval must be set to 14 pt for this court.

## Form of every principal and reply brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA11-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inch. Text is double-spaced; quotations more than two lines long may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. Margins are at least one inch on all four sides. Page numbers may sit in the margins; no text may. | FRAP 32(a)(4); CIRR 32-3 | M |
| CA11-FORM-002 | Proportionally spaced face must include serifs (sans-serif allowed in headings and captions) and must be 14-point or larger. A monospaced face may not exceed 10½ characters per inch. | FRAP 32(a)(5)(A)-(B) | M |
| CA11-FORM-003 | Plain roman type; italics or boldface for emphasis only. Case names are italicized or underlined. | FRAP 32(a)(6) | M |
| CA11-FORM-004 | Only the cover page, certificate of service, direct quotes, headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. The table of contents and table of citations are double-spaced. | CIRR 32-3 | M |
| CA11-FORM-005 | Cover (front): case number centered at top; name of the court; case title; nature of the proceeding and the court or agency below; title identifying the party or parties for whom the brief is filed; name, office address and telephone number of counsel. | FRAP 32(a)(2)(A)-(F); CIRR 32-2 | M |
| CA11-FORM-006 | Cover color: appellant's brief blue; appellee's brief red; reply brief gray; amicus or intervenor green. | FRAP 32(a)(2); CIRR I.O.P. 1 | M |
| CA11-FORM-007 | Binding is secure, does not obscure text, and lies reasonably flat. Covers are durable (at least 90 lb.), bound along the left margin; exposed metal prong fasteners are prohibited. | FRAP 32(a)(3); CIRR 32-2 | M |
| CA11-LEN-001 | Length: principal brief not more than 30 pages or 13,000 words (or 1,300 lines if monospaced); reply brief not more than 15 pages or half the principal-brief word limit (6,500 words). | FRAP 32(a)(7)(A)-(B) | M |
| CA11-LEN-002 | Pages are consecutively numbered. The cover, certificates listed in CIRR 28-1(a)-(g), (m) and (n), and any addendum of statutes do not need numbers and do not count toward length. | CIRR 32-4 | M |
| CA11-CERT-001 | Certificate of compliance with the type-volume and typeface limits under FRAP 32(g), shown in the brief. | FRAP 32(g); CIRR 28-1(l) | M |
| CA11-CERT-002 | Certificate of service at the end of the brief, when service is required. | FRAP 25(d); CIRR I.O.P. 3(a); CIRR 28-1(m) | M |
| CA11-CONT-001 | Principal brief contents in this order: (a) cover page; (b) statement regarding oral argument; (c) table of contents; (d) table of citations; (e) statement regarding adoption of other parties' briefs; (f) statement of subject-matter and appellate jurisdiction; (g) statement of the issues; (h) statement of the case; (i) summary of the argument; (j) argument and citations of authority; (k) conclusion; (l) certificate of compliance; (m) certificate of service. | CIRR 28-1(a)-(m) | M |
| CA11-ELEC-001 | Filers redact personal identifiers under FRAP 25(a)(5) and CIRR 25-5. | FRAP 25(a)(5); CIRR 25-5 | M |
