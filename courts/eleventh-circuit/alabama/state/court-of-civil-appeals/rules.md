# Alabama Court of Civil Appeals: briefs

Alias `alacivapp`. Scope: opening, answering and reply briefs in civil appeals. Verified against Alabama Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 28 (amended eff. Oct. 1, 2024) and 32 (amended eff. July 9, 2021), on 2026-10-21.

## Opening, answering and reply briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALACIVAPP-FORM-001 | All documents are on 8½ × 11 inch paper. | ARAP 32(a)(6) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-FORM-002 | Text is double-spaced. Quotations of more than 2 but not more than 25 lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings, footnotes, and quotations from statutes, evidentiary materials, and other record matter may be single-spaced. | ARAP 32(a)(6) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-FORM-003 | Margins are at least one inch on all four sides. Page numbers may be placed in the margins; no other text may appear there. | ARAP 32(a)(6) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-FORM-004 | The font of all text, including footnotes, is Century Schoolbook 14 point. The type style is plain roman; italics or boldface may be used for emphasis; case names are italicized or underlined. The margins of headings, sentences, and paragraphs in text and footnotes are fully justified. Leading, kerning, or tracking adjustments to evade limits are prohibited. | ARAP 32(a)(7) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-FORM-005 | Century Schoolbook 14 is the rule's face. A filer who lacks it must certify the closest approximation (ARAP 32(a)(7)); the template uses the Century Schoolbook clone and needs no such certificate. | ARAP 32(a)(7) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-LEN-001 | A principal brief may not exceed 14,000 words (70 pages pro se); a reply brief may not exceed 7,000 words (35 pages pro se). The limit counts from the statement of the case through the conclusion, including headings, footnotes, and quotations, and excludes the cover, oral-argument statement, table of contents, jurisdiction statement, table of authorities, certificates, signature block, and appendices. | ARAP 28(j)(1); ARAP 32(c) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-LEN-002 | Pages are numbered in Arabic numerals, with pagination beginning on the cover; the cover shows no page number. | ARAP 28(j)(1) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-COV-001 | The cover shows, in order: the appellate court docket number (if assigned), centered at the top; the name of the appellate court; the style of the case (ARAP 12(a)); the nature of the proceeding and the court, agency, or board below; the title of the brief identifying the party; counsel's name, office address, telephone, and e-mail. | ARAP 28(d)(1)-(6) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-COV-002 | "Oral Argument Requested" appears on the cover when oral argument is requested. | ARAP 28(a)(1), 28(d)(7) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-COV-003 | Where content falls within Rule 52 or Rule 56(b), the cover carries "Rule 52" or "Rule 56" in capitalized bold lettering, at least one inch high, at the top center. | ARAP 28(d)(8); ARAP 32(a)(2)(F) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-COV-004 | The cover color is blue for the appellant, red for the appellee, green for an intervenor or amicus, and gray for a reply. Where the cover is not printed in color, it is white. | ARAP 28(d) | A |
| ALACIVAPP-SEC-001 | Appellant's brief sections appear under headings in this order: statement regarding oral argument; table of contents; statement of jurisdiction (not required in criminal cases); table of authorities; statement of the case (for civil cases, identifying the adverse rulings with record references); statement of the issues; statement of the facts; statement of the standard of review; summary of the argument; argument; conclusion stating the precise relief; certificate of compliance; certificate of service. | ARAP 28(a)(1)-(13) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-SEC-002 | The summary of the argument seldom exceeds two pages and never exceeds five. | ARAP 28(a)(9) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-SEC-003 | Appellee's brief conforms to ARAP 28(a)(1)-(12). The jurisdiction, case, issues, facts, and standard of review need not appear unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement. | ARAP 28(b) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-SEC-004 | A reply brief contains a table of contents, a table of authorities, a summary of the argument, an argument, and a certificate of service. | ARAP 28(c) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-CERT-001 | A certificate of compliance states the number of words in the document and the type and size of the font used. | ARAP 32(d), 28(a)(12) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-CERT-002 | A certificate of service lists the names and addresses of all attorneys or pro se parties on whom the brief was served. | ARAP 28(a)(13), 31(b) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-CITE-001 | Citations follow The Bluebook, the ALWD Citation Manual, or the style of Supreme Court of Alabama opinions, and reference the specific page numbers that support each proposition. | ARAP 28(a)(10) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-REC-001 | References to the clerk's record are preceded by "C."; references to the reporter's transcript are preceded by "R." | ARAP 28(g) | M |
| ALACIVAPP-STY-001 | Counsel minimize references to the parties as "appellant" and "appellee," preferring the trial-court designations or the parties' names. | ARAP 28(f) | A |
