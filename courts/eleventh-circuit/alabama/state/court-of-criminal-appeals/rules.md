# Alabama Court of Criminal Appeals: briefs, petitions for writ, amicus briefs

Alias `alacrimapp`. Scope: amicus curiae briefs, answering briefs (appellee's brief) and petitions for an extraordinary writ filed in the Court of Criminal Appeals. Verified against Alabama Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 21 (amended eff. Oct. 1, 2025, as shown on the rule text), 28 (amended eff. Oct. 1, 2024), 29 (amended eff. May 7, 2015) and 32 (amended eff. July 9, 2021), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the Court of Criminal Appeals' administrative e-filing procedures and any internal operating procedures are not on the Alabama court's website (https://judicial.alabama.gov/, refused connections from this box, HTTP 000 on 2026-10-21, twice); no Wayback copy of those procedures was located. Rows that depend on them are absent, not guessed. The rules were read from Wayback copies because the court's own site refused the connection.

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALACRIMAPP-AMI-001 | An amicus brief may be filed only by leave of court granted on motion, or at the request of the court; the motion identifies the amicus's interest and why the brief is desirable. | ARAP 29(a), (b) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-AMI-002 | The brief follows the form prescribed for the brief of an appellee: the sections of ARAP 28(a)(1)-(12) apply, with the jurisdiction, case, issues, facts and standard of review omitted unless the amicus is dissatisfied with the parties' statements. | ARAP 29(c); ARAP 28(b) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-AMI-003 | The amicus brief is filed within the time allowed the party whose position it supports, unless the court orders otherwise. | ARAP 29(d) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-AMI-004 | An amicus curiae may not file a reply brief except by the court's permission. | ARAP 29(e) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-AMI-005 | An amicus curiae may participate in oral argument only upon motion and leave of court. | ARAP 29(f); ARAP 28(d)(7) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-COV-004 | The cover of an amicus curiae's brief is green. | ARAP 28(d) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-AMI-006 | No separate word limit for an amicus brief appears in Rule 29; the appellee's form (ARAP 28(j)(1), 14,000 words for a principal brief) is the nearest limit, and the certificate of compliance reports the count. | ARAP 29(c); ARAP 28(j)(1) | A |

## Answering briefs (appellee's brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALACRIMAPP-FORM-001 | All documents are on 8½ × 11 inch paper. | ARAP 32(a)(6) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-FORM-002 | Text is double-spaced. Quotations of more than 2 but not more than 25 lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings, footnotes, and quotations from statutes, evidentiary materials, and other record matter may be single-spaced. | ARAP 32(a)(6) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-FORM-003 | Margins are at least one inch on all four sides. Page numbers may be placed in the margins; no other text may appear there. | ARAP 32(a)(6) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-FORM-004 | The font of all text, including footnotes, is Century Schoolbook 14 point. The type style is plain roman; italics or boldface may be used for emphasis; case names are italicized or underlined. The margins of headings, sentences, and paragraphs in text and footnotes are fully justified. Leading, kerning, or tracking adjustments to evade limits are prohibited. | ARAP 32(a)(7) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-FORM-005 | Century Schoolbook 14 is the rule's face. A filer who lacks it must certify the closest approximation (ARAP 32(a)(7)); the template uses the Century Schoolbook clone and needs no such certificate. | ARAP 32(a)(7) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-LEN-001 | A principal brief, including an appellee's brief, may not exceed 14,000 words (70 pages pro se), counting from the statement of the case through the conclusion, including headings, footnotes and quotations; the cover, oral-argument statement, table of contents, table of authorities, certificates, signature block and appendices are excluded. | ARAP 28(j)(1); ARAP 32(c) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-LEN-002 | Pages are numbered in Arabic numerals, with pagination beginning on the cover; the cover shows no page number. | ARAP 28(j)(1) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-COV-001 | The cover shows, in order: the appellate court docket number (if assigned), centered at the top; the name of the appellate court; the style of the case (ARAP 12(a)); the nature of the proceeding and the court, agency, or board below; the title of the brief identifying the party; counsel's name, office address, telephone number and e-mail address. | ARAP 28(d)(1)-(6) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-COV-002 | "Oral Argument Requested" appears on the cover when oral argument is requested. | ARAP 28(a)(1), 28(d)(7) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-COV-003 | Where content falls within Rule 52 or Rule 56(b), the cover carries "Rule 52" or "Rule 56" in capitalized bold lettering, at least one inch high, at the top center. | ARAP 28(d)(8); ARAP 32(a)(2)(F) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-COV-005 | The cover of an appellee's brief is red. | ARAP 28(d) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-SEC-001 | The appellee's brief conforms to ARAP 28(a)(1)-(12): statement regarding oral argument; table of contents; table of authorities (no statement of jurisdiction in a criminal case); statement of the case; statement of the issues; statement of the facts; standard of review; summary of the argument; argument; conclusion stating the precise relief; certificate of compliance; certificate of service. The jurisdiction, case, issues, facts and standard of review need not appear unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement. | ARAP 28(b); ARAP 28(a)(1)-(13) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-SEC-002 | The summary of the argument seldom exceeds two pages and never exceeds five. | ARAP 28(a)(9) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-SEC-003 | Where the appellee cross-appeals, the brief also contains the issues and argument of the cross-appeal, as well as the answer to the appellant. | ARAP 28(i) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-CERT-001 | A certificate of compliance states the number of words in the document and the type and size of the font used. | ARAP 32(d), 28(a)(12) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-CERT-002 | A certificate of service lists the names and addresses of all attorneys or pro se parties on whom the brief was served. | ARAP 28(a)(13), 31(b) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-CITE-001 | Citations follow The Bluebook, the ALWD Citation Manual, or the style of Supreme Court of Alabama opinions, and reference the specific page numbers that support each proposition. | ARAP 28(a)(10) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-REC-001 | References to the clerk's record are preceded by "C."; references to the reporter's transcript are preceded by "R." | ARAP 28(g) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-STY-001 | Counsel minimize references to the parties as "appellant" and "appellee," preferring the trial-court designations or the parties' names. | ARAP 28(f) | A |

## Petitions for an extraordinary writ

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALACRIMAPP-PET-001 | A petition for a writ of mandamus or prohibition directed to a judge is filed with the clerk, with a certificate of service on the respondent judge and on all parties to the action in the trial court. | ARAP 21(a)(1) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-PET-002 | Other extraordinary writs are sought by petition filed with the clerk, with proof of service on the parties named as respondents. | ARAP 21(c) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-PET-003 | The petition contains, under appropriate headings and in this order: statement of jurisdiction; table of authorities; statement of the case (with the adverse ruling and its appendix page references); statement of facts; statement of issues and relief sought; statement why the writ should issue, with authorities; and an appendix. | ARAP 21(a)(1)(A)-(G) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-PET-004 | The appendix includes copies of all parts of the record essential to the petition (the order sought to be reviewed, the court filings directly connected to it, and any transcripts), has an index listing each document, and separates the petition from the appendix and each document from the next by a divider or tab. | ARAP 21(a)(1)(G) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-PET-005 | The docket fee is not payable in the Court of Criminal Appeals. | ARAP 21(a)(2), 21(c) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-PET-006 | The petition, and any answer, conforms to the form of ARAP 32(a) and may not exceed 6,000 words (30 pages pro se). | ARAP 21(d); ARAP 32(b)(3) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-PET-007 | A petition for an extraordinary writ is filed within a reasonable time; the presumptively reasonable time for review of a trial court or lower appellate order is the time for taking an appeal, and a later petition states good cause. | ARAP 21(a)(3) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-COV-006 | The cover of a petition for an extraordinary writ is white. It shows the docket number (if available), the name of the appellate court, the style of the case, the nature of the proceeding and the court or agency below, and the title of the petition identifying the petitioner. | ARAP 32(a)(2)(A)-(E), 32(b)(3) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-COV-007 | The petition cover shows counsel's name, office address, telephone number and e-mail address; "Oral Argument Requested" if oral argument is requested; and "Rule 52" or "Rule 56" in capitalized bold lettering, at least one inch high, at the top center where Rule 52 or Rule 56(b) applies. | ARAP 32(a)(3)(A)-(C) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-CERT-003 | A petition using a word limit includes a certificate of compliance by counsel stating the number of words and the type and size of the font. | ARAP 32(d) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-SEC-004 | The petition's statement of the case identifies the adverse ruling as to which review is sought, with a reference to the appendix page at which it is found. | ARAP 21(a)(1)(C) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-FORM-006 | The petition meets ARAP 32(a)(1)-(7): 8½ × 11 inch paper, double-spaced text with the single-spacing exceptions of ARAP 32(a)(6), margins of at least one inch on all four sides, Century Schoolbook 14 point for all text including footnotes, and fully justified margins. | ARAP 32(a)(1), (6), (7) | M |
| ALACRIMAPP-CITE-002 | Citations follow The Bluebook, the ALWD Citation Manual, or the style of Supreme Court of Alabama opinions, with page references. | ARAP 21(a)(1)(F); ARAP 28(a)(10) | M |
