# Supreme Court of Alabama: briefs, motions, petitions for certiorari and petitions for extraordinary writs

Alias `ala`. Scope: the petitioner's brief on the merits after a writ of certiorari issues, the appellee's (respondent's) answering brief, reply briefs, amicus curiae briefs, motions and supporting memoranda (Rule 27), petitions for a writ of certiorari to review a Court of Criminal Appeals or Court of Civil Appeals decision (Rule 39), and petitions for an extraordinary writ filed in the Supreme Court (Rule 21). Verified against the Alabama Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 21, 27, 28, 29, 32, 39, 52 and 56, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the Supreme Court's administrative e-filing procedures and clerk's office standards are not on the Alabama court's website (https://judicial.alabama.gov/, refused connections from this box, HTTP 000 on 2026-10-21, twice); no Wayback copy of those procedures was located. Rows that depend on them are absent, not guessed. The rule texts were read from Wayback copies because the court's own site refused the connection.

## Petitions for certiorari (Rule 39)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALA-PET-001 | A petition for a writ of certiorari to review a Court of Criminal Appeals decision is filed with the clerk of the Supreme Court within 14 days of the decision of that court on the application for rehearing; an application for rehearing in the Court of Criminal Appeals is a prerequisite, except as Rule 39(c)(1) provides. | ARAP 39(c)(1)-(2) | M |
| ALA-PET-002 | A petition for a writ of certiorari to review a Court of Civil Appeals decision is governed by ARAP 39(b); its statement of facts follows ARAP 39(d)(5). | ARAP 39(b), (d)(5) | M |
| ALA-PET-003 | No brief is filed by the petitioner or the respondent before the writ issues unless the Court orders it. | ARAP 39(c)(3) | M |
| ALA-PET-004 | The petition contains: the style of the case, the name of the petitioner, the circuit court from which the cause is on appeal, and the name of the court of appeals to which the petition is directed; the date of the decision sought to be reviewed and, if rehearing was sought, the date of the order overruling it; a concise statement of the grounds under ARAP 39(a)(1)(A)-(E); and a direct and concise argument amplifying the grounds. | ARAP 39(d)(1), (2), (3), (6) | M |
| ALA-PET-005 | Where the grounds are ARAP 39(a)(1)(D) (conflict), the petition quotes the part of the opinion and of the prior decision that conflict, or states with particularity how they conflict where quotation is not feasible. | ARAP 39(d)(3)(A)-(B) | M |
| ALA-PET-006 | A copy of the opinion or unpublished memorandum of the court of appeals, and the order or notice on any application for rehearing, is attached to the petition as an exhibit. | ARAP 39(d)(4) | M |
| ALA-PET-007 | A statement of facts is either included or attached, and where the petitioner is not satisfied with the facts in the court of appeals' opinion, the petition says so and gives the record references; a verbatim copy of any rehearing statement of facts is included. | ARAP 39(d)(5) | M |
| ALA-PET-008 | The petition conforms to ARAP 32(a) and does not exceed 3,000 words (15 pages pro se), except in capital cases. Its cover is white. | ARAP 39(d); ARAP 32(b)(2) | M |
| ALA-PET-009 | Oral argument on a petition is not held on preliminary examination; a request for it is made in the briefs after the writ issues, with reasons. | ARAP 39(h) | A |
