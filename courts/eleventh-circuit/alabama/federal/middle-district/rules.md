# Middle District of Alabama: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `almd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules for the United States District Court, Middle District of Alabama (PDF as posted; header reads "Effective December 1, 2010, amended October 1, 2011") and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Note: the local rules state no typeface, point size, or brief page limit. The repo default eval (1 in margins, 12 pt) applies, and no page limit is set.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALMD-FORM-001 | Papers are on 8½ × 11 inch white paper, flat and unfolded, bound only by easily removable clips, not stapled or mechanically bound, plainly typewritten or printed, double-spaced except quoted material, and each page numbered consecutively. | LR 5.6 | M |
| ALMD-FORM-002 | No typeface, point size, or margin rule appears in the local rules. Margins are not stated; use the one-inch default. | LR 5.6 (silent) | A |
| ALMD-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| ALMD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| ALMD-CERT-001 | Each attorney who signs a paper places the typewritten name, address, and telephone of the attorney on it. | LR 11.1; FRCP 11(a) | M |
| ALMD-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing follows the court's General Order on electronic case filing and the CM/ECF procedures. | LR 5.5 | M |
| ALMD-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALMD-FORM-003 | Motion briefs follow the same paper and spacing rule as complaints (LR 5.6, ALMD-FORM-001). | LR 5.6 | M |
| ALMD-LEN-001 | No brief page limit appears in the local rules. A limit applies only if a judge sets one. | LR (silent) | A |
| ALMD-CONT-003 | Letter-form briefs and motions are not accepted; file under the Federal Rules and the local rules. | LR 5.7 | M |
| ALMD-CONT-004 | Page substitutions in complaints or other papers are not accepted. | LR 5.8 | M |
