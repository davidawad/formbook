Example Credit Union (the “Movant”), a creditor of the Debtor, moves under 11 U.S.C. § 362(d)(1) and Fed. R. Bankr. P. 4001(a) and 9014 for relief from the automatic stay as to the vehicle described below, and in support states as follows:

# JURISDICTION

1\. This Court has jurisdiction over this contested matter under 28 U.S.C. §§ 157 and 1334. This is a core proceeding under 28 U.S.C. § 157(b)(2)(G).

# BACKGROUND

2\. The Debtor filed a petition under chapter 13 of the Bankruptcy Code on January 5, 2026. The Movant holds a first-priority lien on the Debtor’s 2021 sedan, perfected by notation on its certificate of title.

3\. The Debtor’s confirmed plan provides for the Movant to be paid directly. No direct payment has been received for the months of December 2025 through February 2026, and the Debtor’s insurance on the vehicle has lapsed.

# ARGUMENT

## I. Cause exists to lift the stay

Section 362(d)(1) requires the Court to grant relief from the stay “for cause, including the lack of adequate protection of an interest in property.” A debtor’s failure to make the payments a plan requires, and to keep collateral insured, is cause. The Movant’s interest is not adequately protected while the vehicle depreciates, uninsured, without payment.

## II. The fourteen-day stay of the order should be waived

Rule 4001(a)(4) stays an order granting relief for fourteen days “unless the court orders otherwise.” Because the vehicle is uninsured, any delay exposes the Movant to a loss it cannot recover.

WHEREFORE, the Movant asks the Court to enter an order granting relief from the automatic stay as to the vehicle, waiving the stay of Rule 4001(a)(4), and granting such other relief as is just.
