# Bankruptcy Court, Southern District of Alabama: adversary complaints and motions

Alias `alsb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints (`adversary-complaint`) and motions with their briefs (`motion-brief`). Verified against the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (Dec. 1, 2024 edition as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Local rules: https://www.alsb.uscourts.gov/sites/alsb/files/ALSB%20Local%20Rules%207-1-2026.pdf (effective July 1, 2026; a 16-page scan without a text layer) was read on 2026-10-29 by OCR (tesseract, pages rendered at 300 dpi); its table of contents and the text of LBR 5005, 9007-1 and 9029-3 were read in the OCR output, and the quotations here are from it. No rule in the table of contents concerns the form of papers (paper, type, margins, spacing, length); LBR 9029-3(d) rescinds every earlier local rule. The rows tagged LBR below come from it; the adversary-complaint rows cover the national rules only.

## Motion brief

Scope: a motion, or a brief in support of one (`motion-brief`). ALSB-FORM-001, ALSB-CONT-001, ALSB-ELEC-001 and ALSB-CERT-001 above apply to it unchanged.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALSB-MOT-001 | A request for an order is made by written motion that states its grounds with particularity and sets out the relief or order requested. | FRBP 9013; FRBP 7007 (FRCP 7(b)(1)) | M |
| ALSB-MOT-002 | A motion filed on negative notice carries "a negative notice legend prominently displayed on the face of the first page of the paper", in a form substantially as the rule gives it, and is filed with proof of service. | LBR 9007-1(b)(1)-(2) | M |
| ALSB-MOT-003 | The objection period in the legend is 21 days, 14 days for motions to approve agreements under FRBP 4001(d) (stay relief, use of cash collateral, adequate protection, credit), 30 days for objections to claims under FRBP 3007. No motion seeking relief against the United States may be filed on negative notice. | LBR 9007-1(b)(3), (f) | A |
| ALSB-MOT-004 | The signer's address, e-mail address and telephone number appear with the signature. | FRBP 9011(a) | M |
| ALSB-ELEC-002 | Papers sent to the court by facsimile or e-mail are not accepted for filing. | LBR 5005 | M |

The local rules set no page limit, typeface, type size, margin or line spacing; the template's letter page, one-inch margins, 12-point Times New Roman and double spacing are the convention of the three filed exemplars (motion-brief/usage/SOURCE.md).
