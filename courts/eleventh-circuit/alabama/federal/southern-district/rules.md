# Southern District of Alabama: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `alsd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules and Standing Orders of the United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama (PDF "local-rules.pdf" as posted; header reads "Effective August 1, 2015") and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALSD-FORM-001 | Papers are formatted for 8½ × 11 inch paper. Text is double-spaced, except that quotations more than two lines long may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | General L.R. 5(a)(1) | M |
| ALSD-FORM-002 | Margins are at least 1 inch on all four sides. Page numbers may be in the margins; no text or footnotes may be there. | General L.R. 5(a)(1) | M |
| ALSD-FORM-003 | Typeface is a plain roman style (or similar). Italics or boldface may be used for emphasis; case names are italicized or underlined. Font is 12 point or larger, including footnotes. | General L.R. 5(a)(2) | M |
| ALSD-CERT-001 | For represented parties, at least one attorney of record signs each document, and the typed or printed name, address, and telephone number of all attorneys of record for that party appear beneath the signature line. | General L.R. 5(a)(3) | M |
| ALSD-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| ALSD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| ALSD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALSD-FORM-004 | Motion briefs follow General L.R. 5(a)(1)-(3) (ALSD-FORM-001 to -003, ALSD-CERT-001). | General L.R. 5(a) | M |
| ALSD-LEN-001 | Principal briefs in support of, or opposition to, any motion do not exceed 30 pages; reply briefs do not exceed 15 pages. The limits exclude the caption, cover page, table of contents, table of authorities, and signature block. A brief over the limit needs prior leave of court. | Civil L.R. 7(e) | M |
| ALSD-LEN-002 | A post-briefing notice (such as a notice of supplemental authority) may not exceed two pages and may not present a new argument. | Civil L.R. 7 (post-briefing notice provision) | A |
