# Bankruptcy Court, Northern District of Alabama: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `alnb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Alabama (PDF "Local Rules Mar 1 2023", effective March 1, 2023) and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (Dec. 1, 2024 edition as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Note: the local rules state no typeface, point size, margin, or page limit for complaints or motions that was located. FRBP 9004 governs legibility and caption.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALNB-FORM-001 | Papers are clearly legible; commonly used English abbreviations are acceptable. | FRBP 9004(a) | M |
| ALNB-CONT-001 | Caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document's character. | FRBP 9004(b)(1)-(4) | M |
| ALNB-ELEC-001 | Filing is electronic under LBR 5005-4, and counsel follows the Administrative Procedures for Filing, Signing, Retaining and Verification of Pleadings and Papers in the CM/ECF system. | LBR 5005-4 | M |
| ALNB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are redacted under FRBP 9037. | FRBP 9037 | M |
| ALNB-CERT-001 | Counsel's signature and identification follow FRBP 9011. | FRBP 9011 | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALNB-FORM-002 | Motions carry the FRBP 9004(b) caption (ALNB-CONT-001). No separate typeface, margin, or spacing rule was located in the local rules. | FRBP 9004(b); LBR (silent) | M |
