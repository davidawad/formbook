# Bankruptcy Court, Middle District of Alabama: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `almb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Middle District of Alabama (PDF "ALMB Local Rules, April 1, 2024") and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (Dec. 1, 2024 edition as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Note: the local rules state no typeface, point size, margin, or page limit for motions or complaints that was located. FRBP 9004 requires legibility and a caption, and nothing more on form.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALMB-FORM-001 | Papers are clearly legible; commonly used English abbreviations are acceptable. | FRBP 9004(a) | M |
| ALMB-CONT-001 | Caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document's character. | FRBP 9004(b)(1)-(4) | M |
| ALMB-ELEC-001 | Filing is electronic under LBR 5005-4 (electronic filing). | LBR 5005-4 | M |
| ALMB-ELEC-002 | Responsibility for redacting personal identifiers rests with counsel and the parties. | LBR 9037-1; FRBP 9037 | M |
| ALMB-CERT-001 | Counsel's signature and identification follow FRBP 9011. | FRBP 9011 | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|

| ALMB-MOT-001 | Motion practice follows LBR 9013-1; a certificate of service complying with LBR 9013-3 reflects service of the paper. | LBR 9013-1; LBR 9013-3 | M |
| ALMB-MOT-002 | A notice of hearing or negative-notice legend, where the local rule requires one, appears on the first page in all capital letters, bolded, and centered under the case style. | LBR 9007-1 | C |
