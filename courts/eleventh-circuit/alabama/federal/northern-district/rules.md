# Northern District of Alabama: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `alnd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama (effective December 4, 2019, "Revised 12-04-2019" PDF) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Note: the local rules state no typeface, point size, margin, or brief page limit. The repo default eval (1 in margins, 12 pt) applies, and no page limit is set. The local rules list no general order on paper format that was retrieved; check the court's general orders before finalizing a template.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALND-FORM-001 | No paper-form rule (paper size, spacing, typeface, margins) appears in the local rules. The pleading form is set by FRCP 10. | LR (silent); FRCP 10 | A |
| ALND-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| ALND-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| ALND-CONT-003 | A completed civil cover sheet (Form JS-44c) is submitted with the initial complaint or notice of removal. | LR 3.1 | M |
| ALND-ELEC-001 | Documents may be filed electronically to the extent allowed by the court's General Order on electronic case filing, and counsel follows the CM/ECF administrative procedures. | LR 5.5 | M |
| ALND-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |
| ALND-CERT-001 | Every paper is signed as FRCP 11(a) requires; attorney admission and practice are governed by LR 83.1. | FRCP 11(a); LR 83.1 | M |
