# Court of Appeals of Georgia: briefs, applications, amicus briefs, and motions

Alias `gactapp`. Scope: briefs (appellant, appellee, reply), amicus curiae briefs, discretionary and interlocutory applications, motions and motions for reconsideration filed in the Court of Appeals of Georgia. Verified against the Court of Appeals of Georgia Rules, effective March 26, 2026, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.gaappeals.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/COA-Rules-Complete-Eff-26-March-26.pdf returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21 (twice, the second time after a wait). The text below is the Internet Archive capture of that same court-posted PDF (its title page reads "Effective March 26, 2026"). Confirm against the court-posted PDF before relying on a number. Filing is by the court's eFast system (Rule 46); the eFast instructions at http://www.gaappeals.us/eFile2/ were not retrieved.

Rule 2 (c) (3) names Times New Roman as a compliant face, as an example and not as a requirement. Rules 25 and 41 set no cover contents beyond the signature information of Rule 2 (a); cover layout is convention (C).

## Signature, service, and word-count certificate (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GACTAPP-SIG-001 | Each document is signed by an attorney of record (or pro se party) and shows mailing address, telephone number, e-mail address (if any), and the State Bar of Georgia membership number of every submitting attorney. Corporate filers sign through an attorney authorized to practice before the Court. | RULES Rule 2 (a), 2 (d). | M |
| GACTAPP-SIG-002 | Electronic signature: typed name preceded by "/s/" and underlined, with the typed name again below the underline. Multiple signers: the filing attorney's login and conformed signatures of the others. | RULES Rule 2 (a) (2) (ii). | M |
| GACTAPP-SVC-001 | Certificate of service on opposing counsel, showing the method (U.S. Postal Service, personal service, or electronic service). Service is contemporaneous with or before filing; eFast filing alone is not service. A document without a compliant certificate is not accepted. | RULES Rule 2 (a), 6 (b) (2), 6 (c), 6 (d). | M |
| GACTAPP-SVC-002 | E-mail service requires the filer's certification of a prior agreement to accept PDF service by e-mail, and the certificate lists each e-served recipient by full name, e-mail address, telephone number, and mailing address. | RULES Rule 6 (b) (2). | M |
| GACTAPP-CERT-001 | Each submission carries, just above the signature block of the submitting attorney, the sentence: "This submission does not exceed the word count limit imposed by Rule 24." | RULES Rule 24 (f) (1); Rule 37 (a). | M |
| GACTAPP-CERT-002 | Tables of contents, tables of citations, cover sheets, certificates of service, and the word-count certificate do not count toward the word limit. | RULES Rule 24 (f) (3). | M |

## Briefs (appellant, appellee, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GACTAPP-LEN-001 | Briefs and responsive briefs are limited to 8,400 words in civil cases and 14,000 words in criminal cases, except on written motion approved by the Court. | RULES Rule 24 (f) (1). | M |
| GACTAPP-LEN-002 | An appellant's reply brief is limited to 4,200 words (single reply per appellant). | RULES Rule 24 (a), (f) (1); Rule 25 (c). | M |
| GACTAPP-CONT-001 | An appellant's brief includes, at a minimum, (3) a jurisdictional statement (basis for the Court's jurisdiction, basis for the Court of Appeals rather than the Supreme Court, and filing dates showing timeliness), (4) the enumeration of errors, (5) the statement of the case with preservation of each error, and (7) the argument with the standard of review. Items (1), (2), and (6) (contents, introduction, summary of argument) are for briefs of significant length or complexity; the template always renders the contents. | RULES Rule 25 (a) (1)-(7). | M |
| GACTAPP-CONT-002 | Table of contents and table of cited authorities, each with page references, for briefs of significant length or complexity. | RULES Rule 25 (a) (1). | M |
| GACTAPP-CONT-003 | Appellee's brief follows the Rule 25 (a) arrangement except item (4); items (3) and (5) may be omitted if the appellee accepts the appellant's presentation. A disagreement with the statement of the case is identified with record citations. | RULES Rule 25 (b). | C |
| GACTAPP-CONT-004 | Cases are cited by name with volume, page, and year of the Official Report; unreported cases by court and case number and date of decision. | RULES Rule 24 (d). | M |
| GACTAPP-CONT-005 | Each enumerated error is supported by a specific record or transcript reference. Electronic record citations give volume and PDF page (for example, V2-46). | RULES Rule 25 (d) (1)-(2). | M |
| GACTAPP-CONT-006 | A brief with a criminal defendant states the defendant's address on the face of the brief. | RULES Rule 24 (h). | M |
| GACTAPP-CONT-007 | No letter briefs; communications on recent authority are made by supplemental brief on leave. | RULES Rule 27 (a), (b). | M |

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GACTAPP-AMC-001 | An amicus brief supporting a party is filed without leave within 10 days after that party's initial brief or petition is due; a brief supporting neither party, within 10 days after the response brief is due. | RULES Rule 26 (a) (1). | M |
| GACTAPP-AMC-002 | For interlocutory and discretionary applications, an amicus brief is filed without leave within five days after the applicant's or respondent's application or response is due. | RULES Rule 26 (a) (2). | M |
| GACTAPP-AMC-003 | The amicus brief discloses the identity of each person on whose behalf it is filed. | RULES Rule 26 (c). | M |
| GACTAPP-AMC-004 | Rule 26 sets no separate format or length. The template applies the Rule 24 format, the 8,400-word limit, and the Rule 24 (f) (1) certificate, which the court accepts for amicus briefs that conform to Rule 26 (Rule 24 (a)). Reading: conservative. | RULES Rule 24 (a), (f) (1); Rule 26. | C |

## Applications for interlocutory and discretionary appeal

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GACTAPP-APP-001 | Interlocutory application (Rule 30): filed within 10 days of the entry of the trial court's order granting the certificate of immediate review. Discretionary application (Rule 31): generally within 30 days of entry of the order (seven days in a dispossessory action under OCGA 44-7-56). Entry is the filing date with the trial court clerk. | RULES Rule 30 (a); Rule 31 (a). | M |
| GACTAPP-APP-002 | The application contains a jurisdictional statement (interlocutory) and attaches a stamped "filed" copy of the trial court's order and, for interlocutory applications, of the certificate of immediate review, each bearing the judge's signature. Conformed or stamped signatures are not accepted unless the judge's official practice is electronic signature. | RULES Rule 30 (c); Rule 31 (c). | M |
| GACTAPP-APP-003 | The application attaches a copy of any petition or motion that led directly to the order, and any responses. | RULES Rule 31 (e). | M |
| GACTAPP-APP-004 | An attorney relying on Rule 31 (b) (3) or (4) includes the certificate of good faith quoted in Rule 31 (b), signed and dated. | RULES Rule 31 (b). | M |
| GACTAPP-APP-005 | Application brief follows Rule 24 (format and the 8,400-word limit with the Rule 24 (f) (1) certificate) and the general format of Rule 2 (c). | RULES Rule 30 (g) (1) (i) (interlocutory); Rule 31 (g) (1) (i) (discretionary). | M |
| GACTAPP-APP-006 | Exhibits are uploaded as separate, independent exhibits without cover pages, titled to match the application index; the application index is uploaded immediately after the brief. Exhibits total no more than 100 pages unless a good-faith certificate is added. | RULES Rule 31 (g) (1) (ii)-(vi). | M |
| GACTAPP-APP-007 | Paper applications are limited to 30 pages in civil cases (exclusive of exhibits and parts of the record), bound at the top, with supporting record material indexed and tabbed. | RULES Rule 31 (g) (2). | M |
| GACTAPP-APP-008 | The application must contain sufficient material to apprise the Court of the issues, in context, and to support the arguments. | RULES Rule 31 (f). | A |

## Motions and motions for reconsideration

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GACTAPP-MOT-001 | A motion is a separate document, not a joint, compound, or alternative motion, and is not placed in a brief or application. | RULES Rule 41 (a), (b). | M |
| GACTAPP-MOT-002 | A motion is prepared under Rule 24, limited to 4,200 words when efiled, and carries the Rule 24 (f) (1) certificate. Parties may cite the record but may not attach documents, except the exceptions in Rule 41 (b). | RULES Rule 41 (b); Rule 24 (f) (1). | M |
| GACTAPP-MOT-003 | A motion for reconsideration is prepared under Rule 24, limited to 4,200 words, with the Rule 24 (f) (1) certificate. | RULES Rule 37 (a). | M |
| GACTAPP-MOT-004 | A motion for reconsideration is filed within 10 days from the rendition of the judgment or dismissal, received by 4:30 p.m. (later receipt is the next business day). | RULES Rule 37 (b). | M |
| GACTAPP-MOT-005 | An emergency motion (Rule 40 (b)) explains why an order is necessary and why it is time-sensitive; attaches stamped "filed" copies of the order appealed and of the notice of appeal; shows contemporaneous service; and includes the filing fee or a Rule 5 fee-waiver basis. | RULES Rule 40 (b) (1)-(5). | M |
