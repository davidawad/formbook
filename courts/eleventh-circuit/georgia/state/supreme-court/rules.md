# Georgia Supreme Court: briefs, petitions, motions and amicus briefs

Alias `ga`. Scope: filings in the Supreme Court of Georgia: briefs on appeal (opening, answering, reply), briefs on the merits after certiorari, petitions for certiorari and discretionary appeal, original petitions for writs, motions, and amicus briefs. Verified against *Rules of the Supreme Court of Georgia (Effective July 2, 2026)*, read from the PDF on 2026-10-21.

## Length limits

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GA-SC-LIMIT-001 | Principal briefs (appellant's and appellee's initial briefs) in civil and criminal cases: 10,000 words, or 30 pages if mechanically typed or handwritten. Death-penalty principal briefs: 20,000 words or 80 pages. | SCt R 20(1), 20(4) | M |
| GA-SC-LIMIT-002 | Reply briefs: 7,000 words or 20 pages (death-penalty reply briefs: 10,000 words or 40 pages). Petitions for certiorari, applications for appeal, habeas appeals, motions, responses and replies to those filings: 7,000 words or 20 pages. Amicus curiae briefs: 7,000 words. Motions for reconsideration: 5,000 words or 10 pages. | SCt R 20(2), 20(3), 20(5), 27(1) | M |
| GA-SC-LIMIT-003 | Cover page, table of contents, table of citations, appendices, certificate of service, signature block and the word-count statement do not count toward the limit. | SCt R 20(6) | M |
| GA-SC-CERT-001 | Each submission carries this statement immediately above the signing attorney's signature block: "This submission does not exceed the word-count limit imposed by Rule 20." | SCt R 20(7) | M |
| GA-SC-LIMIT-004 | A word or page extension motion is at most 1,500 words or three pages; any order granting it is appended to the filing. A document over the limit may be struck without leave to refile. | SCt R 20(8), 20(9) | M |

## Content and citation (briefs)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GA-SC-ORDER-001 | Appellant's principal brief contains, in order: a table of contents and table of citations for significant length; a concise introduction; a jurisdictional statement (basis for this Court's jurisdiction, why the Supreme Court rather than the Court of Appeals, and filing dates showing timeliness); an enumeration of errors; a statement of the case with record citations; a summary of argument for significant length; and the argument with the standard of review. Items (c), (d), (e) and (g) are the minimum. | SCt R 19(1) | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-002 | Appellee's brief follows Rule 19(1) except item (d); items (c) and (e) may be omitted if the appellee accepts the appellant's presentation, and any disagreement with the statement of the case is identified with record cites. | SCt R 19(2) | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-003 | Reply brief carries item (g); items (a) and (b) are encouraged for significant length. A reply may not expand the enumeration of errors. | SCt R 19(3) | M |
| GA-SC-CITE-001 | Briefs cite Georgia cases to the official reporter (case name, volume and page). Unreported cases cite the court, case name, number and date of decision. Record cites read Vol.-Page (electronic record: Vol.-PDF page), for example V1-77. | SCt R 22(2) | M |
| GA-SC-CITE-002 | An enumerated error or subpart not supported by argument, authority and record citations is deemed abandoned. | SCt R 22(1) | M |
| GA-SC-SERVE-001 | Before filing, service on opposing counsel is certified, with the names and addresses served and the manner of service (USPS, personal, or e-mail of the PDF). E-mail service needs the prior-agreement certification in substance. Filings not so certified are not accepted. In death, murder, aircraft hijacking and treason appeals, service also goes to the Attorney General and the District Attorney. | SCt R 14 | M |
| GA-SC-CIP-001 | Counsel for the appellant, applicant, petitioner or movant files a separate Certificate of Interested Persons with the first filing (including a first motion for extension), listing all parties ever named, all attorneys who appeared below, and witnesses called. | SCt R 26.1(1) | M |

## Granted certiorari (brief on the merits)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GA-SC-ORDER-004 | After certiorari is granted, the parties brief only the questions the Court posed in its grant order. The Rule 10 schedule runs from the date of the grant order. The enumeration of errors is replaced by the questions the Court listed. | SCt R 19(1)(d), 45 | M |

## Petitions for certiorari (review of a Court of Appeals decision)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GA-SC-TIME-002 | The petition for certiorari is filed within 20 days after entry of judgment, or after the disposition of a motion for reconsideration if one is filed, with the fee when Rule 5 requires it. Compliance is mandatory. | SCt R 38 | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-005 | The petition states, under a separate heading at the very beginning (before any table of contents or authorities), the question or questions presented, which must meet the Rule 40 standard. | SCt R 41(2) | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-006 | Argument explains why the questions meet the Rule 40 standard, not merely why the decision was wrong. A brief of facts and law is included in the petition, not filed separately. The petition follows Rules 14–22. | SCt R 41(1), 41(3) | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-007 | Attach a copy of the Court of Appeals decision and the order on any motion for reconsideration. | SCt R 39 | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-008 | A reply to the response is permitted within ten days after the response, limited to 7,000 words or 20 pages; no sur-replies. | SCt R 42.1 | M |

## Discretionary appeals and interlocutory applications

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GA-SC-ORDER-009 | An application for discretionary appeal (OCGA § 5-6-35) has a concise jurisdictional statement and an enumeration of errors under Rule 19(1)(c)–(d), and attaches a stamped copy of the trial court order showing the filing date. The application follows Rule 20(3). Affidavits and transcript excerpts are attached to show what the record will show. | SCt R 20(3), 33, 34 | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-010 | An application for interlocutory appeal has the same content and attachments, plus the stamped certificate of immediate review. | SCt R 30, 31 | M |

## Original petitions (writs)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GA-SC-ORDER-011 | An original petition for mandamus, prohibition, specific performance, quo warranto or injunction is granted only in extremely rare circumstances and is not used to circumvent the trial court. A petition for mandamus against a superior court judge is considered only after a stand-alone mandamus petition was first filed in the same superior court before another judge and proved ineffective. | SCt R 36(2) | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-012 | The petition (a) explains why an order of this Court is necessary and why jurisdiction lies here rather than in the Court of Appeals or another court; (b) includes sufficient material to apprise the Court of the issues in context; and (c) contains a certificate of service showing contemporaneous service on all opposing parties, with full names and mailing or e-mail addresses. Non-compliance may result in dismissal. | SCt R 36(2)(a)–(c) | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-013 | Responses are not required unless ordered. | SCt R 36(2) | M |

No word limit is stated for Rule 36(2) petitions. Rule 20(3) lists petitions for certiorari, applications, habeas appeals, motions and responses, not original petitions. The template counts none.

## Motions and amicus briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GA-SC-AMICUS-001 | An amicus brief in support of a party may be filed without leave within ten days after that party's initial brief, petition or application is due; in support of neither party, within ten days after the response or reply is due. Later briefs need leave, sought by motion attaching the brief as Exhibit 1. | SCt R 23(1)–(3) | M |
| GA-SC-AMICUS-002 | An amicus brief discloses the identity and interest of the person on whose behalf it is filed. The Court may strike a brief that could cause a justice's disqualification. | SCt R 23(4) | M |
| GA-SC-ORDER-014 | A motion for reconsideration (the Court's rehearing vehicle) is received within ten days after the ruling, attaches the ruling, and is limited to 5,000 words or 10 pages. A second motion needs leave. | SCt R 27(1)–(2) | M |
