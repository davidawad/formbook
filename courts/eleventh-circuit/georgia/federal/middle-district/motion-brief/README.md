# gamd: motion brief

Doc type: **motion-brief**: the brief (memorandum of law) supporting a Rule 12(b)(6) motion to dismiss, in the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia (`gamd`). The motion itself is a separate, short document in this court's practice (the exemplar's ECF entry 10 is the motion; entry 10-1 is the brief). This template produces the brief.
Folder: `courts/eleventh-circuit/georgia/federal/middle-district/motion-brief/`.

## Layout

Set by `typst/layout.yaml` (the generic FRCP brief, `lib/layouts/frcp-motion-brief.yaml`, with this court's overlay; each value cites its rule or the exemplar where the rules are silent, GAMD-FORM-001):

- Court name ("IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT", "FOR THE MIDDLE DISTRICT OF GEORGIA") and the division bold and centered.
- Caption: the parties in a 3.3 in column at the left (each party's names, its designation a line below, "v." a line from each), a ")" beside every line, "CIVIL ACTION FILE NO." (`case_label`) and the number a line apart at the right, centered on the parties' height; a jury demand when `jury_demand` is set. Title bold and centered.
- Body double-spaced at 27.6 pt, first lines indented half an inch, unnumbered. Footnotes 11 pt, single-spaced (the exemplar). Headings bold on the body pitch: level 1 centered, level 2 at the margin, level 3 bold italic; text as typed.
- Signature (FRCP 11(a), L.R. 83.1.2(B)): "Respectfully submitted," at 3 in, the firm, each signer's lines (the "/s/" signature, the signature line, name, Georgia Bar number); then the address at the margin beside whom they represent. Kept whole and with the last paragraph (the sample's date line).
- The sample's CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE section on a new page after the signature, signed again as the exemplar signs it (`tail`, `after_tail`).
- Page one unnumbered; the rest numbered at the bottom center.

## Markdown contract (for samples)

- Frontmatter: `division`, `case_label` (default "CIVIL ACTION FILE NO."), `case_number`, `caption.parties` (`names`, a list of lines; `designation`), `jury_demand` (optional), `title`, `signature` (`firm`, `signers`: a list of line lists, `address`, `attorney_for`). The court's name is the layout's (`constants`), no longer a frontmatter field.
- Body: markdown set by the shared document model (`#` to `###`, paragraphs, lists, quotations, footnotes, `*italics*`). A section headed `# CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE` is set after the signature and signed again; the old `<!--signature-->` markers are gone.

## Known gaps and open choices

1. **Only one exemplar.** EVAL-LOOP's tier-2 rule asks for 2 or 3 filers from different sources. Not collected for this doc type. The M.D. Ga. search (CourtListener RECAP, `court=gamd`, `type=r`, "motion to dismiss memorandum", filed 2015-2021, filtered to stored PDFs) also found Sims v. Evans (5:15-cv-285, brief in Calibri, 8 pages), Zavala v. Ward (5:19-cv-383, memorandum 4 pages), and Thrower v. Matrix Warranty (3:19-cv-66, motion 4 pages; memorandum not separately saved). **Tier 2 is therefore not judged against two filers.**
2. **Font.** The court names no face, so the template sets the house face, **Times New Roman at 12 pt** (`docs/FONTS.md` rule 6), which is also the exemplar's face and size (until 2026-10-17 the template set EB Garamond at 13 pt).
3. **M.D. Ga. typeface and margin rule.** None found in the consolidated Local Rules text (PDF dated 3-7-22, fetched and text-extracted 2026-10-14). The layout follows the exemplar, as the brief says. The current text was not confirmed beyond that version.
4. **Page limit (L.R. 7.4, 20 pages).** Checked on every render (`eval.yaml` `page_limit: 20`). The sample is 9 pages.
5. **Judge's standing orders** were not checked.
6. **The motion is not templated.** The exemplar's ECF entry 10 (the motion) is separate; a complete filing needs the motion as its own document.
7. **Italics.** The sample is roman; the exemplar's case-name italics are not in its text layer. The template supports `*...*`.
8. **Headings** are typed by the author; the layout does not number them.
9. **Filed content is kept.** The sample keeps the exemplar's text, citations and the "no set of facts" standard as filed. The template does not check authorities (L.R. 7.1 requires citations) or their accuracy.
10. **Lead counsel** (L.R. 83.1.2(A)) is absent here, because a brief is not the first pleading; the rule text read above supports that reading.
11. **Overlay pairing.** Our pages 8 and 9 pair with the filed pages 9 and 8 (the two signature blocks are split across them in the render and in the filing).
12. **Gates run:** `tools/check.sh` on typst, latex and docx, every gate green (2026-10-29).
13. **Exemplar PDF** in the folder: `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-motion-brief.pdf` (`usage/SOURCE.md`).
