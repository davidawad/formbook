# gamd: motion brief

Doc type: **motion-brief**: the brief (memorandum of law) supporting a Rule 12(b)(6) motion to dismiss, in the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia (`gamd`). The motion itself is a separate, short document in this court's practice (the exemplar's ECF entry 10 is the motion; entry 10-1 is the brief). This template produces the brief.

## Layout

- Letter size, 1 inch on every side (exemplar).
- Page 1 carries no page number; pages 2 onward are numbered at the bottom centre (exemplar).
- Court heading centred bold; caption with party names, a column of ")" marks, and at the right "CIVIL ACTION FILE NO." with the number (the exemplar's label).
- Title centred bold below the caption, natural line wrap (no forced breaks).
- Body: double-spaced at a 27.6 pt baseline pitch (the exemplar's 12-pt Times double spacing, measured on the exemplar with `pdftohtml -xml`; the render measures 27.6 pt), first-line indent 0.5 inch on every paragraph (including the first after a heading). Paragraphs are not numbered (briefs are not numbered pleadings).
- Headings are typed by the author (no case or numbering applied by the template): `#` centred bold; `##` bold, flush left (the exemplar's "I. Motion to Dismiss Standard.", and so on); `###` bold italic.
- Footnotes: markdown footnotes (`[^1]`), 11 pt, single-spaced, rendered at the foot of the page where they are referenced (verified on pages 2 and 3).
- Signature block: "Respectfully submitted," then the firm and signers indented 3 inches, then the address flush left and "Attorneys for ..." (exemplar).
- A line reading `[[signature]]` places a signature block at that point; the certificate of service repeats it. Without the marker, the signature closes the document.

## Markdown contract (for samples)

- YAML frontmatter, then the body. Frontmatter fields: `court` (list of lines), `division`, `case_label` (default "Civil Action No."), `case_number`, `caption.parties` (list of `{names, designation}`), `title`, `date` (the phrase after "This"), `signature` (`firm`, `signers` (list of lines per attorney), `address` (list of lines), `attorney_for`, optional `lead_counsel`).
- Body: markdown. Headings `#`, `##`, `###`; paragraphs; footnote definitions (`[^n]: ...`, placed before the first `[[signature]]` line, because cmarker renders a footnote only in the piece that holds its reference); `[[signature]]` markers.
- Case-name italics: `*...*` is supported by the template; the sample is roman (the exemplar's italics are not visible in the text layer).

## Known gaps and open choices

1. **Only one exemplar.** EVAL-LOOP's tier-2 rule asks for 2 or 3 filers from different sources. Not collected for this doc type. The M.D. Ga. search (CourtListener RECAP, `court=gamd`, `type=r`, "motion to dismiss memorandum", filed 2015-2021, filtered to stored PDFs) also found Sims v. Evans (5:15-cv-285, brief in Calibri, 8 pages), Zavala v. Ward (5:19-cv-383, memorandum 4 pages), and Thrower v. Matrix Warranty (3:19-cv-66, motion 4 pages; memorandum not separately saved). **Tier 2 is therefore not judged against two filers.**
2. **Font.** Times New Roman is not vendored (only EB Garamond is in `fonts/`). The template sets **EB Garamond at 13 pt** against the exemplar's 12-pt Times, because EB Garamond sets smaller at the same size; the 13 pt choice is a judgment call for David.
3. **M.D. Ga. typeface and margin rule.** None found in the 2022 consolidated Local Rules text. The layout follows the exemplar, as the brief says. The consolidated text is dated 3-7-22; the current text was not confirmed.
4. **Page limit (L.R. 7.4, 20 pages).** Not enforced by the template (no page counter). The sample is 9 pages.
5. **Judge's standing orders** were not checked.
6. **The motion is not templated.** The exemplar's ECF entry 10 (the motion) is separate; a complete filing needs the motion as its own document.
7. **Italics.** The sample is roman; the exemplar's case-name italics are not in its text layer. The template supports `*...*`.
8. **Headings** are typed by the author; the template does not number them.
9. **Filed content is kept.** The sample keeps the exemplar's text, citations and the "no set of facts" standard as filed. The template does not check authorities (L.R. 7.1 requires citations) or their accuracy.
10. **Lead counsel** (L.R. 83.1.2(A)) is optional and absent here, because a brief is not the first pleading; the rule text read above supports that reading.
11. **Overlay pairing.** Our pages 8 and 9 pair with the filed pages 9 and 8 (the two signature blocks are split across them in the render and in the filing).
12. **Not run:** `tools/check.sh` and `tools/check-selftest.sh` (see the complaint README, gap 10: same folder shape, and the brief forbids editing `tools/`).
