# Middle District of Georgia: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `gamd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia (PDF "Local_Rules-12_1_2022", amended March 7, 2022 per the file's header) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Note: the local rules state no typeface, point size, margin, or paper-size rule that was located. The repo default eval (1 in margins, 12 pt) applies to this court, and only the brief page limit is set below.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAMD-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| GAMD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| GAMD-FORM-001 | No paper-form rule (paper size, spacing, typeface, margins) appears in the local rules. | LR (silent) | A |
| GAMD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAMD-LEN-001 | Except on good cause shown and leave given, a brief in support of or in response to a motion is limited to 20 pages; the movant's reply is limited to 10 pages. | LR 7.4 | M |
| GAMD-LEN-002 | Objections to a report and recommendation are limited to 20 pages. | LR 7.4 | M |
| GAMD-LEN-003 | A party seeking to exceed the limits files a written motion no later than five days before the deadline and states the number of pages requested. | LR 7.4 | M |
