# Southern District of Georgia: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `gasd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia (PDF "SDGA_Local_Rules_Effective_10-07-2026") and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Note: the local rules state no typeface, point size, margin, or paper-size rule that was located. The repo default eval (1 in margins, 12 pt) applies. Length is set by word count for typed filings, which the default eval does not check; it is recorded as a row for the template and the PR.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GASD-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| GASD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| GASD-FORM-001 | No paper-form rule (paper size, spacing, typeface, margins) appears in the local rules. | LR (silent) | A |
| GASD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GASD-LEN-001 | A combined motion and brief, and a response, typed, do not exceed 6,500 words. A reply does not exceed 2,500 words. | LR Civ. 7(e)(1) | M |
| GASD-LEN-002 | Handwritten filings: a combined motion and brief, and a response, do not exceed 25 pages; a reply does not exceed 10 pages. Page numbering starts from the first page. | LR Civ. 7(e)(2) | M |
| GASD-CERT-001 | Anyone who files or signs a motion, response, or reply automatically certifies that it complies with the word limit. | LR Civ. 7(e)(1) | M |
