# Northern District of Georgia: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `gand`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia (PDF "NDGARulesCV", the civil rules as posted) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Typeface note: LR 5.1(C) approves four typefaces with minimum sizes: Times New Roman at least 14 point, Courier New at least 12 point, Century Schoolbook at least 13 point, or Book Antiqua at least 13 point. The repo default eval (12 pt) is weaker than the Times New Roman rule, so the eval must state the size for the typeface chosen.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAND-FORM-001 | Paper is white, opaque, good-quality 8½ × 11 inches, with writing on one side only. | LR 5.1(B)(1) | M |
| GAND-FORM-002 | Text is double-spaced between lines. Computer documents use Times New Roman (at least 14 point), Courier New (at least 12 point), Century Schoolbook (at least 13 point), or Book Antiqua (at least 13 point). Typewritten documents use no more than 10 characters per inch. | LR 5.1(C)(2)-(3) | M |
| GAND-FORM-003 | Footnotes, headings, and indented citations may be single-spaced. | LR 5.1(C) | M |
| GAND-FORM-004 | Margins at the top, bottom, left, and right are at least one inch. | LR 5.1(D) | M |
| GAND-FORM-005 | Pages are numbered consecutively at the bottom center of the page. | LR 5.1(E) | M |
| GAND-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| GAND-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| GAND-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |
