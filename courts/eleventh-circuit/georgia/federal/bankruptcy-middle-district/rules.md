# Bankruptcy Court, Middle District of Georgia: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `gamb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Middle District of Georgia (PDF "Local_Rules_Oct_2026", as of October 1, 2026) and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (Dec. 1, 2024 edition as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Note: the local rules state no typeface, point size, or margin rule for complaints or motions that was located. The repo default eval (1 in margins, 12 pt) applies. The page limit in LBR 9013-1(d) is set below.

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAMB-FORM-002 | Motion briefs carry the FRBP 9004(b) caption and the LBR 9004-2 caption content (GAMB-CONT-001 to -002). | FRBP 9004(b); LBR 9004-2 | M |
| GAMB-MOT-001 | Motions for relief from stay may not be combined with other relief, except that relief allowed by 11 U.S.C. §§ 362 and 1205 may be combined, and § 1301 motions may be combined with § 362 motions. A combined filing carries the notice required for each motion type. | LBR 9004-1(a)(1) | M |
| GAMB-MOT-002 | An objection to confirmation of a Chapter 13 plan may not be combined with other requests for relief. | LBR 9004-1(a)(2) | M |
| GAMB-MOT-003 | Every motion states applicable code sections, rules, or other authority, and is accompanied by a notice of motion. | LBR 9013-1(b)-(c) | M |
| GAMB-MOT-004 | A certificate of service states the parties served (with addresses), the method of service, the date of service, and an attorney's signature, except for parties served through the ECF system. | LBR 9013-3(a) | M |
| GAMB-LEN-001 | Motions, responses, and briefs are limited to 20 pages, except on good cause and leave given. A movant's reply is limited to 10 pages. Exhibits are not counted. | LBR 9013-1(d)(1) | M |
| GAMB-LEN-002 | A party seeking to exceed the limit files a written motion no later than five days before the applicable deadline and states the number of pages requested. | LBR 9013-1(d)(2) | M |
