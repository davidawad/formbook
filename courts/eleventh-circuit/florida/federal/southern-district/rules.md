# Southern District of Florida: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `flsd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida (PDF "Local Rules effective 120125", revised December 1, 2025) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Scope note: Local Rule 5.1(a) states the paper form for papers exempt from CM/ECF and tendered for conventional filing. Electronically filed papers follow the CM/ECF administrative procedures, which were not retrieved in full; the paper-form rows below are the only mandated form rules located.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLSD-FORM-001 | Paper is standard size 8½ × 11 inches, white and opaque. | LR 5.1(a)(3) | M |
| FLSD-FORM-002 | The document is typed or written on one side with 1-inch margins on the top, bottom, and each side. Lines are spaced at least one and one-half. Quoted material of fifty words or more and footnotes may be single-spaced. | LR 5.1(a)(4) | M |
| FLSD-FORM-003 | Fonts, including footnotes and quotations, are at least 12-point. | LR 5.1(a)(4) | M |
| FLSD-FORM-004 | Pages are numbered consecutively at the bottom center of each page. | LR 5.1(a)(4) | M |
| FLSD-FORM-005 | Attachments are not tabbed; reference characters are printed on a blank separating sheet. Only one side of the paper is used. | LR 5.1(a)(1), (4) | M |
| FLSD-CONT-001 | Caption, in order: the court's name, centered across the page; the docket number, category, and the last names of the assigned District Judge and Magistrate Judge, centered; the style of the action, filling no more than the left side of the page; and the title of the document, naming and designating the party. | LR 5.1(a)(5)(A)-(D) | M |
| FLSD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| FLSD-CONT-003 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| FLSD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |
