# JURISDICTION AND VENUE

This action arises under the Constitution and laws of the United States, particularly 42 U.S.C. §§ 1983 and 1988, and the Fourth Amendment to the United States Constitution.

The jurisdiction of this Court is invoked pursuant to 28 U.S.C. §§ 1331 and 1343.

This action arises from the wrongful arrest of BRACHO CARDELLA by HALL, TILAN and LYONS on October 14, 2023.

The damages sought in this action exceed $75,000, exclusive of interest, costs, and attorney's fees.

The § 768.28 Notice of Claim was provided more than six (6) months ago on or about July 3, 2024. Defendant, CITY, has denied the claim. There is no administrative exhaustion requirement regarding Plaintiff's civil rights claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983.

Venue is proper in the Middle District of Florida because the wrongful arrest of BRACHO CARDELLA occurred in Pinellas County, Florida.

This Court has jurisdiction over the state law claims asserted herein, and it also has concurrent jurisdiction over the federal claims for violations of BRACHO CARDELLA's Civil Rights asserted herein. The Court has both personal jurisdiction over the defendants and subject matter jurisdiction to hear Plaintiff's claims herein.

# FACTS

On October 14, 2023, Officer Alexandre Tilan conducted a traffic stop of Mr. Bracho Cardella for an alleged red-light violation.

Officer Williams Lyons subsequently conducted a DUI investigation.

Mr. Bracho Cardella performed extremely well on the exercises and did not appear intoxicated.

Nonetheless, Mr. Bracho Cardella was arrested by Officer Dustin Hall for DUI and taken to the jail.

At the jail he willingly provided two breath samples of 0.00.

A urine sample was also provided. Toxicology reports confirmed no controlled substances were present.

As a result of the arrest, Mr. Bracho Cardella spent 1 day in jail.

The criminal charge was ultimately dismissed on December 14, 2023.

The civil infraction was Nolle Prosse on December 18, 2023.

# COUNT I

## SECTION 1983 FALSE ARREST CLAIM AGAINST TILAN

Plaintiff re-alleges and incorporates by reference the allegations contained in paragraphs 1 – 21 above, and further alleges:

Plaintiff was unlawfully seized by Defendant, TILAN, through the intentional confinement of Plaintiff when Defendant placed him in handcuffs, in the back of his patrol car, and transported him to the Pinellas County Jail.

The warrantless arrest of Plaintiff without even arguable probable cause violated Plaintiff's Fourth Amendment rights.

The facts and circumstances within Defendant's knowledge, assuming he had reasonably trustworthy information, would not cause a prudent officer to believe, under the circumstances shown, that Plaintiff had committed or was committing a criminal offense.

A reasonable officer in the same circumstances and possessing the same knowledge as Defendant could not have believed that probable cause existed to arrest Plaintiff.

The unlawful seizure caused the Plaintiff to suffer mental anguish, loss of capacity for the enjoyment of life, loss of liberty, humiliation, embarrassment, shame, fright, disgrace, injury to his feelings, loss of employment, lost wages and loss of reputation.
