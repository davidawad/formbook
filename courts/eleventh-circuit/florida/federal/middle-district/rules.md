# Middle District of Florida: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `flmd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida (PDF "amended local rules effective November 01, 2025") and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Re-check cadence: annually and within 30 days after a published amendment or new standing order affecting this document type.

Typeface note: Rule 1.08 requires 13-point main text for the listed typefaces (Book Antiqua, Calisto MT, Century Schoolbook, Georgia, Palatino), or 14-point main text if Times New Roman is used. The repo default eval (12 pt) is weaker than this rule, so the eval must state 13 pt (or 14 pt for Times New Roman) for this court.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLMD-FORM-001 | Paper size is 8½ × 11 inches. | LR 1.08(a) | M |
| FLMD-FORM-002 | Margins are 1 inch. | LR 1.08(a) | M |
| FLMD-FORM-003 | Main text is at least 13-point and double-spaced (2.0). Indented quotations are at least 12-point and single-spaced. Footnotes are at least 11-point and single-spaced. | LR 1.08(a) | M |
| FLMD-FORM-004 | Typeface is one of Book Antiqua, Calisto MT, Century Schoolbook, Georgia, or Palatino. Times New Roman is allowed only with main text at least 14-point, indented quotation at least 13-point, and footnote at least 12-point. | LR 1.08(a)-(b) | M |
| FLMD-FORM-005 | Page numbers are centered at the bottom; numbering on page one is not required. Character spacing is normal, scale 100%. | LR 1.08(a) | M |
| FLMD-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| FLMD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| FLMD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |

| FLMD-LEN-001 | A motion, with its incorporated legal memorandum, is no longer than 25 pages. A response memorandum is no longer than 20 pages. | FLMD-LEN-002 | A motion for leave to exceed the page limit is no longer than 3 pages and must not include the proposed paper. | LR 3.01(b)-(c) | M |
