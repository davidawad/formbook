# Bankruptcy Court, Southern District of Florida: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `flsb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Florida (PDF "2026 Local Rules", effective June 1, 2026) and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (Dec. 1, 2024 edition as posted) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLSB-FORM-001 | Every document created for CM/ECF filing is in text-searchable PDF format. | LBR 9004-1(a)(1) | M |
| FLSB-FORM-002 | Margins are at least one inch on the top, bottom, and both sides. | LBR 9004-1(a)(2) | M |
| FLSB-FORM-003 | Text is 12-point; footnotes may be 10-point. | LBR 9004-1(a)(3) | M |
| FLSB-FORM-004 | Text is double-spaced. Captions, titles, bulletins, headings, quotations longer than 50 words, footnotes, signature blocks, and certificates of service are single-spaced. | LBR 9004-1(a)(4) | M |
| FLSB-FORM-005 | Exhibits to a CM/ECF document are exempt from LBR 9004-1(a). | LBR 9004-1(b)(1) | M |
| FLSB-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document's character. | FRBP 9004(b)(1)-(4) | M |
| FLSB-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted under FRBP 9037. | FRBP 9037 | M |
| FLSB-CERT-001 | Counsel's signature and identification follow FRBP 9011. | FRBP 9011 | M |
