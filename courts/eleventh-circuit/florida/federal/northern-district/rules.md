# Northern District of Florida: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `flnd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida (PDF "local_rules_0.pdf"; header reads "Effective November 24, 2015", with amendments as printed) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Typeface note: Local Rule 5.1(C) requires at least 14-point font, double spacing, and at least one-inch margins. The repo default eval (12 pt) is weaker than this rule, so the eval must state 14 pt for this court.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLND-FORM-001 | A document is double-spaced, in at least 14-point font, with at least one-inch margins on the top, bottom, left, and right of each page. | LR 5.1(C) | M |
| FLND-FORM-002 | Pages are numbered. Handwritten documents are legible with adequate spacing. Hard copies are fastened in the upper left-hand corner. | LR 5.1(C) | M |
| FLND-CONT-001 | The first page begins with the case style: the court's name, the case number with the initials of the assigned judges, and the name of at least one party on each side. A heading that identifies the document follows the case style and precedes all other content. | LR 5.1(B) | M |
| FLND-CONT-002 | Caption has the court's name, title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint's title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a); LR 5.1(B) | M |
| FLND-CONT-003 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| FLND-CERT-001 | A document filed by an attorney has a signature block with the handwritten or electronic signature, typed name, bar number, street and email addresses, and telephone number, and identifies the parties on whose behalf it is filed. | LR 5.1(D) | M |
| FLND-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted. | FRCP 5.2 | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLND-FORM-003 | Motion briefs follow FLND-FORM-001 to -002, FLND-CONT-001, and FLND-CERT-001. | LR 5.1(B)-(D) | M |
| FLND-LEN-001 | A memorandum does not exceed 8,000 words and includes a certificate stating its word count. | LR 7.1(F) | M |
| FLND-LEN-002 | A party ordinarily may not file a reply memorandum; a permitted reply does not exceed 3,200 words, counted and certified as for other memoranda. | LR 7.1(I) | M |
| FLND-LEN-003 | A pro se petition, motion, or complaint (civil-rights or collateral attack) together with any memorandum does not exceed 25 pages unless the Court authorizes it. This applies to pro se filings only. | LR 5.7(B) | A |
