# Bankruptcy Court, Northern District of Florida: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `flnb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Florida (PDF "local_rules_02-26-2024", effective February 26, 2024), the district Local Rules of the Northern District of Florida that the bankruptcy rules incorporate (LBR 1(C)), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (Dec. 1, 2024 edition as posted) on 2026-10-20.

Typeface note: the bankruptcy local rules incorporate the district's Local Rule 5.1(C), which requires at least 14-point font, double spacing, and one-inch margins. The repo default eval (12 pt) is weaker, so the eval must state 14 pt for this court's adversary complaints and motions, subject to LBR 1(C)'s "to the extent applicable" limit.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLNB-FORM-001 | Papers are clearly legible; commonly used English abbreviations are acceptable. | FRBP 9004(a) | M |
| FLNB-FORM-002 | Font is at least 14 point, double-spaced, with one-inch margins on the top, bottom, left, and right, and pages are numbered, by incorporation of the district rule through LBR 1(C). | LBR 1(C); LR 5.1(C) | M |
| FLNB-CONT-001 | The first page carries a caption as in the Official Forms. The title states the designation of the party, the party's name if the designation is insufficiently specific, and by whom the paper is filed, and it describes the contents or relief requested. A memorandum combined with a motion is so stated in the caption. | LBR 9004-1(A); FRBP 9004(b) | M |
| FLNB-CONT-002 | A paper seeking injunctive relief under FRBP 7065 says "AND PRAYER FOR INJUNCTIVE RELIEF" in its title. | LBR 9004-1(B) | M |
| FLNB-CONT-003 | Caption carries the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary number, and the designation of the document. | FRBP 9004(b)(1)-(4) | M |
| FLNB-CERT-001 | A document filed by an attorney has a signature block with the handwritten or electronic signature, typed name, bar number, street and email addresses, and telephone number, and identifies the parties on whose behalf it is filed. | LR 5.1(D) (through LBR 1(C)) | M |
| FLNB-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted under FRBP 9037. | FRBP 9037 | M |

|---|---|---|---|
