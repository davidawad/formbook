# Florida Third District Court of Appeal: briefs, petitions, motions, amicus briefs

Alias `fladistctapp3`. Scope: papers filed in the Third District Court of Appeal under the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure (Fla. R. App. P.). The statewide form rule (Rule 9.045) and the word limits of Rules 9.100, 9.210 and 9.370 apply to every district court of appeal; this file records the rule text as fetched on 2026-10-21. Verified against the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 9.045, 9.100, 9.210 and 9.370, as shown on the copies cited below, and Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.520(a), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the Third District's own website (https://www.3dca.flcourts.gov/, HTTP 200 on 2026-10-21) shows no local rule or form-of-papers page in its static HTML, and no local rule on form was located. The official Florida Supreme Court rules PDF (https://supremecourt.flcourts.gov/Portals/0/Rules/RulesofAppellateProcedure/, HTTP 200 but JavaScript-rendered; no PDF link in the static HTML) was not reached. The rule text was read from secondary copies that are cited below; where a row depends on text not read, it says so.

Note for template authors: Rule 9.045(b) requires computer-generated documents to be filed in **Arial 14-point** or **Bookman Old Style 14-point**. This template uses Arial 14 (Liberation Sans, metric-compatible), and sets its footnotes and quotations at 14 point as well. Times New Roman, which the pre-2021 rules named, is not permitted. Several public exemplars in `usage/` use Times New Roman or Garamond; they do not set the standard.

## Petitions (writ, review, discretionary review)

Petitions for writ and for review of non-final agency orders (Rule 9.100) and discretionary review by certiorari (Rule 9.030(b)(2) with Rule 9.100; 9.030 not retrieved) are original proceedings under Rule 9.100. The word limits below are the Rule 9.100 limits.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLADISTCTAPP3-PET-001 | The caption of a petition contains the name of the court and the name and designation of all parties on each side. | R9100 (g) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP3-PET-002 | The petition contains: the basis for invoking the court's jurisdiction; the facts relied on; the nature of the relief sought; and argument in support of the petition with appropriate citations of authority. | R9100 (g)(1)-(4) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP3-PET-003 | Where the petition seeks an order directed to a lower tribunal, it is accompanied by an appendix (Rule 9.220, not retrieved) and cites the appropriate pages of the appendix. | R9100 (g) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP3-LEN-003 | A computer-generated petition does not exceed **13,000 words**. A response does not exceed **13,000 words**. A reply, and any supplemental appendix, does not exceed **4,000 words**. | R9100 (g), (j), (k) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP3-PET-004 | A response includes argument in support, appropriate citations of authority, and references to the appropriate pages of the supporting appendices. | R9100 (j) | M |
