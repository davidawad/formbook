# Florida District Courts of Appeal: briefs, petitions, motions, amicus briefs

Alias `fladistctapp`. Scope: papers filed in the Florida District Courts of Appeal (DCAs) under the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure, for every DCA in the state (First, Second, Third, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth). This folder is the umbrella for the state's DCAs; sibling DCA folders reuse these rows and add only court-specific rules. Verified against the Florida Supreme Court opinion amending Rule 9.045 (No. SC2025-1458, corrected March 19, 2026, effective July 1, 2026) and the Rules of Appellate Procedure and General Practice and Judicial Administration as shown on the copies cited below, on 2026-10-21.

Note for template authors: Rule 9.045(b) permits only Arial 14-point or Bookman Old Style 14-point for computer-generated documents. Times New Roman and Courier New, which the pre-2021 rules named, are no longer permitted. Some public exemplars in `usage/` (the writ and the discretionary-review petition) use Times New Roman; they do not set the standard.

## Petitions (writ, review, discretionary review)

Petitions for writ and for review of non-final agency orders (Rule 9.100) and discretionary review of district court decisions by certiorari (Rule 9.030(b)(2) with Rule 9.100) are original proceedings under Rule 9.100. The word-count limits below apply to the petition, response and reply. The subdivision letters are the ones cited in the copies read; confirm against the official rule text before relying on the letter.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLADISTCTAPP-PET-001 | The petition contains: the basis for invoking the court's jurisdiction; the facts relied on; the nature of the relief sought; and argument in support with citations of authority. | FRAP 9.100(g) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP-LEN-003 | Computer-generated petition does not exceed **13,000 words**. Response does not exceed **13,000 words**. Reply does not exceed **4,000 words** and any supplemental appendix. | FRAP 9.100(g), (j), (k) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP-PET-002 | A response includes argument, citations of authority, and references to the appropriate pages of the supporting appendices. | FRAP 9.100(j) | M |
