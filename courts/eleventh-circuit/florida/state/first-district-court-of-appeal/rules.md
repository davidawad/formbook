# Florida First District Court of Appeal: briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `fladistctapp1`. Scope: the initial (opening) brief, answer brief, reply brief, amicus curiae brief, motion and supporting memorandum, petition for writ, petition for review of agency action, and petition for discretionary review (certiorari). Verified against the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 9.045 and 9.210, on 2026-10-21, from the two opinion texts listed below.

Local rules not retrieved: the court's own local rules and clerk's e-filing standards were not retrieved. The Florida Supreme Court's rules PDF returned an HTML page instead of the rules (https://flcourts-media.ccplatform.net/Portals/0/Rules/RulesofAppellateProcedure/RulesofAppellateProcedure.pdf, HTTP 200, HTML; https://supremecourt.flcourts.gov/Portals/0/Rules/RulesofAppellateProcedure/RulesofAppellateProcedure.pdf, HTTP 200, "page not found" body). Rules 9.030, 9.100, 9.300, 9.330 and 9.370 (petitions, motions, discretionary review and amicus briefs) were not retrieved: state-rules.com, ruledex.com, courtrules.net and floridabar.org returned HTTP 403. Rows that depend on those rules are absent, not guessed. The template therefore sets no page or word limit for the petition, motion or amicus types.
