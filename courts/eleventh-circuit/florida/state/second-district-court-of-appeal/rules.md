# Florida Second District Court of Appeal: briefs, petitions, motions, amicus briefs

Alias `fladistctapp2`. Scope: papers filed in the Second District Court of Appeal under the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure: initial (opening) briefs, answer briefs, reply briefs, petitions for writ, petitions for discretionary review, petitions for review of non-final agency action, motions, and amicus curiae briefs. The statewide rules are the court's rules; the Second District has no form rule of its own that this pass retrieved. Verified against the Florida Supreme Court opinion amending Rule 9.045 (No. SC2025-1458, corrected March 19, 2026, amendments effective July 1, 2026), the court-hosted Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure compilation dated January 1, 2024, and the court-hosted Rule 2.520 text, on 2026-10-21.

Tier 1 for this court is Rule 9.045(b): computer-generated papers are set in **Arial 14-point** or **Bookman Old Style 14-point** and nothing else. The template uses Arial 14 point (metric twin Liberation Sans, vendored). The default body size of 12 pt is therefore not the rule's size; `eval.yaml` records 14 pt with the cite.

Local rules not retrieved: no Second District local form rule or administrative order was located in this pass (the court's own site was not searched for one). Rows that would depend on one are absent, not guessed.

Note for template authors: the statewide rule text cites Arial and Bookman Old Style. Several public exemplars in `usage/` use Times New Roman (the discretionary-review petition's body, the writ's body, the review petition). Those are filed copies that predate the current text or were not conformed; they do not set the standard.

## Petitions (writ, discretionary review, review of non-final agency action)

Petitions for writ, for discretionary review by certiorari, and for review of non-final agency action are original proceedings under Rule 9.100.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLADISTCTAPP2-PET-001 | The caption contains the name of the court and the name and designation of all parties on each side. The name of the judge or lower tribunal is omitted from the caption. | RAP24 9.100(g) and subdivision (g) caption text | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP2-LEN-003 | A computer-generated petition does not exceed **13,000 words**; a response does not exceed **13,000 words**; a reply does not exceed **4,000 words**. | RAP24 9.100(g), (j), (k) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP2-PET-002 | The petition contains: the basis for invoking the court's jurisdiction; the facts relied on; the nature of the relief sought; and argument in support with citations of authority. An order directed to a lower tribunal is accompanied by an appendix under Rule 9.220, and the petition refers to the pages of that appendix. | RAP24 9.100(g)(1)-(4) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP2-PET-003 | A response includes argument, citations of authority, and references to the appropriate pages of the supporting appendices. | RAP24 9.100(j) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP2-COV-002 | The cover of a petition or response shows the court, the style of the cause, the lower tribunal, the party filing, the type of paper, and the name, address and e-mail address of the filing attorney. | RAP24 9.100(g); RAP24 9.210(a)(1) by analogy for the cover contents | C |
