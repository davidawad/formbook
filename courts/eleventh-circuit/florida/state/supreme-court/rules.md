# Supreme Court of Florida: briefs, petitions, motions, rehearing motions and amicus briefs

Alias `fla`. Scope: papers filed in the Supreme Court of Florida under the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure: briefs on jurisdiction and merits (initial, answer, reply), petitions for discretionary review (Rule 9.120), petitions for writs and other original proceedings (Rule 9.100), motions (Rule 9.300), motions for rehearing (Rule 9.330) and amicus curiae briefs (Rule 9.370). Verified against Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure, October 1, 2026 edition (which includes the 7-1-26 amendments to Rule 9.045), and Florida Rules of General Practice and Judicial Administration, July 1, 2026 edition (Rule 2.520), on 2026-10-21.

Rule 9.045(b) is the controlling face rule: computer-generated papers are set in Arial 14-point or Bookman Old Style 14-point. The template uses Arial (Liberation Sans is the metric-compatible Arial clone shipped in `fonts/`; no Bookman Old Style clone is vendored). Times New Roman, which the pre-2021 rules named and several public exemplars use, is not permitted. The exemplars in `usage/` set a tier 2 convention only where the rules leave it open; they do not set the form.

Local rules not retrieved: the Court's own rule page (https://supremecourt.flcourts.gov/Rules/Rules-of-Appellate-Procedure, HTTP 200) links to a JavaScript-rendered index, and its PDF host (https://flcourts-media.ccplatform.net/, HTTP 404) gave no rule PDF. The Bar's copy of the rules was used; the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal standards, Rule 2.425 (minimization of sensitive information), Rule 2.515 (signatures) and Rule 9.420 (service) were not retrieved, so rows that depend on them are marked as such and are not guessed.
