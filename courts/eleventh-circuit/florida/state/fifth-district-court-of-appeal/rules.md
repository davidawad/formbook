# Florida Fifth District Court of Appeal: briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `fladistctapp5`. Scope: the appellate papers the Fifth District takes under the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure: the initial brief (appellant or petitioner), the answer brief, the reply brief, amicus curiae briefs, motions and supporting memoranda, petitions for review and for discretionary review, and petitions for writ. Verified against Fla. R. App. P. 9.045 as amended effective July 1, 2026, and Fla. R. App. P. 9.210 as quoted in the 2020 amendment opinion, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the Florida Supreme Court's current rule PDF (https://supremecourt.flcourts.gov/Portals/0/Rules/RulesofAppellateProcedure/ returned HTTP 200 with no rule links, a JS-rendered page; the portal's "page not found" response on a second URL); the Florida Bar copies of the rules (https://www-media.floridabar.org/uploads/2026/09/Appellate-Court-Rules-09-01-26.pdf, HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21); the current text of Fla. R. App. P. 9.210 and 9.045(d); Fla. R. App. P. 9.100, 9.030, 9.190 and 9.400; Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.520 (https://www.syfert.com/florida/rules-general-practice-judicial-administration/rule-2.520 returned HTTP 200 but only a navigation page). Rows that depend on them are either sourced to the opinion text that quotes them or marked as not verified below. The Fifth District's own administrative orders and the e-filing portal standards were not retrieved.

| FLADISTCTAPP5-FORM-007 | Paper filings are held together by removable paper clips and are not stapled or bound. | RAP-9.045(c) | M |

| FLADISTCTAPP5-FORM-009 | The word count excludes the caption, cover page, table of contents, table of citations, certificate of compliance, certificate of service and signature block. It includes headings, footnotes and quotations. | RAP-9.045(e) | M |
