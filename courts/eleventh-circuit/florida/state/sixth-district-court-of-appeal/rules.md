# Florida Sixth District Court of Appeal: briefs, petitions, motions, amicus briefs

Alias `fladistctapp6`. Scope: papers filed in the Sixth District Court of Appeal under the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure and the Florida Rules of General Practice and Judicial Administration. The Sixth District has no local form rule retrieved for this file; the statewide rules below govern. Sibling umbrella file: `courts/eleventh-circuit/florida/state/district-courts-of-appeal/rules.md`.

Verified against Fla. R. App. P. 9.045 as amended by *In re Amendments to Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure 9.020, 9.045, 9.200, and 9.420*, No. SC2025-1458 (Fla. Mar. 19, 2026, corrected opinion; effective July 1, 2026), and against the rule text of Rules 9.100, 9.210, 9.300 and 9.370 as shown on the secondary copies listed below, on 2026-10-21.

**Official text not reached.** The Florida Supreme Court's rules pages are JavaScript-rendered. `https://supremecourt.flcourts.gov/Rules/Rules-of-Appellate-Procedure` returned HTTP 200 with a "page not found" shell; `https://supremecourt.flcourts.gov/Portals/0/Rules/RulesofAppellateProcedure/RulesofAppellateProcedure.pdf` returned HTTP 200 as `text/html` (a shell, not the PDF); `https://www.floridabar.org/rules/appellate/` returned HTTP 403. Every rule row below therefore cites a secondary copy that was fetched with HTTP 200 and read for the cited text. Re-check each `M` row against the official rule before relying on a subdivision letter.

**Font.** Rule 9.045(b) requires computer-generated documents in **Arial 14-point** or **Bookman Old Style 14-point**. This template uses Arial 14 pt, set in the vendored Liberation Sans (metric-compatible with Arial; `docs/FONTS.md`). Times New Roman, which the pre-2021 rules named, is no longer permitted. Several public exemplars in `usage/` use Times New Roman or Aptos; they do not set the standard.

## Petitions (writ, discretionary review)

The petition exemplars are original proceedings under Rule 9.100. Petitions for writ and for review of non-final agency action are governed by Rule 9.100; discretionary review of district court decisions by certiorari is the Rule 9.030(b)(2) petition processed under Rule 9.100 (9.030 not retrieved; the exemplar cites it).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLADISTCTAPP6-PET-001 | The petition contains: the basis for invoking the court's jurisdiction; the facts relied on; the nature of the relief sought; and argument in support with citations of authority. | R100 (9.100(g)) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP6-LEN-004 | Computer-generated petition does not exceed **13,000 words**. Response does not exceed **13,000 words**. Reply does not exceed **4,000 words** and any supplemental appendix. | R100 (9.100(g), (j), (k)) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP6-PET-002 | A petition seeking an order directed to a lower tribunal is accompanied by an appendix, and the petition contains references to the appropriate pages of the supporting appendix. | R100 (9.100(g)) | M |
| FLADISTCTAPP6-PET-003 | A response includes argument, citations of authority, and references to the appropriate pages of the supporting appendices. | R100 (9.100(j)) | M |
