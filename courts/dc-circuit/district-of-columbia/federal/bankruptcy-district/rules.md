# Bankr. D.D.C. (United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Columbia): rules

Alias `dcb`. Scope: counseled filings in adversary proceedings, filed through NextGen CM/ECF: the complaint commencing an adversary proceeding (`adversary-complaint/`) and a motion with its supporting memorandum, here a motion to dismiss an adversary complaint (`motion-brief/`). Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (Federal Bankruptcy Rules amendments) and whenever the court posts new Local Bankruptcy Rules on https://www.dcb.uscourts.gov/local-rules-orders. **Pending:** on 2026-09-24 the court invited comment (45 days) on amendments to, among others, LBR 5005-1, 5070-1, 7004-2, 7016-1, 9011-1 and 9013-1 (a new 9013-1(d) on improperly scheduled hearings, with the later subsections relettered). Re-check this file when they take effect; the IDs below cite the February 1, 2026 lettering.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a court rule (Local Bankruptcy Rule, LBR appendix, or Federal Rule). **C** = convention (the rules leave it open; taken from exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule of this repo. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

Findings:

1. **No type, spacing or margin rule for counseled filings.** The LBR text was searched (font, point, margin, typeface, double spac-) on 2026-10-17. The only format rule, LBR 5005-1(b) (Page 31), governs pro se paper filings: "letter size paper (8 ½ by 11 inches) with no less than 1-inch margins on all sides, be plainly and legibly typewritten". For represented parties the national rule is FRBP 9004(a): a document "must be clearly legible". Type size, typeface, spacing and margins are therefore C.
2. **Attorney identifiers on page 1.** LBR 5005-1(c) (Page 31) requires, "in either the footer or header of the first page", the attorney's name, bar number, office address, email, telephone and whom the attorney represents. Both exemplars put this block at the top left of page 1, above the court name.
3. **Consent statement.** FRBP 7008 and LBR 7012-1 (Page 39): every party's initial substantive filing states whether it consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy judge; a party that omits it "is deemed to have consented". EX-A2 (consents) and EX-A3 (does not consent) state it; EX-A1, EX-M1 and EX-M2 do not.
4. **Notice of motion.** LBR 9013-1(c) (Page 47): a motion, "including a motion filed in an adversary proceeding, shall include or be accompanied by a conspicuous notice of the motion, objection deadline, and hearing, if applicable", conforming substantially to Official Form B 420A. Neither motion exemplar (EX-M1, EX-M2) includes one in the PDF.
5. **Exemplars.** Adversary complaint: three filers (EX-A1, EX-A2, EX-A3). Motion: two filers (EX-M1, EX-M2). Both LBR 5005-1(c) placements occur: header block at top left (EX-A1, EX-A3, EX-M1) and footer of page 1 (EX-A2); EX-M2 has neither.
