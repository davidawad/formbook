Pursuant to D.C. Code § 34-605, the Office of the People’s Counsel of the District of Columbia (“OPC”), the statutory representative of District of Columbia ratepayers with respect to utility matters,[^1] respectfully seeks review by the District of Columbia Court of Appeals of the following decisions and orders of the District of Columbia Public Service Commission (“Respondent”):

- Formal Case No. 1176, *In the Matter of the Application of the Potomac Electric Power Company for Authority to Implement a Multiyear Rate Plan for Electric Distribution Service in the District of Columbia*, Order No. 22328, rel. November 26, 2024;[^2] and
- Formal Case No. 1176, *In the Matter of the Application of the Potomac Electric Power Company for Authority to Implement a Multiyear Rate Plan for Electric Distribution Service in the District of Columbia*, Order No. 22358, rel. January 28, 2025.[^3]

OPC will be asking the Court to vacate Respondent’s decisions because they are the result of arbitrary and capricious decisionmaking.

The names, addresses, and telephone numbers of the Respondent and all parties to the proceedings below on the attached Certificate of Service.
