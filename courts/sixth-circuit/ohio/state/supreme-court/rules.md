# Ohio Supreme Court: merit briefs, jurisdictional memoranda, motions, original-action complaints

Alias `ohio`. Scope: documents filed in the Supreme Court of Ohio under the Rules of Practice of the Supreme Court of Ohio: merit briefs (appellant, appellee, reply, amicus), memoranda in support of jurisdiction (discretionary and jurisdictional appeals), motions and the motion for reconsideration, and the complaint instituting an original action. ## Merit briefs (brief-on-the-merits, answering-brief)

Relators' or respondents' merit briefs in an original action are merit briefs under RoP 12.07(A), which requires conformity with Section 16.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHIO-MERIT-001 | Contents, in order: table of contents (with table of authorities, statement of facts, argument with numbered propositions of law, appendix); table of authorities arranged alphabetically; statement of facts with record references; argument headed by each proposition of law, each a subheading; appendix numbered separately. | RoP 16.02(B)(1)-(5). | M |
| OHIO-MERIT-002 | Appellant's merit brief: 50 numbered pages at most, excluding the table of contents, table of authorities, certificate of service, signature block and appendix. | RoP 16.02(C)(1); RoP 12.07(A). | M |
| OHIO-MERIT-003 | Appellee's merit brief answers the appellant's propositions and states no additional or contrary propositions; it may omit the statement of facts if it agrees with the appellant's. Limit: 50 numbered pages, with the same exclusions. | RoP 16.03(B)(1)-(2), (C)(1). | M |
| OHIO-MERIT-004 | Reply brief (appellant) limit: 20 numbered pages, excluding the table of contents, table of authorities, certificate of service and appendix. | RoP 16.04(B)(1). | M |
| OHIO-MERIT-005 | Cover page of an appellee or relator-side merit brief carries the same content as OHIO-COVER-001. | RoP 3.07; RoP 16.03. | M |
| OHIO-MERIT-006 | Filing deadlines (40 days for an appellant, 30 days for an appellee, 20 days for reply, from the record and appellant's brief) are not checked by the template. | RoP 16.02(A)(2), 16.03(A)(2), 16.04(A)(2). | A |

## Eval numbers

Body text is 12 pt (OHIO-FORM-004). Margins are 1 in (OHIO-FORM-002). The page limits in `eval.yaml` (`params.page_limit`) are the RoP figures for each doc type, applied to the whole rendered PDF, which counts the cover page as well. That is stricter than the rule's count (which excludes the cover, table of contents, signature block, certificate of service and appendix), so a render that passes DEF-LEN-001 is within the rule.
