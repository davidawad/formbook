# Ohio Court of Appeals: briefs, amicus briefs, motions, and original-action petitions

Alias `ohioctapp`. Scope: briefs of appellant, appellee and reply, amicus curiae briefs, motions (including motions for stay or injunction pending appeal), and petitions for writ filed in the twelve Ohio courts of appeals. Verified against the Ohio Rules of Appellate Procedure as amended through July 1, 2026 (amendment notes in the PDF), on 2026-10-21.

Live host unreachable: `https://www.supremecourt.ohio.gov/docs/LegalResources/Rules/appellate/appellateProcedure.pdf` timed out from this host on 2026-10-21 (curl code 000, repeated). The text below is the Internet Archive capture of that same Supreme Court of Ohio PDF, captured 2026-10-14 00:05:31 UTC.

Local rules not retrieved: each of the twelve districts may adopt local rules (App.R. 41(A); App.R. 19(A) lets a court of appeals set different word or page limits by local rule). The district local rules for the First, Fifth, Sixth, Eighth and Tenth Districts, which the filed examples come from, were not retrieved. Until they are, the statewide numbers below are the controlling figures for this template; a district local rule that sets a different limit must be checked before a filing in that district.

Rules of appellate procedure govern appeals (App.R. 1(A)). An original action in mandamus is not an appeal and the App.R. rules do not by their terms govern it; the petition-for-writ form below applies App.R. 19 by analogy and is marked C throughout.

Typeface: App.R. 19(A) requires "at least a twelve point type" and names no face. The template declares Times New Roman, matching the filed examples (kind C).

## Form of every paper (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHIOCTAPP-FORM-001 | Briefs are typewritten or produced by standard typographic printing on opaque, unglazed paper, with pages not exceeding 8 1/2 by 11 inches. Type matter must not exceed 6 1/2 by 9 1/2 inches, which leaves margins of at least one inch at each side and at least 1 1/2 inches at top and bottom combined. | RULES App.R. 19 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-FORM-002 | All printed matter, including footnotes and captions, is at least twelve-point type. | RULES App.R. 19 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-FORM-003 | Lines of typewritten text are double spaced, except quoted matter, which is single spaced. | RULES App.R. 19 (A) (briefs); App.R. 19 (B) (motions and other papers). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-FORM-004 | Typeface: no face is named. Times New Roman is declared, and the filed examples use it. | RULES App.R. 19 (A) (size only). | C |
| OHIOCTAPP-FORM-005 | Sheets are attached at the left margin when consecutively bound. Paper is opaque and unglazed. Page numbering is conventional and not mandated by App.R. 19. | RULES App.R. 19 (B). | C |
| OHIOCTAPP-FILE-001 | Documents are filed with the clerk of the court of appeals in the county where the case originated, electronically under the court's local e-filing rule. Filing by documents sent to the court itself, a single judge's office, or another clerk is not filing. | RULES App.R. 13 (A), (B) (2). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-FILE-002 | Copies: four copies of each brief to the clerk, and one copy to counsel for each separately represented party, unless the court's local rule waives copies for electronic filing. | RULES App.R. 18 (B). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-SVC-001 | Service on all other parties of every document filed by a party, by the party or a person acting for it. | RULES App.R. 13 (C). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-CERT-001 | Each signed paper bears the signature of the attorney or unrepresented party. The signature certifies that the document complies with the applicable word-count limitation; no separate certificate sentence is mandated. | RULES App.R. 19 (A) (last sentences). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-CERT-002 | Caption and identifying content of a motion or other paper: name of the court, title of the case, case number, and a brief descriptive title showing the paper's purpose. | RULES App.R. 19 (B). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-ATT-001 | Parties are discouraged from attaching legal authority generally accessible through online research databases. Authority not available online is reproduced in an addendum or supplied in pamphlet form. | RULES App.R. 16 (E). | A |

## Briefs (appellant, appellee, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHIOCTAPP-LEN-001 | Without prior leave, an initial brief of appellant or cross-appellant and an answer brief of appellee or cross-appellee contain no more than 9,000 words, and a reply brief no more than 4,500 words, excluding cover page, table of contents, table of authorities, oral-argument statement, certificates of counsel, signature blocks, certificate of service, and appendices. The filing is presumed compliant at 30 pages (initial and answer) or 15 pages (reply) at 12-point font. | RULES App.R. 19 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-LEN-002 | No word or page limit applies to post-conviction review of a capital case under Crim.R. 42. | RULES App.R. 19 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-LEN-003 | The 30-page and 15-page figures are presumptions of compliance, not caps. The template sets no page limit (no eval.yaml page_limit), because a brief within 9,000 words can run past 30 pages. The word count is not checked by the template. | RULES App.R. 19 (A). | C |
| OHIOCTAPP-COV-001 | If the brief is separately bound, the front cover shows: (1) the name of the court and the case number; (2) the title of the case; (3) the nature of the proceeding (e.g., Appeal) and the name of the court below; (4) the title of the document (e.g., Brief for Appellant); and (5) the names and addresses of counsel for the party filing. | RULES App.R. 19 (A) (1)-(5). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-COV-002 | "ORAL ARGUMENT REQUESTED" displayed prominently on the cover page of the appellant's opening brief or appellee's brief, where the court's local rule requires a request. | RULES App.R. 21 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-CONT-001 | Appellant's brief, in this order with headings: (1) table of contents with page references; (2) table of cases alphabetically arranged, statutes, and other authorities with page references; (3) statement of the assignments of error with reference to the place in the record; (4) statement of the issues presented, with reference to the assignments of error; (5) statement of the case (nature, course of proceedings, disposition below); (6) statement of facts with record references; (7) argument with contentions and reasons for each assignment of error, with authority and record citations; (8) conclusion stating the precise relief sought. A summary may precede the argument. | RULES App.R. 16 (A) (1)-(8). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-CONT-002 | Appellee's brief conforms to App.R. 16 (A) (1)-(8), except that a statement of the case or of facts need not be made unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement. | RULES App.R. 16 (B). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-CONT-003 | Reply brief: the appellant may file one reply brief to the appellee's brief (and to a cross-appeal response, if any). No further briefs without leave. | RULES App.R. 16 (C); App.R. 18 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-CONT-004 | References to the record give the part of the record and page (for example, "Answer p. 7" or "Transcript p. 231"). Evidence whose admissibility is in controversy is cited to the transcript page where it was identified, offered, and received or rejected. | RULES App.R. 16 (D). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-CONT-005 | Authority accessible online is cited with an internet URL where the authority is not in a database. | RULES App.R. 16 (E). | C |
| OHIOCTAPP-TIME-001 | Appellant's brief within 20 days after the clerk mails the notice under App.R. 11 (B); appellee's brief within 20 days after service of appellant's brief; reply within 10 days after service of appellee's brief. | RULES App.R. 18 (A). | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHIOCTAPP-AMC-001 | An amicus brief is filed only with the written consent of all parties, or by leave of court on motion or at the court's request. The brief may be conditionally filed with the motion for leave. The motion identifies the applicant's interest and why the brief is desirable. | RULES App.R. 17. | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-AMC-002 | Unless all parties consent otherwise, an amicus brief is filed within the time allowed the party whose position it supports. | RULES App.R. 17. | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-AMC-003 | App.R. 17 sets no format or length of its own. The amicus brief takes the App.R. 19 form. App.R. 19 (A) names only appellant, appellee and reply briefs for its word limits; the template applies the 9,000-word figure to an amicus brief as the conservative reading. | RULES App.R. 17; App.R. 19 (A). | C |
| OHIOCTAPP-AMC-004 | A motion of an amicus to participate in oral argument is granted only for extraordinary reasons. | RULES App.R. 17. | A |

## Motions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHIOCTAPP-MOT-001 | A motion states with particularity the grounds on which it is based and the order or relief sought. Supporting briefs, affidavits or other papers are served and filed with it. | RULES App.R. 15 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-MOT-002 | A motion for stay or injunction pending appeal is first sought in the trial court where practicable. A motion in the court of appeals for injunction pending appeal need not show that trial-court application is impracticable. | RULES App.R. 7 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-MOT-003 | Responses are due within ten days after service of the motion; replies within seven days after service of the opposition. | RULES App.R. 15 (A). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-MOT-004 | Motions are filed with the clerk, and a single judge may permit filing with the judge who then notes the filing date and transmits it to the clerk. | RULES App.R. 13 (A); App.R. 15 (C). | M |
| OHIOCTAPP-MOT-005 | Three copies of papers relating to motions are filed with the original, unless the court requires more. The court may waive copies under its local e-filing rule. | RULES App.R. 15 (D). | M |
