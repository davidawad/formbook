# Southern District of Ohio: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `ohsd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules of the Southern District of Ohio (amended October 21, 2024; the edition on the court's Local Rules page, dated 2024-10-21) and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHSD-FORM-001 | Papers are on 8½ × 11 inch paper, double-spaced except block-quoted material, with each page numbered consecutively. Side margins are appropriate and the top margin is not less than one inch; electronically filed documents must print with one-inch margins on all sides. These margins match the default eval (1 in). | LR 5.1(a). | M |
| OHSD-FORM-002 | The local rules set no typeface or point-size rule for complaints. The default eval's at-least-12-point body is not mandated by these sources. | LR 5.1(a) (none stated). | A |
| OHSD-FORM-003 | Filed electronically as a searchable text PDF, in the format of the ECF system set out in the most current ECF Manual (the ECF Manual was not retrieved, so its format details are not verified here). | LR 5.1(c). | M |
| OHSD-FORM-004 | The original complaint is exempt from the rule that every pleading carry a title with the filer's name and party designation; the caption carries the case number, with the names of the District Judge and Magistrate Judge placed below it. | LR 5.1(b). | M |
| OHSD-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court, the parties, the case number, and the names of the assigned District Judge and Magistrate Judge below the case number; claims appear in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances, and each claim founded on a separate transaction or occurrence is stated in a separate count where that promotes clarity. | LR 5.1(b); FRCP 10(a)-(b). | M |
| OHSD-CONT-002 | A class-action complaint prominently includes "Class Action" in its title. | LR 23.1. | M |
| OHSD-CONT-003 | A jury demand made in the pleading is noted on the front page, immediately after the title, as "Demand for Jury Trial" or an equivalent statement. | LR 38.1; FRCP 38(b). | M |
| OHSD-CONT-004 | A complaint initiating a civil action is accompanied by a completed civil cover sheet on the Clerk's form (the rule text requires it with the filing; a complaint tendered without one is filed and the filer must then file it promptly). The cover sheet is administrative only, and it identifies any related case. | LR 3.1(a)-(b). | M |
| OHSD-CONT-005 | A nongovernmental corporate party files the FRCP 7.1 disclosure statement and, in addition, discloses publicly held corporations or affiliates not parties with a substantial financial interest from insurance, a franchise, or an indemnity agreement. | FRCP 7.1(a); LR 7.1.1(b). | M |
| OHSD-CERT-001 | One attorney signs, followed by the designation "Trial Attorney," with name, full office address, telephone number with area code, and email address; Ohio Supreme Court registration number follows the name of Ohio counsel. The signer's address, email, and telephone number are also required by FRCP 11(a). | LR 83.5(a); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| OHSD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are limited to the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number. | FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
| OHSD-ELEC-002 | A document may be filed under seal only with leave of court on motion and good cause; ECF filers file sealed documents electronically as provided in LR 5.1. | LR 5.2.1(a). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHSD-FORM-010 | Motions and memoranda use the same page form as complaints: 8½ × 11 inch paper, double-spaced except block quotes, consecutively numbered pages, one-inch margins on all sides (top margin not less than one inch). | LR 5.1(a). | M |
| OHSD-FORM-011 | Typeface and point size are not set by the local rules for briefs; the default eval's at-least-12-point body is not mandated by these sources. | LR 5.1(a) (none stated). | A |
| OHSD-FORM-012 | The caption of a motion or memorandum carries the title identifying the party and the nature of the paper (for example, "Defendant John Smith's Motion to Dismiss"). Exhibits are identified by a cover page or label with the case caption and case number. | LR 5.1(b); LR 5.1(a) (exhibits). | M |
| OHSD-FORM-013 | "ORAL ARGUMENT REQUESTED" (or its equivalent) on the caption of a motion or memorandum requests oral argument; the grounds are explained succinctly. | LR 7.1(b)(2). | M |
| OHSD-CONT-006 | Every motion is accompanied by a memorandum in support that is a brief statement of the grounds with citation of authorities, and by a certificate of service. | LR 7.2(a)(1). | M |
| OHSD-LEN-001 | The Court prefers memoranda in support of or in opposition to a motion not exceed twenty pages. If a memorandum exceeds twenty pages, it includes a combined table of contents and summary not to exceed five pages. A judge may set page limits by standing order. | LR 7.2(a)(3). | M |
| OHSD-CERT-002 | Signature block as for complaints: one attorney, "Trial Attorney" designation, name, full office address, telephone with area code, email, and Ohio registration number for Ohio counsel. | LR 83.5(a); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| OHSD-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers are removed from the brief and its exhibits, subject to the FRCP 5.2(a) limits. | FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
| OHSD-ELEC-004 | Exhibits are not bound when scanned and uploaded to ECF; paper exhibits are reduced or folded to 8½ × 11 inches where possible. | LR 5.1(a). | M |
