## Form, caption and signature (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHND-FORM-001 | Use 8½ × 11 inch white pages, double-spaced except quoted material; number pages consecutively. The first page has a 1½ inch top margin, with the court title centered below it. | LR 10.1, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| OHND-SIG-001 | Signature carries typed name, address, telephone, fax, email and Ohio bar registration number, if applicable. | LR 10.1, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| OHND-CAP-001 | Caption includes court name, action title, file number and Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a), verified 2026-10-17 | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHND-CONT-001 | State jurisdiction, a short and plain claim, and demand for relief; claims use numbered paragraphs limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 8(a), 10(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| OHND-CONT-002 | A jury demand may be included in a pleading and must be served and filed within the time allowed by Rule 38(b). | FRCP 38(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| OHND-CERT-001 | File a civil cover sheet with a new civil case; nongovernmental corporate parties file a disclosure statement with their first filing. | LR 3.13(a)–(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHND-MOT-001 | A request for a court order is made by written motion stating grounds with particularity and requested relief; the moving party files a supporting points-and-authorities memorandum with the motion. | FRCP 7(b)(1); LR 7.1(b)–(c), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| OHND-LEN-001 | Dispositive memoranda are subject to case-track limits: 10 pages expedited, 20 administrative/standard/unassigned, 30 complex, 40 mass tort; other motion memoranda are limited to 15 pages. Memoranda over 15 pages, except Social Security reviews, include contents, authorities, issues and an argument summary. | LR 7.1(f), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| OHND-CERT-002 | A dispositive memorandum includes a certification naming the assigned case track, if any, and certifying page-limit compliance or compliance with any modified limit set by order. | LR 7.1(f), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
