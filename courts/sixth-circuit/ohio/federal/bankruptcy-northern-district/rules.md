# Northern District of Ohio Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `ohnb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules (cover page: revised May 19, 2022), the Electronic Case Filing Administrative Procedures Manual (latest revision August 11, 2021), and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHNB-FORM-001 | The caption states the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document; it must substantially conform to Official Form 416. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010; OF Form 416. | M |
| OHNB-FORM-002 | Form of pleading otherwise follows FRCP 10: numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (applying FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| OHNB-FORM-003 | A paper filing is printed or typewritten on 8½ × 11 inch white paper, on one side of each sheet only (no duplex printing), and not pre-punched. | LBR 5005-1(a)(1)-(3); APM III.B. | M |
| OHNB-FORM-004 | The LBR set no typeface, point size, line spacing, or margin for a complaint; the 12-point and 20-page limits of LBR 9013-2 apply to motions and responses only. The default eval (1 in margins, at least 12 pt body) is not mandated by these sources. | LBR 9013-2(a) (scope); LBR 5005-1(a) (none stated beyond). | A |
| OHNB-CONT-001 | An Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet in the form prescribed by the Administrative Office must be completed and filed with each complaint that is not filed electronically. | LBR 7003-1; OF (adversary cover sheet). | M |
| OHNB-CONT-002 | A nongovernmental corporate party to an adversary proceeding files a corporate ownership statement identifying any parent and any 10%-or-more public owner, or stating there is none, with its first appearance or pleading. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b). | M |
| OHNB-CERT-001 | Signature block shows the attorney's typewritten name, firm affiliation (if any), address, telephone number, facsimile number, e-mail address, and Bar Registration Number; an electronically filed document shows the signature as "s/name." The signing attorney's name is in the attorney's individual name. | LBR 5005-1(c); FRBP 9011(a); FRCP 11(a) (e-mail address, telephone). | M |
| OHNB-ELEC-001 | Documents are filed electronically through ECF in PDF, unless otherwise authorized, under the APM; documents that form part of one pleading and are filed at the same time by the same party are filed together under one docket number, with the memorandum of law filed separately as a related document. | APM II.A.3, II.B.1. | M |
| OHNB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are protected under FRBP 9037(a): only the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number may appear. | FRBP 9037(a). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHNB-FORM-010 | A motion or response, including written argument and cited authorities, may not exceed 20 pages, exclusive of appendices, unless the party first obtains leave; if leave is granted, a table of contents summarizing all points raised is included. | LBR 9013-2(a). | M |
| OHNB-FORM-011 | Font size, including footnotes, is at least 12 point. (Matches the default eval's 12 pt body; footnotes are included.) | LBR 9013-2(a). | M |
| OHNB-FORM-012 | The LBR set no typeface, line spacing, or margin for motions and briefs; the default eval's 1-inch margins and double spacing are not mandated by these sources. | LBR 9013-2(a) (none stated beyond). | A |
| OHNB-FORM-013 | Paper filings are on 8½ × 11 inch white paper, one side only, not pre-punched. | LBR 5005-1(a); APM III.B. | M |
| OHNB-CONT-003 | A motion tendered for filing is accompanied by a certificate of service (LBR 9013-3) and a notice that any objection must be filed within 14 days (or other time specified by rule or order) from the date of service shown on the certificate, and that the court may grant the relief without further notice absent a timely objection. | LBR 9013-1(a). | M |
| OHNB-CONT-004 | Certificate of service is signed and (1) identifies the document served with specificity, (2) states the date and method of service, (3) identifies each entity served by name and address, and (4) contains or refers to the accompanying notice required by LBR 9013-1(a). | LBR 9013-3(1)-(4). | M |
| OHNB-CONT-005 | A response must specifically designate the motion or application to which it responds and is filed within 14 days (or other time required by the rules or order). | LBR 9013-1(b). | M |
| OHNB-CONT-006 | A memorandum of law is filed separately from the motion and shown as a related document to the motion in ECF. | APM II.A.3. | M |
| OHNB-ELEC-003 | Supporting documentary evidence and photographs relied on, in addition to the affidavits required by the Bankruptcy Rules, are served and filed with the motion, opposition, or reply. | LBR 9013-2(b). | M |
| OHNB-ELEC-004 | Documents are filed electronically in PDF, with the attorney signature shown as "s/name"; the signature block contains the items of OHNB-CERT-001. | APM II.B.1; LBR 5005-1(c). | M |
| OHNB-ELEC-005 | A request to redact personal identifiers in a filing uses the Clerk's standardized form Order Granting Motion to Redact Personal Identifiers, with Redacted Filing; any deviation from the form is explained in bold-faced type in the body of the document; the movant appends the entire redacted document to the proposed order. | LBR 9037-1(a)-(c); FRBP 9037(a). | M |
