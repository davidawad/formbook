# Southern District of Ohio Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `ohsb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules, Forms and Procedures (clean copy, amendments effective October 1, 2020), the court's Administrative Procedures for Electronic Case Filing (effective December 1, 2016), and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHSB-FORM-001 | Filings are typewritten or printed legibly on 8½ × 11 inch bond paper or electronic equivalent, and are double spaced, with side margins and a top margin of not less than one inch. The bottom margin is not stated. | LBR 9004-1(b). | M |
| OHSB-FORM-002 | Typed print is "at least 10-12 font size" as printed in LBR 9004-1(b). The rule does not state one point size, so the default eval's 12 pt body is not confirmed by this text; the lower bound printed is 10 pt. Ambiguous as printed; the template size is not fixed by these sources. | LBR 9004-1(b). | M |
| OHSB-FORM-003 | Caption: the debtor's name, case number, chapter under which the case is filed, adversary proceeding number (if any), name of the judge to whom the case is assigned, a specific statement of the nature of the pleading, and the specific relief requested. The caption must substantially conform to Official Form 416. | LBR 9004-2; FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010; OF Form 416. | M |
| OHSB-FORM-004 | Form of pleading otherwise follows FRCP 10: numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (applying FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| OHSB-FORM-005 | No rule in the LBR or ECF Procedures sets a typeface, line spacing beyond double spacing, or a bottom margin for a complaint; no page limit applies to complaints (LBR 9013-2 limits briefs and memoranda only). | LBR 9004-1(b); LBR 9013-2(a) (scope). | A |
| OHSB-CONT-001 | An Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet in the form prescribed by the Administrative Office, available through the clerk, must be completed and filed with each complaint that is not filed electronically. | LBR 7003-1; OF (adversary cover sheet). | M |
| OHSB-CONT-002 | A nongovernmental corporate party to an adversary proceeding files a corporate ownership statement identifying any parent and any 10%-or-more public owner, or stating there is none, with its first appearance or pleading. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b). | M |
| OHSB-CERT-001 | Every pleading or other paper filed by an attorney shows the attorney's name, mailing address, telephone number, fax number, e-mail address, and state bar number under the signature line. An ECF filing shows a "/s/ Name" signature line with complete address, telephone, fax, e-mail, state bar registration number, and firm affiliation (if any). | LBR 5005-1(c); ECF Procedure 8(d)(1); FRBP 9011(a); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| OHSB-ELEC-001 | Papers required to be filed are filed with the clerk under the ECF Procedures. Documents are Electronically Generated Text in PDF so the text can be searched and copied (scanned material only as ECF Procedure 5(c) allows). Each transmission may not exceed 35 megabytes; larger files are split. | LBR 5005-1(a); ECF Procedure 5(b), 5(c), 5(d). | M |
| OHSB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are protected under FRBP 9037(a) and ECF Procedure 13: only the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number may appear, in the filing and its exhibits. The filer, not the court, is responsible for redaction. | FRBP 9037(a); ECF Procedure 13(a)-(d); LBR 9037-1. | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHSB-FORM-010 | Motions, memoranda, and other papers follow the paper-and-form rule: 8½ × 11 inch bond paper or electronic equivalent, double spaced, side margins and top margin of not less than one inch, and typed print at the size stated in OHSB-FORM-002 (the rule text reads "at least 10-12 font size"). | LBR 9004-1(b). | M |
| OHSB-FORM-011 | No rule in the sources sets a typeface, footnote size, or bottom margin for briefs; the default eval's 12 pt body and 1-inch bottom margin are not mandated by these sources. | LBR 9004-1(b); LBR 9013-2(a) (none stated beyond). | A |
| OHSB-FORM-012 | Caption for a motion or response meets the requirements of OHSB-FORM-003; the caption states the specific relief requested and is phrased so that its relationship to prior filings is explicit. | LBR 9004-2; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| OHSB-LEN-001 | Briefs and memoranda may not exceed 20 pages except on leave of court for good cause; when leave is granted, the brief includes a table of contents summarizing all points raised. Exhibits, including discovery documents, are limited to those referred to in the brief or memorandum. | LBR 9013-2(a). | M |
| OHSB-CONT-003 | A motion or application is accompanied by (A) a memorandum in support, (B) a certificate of service under LBR 9013-3 (except ex parte relief or clerk-noticed motions), and (C) a 21-day notice (substantially in the sample form or Official Form 420A), except where a federal rule, General Order #12, another local rule, or court order provides otherwise. | LBR 9013-1(a)(1)(A)-(C), (a)(2). | M |
| OHSB-CONT-004 | Certificate of service is signed, subject to ECF Procedure 8, identifies the filing served with specificity, states the date and method of service, and identifies each entity served by name and address; an ECF-served filing may state that all ECF participants registered in the case were served electronically at the email address registered with the court. | LBR 9013-3(e)(1)-(4)(ii); LBR 9013-3(f) (exceptions). | M |
| OHSB-CONT-005 | If unreported or unofficially published opinions are cited, copies are made available on request by the court or the opposing attorney. | LBR 9013-2(b). | M |
| OHSB-CERT-002 | Signature block as in OHSB-CERT-001 (LBR 5005-1(c); ECF Procedure 8(d)(1)). | LBR 5005-1(c); ECF Procedure 8(d)(1). | M |
| OHSB-ELEC-003 | Filed electronically under the ECF Procedures as Electronically Generated Text in PDF; no transmission over 35 megabytes. | LBR 5005-1(a); ECF Procedure 5(b)-(d). | M |
| OHSB-ELEC-004 | Personal identifiers in the motion, brief, and exhibits are protected under FRBP 9037(a) and ECF Procedure 13; the filer is responsible for redaction. | FRBP 9037(a); ECF Procedure 13; LBR 9037-1. | M |
