## Form and length (opening, answering and reply briefs)

| ID | Requirement | Primary source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP6-FORM-001 | Use 8½ × 11 inch pages, margins of at least one inch, and double spacing, except quotations, headings, footnotes, and certificates of compliance may be single-spaced. | FRBP 8015(a)(4); BAP LBR 8014-1(d), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-FORM-002 | Use a proportionally spaced serif typeface of at least 14 points, or a monospaced typeface of at least 12 points and no more than 10½ characters per inch. Case names in the body and footnotes are italic or underlined. | FRBP 8015(a)(5)(A), (a)(6), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-COVER-001 | Include case number centered at the top, court, case title, nature of proceeding and court below, brief title identifying the party, and counsel name, address and telephone. | FRBP 8015(a)(2), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-CONT-001 | Include a table of contents with page references and a table of authorities with cases alphabetized and other authorities organized by type. | FRBP 8014(a)(2)–(3), (c), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-CONT-002 | Opening brief includes jurisdictional statement, issues, case, summary, argument and conclusion. Answering brief includes summary, argument and conclusion; it may omit items it accepts from the appellant's brief. Reply is limited to reply argument. | FRBP 8014(a), (b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-CONT-003 | A statement regarding oral argument may not exceed one page and follows the table of authorities. | FRBP 8019(a); BAP LBR 8014-1(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-LEN-001 | Principal briefs are limited to 13,000 words; reply briefs are limited to 6,500 words. | FRBP 8015(a)(7)(B), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-CERT-001 | Include a certificate of compliance stating word count and typeface compliance, signed by counsel or the self-represented party. | FRBP 8015(h), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP6-DISC-001 | Include corporate ownership disclosure required by Rule 8012 in the opening brief, or in an earlier filing if the rule allows. | FRBP 8012; 8014(a)(1), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
