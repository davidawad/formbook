# Western District of Tennessee Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `tnwb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules of the Western District of Tennessee (effective September 24, 2026), the Electronic Case Filing Guidelines (as of April 3, 2017), which supplement the local rules under LBR 1001-1(b), and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNWB-FORM-001 | Papers are clearly legible. | FRBP 9004(a). | M |
| TNWB-FORM-002 | The local rules set no paper-size, margin, typeface, point-size, or line-spacing rule for complaints. The default eval's one-inch margins and at-least-12-point body are not mandated by these sources. | LBR (none stated); ECF (none stated). | A |
| TNWB-FORM-003 | The Court requests that PDFs use Arial, Courier, Helvetica, Time, Times New Roman, or Symbol (or close approximations). This is a request, not a requirement. | ECF Guideline 22(C). | A |
| TNWB-FORM-004 | Each PDF is no more than 15 MB and 40 pages; larger files are rejected automatically at filing. | ECF Guideline 22(A); LBR 1001-1(b). | M |
| TNWB-FORM-005 | Caption gives the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document; the caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416. | FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010. | M |
| TNWB-CONT-001 | The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to the entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court, with the jurisdictional allegation naming the underlying case and its number and Code chapter, and the district and division. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| TNWB-CONT-002 | Adversary complaints, summonses, and returns of service are filed electronically. A new adversary proceeding opened with a new bankruptcy case requires a judge to be assigned to the associated case first. | ECF Guideline 20. | M |
| TNWB-CONT-003 | A nongovernmental corporation that is a party files the corporate ownership statement (parent corporation and 10% public holders, or a statement that there is none) with its first appearance or pleading. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b). | M |
| TNWB-CERT-001 | At least one attorney of record signs, and the document includes the attorney's address, zip code, telephone number, email address, fax number if any, and state bar disciplinary number. A conformed "/s/" signature is used for electronic signing; digital signatures are not permitted. | LBR 1001-3(h); ECF Guideline 5; ECF Guideline 22(D). | M |
| TNWB-CERT-002 | The signer's name, address, and telephone number are stated on the paper. | FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| TNWB-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are limited to the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number. | FRBP 9037(a). | M |
| TNWB-ELEC-002 | A PDF other than Official Form 121 shows only the first five digits of a Social Security number as redacted; the last four digits are the only digits visible on the docket. | ECF Guideline 13. | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNWB-FORM-010 | Motions and briefs are clearly legible. | FRBP 9004(a). | M |
| TNWB-FORM-011 | The local rules set no paper-size, margin, typeface, point-size, or line-spacing rule for briefs; the default eval's one-inch margins and at-least-12-point body are not mandated by these sources. | LBR (none stated); ECF (none stated). | A |
| TNWB-FORM-012 | The Court requests the same PDF font list as for complaints (ECF Guideline 22(C)); this is a request, not a requirement. | ECF Guideline 22(C). | A |
| TNWB-CONT-004 | A written motion states its grounds with particularity and sets forth the relief requested. | FRBP 9013(a)-(b); LBR 9013-1(a)-(b). | M |
| TNWB-CONT-005 | A motion, application, or objection is submitted through ECF with a conformed signature of the attorney for the movant (or of the unrepresented moving party). | ECF Guideline 19(A). | M |
| TNWB-CONT-006 | A memorandum of facts and law should not exceed twenty-five pages without prior approval of the court. It is served on the parties, their counsel, any trustee, and the United States Trustee. | LBR 7016-1(a). | M |
| TNWB-CONT-007 | Each cited opinion is retrievable through Westlaw or Lexis, or a copy of the cited case is attached. | LBR 7016-1(b). | M |
| TNWB-CONT-008 | A motion for relief from stay files its exhibits in PDF; if not feasible, the exhibits are summarized and the motion says where copies may be obtained. | LBR 4001-1(b); ECF Guideline 22(A). | M |
| TNWB-CONT-009 | If no objection is timely filed to a motion, the moving party may submit a proposed order with a certificate that the docket was reviewed and no objection was filed (Local Form 008F). | LBR 9013-1(b)(2). | M |
| TNWB-CERT-003 | Service: the Notice of Electronic Filing is the certificate of service for parties who have consented in writing to electronic service; other parties are served by the means FRBP 7004 and 9013 require. | ECF Guideline 10; ECF Guideline 19(B); FRBP 9013(b). | M |
| TNWB-CERT-004 | Signature block as for complaints: at least one attorney of record, with address, zip code, telephone, email, and state bar disciplinary number, and a conformed "/s/" signature. | LBR 1001-3(h); ECF Guideline 5. | M |
| TNWB-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers in the brief and its exhibits are limited as in TNWB-ELEC-001; a Social Security number follows TNWB-ELEC-002. | FRBP 9037(a); ECF Guideline 13. | M |
