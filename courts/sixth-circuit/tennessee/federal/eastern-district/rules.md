# Eastern District of Tennessee rules

Primary sources checked 2026-10-14. Re-check every six months and before changing a template. Sources: [E.D. Tenn. Local Rules](https://www.tned.uscourts.gov/local-rules), revised April 8, 2026 (LR 5.1); FRCP 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 38, https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-civil-procedure.

Kinds: **M** mandatory; **C** convention; **T** template contract; **A** author responsibility.

## Form and caption (complaints and motion briefs)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNED-FORM-001 | Use 8½ × 11 inch white pages, one-inch margins, double spacing except indented single-spaced quotations and single-spaced headings and footnotes; type no smaller than 12 points, including footnotes; number pages consecutively at bottom. | LR 5.1, Local Rules revised 2026-04-08, p. 11; verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNED-SIG-001 | The filing attorney's name, address, telephone number and BPR number appear in the signature block; pleadings and motions are signed. | LR 5.1; FRCP 11(a); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNED-CAP-001 | Pleading captions state the court, title of the action, file number and pleading designation. | FRCP 10(a); verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNED-CONT-001 | State jurisdiction, a short and plain claim, and demand for relief; present claims in numbered paragraphs and separate counts where clarity requires. | FRCP 8(a), 10(b); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNED-CONT-002 | A jury demand may be included in the complaint and must be served no later than 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue. | FRCP 38(b)(1); verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNED-MOT-001 | A request for a court order is made by written motion stating its grounds with particularity and the relief sought. | FRCP 7(b)(1); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNED-MOT-002 | A Rule 12 motion states the applicable defense and is filed within the timing prescribed by Rule 12. | FRCP 12(b), (a); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNED-SERV-001 | No certificate of service is required for a paper served through CM/ECF; include service details when service occurs outside the electronic system. | FRCP 5(b)(2)(E), 5(d)(1)(B); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
