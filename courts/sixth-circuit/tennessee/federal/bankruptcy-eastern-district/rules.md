# Eastern District of Tennessee Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `tneb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules of the Eastern District of Tennessee Bankruptcy Court (as amended December 1, 2024; the proposed 2026 amendments under General Order 2026-01 are not in force and are not applied) and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNEB-FORM-001 | Papers are 8½ × 11 inches, typed or legibly printed, double-spaced (except official forms and quoted material), and paginated consecutively at the bottom beginning with the second page. The first page is not numbered. | LR 9004-1(a). | M |
| TNEB-FORM-002 | The local rules and FRBP set no typeface, point size, margin, or line-spacing rule beyond double spacing. The default eval's one-inch margins and at-least-12-point body are not mandated by these sources. | LR 9004-1(a) (none stated). | A |
| TNEB-FORM-003 | Caption identifies the chapter, the division in which the adversary proceeding is pending, and the adversary proceeding number, including the initials of the judge assigned; the caption also includes the court's name, the case title, the case number, and a brief designation of the document. The caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416. | LR 9004-2(a); FRBP 9004(b); FRBP 7010. | M |
| TNEB-FORM-004 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances, with separate counts for claims founded on separate transactions where that promotes clarity. | FRBP 7010 (applying FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| TNEB-CONT-001 | The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to the entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court, and states the jurisdictional allegation with the name, number, and Code chapter of the underlying case and the district and division. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| TNEB-CONT-002 | An Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet (Bankruptcy Form B 1040) is completed and filed with each complaint that is not filed electronically. | LR 7003-1. | M |
| TNEB-CONT-003 | A nongovernmental corporation that is a party to the adversary proceeding files the corporate ownership statement (parent corporation and 10% public holders, or a statement that there is none) with its first appearance or pleading. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b). | M |
| TNEB-CERT-001 | Signed by an attorney or party, with typed name, mailing address, and telephone number; the attorney's bar number, and the state of issue if other than Tennessee, should also be included. | FRBP 9011(a); LR 9011-4. | M |
| TNEB-ELEC-001 | Filed in PDF through ECF. Imaged (scanned) documents are avoided where possible, and PDFs should comply with Adobe accessibility guidelines. | LR 5005-4(e). | M |
| TNEB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers are limited to the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number. | FRBP 9037(a). | M |
| TNEB-ELEC-003 | An electronically filed document carries the Registered User's signature, either an image or an "s/" followed by the typed name in the signature block. Handwritten signatures are required on affidavits, declarations, and verified documents. | LR 5005-4(h)(1)-(2). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNEB-FORM-010 | Motions and briefs use the same form as complaints: 8½ × 11 inches, double-spaced except official forms and quoted material, paginated from the second page. | LR 9004-1(a). | M |
| TNEB-FORM-011 | Typeface, point size, and margins are not set by these sources; the default eval's one-inch margins and at-least-12-point body are not mandated. | LR 9004-1(a) (none stated). | A |
| TNEB-FORM-012 | Exhibits are numbered prominently with a number or letter, numbered consecutively where a specific page is referenced, and not two-sided. An exhibit filed as a separate docket entry carries a cover sheet with the case caption and a description of the paper it supports. | LR 9004-1(b); LR 9004-2(b). | M |
| TNEB-CONT-004 | A motion in an adversary proceeding is accompanied by a brief setting forth the facts and law supporting it, unless excepted in LR 7007-1(b). | LR 7007-1(a)-(b). | M |
| TNEB-CONT-005 | A motion is in writing, titled (for example, "Motion of [Movant] for [Relief]"), states its grounds with particularity, and requests specific relief. | LR 9013-1(b)(1)-(4) (contested matters); FRBP 9013(b); LR 7007-1 governs motions in adversary proceedings. | M |
| TNEB-CONT-006 | A proposed order granting the requested relief, approved for entry by the movant, is served with the motion. | LR 7007-1(c); LR 9013-1(c). | M |
| TNEB-CONT-007 | Each motion or paper served on parties in interest has a certificate of service describing the paper, and for each person served the name, service address, manner of service, and date of service. An order limiting notices is referenced by date and document number when relied on. | LR 9013-3(a)-(c). | M |
| TNEB-CERT-002 | Signature block as for complaints: typed name, mailing address, telephone, and the attorney's bar number. | LR 9011-4; FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| TNEB-LEN-001 | The local rules state no page limit for briefs or memoranda in adversary proceedings or contested matters. | LR 7007-1; LR 9004-1 (none stated). | A |
| TNEB-ELEC-004 | Filed in PDF through ECF; personal identifiers limited as in TNEB-ELEC-002. | LR 5005-4(e); FRBP 9037(a). | M |
