# Middle District of Tennessee Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `tnmb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules of Court, U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Middle District of Tennessee (amended and restated as of July 28, 2025), the Administrative Procedures for Electronic Case Filing (August 10, 2026 revision), and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNMB-FORM-001 | All filings are 8½ × 11 inches and clearly legible in 12-point font with one-inch margins throughout the document. This matches the default eval's margin and body size; no typeface or footnote size is stated. | LBR 5005-3(a); ECF 8.5. | M |
| TNMB-FORM-002 | Every page is numbered sequentially at the bottom, with room for the CM/ECF docket entry footer. | LBR 5005-3(b); ECF 8.5. | M |
| TNMB-FORM-003 | The local rules set no typeface, line-spacing, or footnote-size rule for complaints. The default eval's typeface and double spacing are not mandated by these sources. | LBR 5005-3(a) (none stated). | A |
| TNMB-FORM-004 | Filed in PDF; the PDF meets the PDF-A profile standards. | LBR 5005-4(c); ECF 8.5. | M |
| TNMB-CONT-001 | An Adversary Cover Sheet in the form prescribed by the Administrative Office is completed and filed with each complaint filed in paper format. The rule text does not require it for an electronically filed complaint. | LBR 7003-1. | M |
| TNMB-CONT-002 | Caption includes the bankruptcy case number, the chapter of the case, the name of the judge assigned, a title describing the content of the document, and the adversary proceeding number. | LBR 9004-2(a)(1), (b). | M |
| TNMB-CONT-003 | Caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416 for an adversary complaint; the pleading otherwise follows FRCP 10. | FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| TNMB-CONT-004 | The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to the entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court, with the jurisdictional allegation naming the underlying case, its number and Code chapter, and the district and division. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| TNMB-CONT-005 | A nongovernmental corporate party to the adversary proceeding files the corporate ownership statement with its first appearance or pleading. (LBR 7007.1-1 extends FRBP 7007.1 to contested matters; the adversary requirement comes from the rule itself.) | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b); LBR 7007.1-1. | M |
| TNMB-CERT-001 | Electronic signature block is "/s/ [name]," placed where the signature normally appears, with the filer's name, bar number, telephone, e-mail, and firm affiliation. Scanned or embedded signatures are not permitted. The signer's address and telephone are required by FRBP 9011(a). | ECF 5.1, 5.3; FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| TNMB-ELEC-001 | The filer redacts personal identifiers before filing; the clerk does not redact. Only the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number may appear. | ECF 12.1; FRBP 9037(a). | M |
| TNMB-ELEC-002 | Practitioners' Handbook section 4.7 describes motions to restrict personally identifiable information; the movant attaches a redacted document for the clerk to file. | HB 4.7 (guidance); FRBP 9037. | A |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNMB-FORM-010 | Motions and briefs are subject to the same paper-size, legibility, 12-point, one-inch-margin, and page-numbering rules as complaints. | LBR 5005-3(a)-(b). | M |
| TNMB-FORM-011 | The local rules set no typeface or line-spacing rule for briefs; the default eval's typeface and double spacing are not mandated. | LBR 5005-3(a) (none stated). | A |
| TNMB-CONT-006 | A motion is made by written motion (LBR 9013-1). Under the Motion Practice Procedures, a motion is filed as one PDF in the order: LBR 9013-1 Notice, the motion, then the proposed order, unless size requires otherwise. The notice and the motion captions match. | LBR 9013-1; MPP (1), Motion Caption. | M |
| TNMB-CONT-007 | A brief or memorandum of law may be submitted no later than noon on the second business day before a scheduled hearing. Each cited document is retrievable through Westlaw or Lexis, or a copy is provided. | LBR 9013-2(a)-(b). | M |
| TNMB-CONT-008 | A proposed order accompanies the motion (as the attachment described in MPP (1)). The proposed order has a four-inch margin at the top of the first page and one-inch margins elsewhere, with room at the bottom for the CM/ECF footer. | LBR 5005-3(c); LBR 5005-4(d); ECF 8.5 (which cross-refers to ECF 7.5 for proposed orders). | M |
| TNMB-CERT-002 | A certificate of service is filed within 3 business days after service and states the manner of service and the names and addresses of all parties served; a copy of the papers served is attached. The certificate is filed in CM/ECF or accompanies the notice, motion, and proposed order. | LBR 9013-3(a)-(b); MPP "Certificate of Service". | M |
| TNMB-CERT-003 | Signature block as for complaints: electronic "/s/" signature block with name, bar number, telephone, e-mail, and firm affiliation. | ECF 5.3; FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| TNMB-LEN-001 | The local rules state no page limit for briefs or memoranda; none is imposed by these sources. | LBR (none stated). | A |
| TNMB-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers in the brief and its exhibits are limited as in TNMB-ELEC-001. | ECF 12.1; FRBP 9037(a). | M |
| TNMB-CONT-009 | Corporate ownership statement for a contested matter: FRBP 7007.1 applies to contested matters under FRBP 9014. | LBR 7007.1-1; FRBP 7007.1. | M |
