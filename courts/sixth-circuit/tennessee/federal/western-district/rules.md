# Western District of Tennessee: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `tnwd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules of the Western District of Tennessee (revised November 17, 2025, with the ECF Policies and Procedures appended) and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNWD-FORM-001 | Papers are on 8½ × 11 inch white paper, typewritten, with one-inch margins on all sides. This matches the default eval's margins. | LR 7.1(a). | M |
| TNWD-FORM-002 | Lines are double-spaced; quotations may be indented and single-spaced, and headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. Font size, for footnotes as well as the body, is no smaller than 12 point. This matches the default eval's at-least-12-point body. No typeface is specified. | LR 7.1(b). | M |
| TNWD-FORM-003 | Page limits in the local rules do not count captions, signature lines, or certificates of service and consultation. | LR 7.1(d). | M |
| TNWD-CONT-001 | Caption has a title, a file number, and a Rule 7(a) designation, and the complaint's title names all parties. Claims are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(a)-(b). | M |
| TNWD-CONT-002 | A civil cover sheet, on Form JS-44, accompanies every complaint or other document initiating a civil action, except a pro se complaint. Section VIII identifies related cases. | LR 3.1. | M |
| TNWD-CONT-003 | A jury demand made in the complaint is contained in its last paragraph, and the phrase "JURY DEMAND" appears immediately opposite the case style on the first page and on all subsequent filings. | LR 7.1(c); FRCP 38(b). | M |
| TNWD-CONT-004 | A complaint seeking to assert a class claim bears the legend "Complaint – Class Action" next to its caption. | LR 23.1. | M |
| TNWD-CONT-005 | A nongovernmental corporate party files the FRCP 7.1 disclosure statement with its first appearance. | FRCP 7.1(a)(1), (b). | M |
| TNWD-CERT-001 | Signature block: a "s/" typed before the filing user's name, the attorney bar number, firm name, street address, city, state, zip, telephone number, and primary email address. The papers also state the attorney's Board of Professional Responsibility number. | LR 11.1(a)-(b); LR 7.1(a). | M |
| TNWD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are limited to the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, the last four digits of a financial-account number, and the city and state of a home address. | ECF Policies 13.4.2; FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
| TNWD-ELEC-002 | Bookmarks to locations within the same document are permitted; hyperlinks to other documents or external sites are not permitted. | ECF Policies 7.4. | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNWD-FORM-010 | Motions and briefs follow the same paper form as complaints: 8½ × 11 inches, one-inch margins, double-spaced lines, and at least 12 point type for body and footnotes. | LR 7.1(a)-(b). | M |
| TNWD-FORM-011 | No typeface is specified; the default eval's typeface is not mandated. | LR 7.1(b) (none stated). | A |
| TNWD-CONT-006 | The Clerk accepts a motion in a civil case only if it is accompanied by a supporting memorandum of facts and law, so identified. | LR 7.2(a)(1). | M |
| TNWD-CONT-007 | A motion (except under FRCP 12, 56, 59, or 60) is accompanied by a proposed order in word-processing format, sent to the ECF mailbox for the presiding judge only. | LR 7.2(a)(1)(A). | M |
| TNWD-CONT-008 | A motion (except under FRCP 12, 56, 59, or 60) is accompanied by a certificate of counsel affirming consultation between the parties, with the names of participating counsel and the date and manner of consultation. | LR 7.2(a)(1)(B). | M |
| TNWD-CONT-009 | A response to a motion, with its supporting memorandum, is filed within 14 days after service and accompanied by a proposed order in word-processing format sent to the ECF mailbox for the judge only (unless the motion is under FRCP 12(b)-(c) or 56; see LR 12.1(b)). | LR 7.2(a)(2). | M |
| TNWD-LEN-001 | Memoranda in support of and in opposition to a motion do not exceed twenty pages, and a reply memorandum (if permitted) does not exceed five pages, unless the Court orders otherwise. | LR 7.2(e). | M |
| TNWD-LEN-002 | A memorandum supporting or opposing a motion to dismiss under FRCP 12(b) or (c) does not exceed twenty pages; a reply by the moving party does not exceed ten pages. | LR 12.1(a)-(c). | M |
| TNWD-LEN-003 | A memorandum supporting a motion for summary judgment does not exceed twenty pages, and the separate statement of material facts does not exceed ten pages, without prior Court approval. | LR 56.1. | M |
| TNWD-CERT-002 | Signature block as for complaints: "s/" before the filing user's name, bar number, firm, street address, city, state, zip, telephone, and primary email. | LR 11.1(b); LR 7.1(a). | M |
| TNWD-CERT-003 | A certificate of service is not required when the paper is served by filing it through the ECF system; a paper served by other means needs a certificate filed with it or within a reasonable time after service. | FRCP 5(d)(1)(B). | M |
| TNWD-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers in the brief and its exhibits are limited as in TNWD-ELEC-001; hyperlinks are not permitted as in TNWD-ELEC-002. | ECF Policies 13.4.2, 7.4; FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
