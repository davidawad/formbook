## Form and caption

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNMD-FORM-001 | Use 8½ × 11 inch pages, one-inch margins, and at least 12-point type. Material other than headings, quotations and footnotes is double-spaced; footnotes are single-spaced. Pages are numbered at the bottom. | LR 7.03(a), M.D. Tenn. Local Rules (2025), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNMD-CAP-001 | Caption includes court, title, file number and judges; judge names are placed below the case number on filings after the initial filing. | FRCP 10(a); LR 7.03(a); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNMD-SIG-001 | Signature line has typed or printed names beneath the signature. | LR 7.03(a); FRCP 11(a); verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNMD-CONT-001 | State jurisdiction, a short and plain statement of the claim, and the relief sought; claims use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 8(a), 10(b); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNMD-CONT-002 | If the complaint makes a jury demand, place “JURY DEMAND” opposite the case style on the first page. | LR 7.03(c); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNMD-CERT-001 | A civil cover sheet (JS-44) accompanies a complaint where required; it is a separate filing. | LR 3.03; verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNMD-MOT-001 | A motion requiring a determination of law must be accompanied by a memorandum citing authorities. A memorandum incorporated into a motion is titled “MOTION AND MEMORANDUM OF LAW.” | LR 7.01(a)(2); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNMD-LEN-001 | A supporting memorandum may not exceed 25 pages without leave. | LR 7.01(a)(2); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| TNMD-SERV-001 | A certificate of service identifying person served, document, method and date is included when service is not through CM/ECF. | LR 5.01; verified 2026-10-14 | M |
