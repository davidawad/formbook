# Court of Appeals of Tennessee: briefs, applications for permission to appeal, and amicus briefs

Alias `tennctapp`. Scope: appellant's, appellee's and reply briefs, amicus curiae briefs, and applications for interlocutory appeal by permission under Tenn. R. App. P. 9 (the Court of Appeals' discretionary vehicle; the Rule 11 application to the Supreme Court is filed in that court). Verified against Tenn. R. App. P. 9, 20, 27, 29, 30 and 31 and Tenn. Ct. App. R. 6 (tncourts.gov rule pages as captured by the Internet Archive) and Tenn. Sup. Ct. R. 46 (Electronic Filing, amended July 7, 2022) on 2026-10-21.

Filings are e-filed through the appellate courts' e-filing system (Tenn. Sup. Ct. R. 46). Rule 46 section 3.02(a) makes its format requirements govern over the paper-format rules where they conflict, so the e-filed PDF follows Rule 46 section 3.02 for typeface, size, spacing and length. Rule 30(a)'s paper figures (Times New Roman 12-point, double spacing) apply only to paper filings and are not used.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.tncourts.gov/rules/rules-appellate-procedure and https://www.tncourts.gov/courts/supreme-court/rules/supreme-court-rules/rule-46-electronic-filing returned a browser-check page (HTTP 200, no rule text) on 2026-10-21; the rule text below is the Internet Archive capture of each page (dates in the Sources table). The Court of Appeals Rules were read from their Internet Archive capture (Rule 6 only is cited).

Typeface: the rule names the Century family (Rule 46 section 3.02(3)(a)). The template uses TeX Gyre Schola, the vendored Century Schoolbook substitute (docs/FONTS.md); Times New Roman is not declared for this court. Size 14 pt is the rule's figure; the default 12 pt is not used.

Page limits: the limits are in words (Rule 46 section 3.02(1); Rule 30(e)), not pages. No page limit is set in `eval.yaml`; the word limits are tier-1 requirements recorded below and are not counted by the default checks (see the README).

## Form of every e-filed paper (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-001 | Letter-size pages (8 1/2 x 11 in). The e-filed PDF is the page size; the paper rule's printing and paper-type terms do not apply to e-filed documents. | RAP-30 (a); SCR-46 3.02 (a) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-002 | Typeface: any font within the Century family. The template declares TeX Gyre Schola (Century Schoolbook). Times New Roman is not used. | SCR-46 3.02 (3) (a) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-003 | Font size 14-point; footnotes in the same font and size as the text. | SCR-46 3.02 (3) (b), (c) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-004 | Line spacing 1.5 for body text. Block quotations, headings and footnotes are single-spaced (TENNCTAPP-FORM-010). | SCR-46 3.02 (2) (a) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-005 | Margins 1 inch on all sides. | SCR-46 3.02 (2) (b) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-006 | Full justification of margins (justified body text). | SCR-46 3.02 (4) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-007 | Pagination begins with page 1, which is the cover page; Arabic numerals, numbered at the bottom of the page. | SCR-46 3.02 (5); RAP-30 (a) (numbered on the bottom) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-008 | The e-filed PDF is made by direct conversion from the word-processing file, not by scanning, and is text searchable where possible. Attachments may be scanned. | SCR-46 3.02 (b) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-009 | Internal hyperlinks and bookmarks are encouraged (the template emits PDF bookmarks from the section headings). | SCR-46 3.02 (6) | C |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-010 | Block quotations are indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes are single-spaced. The rule's 1.5 line spacing is kept for body text. Convention, with the exemplars. | SCR-46 3.02 (2) (a) (body); exemplar convention | C |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-011 | Paper-only terms do not apply to e-filed papers: cover colour (blue appellant, red appellee, gray reply, green amicus, blue application, red answer), number of copies, paper stock, and fastening on the left. | RAP-30 (a), (c); RAP-9 (d); SCR-46 3.02 (a) | A |
| TENNCTAPP-FORM-012 | Hyperlinks to legal authorities may be included but are not part of the record and do not replace the citation in the text. | SCR-46 3.02 (7) | A |

## Briefs: content and order

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-001 | Appellant's brief, in this order under headings: (1) table of contents with page references; (2) table of authorities; (3) jurisdictional statement (only for direct appeals to the Supreme Court, not used here); (4) statement of the issues; (5) statement of the case; (6) statement of facts with record references; (7) argument, with (A) the contentions and reasons with authorities and record references and (B) for each issue a concise statement of the standard of review; (8) short conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | RAP-27 (a) (1)-(8) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-002 | Appellee's brief conforms to the appellant's order, except that items (3), (4), (5), (6) and (7)(B) need not be included unless the appellant's presentation is deemed unsatisfactory. Where the appellee seeks relief from the judgment, its brief states those issues and arguments. | RAP-27 (b) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-003 | Reply brief (appellant only, or appellee where it also seeks relief): answers the appellee's brief (or the appellant's response on the appellee's own request). The reply is a separate filing with its own table of contents and authorities. | RAP-27 (c) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-004 | Table of authorities lists cases (alphabetical), statutes and other authorities, with the pages where each is cited. | RAP-27 (a) (2) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-005 | Citation of cases: by title, to the page of the volume where the case begins and the pages where the matter appears; the year of decision is included. Tennessee cases may be cited to the official or S.W. reporter. | RAP-27 (citation of cases paragraph) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-006 | Record references: each argument refers to the record page where the matter appears. | CTA-6 (b); RAP-27 (record references paragraph) | A |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-007 | Parties are referred to as in the trial court, by actual name, or by descriptive term. | RAP-27 (party references paragraph) | C |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-008 | Argument headings and the standard of review headings are the author's; the template numbers argument points (I., A., 1., a.) by convention. | exemplars (usage/SOURCE.md) | C |
| TENNCTAPP-SEC-009 | Reproduce constitutional provisions, statutes, rules and regulations in pertinent part in the brief or in an addendum. | RAP-27 (e) | A |

## Applications for permission to appeal (Rule 9)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| TENNCTAPP-APP-001 | The application contains (1) a statement of the questions presented for review; (2) a statement of the facts necessary to show why an appeal by permission lies, with references to the appendix; and (3) a statement of the reasons supporting an immediate appeal (incorporation of the trial court's reasons is sufficient). | RAP-9 (d) | M |
| TENNCTAPP-APP-002 | The application is accompanied by an appendix with (1) the order appealed from, (2) the trial court's statement of reasons, and (3) other needed record parts. The appendix is filed with the application and is not produced by the template. | RAP-9 (d) | A |
| TENNCTAPP-APP-003 | The application is filed within 10 days of the entry of the trial court's order or of its statement of reasons, whichever is later; an answer is due within 10 days after filing of the application. | RAP-9 (c), (d) | A |
| TENNCTAPP-APP-004 | If permission is granted, the briefs are governed by Rules 27 to 30 (Rule 9 (e)); the application itself is not given a word limit by Rule 9. The template applies no length limit to the application; the Rule 30 (e) limits are recorded above for briefs. | RAP-9 (e), (d) | C |
