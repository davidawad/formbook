# Tennessee Supreme Court: briefs, applications, petitions for rehearing, amicus briefs and motions

Alias `tenn`. Scope: documents filed in the Supreme Court of Tennessee: principal briefs (brief on the merits, answering brief), reply briefs, applications for permission to appeal under Tenn. R. App. P. 11 (including the petition for discretionary review shape), petitions for rehearing under Tenn. R. App. P. 39, amicus curiae briefs under Tenn. R. App. P. 31, and motions under Tenn. R. App. P. 22.

Verified against the Tennessee Rules of Appellate Procedure (Rules 11, 22, 27, 30, 31 and 39) and Rule 46 of the Rules of the Tennessee Supreme Court (electronic filing, section 3.02, format of e-filed documents), as published by the Tennessee Administrative Office of the Courts and read from the Internet Archive captures listed below, on 2026-10-21.

Live host: the court's own pages (`https://www.tncourts.gov/...`) returned a JavaScript browser-validation page ("Please wait while we validate your browser") with no rule text when requested on 2026-10-21, both for the appellate rules index and for Rule 46. The rule text was therefore read from the Internet Archive (web.archive.org) captures recorded in the Sources table, per the fallback in the worker brief. The live host was unreachable for rule text; it was not retried further.

Local rules not retrieved: the Supreme Court's internal operating procedures and the clerk's e-filing user guide (a PDF linked from the Rule 46 page) were not retrieved.

Conflict between sources: Tenn. R. App. P. 30(a) sets double spacing and Times New Roman 12 point for paper copies, and Tenn. Sup. Ct. R. 46 sets 1.5 line spacing and a 14-point Century-family face for e-filed documents. Rule 46 section 3.02 says that, to the extent another procedural rule conflicts with it on the format of e-filed documents, Rule 46 governs. The Supreme Court is an e-filing court and every template here is an e-filed document, so Rule 46 governs and this file uses its figures. Rule 30(a) paper-copy rules are not used, and the rule 30(c) cover colors do not apply (Rule 46 section 3.02(a) excludes cover color and paper type).

Typeface: Rule 46 section 3.02.3(a) names "any font within the Century Family". The templates declare TeX Gyre Schola (a Century Schoolbook clone, per `docs/FONTS.md`). Times New Roman is not used. The 2003 Workman petition for rehearing exemplar is set in Times New Roman, but it predates the current format rule and is not the standard.

No limit is loosened. Every word, page, font and spacing figure here is the rule's own figure, quoted in the requirement rows.
