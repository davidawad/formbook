## Form and signature (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIWB-FORM-001 | Documents are double-spaced and typewritten in at least 12-point type, except exhibits, official forms and software-generated forms. | LBR 9004(f), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-CAP-001 | A caption identifies the court, case title, number, and document character. The debtor's current chapter appears immediately below the case number. | LBR 9004(a)–(b); FRBP 9004(b), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-FORM-002 | Motions, pleadings and requests for relief state the petition date and any conversion, dismissal or reinstatement. | LBR 9004(c), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-SIG-001 | Attorney telephone, office address, email and state bar identification number appear directly below the attorney's signature. | LBR 9004(d), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-ELEC-001 | ECF PDF is standard letter size and does not exceed 2 MB per document. | ECF Administrative Procedures IV.B, V.B, verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIWB-CONT-001 | Complaint's caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416A; allegations use numbered paragraphs limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010; FRCP 10(a), (b), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-CONT-002 | State jurisdiction, related case name, number, chapter, district and division, core/non-core status, and consent to final orders or judgment. | FRBP 7008(a); LBR 7008, verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-CONT-003 | State the petition date and any conversion, dismissal or reinstatement immediately after the jurisdictional paragraph. | LBR 9004(c), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-CERT-001 | Summons and adversary cover sheet are separate required filings; the complaint is served under Rule 7004. | FRBP 7004; ECF Administrative Procedures II.A.2, verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIWB-MOT-001 | Request relief by a written motion stating grounds with particularity and the requested order. A supporting brief is filed as an attachment to the motion. | FRBP 9013; LBR 9013(c)(4), (g); ECF Administrative Procedures II.A.2, verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-MOT-002 | Motions in adversary proceedings may not use the notice-with-opportunity-to-object procedure. | LBR 9013(c)(4), verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-MOT-003 | Before a motion, make a reasonable effort to meet and confer with opposing counsel. | LBR 7007, verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| MIWB-LEN-001 | No local page limit applies to briefs in adversary proceedings; any case-specific order controls. | LBR 9013, verified 2026-10-14 | M |
