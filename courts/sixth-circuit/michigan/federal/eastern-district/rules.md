Alias `mied`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules (rules page states they are current as of January 1, 2026), Electronic Filing Policies and Procedures (Revised November 2025), and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIED-FORM-001 | All papers are on 8½ × 11 inch white paper of good quality, plainly typewritten, printed, or prepared by a clearly legible duplication process, and double-spaced except quoted material and footnotes. Margins are at least one inch on the top, sides, and bottom. Each page is numbered consecutively. | LR 5.1. | M |
| MIED-FORM-002 | Type size of all text and footnotes is no smaller than 14 point (proportional) or 10½ characters per inch (non-proportional), except standard preprinted forms in general use. This is larger than the default eval's 12 pt body. | LR 5.1. | M |
| MIED-FORM-003 | LR 5.1 sets a minimum type size but no specific typeface; the only font rules are the minimum size and the embeddable-font rule in MIED-ELEC-002. | LR 5.1; ECF R5(c). | A |
| MIED-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, a title naming all parties, a file number, and a Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a). | M |
| MIED-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances; each claim founded on a separate transaction or occurrence is stated in a separate count where that promotes clarity. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| MIED-CONT-003 | Front-page notation to the right of the caption, where applicable: "Demand For Jury Trial" (when a jury demand is included in the pleading); "Class Action" (class claims); "Claim of Unconstitutionality" (when a constitutional challenge to a statute is drawn in question); "Three-Judge Court Requested" (where a three-judge court is required). The failure to make a notation is not a waiver. | LR 9.1; FRCP 38(b)(1). | M |
| MIED-CONT-004 | Civil case cover sheet: an electronic filer provides the information normally entered on the civil case cover sheet; a paper filer completes and files the civil case cover sheet. | LR 3.1. | M |
| MIED-CONT-005 | A nongovernmental entity party files the Statement of Disclosure of Entity Affiliations on the form the Clerk provides, as part of its first pleading or paper, with a negative report if none; a corporate plaintiff files it with its initiating papers. | LR 83.4(a)-(d); ECF R7(c); FRCP 7.1(a). | M |
| MIED-CERT-001 | A paper filed electronically includes a signature block with "s/" or "/s/" (or a scanned signature), firm name (if any), street address, telephone number, primary e-mail address, and bar ID number (where applicable). | ECF R10(b); FRCP 11(a) (address, e-mail address, telephone number). | M |
| MIED-ELEC-001 | Civil initiating papers and requests for issuance of a summons are filed electronically; a pro se party may not file initiating papers electronically in ECF. Filing fees may be paid electronically at filing. | ECF R7(a), (b), (d); LR 5.1.1. | M |
| MIED-ELEC-002 | A word-processor file or scanned paper is converted to PDF (text-searchable where possible); fonts embedded in the PDF must be publicly identified as legally embeddable; no embedded interactive content. Listed common styles include Courier, Arial, Times New Roman, Calibri, Century Schoolbook, Symbol, and ZapfDingbats. | ECF R5(c). | M |
| MIED-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers are protected under FRCP 5.2(a): only the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number may appear. Counsel and parties, not the Clerk's Office, are responsible for redaction. | FRCP 5.2(a); ECF R21. | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIED-FORM-010 | Motions, responses, and briefs comply with LR 5.1: 8½ × 11 inch paper, double spacing (except quotations and footnotes), margins of at least one inch on all sides, consecutively numbered pages, and text and footnotes at least 14 point (proportional). This is larger than the default eval's 12 pt body. | LR 5.1; LR 7.1 (motions and briefs must comply with LR 5.1). | M |
| MIED-FORM-011 | A brief in support of or opposition to a motion is filed with the motion or response, or as a separate document; if contained within the motion or response, it must begin on a new page and be clearly identified as the brief. | LR 7.1 (briefs required and permitted). | M |
| MIED-FORM-012 | A brief supporting a motion or response must begin with a concise statement of the issues presented and, on the following page, the controlling or most appropriate authority for the relief sought. A table of contents, index of authorities, and index of exhibits are permitted. | LR 7.1 (form of required briefs). | M |
| MIED-FORM-013 | Motions must not be combined with any other stand-alone document; a counter-motion must not be combined with a response or reply. Papers filed in violation are stricken. | LR 7.1(i). | M |
| MIED-LEN-001 | The text of a brief supporting or opposing a motion, including footnotes and signatures, may not exceed 25 pages; a longer brief requires a prior ex parte written application stating reasons. | LR 7.1 (length of briefs). | M |
| MIED-LEN-002 | The text of a reply brief, including footnotes and signatures, may not exceed 7 pages. | LR 7.1 (length of briefs). | M |
| MIED-CERT-002 | A paper filed electronically includes the signature block of ECF R10(b) (s/ name, firm, address, telephone, primary e-mail, bar ID). | ECF R10(b); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| MIED-ELEC-004 | Briefs and exhibits are filed as PDF meeting ECF R5(c) (text-searchable, legally embeddable fonts, no embedded interactive content). | ECF R5(c). | M |
| MIED-ELEC-005 | Personal identifiers in the brief and its exhibits are redacted under FRCP 5.2(a). | FRCP 5.2(a); ECF R21. | M |
| MIED-ELEC-006 | A proposed order is submitted via the judge's proposed-order link in ECF (Utilities), not filed as an attachment; it must not include the judge's electronic signature. | ECF R12(a). | M |
