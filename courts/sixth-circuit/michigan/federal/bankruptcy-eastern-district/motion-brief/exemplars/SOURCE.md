## Why this one

Searched CourtListener RECAP (`type=r`, `court=mieb`) for motions with a brief in support in this court. Candidates with a text layer: Saad's motion to dismiss Heitmann (this one, 24 pp., motion and brief in one filing); Stockman's motion to dismiss the adversary in Jayakumar v. Perez (26-02010, 30 pp., motion and brief as Exhibit 3, and a third-party complaint in the same filing, so less typical); Ashmark Construction's motion to dismiss its own case (25-46693, 10 pp., motion with no brief). Saad's filing is the one that has a motion, a brief in support, the required notice, and a certificate of service in one document, in the format the local rules describe (LBR 9014-1(c) exhibit list). It is a single filer; a second filer was not collected (see `../README.md`).
