# Eastern District of Michigan Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `mieb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules (includes amendments through December 10, 2018; PDF dated 2018-02-27), Administrative Procedures for Electronic Case Filing (dated June 1, 2021), and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIEB-FORM-001 | The caption must substantially conform to the appropriate version of Official Form 416, and must also state the chapter number, the judge to whom the case is assigned, and a concise statement of the nature of the document. | FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b); LBR 9004-1(a)(1)-(3); OF Form 416. | M |
| MIEB-FORM-002 | Form of pleading otherwise follows FRCP 10: numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances; a separate count for each claim founded on a separate transaction or occurrence where that promotes clarity. | FRBP 7010 (applying FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| MIEB-FORM-003 | Under LBR 9004-1(b), a paper other than a proof of claim may use only one side of a page; pages, excluding exhibits, must be numbered. | LBR 9004-1(b). | M |
| MIEB-FORM-004 | Electronically generated text in PDF, so the text can be searched and copied (scanned material only as provided in ECF Procedure 8(c)); fonts embedded in the PDF must be legally embeddable. The ECF Procedures list the most commonly used font styles (Courier, Arial, Times New Roman, Symbol, ZapfDingbats) but do not mandate a typeface. | ECF Procedure 8(a), 8(c). | M |
| MIEB-FORM-005 | No rule in the sources sets a typeface, point size, line spacing, or margin for an adversary complaint; the default eval (1 in margins, at least 12 pt body) is not mandated by these sources. | LBR; ECF; FRBP 7010 (none stated). | A |
| MIEB-CONT-001 | Corporate ownership statement by a nongovernmental corporate party is a separate paper, not an attachment to another paper, filed with its first appearance. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b); LBR 7007.1-1. | M |
| MIEB-CONT-002 | The court posts an Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet form; the rule text in these sources does not state when it must be filed with a complaint. | LBR, "Items Available on the Court's Website" (form listed). | A |
| MIEB-CERT-001 | Signed by at least one attorney of record in the attorney's individual name, stating the signer's address and telephone number (FRCP 11(a) also requires e-mail address). Under ECF Procedure 11(d)(1), the electronic signature block is "/s/ Name," address, city/state/zip, phone, e-mail, and bar number if applicable. | FRBP 9011(a); FRCP 11(a) (via FRBP 7012 and 7010); ECF Procedure 11(d)(1). | M |
| MIEB-ELEC-001 | File through ECF in PDF; each transmission may not exceed 5 MB, and larger sets are split into multiple transmissions. | LBR 9004-1(b) (ECF Procedures); ECF Procedures 8(d). | M |
| MIEB-ELEC-002 | Personal identifiers: only the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number may appear, in the filing and its exhibits. | FRBP 9037(a); ECF Procedure 15(a)-(d). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIEB-FORM-010 | Caption must identify the movant and specific relief sought for a motion; for a response, identify the respondent and the title of the motion, including the movant's name and the specific relief sought. | LBR 9004-1(a)(3)(A)-(B); FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| MIEB-FORM-011 | A motion must state its grounds with particularity and set forth the relief requested. | FRBP 9013(b). | M |
| MIEB-FORM-012 | Paper other than a proof of claim: one side of a page; pages excluding exhibits numbered; signature block under the signature line stating counsel's name, mailing address, telephone number, and the client's name (LBR 9004-1(b)). | LBR 9004-1(b). | M |
| MIEB-FORM-013 | No rule in the sources sets a typeface, point size, line spacing, or margin for briefs; the default eval (1 in margins, at least 12 pt body) is not mandated by these sources. | LBR 9014-1(f); ECF (none stated). | A |
| MIEB-LEN-001 | A brief in support of or opposition to a motion in an adversary proceeding (and, in a chapter 11 case, a motion for relief from stay or abandonment, a motion for appointment of a trustee or examiner, an objection to exemptions, or a motion for payment of prepetition claims) may not exceed 25 pages, including footnotes and signatures. | LBR 9014-1(f)(1)-(5). | M |
| MIEB-LEN-002 | A reply brief may not exceed 7 pages, including footnotes and signatures; it must be filed and served at least three Business Days before the hearing. A party seeking a longer brief must first file an ex parte motion stating reasons and the requested length. | LBR 9014-1(f). | M |
| MIEB-CONT-003 | A motion filed under LBR 9014-1 is filed as one PDF, with each of Exhibits 1 to 6 (proposed order, notice of opportunity to respond, brief in support, proof of service, affidavits, documentary exhibits) separately bookmarked; each may instead be filed as a separate attachment at the filer's option. | ECF Procedure 6(a); LBR 9014-1(c). | M |
| MIEB-CONT-004 | Proposed order submitted with the motion is in Word format, not PDF, with no judge signature block, blank after the text, and six single-spaced lines left at the end for the court's signature block and date stamp. | ECF Procedure 7(b), 7(h). | M |
| MIEB-CERT-002 | Signed by at least one attorney of record in the attorney's individual name, stating address and telephone; electronic signature block per ECF Procedure 11(d)(1). | FRBP 9011(a); FRCP 11(a); ECF Procedure 11(d)(1). | M |
| MIEB-ELEC-003 | Refrain from personal identifiers in the brief and its exhibits, or partially redact them. | FRBP 9037(a); ECF Procedure 15. | M |
| MIEB-ELEC-004 | Each transmission to ECF may not exceed 5 MB; PDF with electronically generated text and embedded fonts as in MIEB-FORM-004. | ECF Procedures 8(a), 8(d). | M |
