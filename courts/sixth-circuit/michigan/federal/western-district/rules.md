# Western District of Michigan: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `miwd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Civil Rules (rules that include amendments through August 13, 2026, adopted by Administrative Order No. 26-RL-067 dated August 13, 2026, effective sixty days later, i.e. October 12, 2026), the court's Local Rules Governing Electronic Filing page, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (December 1, 2025 edition) on 2026-10-20.

|---|---|---|---|
| MIWD-FORM-001 | All documents are double spaced in 8½ × 11 inch format, with writing on only the face of each sheet; type is no smaller than 12 point; all margins are at least one inch. Electronically filed documents are rendered PDF. | LR 10.1. | M |
| MIWD-FORM-002 | LR 10.1 sets no typeface and no separate footnote size; its 12-point minimum is stated for the document's type. | LR 10.1 (none stated beyond). | A |
| MIWD-CONT-001 | Caption has the court's name, a title naming all parties, a file number, and a Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a). | M |
| MIWD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances; separate counts where that promotes clarity. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| MIWD-CONT-003 | A nongovernmental corporate party files a disclosure statement with its first appearance or pleading (parent corporations and any publicly held 10% owner, or a statement that there is none); in a diversity case, the statement identifies the citizenship of every individual or entity attributed to the party. | FRCP 7.1(a)(1)-(2), (b)(1). | M |
| MIWD-CONT-004 | Date of signing, and the address, telephone number, and e-mail address of the signing attorney or pro se party, appear on every pleading and paper. | LR 10.3. | M |
| MIWD-CERT-001 | The filing attorney's identity appears at the end of the document as an "s/ [attorney's name]" block with business address, telephone number, and e-mail address. Graphic and other electronic signatures are discouraged. | LR 5.7(e)(i); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| MIWD-ELEC-001 | Initial pleadings in civil cases are submitted electronically (unrepresented parties file on paper); filing fees are paid, or an in forma pauperis motion filed, electronically at submission; a civil case is not commenced until the ECF system accepts the pleading and a Notice of Electronic Filing issues. | LR 5.7(c); LR 5.7(d)(ii)(A) (unregistered pro se filers on paper). | M |
| MIWD-ELEC-002 | All electronically filed documents are in PDF format and submitted under the court's website instructions. Documents exceeding the posted file size limit must be divided into parts, with the docket entry showing the filing is in parts. | LR 5.7(d)(i); LR 5.7(d)(vii)(A). | M |
| MIWD-ELEC-003 | Personal identifiers are protected under FRCP 5.2(a): only the last four digits of a Social Security or taxpayer-identification number, the birth year, a minor's initials, and the last four digits of a financial-account number may appear. | FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
| MIWD-ELEC-004 | Exhibits and attachments carry a prominent exhibit number or letter on their face. | LR 10.8. | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIWD-FORM-010 | Motions and briefs follow LR 10.1: double spaced, 8½ × 11 inch, face-only pages, text at least 12 point, margins at least one inch. This matches the default eval's 12 pt body and 1 in margins. | LR 10.1. | M |
| MIWD-FORM-011 | A motion must be accompanied by a supporting brief, except oral motions made during a hearing or trial; a response must be a brief conforming to these rules. Briefs contain a concise statement of the reasons and cite all applicable federal rules, local rules, and other authorities; motions and briefs may not be submitted as a letter to the judge. | LR 7.1(a). | M |
| MIWD-FORM-012 | Motion for expedited consideration: insert "EXPEDITED CONSIDERATION REQUESTED" in boldface below the case caption, and identify in the motion the reason expedited consideration is needed. | LR 7.1(e). | M |
| MIWD-FORM-013 | A request for oral argument goes in the caption and the heading of the party's brief. | LR 7.2(d), 7.3(d). | M |
| MIWD-CONT-005 | Nondispositive motions are accompanied by a separately filed Certificate Regarding Motion Concurrence (date, time, duration, participants, and issues of the conference) if court intervention remains necessary. | LR 7.1(d)(ii)(B). | M |
| MIWD-LEN-001 | Dispositive-motion briefs produced on a computer may not exceed 10,800 words, including headings, footnotes, citations, and quotations; the caption, cover sheets, tables of contents and authorities, signature block, attachments, exhibits, affidavits, and addenda are excluded. Hand-written or typewritten briefs may not exceed 25 pages. | LR 7.2(b)(i). | M |
| MIWD-LEN-002 | Dispositive-motion reply briefs produced on a computer may not exceed 4,300 words (same inclusions and exclusions); hand-written or typewritten replies may not exceed 10 pages. | LR 7.2(c). | M |
| MIWD-LEN-003 | Nondispositive-motion briefs (in support or opposition) produced on a computer may not exceed 4,300 words (same inclusions and exclusions); hand-written or typewritten briefs may not exceed 10 pages. Reply briefs on nondispositive motions require leave of court. | LR 7.3(b)(i), 7.3(c). | M |
| MIWD-CERT-002 | A brief over the word limit or over 1,000 words (nondispositive) is accompanied by a Certificate of Compliance Regarding Word Count stating the word count and the name and version of the word processing software used. Dispositive briefs require the certificate in all cases. | LR 7.2(b)(ii); LR 7.3(b)(ii). | M |
| MIWD-CERT-003 | Signing attorney's date and contact information (address, telephone, e-mail) on the motion and brief; electronic signature per LR 5.7(e). | LR 10.3; LR 5.7(e)(i); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| MIWD-ELEC-005 | Proposed orders are submitted electronically in PDF (attached as an exhibit to a motion or stipulation, contained in a stipulation, or submitted separately), must not include the word "proposed" in the caption or title, and are also submitted in Word format through CM/ECF per the court's procedure. | LR 5.7(g). | M |
| MIWD-ELEC-006 | Briefs or exhibits that exceed a word count or page limit are filed only with leave; the proposed document is attached as an exhibit to the motion for leave. | LR 5.7(f). | M |
| MIWD-ELEC-007 | Personal identifiers in the brief and its exhibits are redacted under FRCP 5.2(a). | FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
