# Michigan Supreme Court: briefs, applications, motions and original complaints

Alias `mich`. Scope: the papers filed with the Clerk of the Michigan Supreme Court: briefs in calendar cases and cases argued on the application (appellant's brief on the merits, appellee's brief, reply brief, amicus curiae brief, response to an adverse amicus brief), applications for leave to appeal (petition for review and petition for discretionary review: both are MCR 7.305 applications), motions (including motion for reconsideration and motion for rehearing), and original complaints with a supporting brief (petition for writ, MCR 7.306). Verified against the Michigan Court Rules of 1985, Chapters 1, 2 and 7, from the official courts.michigan.gov HTML (Chapter 7 banner: "Updated with MSC Order(s) Effective on September 1, 2026"), and MSC ADM File No. 2023-04 (order of March 19, 2025), on 2026-10-21.

Typeface: no Michigan rule names a face. The template declares Times New Roman, the standard for this repo, at 12 point, which is the size MCR 7.212(B)(5) fixes. Liberation Serif is not used.

Where a rule is cited as "via 7.312(A)(1)", the calendar-case rule borrows subrule (B), (C), (D) or (G) of MCR 7.212 "as to form and length". The Chapter 7 text of MCR 7.212(C) (contents) is therefore followed as the filing order for briefs, which is what the exemplars do; the rule's own words limit the borrowing to form and length, so the section order is recorded as `M` and the rows say so.

Exemplar layout: the cover, the table of contents with roman front-matter pages, the index of authorities and the signature block follow MCR 7.312(C) and the filed answering-brief exemplar (checked page 1 and the contents page; `usage/SOURCE.md`).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSC-APP-001 | The application is one signed copy, prepared in conformity with MCR 7.212(B), with: (a) a statement identifying the judgment or order appealed and the date of its entry; (b) the questions presented, in concise terms related to the facts; (c) a table of contents and index of authorities conforming to 7.212(C)(2) and (3); (d) a concise statement of the material proceedings and facts conforming to 7.212(C)(6); (e) a concise argument conforming to 7.212(C)(7), establishing a ground under subrule (B); and (f) the relief sought. | MCR7 7.305(A)(1)(a)-(f) | M |
| MSC-APP-002 | The application attaches one copy of the trial-court opinion, findings or judgment relevant to the question, and one copy of the Court of Appeals opinion or order, unless review of a pending case is sought. | MCR7 7.305(A)(2) | M |
| MSC-APP-003 | The application shows a ground in 7.305(B): (1) a substantial question about the validity of a legislative act; (2) significant public interest in a case by or against the state or its officers in their official capacity; (3) a legal principle of major significance to the state's jurisprudence; (4) before a Court of Appeals decision, delay likely to cause substantial harm, or a ruling that a provision of a constitution, statute, rule or regulation, or other state action is invalid; (5) for an appeal from a Court of Appeals decision, that the decision is clearly erroneous and will cause material injustice, or conflicts with a Supreme Court or Court of Appeals decision; (6) an appeal from the Attorney Discipline Board, where the decision is clearly erroneous and will cause material injustice. | MCR7 7.305(B)(1)-(6) | M |

| MSC-APP-005 | The application is served on all other parties, with proof of service, and a notice of the filing is served on the clerks of the Court of Appeals and the trial court or tribunal; the filing fee in MCR 7.319(D)(1) is paid. | MCR7 7.305(A)(3)-(4) | M |
