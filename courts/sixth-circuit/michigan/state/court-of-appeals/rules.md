# Michigan Court of Appeals: briefs, applications, motions and original-action filings

Alias `michctapp`. Scope: the doc types with exemplars under `usage/` of this court root: appellant's brief (opening brief), reply brief, amicus curiae brief, motion with supporting brief, application for leave to appeal (petition for discretionary review), and original-action complaint for a writ (petition for writ). Verified against the Michigan Court Rules, Chapter 7 (Appellate Rules), "Updated with MSC Order(s) Effective on September 1, 2026", and Chapter 1, "Updated with MSC Order(s) Effective on January 1, 2026", as posted by the Michigan Supreme Court on 2026-10-21.

Typeface: the templates declare Times New Roman, the face of every Michigan exemplar in `usage/` (the exemplars embed TimesNewRomanPS). Liberation Serif is listed only as the metric-compatible fallback required by `docs/FONTS.md` when Times New Roman is not installed; no rule names a different face. Body text and footnotes are 12 point (MCR 7.212(B)(5), MCR 1.109(D)(1)).

The appellee's brief is not an exemplar here (no public appellee brief was found; see `usage/SOURCE.md`), so no `answering-brief` folder is delivered. The appellee's brief rules are recorded below for completeness.

| MICHCTAPP-CAP-001 | The first part of every document is a caption stating the court name; the case number with its year prefix and two-letter case-type suffix; the parties and their designations; the name, business address, telephone number and state bar number of each attorney; and the name, address and telephone number of any unrepresented party. ## Briefs (appellant's brief, reply brief, amicus brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-001 | Margins are at least one inch; text is 12-point type; text is 1.5-line spaced; quotations and footnotes may be single-spaced. | MCR-7 7.212(B)(5); ADM-2019-16 (amended text) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-002 | Length: no more than 16,000 words for an appellant's or appellee's brief, 3,200 words for a reply brief. Only the statement of facts, the arguments and the relief (subrule (C)(6)-(8)) count toward the limit; footnotes and text in embedded graphics also count. | MCR-7 7.212(B)(1)-(2), (G) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-003 | A brief filed under the word limit states the number of countable words in a statement after the signature block. | MCR-7 7.212(B)(3) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-004 | The appellant's brief contains, in this order: (1) a title page with the full title of the case and, in capital letters or boldface, "ORAL ARGUMENT REQUESTED" or "ORAL ARGUMENT NOT REQUESTED" (with the constitutional-invalidity statement when it applies); (2) a table of contents; (3) an index of authorities; (4) the statement of the basis of appellate jurisdiction; (5) the statement of questions involved, each numbered and answered "Yes" or "No" where possible; (6) the statement of facts with page references; (7) the arguments; (8) the relief requested in a distinct concluding section; (9) a signature; (10) a separate appendix only as subrule (J) provides. | MCR-7 7.212(C)(1)-(10) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-005 | Each argument portion is prefaced by its principal point in capital letters or boldface type, and states the applicable standard of review with supporting authority. | MCR-7 7.212(C)(7) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-006 | The appellee's brief conforms to subrule (C), with the jurisdictional and standard-of-review statements and the counter-statements required by subrule (D). | MCR-7 7.212(D)(1)-(3) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-007 | A reply brief is confined to rebuttal of the appellee's arguments, is filed within 21 days after service of the appellee's brief, and is governed by subrule (B). | MCR-7 7.212(G) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-008 | An amicus curiae brief is filed only on a motion granted by the Court, or under an exemption in subrule (H)(2), and is limited to the issues the parties raised. Except for a brief under an (H)(2) exemption, it identifies in the first footnote on the first page of text whether a party's counsel authored it in whole or in part, and whether a party or counsel made a monetary contribution to fund it, naming each such contributor. | MCR-7 7.212(H)(1)-(4) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-009 | An amicus brief filed under the 501(c)(3) or 501(c)(6) exemption ends with the tax-exempt attestation required by subrule (H)(2)(f). | MCR-7 7.212(H)(2)(f) | M |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-010 | A one-page "supplemental authority" communication, filed without leave, is limited as subrule (F) provides. | MCR-7 7.212(F) | A |
| MICHCTAPP-BRF-011 | A nonconforming brief may be ordered corrected or struck. | MCR-7 7.212(I) | A |
| MICHCTAPP-CONV-001 | Tables of contents and authorities list each heading or authority with its page number and dot leaders; pages are numbered in Arabic numerals at the bottom center. | (convention; exemplars) | C |
