# Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals rules

Primary sources were checked 2026-10-17. Re-check the court rules and FRAP at least every six months and before changing a template. The court's [combined rules PDF](https://www.ca6.uscourts.gov/sites/ca6/files/documents/rules_procedures/Full%20Rules%20w%20FRAP_0.pdf) states Sixth Circuit Rules last amended July 13, 2026 and FRAP last amended December 1, 2025. The [Briefs Checklist](https://www.ca6.uscourts.gov/sites/ca6/files/documents/rules_procedures/Briefs%20Checklist.pdf) is an official filing aid.

Kinds: **M** mandatory by rule; **C** convention; **T** template contract; **A** author responsibility. Requirements below are shared by the doc types unless a section narrows them.

## Form and length

| ID | Requirement | Primary source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA6-FORM-001 | Use paper 8½ × 11 inches; margins at least one inch on all sides. Text double-spaced; headings, footnotes and quotations longer than two lines may be single-spaced. Page numbers may appear in the margins. | FRAP 32(a)(4); https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-appellate-procedure (verified 2026-10-17) | M |
| CA6-FORM-002 | Use a proportionally spaced serif typeface of at least 14 points, or a monospaced typeface of at least 12 points and no more than 10½ characters per inch. Case names in the body and footnotes are italic or underlined. | FRAP 32(a)(5)(A), (a)(6); same source and verification date | M |
| CA6-FORM-003 | A brief's cover contains the case number centered at the top, court name, case title, proceeding and court below, brief title identifying the filing party, and counsel name, address and telephone. | FRAP 32(a)(2)(A)–(F); Briefs Checklist Part I (official link above), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA6-LEN-001 | Principal brief: 13,000 words or 30 pages; reply: 6,500 words or 15 pages. Cross-appeal appellee principal brief: 15,300 words or 35 pages; cross-appeal reply: 6,500 words or 15 pages. | FRAP 32(a)(7)(A), (B); FRAP 28.1(e)(2); 6 Cir. R. 28(e)(1)–(2), combined rules PDF, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA6-CERT-001 | A brief using a word count includes a certificate stating the number of words, the word processor, and compliance with typeface requirements, signed by counsel. | FRAP 32(g); 6 Cir. R. 32(a), combined rules PDF, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA6-ELEC-001 | File briefs electronically under the court's CM/ECF procedures; a PDF must be searchable and comply with the court's current technical requirements. | 6 Cir. R. 25; CM/ECF User Manual, https://www.ca6.uscourts.gov/cmecf-user-manual (verified 2026-10-17) | M |

## Opening brief

| ID | Requirement | Primary source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA6-CONT-001 | Include the disclosure statement required by FRAP 26.1 and Circuit Rule 26.1, followed by contents, authorities, oral-argument statement, jurisdiction, issues, case, argument summary, argument and conclusion in the applicable required order. | FRAP 28(a); 6 Cir. R. 26.1; Briefs Checklist Parts II–XIV, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA6-CONT-002 | Include jurisdictional basis and dates establishing timeliness; identify issues; set out relevant facts and procedural history with record citations; summarize argument; argue each issue with standard of review; state requested relief. | FRAP 28(a)(4)–(9), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA6-CONT-003 | Include a certificate of service unless service is through CM/ECF. | FRAP 25(d); 6 Cir. R. 25(f), verified 2026-10-17 | M |

## Answering brief

| ID | Requirement | Primary source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA6-CONT-101 | Include the required contents and authorities, oral-argument statement, argument, conclusion and certificates. Respond to the appellant's arguments and include any appellee cross-appeal issues in the manner required by FRAP 28. | FRAP 28(b), 28.1; 6 Cir. R. 28; Briefs Checklist, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA6-LEN-101 | Apply the appellee principal-brief word/page limits, including the larger cross-appeal limit where applicable. | FRAP 32(a)(7); FRAP 28.1(e)(2); 6 Cir. R. 28(e), verified 2026-10-17 | M |

## Reply brief

| ID | Requirement | Primary source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA6-CONT-201 | A reply brief contains the argument in reply and may not introduce a new issue unless responding to an appellee cross-appeal. | FRAP 28(c), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA6-LEN-201 | Reply brief does not exceed 6,500 words or 15 pages; certificate of compliance is required when using the word limit. | FRAP 32(a)(7), (g); verified 2026-10-17 | M |

## Author responsibilities

| ID | Requirement | Primary source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA6-AUTH-001 | Confirm the applicable briefing schedule, record citations, disclosure duties, and any case-specific order before filing. | FRAP 25, 26.1, 28; 6 Cir. R. 25, 26.1, 28; verified 2026-10-17 | A |
| CA6-AUTH-002 | The template's chosen style details that are not prescribed above remain conventions; the Rules and case-specific orders control. | FRAP 32 and court's Briefs Checklist; verified 2026-10-17 | A |
