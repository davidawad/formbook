# MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT

## I. Introduction

The Complaint seeks to avoid a transfer made more than two years before the petition date. The transfer falls outside the reach of 11 U.S.C. § 548(a)(1), and the Complaint pleads no facts that would bring it within any other avoidance provision.

## II. Background

The Debtor filed a voluntary petition under chapter 7 on a fictional date. The Plaintiff, as trustee, filed this adversary proceeding on a fictional date and pleads two counts.

### A. The Complaint

1. Count I seeks avoidance under section 548.
1. Count II seeks recovery under section 550.

## III. Argument

### A. The Complaint does not plead a transfer within the look-back period

A complaint must contain enough facts to state a claim that is plausible on its face. *Ashcroft v. Iqbal*, 556 U.S. 662, 678 (2009) (fictional citation, for layout only).

### B. Count II depends on Count I

Recovery under section 550 requires an avoidable transfer. Without Count I, Count II has nothing to rest on.

## IV. Conclusion

The Court should dismiss the Complaint.
