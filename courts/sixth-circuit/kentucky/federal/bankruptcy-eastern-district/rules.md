# Eastern District of Kentucky Bankruptcy Court rules

Primary sources checked 2026-10-17. Re-check every six months and before changing a template. Sources: [Local Bankruptcy Rules, effective December 1, 2024](https://www.kyeb.uscourts.gov/sites/kyeb/files/KYEB%20120124%20Local%20Rules%20POST%20COMMENT%20CLEAN%20FINAL%20110424_0.pdf); [Administrative Procedures Manual](https://www.kyeb.uscourts.gov/administrative-procedures-manual); and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure 7008, 7010, 9004, 9011, 9013 and 9014 at https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-bankruptcy-procedure.

Kinds: **M** mandatory; **C** convention; **T** template contract; **A** author responsibility. Local Rule 1001-3 provides that district court local rules apply only when included in the APM's district-rule list. The APM list excludes the district rule on form of papers; therefore no general typeface, margin, paper-size or spacing requirement is identified for these documents.

## General form and signature

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYEB-FORM-001 | Documents must be clearly legible. No general local typeface, margin, paper-size or spacing specification was found. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR 1001-3 and APM § I, verified 2026-10-17 | M (legibility); C (other form choices) |
| KYEB-CAP-001 | Caption states court name, case title, case number and (when applicable) adversary number, and briefly designates the document's character. | FRBP 9004(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| KYEB-SIG-001 | Pleading signed by at least one attorney of record in the attorney's individual name, with address and telephone number if any; an unrepresented party signs personally. | FRBP 9011(a), verified 2026-10-17 | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYEB-CONT-001 | Caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416A; allegations use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (applying FRCP 10(a), (b)); verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| KYEB-CONT-002 | Complaint states jurisdiction, venue and whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court; it identifies the related bankruptcy case, case number, chapter, district and division. | FRBP 7008(a), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| KYEB-CERT-001 | Adversary complaint is served under FRBP 7004; the summons is issued separately. | FRBP 7004, 7005; verified 2026-10-17 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYEB-MOT-001 | A request for an order is made by written motion stating grounds with particularity and setting out requested relief. | FRBP 9013(a), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| KYEB-MOT-002 | Motion notice states the objection deadline and hearing details when applicable; motions seeking relief without notice must state the basis and comply with the rule's exceptions. | LBR 9013-1(a), (c), verified 2026-10-17 | M (when applicable) |
| KYEB-MOT-003 | A proposed order accompanies a motion as a separate attachment and follows the APM's proposed-order procedure. | LBR 9013-1(d); APM § XVIII, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| KYEB-SERV-001 | A contested-matter motion is served as required by FRBP 9014(b), including the manner prescribed by FRBP 7004 where applicable. | FRBP 9014(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
