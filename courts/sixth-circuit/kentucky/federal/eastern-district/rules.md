# Eastern District of Kentucky: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `kyed`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Joint Local Rules of Civil Practice for the Eastern and Western Districts of Kentucky, as posted in the file KY_Amended_Civil_Rules_August_2026.pdf (the PDF prints no effective date; its Introduction describes the 2023 revision, and LR 86.1 says amendments take effect upon entry of each Joint General Order), and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition (printed on the rules PDF cover), on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYED-FORM-001 | Pleadings are on 8½ × 11 inch pages with margins of at least one inch on all sides, double spaced (except block quotes, headings, and footnotes), in at least 12-point proportionately spaced font. Matches the default eval for margins and body size; the rule does not state a footnote size. | JLR LR 5.2(a) | M |
| KYED-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court's name, a title naming all parties, a file number, and a Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| KYED-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| KYED-CONT-003 | A claim for relief states the grounds for jurisdiction, a short and plain statement of the claim, and a demand for relief. | FRCP 8(a)(1)-(3) | M |
| KYED-CONT-004 | The Civil Cover Sheet is among the forms a filer prepares and presents to the Clerk with process. | JLR LR 4.3(a)(9) | M |
| KYED-CONT-005 | A nongovernmental corporate party files its Rule 7.1 disclosure statement with its first appearance, pleading, motion, response, or other request to the court. | FRCP 7.1(a)(1), (b)(1) | M |
| KYED-CONT-006 | In a diversity action, a party that is an LLC, LLP, LP, or partnership lists in its Rule 7.1 disclosure the states of citizenship of its owners, members, or partners. | JLR LR 7.1.1(a); FRCP 7.1(a)(2) | M |
| KYED-CONT-007 | A jury demand is made in a pleading or by a separate written demand served within 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue, and filed under Rule 5(d). | FRCP 38(b) | M |
| KYED-CERT-001 | The signed paper states the signer's address, e-mail address, and telephone number. | FRCP 11(a) | M |
| KYED-ELEC-001 | Users of the Court's electronic filing system comply with the Court's Electronic Case Filing Administrative Policies and Procedures. | JLR LR 5.5 | M |
| KYED-ELEC-002 | Filings containing an individual's social-security or taxpayer-identification number, birth date, minor's name, or financial-account number include only the permitted partial identifiers. | FRCP 5.2(a) | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYED-FORM-002 | Motions and other papers use the LR 5.2(a) format: 8½ × 11 inch, margins of at least one inch on all sides, double spaced (except block quotes, headings, and footnotes), at least 12-point proportionately spaced font. | JLR LR 5.2(a) | M |
| KYED-LEN-001 | Motions and responses may not exceed 25 pages without leave of Court; replies may not exceed 15 pages without leave. The rule does not state what the page count excludes. | JLR LR 7.1(d) | M |
| KYED-CONT-008 | A motion states with particularity the grounds for the motion, the relief sought, and the legal argument necessary to support it. | JLR LR 7.1(a) | M |
| KYED-CONT-009 | A party filing a motion also files a separate proposed order. A proposed order imposing sanctions is provided separately from any other proposed order. | JLR LR 7.1(e) | M |
| KYED-CONT-010 | Copies of cited authority not available electronically are attached to the motion, response, or reply. | JLR LR 7.1(h) | M |
| KYED-CONT-011 | A discovery motion attaches a certification that counsel conferred, or attempted to confer, in good faith to resolve the dispute extrajudicially. | JLR LR 37.1 | M |
| KYED-SEAL-001 | Absent a statute, rule, local rule, or standing order authorizing sealing, a party seeking to file a sealed document electronically files a motion for leave to seal stating why sealing is required; a stipulation that designates documents as confidential is not sufficient. | JLR LR 5.6(b)-(c) | M |
| KYED-CERT-002 | The signed motion or brief states the signer's address, e-mail address, and telephone number. | FRCP 11(a) | M |
| KYED-ELEC-003 | Electronic filing complies with the Court's Electronic Case Filing Administrative Policies and Procedures (not retrieved). | JLR LR 5.5 | M |
| KYED-ELEC-004 | Redaction of personal identifiers under Rule 5.2(a) in the brief and in exhibits. | FRCP 5.2(a) | M |
