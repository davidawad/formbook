## Why this one

Searched CourtListener's RECAP index for available motion-with-memorandum filings in `court=kywb`. Most motions found were not in RECAP, or were in RECAP only as orders, opinions or certificates. This memorandum is a full filed brief in a 2024 adversary proceeding: caption, numbered paragraphs, headings in Roman numerals and letters, footnotes, a signature page and a certificate of service. Its text layer reads cleanly.

Candidates not used: the Logsdon / Wells Fargo motion to dismiss (2012, dkt. 29; not in RECAP, only the court's order is); the Community Financial Services Bank v. Little motion to dismiss (2025, dkt. 5; not in RECAP, only the opposition at dkt. 21); the Jackson v. Truist motion to dismiss (2022; only the opinion is available); the Hull Organization motion for relief (dkt. 554, not retrieved).
