# Western District of Kentucky Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `kywb`. Scope: adversary-proceeding complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Western District of Kentucky, effective December 1, 2025 (file "2025 Local Rules Final & Approved", PDF cover "Effective December 1, 2025"), and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition (printed on the rules PDF cover), on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYWB-FORM-001 | Motions, pleadings, tendered orders, and other related matters are typewritten and include the address, telephone number, and e-mail address of the filer. | LBR 9004-1 | M |
| KYWB-FORM-002 | No page-size, typeface, point-size, line-spacing, or margin rule was found in the W.D. Ky. bankruptcy local rules or FRBP 9004. The civil format rule (JLR LR 5.2: one-inch margins, double spacing, 12-point) is written for civil practice and is not restated in these rules; this file does not apply it. | LBR 9004-1; FRBP 9004 | A |
| KYWB-CAP-001 | The caption sets out the court's name, the case title, the case number and, if appropriate, the adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document's character. | FRBP 9004(b) | M |
| KYWB-CAP-002 | The adversary complaint caption substantially conforms to the appropriate version of Official Form 416. | FRBP 7010 | M |
| KYWB-CONT-001 | The jurisdiction allegation refers to the name, number, and Code chapter of the case to which the adversary proceeding relates, and to the district and division where it is pending. | FRBP 7008(a) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-002 | The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to the entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008(a) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-003 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances, and the rest of Rule 10 applies (FRBP 7010). | FRBP 7010; FRCP 10(b) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-004 | A jury demand is made in a pleading or by a separate written demand served within 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue, and filed under Rule 5(d). | FRCP 38(b) (applied by FRBP 9015(a); the demand is filed under FRBP 5005) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-005 | Each non-individual party to an adversary proceeding other than a governmental unit, including an intervenor, files a corporate ownership statement identifying each person owning 10% or more of its equity. | LBR 7007.1-1; FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b) | M |
| KYWB-CERT-001 | The complaint is signed by at least one attorney of record in the attorney's individual name and states the signer's address and telephone number; an unsigned paper is stricken. | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 9011-4 | M |
| KYWB-CERT-002 | An image of a signature used on an electronically filed document is backed by the filer's certification and, where an original signature is required, the filer keeps the original for two years after appeal periods expire. | LBR 9011-1(b) | M |
| KYWB-ELEC-001 | All pleadings and documents are filed electronically, except as the local rules permit. | LBR 5005-4(a) | M |
| KYWB-ELEC-002 | Pleadings use only the last four digits of any social-security number; the Statement of Social Security Number (Official Form B 121) is filed separately. | LBR 1007-5 | M |
| KYWB-ELEC-003 | Filings containing a social-security or taxpayer-identification number, birth date, minor's name, or financial-account number include only the permitted partial identifiers. | FRBP 9037(a) | M |
| KYWB-ELEC-004 | Attachments are filed in electronic form unless the Court permits otherwise; attachments over 40 pages are limited to excerpts directly germane to the matter. | LBR 5005-1(a)-(b) | M |
| KYWB-AI-001 | Counsel is responsible for the accuracy of any content generated with artificial intelligence; the local rule does not prohibit its use. | LBR 9011-2 | A |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYWB-FORM-003 | Motions are typewritten and include the filer's address, telephone number, and e-mail address. | LBR 9004-1 | M |
| KYWB-FORM-004 | No font, size, margin, or spacing rule was found for motions in the W.D. Ky. bankruptcy rules; see KYWB-FORM-002. | LBR 9004-1 | A |
| KYWB-CAP-003 | The caption sets out the court's name, case title, case number and adversary number if any, and a brief designation of the motion. | FRBP 9004(b) | M |
| KYWB-CAP-004 | A motion for expedited relief or an emergency hearing has "EMERGENCY MOTION TO . . ." or "REQUEST FOR EXPEDITED HEARING" in its heading, explains the basis, and the movant calls the Clerk's office. | LBR 9013-1(b)(1)(i)-(iii) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-006 | A motion is not accepted for filing unless accompanied by a separate proposed order. | LBR 9013-1(a)(1) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-007 | No motion or proposed order waives redemption rights. | LBR 9013-1(a)(2) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-008 | A motion requests one form of relief, except that a Chapter 7 motion may combine stay relief with abandonment; a Chapter 11 motion may request more than one form of relief only if its caption sets out each form, alternative orders are tendered, and the forms are directly related; a Chapter 11 motion may seek conversion or dismissal. | LBR 9013-1(a)(3) | M |
| KYWB-CONT-009 | The motion states with particularity the grounds for seeking the order and the relief sought. | FRBP 9013(a)-(b) | M |
| KYWB-CERT-003 | The signed motion or brief is signed by an attorney of record, who states the signer's address and telephone number. | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 9011-4 | M |
| KYWB-ELEC-005 | Electronic filing and attachments follow LBR 5005-4 and LBR 5005-1 as for complaints. | LBR 5005-4; LBR 5005-1 | M |
| KYWB-ELEC-006 | Redaction of personal identifiers under FRBP 9037(a) in the brief and in exhibits. | FRBP 9037(a) | M |
