# Western District of Kentucky rules

Primary sources checked 2026-10-14. Re-check every six months and before changing a template. Sources: Joint General Order 2026-01 (re-states the Joint Local Rules), https://www.kywd.uscourts.gov/sites/kywd/files/Joint%20General%20Order%2026-01-1.pdf; FRCP 10, https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-civil-procedure; Joint General Order 2017-01A, https://www.kyed.uscourts.gov/sites/kyed/files/JGO17-01A.pdf; and Joint General Order 2018-02, https://www.kywd.uscourts.gov/sites/kywd/files/general-ordes/Joint_General_Order_2018-02.pdf.

Kinds: **M** mandatory; **C** convention; **T** template contract; **A** author responsibility.

## Form (complaint and motion brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYWD-FORM-001 | Use 8½ × 11 inch pages, at least one-inch margins, double-spaced text except block quotes, headings and footnotes, and proportionally spaced type of at least 12 points. | Joint LR 5.2(a), as restated in JGO 2026-01; verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| KYWD-CAP-001 | A pleading caption identifies the court, action, case number, and Rule 7(a) pleading title; the title names the parties. | FRCP 10(a); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| KYWD-SIG-001 | Pleadings and motions are signed in compliance with Rule 11(a). | FRCP 11(a); verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYWD-CONT-001 | State a short and plain jurisdictional statement, claim, and demand for relief; present claims in numbered paragraphs and separate counts where clarity requires. | FRCP 8(a), 10(b); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| KYWD-CERT-001 | A civil cover sheet accompanies a complaint where required by the court's civil rules; the cover sheet is a separate filing. | Joint Local Rules; court filing instructions, https://www.kywd.uscourts.gov/; verified 2026-10-14 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYWD-MOT-001 | A motion states with particularity its grounds, requested relief, and supporting legal argument, subject to routine-motion exceptions. | LR 7.1(a), as amended by JGO 2017-01A; verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| KYWD-LEN-001 | Motions and responses are limited to 25 pages and replies to 15 pages absent leave of court. | LR 7.1(d), JGO 2018-02; verified 2026-10-14 | M |
| KYWD-SERV-001 | A certificate of service is not needed for documents served through CM/ECF; other service follows FRCP 5. | FRCP 5(b), (d); verified 2026-10-14 | M |
