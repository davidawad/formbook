# Court of Appeals of Kentucky: briefs, amicus briefs, motions for intermediate relief, and original-action petitions

Alias `kyctapp`. Scope: appellant's and appellee's briefs, reply briefs, amicus curiae briefs, motions (RAP 7, RAP 21), and petitions for writ in original actions (RAP 60) filed in the Kentucky Court of Appeals. Verified against the Kentucky Rules of Appellate Procedure (RAP), created by Supreme Court of Kentucky Order 2022-49 and effective January 1, 2023, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.kycourts.gov/Courts/Supreme-Court/Rules/Pages/default.aspx returned HTTP 403 to scripted fetches on 2026-10-21, and the court-site RAP page (https://kycourts.gov/Courts/Supreme-Court/Rules/Pages/Rules-of-Appellate-Procedure.aspx) also returned HTTP 403. The text used here is the Internet Archive capture of Order 2022-49, which contains the full RAP text as adopted. Amendments made after that order were not retrieved; confirm against the court-posted RAP before relying on a number. The civil rules the RAP incorporates (CR 5.01-5.03, CR 7.03) were not retrieved; the RAP text cites them and the template relies only on the RAP.

Two exemplars predate the RAP. The writ exemplar (filed June 30, 2022) and the motion exemplar (filed July 28, 2022) were filed under the former CR 76 and CR 65.07 practice, which Order 2022-49 deleted (CR 76.01-76.46) and replaced with RAP 7, 21 and 60. The template follows the RAP, as the court's current rules require.

The cover colors required by RAP 31(C)(2) and RAP 34(B)(4) are paper-cover requirements; the e-filed PDF exemplars carry no cover color, and the template prints white covers. That is recorded as `A` below.

Word and page limits: RAP 31(G), RAP 34(B)(4) and RAP 60(F) state a word limit and a page limit for computer-generated documents. The word limit controls where a document exceeds the page limit (RAP 15(C)), so a computer-generated brief may run past the page figure if it stays within the word limit and carries a word-count certificate. The template does not count words, so no `page_limit` is set in `eval.yaml`; DEF-LEN-001 is SKIP for every doc type here. Word counts are a filer check.

## Briefs (appellant, appellee, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KYCTAPP-LEN-001 | Appellant's initial brief and appellee's response brief: no more than 8,750 words or 20 pages if computer generated (25 pages if typed). | KYCTAPP-LEN-002 | Appellant's reply brief: no more than 1,750 words or 4 pages if computer generated (5 pages if typed); plus 1,750 words or 4 pages for each additional appellee brief answered. | RAP 31(G)(2)(b). | M |
| KYCTAPP-LEN-003 | Combined cross-appeal briefs: 14,000 words or 30 pages (cross-appellant's initial and response) and 10,500 words or 25 pages (cross-appellee's reply and response). | RAP 31(G)(2)(c). | M |
| KYCTAPP-LEN-004 | Excluded from the word and page limits: the cover, introduction, statement concerning oral argument, statement of points and authorities, signature block, exhibits and appendices. | RAP 15(D); RAP 31(G)(5). | M |
| KYCTAPP-CONT-001 | Appellant's opening brief, in this order: (1) introduction indicating the case and a statement concerning oral argument (the two together no more than one page); (2) statement of points and authorities listing the authorities under each point, with page references; (3) statement of the case with record references; (4) argument conforming to the statement of points and authorities, opening with a statement of how the issue was preserved; (5) conclusion stating the relief sought; (6) signature; (7) appendix. | RAP 32(A)(1)-(7). | M |
| KYCTAPP-CONT-002 | A statement of points and authorities is required for a brief of more than five pages. | RAP 32(D). | M |
| KYCTAPP-CONT-003 | Appellee's response brief, in this order: oral-argument statement (with an optional introduction, together no more than one page); counterstatement of points and authorities; counterstatement of the case (accepting or correcting the appellant's statement); argument conforming to the counterstatement; signature; optional appendix. | RAP 32(B)(1)-(6). | M |
| KYCTAPP-CONT-004 | Reply brief: a statement of authorities conforming to the appellant's statement; the purpose of the brief and the issues it addresses; argument; relief sought if pertinent; confined to points raised in the briefs answered, without reiterating argument. | RAP 32(C)(1)-(2). | M |
| KYCTAPP-CONT-005 | Authorities: Kentucky statutes cited from the official KRS; Kentucky cases cited by reporter (S.W.2d or S.W.3d) with court and date; case names italicized; unpublished opinions per RAP 41. | RAP 31(E)(1)-(2). | M |
| KYCTAPP-CONT-006 | Record citations: "TR" pages as numbered by the clerk; video recording references "VR" with month, day, year and time. | RAP 31(E)(3)-(4). | M |
| KYCTAPP-CONT-007 | References to parties: counsel should minimize "appellant" and "appellee" and use names or descriptive terms. | RAP 31(D). | C |
| KYCTAPP-COVER-001 | Front cover, in this content: the case number of the appeal; the case number of the action from which the appeal was taken; a caption identifying the lead appellant and appellee; the name of the party on whose behalf the brief is filed. | RAP 31(C)(1)(a). | M |
| KYCTAPP-COVER-002 | Front cover, or the white page immediately following it when the cover is full: a signed certificate of service identifying by name each person served, in accordance with RAP 30(B). | RAP 31(C)(1)(b). | M |
| KYCTAPP-COVER-003 | Certificate regarding record: the certificate states that the record on appeal has been returned to the clerk of the court from which it was withdrawn, or that it was not withdrawn by the filing party.
