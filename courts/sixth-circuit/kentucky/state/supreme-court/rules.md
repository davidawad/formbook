# Kentucky Supreme Court: briefs, motions, petitions, and amicus briefs

Alias `ky`. Scope: filings in the Supreme Court of Kentucky: appellant's and appellee's briefs on appeal (answering and reply briefs), briefs on the merits after discretionary review, motions for discretionary review, petitions for rehearing, modification, or extension, original petitions for writs (RAP 60), motions, and amicus curiae briefs. Verified against the Kentucky Rules of Appellate Procedure as adopted by Ky. Sup. Ct. Order 2022-49 (effective January 1, 2023; archived copy of the kycourts.gov PDF, 89 pp.) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://kycourts.gov/Courts/Supreme-Court/Supreme%20Court%20Orders/202249.pdf (kycourts.gov returned HTTP 403 to this host on 2026-10-21, so the current official copy and any amendments after Order 2022-49 were not checked). The Supreme Court's electronic-filing standards for PDFs were not retrieved either; PDF rules below are limited to the RAP text.

## Briefs on appeal (appellant, appellee, reply, merits)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KY-ORDER-001 | Appellant's opening brief, in order: introduction and statement concerning oral argument (together one page, not counted toward the limit); statement of points and authorities; statement of the case with record references; argument, beginning with a preservation statement; conclusion stating the relief sought; signature; appendix. | RAP 32(A)(1)–(7) | M |
| KY-ORDER-002 | Appellee's response brief, in order: statement concerning oral argument (may follow an introduction; together one page); counterstatement of points and authorities in the appellant's format; counterstatement of the case; argument; signature; appendix (optional). | RAP 32(B)(1)–(6) | M |
| KY-ORDER-003 | Reply brief: confined to points raised in the briefs it answers and does not reiterate arguments already presented. Other briefs have a statement of authorities, state the purpose and the issues addressed, and conclude with the relief sought. | RAP 32(C)(1)–(2) | M |
| KY-ORDER-004 | A statement of points and authorities is required for a brief of more than five pages and not for a brief of five pages or less. | RAP 32(D) | M |
| KY-ORDER-005 | Briefs on the merits after discretionary review: the movant is the appellant and the respondent is the appellee; the brief follows RAP 31 and 32; briefing times run from the order granting review. | RAP 44(J)(1); RAP 30 | M |
| KY-CITE-001 | Kentucky statutes are cited to the official edition of the KRS. Kentucky cases are cited to the S.W.2d or S.W.3d reporters with the court and date; case names are italicized. | RAP 31(E)(1) | M |
| KY-CITE-002 | Record cites use the clerk's page numbering (for example, "TR __"); video references use "VR" with the month, day, year, and time. | RAP 31(E)(3)–(4) | A |
| KY-CITE-003 | Unpublished opinions are cited under RAP 41; a copy is attached to the brief when RAP 41(C) requires it. | RAP 31(E)(2); RAP 41(C) | A |
| KY-REF-001 | Briefs minimize "appellant" and "appellee" and use the parties' names or descriptive terms. | RAP 31(D) | C |
| KY-LEN-001 | Appellant's initial brief and appellee's response brief in the Supreme Court: not more than 40 pages or 17,500 words if computer generated (50 pages if handwritten or typewritten). | RAP 31(G)(3)(a) | M |
| KY-LEN-002 | Reply brief in the Supreme Court: not more than 7 pages or 3,500 words if computer generated (10 pages if handwritten or typewritten); plus 4 pages or 1,750 words for each additional appellee brief answered. | RAP 31(G)(3)(b) | M |
| KY-LEN-003 | Cover, introduction, oral-argument statement, statement of points and authorities, signature block, exhibits, and appendices are excluded from brief limits. | RAP 31(G)(5); RAP 15(D) | M |
| KY-LEN-004 | Briefs combining a cross-appeal are limited to 22,750 words or 50 pages (cross-appellant's brief) and 8,750 words or 20 pages (cross-appellee's reply). | RAP 31(G)(3)(c) | A |
| KY-LEN-005 | Death-penalty cases may be extended on motion filed at least 20 days before the deadline (briefs up to 52,500 words or 120 pages; reply up to 8,750 words or 20 pages). | RAP 31(G)(4) | A |
| KY-PENAL-001 | A brief may be stricken for failure to substantially comply with the RAP format rules. | RAP 31(H)(1) | A |

## Motions (RAP 7) and motions for discretionary review (RAP 44)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KY-MOT-001 | An application for an order is made by written motion that complies with RAP 5, states the grounds with particularity, and sets out the relief sought. | RAP 7(A) | M |
| KY-MOT-002 | A response to a motion may be filed within 10 days after the motion is filed, or within the time the court sets. | RAP 7(C) | A |
| KY-DR-001 | A motion for discretionary review of a Court of Appeals decision conforms to RAP 5 and states the name of the movant and each respondent, with counsel's names and addresses. | RAP 44(C)(1), (G) | M |
| KY-DR-002 | The motion states the date of entry of the judgment sought to be reviewed; whether a supersedeas or bail bond has been executed; whether the movant has a petition for rehearing or motion for reconsideration pending in the Court of Appeals, and whether any other party has one; and a clear and concise statement of the material facts, the questions of law, and the specific reasons for review. | RAP 44(C)(2)–(5) | M |
| KY-DR-003 | The motion is not more than 5,250 words or 13 pages if computer generated (15 pages if handwritten or typewritten). The caption, certificate of service, and the items in RAP 44(C)(1)–(4) are excluded. | RAP 44(D) | M |
| KY-DR-004 | The motion is filed within 30 days after the order or opinion sought to be reviewed, or after the order disposing of a timely RAP 43 petition. The motion attaches the final order, the trial court's findings and opinion, and the Court of Appeals opinion or order. The filing fee under RAP 13 accompanies the motion. | RAP 44(B)(2), (F), (K) | A |
| KY-DR-005 | A response is filed within 30 days; no reply to a response is filed. | RAP 44(E) | A |

| KY-REH-002 | The petition is filed within 20 days from the date the opinion was issued. | RAP 43(B)(3) | M |

| KY-REH-004 | The petition is not more than 3,500 words or 8 pages if computer generated (10 pages if handwritten or typewritten). Covers, signatures, and appendices are excluded. | RAP 43(B)(5) | M |

## Original proceedings (RAP 60)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KY-WRIT-001 | The petition is in the form prescribed by RAP 5 and RAP 7 and states, in order: the name of each respondent; the name and counsel of each real party in interest; the style and case number of any underlying action; an explicit statement of the relief sought; a clear and concise statement of the material facts (with reference to the order sought to be reviewed and the ground for jurisdiction), the questions of law with authority, and the reasons relief should be granted; the appendix; and proof of service on each respondent and real party in interest. | RAP 60(B)–(C) | M |
| KY-WRIT-002 | The petition, excluding accompanying documents, is not more than 14,000 words or 30 pages if computer generated (40 pages if handwritten or typewritten), except by the court's permission. | RAP 60(F) | M |
| KY-WRIT-003 | Filing fee under RAP 13 with the petition; five copies (4 bound and 1 unbound). | RAP 60(B) | A |

## Amicus curiae (RAP 34, RAP 43(C), RAP 45)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KY-AMICUS-001 | An amicus brief is filed only on order of the court, on a motion filed with the tendered brief that states the movant's interest, the points to be presented, and their relevance. The motion is filed within 15 days after the appellant's brief is filed or due, whichever is later; the filing fee under RAP 13 accompanies it. | RAP 34(B)(1)–(3) | A |
| KY-AMICUS-002 | An amicus brief conforms to RAP 30, 31, and 32(C)(1), has a tan cover, is not more than 5,250 words (with a RAP 15 word-count certificate), and has no appendices other than unpublished opinions required by RAP 41(C). It states the purpose of the brief and the issues addressed and contains a statement of authorities. | RAP 34(B)(4); RAP 32(C)(1) | M |
| KY-AMICUS-003 | An amicus memorandum on a petition for rehearing or a motion for discretionary review is not more than 1,750 words, excluding the cover and signature block (and certificate), with a word-count certificate and no appendices other than unpublished opinions. | RAP 43(C)(1)–(2); RAP 45(A)–(B) | M |
