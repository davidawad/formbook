## Search (how this exemplar was chosen)

CourtListener RECAP search, `type=r`, `court=ctb`, query `complaint`, sorted by date filed. Adversary complaints by the Chapter 11 Trustee in *Ho Wan Kwok* were the most common results. This one was chosen because it is a complete complaint in this court with a text layer, a caption with the full bankruptcy case, numbered paragraphs, a prayer for relief, and a signature block with bar numbers.
