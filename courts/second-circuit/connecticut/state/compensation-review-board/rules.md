# Connecticut Compensation Review Board: briefs

Alias `connworkcompcom`. Scope: briefs filed with the Compensation Review Board (CRB) of the Workers' Compensation Commission. No public filed exemplar was obtained (no court-root usage folder), so no doc-type folder is built yet. Verified against Workers' Compensation Commission Memorandum 2026-03 (dated May 12, 2026; effective July 1, 2026) on 2026-10-22.

## Sources

| Tag | Source | URL |
|---|---|---|
| WCC 2026-03 | Workers' Compensation Commission, Memorandum 2026-03, CRB brief guidelines (May 12, 2026) | https://portal.ct.gov/wcc/knowledge-base/articles/news-and-memos/2026/memo-2026-03 |

Local rules not retrieved: the CRB's standalone rules of practice were not found. Connecticut Practice Book chapter 67 (appellate briefs, typeface and margins) is cited by Memorandum 2026-03 as the model for the page limit, but the chapter was not retrieved for this court and no typeface or margin rule for CRB briefs is recorded here.

## Briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CONNWORKCOMPCOM-BRIEF-001 | Briefs submitted to the CRB are limited to 35 pages, effective July 1, 2026. | WCC 2026-03 | M |
| CONNWORKCOMPCOM-BRIEF-002 | The page limit excludes party appendices, the cover page, the table of contents, the table of authorities, and attachments. | WCC 2026-03 | M |
| CONNWORKCOMPCOM-BRIEF-003 | A brief may exceed the page limit only by permission of the Chairperson, requested with the compelling reason and the number of additional pages sought. | WCC 2026-03 | M |
