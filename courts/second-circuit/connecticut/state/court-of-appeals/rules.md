# Connecticut Appellate Court: appellate filings

Alias `connappct`. Scope: briefs (opening, answering, reply, amicus), motions and oppositions (motion-brief), and petitions for review (petition-for-review). Verified against the Connecticut Practice Book, 2026 edition (Rules of Appellate Procedure, Chapters 62, 66, 67 and 77), on 2026-10-22. Every brief in this court is e-filed (Sec. 60-8), so the e-filing format in Sec. 67-2A governs the templates; Sec. 67-2 governs only filers excluded or exempt from e-filing. The 2026 amendments removed the "Filed Under the Electronic Briefing Rules" cover legend that the 2023 to 2025 exemplars carry.
