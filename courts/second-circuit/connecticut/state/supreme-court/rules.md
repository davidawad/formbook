# Connecticut Supreme Court: appellate filings

Alias `conn`. Scope: briefs on the merits (appellant's brief, appellee's brief, reply brief, amicus brief), motions and supporting memoranda (motion-brief), petitions for certification to appeal from the Appellate Court (petition-for-discretionary-review), and motions for reconsideration en banc (petition-for-rehearing). Verified against the Connecticut Practice Book, Rules of Appellate Procedure (2026 edition, amendments effective January 1, 2026), on 2026-10-22. Section numbers are Practice Book sections; "P.B." refers to that edition.

Counsel must file all appellate papers electronically unless an exemption is granted (P.B. 60-7(a), 60-8). The electronic form, P.B. 67-2A, is therefore the default for every brief and motion below. The paper form, P.B. 67-2, applies only to filers excluded or exempt from electronic filing. Electronic filers have the word limits in the tables below. Paper filers have page limits (P.B. 67-3: 35 pages for an appellant's or appellee's brief; P.B. 67-5A: 15 pages for a reply).

## Word limits (briefs, motions, petitions)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CONN-LEN-001 | The appellant's brief does not exceed 13,500 words. A cross-appeal brief of the cross appellant, combined with the appellee's brief, does not exceed 18,000 words; the cross appellee's brief does not exceed 16,000 words. | P.B. 67-3A | M |
| CONN-LEN-002 | The appellee's brief does not exceed 13,500 words, except as stated in CONN-LEN-001 for a cross appeal. | P.B. 67-3A | M |
| CONN-LEN-003 | The reply brief does not exceed 6,500 words for electronic filers (fifteen pages for paper filers). | P.B. 67-5A | M |
| CONN-LEN-004 | Word counts exclude the cover page, table of contents, table of authorities, statement of issues, signature block, certifications, and (for an amicus brief) the statement of interest required by P.B. 67-7A, and appendices. | P.B. 67-3A | M |
| CONN-LEN-005 | Motions and oppositions do not exceed 3,500 words, excluding caption, signature block, certifications and appendix. | P.B. 66-2(b) | M |
| CONN-LEN-006 | A petition for certification does not exceed 4,000 words, excluding caption, signature block, certifications and appendix. An opposition to it has the same limit. | P.B. 84-5(b), 84-6(a) | M |
| CONN-LEN-007 | A letter citing supplemental authorities does not exceed 350 words. | P.B. 67-10 | M |
| CONN-LEN-008 | An amicus application and any objection do not exceed 3,500 words. The 2026 text sets no word cap for the amicus brief itself; the repealed P.B. 67-7(b) cap of 4,000 words is not in force (repealed January 1, 2025). | P.B. 67-7A(b) | M |
| CONN-LEN-009 | Requests to exceed a brief's word limit are filed with the appellate clerk and state the compelling reason and the number of additional words sought; the chief justice or chief judge grants them. | P.B. 67-3A | A |

## Motions (motion-brief; motion for reconsideration en banc)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CONN-MOT-001 | A motion is specific and requests one form of relief; it sets out in separately captioned paragraphs (1) a brief history of the case, (2) the specific facts relied on, and (3) the legal grounds relied on. | P.B. 66-2(a) | M |
| CONN-MOT-002 | A motion directed to the trial court has both the trial court and the Appellate Court docket numbers in its caption and names in its first paragraph the trial judge or panel to which it is directed. | P.B. 66-2(e) | M |
| CONN-MOT-003 | Motions and oppositions, including footnotes, are set in a 12-point serif font; section captions in 14-point serif; margins 1½ inches on all sides; text left aligned; spacing 1.3 to 1.5 uniform; emphasis by bold or italic, not underlining; one pagination scheme "Page X of XX" centered at the bottom, starting on the first page. | P.B. 66-2 (format paragraph) | M |
| CONN-MOT-004 | Every motion carries the P.B. 66-3 certification: a copy has been delivered to each other counsel of record under P.B. 62-7, and the motion complies with the word-count requirement of P.B. 66-2(b). | P.B. 66-3 | M |
| CONN-MOT-005 | A motion for reconsideration en banc is a motion under P.B. 66-2 and states, under P.B. 71-5, the grounds for reconsideration. | P.B. 71-5; 66-2 | M |
| CONN-MOT-006 | A motion for reconsideration is filed within ten days from the date the decision or order is officially released. | P.B. 71-5 | M |
| CONN-MOT-007 | A motion for reconsideration en banc has "en banc" in its caption; it states briefly why reconsideration en banc is necessary (for example, uniformity of decision or the importance of the decision) and names any decisions with which the decision conflicts. | P.B. 71-5 | M |

## Petitions for certification (petition-for-discretionary-review)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CONN-PET-001 | A petition for certification to appeal from an Appellate Court decision is filed within twenty days of the official release of the opinion or the issuance of notice of a final order, whichever is earlier, subject to P.B. 84-4(a). | P.B. 84-1, 84-4(a) | M |
| CONN-PET-002 | The petition is one document with a single pagination scheme starting on its first page and running through the appendix; page numbers are centered at the bottom and written "Page X of XX". | P.B. 84-5(a) | M |
| CONN-PET-003 | The petition contains, in this order: (1) a brief introduction providing context; (2) the questions presented; (3) a brief history of the case, including the disposition in the Appellate Court; (4) a concise argument on the bases for certification under P.B. 84-2, with no separate memorandum; (5) the appendix; (6) the certification of service and word count. | P.B. 84-5(a)(1)-(4), (6) | M |
| CONN-PET-004 | The appendix contains: a table of contents; the Appellate Court opinion (preferably as published in the Connecticut Law Journal) or order; the trial court memorandum of decision or ruling, if the Appellate Court decision was per curiam, a summary affirmance or a dismissal; any order on a motion that stays or extends the time to file the petition (other than an extension of time); and a list of all parties to the appeal in the Appellate Court with addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses and juris numbers of trial and appellate counsel. | P.B. 84-5(a)(5)(A)-(E) | M |
| CONN-PET-005 | The petition, including footnotes, is set in a 12-point serif font; section captions in 14-point serif; margins 1½ inches; spacing 1.3 to 1.5 uniform; text left aligned; emphasis by bold or italic, not underlining. | P.B. 84-5(b) | M |
| CONN-PET-006 | The petition does not exceed 4,000 words, excluding caption, signature block, certifications and appendix. | P.B. 84-5(b) | M |
| CONN-PET-007 | An opposition to a petition is filed within ten days of the petition, is responsive to it, and has the same format and limit as the petition. | P.B. 84-6(a), (b) | C |
