## Layout and typography (conventions, tier 2)

- Letter paper; one-inch margins; page numbers centered at the foot, arabic from the first body page, lower-case roman for the table of contents, none on the cover (the exemplar does the same).
- Times New Roman 12 pt (embedded), headings at the same size; no court rule names a face (re-checked 2026-10-15). The exemplar is Times New Roman 12 pt, non-embedded, with a 27.6 pt line pitch measured from its text layer. The template sets that pitch exactly (exact line boxes, no extra space between paragraphs; fb-595.68.25) and 13.8 pt single lines (cover, contents, quotations, signature).
- Paragraphs: first line indented 0.5 in, including the first paragraph after a heading (the exemplar does the same). Double-spaced.
- Headings: level 1 centered bold caps; level 2 bold; level 3 bold italic at the margin. Each heading is kept with the text after it.
- Table of contents generated by typst from the headings (page numbers are never typed in). It is the one generated front-matter element.
- Cover: court title centered; caption grid (party column with a right rule and a bottom rule; right column with `Case No.`, `Adv. Proc. No.`, the brief title in bold); the counsel block at the lower right, as in the exemplar.
- Signature: `Dated:` on the left; firm, `By: /s/` signer and the signer's lines on the right.

## Markdown contract (for samples)

- YAML frontmatter, then the body. Frontmatter: `court_name` (list of lines), `case_number` (with the judge's initial in parentheses), `chapter` (optional), `adv_number` (optional; blank line printed if absent), `caption.parties` (list of `{names, designation}`; "v." between parties), `brief_title`, `counsel` (lines, optional), `date`, `signature` (`firm` lines, `signer`, `lines`).
- Body: `#` heading (level 1, centered caps), `##` (level 2), `###` (level 3); paragraphs (not numbered: briefs are not numbered); markdown lists; `*text*` for italics (the sample has none; the exemplar's case-name italics are not in its text layer).
- Not supported: footnotes (none in the sample), tables, a table of authorities (see Known gaps), raw typst.

## Known gaps and open choices

1. **Tier 2 is not met: one filer.** EVAL-LOOP asks for two or three exemplars from different filers. One usable memorandum was found in this court (this one). The adversary complaint in the same proceeding (Doc 1) is not in RECAP, and the CourtListener docket pages checked for other nywb matters did not offer a second filing of this type. Needs a second filing before this doc type can count as tier-2 complete.
2. **No table of authorities.** The exemplar has one (pp. iii-iv of the filing). No W.D.N.Y. rule requires it, and the template does not build one. The sample therefore omits it. This is a tier-2 gap; a TOA would need manual page tracking or a typst `bibliography`-style generator.
3. **Footnotes.** The exemplar has a footnote (fn 1 on the first page of the Preliminary Statement). The sample omits it; the template has not been tested with footnotes.
4. **Italics.** The text layer of the exemplar has no italic markup, so case names are transcribed roman. The template renders `*...*` italic.
5. **Typeface.** The exemplar is Times New Roman, 12 pt; the template sets the same face (fb-595.68.25).
6. **Local-rules version.** The only local-rules text found is a PDF whose creation date is 2013-08-14. Confirm with the Clerk or the current rules that no later amendment changes LBR 9004 or 9013.
7. **Sample scope.** The sample is a 10-page transcription (cover, TOC, preliminary statement, part of the factual background, Argument I.a to I.c, conclusion, signature). Argument I.c (rest) to II, the table of authorities and footnote 1 are omitted.
8. **Notice of motion and proposed order** are separate documents and are not produced by this template.
9. **tools/check.sh.** Since fb-595.68.25: `sh tools/check.sh <this folder> --engine typst` and `--engine docx` both exit 0 (typst 9, docx 13 checks passed, 0 failed).
10. **Page-count target.** The exemplar is 32 pages. The sample renders to 10 pages, within the 8-14 target.

File names follow the tools and CLAUDE.md contract (`INPUT-`, `OUTPUT-`, `OVERLAY-`), not the brief's `<id>.md` and `<id>-overlay.pdf` (the tools read and write the prefixed names).

## Engines

docx (`docx/`): `reference.xml` (the Word styles: `base="xengine"` in the typst face `{font}`, 12 pt, 552-twip body and 276-twip single line boxes; `fb render` builds the reference document from it) and `nywb-motion-brief.lua` (cover, caption, table of contents, headings, quotations, signature block, the three sections and their 10 pt page-number footer, every paragraph as WordprocessingML on `lib/docx/xengine.lua`). No script: the contents' page numbers come from needles (each heading with the words after it) that `fb render` finds in the LibreOffice PDF of a first pass (FORMBOOK_NEEDLES / FORMBOOK_PAGES; was `render.sh` with `toc-pages.pl`). Sections: cover without footer, table of contents in lower-case roman from i, body from 1. Output: `usage/OUTPUT-motion-brief.docx` and `.docx.pdf` (LibreOffice conversion, 11 pages).

- Cross-engine (fb-595.68.25): typst and docx share the layout model (`lib/typst/xengine.typ`, `lib/docx/xengine.lua`): Times New Roman 12 pt, 27.6 pt double pitch, 13.8 pt single lines, headings at 12 pt on the body pitch with no space of their own, contents rows with the page in a 36 pt column (typst `listing-row`), the last paragraph kept with the signature block. `check.sh --engine docx`: XENG-001..003 PASS (246/246 typst lines break after the same word, 11 = 11 pages); `params.cross_engine: gate` in `eval.yaml`. Word may paginate slightly differently from LibreOffice (docs/DOCX.md).
