# U.S. Bankruptcy Court, S.D. New York: rules

Alias `nysb`. Scope: papers filed by counsel through CM/ECF in a case or adversary proceeding before the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York; doc types `adversary-complaint` (the complaint that commences an adversary proceeding, Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7001, 7003) and `motion-brief` (a memorandum of law in support of or in opposition to a motion, Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9013, LBR 9013-1). Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (when amended Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure take effect) and whenever the court posts amended Local Bankruptcy Rules or a new general order on its "Local Rules" or "Procedural Guidelines, Policies and General Orders" page.

## Findings

1. **No rule in this court sets typeface, type size, line spacing, margins, page size or a length limit.** LBR 9004-1 ("Form of Papers") requires only that papers be "plainly typed or printed", not bound or stapled, free of defacing erasures, and that the face show the attorney's name, addresses and telephone. Its Comment says it "is an adaptation of Civil Rule 11.1 of the Local District Rules", and that "[t]he general rules for form of papers are set forth in Bankruptcy Rule 9004 and Official Bankruptcy Forms B416A, B416B and B416D." FRBP 9004 requires only that a document be "clearly legible" and carry a caption. A full-text search of the LBR finds no font, point-size, spacing or margin rule and no page or word limit for a complaint or a memorandum (the only length rule is LBR 7056-1(a)'s two-page pre-motion letter).
2. **SDNY Local Civil Rule 7.1(b)-(c) does not bind this court.** The Joint Local Rules (eff. Jan. 2, 2026) withdrew Local Civil Rule 11.1 and moved the formatting rule to Local Civil Rule 7.1(b): "(1) all text must be 12-point type or larger, except for text in footnotes which may be 10-point type; (2) all documents must have at least one-inch margins on all sides; (3) all text must be double-spaced, except for headings, text in footnotes, or block quotations, which may be single-spaced", and 7.1(c) sets 8,750 words for a memorandum with a word-count certificate. Local Civil Rule 1.1 says those rules "apply in all civil actions and proceedings governed by the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure". The LBR define "Local District Rules" (LBR 9001-1(13)) and incorporate them only by name where they mean to (Civil Rule 26.3, in LBR 2004-1 and 7026-1); LBR 9004-1 does not incorporate Civil Rule 7.1 or 11.1. So the 12-point / double-spaced / one-inch values are **conventions** here (all four exemplars, from four filers, follow them), and **no 8,750-word limit or word-count certificate applies** (EX-REV is 68 pages, EX-TET 57 pages, neither has a word-count certificate). A template that meets JLR 7.1(b) is safe in either court.
3. **A memorandum of law is its own ECF filing.** ECF Procedures II.A.2: documents filed together by one party may go under one docket number "with the exception of a memorandum of law. A memorandum of law must be filed separately and linked to the document (e.g., motion) to which the memorandum pertains." Both memorandum exemplars are standalone docket entries (EX-REV ECF 16, the notice is ECF 15; EX-TET ECF 33, the motion is ECF 32).
4. **Caption carries the judge's initials and, on motions, the deadlines.** LBR 9004-2(a) requires the judge's initials with the case number; LBR 9004-2(b) requires the return date and time and the response deadline in the upper right-hand corner of the caption of a motion "and all related pleadings", and the related CM/ECF docket number on responsive papers. EX-TET shows the deadlines and "Related CM/ECF Docket Number" block top right; EX-REV (whose notice of motion is a separate filing) does not.
5. **Adversary complaint must state consent or non-consent to final orders** (FRBP 7008, last sentence). EX-CEL states it ("Plaintiffs consent to the entry of final orders and judgments by the Bankruptcy Court"); EX-EHR (filed 2019) does not, which is the filer's omission, not a convention.
6. **Judges' individual chambers rules** are made applicable by reference (LBR 1001-1(c)(3)) and may add page limits or formatting. The chambers-rules page (https://www.nysb.uscourts.gov/chambers-rules-0) returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-17, and the individual judge pages fetched (Judges Glenn and Jones) carry no formatting rules in their HTML. Not verified; see "Not verified".
