# Supreme Court of the State of New York: papers filed in the trial court (complaint, motion papers)

Alias `nysupct`. Scope: papers filed in the Supreme Court of the State of New York (the trial court of general jurisdiction, county by county): summons and complaint, and motion papers (notice of motion, affirmation or affidavit, memorandum of law). Verified against 22 NYCRR 202.5 (form of papers), 22 NYCRR 202.8-b (length of papers) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the official Unified Court System pages returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21: https://www.nycourts.gov/rules/trialcourts/202.shtml (also tried https://ww2.nycourts.gov/rules/trialcourts/202.shtml and https://nycourts.gov/rules/trialcourts/202.shtml). The text below is the Cornell LII reproduction. The LII page for 202.8-b shows the version "[Effective until 7/7/2025]" and no later version was retrieved, so the word limits in NYSUPCT-LEN-001 are stated as that copy gives them. CPLR 2101 (cited by 202.5(a)) was not retrieved (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/CPL/2101 is a JavaScript-only page). County-specific local rules and individual justices' part rules were not retrieved and are not reflected here.

No court-root FILED-EXAMPLE is held for this court; the doc-folder exemplars under `complaint/usage/` and `motion-brief/usage/` are the only filed examples.

## Form of papers (all papers filed in the Supreme Court)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYSUPCT-FORM-001 | Every paper filed in court complies with CPLR 2101 and has proof of service annexed where required. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a) (Index Number), citing CPLR 2101 (not retrieved). | M |
| NYSUPCT-FORM-002 | If typewritten, the paper is double-spaced between lines, except for quotations and the names and addresses of attorneys appearing in the action. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a). | M |
| NYSUPCT-FORM-003 | Print is no smaller than 12-point. Footnotes are no smaller than 10-point. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a). | M |
| NYSUPCT-FORM-004 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inches, with margins no smaller than one inch. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a). | M |
| NYSUPCT-FORM-005 | Every paper filed in court, other than an exhibit or printed form, has writing on one side only; papers fastened on the side may have writing on both sides. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a). | M |
| NYSUPCT-FORM-006 | The index number is placed on the paper, and the name of the assigned judge (if any) appears to the right of the caption. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a) (Index Number). | M |
| NYSUPCT-FORM-007 | Papers are not rejected for filing because they lack a backer; papers stapled or bound securely are acceptable. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a). | C |

## Length of papers (motion papers and memoranda)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYSUPCT-LEN-001 | Affidavits, affirmations, briefs and memoranda of law in chief prepared on a computer are limited to 7,000 words each; reply papers to 4,200 words each and may not argue points that do not respond to the memoranda in chief. Where a cross-motion is made, the cross-movant's papers are 7,000 words and the principal movant's reply 4,200 words. | 22 NYCRR 202.8-b(a), (e) (LII copy, "Effective until 7/7/2025"). | M |
| NYSUPCT-LEN-002 | For the word limit, the count excludes the caption, table of contents, table of authorities, and signature block. | 22 NYCRR 202.8-b(b). | M |
| NYSUPCT-LEN-003 | A computer-prepared paper ends with a page certifying, by the counsel who files it, the number of words in the document and that it complies with the word limit. The counsel may rely on the word-processing count. | 22 NYCRR 202.8-b(c). | M |
| NYSUPCT-LEN-004 | Typewritten or handwritten briefs and memoranda in chief are limited to 20 pages; typewritten reply papers to 10 pages; no argument in a reply that does not respond to the memoranda in chief. | 22 NYCRR 202.8-b(d)-(e). | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYSUPCT-CONT-001 | The complaint carries the index number and the name of the assigned judge (if any) to the right of the caption. The caption's other content (court, county, parties) is not prescribed by the 202.5 text retrieved. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a) (Index Number). | M |
| NYSUPCT-CONT-002 | Pleading content (numbered allegations, causes of action, demand for relief) is governed by CPLR article 30 (CPLR 3014 not retrieved); the template follows the filed exemplar for layout. | CPLR article 30 (not retrieved). | C |

## Motion papers (notice of motion, affirmation, memorandum of law)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYSUPCT-CONT-003 | A motion memorandum of law is a paper filed in court: NYSUPCT-FORM-001 to -006 apply, and NYSUPCT-LEN-001 to -003 apply to its word count. | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a), 202.8-b(a)-(c). | M |
| NYSUPCT-CONT-004 | Motion papers carry the index number and the assigned judge's name (NYSUPCT-FORM-006). The content of the notice of motion is governed by CPLR 2214 (not retrieved). | 22 NYCRR 202.5(a); CPLR 2214 (not retrieved). | C |
