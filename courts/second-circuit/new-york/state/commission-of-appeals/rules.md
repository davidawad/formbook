# New York Commission of Appeals: no current court or rules

Alias `nycommnapp`. Scope: the temporary Commission of Appeals, which the Judiciary Article of 1869 formed to clear the old Court of Appeals' backlog. It operated from July 1870 to July 1875 and is no longer a court. No current rules, filing standards or form requirements apply to it, so this file records no requirement rows. ## Status

The Commission was a temporary body. Its life was extended by a 1872 constitutional amendment to July 1875, and it has no current successor under that name. The history page places the later Second Division of the Court of Appeals in the 1880s and 1890s, but that body is also not current. The current court of last resort is the Court of Appeals, whose rules are a separate court and are not applied here.

No rules, practice guidelines or document-format standards for the Commission of Appeals were found, and no court web page for it was located in a 2026-10-21 search. Its internal procedure, if any, is unsourced.
