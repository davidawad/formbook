# New York Court for the Trial of Impeachments: constitutional composition and procedure

Alias `nyimpct`. Scope: the court convened to try an impeachment, as set out in Article VI, section 24 of the New York Constitution. The court sits only when the Assembly has voted articles of impeachment; no standing court rules, form requirements or filing standards for it were found. Verified against the Constitution, Article VI, section 24 (NY Senate, Open Legislation, CNS) on 2026-10-21.

## Composition and procedure

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYIMPCT-PROC-001 | The Assembly votes impeachment by a majority of all its elected members. | NY-CONST art. VI, § 24 | M |
| NYIMPCT-PROC-002 | The court is composed of the president of the Senate, the senators, or the major part of them, and the judges of the Court of Appeals, or the major part of them. On an impeachment of the governor or lieutenant-governor, the lieutenant-governor and the temporary president of the Senate do not sit. | NY-CONST art. VI, § 24 | M |
| NYIMPCT-PROC-003 | A judicial officer against whom articles of impeachment have been preferred to the Senate does not exercise office until acquitted. | NY-CONST art. VI, § 24 | M |
| NYIMPCT-PROC-004 | Before the trial, the members of the court take an oath or affirmation to try the impeachment truly and impartially according to the evidence. | NY-CONST art. VI, § 24 | M |
| NYIMPCT-PROC-005 | No person is convicted without the concurrence of two-thirds of the members present. | NY-CONST art. VI, § 24 | M |
| NYIMPCT-PROC-006 | Judgment in an impeachment extends no further than removal from office, or removal and disqualification from holding any public office of honor, trust or profit in this state. The party remains liable to indictment and punishment according to law. | NY-CONST art. VI, § 24 | M |
