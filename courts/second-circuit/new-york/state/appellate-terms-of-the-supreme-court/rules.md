# Appellate Terms of the Supreme Court of New York (First and Second Departments): briefs

Alias `nyappterm`. Scope: briefs filed in the Appellate Terms of the Supreme Court, the First Department Appellate Term (22 NYCRR Part 640) and the Second Department Appellate Term (22 NYCRR Part 730 and Part 731), on appeals from the Civil, Criminal, District, City, Town and Village Courts (CPLR 5701-5703; CPL 450.60). Verified against 22 NYCRR Parts 640, 730 and 731 (Cornell LII reproductions), CPLR 5529 (NYSenate.gov, revision of 2014-09-22) and 22 NYCRR 1250.1 (scope of Part 1250, which covers the Appellate Division only), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad2/AppellateTerm_Rules_Highlights.shtml (HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21); the court's own pages at https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad1/ and https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad2/ returned HTTP 403. The rule text below is the Cornell LII reproduction of each Part. The Appellate Term rules set no typeface, type size, margin, line spacing or cover-color rule for briefs that the retrieved text states; the typeface and layout in the templates are conventions shown by the filed exemplars, and the Appellate Division's Part 1250.8 (14-point type) is by its own terms the Appellate Division's rule (22 NYCRR 1250.1(a)), not the Appellate Term's.

## Form and paper (all Appellate Terms)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-001 | Briefs are reproduced by any method that produces a permanent, legible, black image on white paper, of a quality approved by the chief administrator. | CPLR 5529(a)(1). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-002 | Briefs are on white paper eleven inches along the bound edge by eight and one-half inches (Letter, 8.5 by 11 in). The First Department's record and brief paper is the same size. | CPLR 5529(a)(2); 22 NYCRR 640.2(a), 640.5(a). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-003 | The appellate court may by rule prescribe the margins, type size, typeface, line spacing and length of briefs. Neither the Second Department Part 731 text nor the First Department Part 640 text retrieved prescribes them. Templates use the conventions in NYAPPTERM-FORM-004 and -005. | CPLR 5529(a)(3); 22 NYCRR 730.1, 731.1, 731.4, 640.2, 640.5 (no typeface or margin text). | A |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-004 | Typeface: Times New Roman, the face the filed exemplars use (the Jimenez appellant's and reply briefs are set in Times New Roman; the amicus brief mixes Californian FB for its cover and text). A court rule that names a face would override this; none is retrieved. | Filed exemplars (usage/SOURCE.md); no rule. | C |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-005 | Body text is 12-point, double-spaced, with one-inch margins on all sides (the exemplars' body face is about 12 point by the text-layer font size; no margin figure is stated in the rules retrieved). | Filed exemplars (text-layer font sizes); CPLR 5529(a)(3) (margins and type set by rule, none found). | C |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-006 | Pages of briefs are numbered consecutively; front matter (statement, tables) may use lower-case roman numerals as the exemplars do. | CPLR 5529(b); filed exemplars. | C |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-007 | Quotations from the record are referenced to the source; asterisks or other means mark omissions; a quoted excerpt is referenced to the source. | CPLR 5529(d). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-008 | Decisions are cited from the official reports, if any, and also from the National Reporter System if reported there; unreported decisions are cited from the most available source. | CPLR 5529(e). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-FORM-009 | The answer to a question in the appendix does not begin a new paragraph. | CPLR 5529(f). | M |

## Cover and contents

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYAPPTERM-CONT-001 | The cover names the court (Supreme Court of the State of New York, Appellate Term, and the Department), the calendar number (upper left), the title of the action and the lower court index number; the upper right gives the name of the counsel who will argue or submit the appeal. | 22 NYCRR 640.5(a) (calendar number upper left; counsel name upper right, First Department); filed exemplars (Second Department cover: "CAL NO", "To Be Argued By"). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-CONT-002 | Where the appeal is not to be argued, it is marked submitted, and oral argument is not heard. | 22 NYCRR 640.5(b). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-CONT-003 | The appellant's brief states the questions presented, the statement of the case or facts, the argument, and the conclusion, in that order. | NYAPPTERM-CONT-004 | The appellant in an appeal from the Criminal Court states the statement required by 22 NYCRR 640.3(c). | 22 NYCRR 640.5(d) (last sentence). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-CONT-005 | The statement pursuant to CPLR 5531 (index number, parties, court, nature of action, the appealed order and the method of appeal) is included in the appellant's brief. | NYAPPTERM-CONT-006 | The main brief of any party does not exceed 50 pages (First Department), and reply briefs do not exceed 20 pages unless a justice authorizes more before filing; an additional matter added to a brief is not accepted unless the court or these rules permit it. | 22 NYCRR 640.5(d), (e). ## Filing and copies

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYAPPTERM-FILE-001 | Second Department: the appellant files the original and five copies of the brief with proof of service of one copy, within the time set by Part 731; the respondent files the original and five copies within 21 days after service of the appellant's brief; a reply brief is filed within seven days after service of the respondent's brief. | 22 NYCRR 731.4(c). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-FILE-002 | First Department: appeals from the Civil Court are heard on five copies of the record and briefs, or the clerk's return and five copies of the briefs. | 22 NYCRR 640.2(a). | M |
| NYAPPTERM-FILE-003 | Where the parties proceed on a statement in lieu of a record, the appeal is heard on five copies of the statement and briefs reproduced by an authorized method. | 22 NYCRR 640.2(c). | M |
