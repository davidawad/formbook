# New York Court of Appeals: briefs, motions for leave, reargument and amicus papers

Alias `ny`. Scope: papers filed in the Court of Appeals of the State of New York in normal course appeals and civil and criminal leave matters: principal briefs (appellant, respondent), brief on the merits, reply briefs, amicus curiae briefs, motions for leave to appeal, motions for leave to appear as amicus curiae, and motions for reargument. Verified against 22 NYCRR Part 500 (Rules of Practice of the Court of Appeals), sections 500.1, 500.13, 500.22, 500.23 and 500.24, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the official Court of Appeals pages returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21: https://www.nycourts.gov/ctapps/rules.htm and https://www.nycourts.gov/rules/trialcourts/202.shtml (the Part 202 page is the trial-court rule, not this court's). The text below is the Cornell LII reproduction of each section. Section 500.21 (general motion procedure) was not extracted, so no requirement is drawn from it here. The paper size of Court of Appeals papers is not stated in the 500.1 text retrieved; the exemplars are letter-size (NY-FORM-004).

## Typeface, size, margins and spacing (all papers prepared by word-processing)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NY-FORM-001 | Papers are printed in a serifed, proportionally spaced typeface (such as Times New Roman) or a serifed monospaced typeface (such as Courier). Narrow or condensed typefaces and condensed font spacing are not used. Except in headings, words are not in bold type or all capital letters. | 22 NYCRR 500.1(i), (j). | M |
| NY-FORM-002 | For a proportionally spaced typeface the body is printed in 14-point type; footnotes are no smaller than 12-point. | 22 NYCRR 500.1(i)(1), (j)(1). | M |
| NY-FORM-003 | Margins are one inch on all sides of the page. Text is double-spaced; quotations more than two lines long may be indented and single-spaced. Headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. Pages are numbered consecutively in the center of the bottom margin. | 22 NYCRR 500.1(l). | M |
| NY-FORM-004 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inches (letter), as the exemplars show. Not stated in 500.1 as retrieved. | Exemplars in `usage/`. | C |
| NY-FORM-005 | Typewritten papers (not computer-generated) use type no smaller than elite at no more than 12 characters per inch. | 22 NYCRR 500.1(k). | M |

## Briefs in normal course appeals (appellant, respondent, reply, brief on the merits, amicus)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NY-CONT-001 | Each brief contains a table of contents, a table of cases and authorities, questions presented, point headings, and, if necessary, a disclosure statement under 500.1(f). The disclosure statement is placed before the table of contents in the party's principal brief. | 22 NYCRR 500.13(a). | M |
| NY-CONT-002 | Each brief states the status of related litigation as of the date the brief is completed, placed before the table of contents. | 22 NYCRR 500.13(a). | M |
| NY-CONT-003 | Appellant's brief includes a statement showing that the court has jurisdiction to entertain the appeal and to review the questions raised, with citations to the pages of the record or appendix where the questions were preserved. | 22 NYCRR 500.13(a). | M |
| NY-CONT-004 | The original of each brief is signed and dated, and has the affidavit of service affixed to the inside of the back cover, identified on the front cover as the original. | 22 NYCRR 500.13(a). | M |
| NY-CONT-005 | Brief covers are white and show the caption; the name, address, telephone and facsimile number of counsel (or self-represented litigant) and of the party for whom the brief is filed; and the date the brief was completed. | 22 NYCRR 500.13(b). | M |
| NY-CONT-006 | Upper right corner of the cover shows whether the party proposes to submit without oral argument or, if argument time is requested, the amount of time and the name of the person who will argue. Plastic covers are not used. | 22 NYCRR 500.13(b). | M |
| NY-CONT-007 | Decisions not officially published, or not otherwise readily available, are included in the submission in which they are cited. | 22 NYCRR 500.1(h). | M |
| NY-LEN-001 | Principal briefs of appellant and respondent do not exceed 14,000 words. Reply briefs, amicus briefs, and briefs in response to amicus briefs do not exceed 7,000 words. The signer certifies the total word count for all printed text in the body of the brief; the certification may rely on the word-processing count. | 22 NYCRR 500.13(c)(1). | M |
| NY-LEN-002 | The brief on the merits is not separately addressed in the 500.13 text retrieved; the template treats it as a principal brief, subject to NY-LEN-001 (the exemplar is a brief of the appellant). | 22 NYCRR 500.13(c)(1) (by analogy). | C |

## Motion for leave to appeal to the Court of Appeals (petition for discretionary review)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NY-LEAVE-001 | A motion for permission to appeal in a civil case is made under 500.22. The exemplar cites "Rule 500.22" in its notice of motion. | 22 NYCRR 500.22. | M |
| NY-LEAVE-002 | The motion is a single document, bound on the left, and contains, in order: (1) a notice of motion (CPLR 2214); (2) a statement of the procedural history of the case, including a showing of timeliness, which for a first motion states the date movant was served with the order or judgment, with notice of entry (CPLR 2103(b)). Items (3) and later of 500.22(b) were not extracted; the template follows the exemplar for them. | 22 NYCRR 500.22(b)(1), (2). | M |
| NY-LEAVE-003 | The notice of motion states the return date and place, and that opposing papers are served at least two days before the return date (exemplar practice, not 500.22 text). | Exemplar `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-petition-for-discretionary-review.pdf`. | C |
