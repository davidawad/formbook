# Appellate Division, all four Judicial Departments: briefs, motion papers, leave and writ papers

Alias `nyappdiv`. Scope: papers filed in the four Departments of the Appellate Division of the Supreme Court (First, Second, Third and Fourth Judicial Departments): appellate briefs, motion papers (notice of motion with supporting affirmation or memorandum), applications for leave to appeal to the Court of Appeals, and original special proceedings (habeas corpus and other writs). Verified against 22 NYCRR Part 1250 (uniform Practice Rules of the Appellate Division), 22 NYCRR Part 1000 (Fourth Department), 22 NYCRR 600.10 (First Department), 22 NYCRR 670 (Second Department) and 22 NYCRR Part 850 (Third Department), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the official Appellate Division pages returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21: https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad4/ , https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad4/rules.shtml , https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad1/ , https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad2/ , https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/ad3/ , https://www.nycourts.gov/rules/appellatedivision/ , and the Third Department Part 850 PDF at https://nycourts.gov/ad3/clerk/rules-of-practice/Rules-of-Practice-Part-850.pdf (403). The rule text below is the Cornell LII reproduction of each Part; LII's undated copies were read, not the court-posted text. The statute the rules cite (CPLR 2101, 2214, 5513, 5516, 5531, 5602) was not retrieved: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/CPL/5529 returned a JavaScript-only page, so statutory cites here are to the rule text, not to the statute. Part 800 (cited in older secondary sources for the Third Department) is no longer the Third Department's rule part; a search summary of the court's own rules page says it was rescinded in 2018 and replaced by Part 850, which LII reproduces (see below). The Second Department's Part 670 has no brief-form section on LII (section 670.10 reads "Repealed"), so its briefs are governed by Part 1250.

| NYAPPDIV-MOT-004 | Motion papers do not have a separate typeface rule in Part 1250.
