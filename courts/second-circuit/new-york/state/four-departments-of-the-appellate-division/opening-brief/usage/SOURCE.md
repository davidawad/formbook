## What the exemplar shows (pages viewed)

Contact sheets of all 93 pages at 60 dpi were made and viewed for pages 1–48 (sheets 1–4). Pages 49–93 were not viewed; claims below rest on pages 1–48 and the text layer.

- Cover (p. 1): NYSCEF header line at the top, "To Be Argued By / Time Requested" block on the upper right, court name in small caps, caption with "A.D. No." on the right, title "APPELLANT'S BRIEF" between rules, counsel block lower left.
- Front matter numbered in lowercase roman (TOC = `i`, TOA = `ii`–`vii`). The body restarts at arabic `1` (Preliminary Statement, p. 1).
- TOC: "PRELIMINARY STATEMENT", "STATEMENT OF FACTS", "ARGUMENT" with point titles, "CONCLUSION", with dot leaders and right-aligned page numbers.
- TOA: "CASES" heading with "Page(s)" at right; each entry in italic case name, citation on an indented second line, dot leaders to page numbers.
- Body: double-spaced serif text; first-line indent for paragraphs; section headings centred and underlined or bold capitals ("PRELIMINARY STATEMENT", "STATEMENT OF FACTS", "CONCLUSION"); point headings "POINT I" centred bold above a centred bold title; sub-headings "A." bold, run-in justified.
- Block quotations single-spaced and indented from both sides.
- Record cites in the body are "(A 2-12)" (appendix pages), not "R."
