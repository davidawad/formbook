## Why this exemplar

- Found after a search of CourtListener (no nyappdiv reply briefs indexed) and general web searches for Appellate Division reply briefs (see `../README.md`, "Exemplar search"). It is the only public, text-extractable reply brief from any department found in this run.
- It is a reply brief in this court (the First Department), so it is the right doc type. Its printing specifications statement states the form it was filed under: 22 NYCRR 1250.8(j), Times New Roman, 14 point, double spaced, and a word count of 6,915 (under the 7,000-word limit of 1250.8(f)(2)).
- Its cover (argument notice in the upper right; counsel in the lower right) and its order of parts (cover, table of contents, table of authorities, Preliminary Statement, Argument, Conclusion, printing statement) show the 1250.8 form in practice.

## Limits of the exemplar

- It has no separate proof of service or certificate of service in this copy: the printing statement is the last page. The proof-of-service page in the template is a blank form, not transcribed from this exemplar.
- It has no statement of questions presented and no statement of the case: the Court-of-Appeals-style sections do not appear in a reply under 1250.8(d).
