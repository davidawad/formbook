## Status

The 1846 New York Constitution merged the separate common-law and equity courts into a single court system, so a chancery court with its own rules did not survive into the modern system. The name appears in the record only as the title of an 1840s law reporter, the *Saratoga Chancery Sentinel*, which printed decisions of the state Court of Chancery (1841 to 1847). That reporter is not a court. No current Saratoga court of this name was found; the local court in Saratoga is the Town of Saratoga court, which is not a chancery court.

A 2026-10-21 search for the court's own rules found no court of this name, so there was no rules source to fetch.
