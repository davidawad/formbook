Alias ILNB. This inventory covers the court's existing adversary-complaint and motion-brief document types. Verified 2026-10-17 against the federal bankruptcy rules and official local-rules source below. Check current local rules, judge procedures, and case-specific orders at filing.

## Requirements shared by bankruptcy filings

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILNB-FORM-001 | M | A document is legible and follows the applicable form and caption requirements. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9004(a)-(b) |
| ILNB-FORM-002 | M | A pleading has a caption with the court name, title, case number, and appropriate proceeding designation. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7010; Fed. R. Civ. P. 10(a) |
| ILNB-CERT-001 | M | Each pleading, written motion, and other paper is signed by an attorney of record or an unrepresented party; signature identifies the signer and required contact information. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9011(a) |
| ILNB-CERT-002 | M | Service and proof of service follow applicable Bankruptcy Rules; electronic service does not eliminate service on parties who are not registered for electronic notice. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7004, 7005, 9014; applicable local rules |
| ILNB-ELEC-001 | M | E-filing, service, PDF format, and signature procedures follow the court's current local rules and ECF procedures. | Official local rules source above; Fed. R. Bankr. P. 5005 |
| ILNB-LEN-001 | A | No universal typeface, margin, spacing, or word/page limit is asserted here. Check current local rules, judge procedures, and any case-specific order. | Official local rules source above; Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9029 |

## Document-type differences

### adversary-complaint

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILNB-CONT-001 | M | An adversary proceeding is commenced by complaint; Civil Rules 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11 apply through Bankruptcy Rule 7003. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7003 |
| ILNB-CONT-002 | M | State whether the party consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7008(a) |
| ILNB-CONT-003 | M | A complaint states a short and plain statement of jurisdiction, claim, and requested relief; allegations are numbered as applicable. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 8(a), 10(b), applied by Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7003 |
| ILNB-COVER-001 | C | The complaint starts with the prescribed caption and document title; FRBP 9004 does not prescribe a decorative cover. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7010, 9004 |
| ILNB-CERT-003 | M | Summons and complaint are served as required by Bankruptcy Rules 7004 and 7005; file proof where the rules or local procedure require it. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7004, 7005 |

### motion-brief

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILNB-CONT-004 | M | A motion is written, states with particularity its grounds, and identifies the relief sought. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9013 |
| ILNB-CONT-005 | M | In an adversary proceeding, Civil Rule 12 and other applicable Civil Rules apply through the corresponding Bankruptcy Rules; the supporting brief states the arguments and authorities for the requested relief. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7012, 9014; Fed. R. Civ. P. 7(b)(1) |
| ILNB-COVER-002 | C | Use the case caption and a title that describes the motion or supporting memorandum; no separate cover is prescribed by FRBP 9004. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9004; official local rules source above |
| ILNB-LEN-002 | A | Check the local rule governing the specific motion for briefing, notice, hearing, memorandum, and proposed-order requirements. | Official local rules source above; Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9013, 9029 |
