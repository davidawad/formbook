# ilcb: motion with memorandum (U.S. Bankruptcy Court, C.D. Illinois)

Doc type: a motion filed in an adversary proceeding, with its memorandum of law (FRBP 9013; FRBP 7012(b) for a Rule 12(b) motion). The exemplars are the memoranda filed with a motion to dismiss. Court alias `ilcb`. Court-level status is in `../../court.json` and is not changed here.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `usage/` | Two filed memoranda in support of motions to dismiss, from two different filers, with `SOURCE.md`. |
| `typst/ilcb-motion-brief.typ` | The template: `ilcb-motion-brief(meta, md)`. Content-agnostic; each number is a named parameter at the top. |
| `typst/main.typ` | Entry point; reads the sample named by `--input sample=...`. |
| `usage/INPUT-motion-brief.md` | Transcribed in full from the first exemplar (Menard, Inc., Ottosen DiNolfo, Doc 21). |
| `usage/OUTPUT-motion-brief.typst.pdf` | Our typst render (13 pages); `OUTPUT-motion-brief.docx` and `.docx.pdf` the docx render (13 pages, the same lines and pages). |
| `usage/OVERLAY-reinbold-menard-motion-brief.pdf`, `.txt` | Red/blue overlay against the first exemplar, and the page map. |

File names follow the repo's role convention (`INPUT-`, `OUTPUT-`, `OVERLAY-`) because `tools/render.sh` and `tools/overlay.sh` read and write those names.

Build (from the repo root, with the toolchain on PATH):

```sh
sh tools/render.sh courts/seventh-circuit/illinois/federal/bankruptcy-central-district/motion-brief usage/INPUT-motion-brief.md
sh tools/overlay.sh courts/seventh-circuit/illinois/federal/bankruptcy-central-district/motion-brief reinbold-menard-motion-brief courts/seventh-circuit/illinois/federal/bankruptcy-central-district/motion-brief/usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-motion-brief.pdf
```

The overlay takes the exemplar as an explicit argument, because its default (the alphabetically first exemplar) is the Walmart memorandum, not the Menard memorandum the sample comes from.

## Markdown contract

A sample is one `.md` file: YAML frontmatter, then the body.

| Field | Required | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| `caption.left`, `caption.right` | yes | party lines and case lines, aligned by row (see the adversary README). |
| `document_title` | yes | the title, centered and bold. |
| `dated` | no | "Dated: ..." line above the signature block (the Menard exemplar has none). |
| `signature.firm`, `signature.signer` | yes | the firm (bold) and the typed name after "By: /s/". |
| `signature.counsel` | no | counsel lines at the left margin under the signature. |
| `service.date`, `service.document`, `service.recipients` | no | the certificate of service: "a copy of the foregoing <document> was filed ... on <date>", then each recipient's lines. Omit `service` for no certificate. |

Body: `#` is a centered bold section heading (I. INTRODUCTION, IV. CONCLUSION). `##` is a bold heading at the left (A., B.); `###` bold italic (i., ii.); `####` italic. Headings keep the author's numbers. Block quotations are `>`. Footnotes use `[^key]` with a definition anywhere in the file (the template carries definitions to every render call). Case names are italic because the author italicizes them.

## Overlay

`usage/OVERLAY-reinbold-menard-motion-brief.pdf`: red is our render, blue is the filed Menard memorandum, black is where they agree. The page map (`.txt`) pairs our pages with the filed pages by shared words: our pages 1 to 10 pair with filed pages 1 to 10 (word overlap 44.5% to 86.2%); our page 11 pairs with filed page 12, our 12 with 13, 13 with 14, 14 with 15. So from page 11 on our pagination runs one page ahead of the filed copy in the overlay's reckoning. The differences that the overlay shows are tier 2 or tier 3: the typeface (EB Garamond against the filed copy's Arial), the page breaks (the filer's line and page breaks differ from ours), and the filed copy's own footer, "Page X of 15", where the template prints a centered page number. The filed copy also carries the CM/ECF header ("Case 19-08110 Doc 21 Filed ...") that the court system adds on filing, which the template does not generate.

## Gaps

- No CDIL typography rule exists (the local rules set none for motions). Face, size, spacing and margins are tier 2, from the exemplars. The Walmart exemplar uses Times, the Menard exemplar Arial; the template uses Times New Roman 12 pt, the house standard, because no court rule names a face.
- Only the memorandum is templated, not the motion paper itself (the notice of motion, the motion text, and the hearing notice). No exemplar of a standalone motion was found in CourtListener for this court; the Walmart and Menard exemplars are memoranda. The template prints whatever body the markdown carries.
- No table of contents or table of authorities: neither exemplar has one, and the local rules set no requirement. Page limits: none set by the local rules; none are enforced.
- The proposed order is a separate PDF. The Practice Guide sets its format (8.5 x 11 page size, a 4-inch top margin on page 1, no judge signature line, uploaded as a PDF). That is not in this template.
- FRBP 9004(c) (paper size) was not retrieved (see ILCB-9004-1). US Letter is used, the size of both exemplars.
- FRBP 7012 was not fetched; the rule is cited as the exemplars cite it.
- The CM/ECF header ("Case ... Doc ... Filed ...") is added by the court system on filing, not by the template.
- The second exemplar (Walmart, Sandberg Phoenix, 11 pages) was read as text for the caption, heading and signature pattern. It was not overlaid.

## Engines

Both engines are on the shared cross-engine layout model (`lib/typst/xengine.typ`, `lib/docx/xengine.lua`; `docs/EVAL-LOOP.md`, "Cross-engine gate"; fb-595.68.27), and `eval.yaml` gates it (`params.cross_engine: gate`): the docx breaks every line and page where typst does.

- **Layout** (both engines): Times New Roman 12 pt; the body in 27.6 pt line boxes (Word's double line), a 0.5 in first-line indent; the court lines, caption rows, title, headings, block quotations, signature, counsel lines and certificate of service in 13.8 pt boxes (a single line); block quotations half an inch in on both sides, a single line around them; footnotes 11 pt in 12.65 pt boxes, 7 pt apart; a body line between the court lines and the caption and between the caption and the title, a single line around each heading. The caption is one row per frontmatter line (party line, ")", case line; columns 3.4 in and 0.3 in). Level-1 headings centered bold, level 2 bold, level 3 bold italic, level 4 italic. The last paragraph is kept with the signature block; the certificate of service follows a body line below it, kept together.
- **typst** (primary): `typst/ilcb-motion-brief.typ`, 13 pages.
- **docx**: `docx/reference.xml` (the `base="xengine"` style recipe in the typst face, `{font}`; Body Keep, Body Text Note, Footnote Text; pandoc's fancy lists off) and `docx/ilcb-motion-brief.lua` (court lines, caption table, title, headings, block quotations, signature block and certificate of service with exact spacing; body paragraphs and footnotes left to pandoc). `fb render --engine docx` builds the reference document, runs pandoc and converts with LibreOffice: `usage/OUTPUT-motion-brief.docx` and `.docx.pdf`, 13 pages. No shell script (`build-reference.sh` and the binary `reference.docx` are gone, fb-595.68.27). Not opened in Word.
- **Checks** (2026-10-22): typst `RESULT: 9 checks passed, 0 failed`; docx `RESULT: 13 checks passed, 0 failed`, XENG-001..003 PASS (13 pages each, 332/332 lines). The docx was EB Garamond 13 pt until then (DEF-FONT-002 and TNR-EMBEDDED failed; 96/331 lines alike).
- **Pages reviewed** (2026-10-22, fb-595.68.27): all 13 pages of the typst and of the docx render, side by side at 45 dpi: caption, title, headings at three levels, the block quotations on pages 3 to 4 and 8, footnote 1 on page 7, the signature block and the certificate of service on page 13 are alike in both; no defect found. The overlay and page reviews above were made on the earlier EB Garamond renders.
