## On the FRCP base (fb-595.17.15, 2026-10-29)

## Layout

- Cover (no page number): bold court title with the division; 24 pt; the caption as filed, a party column (names, designations, "v.", a blank single line between the entries) closed by a rule on its right and below, the case number at the right on its first line; 24 pt; the title centered bold in capitals; 48 pt; the counsel block in the right half of the page (3.25 in), a blank single line between its groups.
- Table of contents (page i) and table of authorities (page ii): generated. Each: the title centered bold, a blank double line, the column label ("Page", "Page(s)") at the right, a blank single line above each row; the text wraps before a page column (0.5 in contents, 0.75 in authorities) and a dot leader runs from three spaces after it to the page (the cross-engine `listing-row`). The contents come from the body's headings (levels 1 to 3, level 1 in capitals, each deeper level half an inch in, a wrapped title half an inch further) and the certificate. The authorities come from the links `[text](toa:key)` in the body, grouped Cases, Constitutional Provisions, Statutes, Regulations, Rules, Other Authorities, sorted by name; a key cited on seven or more pages prints "passim".
- Body (arabic numbers from 1): double-spaced, every paragraph indented half an inch; headings at the body pitch as typed, kept with the text after them: level 1 centered bold capitals; level 2 bold, its later lines hanging half an inch; level 3 the same half an inch in; level 4 bold italic. Footnotes 10 pt in 11.5 pt lines, 6 pt apart, kept whole, under a 30 % rule. Block quotations single-spaced, half an inch in on both sides, a single line above and below.
- The last section (from the last level 1 heading: the conclusion) and the signature block are kept on one page: "Dated:" at the left, and in the right half "Respectfully submitted,", the firm, "/s/ name" and the typed lines, a blank single line between the parts.
- Certificate of compliance (only when `type_volume: true`), on its own page, listed in the contents.
- Cross-engine layout model (`lib/typst/xengine.typ`, `lib/docx/xengine.lua`): first-fit ragged-right lines, no kerning, ligatures or hyphenation, exact line boxes; the docx breaks every line and page where typst does.

## Markdown contract (for samples)

- YAML frontmatter, then the body.
- Frontmatter: `case_number`; `division` (required, LR 40.1(E)(1)); `caption.parties` (as in the complaint); `brief_title`; `counsel` (list of `{names, lines}`, the cover counsel block); `date`; `type_volume` (bool; prints the certificate); `signature` (`firm` lines, `signer`, `lines` under the "/s/" line); `authorities` (registry of `{kind, name, cite}`: kind is `case`, `constitution`, `statute`, `regulation`, `rule` or `other`; a case name is printed italic; a key appears in the table only if the body links to it).
- Body: `#` level 1, `##` level 2, `###` level 3, `####` level 4. Paragraphs are not numbered. Footnotes: `text[^key]` and `[^key]: text` at any point in the file. Citations: `[*Case v. Name*, 1 U.S. 1, 2 (2000)](toa:key)`; short forms link to the same key.
- Heading numbers ("I.", "A.") are typed in the markdown text. The ca9 contract numbers headings automatically; this template does not, because the numbering rule depends on the parent section and the rendered text should equal the markdown. The filer types the numbers.
- Not supported: tables (the contents and authorities are generated), images, raw typst.
- The word count is computed from the body markdown: link targets, footnote labels and markers are removed; everything else in the body counts.

## Known gaps and open choices

1. **One exemplar; the sample is a portion.** The brief asks for one exemplar per doc type. The filing is a memorandum of 21 text pages by the Attorney General's office for a state defendant. The sample transcribes the cover, introduction, background, legal standard, the opening of each argument point, the conclusion, the signature and the certificate. Point II and Point III are not transcribed past their openings, and the footnote 1 text stops at the point marked ". . . ." in the sample.
2. **Table of contents and authorities page numbers are ours.** They are generated from our render, not transcribed from the filing. The filing's own tables carry the filing's page numbers, which will not match a transcription of part of the filing. The authorities table lists only the authorities the sample cites, so it is shorter than the filed table.
3. **Heading hanging indent.** The typed label is followed by a space, not a tab, so a wrapped level 2 heading (Points II and III) aligns its second line at 0.5 in, slightly right of the first line's text. Accepted (tier 2, minor); a tab-aligned heading would need a change to the markdown contract.
4. **Word and page limits are not enforced.** The template's word count is an approximation of the word processor's count and is not the filer's certified count: the sample's certificate reads 1,771 words, which is the count of the transcribed portion, not the filed memorandum's 6,810. LR 7.1(B)(4)(c) lets the certificate rely on the word-processing count, so the filer must confirm the number before filing. The 15-page limit (LR 7.1(B)(4)(a)) is not checked: a memorandum over 15 pages needs the certificate; under 15 pages it does not. The template prints the certificate only when `type_volume: true`.
5. **No certificate of service.** The filing has none (CM/ECF generates notice). The template has no service certificate; whether the court wants one is not established here.
6. **Cover layout.** The cover counsel block sits in the right half of the page, as in the filing; the caption is the filing's block caption (party column ruled on its right and below). The filing centers the case number beside the party column; ours stands on the column's first line (a vertical centering the two engines would place differently).
7. **Font.** Times New Roman at 12 pt, the filing's face (LR 5.1(B)(2) names none). LR 5.1(B)(2) allows 14 pt; not decided here.
8. **The motion itself and the proposed order** are separate filings and are not produced. Rule 7.1(B)(1) requires the memorandum to identify the rule under which the motion is filed; the template does not check that the title does.
9. **Selftest.** `fb check` runs the default checks on this doc-type folder (see the checks row above); the folder has no selftest of its own. DEF-LEN-001 is skipped: no machine-readable page limit (the 15-page limit of LR 7.1(B)(4)(a) has the word-count alternative, gap 4).
10. **Footnote 1 (fixed).** The footnote definitions are rendered with the part of the body that holds their references (typst moves them ahead of the split before the conclusion); footnote 1 prints at the foot of body page 2 in both engines.

## Engines

**docx** (`docx/`): `docx/reference.xml` holds the Word styles (`base="xengine"`: the cross-engine recipe of `tools/fb/src/docxxengine.rs` in the typst face `{font}`, Times New Roman 12 pt; Body Text in exact 480-twip lines with a 720-twip first-line indent; single 276 twips; Block Text 276 above and below; Footnote Text 10 pt in 230-twip lines, 120 after, kept whole; added Body Text Noted, without widow control, for a paragraph that anchors a footnote, and Body Text Keep for the conclusion). `docx/ilcd-motion-brief.lua` (on `lib/docx/xengine.lua`) writes the cover, the tables, the headings, the signature block and the certificate with the typst pitches and spaces in twips, and the three sections (cover without a number, tables in lower roman, body from 1) with its own page-number footer in a 13.8 pt line, 735 twips above the page foot; body paragraphs and footnotes are pandoc's. The tables' page numbers come from fb render's passes: the filter writes needles (table titles, headings, citations in document order) and reads back the pages of the LibreOffice PDF until they settle. `fb render --engine docx` builds `reference.docx` from the xml; no shell, no committed `reference.docx`.
