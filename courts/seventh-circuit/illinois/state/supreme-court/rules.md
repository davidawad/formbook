# Illinois Supreme Court: briefs, petitions for leave to appeal, motions, supervisory-order motions and amicus briefs

Alias `ill`. Scope: the filings a party or amicus files in the Illinois Supreme Court: brief (appellant's brief on the merits, appellee's brief, reply brief), petition for leave to appeal (PLA, the petition for review and the petition for discretionary review, which are the same filing in Illinois), motion and supporting memorandum, motion for supervisory order (petition for writ, Rule 383), and amicus curiae brief (Rule 345). Verified against Illinois Supreme Court Rules, compiled October 21, 2026 (PDF), on 2026-10-21. Rule 367 (petition for rehearing) is not covered: no exemplar.

## Briefs (brief on the merits, answering brief, reply brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILL-CONT-001 | Cover shows the case number and the name of the Supreme Court; the court or agency below and the case name as it appeared there, with each party's status in the Supreme Court (for example, plaintiff-appellee); the name of the trial judge who entered the judgment; and the individual names and addresses of the attorneys and their firm. | SCR 341(d) | M |
| ILL-CONT-002 | Appellant's brief sections, in order: (1) table of contents and "Points and Authorities" (headings of the points and subpoints with the authorities under each, and page references); (2) introductory paragraph (nature of the action, judgment and whether a jury verdict, and any pleading question); (3) issues presented for review; the standard of review for each issue; (4) "Jurisdiction"; (5) statutes or provisions involved verbatim, when the case turns on one; (6) statement of facts with record references; (7) argument; (8) short conclusion stating the precise relief, followed by counsel's names; (9) appendix. | SCR 341(h)(1)-(9) | M |
| ILL-CONT-003 | Appellee's brief follows the same form, except that items (2), (3), (4), (5), (6) and (9) need not be included unless the appellant's presentation is unsatisfactory. Its table of contents and Points and Authorities, argument and conclusion remain required. | SCR 341(i) | M |
| ILL-CONT-004 | Reply brief is confined strictly to replying to the appellee's arguments and contains only the argument. | SCR 341(j) | M |
| ILL-CONT-005 | Standard of review: a concise statement for each issue with citation to authority, either in the discussion of the issue or under a separate heading placed before it. | SCR 341(h) (paragraph after (3)) | M |
| ILL-CONT-006 | Citations are made as provided in Rule 6. | SCR 341(g) | M |
| ILL-CONT-007 | Certificate of compliance, signed by the attorney, certifying that the brief conforms to Rules 341(a) and (b) and stating the length in pages or words, excluding the cover, the table of contents and Points and Authorities, the certificate of compliance, the certificate of service and matter appended under Rule 342(a). | SCR 341(c) | M |
| ILL-CONT-008 | Proof of service is filed with all briefs; the brief is served on each other party represented by separate counsel. The template prints a certificate of service when `certificates` includes `service`. | SCR 341(e), 11, 12 | M |
| ILL-CONT-009 | Points not argued are forfeited and may not be raised in the reply brief, at oral argument, or on rehearing. Content rule. | SCR 341(h)(7) | M |
| ILL-CONT-010 | Parties are referred to as in the trial court (plaintiff, defendant, or by name or description), not as appellant and appellee or petitioner and respondent. Juvenile and mental-health cases follow Rule 341(f). | SCR 341(f) | M |
| ILL-CONT-011 | Appendix (appellant's brief): a table of contents to the appendix, the judgment, any opinion or findings, pleadings or record materials that are the basis of the appeal, the notice of appeal and the table of contents of the record; pages numbered with the letter "A" before the number. A separate appendix PDF is labeled "Separate Appendix" when the combined PDF would exceed 150 megabytes. The appendix is a separate filing component, not part of the template's rendered body. | SCR 342(a) | M |
| ILL-LEN-001 | Appellant's brief and appellee's brief: no more than 50 pages or 15,000 words; reply brief: no more than 20 pages or 6,000 words. The limit excludes the cover, the table of contents and Points and Authorities, the certificate of compliance, the certificate of service, and the matter appended under Rule 342(a). The template's `params.page_limit` is set to the page alternative (50 for the appellant's and appellee's briefs, 20 for the reply brief), counted over the whole rendered brief body. | SCR 341(b)(1) | M |
| ILL-LEN-002 | A cross-appellant or cross-appellee has an extra 30 pages or 9,000 words; the cross-appellant's reply brief is limited to 20 pages or 6,000 words. Not templated: the eval page limit is the non-cross default. | SCR 341(b)(1) | M |
| ILL-LEN-003 | A motion to file a brief in excess of the length limit is not favored; it is filed at least 10 days before the brief is due (5 days before a reply), states the excess pages or words and the grounds, and is supported by a Rule 1-109 certification or affidavit. | SCR 341(b)(2) | M |

## Petitions for leave to appeal (petition for review and petition for discretionary review)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILL-PLA-001 | The petition for leave to appeal is filed within 35 days after the Appellate Court judgment, or after the order denying rehearing if a timely rehearing petition was filed. The filing date is content, not template. | SCR 315(b)(1) | M |
| ILL-PLA-002 | Contents in this order: (1) a prayer for leave to appeal; (2) the date of the judgment and whether a rehearing petition was filed, with the date of its denial; (3) the points relied upon for review; (4) a fair and accurate statement of the facts, with record references; (5) a short argument with authorities stating why review is warranted and why the Appellate Court decision should be reversed or modified; (6) an appendix with the Appellate Court opinion or order and any record documents needed. | SCR 315(c)(1)-(6) | M |
| ILL-PLA-003 | The petition is prepared under the brief requirements of Rules 341 through 343 (form, cover, margins, typeface, spacing, page numbering, certificate of compliance, service) except that it is limited to 20 pages or 6,000 words, excluding the items excluded by Rule 341(b)(1). The template's `params.page_limit` is 20. | SCR 315(d) | M |
| ILL-PLA-004 | Cover is white (petitioner's petition). The caption carries "On Petition for Leave to Appeal from the Appellate Court ..." and the lower-court docket and judge. Captioning and the Supreme Court docket number follow the exemplars. | SCR 341(d); usage/SOURCE.md | M |
| ILL-PLA-005 | A petition in a pretrial-release case (Article 110) or child-custody case carries the expedited-disposition caption on the top of the front page (ILL-FORM-012). | SCR 315(i)(1), 315(k)(1) | M |

## Motions (motion and supporting memorandum)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILL-MOT-001 | An application for relief is made by a written motion that states the relief sought and the grounds. Argument not in the motion may be made in a supporting memorandum. | SCR 361(a) | M |
| ILL-MOT-002 | Facts not of record are supported by an affidavit or a verification by certification under section 1-109 of the Code of Civil Procedure. | SCR 361(a) | M |
| ILL-MOT-003 | The motion is filed with proof of service; a proposed order phrased in the alternative ("Allowed" or "Denied") accompanies it and is served on all counsel. The clerk does not accept a motion without the proposed order. The proposed order is a separate document the filer prepares, not generated by this template. | SCR 361(b)(1)-(2) | M |
| ILL-MOT-004 | Responses are due within 7 days after personal or e-mail service (10 days by mail or third-party carrier), unless the court allows further time. | SCR 361(b)(3) | M |
| ILL-MOT-005 | Form: Rule 361 sets no typeface, margin or spacing rule for a motion. The template follows the Rule 341(a) format as the court's convention (tier 2), so the motion is no less than 12-point, double-spaced, with the Rule 341(a) margins. Cover color: none. | SCR 341(a); usage/SOURCE.md | C |
| ILL-MOT-006 | Motions to continue a stay of mandate, and the other motions for relief in the reviewing court, are caption-and-title documents in the same form as the motion above; the title names the relief sought. | SCR 361(a); usage/SOURCE.md | C |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILL-AMI-001 | An amicus brief may be filed only by leave of court, or at the court's request. The motion for leave is accompanied by the proposed brief, states the applicant's interest, and explains how the brief will assist the court. | SCR 345(a) | M |
| ILL-AMI-002 | The amicus brief follows the form prescribed for the appellee's brief, identifies the amicus as such on the cover, and conforms to any conditions the court imposes. Its cover color is the color of the party whose position it supports. | SCR 345(b), 341(d) | M |
| ILL-AMI-003 | Unless the court specifies otherwise, the amicus brief is filed on or before the due date of the initial brief of the party it supports. | SCR 345(b) | M |
| ILL-AMI-004 | Amicus curiae does not argue orally. | SCR 345(c) | M |
| ILL-AMI-005 | No length limit is set for an amicus brief by Rule 345 or 341(b) (which covers the appellant and appellee). The template's `params.page_limit` is not set. Exemplar length is tier 2 only. | SCR 345, 341(b)(1) | C |
