# Appellate Court of Illinois: briefs and petitions

Alias `illappct`. Scope: briefs in civil appeals to the Appellate Court of Illinois (opening, answering, reply, amicus and motion briefs) and the petition for review. Verified against Illinois Supreme Court Rule 341 (amended Sept. 30, 2020) and the Illinois Courts Appellate Court brief guide (edition 04/26) on 2026-10-21.

## Opening brief (appellant's brief)

|---|---|---|---|
| ILAPP-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inch; text is clear black on white pages. | R341 (a) | M |
| ILAPP-FORM-002 | Left margin at least 1½ inch; top, right and bottom margins at least 1 inch. Each page is numbered within the bottom margin. | R341 (a) | M |
| ILAPP-FORM-003 | Typeface is 12-point or larger throughout, including quoted material and footnotes. Condensed type is prohibited. | R341 (a) | M |
| ILAPP-FORM-004 | Text is double-spaced; headings may be single-spaced. Quotations of two or more lines may be single-spaced. Footnotes are discouraged; single-spaced footnotes are allowed. | R341 (a) | M |
| ILAPP-FORM-005 | Typeface is Times New Roman (house standard). Rule 341 names no face. | R341 (a) | C |
| ILAPP-LEN-001 | Appellant's brief is limited to 50 pages or 15,000 words; the limit excludes the cover, Points and Authorities, certificate of compliance, certificate of service and appended matter. | R341 (b); GUIDE | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-001 | Cover contains: the case number and name of the Appellate Court; the name of the lower court or agency; the case name as in the lower tribunal with each party's status in the reviewing court (e.g., plaintiff-appellant); the name of the trial judge; and the names, addresses and firm of the filing attorney. Appellant's brief cover is white. | R341 (d); GUIDE | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-002 | Table of contents with a "Points and Authorities" summary statement listing points argued and authorities cited in the Argument, with page references. | R341 (h)(1) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-003 | Introductory paragraph: nature of the action, the judgment appealed from and whether it rests on a jury verdict, and whether any question is raised on the pleadings. | R341 (h)(2) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-004 | Statement of the issues presented for review, without detail or citation. The standard of review for each issue is stated with citation, in the argument or under a separate heading before it. | R341 (h)(3) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-005 | Statement of jurisdiction under the heading "Jurisdiction", stating the supreme court rule or other law conferring jurisdiction, the facts that bring the appeal within it, and the date of the order appealed and other timeliness facts. | R341 (h)(4)(ii) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-006 | Statement of statutes or provisions involved, verbatim with citation, only when the case involves the construction or validity of a statute, constitutional provision, treaty, ordinance or regulation. | R341 (h)(5) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-007 | Statement of facts, accurate and fair, without argument, with references to the record on appeal. | R341 (h)(6) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-008 | Argument, with citations to authority and to the pages of the record relied on. Evidence is not copied at length. | R341 (h)(7) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-009 | Short conclusion stating the precise relief sought, followed by the names of counsel as on the cover. | R341 (h)(8) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-010 | Appendix as required by Rule 342; its cover is the same color as the brief it accompanies. | R341 (h)(9), (d) | M |
| ILAPP-CERT-001 | Certificate of compliance, signed by counsel: "I certify that this brief conforms to the requirements of Rules 341(a) and (b)," with the length of the brief stated in pages or words. | R341 (c) | M |
| ILAPP-CERT-002 | Proof of service on each other party represented by separate counsel, filed with the brief. | R341 (e) | M |
| ILAPP-CONT-011 | Parties are referred to as in the trial court (plaintiff, defendant), not appellant or appellee, or by name or descriptive term. | R341 (f) | M |
| ILAPP-ELEC-001 | Electronically filed brief is the official original; duplicate paper copies, if required, are one-sided and bound on the left. | R341 (e) | M |
