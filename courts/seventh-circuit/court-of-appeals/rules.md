# Seventh Circuit Court of Appeals: rules

Alias `ca7`. Covers opening, answering, and reply briefs. Verified 2026-10-17 against the current rules linked by the Court's rules page; its posted rules PDF states current through December 1, 2025.

## Requirements shared by the three brief types

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA7-FORM-001 | M | Brief pages are letter size, 8 1/2 by 11 inches. | FRAP 32(a)(4), CA7 PDF p. 86 |
| CA7-FORM-002 | M | At least one-inch margins on all sides; page numbers may be in margins. | FRAP 32(a)(4), CA7 PDF p. 86 |
| CA7-FORM-003 | M | Text is double-spaced; block quotations more than two lines, headings, and footnotes may be single-spaced and indented. | FRAP 32(a)(4), CA7 PDF p. 86 |
| CA7-FORM-004 | M | Body text is at least 12-point proportional type; footnotes are at least 11-point. The rule does not name a typeface; FRAP 32(a)(5)(A) requires a proportionally spaced face with serifs. | Circuit Rule 32(b), CA7 PDF p. 89; FRAP 32(a)(5)(A), PDF p. 86 |
| CA7-FORM-005 | M | Type is plain roman; italics or boldface may be used for emphasis, and case names may be italicized or underlined. | FRAP 32(a)(6), CA7 PDF p. 86 |
| CA7-LEN-001 | M | Principal briefs are limited to 14,000 words; reply briefs to 7,000 words. Footnotes count. | Circuit Rule 32(c), CA7 PDF p. 89; FRAP 32(f), PDF p. 88 |
| CA7-LEN-002 | M | Exclude the items enumerated in FRAP 32(f), including cover, tables of contents and authorities, disclosure statement, certificates, signature, and proof of service. | FRAP 32(f), CA7 PDF p. 88 |
| CA7-COVER-001 | M | Cover includes docket number, court, case title, nature of proceeding and court/agency below, brief title, and counsel name, address, and telephone. | FRAP 32(a)(2), CA7 PDF p. 86 |
| CA7-CERT-001 | M | A word-count brief includes a certificate stating word count, typeface and type-style compliance, and the applicable length limit; it is signed. | FRAP 32(g)(1), CA7 PDF p. 88 |
| CA7-CERT-002 | M | A disclosure statement is filed as required by Circuit Rule 26.1; include it in the brief when the rule requires it. | Circuit Rule 26.1, CA7 PDF; FRAP 26.1 |
| CA7-CERT-003 | M | A certificate of service is required when applicable under FRAP 25(d); electronic service under the appellate system is governed by Circuit Rule 25. | FRAP 25(d); Circuit Rule 25 |
| CA7-ELEC-001 | M | Electronically filed documents must comply with the Court's electronic filing procedures and PDF requirements. | Circuit Rule 25 and Court's ECF procedures, CA7 PDF |
| CA7-ELEC-002 | M | Every filing is signed as required by FRAP 32(d) and Circuit Rule 25; electronic signature follows the Court's ECF procedures. | FRAP 32(d); Circuit Rule 25 |
| CA7-CONT-001 | C | Page numbering, cover arrangement, and typography beyond the express requirements above follow common appellate practice; these choices are compared across the filed examples and are not asserted as mandates. | Rules leave these choices open; exemplars identified in the corresponding `exemplars/SOURCE.md` files |

## Document-type differences

| Doc type | Stable requirement | Kind | Requirement | Primary source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `opening-brief` | CA7-CONT-003 | M | Appellant's brief contains the items and argument organization required by FRAP 28(a), including jurisdiction, issues, case, summary, argument, conclusion, and the required appendix references. | FRAP 28(a), CA7 PDF pp. 76-78 |
| `opening-brief` | CA7-CONT-004 | M | Include the statement of issues, jurisdictional statement, statement of the case with record references, summary, argument with standards of review, and conclusion seeking precise relief. | FRAP 28(a)(2)-(9); Circuit Rules 28, 30 |
| `answering-brief` | CA7-CONT-005 | M | Appellee's brief contains the items required by FRAP 28(b), including the appellee's response to appellant's jurisdictional statement when disputed or incomplete, and the appellee's argument. | FRAP 28(b); Circuit Rule 28(b) |
| `answering-brief` | CA7-LEN-002 | M | Principal brief word-count ceiling is 14,000 words. | Circuit Rule 32(c) |
| `reply-brief` | CA7-CONT-006 | M | Reply brief contains only the reply required by FRAP 28(c); it need not repeat the statement of the case or other components except as permitted by rule. | FRAP 28(c) |
| `reply-brief` | CA7-LEN-003 | M | Reply brief is limited to 7,000 words. | Circuit Rule 32(c) |

## Template contract

| ID | Kind | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| CA7-TOOL-001 | T | Template contains no case-specific text, measured PDF coordinates, or forced line breaks; source content remains supplied by the input. |
| CA7-REVIEW-001 | T | Any visual review covers every rendered page, as required by `docs/EVAL-LOOP.md`. |

The rule names no typeface beyond proportional serif; a named family in a template is a tier-2 choice, not a rule. The Seventh Circuit typography handbook is guidance, not a rule.
