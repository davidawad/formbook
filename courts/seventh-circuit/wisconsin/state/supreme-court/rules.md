Kinds: **M** mandated by a rule; **C** convention where the rule leaves the choice open; **A** filer responsibility; **T** template contract.

Typeface. Wis. Stat. 809.19(8)(b)3.a.-c. and 809.81(3) require a proportional serif font or a monospaced font at 10 characters per inch. No face is named. The templates declare Times New Roman with Liberation Serif, its metric twin (the face docs/FONTS.md assigns to a court that names none), so the typst, LaTeX (newtx) and Word renders set the same advances and the Word file names the face LibreOffice embeds (DEF-FONT-002; docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Generated engines"). Exemplars vary across filers (Century Schoolbook, Times, and Courier monospace); the rule allows any proportional serif face at the stated size.

## Briefs on the merits and answering, reply and nonparty briefs

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| SUPCT-BRIEF-001 | M | Brief parts, in this order: table of contents with page references and a table of cases, statutes and other authorities; statement of the issues presented and how the circuit court and court of appeals decided them; statement with reasons on oral argument and publication; statement of the case (nature, procedural status, dispositions below, facts with record references); argument on each issue, in the order of the statement of issues, each preceded by a one-sentence summary; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | 809.19(1)(a)-(f); 809.63 |
| SUPCT-BRIEF-002 | M | Refer to an individual by initials or a pseudonym where the matter is confidential by law. | 809.19(1)(g), (i); 809.81(8) |
| SUPCT-LEN-001 | M | Brief on the merits (and an appellant's or respondent's brief): the portions referred to in 809.19(1)(d), (e) and (f) may not exceed 11,000 words in a proportional serif font (50 pages if monospaced). Footnotes count. | 809.19(8)(c)1. |
| SUPCT-LEN-002 | M | Reply brief: the portions referred to in 809.19(1)(e) and (f) may not exceed 3,000 words in a proportional serif font (13 pages if monospaced). | 809.19(8)(c)2. |
| SUPCT-LEN-003 | M | Nonparty brief: the portions referred to in 809.19(1)(e) and (f) may not exceed 3,000 words in a proportional serif font (13 pages if monospaced). | 809.19(8)(c)3. |
| SUPCT-AMICUS-001 | M | A nonparty first moves for permission by motion that identifies its interest and states why a brief is desirable. A brief supporting or opposing a petition under 809.62 or 809.70 accompanies the motion and is filed within the time the opposing party has to respond to the petition; a brief in other cases is filed within 14 days after the respondent's brief unless the court sets another time. | 809.19(7)(a)-(c) |
| SUPCT-AMICUS-002 | M | A nonparty brief conforms to 809.19(1)(e) and (f) and to the form and certification rules of 809.19(8). | 809.19(7)(d) |

## Petitions

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
