# Court of Appeals of Wisconsin: briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `wisctapp`. Scope: the seven doc types with a filed exemplar in `usage/`: opening brief, answering (respondent's) brief, reply brief, amicus curiae brief, motion and supporting memorandum, petition for leave to appeal (Wis. Stat. 809.50), and petition for supervisory or original writ (Wis. Stat. 809.51). The court's form and length rules are in Wis. Stat. chapter 809; the Court of Appeals publishes no separate local form rule that the sources below did not already cover. Verified against 2023-24 Wisconsin Statutes (updated through 2025 Wis. Act 82, published 1-30-26), as archived by the Internet Archive, on 2026-10-21.

Retrieval note: the live legislature site (docs.legis.wisconsin.gov) did not answer the fetch attempts made on 2026-10-21 (connection timeout, after a retry), and www.wicourts.gov returned HTTP 403. The rules below were read from Internet Archive captures of the official legislature pages, each fetched with HTTP 200. The Court of Appeals' own rules page (wicourts.gov) was not retrieved.

## Opening brief (appellant's brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WISCTAPP-OPEN-001 | Contents, in order: table of contents with page references and the headings of each argument section; table of cases (alphabetical), statutes and other authorities with the pages on which each is cited; statement of the issues presented and how the trial court decided them; statement on whether oral argument is necessary and whether the opinion should be published, with reasons; statement of the case (nature, procedural status, disposition in the trial court, facts with record references); argument, arranged in the order of the issues, each preceded by a one-sentence summary; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | WS-809.19(1)(a)-(f) | M |
| WISCTAPP-OPEN-002 | Reference to an individual by initials or pseudonym where the record is confidential by law or under s. 809.86; reference to parties by name rather than party designation in the argument (unless "petitioner" must be used). | WS-809.19(1)(g), (i) | M |
| WISCTAPP-OPEN-003 | The appellant's brief is filed with a short appendix, as a single document separate from the brief, containing at a minimum the circuit court's findings or opinion, the essential record portions and rulings, a copy of any unpublished opinion cited, and its own table of contents. | WS-809.19(2)(a), (ae) | M |
| WISCTAPP-LEN-001 | The counted portions of the brief (statement of the case, argument, conclusion; WS-809.19(1)(d)-(f)) do not exceed 11,000 words when a proportional serif font is used. The word count is certified by the filer. | WS-809.19(8)(c)1; WS-809.19(8)(d) | M |

## Petition for leave to appeal (809.50)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WISCTAPP-PET-001 | A petition for leave to appeal a judgment or order not appealable as of right is filed within 14 days after its entry, with a supporting memorandum if any. It contains: the issues presented; the facts necessary to an understanding of the issues; a statement why immediate review will materially advance the termination of the litigation or clarify further proceedings, protect a party from substantial or irreparable injury, or clarify an issue of general importance in the administration of justice; and a copy of the judgment or order. | WS-809.50 (1)(a)-(d) | M |
| WISCTAPP-LEN-005 | The petition and supporting memorandum combined do not exceed 8,000 words when a proportional serif font is used. | WS-809.50 (1) | M |
