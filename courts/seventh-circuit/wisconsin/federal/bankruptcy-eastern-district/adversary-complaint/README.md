# E.D. Wis. Bankruptcy: adversary complaint (`wieb`, doc type `adversary-complaint`)

Governs an adversary complaint filed in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7001 et seq. Template: `typst/wieb-adversary-complaint.typ`; entry point `typst/main.typ`.

## Exemplar

- `exemplars/FILED-EXAMPLE-layng-v-clarke-22-02003-adversary-complaint.pdf`: the U.S. Trustee's complaint to deny a Chapter 7 discharge, *Layng v. Clarke* (In re Marissa J. Clarke), Adv. No. 22-02003-kmp, filed 01/13/2022 (11 pp.). Provenance and checksum in `exemplars/SOURCE.md`.
- Sample: `samples/INPUT-layng-clarke.md` (full transcription, 87 numbered paragraphs, 9 rendered pages). Render: `samples/OUTPUT-layng-clarke.pdf`. Overlay: `samples/OVERLAY-layng-clarke.pdf` and `samples/OVERLAY-layng-clarke.txt`.

## Markdown contract

Frontmatter fields: `debtor` (`name`, `designation`, `case_number`, `chapter`); `plaintiff` and `defendant` (`names` list, `designation`); `adversary_number`; `title`; `consent` (`does` or `does not`); `dated`; `signers` (list of `signer` and `lines`). The body is cmarker markdown. Headings: level 1 is a centered bold section, level 2 and 3 are run-in subheads. Numbered paragraphs are written as `1.`, `2.`, ... and keep their numbers across headings.

## What the overlay shows

`samples/OVERLAY-layng-clarke.pdf`: red is our render, blue is the filed exemplar, and black is where both agree. Each page is paired with the exemplar page sharing the most words (`OVERLAY-layng-clarke.txt`). Pages pair 1:1 for the caption and the first two pages. Later pages pair with an offset (ours p.3 to filed p.4, ours p.8 to filed p.10) because our rendering is shorter per page with different breaks. Word overlap is 46-71%, which reflects the font difference (EB Garamond vs. the filed Times-like face) and paragraph breaks rather than a content mismatch.

## Known gaps / OPEN and ACCEPTED findings

- ACCEPTED: font is EB Garamond 12 pt (the only vendored font). Its x-height is smaller than the filed Times-like face at the same point size, so the text looks smaller. The rule sets the size, not the face; the page still meets LR 9004-1(a).
- OPEN: the consent sentence is generated by the template right after the title, and the transcription also keeps the filed paragraph 6 consent sentence. A drafter would normally keep one. The template statement is the rule requirement; the duplicate is a transcription artifact.
- OPEN: the Count heading subtitle is left-aligned bold; the filing centers it. Cosmetic.
- OPEN: summons (Official Form 417A) and the Civil Cover Sheet are not part of this template. A complaint is filed with them, and the summons is issued by the clerk.
- OPEN: Local chambers procedures (judge-specific) were not checked.
- Not built: ECF attachment and matrix requirements (LR 9013-2(b)) do not apply to complaints in this template.

## Engines

- **typst** (primary, `typst/`): the reference for the court's form.
- **docx** (`docx/reference.docx`, `docx/build-reference.sh`, `docx/wieb.lua`): pandoc on a reference.docx built from pandoc's default (EB Garamond 12 pt, exact 24 pt line pitch, 1 in margins on letter paper, centered PAGE footer, bold centered level-1 headings, bold level-2, italic level-3, no first-line indent on body paragraphs, numbered paragraphs typed by the drafter with the text at 1 in). The caption, consent sentence and signature block are styled paragraphs with tab stops; no tables and no forced line breaks. Every case word comes from the frontmatter. Built to `samples/OUTPUT-layng-clarke.docx`; the zip and XML parts were checked (`unzip -tq`, `xmllint --noout`).
  - Matches the typst form: font and size, double spacing, margins, the caption order (court, debtor and case, rule, parties with adversary number, rule, title), the consent sentence, the "/s/" signature block at 3.25 in, numbered paragraphs.
  - Differs or unverified: the typst caption is a two-column grid with a vertically centred right column; the docx puts the case number on the debtor row and the adversary number on the "v." row, and its rules are paragraph borders. The numbered-paragraph layout (number at 0.5 in, text at 1 in) is a pleading convention, not the typst enum layout. Word's line breaks may differ from typst's. The signature block is kept together but not with the preceding paragraph.
  - BLOCKED on this box: `soffice` (LibreOffice) is not installed, so `samples/OUTPUT-layng-clarke.docx.pdf` was not produced and the docx Gates 0 to 3 cannot run. No page was rendered or reviewed (0 pages). Not opened in Word. The typst template has no table of contents or table of authorities, so the TOC/TOA field caveat does not apply to this doc type.
