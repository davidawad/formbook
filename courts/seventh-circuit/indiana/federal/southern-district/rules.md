# United States District Court, Southern District of Indiana: filing rules

Verified against the primary sources cited in this file on 2026-10-17.

Alias INSD. This rules inventory covers the court's existing complaint and motion-brief document types. Verified 2026-10-17 against the primary sources below. Check the current local rules, assigned judge's procedures, and case-specific orders at filing; those authorities control where they add requirements.

## Requirements shared by civil filings

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| INSD-FORM-001 | M | Pleadings have a caption with court name, title, file number, and designation under Rule 7(a). | Fed. R. Civ. P. 10(a); local rules source above |
| INSD-FORM-002 | M | Allegations and defenses are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 10(b) |
| INSD-CONT-001 | M | A complaint states a short and plain statement of jurisdiction, claim, and requested relief. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 8(a) |
| INSD-CONT-002 | M | A motion states its grounds with particularity, the relief sought, and the rule or authority supporting it. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 7(b)(1) |

| INSD-CERT-002 | M | A certificate of service is not required for a paper served through the court's electronic-filing system. For any other service, comply with Rule 5(d)(1)(B) and Rule 5(b). | Fed. R. Civ. P. 5(b), 5(d)(1)(B) |

## Document-type differences

### complaint

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| INSD-CONT-003 | M | Include each claim for relief, a demand for the relief sought, and a jury demand if a jury trial is requested. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 8(a), 8(d), 38(b) |
| INSD-COVER-001 | C | A complaint begins with its caption and title; a separate decorative cover is not prescribed by FRCP 10. Follow any current local requirement. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 10(a); official local rules source above |
| INSD-CERT-003 | M | The summons is issued and served separately under Rules 4 and 5; filing a complaint does not substitute for service of process. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 4, 5 |

### motion-brief

| ID | Kind | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| INSD-CONT-004 | M | A supporting memorandum states the legal and factual grounds for the requested order. A motion itself must state the grounds with particularity and specify the relief sought. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 7(b)(1); official local rules source above |
| INSD-COVER-002 | C | The motion or supporting memorandum uses a case caption and descriptive title; the rules do not prescribe a separate cover page. | Fed. R. Civ. P. 10(a), 7(b)(1) |
| INSD-LEN-002 | A | Confirm the applicable local briefing schedule, page/word limits, and any requirement to file a separate motion, notice, or proposed order in current local rules and assigned judge procedures. | Official local rules source above; Fed. R. Civ. P. 7, 83 |
