# Indiana Tax Court: briefs, memoranda and Supreme Court review of its decisions

Alias `indtc`. Scope: briefs, memoranda, responses, reply briefs and petitions for rehearing filed in the Indiana Tax Court (original tax appeals), and the form and length of a Petition for Review of a Tax Court decision filed in the Indiana Supreme Court under Ind. App. R. 63. The Tax Court Rules apply first; Rule 1 of those rules makes the Indiana Rules of Trial Procedure apply except where clearly inconsistent. Verified against the Indiana Tax Court Rules "Updated, Effective January 1, 2023", the Indiana Rules of Trial Procedure "Updated, Effective January 1, 2026", and the Indiana Rules of Appellate Procedure "Updated, Effective January 1, 2025", on 2026-10-21.

Local rules retrieved: the live Tax Court rules page https://rules.incourts.gov/Content/tax/default.htm returned HTTP 000 (no connection) on 2026-10-21; the rules below come from the Internet Archive capture of that page (2026-04-02) and its linked PDF.

No exemplar: no public, text-layer, filed brief by an Indiana Tax Court party was obtained (see the court README). Every row below is drawn from the rules text, not from a filed document.

## Petition for review of a Tax Court decision (filed in the Indiana Supreme Court)

Review of a Tax Court Final Judgment is by a petition to the Supreme Court, not in the Tax Court (App. R. 63(A)). The form and length rules below are the Supreme Court's, and the Tax Court Rules do not govern them. Rehearing in the Tax Court is governed by App. R. 63(B) and 54.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| INDTC-SC-001 | App. R. 43 governs the form of a Petition for Review of a Tax Court decision (App. R. 43(A)). Page size 8 1/2 by 11 inches; black type; typeface from the App. R. 43(D) list at 12-point or larger in body text and footnotes; double-spacing with the exceptions of App. R. 43(E); consecutive numbering from the front page, including tables; margins at least one inch on all four sides. | APP 43(A)-(G). | M |
| INDTC-SC-002 | Each page except the front page carries a left-aligned header naming the filing party(ies) and the document name. | APP 43(H). | M |
| INDTC-SC-003 | The front page conforms substantially to Form #App.R. 43-1. The form text was not retrieved; the cover layout is convention (C) drawn from the exemplars. | APP 43(I). | C |
| INDTC-SC-004 | Conventionally filed documents are bound with a single staple or binder clip, not in book or pamphlet form. | APP 43(J). | A |
| INDTC-SC-005 | A Petition for Review of a Tax Court decision is limited to 30 pages; the brief in response is 30 pages; the reply is 15 pages. | APP 44(D). | M |
| INDTC-SC-006 | Alternatively, a Petition for Review of a Tax Court decision, a response, or a reply may exceed the page limit if it does not exceed 14,000 words (petition or response) or 7,000 words (reply), with a certification by the preparing attorney that includes footnotes. | APP 44(E). | M |
| INDTC-SC-007 | The Supreme Court's word count certificate is excluded from the length limit. | APP 44(C). | M |
