# Indiana Court of Appeals: appellant's, appellee's and reply briefs

Alias `indctapp`. Scope: the principal briefs (appellant's brief, appellee's brief) and the reply brief filed in the Indiana Court of Appeals. The Court of Appeals has no local rule of its own for briefs: the Indiana Rules of Appellate Procedure govern practice before both the Supreme Court and the Court of Appeals (App. R. 1). Verified against the Indiana Rules of Appellate Procedure, "Updated, Effective January 1, 2025" (title page), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://rules.incourts.gov/Content/appellate/default.htm returned HTTP 000 (connection timeout) on 2026-10-21, and https://www.in.gov/courts/rules/appellate/appellate.pdf (the URL the court's exemplar index cited) returned HTTP 404. The text below is the Internet Archive capture of the court-posted rules PDF. Confirm against the court-posted PDF before relying on a number. Form #App.R. 43-1 (the front-page form that Rule 43 (I) requires the front page to follow "substantially") is not in the retrieved text; the cover is convention (C) drawn from the exemplars.

Rule 43 (D) names the faces that qualify; Times New Roman is one of them. The template uses Times New Roman (Liberation Serif is its metric substitute, see `docs/FONTS.md`). Rule 43 (D) sets a 12-point minimum, which is the default check size, so no `eval.yaml` is needed for type size or margins. The page limit is not checked by total page count: Rule 44 (C) excludes the cover, tables and certificates from the page limit, and the word limit of Rule 44 (E) is the alternative to the page limit. The word limit is therefore certified by the filer, not counted by the template.

## Appellant's brief (opening brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| INDCTAPP-LEN-001 | An appellant's brief may not exceed 30 pages, or else 14,000 words, including footnotes, with the word certification of Rule 44 (F). Headings and footnotes count toward the word limit. | RULES Rule 44 (D), (E), (C). | M |
| INDCTAPP-LEN-002 | Excluded from both limits: cover information, table of contents, table of authorities, signature block, certificate of service, word count certificate, and the appealed judgment or order. | RULES Rule 44 (C). | M |
| INDCTAPP-LEN-003 | Word count certification, at the end of the brief before the certificate of service, in one of the forms of Rule 44 (F): "I verify that this brief contains no more than 14,000 words" or "I verify that this brief contains (actual number) words." | RULES Rule 44 (F); Rule 46 (A) (10). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-001 | Sections in this order: table of contents; table of authorities; statement of issues; statement of case; statement of facts; summary of argument; argument; conclusion. Each section has a separate heading. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (1), (2), (4)-(9). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-002 | Statement of Supreme Court jurisdiction: required only when the appeal is taken directly to the Supreme Court. Not rendered for a Court of Appeals brief. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (3). | T |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-003 | Table of contents lists each section and each heading and subheading with the page on which it begins. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (1). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-004 | Table of authorities lists each case, statute, rule, and other authority cited, with each page on which it is cited, alphabetically or numerically as applicable. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (2). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-005 | Statement of issues concisely and particularly describes each issue presented for review. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (4). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-006 | Statement of case briefly describes the nature of the case, the course of the proceedings, and the disposition by the trial court or agency, with page references to the Record on Appeal or Appendix under Rule 22 (C). | RULES Rule 46 (A) (5). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-007 | Statement of facts is narrative, relevant to the issues, supported by page references to the Record on Appeal or Appendix under Rule 22 (C), and stated under the standard of review appropriate to the judgment. It is not a witness-by-witness summary. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (6) (a)-(c). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-008 | Summary of argument is succinct, clear and accurate, and is not a repetition of the argument headings. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (7). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-009 | Argument contains the appellant's contentions with cogent reasoning and citations to authorities, statutes, and the Appendix or Record on Appeal. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (8) (a). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-010 | Argument states, for each issue, a concise statement of the applicable standard of review, either within the discussion of each issue or under a separate heading placed before it. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (8) (b). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-011 | Each argument has an argument heading. Asserted errors on substantially the same issue may be grouped under one argument. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (8) (c). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-012 | Where error rests on giving or refusing an instruction, the instruction is set out verbatim in the argument with the objections. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (8) (e). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-013 | Conclusion states the precise relief sought and bears the signature of the attorney or pro se party. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (9). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-014 | The appealed judgment or order, and any written opinion or findings on the issues, is submitted with the brief as a separate attachment. This is a filing step; the template does not render it. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (12). | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-015 | Appendices are submitted separately from the brief. | RULES Rule 46 (F); Rule 51. | M |
| INDCTAPP-CONT-016 | Each heading in the argument is a point heading; the template numbers them and does not number the markdown. Citations use the form of the Indiana Rules of Appellate Procedure and the exemplars. | RULES Rule 46 (A) (8) (c); convention from exemplars. | C |
