# Argument

## THE APPELLEES DO NOT ANSWER THE VALUATION POINT

The Trustee's answering brief relies on the broker's rough estimate as though it were a finding. It is not. The question under [11 U.S.C. § 363(b)](toa:s363) is whether the price reflected value, and the record contains no evidence of value other than the estimate the witness conceded he had not checked against the equipment.

## THE PROCEEDS PROVISION IS A CONCESSION, NOT A REMEDY

The Trustee accepts that Appellant's interest must attach to the proceeds. That acknowledgment confirms that the order as written does not yet say so with the required precision. The Panel should direct the revision the Trustee's own brief describes.

# Conclusion

Appellant renews her request that the Panel vacate the sale order and remand for findings on value.
