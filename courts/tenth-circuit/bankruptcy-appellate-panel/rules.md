# Tenth Circuit Bankruptcy Appellate Panel: opening, answering and reply briefs

Alias `bap10`. Scope: appellant's opening briefs, appellee's answering briefs and reply briefs. Verified against Tenth Circuit Bankruptcy Appellate Panel Local Rules, Effective January 1, 2026, and Part VIII of the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, Effective December 1, 2024 (court-posted PDF) on 2026-10-20.

## Opening brief

Appellant's brief. FRBP 8015 governs form for every brief unless a row says otherwise.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP10-FORM-001 | 8½ × 11 inch paper; text double-spaced; quotations over two lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | FRBP 8015(a)(4). | M |
| BAP10-FORM-002 | Margins at least 1 inch on all four sides; page numbers may sit in the margins and no other text may. | FRBP 8015(a)(4). | M |
| BAP10-FORM-003 | Proportionally spaced face with serifs (sans-serif allowed in headings and captions), 14-point or larger. Deviates from the 12-point default: FRBP 8015(a)(5)(A) sets 14 point. L.R. 8015-1(b) accepts 12-point and says footnotes match body size, so 12-point is accepted, not required. | FRBP 8015(a)(5)(A); BAP L.R. 8015-1(b). | M |
| BAP10-FORM-004 | If monospaced, no more than 10½ characters per inch. | FRBP 8015(a)(5)(B). | M |
| BAP10-FORM-005 | Plain roman type; italics or boldface for emphasis only; case names italicized or underlined. | FRBP 8015(a)(6). | M |
| BAP10-FORM-006 | Front cover shows case number centered at top, name of the court, case title as captioned by the Court, nature of the proceeding and court below, brief title naming the party, and counsel's name, office address, telephone number and email address. | FRBP 8015(a)(2)(A)-(F); BAP L.R. 8015-1(a). | M |
| BAP10-CONT-001 | Order: disclosure statement if required by FRBP 8012; table of contents with page references; table of authorities with page references; jurisdictional statement (bankruptcy court, BAP and timeliness); statement of issues with the standard of review for each; statement of the case with record references; summary of argument; argument; conclusion; certificate of compliance. | FRBP 8014(a)(1)-(10). | M |
| BAP10-CONT-002 | Statement of related cases goes on the last page of the brief (not the table of authorities). | BAP L.R. 8014-1. | M |
| BAP10-CONT-003 | Record and appendix references are to specific appendix pages (e.g., Appellant's App. at 27). | BAP L.R. 8015-1(c). | M |
| BAP10-CONT-004 | Incorporation by reference of lower-court or prior briefs is disapproved. | BAP L.R. 8015-1(f). | M |
| BAP10-CONT-005 | Appendix with the principal brief, containing the docket entries, pleadings, judgment or order, notice of appeal and relevant transcript, unless excused under FRBP 8009(d). | FRBP 8018(b)(1); BAP L.R. 8018-1. | M |
| BAP10-CONT-006 | Combined Statements (admission or pro se status, statement of interested parties, statement regarding oral argument) filed within 14 days after the docketing notice or before the first motion or response, whichever is first. Filed separately from the brief. | BAP L.R. 8003-2. | M |
| BAP10-LEN-001 | Principal brief no more than 13,000 words, or 1,300 lines if monospaced, with a certificate under FRBP 8015(h). A 30-page cap applies only to briefs that do not meet the word limit. Statement of related cases is excluded from the count. | FRBP 8015(a)(7)(A)-(B)(i); BAP L.R. 8015-1(d). | M |
| BAP10-LEN-002 | Excluded from the count: cover, disclosure statement, table of contents, table of authorities, certificates, signature block, proof of service, addendum. Headings, footnotes and quotations count. | FRBP 8015(g). | M |
| BAP10-LEN-003 | Motions to exceed word limits are disfavored and denied absent extraordinary and compelling circumstances. | BAP L.R. 8015-1(f). | M |
| BAP10-CERT-001 | Certificate of compliance with the type-volume limit, stating word or line count, signed by counsel; Official Form 417C conforms. | FRBP 8015(h)(1)-(2); 8014(a)(10). | M |
| BAP10-CERT-002 | Proof of service unless served electronically through the court's system. | FRBP 8011(d). | M |
| BAP10-CERT-003 | Signed by counsel. | FRBP 8011(e). | M |
| BAP10-ELEC-001 | Attorneys file all documents through ECF; exemption requires a motion showing good cause. | BAP L.R. 8001-1(a), 8001-2(a). | M |
| BAP10-ELEC-002 | Filed documents are text-searchable PDFs. | BAP L.R. 8011-2(a). | M |
| BAP10-ELEC-003 | Privacy protection under Fed. R. Civ. P. 5.2 (or Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9037 where it governed the case); the clerk will not redact. | BAP L.R. 8011-1. | M |
| BAP10-SERV-001 | Serve each party's brief as FRBP 8011 requires. | FRBP 8011. | M |

## Answering brief

Appellee's brief. Same form and certificate rules as the opening brief (BAP10-FORM-001 to 005, BAP10-CERT-002, 003); the 14-point floor and 12-point acceptance in BAP10-FORM-003 apply.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP10-FORM-007 | Front cover fields and content as BAP10-FORM-006. No cover color is set by the BAP rules. | FRBP 8015(a)(2); BAP L.R. 8015-1(a). | M |
| BAP10-CONT-007 | Appellee may omit the jurisdictional statement, statement of issues and standard of review, and statement of the case, unless dissatisfied with the appellant's statement; other contents follow FRBP 8014(a). | FRBP 8014(b). | M |
| BAP10-CONT-008 | Statement of related cases on the last page, as in BAP10-CONT-002. | BAP L.R. 8014-1. | M |
| BAP10-CONT-009 | Appellee's brief reference rules as in BAP10-CONT-003 and BAP10-CONT-004. | BAP L.R. 8015-1(c), (f). | M |
| BAP10-LEN-004 | Appellee's brief without a cross-appeal: 13,000 words (1,300 lines if monospaced). In a cross-appeal, appellee's principal and response brief: 15,300 words (1,500 lines if monospaced). | FRBP 8015(a)(7)(B)(i); FRBP 8016(d)(2)(B)(i). | M |
| BAP10-CERT-004 | Certificate of compliance required (Official Form 417C form) with word or line count. | FRBP 8015(h)(1)-(2). | M |
| BAP10-ELEC-004 | ECF, text-searchable PDF and privacy rules as in BAP10-ELEC-001 to 003. | BAP L.R. 8001-1(a), 8011-1, 8011-2(a). | M |

## Reply brief

Appellant's reply. A reply must comply with FRBP 8014(a)(2)-(3).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP10-FORM-008 | Form requirements for paper, spacing, margins, typeface, type style and monospacing are the same as the opening brief (BAP10-FORM-001 to 005). The 14-point floor applies. | FRBP 8015(a)(4)-(6); BAP L.R. 8015-1(b). | M |
| BAP10-FORM-009 | Front cover fields as BAP10-FORM-006. No cover color is set by the BAP rules. | FRBP 8015(a)(2). | M |
| BAP10-CONT-010 | Reply brief includes a table of contents with page references and a table of authorities with page references. | FRBP 8014(c). | M |
| BAP10-LEN-005 | Reply brief limit: 6,500 words (650 lines if monospaced) with a certificate under FRBP 8015(h). A 15-page cap applies only to briefs that do not meet the word limit. | FRBP 8015(a)(7)(A), (B)(ii). | M |
| BAP10-LEN-006 | Cross-appeal: appellant's response and reply brief, 13,000 words (1,300 lines if monospaced); appellee's reply brief, 6,500 words (650 lines if monospaced). | FRBP 8016(d)(2)(A)(i), (d)(2)(B)(i), (d)(2)(C). | M |
| BAP10-CERT-005 | Certificate of compliance required for a reply brief under the word limit, in the Official Form 417C form. | FRBP 8015(h)(1)-(2). | M |
| BAP10-CERT-006 | Proof of service unless served electronically through the court's system; signature by counsel. | FRBP 8011(d)-(e). | M |
| BAP10-ELEC-005 | ECF, text-searchable PDF and privacy rules as in BAP10-ELEC-001 to 003. | BAP L.R. 8001-1(a), 8011-1, 8011-2(a). | M |
