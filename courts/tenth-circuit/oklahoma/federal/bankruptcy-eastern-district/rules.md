## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKEB-FORM-001 | Papers are 8½ × 11 inches, no less than 12 point font including footnotes, margins of no less than one inch, drawn on one side of the page only, and double-spaced if they are pleadings or briefs. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKEB-FORM-002 | A document drafted for another purpose and tendered as an attachment or exhibit is enlarged or reduced to 8½ × 11 unless that makes it unreadable. | LR 9004-1(B). | M |
| OKEB-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court name, case title, bankruptcy case number and the three initials of the assigned judge, the adversary number and the three initials of the judge, and the chapter number directly under the case number; the document is titled to describe its nature. | LR 9004-2. | M |
| OKEB-CONT-002 | Jurisdiction allegation names the case, its number and Code chapter, and the district and division; the complaint states whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| OKEB-CONT-003 | Adversary cover sheet on the Official Forms page accompanies a paper complaint. | FORMS (Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet). | A |
| OKEB-CERT-001 | Signature block gives the attorney's full name, state bar number, mailing address, email address, telephone number, fax number if any, and the party represented; unsigned documents are stricken unless promptly corrected. | LR 9004-1(C); LR 9011-3(A); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| OKEB-CERT-002 | A separate certificate of mailing or service is attached where required. | LR 7005-1; LR 9004-1(F)(4). | M |
| OKEB-ELEC-001 | Redact SSN and taxpayer ID to last four digits, birth date to year, minors to initials, financial account numbers to last four. | FRBP 9037(a). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKEB-FORM-003 | Same page, margin, type and spacing rules as the complaint: 8½ × 11, no less than 12 point font including footnotes, margins of no less than one inch, one side only, double-spaced. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKEB-CONT-004 | Each motion in an adversary proceeding is accompanied by Local Form 20A (Notice of Motion, Notice of Deadline to File Objection and Notice of Hearing) and a proposed order. Motions under LR 7056-1 are excepted from the Form 20A requirement. | LR 7007-1(A); LR 9013-1(B); LR 9072-1(A). | M |
| OKEB-LEN-001 | A motion, application or objection in an adversary proceeding is accompanied by a concise brief not exceeding 20 pages, exclusive of attachments or appendices. A brief in opposition, not exceeding 20 pages, is filed within 14 days after the original; a reply not exceeding 10 pages within 14 days after the opposition. No other briefs without leave. | LR 7007-1(B). | M |
| OKEB-LEN-002 | A summary-judgment brief is limited to 20 pages, exclusive of attachments; the response is limited to 20 pages and is filed within 14 days after the supporting brief; a reply addressing only new matters is limited to 10 pages and filed within 14 days after the response. The supporting brief opens with a numbered statement of material facts. | LR 7056-1(A)-(C). | M |
| OKEB-CERT-003 | Signed by an attorney of record with the full signature block in OKEB-CERT-001. | LR 9011-3(A); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| OKEB-ELEC-003 | Redact personal identifiers in attached filings as for adversary complaints. | FRBP 9037(a). | M |
