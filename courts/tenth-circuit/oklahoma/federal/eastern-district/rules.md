# Eastern District of Oklahoma: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `oked`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the court-posted Local Civil Rules, effective July 5, 2016, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKED-FORM-001 | Papers are clearly legible, 8½ × 11 inches, on one side only, with one-inch margins on the top, bottom, and sides. | LCvR 5.2(a). | M |
| OKED-FORM-002 | Print style, including footnotes, is not smaller than 12 point. Line spacing is no less than 1.5, excluding blocked quotations and footnotes; this is not double-spacing. | LCvR 5.2(a). | M |
| OKED-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court, title, and file number, and conforms to FRCP 10(a). Counsel and pro se parties number each party separately in the caption, plaintiffs consecutively and defendants consecutively. | LCvR 3.1; FRCP 10(a). | M |
| OKED-CONT-002 | Claims are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| OKED-CONT-003 | Each complaint or other initiating document is accompanied by a completed JS-44 civil cover sheet. | LCvR 3.1. | M |
| OKED-CONT-004 | A non-governmental corporate party files its FRCP 7.1 disclosure statement with its first filing. | FRCP 7.1(a)-(b). | M |
| OKED-CERT-001 | Signature block gives the signer's address, email, and telephone. | FRCP 11(a). | M |
| OKED-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing follows the ECF Policy Manual. Redact SSNs and TINs to last four digits, birth dates to year, minors to initials, and financial account numbers to last four digits. Confidential information may be omitted or partially redacted. | LCvR 5.1; LCvR 5.3; FRCP 5.2(a). | M |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKED-FORM-003 | Same page, margin, type, and spacing rules as the complaint: 8½ × 11, one-inch margins, not smaller than 12 point, line spacing not less than 1.5. Briefs follow LCvR 5.2(a). | LCvR 5.2(a); LCvR 7.1(c). | M |
| OKED-CONT-005 | Each motion is a separate pleading that sets out the specific point or points on which it is brought, with a concise brief unless the rules say otherwise. A brief may be combined with the motion if the title says so. | LCvR 7.1(b). | M |
| OKED-CONT-006 | A response to a motion is a separate pleading, filed within 14 days; a response may not include a cross-motion. | LCvR 7.1(d). | M |
| OKED-CONT-007 | Before a non-dispositive motion (including discovery and motions in limine), counsel confers in good faith and advises the court in writing. | LCvR 7.1(f). | M |
| OKED-LEN-001 | Briefs are limited to 25 typewritten pages without leave. Briefs over 15 pages need an indexed table of contents and table of authorities. Reply and supplemental briefs are limited to 10 pages. | LCvR 7.1(c), (e). | M |
| OKED-CONT-008 | A reply addresses new matters in the response and is filed within 14 days after the response. | LCvR 7.1(e). | M |
