The Plaintiff, the chapter 7 trustee, moves under Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(a), made applicable by Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7037, for an order compelling the Defendant to answer the Plaintiff’s first interrogatories and requests for production, and states:

# I. BACKGROUND

1\. The Plaintiff served the discovery requests on January 5, 2026. Responses were due February 4, 2026. None has been served.

2\. On February 10, 2026, counsel for the Plaintiff wrote to the Defendant asking for the responses within ten days, and offered to confer by telephone. The Defendant did not respond.

# II. BRIEF IN SUPPORT

A party may move for an order compelling an answer or production when the other party “fails to answer an interrogatory submitted under Rule 33” or “fails to produce documents ... as requested under Rule 34.” Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(a)(3)(B)(iii)–(iv). A party that does not timely respond waives its objections to the requests unless the court, for good cause, excuses the failure.

The Plaintiff has conferred in good faith, as Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(a)(1) requires, and the Defendant has neither answered nor objected.

WHEREFORE, the Plaintiff asks the Court to order the Defendant to serve complete responses, without objection, within fourteen days, and to grant such other relief as is just.
