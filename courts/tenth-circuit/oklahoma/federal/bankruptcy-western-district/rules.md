# Bankruptcy Court, Western District of Oklahoma: adversary complaints and motions

Alias `okwb`. Scope: adversary complaints (`adversary-complaint`) and motions with their briefs (`motion-brief`). Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Western District of Oklahoma, dated January 15, 2026, effective January 15, 2026, and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKWB-FORM-001 | 8½ × 11 inches; margins at least 1 inch; double-spaced unless the document is one page; one side only; white paper; no color printing or images; 50 MB limit. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKWB-FONT-001 | Printing not smaller than 12 point, including footnotes (default). No typeface is specified. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |

| OKWB-ELEC-001 | Redact all but last four digits of SSN and account numbers, minors' names to initials, and birth date to year, using a permanent redaction tool (text boxes or highlights do not qualify). Filer bears responsibility; Clerk does not review. | LR 1007-1(E); FRBP 9037(a). | M |

## Motion brief

Scope: a motion and its brief in support (`motion-brief`). LR re-read on 2026-10-29 from the same court-hosted PDF (effective January 15, 2026; Rules 9004-1, 9007-1 and 9013-1). OKWB-FORM-001, OKWB-FONT-001, OKWB-CONT-001, OKWB-CERT-001 and OKWB-ELEC-001 above apply to it unchanged.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKWB-CONT-004 | Except for the relief LR 9013-1(H) lists, every motion includes "And Notice of Opportunity for Hearing" in its title and, immediately below the title, the rule's Notice of Opportunity for Hearing with the response time the movant computes (a flat number of days from the date of filing, regardless of the manner of service). | LR 9013-1(G)-(H) | M |
| OKWB-CONT-005 | A motion specifies the points on which it is based and is accompanied by a concise brief (unless LR 9013-1(I) excepts it); the brief may be combined with the motion if the title clearly says so. | LR 9013-1(C), (I) | M |
| OKWB-CONT-006 | The title clearly identifies each request for relief; a motion carries one request for relief, and an affirmative request is not combined with an objection, response or reply, except as Paragraph 7 of the ECF Guidelines allows. | LR 9004-1(C); LR 9013-1(B) | M |
| OKWB-LEN-001 | No brief longer than 20 typewritten pages without prior permission; a brief over 15 pages has a table of contents with its headings and a table of authorities; a reply (optional, within 5 days, on new matter only) and a sur-reply (only with leave) at most 5 pages. | LR 9013-1(C) | M |
| OKWB-CERT-002 | A certificate or affidavit of service accompanies every request for relief: the names and addresses of all persons and entities served other than through the ECF System, the date and the manner of service, and the signature of the person making it with the typed name, address, telephone number and, for an attorney, state bar number; "all parties in interest" and the like do not suffice. | LR 9007-1(D); Local Form 2002-1.G | M |
| OKWB-CONT-007 | A notice of a hearing may be combined with the request for relief if the title says so; a party that obtains a hearing serves notice of it and files a certificate of service. | LR 9013-1(F) | A |
