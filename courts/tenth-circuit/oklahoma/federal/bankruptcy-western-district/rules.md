# Bankruptcy Court, Western District of Oklahoma: adversary complaints

Alias `okwb`. Scope: adversary complaints. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Western District of Oklahoma, dated January 15, 2026, effective January 15, 2026, and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKWB-FORM-001 | 8½ × 11 inches; margins at least 1 inch; double-spaced unless the document is one page; one side only; white paper; no color printing or images; 50 MB limit. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKWB-FONT-001 | Printing not smaller than 12 point, including footnotes (default). No typeface is specified. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKWB-CONT-001 | Caption follows the Official Form; the case number and adversary number are each followed by the letters designating the assigned judge; the chapter appears below the case number; title describes the document. | LR 1005-1; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| OKWB-CONT-002 | Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet (Official Form instructions) is the last page of the complaint, not a separate document, unless the complaint is filed electronically (then no cover sheet). | LR 7003-1; FORMS. | M |
| OKWB-CONT-003 | Jurisdiction allegation names the case, its number and Code chapter, and the district and division; complaint states whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| OKWB-CERT-001 | Signature block beneath the signature line matches the CM/ECF profile: full name, state bar number, address, email, telephone, fax (if any), and party represented. | LR 9004-1(D); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| OKWB-ELEC-001 | Redact all but last four digits of SSN and account numbers, minors' names to initials, and birth date to year, using a permanent redaction tool (text boxes or highlights do not qualify). Filer bears responsibility; Clerk does not review. | LR 1007-1(E); FRBP 9037(a). | M |
