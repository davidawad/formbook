# Western District of Oklahoma: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `okwd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Court Rules of the Western District of Oklahoma, Revised as of April 23, 2026, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition, on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKWD-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inches, double-spaced if typewritten, one side only, clearly legible. | LR LCvR5.2(a). | M |
| OKWD-FONT-001 | Print style, including footnotes, not less than 13 point (above the 12-point default); margins at least 1 inch top, bottom and sides. Applies to all pleadings. | LR LCvR7.1(d); LCvR5.2(a). | M |
| OKWD-FONT-002 | No typeface is specified; choose a legible face at 13 point or larger. | LR LCvR7.1(d). | A |
| OKWD-CONT-001 | Caption gives court name, title, file number and Rule 7(a) designation. Number each party separately in the caption: plaintiffs consecutively, defendants consecutively. | FRCP 10(a); LR LCvR3.1. | M |
| OKWD-CONT-002 | Complaint is accompanied by a completed civil cover sheet (Form JS-44). Without it the Clerk files the complaint and gives notice of the omission. | LR LCvR3.1. | M |
| OKWD-CONT-003 | Claims stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| OKWD-CONT-004 | An LLC or partnership party files a Disclosure Statement Identifying Constituents of LLC or Partnership with its first filing; a diversity-citizenship statement is required where diversity is invoked. Nongovernmental corporations file under FRCP 7.1(a). | LR LCvR7.1.1; FRCP 7.1(a). | M |
| OKWD-CERT-001 | Papers show name, mailing address, daytime telephone, fax and email of the attorney or pro se party; signature complies with Rule 11(a). | LR LCvR5.4; FRCP 11(a). | M |
| OKWD-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing under the ECF Policy Manual. Filings exceeding 40 pages with attachments require a paper copy as set out in ECF Policy Manual II.A(5). | LR LCvR5.2(c). | M |
| OKWD-ELEC-003 | Redact SSN and taxpayer ID to last four digits, birth date to year, minors to initials, financial account numbers to last four. Counsel and parties bear responsibility. | FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
| OKWD-LEN-001 | No page limit for a complaint in the local rules; the 25-page limit covers briefs only. | LR LCvR7.1(e). | A |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKWD-FORM-002 | Same page form as complaints: 8½ × 11 inches, double-spaced if typewritten, one side only. | LR LCvR5.2(a). | M |
| OKWD-FONT-003 | Print style, including footnotes, not less than 13 point; margins at least 1 inch. Quotations longer than two lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | LR LCvR7.1(d)-(e). | M |
| OKWD-CONT-005 | Each motion is a separate document; a response to a motion may not include a motion or cross-motion. A brief is titled to show whether it is opening, response, reply or supplemental, the motion it relates to, and the party it is filed for. | LR LCvR7.1(b)-(c). | M |
| OKWD-LEN-002 | Opening and response briefs limited to 30 pages (summary judgment) or 25 pages (all others); reply briefs to 10 pages. Cover page, tables, signature block, certificate of service and exhibits are excluded. Briefs over 15 pages need an indexed table of contents and table of authorities. Leave to exceed is requested no later than one business day before the due date. | LR LCvR7.1(e). | M |
| OKWD-CERT-002 | Signature block and contact information as for complaints. | LR LCvR5.4; FRCP 11(a). | M |
| OKWD-ELEC-004 | Redaction of personal identifiers in attached filings as for complaints. | FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
