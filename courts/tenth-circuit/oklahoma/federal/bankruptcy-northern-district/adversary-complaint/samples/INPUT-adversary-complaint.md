# Parties

Plaintiff Sample Lender, LLC ("Plaintiff" or "Lender") is a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Oklahoma, with its principal place of business in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

Defendant John Quincy Example ("Defendant" or "Debtor") is an individual who resides in Tulsa County, Oklahoma. Defendant filed a voluntary petition under chapter 7 of title 11 of the United States Code on March 3, 2026, in the above-captioned case.

# Facts

On or about June 1, 2025, Defendant submitted to Plaintiff a written application for a business line of credit (the "Application"). The Application was signed by Defendant on behalf of Example Fabrication Works, LLC, and Defendant personally guaranteed the obligations of that company.

The Application contained the following statements, each of which Defendant made in writing:

- that Example Fabrication Works, LLC had no judgments or pending lawsuits in excess of $10,000 against it;
- that its trailing twelve-month gross receipts were approximately $1,400,000; and
- that no principal of the company had filed for bankruptcy protection within the previous ten years.

Plaintiff relied on those statements when it approved the line of credit and advanced $185,000 to Example Fabrication Works, LLC on June 12, 2025.[^reliance]

Each of the statements identified above was materially false when made. At the time the Application was signed, at least two judgments exceeding $10,000 had been entered against the company, and the company's gross receipts for the trailing twelve months were materially lower than the figure stated. Defendant knew those facts because he managed the company's finances and had personally signed the judgment satisfaction correspondence that same spring.

Defendant made the false statements with the intent that Plaintiff would rely on them in approving the line of credit. Plaintiff did rely on them, and its reliance was justified: Plaintiff had no access to the company's court records that Defendant knew about and did not disclose.

The company defaulted on the line of credit in January 2026. Defendant's personal guaranty was not paid. Plaintiff's claim against Defendant, which includes unpaid principal of $171,400 and accrued interest, remains outstanding as of the date of this complaint.

Plaintiff brings this complaint within the time permitted by Fed. R. Bankr. P. 4007(c).[^deadline]

# First Claim for Relief

## Nondischargeability of Debt Under 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(2)(A)

Plaintiff incorporates the allegations of the preceding paragraphs as though fully set forth here.

Defendant obtained money from Plaintiff, through the advance of $185,000 under the line of credit, by false pretenses, a false representation, or actual fraud, within the meaning of 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(2)(A).

The statements in the Application were not statements respecting the debtor's or an insider's financial condition within the meaning of 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(2)(B), because they were made about the financial condition of a separate entity in which Defendant held an interest.

Accordingly, the debt owed to Plaintiff under the guaranty, in the amount of not less than $171,400 in principal, plus interest, fees and costs as allowed by the loan documents and applicable law, is excepted from discharge under 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(2)(A).
