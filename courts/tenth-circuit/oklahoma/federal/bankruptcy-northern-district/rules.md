# Bankruptcy Court, Northern District of Oklahoma: adversary complaints and motion briefs

Alias `oknb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Bankruptcy Rules of the Northern District of Oklahoma, Effective Date: March 21, 2025, and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKNB-FORM-001 | 8½ × 11 inches, drawn on one side only; pleadings double-spaced; margins at least 1 inch. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKNB-FONT-001 | Print not smaller than 12 point, including footnotes (default). No typeface is specified. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKNB-CONT-001 | Caption gives court name, case title, case number and adversary number, the chapter directly under the case number, and a title describing the document. | LR 9004-2(A); FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| OKNB-CONT-002 | Adversary complaint not filed through ECF is accompanied by the Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet completed per the Official Form instructions. | LR 7003-1; FORMS. | M |
| OKNB-CONT-003 | Jurisdiction allegation names the case, its number and Code chapter, and the district and division; complaint states whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| OKNB-CERT-001 | Signature block beneath signature line: full name, state bar number, address, email, telephone, fax (if any) and party represented. Signed with "s/Name" or scanned signature. | LR 9004-1(C), 9011-4(A); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| OKNB-ELEC-001 | Filed electronically through ECF (LR 5005-1(A)); pro se filers excepted. | LR 5005-1(A). | M |
| OKNB-ELEC-003 | Redact minors' names to initials, all but last four digits of SSN and financial accounts, birth date to year. Filer bears responsibility; Clerk does not review and will not redact or seal without a court order. | LR 5005-1(F); FRBP 9037(a). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKNB-FORM-002 | Same page form as adversary complaints: 8½ × 11 inches, one side, double-spaced, margins at least 1 inch. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKNB-FONT-002 | Print not smaller than 12 point, including footnotes. | LR 9004-1(A). | M |
| OKNB-LEN-001 | Concise brief in an adversary proceeding not exceeding 20 pages, exclusive of attachments; reply to a response not exceeding 10 pages. Summary judgment brief not exceeding 20 pages. No main-case page limit found in the local rules. | LR 7007-1(A), 7056-1(A). | M |
| OKNB-CONT-004 | Motion title includes "And Notice of Opportunity for Hearing" where the Code permits a hearing, and the body carries the NOTICE OF OPPORTUNITY FOR HEARING statement. | LR 9013-1(B). | M |
| OKNB-CERT-002 | Signature block and contact information as for adversary complaints. | LR 9004-1(C), 9011-4(A). | M |
| OKNB-ELEC-004 | Attached filings: redact personal identifiers as for adversary complaints. | LR 5005-1(F); FRBP 9037(a). | M |
