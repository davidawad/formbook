# Northern District of Oklahoma: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `oknd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the Northern District of Oklahoma, Effective Date: 7/31/2026, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, December 1, 2025 edition, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKND-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inches, double-spaced if typewritten, one side of the paper only, clearly legible. | LR LGnR2-4(a). | M |
| OKND-FONT-001 | Print style, including footnotes, not smaller than 12 point; margins at least 1 inch top, bottom and sides (default). "Exactly 24 pt" line spacing is not permitted. No typeface is specified. | LR LGnR2-4(a). | M |
| OKND-FONT-002 | Choose a legible face at 12 point or larger; the rules set no typeface. | LR LGnR2-4(a). | A |
| OKND-CONT-001 | Caption gives court name, title naming all parties, file number and Rule 7(a) designation. Number each party only in the caption. | FRCP 10(a); LR LCvR3-1(c). | M |
| OKND-CONT-002 | Complaint contains no motion (LCvR3-1(d)). Form JS-44 civil cover sheet is a separate document filed with the complaint; pro se plaintiffs need not file it (LCvR3-1(a)). | LR LCvR3-1(a), (d). | M |
| OKND-CONT-003 | Claims stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| OKND-CONT-004 | Nongovernmental party files Disclosure Statement (Form CV-24) with its first filing, covering FRCP 7.1(a) items and related or financially interested entities. | LR LCvR7.1-1(a)(1); FRCP 7.1(a). | M |
| OKND-CERT-001 | Signed filing gives signer's name, mailing address, telephone and email; contact information kept current in CM/ECF. | FRCP 11(a); LR LGnR2-6. | M |
| OKND-CERT-002 | Pro se signature block includes the words "pro se" after the signature. | LR LCvR17-1(g). | M |
| OKND-ELEC-001 | Attorneys file electronically through CM/ECF (LGnR2-1); items that cannot be converted to PDF or are sealed go on paper (LGnR2-2). | LR LGnR2-1, LGnR2-2. | M |
| OKND-ELEC-003 | Redact SSN and taxpayer ID to last four digits, birth date to year, minors' names to initials, financial account numbers to last four. Counsel and parties bear responsibility; the Clerk does not review. | FRCP 5.2(a); LR LCvR5.2-1. | M |
| OKND-LEN-001 | No page limit for a complaint in the local rules; the 25-page limit covers briefs only. | LR LCvR7-1(d). | A |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKND-FORM-002 | Same page form as complaints: 8½ × 11 inches, double-spaced if typewritten, one side only. | LR LGnR2-4(a). | M |
| OKND-FONT-003 | Print style, including footnotes, not smaller than 12 point; margins at least 1 inch. | LR LGnR2-4(a). | M |
| OKND-CONT-005 | Each brief is titled to show whether it is opening, response, reply or supplemental, the motion it supports, and the party on whose behalf it is filed. | LR LCvR7-1(b). | M |
| OKND-CONT-006 | A motion and its brief may be one document only if the title says so. A response brief may not be combined with another motion, reply or supplemental brief. | LR LCvR7-1(b). | M |
| OKND-CONT-007 | Motions that need no brief (e.g., extension, substitution, pro hac vice) state whether opposing counsel agrees or objects and include a separate proposed order. | LR LCvR7-1(c). | M |
| OKND-LEN-002 | Opening and response briefs limited to 25 pages; reply and supplemental briefs to 10 pages. Briefs over 15 pages need an indexed table of contents and table of authorities. Leave to exceed is requested no later than one day before the brief is due. | LR LCvR7-1(d). | M |
| OKND-ELEC-004 | Hyperlinks only to cited legal references; standard citation still appears in the text. | LR LGnR2-5(a)-(b). | M |
| OKND-CERT-003 | Signature block and contact information as for complaints. | FRCP 11(a); LR LGnR2-6. | M |
| OKND-ELEC-005 | Redaction of personal identifiers in attached filings as for complaints. | FRCP 5.2(a); LR LCvR5.2-1. | M |
