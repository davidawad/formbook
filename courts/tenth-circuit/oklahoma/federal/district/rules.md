# Western District of Oklahoma: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `okd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the court-posted Local Court Rules, revised as of April 23, 2026, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKD-FORM-001 | Papers are 8½ × 11 inches, double-spaced if typewritten, on one side only, and clearly legible. Pleadings are formatted under LCvR 7.1(c)-(e). | LCvR 5.2(a). | M |
| OKD-FORM-002 | Print style, including footnotes, is not less than 13 point; margins are at least one inch on the top, bottom, and sides. This is one point above the default 12 pt minimum. | LCvR 7.1(d). | M |
| OKD-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court, title, and file number, and conforms to FRCP 10(a). Counsel and pro se parties number each party separately in the caption, plaintiffs consecutively and defendants consecutively. | LCvR 3.1; FRCP 10(a). | M |
| OKD-CONT-002 | Claims are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| OKD-CONT-003 | Each complaint or other initiating document is accompanied by a completed JS-44 civil cover sheet. Without one, the clerk files it and gives notice of the omission. | LCvR 3.1. | M |
| OKD-CONT-004 | A party formed as an LLC or partnership files a separate Disclosure Statement Identifying Constituents with its first filing, and states citizenship where diversity jurisdiction is invoked. A non-governmental corporate party files its FRCP 7.1 statement. | LCvR 7.1.1; FRCP 7.1(a)-(b). | M |
| OKD-CERT-001 | Signature block gives the signer's address, email, and telephone. | FRCP 11(a). | M |
| OKD-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing follows the ECF Policy Manual. Redact personal data identifiers under FRCP 5.2(a). Official transcripts are redacted under LCvR 5.2.1 before remote electronic availability. An e-filing with attachments over 40 pages requires a paper copy under ECF Policy Manual II.A(5). | LCvR 5.1; LCvR 5.2(c); FRCP 5.2(a). | M |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKD-FORM-003 | Same page, margin, and type rules as the complaint: 8½ × 11, one-sided, double-spaced, 13 point or larger including footnotes, one-inch margins. Headings and footnotes may be single-spaced; quotations longer than two lines may be indented and single-spaced. | LCvR 5.2(a); LCvR 7.1(d)-(e). | M |
| OKD-CONT-005 | Each motion and brief is titled to identify on whose behalf it is presented; each brief is titled as opening, response, reply, or supplemental, with the motion it relates to. | LCvR 7.1(b). | M |
| OKD-CONT-006 | Each motion is a separate document, except that a motion and brief may be filed as one document if the title says so. | LCvR 7.1(c), (k). | M |
| OKD-LEN-001 | Opening and response briefs are limited to 25 pages, or 30 pages for summary-judgment opening and response briefs, without leave. Cover, tables, signature block, certificate of service, and exhibits do not count. Briefs over 15 pages need an indexed table of contents and table of authorities. Reply briefs are limited to 10 pages. | LCvR 7.1(e), (h). | M |
| OKD-CONT-007 | A response is filed within 21 days after the motion; a reply to new matter in the response is filed within 7 days after the response. A reply does not reargue the opening brief. | LCvR 7.1(g)-(h). | M |
| OKD-CONT-008 | A proposed order is served on other counsel and pro se parties before or with the related motion. | LCvR 5.5. | M |
| OKD-CERT-002 | Signature block gives the signer's address, email, and telephone. The signature block and certificate of service do not count toward the page limit. | FRCP 11(a); LCvR 7.1(e). | M |
