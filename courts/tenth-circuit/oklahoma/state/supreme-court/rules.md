# Supreme Court of Oklahoma: briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `okla`. Scope: amicus briefs, answering briefs, briefs on the merits, motions, petitions for writ, and reply briefs filed in the Supreme Court of Oklahoma.

Verified against the filed exemplars listed under Sources on 2026-10-22; the Oklahoma appellate rules themselves were not retrieved.

Local rules not retrieved: Oklahoma Supreme Court Rules, https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/ (connection failed from the build host, curl HTTP 000, 2026-10-22). Oklahoma Supreme Court Rule 1.100 as mirrored at https://www.courtrules.net/oklahoma/oklahoma-supreme-court-rules/rule-1-100 was not fetched on this run. No rule in this file is cited to a source we fetched. The templates use the default tier-1 set (US letter, 1 in margins, 12 pt body; see `data/checks-default.yaml`) and the conventions the filed exemplars share (tier 2). Page limits, typeface, spacing and certificate requirements are open until the rules are fetched.
