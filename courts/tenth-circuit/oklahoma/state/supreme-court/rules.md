Alias `okla`. Scope: amicus briefs, answering briefs, briefs on the merits, motions, petitions for writ, and reply briefs filed in the Supreme Court of Oklahoma.

Verified against the filed exemplars listed under Sources on 2026-10-22; Rule 1.11 only as a search index quotes it (below), the court-posted rule text not retrieved.

## Form of briefs (Okla. Sup. Ct. R. 1.11)

Rule 1.11 (as amended, In re Amendment of Rule 1.11 of Oklahoma Supreme Court Rules, 2022 OK 93) was not fetched: law.justia.com and courtlistener.com refused the build host (HTTP 403 and 202), oscn.net timed out (2026-10-22). The rows below are the rule text as a web search index quotes it (search "Oklahoma Supreme Court Rule 1.11 form of briefs", 2026-10-22); the margins agree with every filed exemplar (body text from x = 90 pt to about 540 pt on US letter). Confirm against the court-posted text before relying on a number.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKLA-FORM-001 | Briefs are on paper 8 1/2 by 11 inches. | R. 1.11, https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/deliverdocument.asp?citeid=493051 (index quotation, 2026-10-22). | M |
| OKLA-FORM-002 | Clear type not less than 12 point; footnotes in clear type not less than 11 point. | R. 1.11 (as above). | M |
| OKLA-FORM-003 | Quoted matter single spaced, unquoted matter double spaced; footnotes may be single spaced. | R. 1.11 (as above). | M |
| OKLA-FORM-004 | Margins 1 1/4 inches on the left side and one inch on the other three sides. | R. 1.11 (as above); the filed exemplars (EX-ANSWER, EX-REPLY, EX-MERITS). | M |
| OKLA-LEN-001 | A brief-in-chief, answer brief or reply brief not combined with another brief does not exceed 30 pages. | R. 1.11 (as above). | M |

## Conventions of the filed exemplars

| ID | Convention | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKLA-EX-001 | The cover is unnumbered; the table of contents and the index of authorities are numbered in lower-roman numerals; the body is numbered from 1. | EX-ANSWER, EX-REPLY. | C |
| OKLA-EX-002 | The cover: the court, the case numbers of this court and of the trial court, the parties and their roles, the court and judge appealed from, the title of the filing, and counsel. | EX-ANSWER, EX-REPLY, EX-MERITS. | C |
| OKLA-EX-003 | A table of contents and an index of authorities (cases, then statutes and other authority) with page references, each on its own pages. | EX-ANSWER, EX-REPLY. | C |
| OKLA-EX-004 | The signature block after the body, single spaced in the right half; the certificate of service or mailing after it, signed. | EX-ANSWER, EX-REPLY, EX-MERITS. | C |
| OKLA-EX-005 | An application to assume original jurisdiction sets the caption on the first page without counsel; counsel sign at the end. | FILED-EXAMPLE-petition-for-writ. | C |
