# Court on the Judiciary of Oklahoma: petitions and appeals

Alias `oklacoj`. Scope: the filings of the Court on the Judiciary: the petition invoking the Trial Division's jurisdiction over removal or compulsory retirement of a judicial officer, the respondent's and prosecutor's filings in the Trial Division, and appeals to the Appellate Division. Verified against Okla. Const. art. VII-A (current text, Oklahoma Legislature) and 20 O.S. § 1659 (current text, Oklahoma Legislature) on 2026-10-21.

## Petition invoking the Trial Division

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKLACOJ-PET-001 | The Trial Division's jurisdiction is invoked by a petition filed by the Supreme Court or its Chief Justice, the Governor, the Attorney General, the Executive Secretary of the Oklahoma Bar Association (when directed by a majority of its Executive Council), or by resolution of the House of Delegates or of the House of Representatives. | OKCONST § 4(a); 20 O.S. § 1659 | M |
| OKLACOJ-PET-002 | The petition states the name of the respondent, the grounds for removal from office or compulsory retirement from office, and any other matter the Trial Division's rules specify. | OKCONST § 4(a) | M |
| OKLACOJ-PET-003 | The petition may be amended by order of either division of the Court. | OKCONST § 4(a) | A |
| OKLACOJ-PET-004 | Immediately upon filing, the Clerk notifies the presiding officer of the Trial Division and the respondent in accordance with the Trial Division's rules. | OKCONST § 4(b) | M |
| OKLACOJ-PET-005 | The matter is set for hearing not less than sixty days after notice of the filing has been given to the respondent. | OKCONST § 4(c) | M |
| OKLACOJ-PET-006 | In procedural matters not covered by the Trial Division's rules, the Code of Civil Procedure and the common law of Oklahoma apply so far as applicable. | OKCONST § 4(c) | A |

## Notice of appeal and Appellate Division filings

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OKLACOJ-APP-001 | A notice of appeal from a judgment of the Trial Division is filed with the Clerk of the Supreme Court within ten days after entry of the judgment, and is served on the opposing party as the Appellate Division's rules prescribe. | OKCONST § 5(a) | M |
| OKLACOJ-APP-002 | Preparation and certification of the record, and all other appellate proceedings not prescribed by the Constitution, are governed by the Appellate Division's rules. | OKCONST § 5(b) | M |
| OKLACOJ-APP-003 | Review in the Appellate Division is an equity appeal as to law and fact; the Division may affirm, modify, or reverse, or enter a new judgment, and may hear additional evidence on a showing that it is material and that there were good reasons for not presenting it to the Trial Division. | OKCONST § 5(c)–(d) | A |
