# Court of Criminal Appeals of Oklahoma: briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `oklacrimapp`. Scope: amicus briefs, answering briefs, briefs on the merits, motions, and petitions for rehearing filed in the Court of Criminal Appeals of Oklahoma.

Verified against the filed exemplars listed under Sources on 2026-10-22; the Oklahoma appellate rules themselves were not retrieved.

Local rules not retrieved: Oklahoma Supreme Court Rules and the Court of Criminal Appeals rules, https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/ (connection failed from the build host, curl HTTP 000, 2026-10-22). No rule in this file is cited to a source we fetched. The templates use the default tier-1 set (US letter, 1 in margins, 12 pt body; see `data/checks-default.yaml`) and the conventions the filed exemplars share (tier 2). Page limits, typeface, spacing and certificate requirements are open until the rules are fetched.
