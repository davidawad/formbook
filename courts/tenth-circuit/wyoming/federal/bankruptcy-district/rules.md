Alias `wyb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules and Forms, effective July 1, 2012, and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WYB-FORM-001 | Letter-size paper, typewritten or legibly printed on one side only, in not less than 13 point type (footnotes included), double-spaced, with a top margin of one inch. Headings of counsel may be in type not smaller than 10 point. The rule states no side or bottom margin; the template uses one inch. | LBR 9004-1(A). | M |
| WYB-FORM-002 | Caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416 for adversary complaints and carries the court name, case title, case number and adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document. | FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| WYB-CONT-001 | Jurisdiction allegation names the case's name, number, and Code chapter and the district and division where the adversary is pending. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| WYB-CONT-002 | Claims are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b), as applied by FRBP 7010. | M |
| WYB-CONT-003 | A non-governmental corporate party files its corporate ownership statement with its first pleading. | LBR 7007.1-1; FRBP 7007.1(b). | M |
| WYB-CONT-004 | A jury demand under FRCP 38(b) is filed in accordance with FRBP 5005. | LBR 9015-1(A); FRCP 38(b). | M |
| WYB-CERT-001 | Top left of the first page: client name and the signer's name, address, telephone, facsimile, and state bar license number. | LBR 9004-1(B). | M |
| WYB-CERT-002 | Each signature line carries the signer's typed or printed name; the signer's address and telephone appear on the document. | LBR 9004-1(A); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| WYB-ELEC-001 | File through ECF under the clerk's electronic filing procedures; a password use is the attorney's signature. Redact SSNs and TINs to last four digits, birth dates to year, minors to initials, financial account numbers to last four digits. | LBR 5005-2(A); FRBP 9037(a). | M |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WYB-FORM-003 | Same format as the complaint: letter paper, 13 point or larger, double-spaced, one-inch top margin, counsel block top left on page one. | LBR 9004-1(A)-(B). | M |
| WYB-CONT-005 | Each request for relief is a separate motion and is not combined with another pleading or motion. A dispositive motion in an adversary proceeding is accompanied by a separate memorandum; a non-dispositive motion may include a short statement of arguments and authorities. | LBR 9013-1(A)(1)-(3); LBR 9013-2. | M |
| WYB-CONT-006 | A motion to vacate or continue a hearing states the opposing party's position or is summarily denied. | LBR 9013-1(A)(4). | M |
| WYB-CONT-007 | Opposition to a non-dispositive motion in an adversary is filed within 14 days of the motion. | LBR 9013-1(B)(2). | M |
| WYB-LEN-001 | A memorandum may not exceed 20 pages, exclusive of the table of contents and exhibits, without prior permission of the court. | LBR 9013-2. | M |
| WYB-CONT-008 | A memorandum is filed and served at least 7 days before the scheduled hearing unless the court orders otherwise. | LBR 9013-2. | M |
| WYB-CONT-009 | A separate proposed order accompanies each motion; it omits the counsel heading. | LBR 9013-1(C); LBR 9004-1(C). | M |
| WYB-CERT-003 | Certificate of service states the name and address of each person served, the capacity, and the manner and date of service; it is filed within 7 days; a certificate showing service on the official mailing list attaches a copy of that list. | LBR 9013-3. | M |
