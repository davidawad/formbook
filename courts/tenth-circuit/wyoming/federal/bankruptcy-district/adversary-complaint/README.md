## Layout

- Letter paper, 1 inch margins, 13 point Times New Roman, double spaced (2.0 em line pitch; Liberation Serif's line box is 0.655 em, so `body-leading` is 1.345 em).
- Counsel block single spaced at top left (11 point).
- Court title centered and bold (two lines), then the caption: parties in the left column (closed by a right rule and a bottom rule), case information (bankruptcy case, chapter, adversary proceeding, judge) in the right column.
- The title (`COMPLAINT`) centered and bold.
- Paragraphs numbered 1, 2, ... with a 0.5 inch first-line indent. Headings are centered (level 1 bold caps; level 2 centered), sticky, and sit on the double-spaced grid. Prayer items are lettered A., B., ... and restart after each WHEREFORE.
- The date line and the signature block are kept together (`breakable: false`).
- Footer: page number centered.

## Markdown contract (for samples)

- YAML frontmatter, then the body. The body is blank-line separated chunks: `#` heading (level 1), `##` heading (level 2), `- ` item (a lettered prayer item), a paragraph (numbered, unless it comes before the first heading or starts with `WHEREFORE`).
- Frontmatter fields: `counsel` (lines), `client`, `debtor` (lines), `bankruptcy_case`, `chapter`, `adversary`, `judge`, `parties` (list of `{names, designation}`; `v.` is inserted between parties), `title`, `date` (printed under the body), `signature` (`firm`, `signer`, `lines`).
- Inline markdown works (`*italic*`, `**bold**`). Do not type paragraph numbers.
- Not supported: tables, footnotes, raw typst.

## Known gaps and open choices

1. **Only one exemplar.** EVAL-LOOP's tier-2 rule asks for two or three filers. The search (CourtListener RECAP, `court=wyb`, adversary complaints) also found Diamond Oak, Inc. v. Bickford (25-02006, doc 1, 18 pages): it is a "Creditor's Complaint and Objection" captioned in the main case, not an FRBP 7001 adversary caption, so it was not used. Bills v. U.S. Dept. of Education (23-02003, doc 1) was listed but its RECAP storage path did not resolve. So tier 2 is **not** judged against two filers yet.
2. **Consent statement (FRBP 7008(a)).** The exemplar has none and the template does not generate one. The rule requires the pleader to state whether it does or does not consent to final orders by the bankruptcy court. A filer must add that paragraph to the body before filing; the template does not check for it. Left open for David: whether to add a generated check or a required frontmatter field.
3. **Type size: the rule says 13 point, the exemplar is smaller.** LBR 9004-1(A) requires not less than 13 point. The exemplar's body text measures about 11.7 pt (median word bounding-box height from `pdftotext -bbox`, 12.2 pt for the Tear Drop memorandum in the sister folder) and is set in Times New Roman. The template follows the rule (13 pt). The template is therefore longer per line than the exemplar; this is a rule-over-exemplar choice, recorded here.
4. **Caption punctuation.** The exemplar says "vs."; the template inserts "v.". Both are conventions; the template keeps "v.".
5. **Counsel block on the first page.** The template prints the counsel block at 11 point, single spaced (LBR 9004-1(A) requires not less than 10 point for counsel headings). The exemplar's counsel size was not measured; the first page of the render holds the counsel block, the court title and the caption without spill.
6. **Transcription.** The sample is the whole 6-page exemplar, typed as written, including its own typos ("Brady's", "December 5, 2020 to March 4, 2020", "an Wyoming corporation"). The exemplar's paragraph numbers are not used; the template numbers the allegations.
7. **Page count.** The exemplar is 6 pages; this render is 5 pages. The brief's target of 8 to 14 pages applies to exemplars of that length; this complaint is shorter.
8. **Overlay pairing.** Overlay pages pair with the exemplar's pages by shared words, not by position; the overlay is a diagnostic, not a score.
9. **Local rules version.** The posted PDF is a "FINAL CLEAN COPY 6-19" of the local rules with rules effective July 1, 2012. If the court has issued a newer edition, LBR 9004-1 must be checked against it.
10. **No tables of contents.** A complaint does not need one; none is generated.
