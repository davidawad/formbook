# District of Wyoming: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `wyd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the court-posted Local Civil Rules, January 2022, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WYD-FORM-001 | Filings are typed, double-spaced, single-sided, on 8½ × 11 inch paper, with one-inch margins on all sides, in black ink, and in not less than 12 point type. | LR 10.1(a). | M |
| WYD-CONT-001 | Caption conforms to FRCP 10(a) and carries an attorney information block: filing attorney's name, bar number (if any), firm (if any), address, telephone, and email. | LR 10.1(b); FRCP 10(a). | M |
| WYD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances; claims from separate transactions go in separate counts. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| WYD-CONT-003 | File a completed JS-44 Civil Cover Sheet at commencement, signed by the attorney of record; pro se individuals are exempt. | LR 3.1. | M |
| WYD-CONT-004 | A non-governmental corporate party files its FRCP 7.1 disclosure statement with its first appearance or pleading. | FRCP 7.1(a)-(b). | M |
| WYD-CONT-005 | A jury demand is made in a pleading, served no later than 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue. | FRCP 38(b). | M |
| WYD-CERT-001 | Signature block gives the signer's address, email, and telephone; the attorney information block in the caption gives the bar number. | FRCP 11(a); LR 10.1(b). | M |
| WYD-ELEC-001 | File under the CM/ECF Procedures Manual; the filing day runs 12:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. Mountain Time. Redact SSNs and TINs to last four digits, birth dates to year, minors to initials, and financial account numbers to last four digits. | LR 5.2(b); LR 8.1; FRCP 5.2(a). | M |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WYD-FORM-002 | Same page, margin, double-spacing, and type rules as the complaint: 8½ × 11, one-inch margins, double-spaced, not less than 12 point. | LR 10.1(a). | M |
| WYD-CONT-006 | A non-dispositive motion states a short, concise statement of arguments and authorities; a dispositive motion is accompanied by a separately filed brief. | LR 7.1(b)(1)(B), (b)(2)(A). | M |
| WYD-CONT-007 | Before filing a non-dispositive motion, counsel confers in good faith and states the efforts and the opposing party's position in the motion. | LR 7.1(b)(1)(A). | M |
| WYD-LEN-001 | Non-dispositive briefs (opposing and supporting) are limited to 10 pages; dispositive and preliminary-injunction briefs to 25 pages. Exceeding the limit requires leave, and the motion states the proposed page count. Reply: 5 pages for non-dispositive, 10 pages for dispositive motions. | LR 7.1(b)(1)(C)-(D), (b)(2)(B)-(C). | M |
| WYD-CONT-008 | A proposed order for each non-dispositive motion is a separate document with its own caption, omits the attorney information block, and is also sent in Word format to chambers. | LR 7.1(b)(1)(E); LR 10.1(b). | M |
| WYD-ELEC-003 | Redact personal data identifiers in exhibits as in WYD-ELEC-001. | LR 8.1; FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
