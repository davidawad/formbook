# Wyoming Supreme Court: amicus briefs, briefs on the merits and reply briefs

Alias `wyo`. Scope: the appellant's and appellee's briefs (briefs on the merits), the appellant's reply brief and the brief of amicus curiae filed in the Supreme Court of Wyoming.

Verified against the Wyoming Rules of Appellate Procedure (W.R.A.P.), the court's 2024 compilation (PDF created 2025-06-26; Rules 1.01, 7.01, 7.02, 7.03, 7.05 and 7.12 as amended through October 12, 2021, effective January 1, 2022) on 2026-10-22. The court's own copy (wyocourts.gov) answered HTTP 202 with an empty body to curl and to a fetch tool on 2026-10-22; the same file was read from the Internet Archive's copy of that URL.

## Brief of appellant (brief on the merits)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WYO-MER-001 | A title page with the appellate court caption and appellate court case number, the identification of the party filing the brief, and the names, addresses and telephone numbers of the attorneys preparing it; members of the Wyoming Bar include their Wyoming Bar number. | WRAP 7.01(a) | M |
| WYO-MER-002 | A table of contents, with page references. | WRAP 7.01(b) | M |
| WYO-MER-003 | A table of cases alphabetically arranged (in one list or by jurisdiction), statutes and other authorities cited, with references to the pages where they appear. | WRAP 7.01(c) | M |
| WYO-MER-004 | Under appropriate headings and in this order: a statement of jurisdiction; a statement of the issues; a statement of the case; an argument (which may be preceded by a summary), with a standard of review for each issue; a short conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | WRAP 7.01(d)-(h) | M |
| WYO-MER-005 | The signatures of counsel submitting the brief, then a certificate of service, then the appendix (the judgment or final order appealed from, the decision letter or reasons for judgment, the statement of costs). | WRAP 7.01(i)-(k) | M |
| WYO-MER-006 | The brief of appellee conforms to Rule 7.01, without the documents appended to the appellant's brief; a statement of the issues or of the case is not required. | WRAP 7.02 | M |

## Open

- The page limits (WYO-FORM-007, WYO-AMI-003) exclude the title page, the tables, the certificate of service and the appendices, which `params.page_limit` (a whole-document count) cannot express; they are not machine-checked.
- The amicus exemplar (2014) predates the 2015 amendment that set the 35-page limit; its 52 pages are not a model for the limit.
