# Colorado Supreme Court: briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `colo`. Scope: briefs on the merits, answering and reply briefs, amicus briefs, motions, petitions for certiorari and for review, and petitions for writ and for discretionary review filed in the Colorado Supreme Court. Verified against Colorado Rule Change 2005(6), which amends C.A.R. 27, 28, 32, 40 and 53 (PDF on the Colorado Judicial Branch site), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: C.A.R. 21 (original proceedings and petitions for writ), C.A.R. 49 to 52 (certiorari review of Court of Appeals decisions, except C.A.R. 53 as amended above), C.A.R. 51 (briefs on the merits), C.A.R. 29 (amicus briefs), and the 2023 amendment of C.A.R. 32 that third-party mirrors describe (the coloradojudicial.gov copy returned no current PDF; state-rules.com and courtrules.net refused the request). Section order is therefore tier 2 (exemplar convention) for every doc type, and the length rule for each doc type is cited only where the 2005 text states it.

|---|---|---|---|

| COLO-FORM-003 | Text is double-spaced. Quotations longer than two lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | CAR 32(b)(2) | M |
