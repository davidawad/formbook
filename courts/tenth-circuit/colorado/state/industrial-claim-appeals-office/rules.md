# Industrial Claim Appeals Office (Colorado): no filed exemplar

Alias `coloworkcompcom`. Scope: the Industrial Claim Appeals Office (ICAO) is an administrative appellate panel that reviews workers' compensation orders. It is not a court that receives appellate briefs from parties, so this folder has no doc type and no exemplar. Appeals from ICAO orders are briefed in the Colorado Court of Appeals, whose rules are in [../../court-of-appeals/rules.md](../court-of-appeals/rules.md) (C.A.R. 3.1, "Appeals from Industrial Claim Appeals Office"). Verified against Colorado Court of Appeals opening brief instructions for ICAO appeals (2017.02.24 edition, COA-INST below) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: ICAO's own filing requirements (procedure before the panel, its forms, and any format rule for documents filed with it), the Division of Workers' Compensation rules (7 CCR 1101-3), and C.R.S. § 8-43-307 (appeal to the Court of Appeals). Searches surfaced these but their text was not fetched from a primary source, so nothing is cited from them here. Public filed briefs for this office: none found (ICAO decisions reach the public; its own filings do not); the 10-minute search for two exemplars is recorded in the bead comment.
