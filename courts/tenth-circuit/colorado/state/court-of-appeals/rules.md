# Colorado Court of Appeals: briefs and motions

Alias `coloctapp`. Scope: appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply, amicus) and motions filed in the Court of Appeals. Verified against Colorado Rule Change 2005(6), which amends C.A.R. 27, 28 and 32 (PDF on the Colorado Judicial Branch site), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: C.A.R. 28(a) (required sections and their order) and C.A.R. 29 (amicus briefs), and the 2023 amendment of C.A.R. 32 that third-party mirrors describe (coloradojudicial.gov rule-text pages returned no current PDF, http://state-rules.com/colorado/appellate/32 and courtrules.net refused the request). Section order and amicus rules are therefore tier 2 (exemplar convention), and the 2005 text is the only rule text cited here.

## Opening, answering and amicus briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| COLOCTAPP-FORM-001 | Only 8½ by 11 inch paper. | CAR 32(b)(1) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-FORM-002 | Typeface is plain roman with serifs (italics or boldface for emphasis), 14-point or larger, footnotes included. The caption may be 12-point if needed to fit on one page. | CAR 32(a)(1)-(2) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-FORM-003 | Text is double-spaced. Quotations longer than two lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | CAR 32(b)(2) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-FORM-004 | Margins are at least 1½ inches at the top and 1 inch at the left, right and bottom. Page numbers are required and are the only text allowed in the margins. | CAR 32(b)(3) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-FORM-005 | Text appears only on the face side of each page. | CAR 32(b)(4) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-CONT-001 | First page carries basic document information in this order on the left: court name and mailing address; lower court, judge and case number; party names; attorney name, address and telephone number and registration number; document title. On the right: a "Court Use Only" area at least 2½ by 1¾ inches, and the case number. | CAR 32(d) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-LEN-001 | Principal briefs (opening, answer, opening-answer, answer-reply) are at most 30 pages or 9,500 words; reply briefs are at most 18 pages or 5,700 words. Headings, footnotes and quotations count. Caption, tables of contents and authorities, certificates and addendum do not count. | CAR 28(g) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-CERT-001 | A brief subject to a word limit includes a certificate by the attorney, or the unrepresented party, that it complies with the limit, stating the number of words. | CAR 32(a)(3) | M |
| COLOCTAPP-CONT-002 | Required sections and their order (statement of the issues, statement of the case, argument, conclusion) follow the exemplars: a convention, not verified against C.A.R. 28(a). | Exemplars (opening, answering) | C |
| COLOCTAPP-CONT-003 | Table of contents and table of authorities precede the body; the caption and tables are not counted toward the limit. | CAR 28(g) | C |
