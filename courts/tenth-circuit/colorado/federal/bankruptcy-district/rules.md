## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| COB-FORM-001 | No Local Bankruptcy Rule sets page size, margins, typeface, point size or spacing for pleadings. The FRBP 9004 general form rule requires legibility only. Do not treat the 12-point and 1-inch defaults as court rules here. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR index. | A |
| COB-CONT-001 | Caption in substantial conformity with Official Form 416D (adversary caption), with the entire case number including the assigned judge's initials. | LBR 7001-1; FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| COB-CONT-002 | Caption gives court name, case title, case number and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document. | FRBP 9004(b)(1)-(4); FRBP 7010. | M |
| COB-CONT-003 | Statement of jurisdiction names the case, its number and Code chapter, and the district and division, and states whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| COB-CONT-004 | Claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (applying FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| COB-CONT-005 | Paper adversary filings include a cover sheet in substantial conformity with Director's Procedural Form 1040. | LBR 7001-1 (paper filings). | M |
| COB-CERT-001 | Signed by at least one attorney of record in the attorney's individual name, stating signer's address and telephone number. | FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| COB-CERT-002 | Signature block lists name, address, telephone, e-mail address and bar number below the signature line. | LBR 9011-4 (Contact Information). | M |
| COB-ELEC-001 | Filed electronically via CM/ECF in text-searchable PDF. | LBR 5005-4 (Format). | M |
| COB-ELEC-003 | Redact SSN and taxpayer ID to last four digits, birth date to year, minor's name to initials, financial account number to last four digits. Filer, not Clerk, redacts. | FRBP 9037(a); LBR 9037-1. | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| COB-FORM-002 | No Local Bankruptcy Rule sets page size, margins, typeface or spacing for motions or briefs. Judge-specific practice standards not retrieved. | LBR index. | A |
| COB-CONT-006 | Each motion, application or other request for relief is in writing, and is accompanied by a proposed order on a separate document. | FRBP 9013(a); LBR 9013-1 (Proposed Orders). | M |
| COB-CONT-007 | Notice of the motion uses the form in substantial conformity with L.B.F. 9013-1.1, states the specific relief and the specific date of the objection deadline (not only a number of days). | LBR 9013-1 (Form of Notice). | M |
| COB-CONT-008 | Motion served as FRBP 7004 and 9014 require, including exhibits, notice and proposed order; certificate of service filed within three days, in substantial conformity with L.B.F. 9013-1.2. | LBR 9013-1 (Documents to be Served; Certificate of Service); FRBP 9013(b). | M |
| COB-CONT-009 | Response to a motion in an adversary proceeding is due within 14 days after service; replies within seven days of the response. | LBR 7007-1. | M |
| COB-CONT-010 | Objections state grounds with supporting legal authority; a movant files a Certificate of Non-contested Matter or Certificate of Contested Matter in substantial conformity with L.B.F. 9013-1.3 or 9013-1.4. | LBR 9013-1 (Objections; Certificates). | M |
| COB-CERT-003 | Signed by at least one attorney of record with signer's address and telephone number, and contact information below the signature line. | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 9011-4. | M |
| COB-ELEC-004 | File electronically in CM/ECF in text-searchable PDF; redact personal identifiers under FRBP 9037. | LBR 5005-4; FRBP 9037(a). | M |
