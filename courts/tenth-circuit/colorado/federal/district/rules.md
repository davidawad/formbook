# District of Colorado: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `cod`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of Practice, effective December 1, 2025, revised 3/4/2026, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (current edition, Civil Rules last amended in 2025) on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| COD-FORM-003 | 8½ × 11 inch white paper; margins 1½ inch top and 1 inch left, right and bottom; double spaced; one side only; black ink. | LR 10.1(b), (c), (e), (f). | M |
| COD-FONT-002 | Not less than 12-point font, unless otherwise ordered. Judge-specific practice standards not retrieved. | LR 10.1(d). | M |
| COD-CONT-005 | Motion involving a contested legal issue states the rule or statute it is filed under and is supported by legal authority in the motion. A motion is a separate document and may not be included in a response or reply. | LR 7.1(d). | M |
| COD-CONT-006 | Duty to confer, with the specific efforts described in the motion or a certificate attached to it. Exempt: unrepresented prisoner or detainee cases, FRCP 12 motions, FRCP 56 motions, and LAttyR 5(a)-(b) motions. | LR 7.1(a)-(b). | M |
| COD-CONT-007 | Unopposed motion titled "Unopposed Motion for ____." | LR 7.1(c). | M |
| COD-CONT-008 | Every citation includes the specific page or statutory subsection. A cited unpublished opinion is provided to any unrepresented party. | LR 7.1(e). | M |
| COD-CONT-009 | Proposed order is a separate document with its own caption; no "it is ordered" or "so ordered" paragraph attached to the motion. | LR 7.1(g). | M |
| COD-CONT-010 | Motions, responses and replies are concise; a verbose or unintelligible filing may be stricken. | LR 7.1(i). | C |
| COD-LEN-001 | No general page limit for motion briefs in the Local Rules. Word limits (10,000 words, Arial 12-point, with certificate of compliance) apply only to patent cases under LPtR 17. Judge-specific practice standards not retrieved. | LR 7.1(i); LPtR 17. | A |
| COD-CERT-002 | Signed by at least one attorney of record with signer's address, e-mail address and telephone number. | FRCP 11(a). | M |
| COD-ELEC-006 | File electronically in CM/ECF; redact personal identifiers under FRCP 5.2(a). | LR 5.1(a); FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
