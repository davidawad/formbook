Alias `ksb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Kansas, effective December 1, 2024, and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (current edition, last amended in 2025) on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KSB-FORM-001 | 8½ × 11 inch paper; type no smaller than 12 points, set no more than an average of 12 characters per inch; double spaced where practicable; all pages numbered from the first page, including cover and tables. No margin rule for pleadings. | LBR 9004.1(a)(1)-(2). | M |
| KSB-CONT-001 | Caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416 (adversary caption), with court name, title, case and adversary number, and a brief designation of the document. | FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b); LBR 9004.1(a)(4). | M |
| KSB-CONT-002 | Caption states a short statement of the relief requested; a pleading may not contain unrelated relief. | LBR 9004.1(d). | M |
| KSB-CONT-003 | Statement of jurisdiction names the case, its number and Code chapter, and the district and division; states whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008. | M |
| KSB-CONT-004 | Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet (Form B1040) completed and submitted with each complaint commencing an action. | LBR 7003.1(a). | M |
| KSB-CONT-005 | Claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010 (applying FRCP 10(b)). | M |
| KSB-CERT-001 | Signed by at least one attorney of record in the attorney's name, stating address and telephone number; comply with D. Kan. Rule 5.1(b) contact information (name, address, telephone, facsimile, e-mail, and state registration number). | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 9011.4(b)(1); D. Kan. Rule 5.1(b). | M |
| KSB-ELEC-001 | File electronically under LBR 5005.1 and Appendix 1-01; PDF format, scanned documents OCR'd to be text-searchable. | LBR 5005.1(c), Appendix 1-01. | M |
| KSB-ELEC-003 | Redact SSN and taxpayer ID to last four digits, birth date to year, minor's name to initials, financial account number to last four digits. Filer redacts; Clerk does not. | FRBP 9037(a); LBR 9037.1. | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KSB-FORM-002 | 8½ × 11 inch paper; type no smaller than 12 points at no more than an average of 12 characters per inch; double spaced where practicable; all pages numbered from the first page. No margin rule for briefs. | LBR 9004.1(a)(1)-(2). | M |
| KSB-LEN-001 | Arguments and authorities section of a brief or memorandum does not exceed 30 pages absent court order. | LBR 9013.1(b). | M |
| KSB-CONT-006 | Brief contains the nature of the matter, a concise statement of facts with record support, the question presented, and argument citing all statutes, rules and authorities relied on. | LBR 9013.1(a)(1)-(4). | M |
| KSB-CONT-007 | Briefs on non-dispositive motions are prohibited unless the court requires one. | LBR 9013.2(d). | M |
| KSB-CONT-008 | Motion filed at least 14 days before hearing unless good cause; motion may carry a separate notice of hearing or notice of objection deadline. | LBR 9013.2(a)-(c). | M |
| KSB-CONT-009 | Unpublished decisions are cited in the form In re Smith, No. 02-12345 (Bankr. D. Kan. Jan. 7, 2005); attached only if not available electronically. | LBR 9004.1(b)(1). | M |
| KSB-CONT-010 | Exhibits to briefs are separately labeled. | LBR 9013.1(c). | M |
| KSB-CONT-011 | Motions and orders prepared and submitted under LBR 9004.1. | LBR 9013.2(e). | M |
| KSB-CERT-002 | Signed by at least one attorney of record, comply with D. Kan. Rule 5.1(b). Motion filed with a certificate of service indicating service on required parties. | FRBP 9011(a); LBR 9011.4(b)(1); LBR 9013.2(a), 9013.3(a). | M |
| KSB-ELEC-004 | File electronically under LBR 5005.1 and Appendix 1-01 in PDF, text-searchable. Redact personal identifiers under FRBP 9037 and LBR 9037.1. | LBR 5005.1; FRBP 9037(a); LBR 9037.1. | M |
| KSB-FORM-003 | Subsequent filings endorsed in the upper right-hand corner of the first page with the case number; title describes contents and states on whose behalf filed. | LBR 9004.1(a)(3). | M |
