# District of Kansas: complaints and motion briefs

Alias `ksd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the Local Rules for Kansas District Court, effective April 1, 2026, and the Administrative Procedures for Filing, Signing, and Verifying Pleadings and Papers by Electronic Means (revised February 2025), and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (current edition, Civil Rules last amended in 2025) on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KSD-FORM-001 | Letter-size paper, double spaced; text typewritten or printed in uncompressed Times New Roman or similar. No margin rule appears in the Local Rules or the Administrative Procedures. | LR 5.1(a). | M |
| KSD-FONT-001 | Not less than 12-point type; footnotes not less than 10-point (a local deviation from the 12-point default for footnotes). | LR 5.1(a). | M |
| KSD-CONT-001 | Caption gives court name, title naming all parties, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation; claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(a)-(b). | M |
| KSD-CONT-002 | Civil docket cover sheet, supplied by the clerk, submitted with any complaint commencing an action; represented filers submit it through the System at case opening. | LR 3.1; ADMIN II(A). | M |
| KSD-CONT-003 | Nongovernmental corporate party files its Rule 7.1 disclosure statement with its first appearance or pleading. | FRCP 7.1(b)(1). | M |
| KSD-CERT-001 | Signing party's name, address, telephone number, facsimile number and e-mail address. Attorneys add their state supreme court registration number, or equivalent for non-Kansas admission. | LR 5.1(b)(1)-(2); FRCP 11(a) (address, e-mail, telephone). | M |
| KSD-ELEC-001 | File through the electronic filing System, PDF format; scanned documents are text-searchable (OCR performed before filing). Pro se initiating complaints may be filed by mail, in person or by e-mail under ADMIN II(A). | LR 5.1(a); ADMIN I(B), II(A), IV(A)(1). | M |
| KSD-ELEC-003 | Redact SSN to last four digits, minors' names to initials, birth dates to year, financial account numbers to last four digits. | FRCP 5.2(a); ADMIN II(I)(1). | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KSD-FORM-002 | Letter-size paper, double spaced; uncompressed Times New Roman or similar. No margin rule in the Local Rules or Administrative Procedures. | LR 5.1(a). | M |
| KSD-FONT-002 | Not less than 12-point type; footnotes not less than 10-point. Court may strike a filing that does not substantially comply. | LR 5.1(a). | M |
| KSD-LEN-001 | Principal brief on a motion other than discovery, summary judgment or class certification: 15 pages maximum; reply 5 pages maximum. Discovery motions: 10 and 3 pages. Summary judgment and class certification: 40 and 15 pages. Motion to exceed filed at least 3 days before the brief deadline. | LR 7.1(d)(1)-(4). | M |
| KSD-CONT-004 | Motion or opening brief states the specific relief sought, the nature of the matter, a concise statement of facts with record support for each fact, and argument citing all statutes, rules and authorities relied on. | LR 7.1(a)(1)-(4). | M |
| KSD-CONT-005 | Joint or unopposed motion states so in the caption and body. | LR 7.1(b). | M |
| KSD-CONT-006 | Exhibits attached to motions or briefs are separately labeled and indexed. | LR 7.1(e). | M |
| KSD-CONT-007 | Notice of supplemental authority filed on CM/ECF, stating reasons and page or point, body not over 350 words; response within 5 days. | LR 7.1(f). | M |
| KSD-CERT-002 | Signing party's name, address, telephone, facsimile and e-mail; attorney registration number. | LR 5.1(b). | M |
| KSD-ELEC-004 | File in PDF through the System, text-searchable; exhibits scanned as PDF with OCR, in segments of no more than 75 MB. | ADMIN IV(A)(1). | M |
| KSD-ELEC-005 | Redact personal identifiers under FRCP 5.2(a) and the privacy section of the Administrative Procedures. | FRCP 5.2(a); ADMIN II(I)(1). | M |
