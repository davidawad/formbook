# Supreme Court of Kansas: briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `kan`. Scope: answering and brief-on-the-merits filings, amicus briefs, reply briefs, motions, petitions for review, petitions for discretionary review, petitions for writ and petitions for rehearing filed in the Supreme Court of Kansas.

Verified against the filed exemplars listed under Sources on 2026-10-22; the Kansas Supreme Court Rules themselves were not retrieved.

Local rules not retrieved: Kansas Supreme Court Rules, https://www.kscourts.gov/Rules-Procedures-Forms/Supreme-Court-Rules (HTTP 403 on every attempt from the build host, 2026-10-22). No rule in this file is cited to a source we fetched. The templates therefore use the default tier-1 set (US letter, 1 in margins, 12 pt body, see `data/checks-default.yaml`) and the conventions the filed exemplars share (tier 2). Every page-limit, typeface, spacing and certificate requirement of the Kansas Supreme Court Rules is open until the rules are fetched.
