# Court of Appeals of Kansas: briefs and motions

Alias `kanctapp`. Scope: appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply, amicus) and motions filed in the Court of Appeals. No Kansas rule text was retrieved, so every requirement below is either a convention seen in the filed exemplars (`C`) or a template-only rule (`T`). Nothing here is a mandate (`M`) until the Kansas rules are read from a primary source.

Local rules not retrieved: Kan. Sup. Ct. ## Opening, answering and amicus briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| KANCTAPP-FORM-001 | Letter-size paper (exemplars are 8½ × 11 in). | EX-OPEN, EX-ANS, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-FORM-002 | Body text in Times New Roman (exemplars use Times), about 13 pt; footnotes and block quotations at the same or smaller size. | EX-OPEN, EX-ANS, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-FORM-003 | Body text double-spaced; block quotations, headings and footnotes single-spaced. | EX-OPEN, EX-ANS, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-FORM-004 | One-inch margins on all sides; page numbers at the bottom of each page, the cover numbered 1. | EX-OPEN, EX-ANS, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-CONT-001 | Cover: "No." and the appellate case number; the court name ("IN THE COURT OF APPEALS OF THE STATE OF KANSAS"); caption with each party's designation; the appeal line (trial court and judge) and the district court case number; the document title; counsel block with bar numbers, address, telephone, and e-mail. | EX-OPEN, EX-ANS, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-CONT-002 | Table of contents and table of authorities precede the body (the exemplars' opening brief and amicus brief carry both). | EX-OPEN, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-CONT-003 | Body sections in exemplar order: Nature of the Case, Statement of the Issue(s), Statement of the Facts, Argument and Authorities, Conclusion. | EX-OPEN | C |
| KANCTAPP-CERT-001 | Signature block: firm, signer(s) with bar numbers and contact lines, date line. | EX-OPEN, EX-ANS, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-CERT-002 | Certificate of service on opposing counsel. | EX-OPEN, EX-AMICUS | C |
| KANCTAPP-LEN-001 | Page or word limits for principal briefs: not verified (Kan. Sup. Ct. R. 6.07 not retrieved). The template sets no limit. | (none) | A |
