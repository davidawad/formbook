# Tenth Circuit Court of Appeals: opening, answering and reply briefs

Alias `ca10`. Scope: appellant's opening briefs, appellee's answering briefs and reply briefs. Verified against Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure (effective December 1, 2025) and Tenth Circuit Rules (effective January 1, 2026), court-posted PDF, on 2026-10-20.

## Opening brief

Appellant's principal brief. Rules marked FRAP 32 apply to every brief in this court unless a row says otherwise.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|

| CA10-FORM-002 | Margins at least 1 inch on all four sides; page numbers may sit in the margins and no other text may. | FRAP 32(a)(4). | M |
| CA10-FORM-003 | Proportionally spaced serif face, 14-point or larger (sans-serif allowed in headings and captions). Deviates from the 12-point default: the national floor is 14 point. 10th Cir. R. 32(A) prefers 14 and accepts 13; footnotes match body size. | FRAP 32(a)(5)(A); 10th Cir. R. 32(A). | M |
| CA10-FORM-004 | If monospaced, no more than 10½ characters per inch. | FRAP 32(a)(5)(B). | M |
| CA10-FORM-005 | Plain roman type; italics or boldface for emphasis only; case names italicized or underlined. | FRAP 32(a)(6). | M |
| CA10-FORM-006 | Front cover is blue. Shows case number centered at top, court name, case title, nature of proceeding and court or agency below, brief title naming the party, counsel's name, office address and telephone. | FRAP 32(a)(2)(A)-(F). | M |

## Answering brief

Appellee's principal and response brief. In a cross-appeal the appellee brief has its own limits (FRAP 28.1).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA10-FORM-007 | Form requirements for paper, spacing, margins, typeface, type style and monospacing are the same as the opening brief (CA10-FORM-001 to 005). The 14-point floor applies. | FRAP 32(a)(4)-(6). | M |
| CA10-FORM-008 | Front cover is red. Cover fields match the opening brief (CA10-FORM-006). | FRAP 32(a)(2). | M |
| CA10-CONT-008 | Appellee may omit the jurisdictional statement, statement of issues, statement of the case and standard-of-review statement unless dissatisfied with the appellant's statement; all other contents follow FRAP 28(a). | FRAP 28(b). | M |
| CA10-CONT-009 | Front cover states whether oral argument is requested (first brief of each party). | 10th Cir. R. 28.2(C)(2). | M |
| CA10-CONT-010 | Statement of related cases at the end of the table of authorities in the first brief. | 10th Cir. R. 28.2(C)(3). | M |
| CA10-CONT-011 | If the appellant omitted required attachments (FRAP 28.1 / 10th Cir. R. 28.2(A)), appellee attaches them. | 10th Cir. R. 28.2(B). | M |
| CA10-LEN-004 | Appellee's brief without a cross-appeal: principal brief limit of 13,000 words (1,300 lines if monospaced). In a cross-appeal, appellee's principal and response brief limit is 15,300 words (1,500 lines if monospaced). | FRAP 32(a)(7)(B)(i); FRAP 28.1(e)(2)(B). | M |
| CA10-LEN-005 | Cross-appeal page cap for appellee's principal and response brief: 35 pages. Word limit controls when compliant. | FRAP 28.1(e)(1). | M |
| CA10-CERT-005 | Certificate of compliance required (Form 6 form) with word or line count. | FRAP 32(g)(1)-(2); FRAP 28.1(e)(2). | M |
| CA10-CERT-006 | Proof of service unless served through ECF; signature by counsel. | FRAP 25(d); FRAP 32(d). | M |
| CA10-CERT-007 | Unpublished decision citation and copy rules as in CA10-CERT-004. | 10th Cir. R. 32.1(A)-(B). | M |
| CA10-ELEC-005 | CM/ECF filing and privacy redaction rules as in CA10-ELEC-001 and CA10-ELEC-003. | 10th Cir. R. 25.3, 25.5. | M |

## Reply brief

Appellant's reply brief (no cross-appeal). In a cross-appeal the appellant's response and reply brief and appellee's reply brief follow FRAP 28.1(e).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA10-FORM-009 | Form requirements for paper, spacing, margins, typeface, type style and monospacing are the same as the opening brief (CA10-FORM-001 to 005). The 14-point floor applies. | FRAP 32(a)(4)-(6). | M |
| CA10-FORM-010 | Front cover is gray. Cover fields match the opening brief (CA10-FORM-006). | FRAP 32(a)(2). | M |
| CA10-CONT-012 | Reply brief includes a table of contents with page references and a table of authorities with page references. Further briefs are barred unless the court permits. | FRAP 28(c). | M |
| CA10-LEN-006 | Reply brief limit: 6,500 words, or 650 lines if monospaced; 15 pages cap applies only to briefs that do not meet the word limit. | FRAP 32(a)(7)(A), (B)(ii). | M |
| CA10-LEN-007 | Cross-appeal appellant's response and reply brief: 13,000 words (1,300 lines if monospaced). Appellee's reply in a cross-appeal: 6,500 words (650 lines if monospaced). | FRAP 28.1(e)(2)(A), (C). | M |
| CA10-CERT-008 | Certificate of compliance required for a reply brief subject to the word limit, in the Form 6 form. | FRAP 32(g)(1)-(2). | M |
| CA10-CERT-009 | Proof of service unless served through ECF; signature by counsel. | FRAP 25(d); FRAP 32(d). | M |
| CA10-ELEC-006 | CM/ECF filing and privacy redaction rules as in CA10-ELEC-001 and CA10-ELEC-003. | 10th Cir. R. 25.3, 25.5. | M |
