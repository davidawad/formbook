# District of New Mexico Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `nmb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the New Mexico Local Bankruptcy Rules (NM LBR), adopted November 15, 2014, amended December 1, 2025, and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMB-FORM-001 | Caption substantially conforms to the appropriate Official Form 416 (adversary caption) and states the court name, case title, case and adversary numbers, and a designation of the document. | FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| NMB-FORM-002 | Paper must be clearly legible; common abbreviations are acceptable. FRBP sets no typeface, point size or margin rule; NM LBR sets none either. | FRBP 9004(a); LBR 1001-1(c). | C |
| NMB-FORM-003 | Every paper filed after the petition carries the year, case number, assigned judge's initial and chapter, e.g. 24-12345-j7. | LBR 9004-2. | M |
| NMB-CONT-001 | Jurisdiction allegation names the case's name, number and Code chapter and the district and division where pending. | FRBP 7008(a); FRCP 8(a). | M |
| NMB-CONT-002 | The complaint states whether the pleader does or does not consent to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008(a). | M |
| NMB-CONT-003 | Claims stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) (via FRBP 7010). | M |
| NMB-CONT-004 | A nongovernmental corporation that is a party to the adversary files its ownership statement under FRBP 7007.1 with its first pleading. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b). | M |
| NMB-CONT-005 | A jury demand is filed under FRBP 5005; if a jury is demanded the court sets a deadline for consent to a bankruptcy judge conducting the trial. | FRBP 9015(a); LBR 9015-1. | M |
| NMB-CERT-001 | Attorney signature: name, address, telephone number and email address appear below the signature line. Signature is by attorney of record. | LBR 9011-1(a); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| NMB-CERT-002 | Wet-ink signature is required for any paper requiring a person's signature; the court does not accept digitally signed documents. | LBR 9011-1(b). | M |
| NMB-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing through CM/ECF follows the Clerk's online manual; unrepresented parties file on paper unless granted pro se drop-box access. | LBR 5005-2(a); LBR 5005-3. | M |
| NMB-ELEC-003 | Redact SSNs and taxpayer IDs to last four digits, birth dates to year, minors to initials, and financial-account numbers to last four digits. | FRBP 9037(a). | M |
| NMB-ELEC-004 | Filer reviews the scanned image of any paper filing within 14 days after filing. | LBR 5005-1. | A |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMB-FORM-005 | Title of the motion describes the relief sought. | LBR 9013-1(a). | M |
| NMB-FORM-006 | Same case-number line as adversary complaints (NMB-FORM-003) and the same caption as NMB-FORM-001. | LBR 9004-2; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| NMB-CONT-006 | Motion states whether it is opposed; if concurrence was not obtained, the motion recites the attempts made. | LBR 7007-1; LBR 9013-1(b). | M |
| NMB-CONT-007 | For opposed motions, a separate notice of deadline to object (not on the motion paper) is filed and served; objections are due 21 days after service; an optional reply is due 14 days after service of the objection or response. | LBR 9004-1; LBR 9013-1(d); LBR 7007-1. | M |
| NMB-CONT-008 | Briefs and supporting authority are optional unless the court orders otherwise. | LBR 7007-1; LBR 9013-1(i). | C |
| NMB-CONT-009 | Sur-replies require leave of court. | LBR 7007-2. | M |
| NMB-CONT-010 | Exhibits are provided to all opposing parties (email is sufficient) and three sets are provided at a final evidentiary hearing. | LBR 9070-1. | M |
| NMB-LEN-001 | NM LBR sets no page or word limit for adversary motions or briefs; the presiding judge's practices govern. | LBR 1001-1(c). | A |
| NMB-SERV-001 | A certificate of service is filed for all motions and notices within a reasonable time after service; it may be part of the motion. | LBR 9013-1(j). | M |
| NMB-CERT-003 | Signature and contact block as in NMB-CERT-001 and NMB-CERT-002. | LBR 9011-1(a)-(b). | M |
