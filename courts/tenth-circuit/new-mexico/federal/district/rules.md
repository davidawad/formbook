# District of New Mexico: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `nmd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules of Civil Procedure for the District of New Mexico, adopted October 1, 2020 (amended effective October 1, 2020), and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure as amended to December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.
Re-check cadence: check the court rules page for amendments before use and after each local-rule revision; re-check the applicable federal rules after each annual amendment cycle and at least annually (next routine review by 2027-10-20).

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMD-FORM-001 | 8½ × 11 inch white, opaque, unglazed paper, printed on one side. Letter-quality type of at least 12 pitch (LR states no point size). Margins: left, right and bottom at least 1 inch; top at least 1½ inches (deviates from the 1 inch default). Text double-spaced except footnotes and quotations. | LR 10.1; FRCP 10. | M |
| NMD-FORM-002 | The first page carries the case file number and the initials of the assigned Judges. | LR 10.1. | M |
| NMD-CONT-001 | Caption gives the court name, title, file number and Rule 7(a) designation; the complaint title names all parties. Title clearly identifies the substance of the document. | FRCP 10(a); LR 10.2. | M |
| NMD-CONT-002 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances; separate transactions go in separate counts. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| NMD-CONT-003 | Attach the Clerk's civil cover sheet to the complaint initiating the action. | LR 3.1. | M |
| NMD-CONT-004 | A nongovernmental corporate party files its disclosure statement with its first appearance or pleading. | FRCP 7.1(a)-(b). | M |
| NMD-CONT-005 | A jury demand is made in a pleading or separate written demand served within the time set by FRCP 38(b). | FRCP 38(b). | M |
| NMD-CERT-001 | Attorney entry of appearance and initial pleading signature: name, firm, address, telephone, electronic address and facsimile number; signed initial pleading. | LR 83.4(a); FRCP 11(a). | M |
| NMD-CERT-002 | Unsigned paper is stricken unless signed within 14 days after the omission is raised; duplicate and electronic signatures are originals. | LR 10.3(b), 11.1. | M |
| NMD-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing through CM/ECF is mandatory (except pro se); filing and procedure follow the CM/ECF Administrative Procedures Manual. | LR 5.1(a). | M |
| NMD-ELEC-003 | Redact SSNs, taxpayer IDs, birth dates, minors' names and financial-account numbers to the FRCP 5.2(a) forms. | FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
| NMD-LEN-001 | No page limit is set for the complaint in the local rules. | LR 7.5 (motions and briefs only). | A |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMD-FORM-003 | Same page form as NMD-FORM-001: 8½ × 11 inch, 12 pitch letter-quality type, 1 inch left/right/bottom margins, 1½ inch top margin, double-spaced except footnotes and quotations. | LR 10.1. | M |
| NMD-FORM-004 | First page carries the case file number and the initials of the assigned Judges; title identifies the relief and any filing it responds to. | LR 10.1, 10.2. | M |
| NMD-LEN-002 | A motion, or motion plus separate supporting brief combined, may not exceed 27 double-spaced pages; a response may not exceed 24; a reply may not exceed 12. | LR 7.5. | M |
| NMD-LEN-003 | Exhibits to a motion, response or reply, including deposition excerpts, total no more than 50 pages unless all parties agree. | LR 10.5. | M |
| NMD-CONT-006 | A motion states the grounds with particularity and the relief sought; it recites a good-faith request for concurrence or may be summarily denied. | LR 7.1(a). | M |
| NMD-CONT-007 | Each legal position cites authority; fact allegations are supported by declarations, affidavits, deposition excerpts or other documents. Authority may be filed in a separate brief served with the motion. | LR 7.3(a)-(b). | M |
| NMD-CONT-008 | An unopposed motion is accompanied by a proposed order approved by each party. | LR 7.2. | M |
| NMD-CONT-009 | Response is due 14 calendar days after service of the motion; reply 14 days after service of the response. Extensions by agreement require a notice filed with the Court. | LR 7.4(a). | M |
| NMD-CONT-010 | A surreply requires leave of court. Notice of Supplemental Authorities body is limited to 350 words. | LR 7.4(b), 7.8(c). | A |
| NMD-SERV-001 | Every motion, response and reply includes a certificate of service on each party. | LR 7.1(b). | M |
| NMD-CERT-003 | Filed papers carry counsel's signature block with the entry-of-appearance information in NMD-CERT-001. | LR 83.4(a), 11.1. | M |
