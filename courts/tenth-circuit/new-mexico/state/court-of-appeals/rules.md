# New Mexico Court of Appeals: briefs, motions and writ petitions

Alias `nmctapp`. Scope: appellate briefs (brief in chief, answer brief, reply brief, amicus brief), motions (Rule 12-309 NMRA), and petitions for writ of error (Rule 12-503 NMRA) filed in the Court of Appeals. Verified against the New Mexico Rules of Appellate Procedure, NMRA Part 12 (compiled PDF, created 2026-08-11, as posted on NMOneSource), on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the Court of Appeals' own mailing address (https://coa.nmcourts.gov/ and https://www.nmcourts.gov/courts/court-of-appeals/ returned HTTP 302 and no page body; not required by Rule 12-305 NMRA, so the template prints the court name only); the Electronic Filing System user guide referred to in Rule 12-307.2(H) NMRA (not retrieved, so no EFS format beyond the rule text is cited); the Appendix to Rule 23-112 NMRA (citation format, Rule 12-318(E) NMRA; not retrieved). Exemplar conventions are marked `C`.

## Brief in chief (opening brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMCTAPP-CONT-002 | Brief in chief sections, in order: table of contents (listing each section heading and its page), table of authorities (separate headings by type of authority; New Mexico decisions listed separately), summary of proceedings (nature of the case, course of proceedings, disposition below, and facts with record citations), argument (standard of review, contentions, preservation statement, authorities), and conclusion stating the relief sought. | NMRA 12-318(A)(1)-(5) | M |
| NMCTAPP-LEN-001 | The body of the brief in chief is at most 35 pages, or 11,000 words if proportionally spaced (Times New Roman), except by permission of the court. The body is headings, footnotes, quotations and all other text except the cover, caption, tables, signature blocks, oral-argument statement and certificate of service. | NMRA 12-318(F)(1)-(3) | M |
| NMCTAPP-CERT-001 | If the brief exceeds 35 pages, the table of contents includes a statement of compliance, and the brief states the body word count and the word-processing program and version. | NMRA 12-318(A)(1)(c), (G) | M |
| NMCTAPP-CONT-003 | Cover, caption and the certificate of compliance (the template prints it as the exemplars do) are not part of the body. | NMRA 12-318(F)(1) | C |

## Answer brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMCTAPP-CONT-005 | An answer brief conforms to the brief in chief requirements, but a summary of proceedings is not included unless deemed necessary. Consolidated answers are titled as such on the cover. | NMRA 12-318(B) | M |
| NMCTAPP-LEN-002 | The body of an answer brief is subject to the same limits as the brief in chief (35 pages or 11,000 words). | NMRA 12-318(F)(2)-(3) | M |

## Reply brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMCTAPP-CONT-006 | A reply brief conforms to 12-318(A)(1), (2) and (4): table of contents, table of authorities, and argument, and replies only to arguments or authorities in the answer brief. Consolidated replies are titled as such on the cover. | NMRA 12-318(C) | M |

## Writ of error petition

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMCTAPP-CONT-009 | A petition for writ of error attaches the district court order with its filing date, and includes a concise statement of the case and proceedings, a statement of how the order conclusively determines the question, is separate from the merits, and is effectively unreviewable on appeal, and any other matters of record. | NMRA 12-503(E)(1)-(3) | M |
| NMCTAPP-LEN-005 | The body of the petition is at most 10 pages, or 3,150 words if proportionally spaced, except by permission of the court. | NMRA 12-503(F)(1)-(3) | M |
| NMCTAPP-CERT-002 | If the body exceeds 10 pages, the petition states compliance with the word limit, giving the word count and the program used. | NMRA 12-503(G) | M |
