# New Mexico Supreme Court: briefs, petitions, motions and amicus briefs

Alias `nm`. Scope: papers filed in the Supreme Court of New Mexico: briefs in chief (opening), answer briefs, reply briefs, briefs on the merits, petitions for writ of certiorari under Rule 12-502 NMRA, petitions for extraordinary writs under Rule 12-504 NMRA, motions with supporting memoranda, and amicus curiae briefs. Verified against Rule 12-305 NMRA (form of papers prepared by parties) as printed in the New Mexico Supreme Court's Proposal 2025-031 (file dated March 2025), read from the PDF on 2026-10-21.

## Caption and cover (briefs, motions and petitions)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NM-CAP-001 | The front page has a caption naming the appellate court (the Supreme Court of the State of New Mexico). | NMRA 12-305(F)(1)(a) | M |
| NM-CAP-002 | The caption names the parties and their status below and on appeal, with the party initiating the proceeding in the trial court or agency listed first (for example "Plaintiff-Appellee" against "Defendant-Appellant"). | NMRA 12-305(F)(1)(b) | M |
| NM-CAP-003 | For an extraordinary writ under Rule 12-504, the caption lists the party seeking the writ first, then the respondent(s), then the real parties in interest, if any. | NMRA 12-305(F)(1)(b) | M |
| NM-CAP-004 | The caption gives the docket number in the Supreme Court if one has been assigned, and the title of the paper. | NMRA 12-305(F)(1)(c)-(d) | M |
| NM-CAP-005 | Parties and attorneys may add Ms., Mrs., Mr. or Mx. and a personal pronoun after the party name in the caption. | NMRA 12-305(F)(2) | A |
| NM-COVER-001 | The front cover of a brief or docketing statement shows the county or administrative body in which the case was filed or tried, and the name of the trial judge or administrative officer. Both are omitted for briefs filed in the Supreme Court under Rule 12-502. | NMRA 12-305(G)(1)-(2) | M |
| NM-COVER-002 | The front cover shows the name, mailing address and telephone number of counsel filing the paper, or of the party if unrepresented. | NMRA 12-305(G)(3) | M |
| NM-COVER-003 | If oral argument is requested under Rule 12-319(B)(1), the front cover of the brief states that oral argument is requested. | NMRA 12-305(G)(4) | M |
| NM-COVER-004 | The cover of a brief shows its title in capitals (for example BRIEF IN CHIEF, ANSWER BRIEF, REPLY BRIEF, BRIEF ON THE MERITS, AMICUS CURIAE BRIEF, PETITION FOR WRIT OF CERTIORARI). | Filer convention, FILED-EXAMPLE covers | C |
