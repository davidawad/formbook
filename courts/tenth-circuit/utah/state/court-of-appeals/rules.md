# Utah Court of Appeals: briefs

Alias `utahctapp`. Scope: principal briefs (opening, answering, reply, amicus) filed in the Court of Appeals. Verified against the Utah Courts "Checklist for Briefs" (Utah Rules of Appellate Procedure 24, 26 and 27, revised December 2025) and "Checklist for Reply Briefs" (revised May 2026), both on utcourts.gov, on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the text of URAP 24, 26 and 27 itself. The URAP 27 text was not reachable: https://state-rules.com/utah/appellate/27 returned HTTP 403, https://www.courtrules.net/utah/utah-appellate-procedure/rule-27 returned HTTP 403, and the utcourts.gov rules pages returned HTTP 406 or 404 to curl. Every requirement below therefore cites the court's own checklist, which states the rule and its numbers; the checklist is the source for the mandated values, and the rule text should be checked before filing. The checklist is not the rule itself: a value it states that disagrees with the rule text wins. The 13-point minimum and the one-inch margins come from the checklist, not from the rule text, and are marked M on that basis.

## Content and order (principal briefs)

|---|---|---|---|

## Length

|---|---|---|---|

| UTAHCTAPP-LEN-002 | Appellant's reply brief (and appellee's cross-appeal reply) is at most 7,000 words, or 15 pages if the brief does not comply with the word limit. | CL-BRIEF, Length of Brief; CL-REPLY | M |
