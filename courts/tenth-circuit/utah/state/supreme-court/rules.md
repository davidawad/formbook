# Utah Supreme Court: briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `utah`. Scope: briefs (principal, reply, amicus), petitions (rehearing, discretionary review, extraordinary writ) and motions filed in the Utah Supreme Court. Verified against the Utah Courts "Checklist for Briefs" (Utah Rules of Appellate Procedure 24, 26 and 27; revised December 2025) and "Checklist for Reply Briefs" (revised May 2026) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the text of URAP 24, 27 and 21 themselves. `https://legacy.utcourts.gov/rules/view.php?type=urap&rule=27` returned no connection (HTTP 000, repeated); the proposed 2025 URAP 27 for public comment on legacy.utcourts.gov returned HTTP 000; state-rules.com/utah/appellate/27 and courtrules.net returned HTTP 403. Every requirement below is therefore cited to the court's own checklists, which summarise those rules, and not to rule text. Requirements that the checklists do not state (URAP 5, 19, 23C, 35 text; amicus rules; petition length) are tier 2 (exemplar convention) or advisory. The "Revised May 2026" reply checklist is the newer of the two and is used where they differ.

## Principal briefs (appellant, appellee, petitioner's merits brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| UTAH-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ by 11 inches. | CHK, Size and Binding | M |
| UTAH-FORM-002 | Margins are at least one inch on top, bottom and both sides of each page. | CHK, Printing Requirements | M |
| UTAH-FORM-003 | Proportionally spaced typeface is 13-point or larger for both text and footnotes. A monospaced typeface may not exceed ten characters per inch. | CHK, Printing Requirements | M |
| UTAH-FORM-004 | Text is double-spaced; 1½ line spacing is not acceptable. Block quotations, headings and footnotes may be single-spaced (exemplar convention). | CHK, Printing Requirements; exemplars | M |
| UTAH-FORM-005 | Page numbers appear on the body pages; cover and tables use roman numerals (exemplar convention). | Exemplars | C |
| UTAH-FORM-006 | Briefs are printed on both sides of the page. | CHK, Printing Requirements | A |
| UTAH-CONT-001 | Cover carries: the full case title as it appeared in the trial court; party designations as in the trial court and as in the appellate court (e.g. appellant/appellee); "In the Utah Supreme Court"; public or non-public designation; appellate case number; document title; nature of the proceeding (e.g. "Appeal"); trial court name and judge; the filing attorney's name and address at lower right and opposing counsel's at lower left. | CHK, Cover Requirements | M |
| UTAH-CONT-002 | Contents in this order: list of parties; table of contents with page references; table of authorities with page references (cases alphabetically with parallel citations, rules, statutes, other authorities); introduction; statement of the issues, each with standard of review and supporting authority; citation to the record showing the issue preserved (or grounds for review if not preserved); statement of the case with record page references (facts, procedural history, disposition below); summary of the argument; argument; conclusion stating the relief sought. | CHK, Content Requirements | M |
| UTAH-CONT-003 | Original signature of counsel on one copy; reproduced signatures on the others. The template prints the typed "s/" signature. | CHK, Content Requirements; exemplars | A |
| UTAH-LEN-001 | Appellant and appellee briefs are 14,000 words or less, or 30 pages if the brief does not comply with the word limit. Headings, footnotes and quotations count; caption, tables and addendum do not (word limit as stated by the checklist). | CHK, Length of Brief | M |
| UTAH-CERT-001 | A certificate of compliance states that the brief complies with the word limitation, with the word count. | CHK, Certificate of Compliance | M |
| UTAH-CERT-002 | The certificate of compliance states that the filing complies with URAP 21 regarding public and non-public records. | CHK, Certificate of Compliance | M |
| UTAH-CONT-004 | Certificate of service on the attorneys for all parties (on the Attorney General for a felony appeal, and on self-represented parties), with the signature of the filer. | CHK, Service of Briefs; exemplars | M |
| UTAH-ADD-001 | An addendum (opinion, findings, orders, determinative provisions) may be attached at the end or filed separately; it is not counted against the page or word limit. | CHK, Addendum | M |
