# District of Utah Bankruptcy Court: adversary complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `utb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the Local Rules of Practice of the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Utah, effective December 1, 2025, and Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| UTB-FORM-001 | Caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416D (adversary caption): court name, district, debtor caption, bankruptcy number, chapter, adversary proceeding number, judge's name, and plaintiff/defendant block. | LBR 9004-1(a)(2); FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b). | M |
| UTB-FORM-002 | Top left attorney block: submitting attorney with Utah State Bar number, address, telephone, email (recommended) and "Attorney for". | LBR 9004-1(a)(2) (Form 416D). | M |
| UTB-FORM-003 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inch white; text at least 12 point; one-sided; double-spaced except quotations, footnotes and court forms; each page numbered consecutively at the bottom. Left margin at least 1 inch. Top margin at least 1½ inches: LBR 5005-3(a)(2)(A) sets 1 inch, but DUCivR 10-1(1) (applied via LBR 1001-1(a)) sets 1½ inches; the 1½ inch figure is the conservative choice. | LBR 5005-3(a)(1)-(7); DUCivR 10-1(1), (3)-(5). | M |
| UTB-FORM-004 | Footnotes are at least 12 point (DUCivR 10-1(3)). Font otherwise at least 12 point under LBR 5005-3(a)(4); no typeface is named. | DUCivR 10-1(3); LBR 5005-3(a)(4). | M |
| UTB-FORM-005 | Title designates the paper's purpose and names the filing party. | LBR 9004-1(b). | M |
| UTB-CONT-001 | Jurisdiction allegation names the case name, number and Code chapter and the district and division where pending; the complaint states whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008(a). | M |
| UTB-CONT-002 | Claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) (via FRBP 7010). | M |
| UTB-CONT-003 | Adversary cover sheet filed at commencement unless the complaint is filed electronically. | LBR 7003-1. | M |
| UTB-CONT-004 | A nongovernmental corporation that is a party files its ownership statement under FRBP 7007.1 with its first pleading. | FRBP 7007.1(a)-(b). | M |
| UTB-CONT-005 | Jury demand: "JURY DEMANDED" in capitals on the first page immediately below the title; demand filed under FRBP 5005. | LBR 9015-1(a)-(b); FRBP 9015(a). | M |
| UTB-CERT-001 | Signed by an attorney admitted under LBR 2090-1; a "/s/" or graphical electronic signature is permitted in the signature space. | LBR 9011-1; LBR 5005-2(g)(2); FRBP 9011(a). | M |
| UTB-CERT-002 | A corporation or other non-individual party appears through counsel; no unrepresented filing for entities. | LBR 9011-2(a). | M |
| UTB-ELEC-001 | Attorneys file all papers through ECF; ECF filer login is the signature. | LBR 5005-1(a)(1); LBR 5005-2(a), (g)(1). | M |
| UTB-ELEC-003 | Redact personal identifiers per FRBP 9037 (SSN and taxpayer ID to last four digits, birth date to year, minors to initials, financial-account numbers to last four digits); filing party is responsible. | LBR 5005-1(b); FRBP 9037(a). | M |
| UTB-ELEC-005 | Caption must comply with LBR 9004-1(a) on each electronically filed document, including attachments and exhibits. | LBR 5005-2(e). | M |
| UTB-LEN-001 | No page limit for the complaint in the local rules. | LBR 9013-1(h) (motions and responses only). | A |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| UTB-FORM-006 | Same page form and caption as UTB-FORM-001 to UTB-FORM-004; use the adversary caption (Form 416D) for motions filed in an adversary proceeding and Form 416B in the main case. | LBR 5005-3(a); LBR 9004-1(a)(1)-(2). | M |
| UTB-CONT-006 | The motion and supporting memorandum are one document: an initial separate section states the precise relief sought and specific grounds, followed by sections on facts, authority with controlling citations, and argument. | LBR 9013-1(c)(1). | M |
| UTB-CONT-007 | Notice of Motion and Notice of Hearing in substantial conformity with Local Form 9013-1: relief requested, last date for response or objection, statement that the hearing may be stricken if no response is timely filed, and the responding party's duty to attend. Filed in original form with a certificate of service. | LBR 9013-1(d)(1)-(6). | M |
| UTB-LEN-002 | A motion or response may not exceed 15 pages (excluding face sheet, table of contents, statements of issues and facts, and exhibits); a reply may not exceed 10 pages. Overlength requires a prior court order showing the pages needed and good cause. | LBR 9013-1(h)-(i). | M |
| UTB-CONT-008 | Response is one document with recitation of facts, concise statement of each opposing basis with authority, and argument; it is filed by the date in the notice. | LBR 9013-1(e)(1). | M |
| UTB-CONT-009 | Reply is limited to rebuttal of the response; no motion may be made in a response or reply; motions go in a separate document. | LBR 9013-1(e)(2), (e)(4). | M |
| UTB-CONT-010 | A request to strike a hearing is made at least two business days before the hearing. | LBR 9013-1(g). | A |
| UTB-SERV-001 | Certificate of service accompanies the Notice of Motion and Hearing and evidences service on the entities required by FRBP 9013 and LBR 9013-1(d)(6). | LBR 9013-1(d); FRBP 9013. | M |
| UTB-CERT-003 | Signed by an attorney admitted under LBR 2090-1; ECF signature as in UTB-CERT-001; signature block and contact information under the signature. | LBR 9011-1; LBR 5005-2(g). | M |
| UTB-ELEC-004 | Exhibits submitted electronically; limited to relevant excerpts, clearly identified as excerpts; cover page per exhibit. | LBR 5005-2(d)(1)-(2). | M |
| UTB-ELEC-006 | Redaction under FRBP 9037 as in UTB-ELEC-003. | LBR 5005-1(b); FRBP 9037(a). | M |
