# District of Utah: complaints and motion memoranda

Alias `utd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the Local Rules of Civil Practice, District of Utah, effective December 2025, and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, December 1, 2025, on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| UTD-FORM-001 | 8½ × 11 inch plain white paper, single-sided. Top margin at least 1½ inches (deviates from the 1 inch default); remaining margins at least 1 inch. | DUCivR 10-1(1). | M |
| UTD-FONT-001 | Font clearly legible, minimum 12 point, including footnotes. | DUCivR 10-1(3). | M |
| UTD-FORM-002 | Text double-spaced, except block quotes and footnotes. Document pages numbered consecutively. | DUCivR 10-1(4)-(5). | M |
| UTD-CONT-001 | First page upper-left block above the caption: counsel's name and Utah State Bar number (or party's name if self-represented), firm, mailing address, telephone, email, party represented, and "Limited Appearance" when applicable. | DUCivR 10-1(6)(A). | M |
| UTD-CONT-002 | Caption table gives party names, document title, case number, and assigned judge and referred magistrate judge if applicable. | DUCivR 10-1(6)(B); FRCP 10(a). | M |
| UTD-CONT-003 | Numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b). | M |
| UTD-CONT-004 | Civil Cover Sheet, Form JS-44, filed with the complaint. | DUCivR 3-1(b)(2). | M |
| UTD-CONT-005 | A motion may not be included in the complaint; any accompanying motion is a separate document. | DUCivR 3-1(b)(3). | M |
| UTD-CONT-006 | If a jury is requested, "JURY DEMANDED" appears in capital letters in the caption of the initial pleading, immediately below the title. | DUCivR 10-1(7). | M |
| UTD-CONT-007 | A nongovernmental corporate party files its disclosure statement with its first appearance or pleading. | FRCP 7.1(a)-(b). | M |
| UTD-CERT-001 | Every document, excluding exhibits, signed by counsel or the unrepresented party; electronic signature per the ECF Procedures Manual. | DUCivR 10-1(8)(A). | M |
| UTD-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing is signed and filed as the ECF Procedures Manual requires; the Clerk may require refiling of non-conforming documents. | DUCivR 5-1(a); DUCivR 10-1(2), last paragraph. | M |
| UTD-ELEC-003 | Redact the personal identifiers listed in FRCP 5.2 in every court filing. | DUCivR 5.2-1; FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
| UTD-LEN-001 | No page limit for the complaint in the local rules. | DUCivR 7-1(a)(4) (motions and briefs only). | A |

## Motion memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| UTD-FORM-003 | Same page form as UTD-FORM-001 and UTD-FORM-002: 8½ × 11 inch, 1½ inch top and 1 inch other margins, 12 point minimum including footnotes, double-spaced except block quotes and footnotes, consecutive pagination. | DUCivR 10-1(1)-(5). | M |
| UTD-CONT-008 | The motion and memorandum are one document: an initial separate section states the specific relief sought and the grounds, followed by facts, supporting authority and argument. | DUCivR 7-1(a)(1). | M |
| UTD-CONT-009 | No motion, including a Rule 56(d) motion or cross-motion, appears inside a response or reply; each is separately filed. | DUCivR 7-1(a)(3). | M |
| UTD-LEN-002 | Motions under FRCP 12(b), 12(c) or 23(c): motion or response 25 pages or 7,750 words; reply 10 pages or 3,100 words. Motions under FRCP 56(a): 40 pages or 12,400 words; reply 20 pages or 6,200 words. Motions under FRCP 65: 25 pages or 7,750 words; reply 10 pages or 3,100 words. All other motions, responses and replies: 10 pages or 3,100 words. | DUCivR 7-1(a)(4)(A)-(D). | M |
| UTD-LEN-003 | Headings, citations, quotations and footnotes count toward the limit; caption, face sheet, tables, signature block, certificate of service and exhibits do not. | DUCivR 7-1(a)(6)(A)-(B). | M |
| UTD-LEN-004 | A document over the limit needs a prior court order (motion stating pages or words needed and good cause), must contain a table of contents, and carries a word-count certification at the end. | DUCivR 7-1(a)(6)(C), (a)(7)(A)-(B). | M |
| UTD-CONT-010 | Response due 28 days after service for FRCP 12(b), 12(c), 23(c) and 56(a) motions; 14 days for FRCP 65 and all other motions. Reply due 14 days after service of the response. Stipulated extensions require a motion filed before the deadline; a stipulation alone is ineffective. | DUCivR 7-1(a)(4), (a)(5). | M |
| UTD-CONT-011 | Notice of Supplemental Authority is limited to 2 pages and contains no argument; a response to it is limited to 2 pages within 7 days. | DUCivR 7-1 (Supplemental Authority). | M |
| UTD-CONT-012 | Exhibits are separated by a cover page clearly identifying each exhibit. | DUCivR 10-1 (Exhibits). | M |
| UTD-CERT-002 | Signature by counsel as in UTD-CERT-001; signature block and certificate of service sit outside the page count. | DUCivR 10-1(8), 7-1(a)(6)(B). | M |
| UTD-SERV-001 | No certificate of service is required for a paper served by CM/ECF filing; a paper served by other means needs a certificate of service filed with it or within a reasonable time after service. | FRCP 5(d)(1)(B). | M |
| UTD-ELEC-004 | Redact the FRCP 5.2 personal identifiers in every filing. | DUCivR 5.2-1; FRCP 5.2(a). | M |
