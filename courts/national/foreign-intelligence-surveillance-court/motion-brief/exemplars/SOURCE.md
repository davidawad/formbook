# Exemplar sources (FISC motion-brief)

Both are publicly released FISC filings posted by the court at fisc.uscourts.gov ("Public Filings"). Both are scanned pages with an OCR text layer (pdftotext yields text; the scan is image-based). Date fetched for both: 2026-10-14.

## 1. FILED-EXAMPLE-accuracy-concerns-misc-19-02-motion-brief.pdf

- URL: https://www.fisc.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/FISC%20Misc%2019%2002%20Motion%20to%20Modify%20Reporting%20Period%20June%204%202020%20200714.pdf
- Case: In re Accuracy Concerns Regarding FBI Matters Submitted to the FISC
- Docket no.: Misc. 19-02
- Document: Motion to Modify Reporting Period (5 pages, with verification on p. 5)
- Filer: United States of America, by the Department of Justice, National Security Division, Office of Intelligence (government filer)
- Date filed: June 4, 2020 (received stamp on p. 1: "2020 JUN -4 PM 1:01")
- Verification: Dawn M. Browning, Deputy General Counsel, Federal Bureau of Investigation, 28 U.S.C. § 1746

## 2. FILED-EXAMPLE-yahoo-directives-105b-07-01-motion-brief.pdf

- URL: https://www.fisc.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/105B%28g%29%2007-01%20Motion-3.pdf
- Case: In re Directives Pursuant to Section 105B of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act
- Docket no.: No. 105B(g) 07-01
- Document: Yahoo! Inc.'s Unclassified Motion under FISC Rule 62 to Allow Pre-Publication Review of Documents Produced in Response to This Court's July 15, 2013 Order (6 pages, certificate of service on p. 6)
- Filer: Yahoo! Inc. (private party; counsel Marc J. Zwillinger and Jacob A. Sommer, ZwillGen PLLC)
- Date filed: September 30, 2013 (dated line and certificate of service; the scan's received stamp reads "SEP 30 2013")

Search: the court's public-filings index (fisc.uscourts.gov/public-filings, pages 0-17) lists 354 PDFs. The 90 that are motions, responses or related filings from 2007-2025 were downloaded and tested with pdftotext for a text layer (about 40 of the 90 have one); image-only scans were skipped. The four motions first tried (2023 and 2024 government and movant motions) are image-only. Among the motions with text, these two are chosen as the two exemplars: a government motion (2020) and a private-party motion (2013), covering two filer conventions. Other text-bearing motions (e.g. the 2013 Provider Rule 62 motion, Misc. 13-xx and BR 14-01 government motions) are alternates.
