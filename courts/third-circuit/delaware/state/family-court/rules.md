# Delaware Family Court: civil and criminal filings

Alias `delfamct`. Scope: papers filed in the Family Court of the State of Delaware, in its civil and criminal dockets (petitions, motions, briefs, letter memoranda). No court-root FILED-EXAMPLE exists and no public filed brief was collected for this court, so this file is rules only.

## Civil: form of papers (Rules 7, 10, 11)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELFAMCT-FORM-001 | Pleadings, motions and other papers are typewritten or handwritten on opaque, unglazed white paper not exceeding 8 1/2 by 11 inches, filed without backer. Printing on one or both sides is permitted if legible. | FC-CIV Rule 7(c). | M |
| DELFAMCT-SIG-001 | Every pleading, motion and other paper is signed by at least one attorney of record (or by the party), and states the signer's address and telephone. | FC-CIV Rule 11(a). | M |
| DELFAMCT-CAP-001 | A pleading's caption sets out the name of the Court, the title of the action, the title of the pleading under Rule 7(a), and the file number and petition number if known. A petition names all parties; other pleadings name the first party on each side with an indication of others. Family Court forms completed in full satisfy this. | FC-CIV Rule 10(a). | M |
| DELFAMCT-CAP-002 | A motion or other paper has a caption with the name of this Court, the caption of the case, the file number, the petition number, the date of filing, and a brief descriptive title. | FC-CIV Rule 7(b)(5). | M |
| DELFAMCT-CONT-001 | All averments of claim or defense are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances; each separate transaction is a separate count where separation aids clarity. | FC-CIV Rule 10(b). | M |

## Civil: briefs and letter memoranda (Rule 107)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELFAMCT-CAP-003 | Every brief and letter memorandum states the name of this Court; the title of the case and its number in this Court; and the name and address of counsel for the filing party, with office addresses of any counsel resident outside the State. | FC-CIV Rule 107(c)(1). | M |
| DELFAMCT-FORM-002 | Briefs may be printed, typed or handwritten, reproduced by a process that gives a clear black image on opaque unglazed white paper. Carbon copies are not accepted without permission. All printed matter is at least 11-point type. | FC-CIV Rule 107(c)(2). | M |
| DELFAMCT-FORM-003 | Briefs are firmly bound at the left margin. A brief produced by a process other than printing has pages not exceeding 8 1/2 by 11 inches, with double spacing between lines except for quotations and footnotes. Printed briefs have pages of approximately 7 by 9 1/2 inches. | FC-CIV Rule 107(c)(3). | M |
| DELFAMCT-MARGIN-001 | Side margins of briefs are not less than 1 1/2 inches. This is stricter than the one-inch margin of the general rules. | FC-CIV Rule 107(c)(3). | M |
| DELFAMCT-FORM-004 | Typed text size is not stated for briefs; only printed matter has the 11-point minimum. Typed or word-processed text size is a tier 2 or 3 choice and must be judged against exemplars. | FC-CIV Rule 107(c)(2)-(3). | A |
| DELFAMCT-CITE-001 | Citations follow the Bluebook, with no parallel reporter citations. Computer-database citations (Lexis, Westlaw, Fastcase) and unreported opinions take the forms printed in Rule 107(c)(5)(i)-(ii). | FC-CIV Rule 107(c)(5). | M |
| DELFAMCT-CITE-002 | An unreported or memorandum opinion cited is attached to the brief with its file number stated; if it lacks a statement of facts sufficient to show its pertinency, a statement of facts is attached too. | FC-CIV Rule 107(f). | M |
| DELFAMCT-CONT-003 | A brief contains, in order: a table of contents or index; a table of citations arranged alphabetically with the pages on which each appears; in the first brief of each party, a statement of the case (nature of the proceedings and a concise chronological statement of facts, with page references to transcript, pleadings and exhibits); a statement of the questions involved; and argument divided under headings, one section per question. | FC-CIV Rule 107(d)(1)-(5). | M |
| DELFAMCT-LEN-001 | Without leave, an opening or answering brief is at most 35 pages, and a reply brief at most 20 pages, excluding the appendix. The table of contents and table of citations are excluded from the count; the statement of the case and the other required parts are included. | FC-CIV Rule 107(g). | M |
| DELFAMCT-FILE-001 | The original and a copy of every brief and letter memorandum are filed with the Clerk of the county where the case is pending; the Clerk delivers the original to the judge. A letter from counsel containing argument is sent to the Clerk for filing. | FC-CIV Rule 107(a). | M |
| DELFAMCT-TIME-001 | Unless the Court orders otherwise, briefs and letter memoranda are filed and copied to opposing counsel not less than 5 days before the argument they pertain to. | FC-CIV Rule 107(b). | M |
| DELFAMCT-PRINT-001 | Briefs may be printed or copied on one or both sides of the page if legibility is maintained. | FC-CIV Rule 107(c)(4). | A |

## Criminal: motions and briefs (Rule 47)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELFAMCT-CRIM-001 | The form of briefs and letter memoranda in criminal matters is the form in Family Court Civil Rule 107 (all DELFAMCT-CAP-003, FORM-002 to 004, CITE, CONT-003, LEN-001 apply). | FC-CRIM Rule 47(g). | M |
| DELFAMCT-CRIM-002 | The form of motions and other papers in criminal matters is the form in Family Court Civil Rule 7 (DELFAMCT-FORM-001, CAP-002 apply). | FC-CRIM Rule 47(g). | M |
| DELFAMCT-ORD-001 | A written motion consists of a notice of motion, the motion, a proposed form of order, and supporting affidavits and material, filed with the Court and served on the opposing party or counsel. The opposing party files its response, with a proposed form of order, within 10 days unless the Rules or leave of Court provide otherwise. | FC-CRIM Rule 47(a). | M |

## Notes for the template (not checked mechanically)

- The civil rules set no typeface family, point size for typed text, line-spacing value for pleadings, or margin for pleadings (only the 1 1/2-inch side margin for briefs). Those choices are tier 2 or 3 and need exemplars before a template commits to them.
- Civil Rule 107(c)(3) allows a printed brief at about 7 by 9 1/2 inches. A template targets the 8 1/2 by 11 inch form, which the rule expressly permits for briefs produced by any process other than printing.
