# Delaware Court on the Judiciary: filings

Alias `deljudct`. Scope: papers filed with the Court on the Judiciary, its Clerk and its Boards and Panels (complaints, exceptions, briefs, papers of service). No court-root FILED-EXAMPLE exists, and no public filing was found: proceedings are confidential under Rule 17 (see `usage/SOURCE.md`). This file is rules only.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELJUDCT-SIG-001 | A complaint bears the complainant's original signature, the complainant's address, and the name of the judicial officer involved. | COJ Rule 5(a). | M |
| DELJUDCT-CONT-001 | A complaint states with particularity the facts for the charge (wilful misconduct in office; wilful and persistent failure to perform duties; commission after appointment of an offense involving moral turpitude; or other persistent misconduct under the Delaware Judges' Code of Judicial Conduct). It attaches any transcript or record documentation needed for a fair account of the context. | COJ Rule 5(a). | M |
| DELJUDCT-VER-001 | A complaint is executed by oath or affirmation under penalty of perjury before a notary public or other person authorized to administer oaths. | COJ Rule 5(b). | M |

## Filing and service

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELJUDCT-FILE-001 | The original of every paper filed with the Court is filed with the Clerk or a Deputy Clerk in any county; filing by mail is complete only on receipt. Copies for individual members may be filed with the original or provided on notice to the Clerk. | COJ Rule 11(a). | M |
| DELJUDCT-FILE-002 | Every paper filed with the Board is filed directly with each Board member, with notice of filing to the Clerk, unless the Rules provide otherwise. Filing by mail is complete on receipt by the Board. | COJ Rule 11(b). | M |
| DELJUDCT-SERV-001 | Service is by personal service (including delivery to a clerk or responsible person at the attorney's office) or by mail, complete on mailing. | COJ Rule 11(c). | M |
| DELJUDCT-SERV-002 | Papers presented for filing contain an acknowledgment of service by the person served, or proof of service stating the date, the manner and the name of the person served, certified by the person who served. | COJ Rule 11(d). | M |

## Exceptions and briefing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELJUDCT-BRIEF-001 | Within 10 days of service of the final report, the judicial officer and presenting counsel may file exceptions to it. | COJ Rule 15(b)(2). | M |
| DELJUDCT-BRIEF-002 | If the exceptions raise only issues of law, the Court schedules briefing and argument under the practices and procedures of the Delaware Supreme Court. Briefing format is not set in the COJ rules; see the local-rules note above. | COJ Rule 15(b)(3). | M |
| DELJUDCT-TIME-001 | The Chief Justice (or the Board, for cause) may enlarge or shorten a time period, with or without motion. | COJ Rule 19(b). | M |

## Confidentiality

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELJUDCT-CONF-001 | Records and proceedings of the Preliminary Investigatory Committee panels are private and confidential. All hearings and proceedings of the Board and the Court are private, and all records except a final order of suspension, removal or retirement are confidential unless the Court orders otherwise on the judicial officer's request. | COJ Rule 17. | M |

## Form of papers

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DELJUDCT-FORM-001 | The COJ rules set no typeface, size, spacing, margin or paper rule.
