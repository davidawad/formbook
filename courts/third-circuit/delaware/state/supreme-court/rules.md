# Delaware Supreme Court: briefs, amicus briefs and motions

Alias `del`. Scope: briefs (opening, answering, reply, brief on the merits), amicus curiae briefs, and motions in the Supreme Court of the State of Delaware. Verified against the Rules of the Supreme Court of the State of Delaware, "SC Rules as amended through 2-2-26" (PDF dated 2026-02-03), on 2026-10-21. Rule numbers below are the Supreme Court Rules unless marked otherwise.

## Opening, answering and brief-on-the-merits (`answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`)

The Delaware rules have one brief form for the appellant's opening brief and the appellee's answering brief (R. 14(b)). A brief on the merits (the appellant's opening brief in the exemplar) follows the same rule; the doc-type name is kept for the court's exemplar set.

## Amicus curiae brief (`amicus-brief`)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEL-FORM-301 | An amicus brief must comply with R. 13 (Times New Roman 14-point, double spacing, margins of at least 1 inch, 8 1/2 x 11 pages). | SCT R. 28(c) | M |
| DEL-FORM-302 | The cover is green, identifies the party or parties supported, and states whether the brief supports affirmance or reversal. | SCT R. 28(c), 14(a)(ii) | M |
| DEL-FORM-303 | Order, each under a distinctive title on a new page: table of contents with page references; table of citations with page references; concise statement of the amicus's identity, interest and source of authority to file; the authorship and funding statement of R. 28(c)(4); the argument (may be preceded by a summary; a standard of review is not required). | SCT R. 28(c)(1)-(5) | M |

| DEL-LEN-301 | Length: an amicus brief may be no more than one-half the maximum length for a party's principal brief (5,000 words for an opening or answering brief). The cover, the material required by R. 28(c)(1)-(4) and the signature block are not counted. | SCT R. 28(d) | M |
