## Why this one

A defense memorandum on a Rule 12(b)(6) motion, filed in this court, with a caption, a legal standard, argument headings, a conclusion, a signature block and a certificate of service. It is 11 pages, which is in the target range. Port Hamilton Refining & Transportation v. Limetree Bay Terminals (1:24-cv-00004, ECF 17, 10 pages) is a second candidate with storage; it was not saved.

It is a memorandum, not the notice-of-motion packet. The notice is a separate filing (ECF 13 here). The template therefore produces the memorandum of law; the notice and proposed order are separate documents.
