# Motion with memorandum of law: United States Bankruptcy Court, D.V.I. (`vib`)

Doc type `motion-brief`, under [`../`](../) (the bankruptcy district court). A motion in an adversary proceeding, with the memorandum of law attached as the brief (LBR 7008-1(C)). Governing rules: FRBP 9013, 7008 (by way of LBR 7008-1), 9004; LBR 5005-1, 7008-1, 9013-1 of the District Court of the Virgin Islands, Bankruptcy Division.

## Governing rules (verified 2026-10-14)

| ID | Requirement | Source (verified text) |
|---|---|---|
| VIB-MB-CAPTION | The caption is complete: court, case title, case and adversary numbers, designation of the document (FRBP 9004(b)). In an adversary proceeding the caption conforms to Official Form 416D (LBR 7008-1(A)). | FRBP 9004(b): <https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frbp/rule_9004>; LBR 7008-1(A) in the VI bankruptcy local rules: <https://www.vid.uscourts.gov/sites/vid/files/local_rules/NewBK_RulesDec2017.pdf> |
| VIB-MB-TITLE | The motion has a descriptive title in addition to the complete adversary caption (LBR 7008-1(A)). | LBR 7008-1(A), same PDF |
| VIB-MB-GROUNDS | The motion states its grounds with particularity and the relief requested (FRBP 9013). | FRBP 9013: <https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frbp/rule_9013> |
| VIB-MB-PROPOSED | A written motion is accompanied by a proposed order filed as an attachment (LBR 9013-1(A)). Not generated by this template: the proposed order is a separate document. | LBR 9013-1(A), same PDF |
| VIB-MB-LENGTH | A brief filed with a motion is limited to 20 pages (LBR 7008-1(C)). The template does not count pages; the author must. | LBR 7008-1(C), same PDF |
| VIB-MB-FONT | Font size no smaller than 12 Courier, or equivalent (LBR 5005-1(B)). The template uses 13 pt EB Garamond, for the same reason as the complaint's VIB-AC-FONT. Typeface: face required = none (any other face is allowed at an equivalent size), so the template keeps EB Garamond (visual-only: not court-named, no substitute). | LBR 5005-1(B), same PDF; text verified 2026-10-15 |
| VIB-MB-PAGE | 8½ × 11 paper; pages sequentially numbered (LBR 5005-1(A)). Footer carries "Page n of N". | LBR 5005-1(A), same PDF |
| VIB-MB-SPACING | Double-spaced body (the exemplar's line pitch). A convention, not a rule of this court (tier 2). | The exemplar |
| VIB-MB-HEADINGS | Level 1 is an unnumbered section (FACTUAL BACKGROUND, CONCLUSION). Under it, level 2 is I., level 3 is A., level 4 is 1., level 5 is a. The author types no numbers. | The exemplar's structure; a convention |
| VIB-MB-SIGNATURE | Dated; "Respectfully submitted"; each signing counsel with `/s/`, firm and address (FRBP 9011 signing convention). | The exemplar's signature pages |

## Frontmatter contract

| Field | Required | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| `division`, `debtor`, `chapter`, `case_number`, `caption`, `adversary` | yes | As in the complaint's contract (see `../adversary-complaint/README.md`). |
| `title` | yes | The cover title, in capitals. |
| `footer` | no | Short title for the footer; defaults to `title`. |
| `counsel` | yes | Cover counsel blocks, lower right: `firm` (printed in capitals) and `lines` (markdown, so `*...*` is italic). |
| `date` | yes | "Dated:" line. |
| `signature` | yes | Groups: `signer` (printed `/s/ name`) and `lines`. Groups separated by "- and –". |

Markdown body: `#` is a centered bold section heading; `##`, `###`, `####`, `#####` are numbered as above (typed numbers are a doubling error, which the sample once had). Footnotes `[^n]`. Ordinary markdown lists are unnumbered bullets. Body paragraphs are double-spaced with a first-line indent.

## Layout decisions

- The cover is one page: the caption, the title and the first counsel block. Spacing between the cover's parts was tightened (never the type) so both counsel blocks fit. A longer counsel list will spill to page 2 and must be shortened by the author.
- The TOC lists levels 1–4 with dot leaders and page numbers. Page numbers come from the document, so the TOC is correct only after the render; the render is checked against the TOC page numbers in this review.

## Pages reviewed (Gate 2, this PDF)

All 8 pages of `samples/OUTPUT-prosser-motion-to-dismiss.pdf` were rendered at 60 dpi and looked at:

- p.1: cover. Fixed in this pass: the second counsel block spilled to page 2 (fixed by tightening the spacing between the cover's parts, widening the caption's left column to 3.9 in, and widening the counsel column). The Hunton block now fits, with its last line close to the footer.
- p.2: TOC. Fixed in this pass: the entries wrapped with the leader and page number in the wrong place (now the text wraps before the leader); the TOC labels were one step behind (the counter was read before the heading stepped it; now read after), and the sample had typed letters into its headings, doubling the labels (removed from the sample).
- p.3–p.7: body, headings A, B, 1, 2 (labels correct), footnotes 1–5 at the foot of their pages, paragraph text double-spaced with first-line indent.
- p.8: signature block, "Dated", "Respectfully submitted", both counsel signature groups, "- and –" separator.

Open findings (Gate 2):

- OPEN (tier 2): no table of authorities. The filed exemplar has one (its pages 2–6 are the TOC and TOA). LBR 7008-1(C) does not require one, but a clerk expects one in a 20-page brief. The ca9 template generates one from `toa:` links; not built here.
- OPEN (tier 2): the signature block falls alone on page 8, with no text of the body on the page. Common in filings, but a clerk may object. The fix is to keep the last paragraph of the conclusion with the signature block; not built here.
- OPEN (tier 2): the cover's second counsel block sits close to the first; the exemplar leaves more room.
- ACCEPTED (tier 3): the exemplar's firm document-ID line in the footer, and its third signature (Scot McChain, local counsel), which the excerpt does not transcribe.

## Known gaps

- EXCERPT. The sample is 8 of the 60 pages of the filing, so the page count of a full brief (up to 20 pages under LBR 7008-1(C)) is not tested. The exemplar itself exceeds the 20-page limit; the template follows the rule, not the exemplar.
- One exemplar, one filer (tier 2 asks for two or three). A second VI bankruptcy motion to dismiss was searched for: the Boyson motion (Adv. docket 6381869, ECF 33, 2018) is listed on CourtListener but its PDF is not available (`is_available: false`). ## Engines

`docx/` (pandoc 3.6.4, `reference.docx` from `docx/build-reference.sh`, cover, table of contents, heading labels and signature in `docx/vib.lua`). Built from the court folder:

```sh
sh motion-brief/docx/build-reference.sh motion-brief/docx/reference.docx
pandoc --from markdown+smart --to docx --reference-doc=motion-brief/docx/reference.docx \
  --lua-filter=motion-brief/docx/vib.lua motion-brief/samples/INPUT-prosser-motion-to-dismiss.md \
  -o motion-brief/samples/OUTPUT-prosser-motion-to-dismiss.docx
```

- **Matches the typst template:** EB Garamond 13 pt; every line at the 19.5 pt pitch measured on the typst render; 1 in margins on US Letter; 0.5 in first-line indent; footnotes at 13 pt; the cover (court header, caption with the Form 416 rules, title, counsel blocks at 2.3 in) on its own page; a table-of-contents page; level 1 headings centered, bold, capitals; levels 2 to 5 hanging at 0.5 in steps with the labels I., A., 1., a. typed by the filter (the author types none); the signature block (Dated, "Respectfully submitted," the counsel groups with "- and –"); footer "Page n of N".
- **Differs (pandoc and Word limits):** the footer carries no short title; the table of contents is a TOC field (see BLOCKED); the caption row padding is approximate; pandoc's metadata title and date are not written into the body and the document title property is empty; pagination can differ in Word.
- **Not checked here:** the docx has not been opened in Word or LibreOffice (LibreOffice is not installed in the build box). The `.docx` is a valid zip (ziptool reads every entry) and every XML part is well formed (xmllint). The text of the sample is in the docx, in order (checked word by word against the markdown body).
- **BLOCKED (TOC page numbers):** the TOC field is empty until Word or LibreOffice updates fields, and the page numbers cannot be computed without a rendered PDF. The typst TOC shows them; the docx TOC does not, until updated. A `.docx.pdf` cannot be made here, so this cannot be checked.
- **BLOCKED (docx.pdf):** this court folder has no checks file (no `checks.yaml`), so no gate check ID is readable. The requirement IDs of `README.md` (VIB-MB-CAPTION, VIB-MB-TITLE, VIB-MB-GROUNDS, VIB-MB-PROPOSED, VIB-MB-LENGTH, VIB-MB-FONT, VIB-MB-PAGE, VIB-MB-SPACING, VIB-MB-HEADINGS, VIB-MB-SIGNATURE) and the TOC page numbers are all measured on the `.docx.pdf`, so all are BLOCKED with the reason: LibreOffice cannot run in the build box, so the .docx.pdf cannot be produced or checked.
- **Finding for the template (tier 2, not changed here):** the typst body pitch is 19.5 pt, about 1.5 times the 13 pt type size, not the double spacing the README calls it. The docx copies the measured typst pitch. The 20-page limit of LBR 7008-1(C) is not measured in either engine.
