# Motion with memorandum of law: United States Bankruptcy Court, D.V.I. (`vib`)

Doc type `motion-brief`, under [`../`](../) (the bankruptcy district court). A motion in an adversary proceeding, with the memorandum of law attached as the brief (LBR 7008-1(C)). Governing rules: FRBP 9013, 7008 (by way of LBR 7008-1), 9004; LBR 5005-1, 7008-1, 9013-1 of the District Court of the Virgin Islands, Bankruptcy Division.

## What is here

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `typst/main.typ` | Entry point. Reads `--input sample=...`, splits frontmatter, calls the template. |
| `typst/vib-motion-brief.typ` | The template: `motion(meta, md)`, on the shared cross-engine layout model (`lib/typst/xengine.typ`). Every number is a named parameter or traces to a rule. |
| `docx/reference.xml`, `docx/vib-motion-brief.lua` | The docx engine: the Word styles (built by `fb render`) and the filter on `lib/docx/xengine.lua`. No shell script. |
| `eval.yaml` | `params.cross_engine: gate`: the docx must break every line and page as typst does. |
| `usage/INPUT-motion-brief.md` | An EXCERPT (8 of 60 pages of the filing, see below) transcribed from the filed omnibus memorandum: cover, preliminary statement, factual background A and B.1–B.2 with footnotes, conclusion, signature. |
| `usage/OUTPUT-motion-brief.typst.pdf` | Our typst render, 9 pages. |
| `usage/OUTPUT-motion-brief.docx`, `.docx.pdf` | The docx render and its LibreOffice PDF, 9 pages. |
| `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-motion-brief.pdf` | The filed exemplar (60 pp., text layer). |
| `usage/SOURCE.md` | Where it came from, filer, case, date filed, URL, date fetched. |

Build and check (from the repo root):

```sh
sh tools/render.sh courts/third-circuit/virgin-islands/federal/bankruptcy-district/motion-brief usage/INPUT-motion-brief.md
sh tools/check.sh courts/third-circuit/virgin-islands/federal/bankruptcy-district/motion-brief
sh tools/check.sh courts/third-circuit/virgin-islands/federal/bankruptcy-district/motion-brief --engine docx
just overlay courts/third-circuit/virgin-islands/federal/bankruptcy-district/motion-brief
```

Rendering uses the vendored fonts only (`--ignore-system-fonts`); any warning fails the render. Both PDFs embed Times New Roman only (regular, bold, italic).

## Frontmatter contract

| Field | Required | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| `division`, `debtor`, `chapter`, `case_number`, `caption`, `adversary` | yes | As in the complaint's contract (see `../adversary-complaint/README.md`). |
| `title` | yes | The cover title, in capitals. |
| `footer` | no | Short title for the footer, in capitals; defaults to `title`. It wraps before the page column (a second line stacks upward, as Word sets a footer). |
| `counsel` | yes | Cover counsel blocks, lower right: `firm` (printed in bold capitals) and `lines` (markdown, so `*...*` is italic). |
| `date` | yes | "Dated:" line. |
| `signature` | yes | Groups: `signer` (printed `/s/ name`) and `lines`. Groups separated by "- and –". |

Markdown body: it opens with a heading (the first page after the contents) and ends with a paragraph without a footnote (kept on one page with the signature block). `#` is a centred bold section heading; `##`, `###`, `####`, `#####` are numbered as above (typed numbers are a doubling error, which the sample once had). Footnotes `[^n]`. Body paragraphs are double-spaced with a first-line indent. Lists and block quotations are not supported (the docx filter stops on them).

## Layout

- Letter paper, 1 in margins, Times New Roman 12 pt. The cross-engine layout model: the body in 24 pt line boxes, single lines (court header, caption, title, counsel, contents, headings, signature, footnotes, footer) in 13.8 pt boxes, every space between blocks explicit.
- Cover, one page: two centred bold court lines; the caption (Form 416): two rows, the party column 3.9 in wide, ruled on its right and below each row (0.75 pt), the text 0.2 in off the rule, 6 pt below a row's top and 10 pt above its rule; designations 0.5 in in from the names; the title in centred bold capitals 24 pt below; the counsel blocks single-spaced from 2.3 in, 24 pt below the title, a blank single line between them.
- Table of contents on its own page: levels 1 to 4, each row single-spaced 12 pt below the one before, the label in a 0.5 in box after the level's step (a level-1 title at the first step), the title hanging, a dot leader to the page number in the last 36 pt of the line (docx: the page numbers from the LibreOffice render, `fb render`'s needles).
- Body from the next page: headings single-spaced, 12 pt above and below, kept with the next block (level 1 centred bold capitals; below it the label in a 0.5 in box after the level's step, the title hanging, levels 2 and 3 bold); paragraphs with a 0.5 in first-line indent; footnotes 12 pt single-spaced, 7 pt apart, under a short rule.
- Signature block, 24 pt below the last paragraph and kept on one page with it: "Dated:" at the margin, "Respectfully submitted," and each counsel group from 3.0 in, a blank single line between the parts and "- and –" between two groups.
- Footer: the short title at the left (wrapping before a 1.3 in page column), "Page n of N" at the right on its last line, the last line ending 0.3 in above the page edge.

## Engines

Both engines are on the shared cross-engine layout model (`lib/typst/xengine.typ`, `lib/docx/xengine.lua`; docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Cross-engine gate"): the docx render breaks every line and every page where the typst render does, and `eval.yaml` gates it (`params.cross_engine: gate`, fb-595.68.35).

- **typst** (primary): the reference.
- **docx**: `docx/reference.xml` (`base="xengine"`, the typst face `{font}` = Times New Roman, 12 pt; Body Text in exact 24 pt line boxes with a 0.5 in first-line indent, single lines and footnotes 13.8 pt, 7 pt after a note, Footnote Text without the recipe's keepLines (a note splits over a page end as in typst); "Body Text Note" without widow control for a paragraph with a footnote mark, "Body Text Keep" for the last paragraph; pandoc reads CommonMark with smart punctuation and footnotes, as cmarker does) and `docx/vib-motion-brief.lua`, which writes the cover, the contents (page numbers from `fb render`'s passes: a needle per heading with the words after it, pages numbered from the cover), the headings with their labels, the signature block, the footer (a borderless two-cell table: the short title, and "Page" PAGE "of" NUMPAGES; `$FORMBOOK_FOOTERS`) and the last section (`$FORMBOOK_FINAL`). Footnotes are Word footnotes. No shell script (fb-595.68).
