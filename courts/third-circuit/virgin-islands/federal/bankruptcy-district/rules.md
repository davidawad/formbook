# District Court of the Virgin Islands, Bankruptcy Division (adversary complaint, motion brief): rules

Alias `vib`. Scope: a complaint commencing an adversary proceeding (`adversary-complaint/`) and a brief or memorandum supporting a motion in an adversary proceeding, typically a motion to dismiss (`motion-brief/`), filed by counsel through the Bankruptcy Division's CM/ECF (`ecf.vib.uscourts.gov`). Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (FRBP amendment date) and whenever the District Court posts new Local Bankruptcy Rules (current set effective February 1, 2026, adopted by order of January 30, 2026 in Misc. No. 3:25-mc-0003; the prior set was effective December 1, 2017).

Access note: on 2026-10-17 `www.vid.uscourts.gov` rejected direct requests from this box; the Local Rules page and the LBR PDF were retrieved through the WebFetch tool from the court's own URLs.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (FRBP, FRCP as applied, LBR). **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **Font floor in Courier terms, footnotes included**: "All documents shall be created in a font size no smaller than 12 Courier or an equivalent size if a different font face is used. Footnotes are subject to the same provision concerning font size." (LBR 5005-1(B)). Unlike the district court's civil rules (10 pt footnotes allowed), bankruptcy footnotes are full size.
2. **Every paragraph of every pleading is numbered** (LBR 5005-1(A)).
3. **Briefs are attachments**: in an adversary proceeding "The moving party may file a brief and any supporting affidavits as an attachment to the motion" (LBR 7008-1(C)); the motion carries the full Form 416D caption and a descriptive title (LBR 7008-1(A)).
4. **Consent statements** are required in the adversary complaint (LBR 7004-2(A), FRBP 7008) and in every motion (LBR 9013-6(A)).
5. **Proposed order with every request for relief, including complaints** (LBR 9013-4(A)).
6. No margin, line-spacing or brief-length rule in the LBR.
