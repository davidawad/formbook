# U.S. District Court, Western District of Pennsylvania (complaint, motion brief): rules

Alias `pawd`. Scope: a civil complaint (`complaint/`) and a memorandum or brief in support of a motion (`motion-brief/`, typically a Rule 12(b) motion to dismiss), filed by counsel through CM/ECF. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (FRCP amendment date), whenever the court posts amended Local Rules (the court's page, capture of 2026-06-18, shows "PAWD Local Rules of Court Effective: November 1, 2016"), and for each case, the assigned judge's posted Practices and Procedures (LCvR 7.A), which set the brief page limits.

Access note: on 2026-10-17 `www.pawd.uscourts.gov` answered this box with a "Request Rejected" firewall page. The court's own files were read from Internet Archive captures of the court's URLs.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (FRCP or W.D. Pa. LCvR). **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **12-point minimum** (LCvR 5.1.B), unlike the Middle District's 14 point; footnotes in the same font and size as the body.
2. **No brief length or section rule in the Local Rules.** LCvR 7.A sends motions to "the orders of the assigned Judge and the practices and procedures of the assigned Judge" posted on the court's site; page limits and any meet-and-confer certificate live there, judge by judge. The template must take a page limit as a parameter.
3. **Proposed order with every motion** (LCvR 7.D), and each motion and each brief is a separate document (LCvR 5.1.F).
4. **Corporate disclosure under the local rule is broader than FRCP 7.1**: any corporation, association, joint venture, partnership, syndicate or similar entity identifies all parent companies, subsidiaries and affiliates that have issued shares or debt securities to the public, on the form in Appendix LCvR 7.1.A (LCvR 7.1.A). A separate filing.
