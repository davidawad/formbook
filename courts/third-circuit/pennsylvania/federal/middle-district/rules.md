# U.S. District Court, Middle District of Pennsylvania (complaint, motion brief): rules

Alias `pamd`. Scope: a civil complaint (`complaint/`) and a brief in support of a motion (`motion-brief/`, typically a Rule 12(b) motion to dismiss), filed by counsel through CM/ECF. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (FRCP amendment date) and whenever the court posts amended Local Rules (the court's page states they were "last amended December 1, 2014").

Access note: on 2026-10-17 `www.pamd.uscourts.gov` answered this box with a "Request Rejected" firewall page. The court's own files were read from Internet Archive captures of the court's URLs (capture URLs below). The court's "Local Rules and Orders" page (capture of 2025-01-06) links "Local Rules PDF" to `/sites/pamd/files/LR120114.pdf` and states "These Local Rules were last amended December 1, 2014."

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (FRCP or M.D. Pa. LR). **C** = convention (from the exemplars; the rules leave it open). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **14-point minimum for every paper** (LR 5.1(c)), not the 12 point common in other districts; footnotes in the same font and size as the body.
2. **Typed area 6 1/2 by 9 1/2 inches** (LR 5.1(a)), i.e. 1 inch side margins and 3/4 inch top and bottom on letter paper, while LR 5.1(c) separately requires margins "at least one inch on all four sides". One inch on all four sides satisfies both (the typed area is then 6 1/2 by 9 inches, inside the LR 5.1(a) box).
3. **Brief length 15 pages, or 5,000 words with a word-count certificate** (LR 7.8(b)); TOC and table of citations required only above 15 pages (LR 7.8(a)).
4. **Concurrence certificate is in the motion** (LR 7.1), and each motion and each brief is a separate document (LR 5.1(h)); a proposed order accompanies each motion (LR 5.1(g), 7.1).
