# U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Western District of Pennsylvania (adversary complaint, motion brief): rules

Alias `pawb`. Scope: a complaint commencing an adversary proceeding (`adversary-complaint/`) and a brief in support of a motion in an adversary proceeding, typically a motion to dismiss (`motion-brief/`), filed by counsel through CM/ECF. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (FRBP amendment date) and whenever the court posts a new Local Bankruptcy Rules edition (the current edition took effect July 1, 2026, W.PA.LBR 1001-3) or a Standing Order amending them (W.PA.LBR 1001-4).

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (FRBP, FRCP as applied, W.PA.LBR). **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **A local font floor stated in Courier terms**: documents are "created in a font size no smaller than 12 Courier or an equivalent font size, including footnotes" (W.PA.LBR 5005-13(b)(4)). No face, spacing or margin rule.
2. **Paragraph numbering is mandatory except in briefs**: documents are "sequentially paragraph numbered except as to briefs" (W.PA.LBR 5005-13(b)(3)), so the complaint numbers every paragraph and the brief does not have to.
3. **PDF must be born-digital, flattened and fully text-searchable** (W.PA.LBR 5005-13(b)(5), (d)).
4. **Brief length 20 pages; briefs are optional** and due at least one week before the hearing on the motion (W.PA.LBR 7008-1(e)).
5. **Judge's initials** are appended to the bankruptcy case number in an adversary caption (W.PA.LBR 7010-1(a)).

## Doc type: adversary-complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAWB-CONT-001 | FRCP 8 applies; the jurisdiction allegation "must include a reference to the name, number, and Code chapter of the case that the adversary proceeding relates to and the district and division where it is pending." | FRBP 7008; FRCP 8(a) | M |
| PAWB-CONT-002 | The complaint "must state whether the pleader does or does not consent to the entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court." | FRBP 7008 | M |
| PAWB-CONT-003 | Numbered paragraphs (also W.PA.LBR 5005-13(b)(3)); separate counts where that promotes clarity. | FRCP 10(b); FRBP 7010 | M |
| PAWB-CONT-004 | Corporate ownership statement by a nongovernmental corporate party other than the debtor, with its first filing. Separate document. | FRBP 7007.1 | M (separate filing) |
| PAWB-CONT-005 | Structure seen in EX-SNYDER: Form 416D caption; numbered paragraphs; counts headed "COUNT I- <claim> PURSUANT TO 11 U.S.C. § ...", each closed by a WHEREFORE paragraph; signature block. One filer: that filer's style. | EX-SNYDER | C (one filer) |

## Doc type: motion-brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAWB-LEN-001 | "Unless otherwise directed by the Court, briefs by the moving party and responding party are optional, are limited to twenty (20) pages, and are due at least one week prior to the hearing date on the motion." Reply and surreply briefs only if authorized, limited to matters not addressed in the initial brief. | W.PA.LBR 7008-1(e) | M |
| PAWB-CONT-010 | Paragraph numbering is not required in a brief. | W.PA.LBR 5005-13(b)(3) | M |
| PAWB-CONT-011 | The motion (not the brief) is in writing with a descriptive title and the full adversary caption, and a proposed order of court is filed as an attachment to it; without a proposed order the Court dismisses the matter without a hearing. | W.PA.LBR 7008-1(a)-(c); W.PA.LBR 9013-1(a); W.PA.LBR 9013-4(a) | M (motion) |
