# U.S. District Court, Eastern District of Pennsylvania (complaint, motion brief): rules

Alias `paed`. Scope: a civil complaint (`complaint/`) and a motion with its supporting brief, typically a Rule 12(b) motion to dismiss (`motion-brief/`), filed by counsel through CM/ECF. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 (FRCP amendment date), whenever the court's "Local Civil Rules" page lists a new edition or amendment (current edition dated 05/08/2023; Rule 40.1 amended 10/08/2024), and for each case, the assigned judge's policies and procedures (L.Civ.R. 7.1(c) directs counsel to them; they set page limits).

Access note: on 2026-10-17 `www.paed.uscourts.gov` rejected direct requests from this box; the rules PDF and the rules page were retrieved through the WebFetch tool from the court's own URLs.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (FRCP or E.D. Pa. Local Civil Rule). **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **No typeface, size, spacing or margin rule in the Local Civil Rules.** Unlike the Middle (14 pt) and Western (12 pt) Districts, E.D. Pa. sets none; documents must be "in the form prescribed by Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 10(a)" (L.Civ.R. 5.1.2(4)(d)). Format choices are C, and judges' policies may add requirements.
2. **Every e-filed document carries a certificate of service** stating that it was filed electronically and is available for viewing and downloading from ECF, and identifying how each party was served (L.Civ.R. 5.1.2(7)(b)). This is stricter than FRCP 5(d)(1)(B).
3. **Every contested motion is accompanied by a brief** with "a concise statement of the legal contentions and authorities", a form of order, and a written statement of the date and manner of service (L.Civ.R. 7.1(a), (c), (d)). Page limits come from the judge's policies, not the Local Rules.
4. **Signature block**: name, address, telephone, email and state bar identification number, with "s/" before the typed name of the ECF filer (L.Civ.R. 5.1.2(8)(a)).

## Doc type: motion-brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAED-LEN-001 | No page or word limit in the Local Civil Rules for motion briefs; "Counsel are directed to consult each judge's policies and procedures." The template takes the limit as a parameter. | L.Civ.R. 7.1(c) | M (by reference to the judge's policies) |
| PAED-CONT-010 | "Every motion not certified as uncontested ... shall be accompanied by a brief containing a concise statement of the legal contentions and authorities relied upon in support of the motion." | L.Civ.R. 7.1(c) | M |
| PAED-CONT-011 | "Every motion shall be accompanied by a form of order which, if approved by the court, would grant the relief sought by the motion." | L.Civ.R. 7.1(a) | M (motion) |
| PAED-CERT-010 | "Every motion not certified as uncontested shall be accompanied by a written statement as to the date and manner of service of the motion and supporting brief." | L.Civ.R. 7.1(d); L.Civ.R. 5.1.2(7)(b) | M |
| PAED-CONT-012 | An uncontested motion carries a certificate of counsel that it is uncontested. | L.Civ.R. 7.1(b) | M (conditional) |
| PAED-CONT-013 | Structure seen in both filers: the motion and brief are one ECF filing set (EX-HARVIE: motion, proposed order, certificate of service and brief in one PDF; EX-SALAAM: brief as attachment 26-1 to the notice of motion); caption title naming the motion; table of contents and table of authorities; capitalized section headings (INTRODUCTION, LEGAL STANDARD(S), ARGUMENT with roman-numbered points, CONCLUSION); "/s/" signature with bar number. | EX-HARVIE, EX-SALAAM | C |
