Alias `cjdpa`. Scope: motions filed in the Court of Judicial Discipline (the `motion-brief` doc type). Verified against 207 Pa. Code ch. 4001 (the Court's procedural rules, General Provisions) as published on the Pennsylvania Code site, on 2026-10-21.
