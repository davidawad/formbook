# Supreme Court of Pennsylvania: briefs, applications, petitions and amicus briefs

Alias `pa`. Scope: filings in the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania under the Pennsylvania Rules of Appellate Procedure (Pa.R.A.P.): briefs on the merits, answering and reply briefs, applications for relief, petitions for review (original jurisdiction), petitions for allowance of appeal, petitions for King's Bench relief, applications for reargument, and amicus briefs. Verified against the official Pennsylvania Code text of Pa.R.A.P. (Title 210) on pacodeandbulletin.gov, which shows "changes effective through 56 Pa.B. 5370 (August 8, 2026)", on 2026-10-21. Pa.R.A.P. 124 and 2135 were last amended effective 2022-04-01 and 2025-01-01 respectively, as shown in each rule's Source note.

|---|---|---|---|
| PA-CONT-001 | Brief of the appellant (and the brief on the merits in an allowed appeal) appears in this order: statement of jurisdiction; order or determination in question (text verbatim immediately after the jurisdiction statement); statement of scope and standard of review; statement of questions involved, each followed by an answer; statement of the case; summary of argument; argument; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; appended opinions and pleadings; statement of the reasons to allow an appeal to challenge the discretionary aspects of a sentence (if applicable); certificates of compliance. | Pa.R.A.P. 2111(a)(1)-(12), 2115(a), 2116(a). | M |

| PA-CONT-004 | Reply brief answers only matters raised by the appellee's brief (or amicus brief) not previously addressed in the appellant's brief; no further brief without leave of court. Includes the Rule 127 and 2135(d) certificates. | Pa.R.A.P. 2113(a), (c). | M |
| PA-CONT-005 | In a cross-appeal the designated appellant and appellee file the briefs in the sequence of Pa.R.A.P. 2136(b); in multiple-party cases parties may join one brief. | Pa.R.A.P. 2136(a)-(b), 2137. | M |
| PA-CONT-006 | Copies of opinions below are appended to the brief; reported opinions give their citation. | Pa.R.A.P. 2111(b). | M |
| PA-CONT-007 | Parties are referred to by the designations used below, by their names, or by descriptive terms, to keep references to "appellant" and "appellee" to a minimum. | Pa.R.A.P. 2131. | A |
| PA-CONT-008 | Heading and sub-heading numbering: the template numbers the argument headings; authors do not type the numbers. | Template-only. | T |
| PA-LEN-001 | Principal brief not more than 14,000 words; reply brief not more than 7,000 words (cross-appeal designated appellee/appellant briefs under 2136(b)(2)-(3): 16,500 words; capital direct appeal principal 17,500 and reply 8,500). | PA-LEN-002 | A certificate of compliance with the word count limit is required when a principal brief is longer than 30 pages or a reply brief is longer than 15 pages when prepared on a word processor. The template always includes it. | Pa.R.A.P. 2135(a)(1), (d). | M |
| PA-CERT-002 | Certificate of compliance with the word count limit (2135(d)) sits with the Rule 127 certificate at the end of the brief. | Pa.R.A.P. 2135(d); 127(a). | M |
