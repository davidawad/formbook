# Commonwealth Court of Pennsylvania: briefs, applications, petitions for review and original-jurisdiction petitions

Alias `pacommwct`. Scope: principal and reply briefs, applications and motions, petitions for review (appellate jurisdiction), petitions and complaints for writ (original jurisdiction), and amicus briefs. Verified against the Pennsylvania Rules of Appellate Procedure as they appear in the Pennsylvania Code (210 Pa. Code), text current through 56 Pa.B. 5370 (August 8, 2026), on 2026-10-21. No Commonwealth Court internal operating procedure or local rule was retrieved; see the last line of Sources.

## Briefs (opening, answering, reply) and amicus briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PACOMMWCT-FORM-001 | Paper is 8 1/2 by 11 inch white, lettering on one side of a page (briefs may be lettered on both sides). Originals are unbound; copies are firmly bound; no backers. | PA-RAP 124(a)(1), (4), (5), (6); PA-RAP 124(a)(4) (briefs). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-FORM-002 | Text is double spaced; block quotations longer than two lines may be indented and single spaced; footnotes may be single spaced. Margins are at least one inch on all four sides. | PA-RAP 124(a)(3). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-FORM-003 | Lettering is clear and legible and no smaller than 14 point in the text; footnotes are at least 12 point. The Official Note says the Commonwealth Court's own font, Times New Roman, is preferred for its briefs; the template face is Times New Roman (Liberation Serif, its metric twin, as fallback). | PA-RAP 124(a)(4) and Official Note. | M |
| PACOMMWCT-FORM-004 | The first page carries a three-inch clear space from the top of the document for court stampings and filing notices. | PA-RAP 124(a)(2). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-001 | Brief order: statement of jurisdiction; order or other determination in question; statement of scope and standard of review; statement of the questions involved; statement of the case; summary of argument; (discretionary-sentence statement if applicable); argument; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; appended opinions and pleadings; certificates. | PA-RAP 2111(a), (b). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-002 | The statement of jurisdiction gives a precise citation to the statute or general rule conferring jurisdiction. | PA-RAP 2114. | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-003 | The summary of argument is a concise but accurate summary of the arguments in support of the issues in the statement of questions. | PA-RAP 2118. | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-004 | The brief appends a copy of any opinion delivered below relating to the order under review, and states the reporter citation if the opinion is reported. | PA-RAP 2111(b). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-005 | Where parties join in one brief, or adopt one another's parts by reference, the brief says so. | PA-RAP 2137. | M |
| PACOMMWCT-LEN-001 | Principal brief not to exceed 14,000 words; reply brief not to exceed 7,000 words, unless the court orders otherwise. Supplementary matter (cover, tables, proof of service, addenda) is excluded from the count. | PA-RAP 2135(a)(1), (b). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-LEN-002 | A certificate of compliance with the word limit is required if the principal brief is longer than 30 pages or the reply brief is longer than 15 pages when prepared on a word processor. | PA-RAP 2135(a)(1), (d). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-LEN-003 | Amicus briefs filed during merits briefing are limited to 7,000 words; amicus briefs filed in support of or against a petition for allowance of appeal are limited to 4,500 words; amicus briefs filed by leave of court are limited to the length the court specifies or half the length a party may file. | PA-RAP 531(b)(3). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CERT-001 | Each brief carries the certificate of compliance with the Case Records Public Access Policy of the Unified Judicial System of Pennsylvania, in substantially this form: "I certify that this filing complies with the provisions of the Case Records Public Access Policy of the Unified Judicial System of Pennsylvania that require filing confidential information and documents differently than non-confidential information and documents." | PA-RAP 127(a) and Comment (Sections 7.0(D) and 8.0(D) of the Public Access Policy); PA-RAP 2111(a)(12). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CERT-002 | An amicus brief states the amicus's interest and discloses any person other than the amicus, its members, or counsel that paid for or wrote the brief in whole or part; it contains the Rule 127 certificate. It needs no statement of the case and no jurisdiction statement. | PA-RAP 531(b)(2). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CERT-003 | The brief signature block carries counsel's name, Pennsylvania attorney number, address, telephone and e-mail (exemplar convention; the rules do not fix the layout). | Exemplars (see usage/SOURCE.md). | C |
| PACOMMWCT-ELEC-001 | A confidential information or document is protected under the Public Access Policy; confidential information is filed on the Confidential Information Form where the Policy requires it. Sealed material remains sealed on appeal unless the court orders otherwise. | PA-RAP 127(a), (b). | M |

## Petitions for review (appellate jurisdiction)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-008 | The petition is divided into consecutively numbered paragraphs, each containing as nearly as possible a single allegation of fact or other statement. | PA-RAP 1513(c). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-009 | The petition states: the basis for jurisdiction; the petitioner; the government unit whose order is reviewed; the order and its date; a general statement of objections; a short statement of relief; and a copy of the order attached as an exhibit. | PA-RAP 1513(d)(1)-(7). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-010 | The caption names the aggrieved party as petitioner and the government unit as respondent (or identifies a disinterested government unit in parentheses). No notice to plead or verification is required. | PA-RAP 1513(a), (d). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CONT-011 | If other parties to the agency proceeding are not named in the caption, the petition gives the notice to participate. | PA-RAP 1513(d). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-CERT-005 | The petition for review carries the certificate of compliance required by PA-RAP 127. | PA-RAP 1513(d)(8). | M |
| PACOMMWCT-ELEC-002 | The petition is served on the government unit that made the determination and on the Attorney General (Strawberry Square, Harrisburg, PA 17120), with proof of service filed with the prothonotary. | PA-RAP 1514(a), (c) and Note. | M |
