# New Jersey Tax Court: complaints and motions

Alias `njtaxct`. Scope: papers filed in the New Jersey Tax Court under Part VIII of the Rules of Court (R. 8:1 et seq.): complaints and counterclaims (R. 8:3), motions and orders to show cause (R. 8:7). The Tax Court has no appellate briefs; the court has no filed exemplars in usage/ (see the court README). Verified against the Rules of Court, Part VIII (copy at courtcaddy.com, fetched HTTP 200) on 2026-10-21. That copy's notes end with amendments of 1994 (R. 8:7) and later; it is not the official njcourts.gov text, and the currency of each rule must be confirmed there.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.njcourts.gov/courts/tax (HTTP 403, 2026-10-21); https://www.njcourts.gov/attorneys/court-rules (HTTP 403, 2026-10-21). The form-of-papers rule for Tax Court filings (R. 1:4-9, which governs papers filed in the trial courts, and whatever Part VIII or the Tax Court's own filing notice adds) was not retrieved; no typeface, size, spacing or margin requirement is set here for the Tax Court from a rule we read. OPEN (for David): confirm whether R. 1:4-9 and the Tax Court's e-filing directive set the trial-court form, and whether the 14-point Times New Roman used for the Supreme Court templates applies here.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NJTAX-CONT-001 | Part VIII governs practice in all actions in the Tax Court. | R8 R. 8:1 | M |
| NJTAX-CONT-002 | A complaint or counterclaim has a Case Information Statement, in the form the Tax Court prescribes, attached to its face. | R8 R. 8:3 | M |
| NJTAX-CONT-003 | A complaint to review a tax determination attaches a copy of the judgment, memorandum of judgment, order or determination reviewed, as R. 8:3 provides for each class of case (county board judgments; Division of Taxation or other state agency actions; county recording officer actions). | R8 R. 8:3 | M |
| NJTAX-CONT-004 | A complaint that omits the Case Information Statement is a nonconforming paper, returned stamped "Received but not filed (date)". | R8 R. 8:3 | M |
| NJTAX-CONT-005 | A complaint seeking inclusion or exclusion of a sale in an equalization analysis identifies the sale or sales, any Form SR1A identification number, and the reasons alleged. | R8 R. 8:3 | M |
