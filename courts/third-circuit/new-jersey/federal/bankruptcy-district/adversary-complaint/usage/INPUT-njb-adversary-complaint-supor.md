# JURISDICTION AND VENUE

On April 2, 2024, (the “Petition Date”) Plaintiffs and affiliates Supor Properties Breiderhoft LLC; Supor Properties Devon LLC; Shore Properties Associates North LLC; JS Realty Properties, LLC; and Supor Properties Harrison Avenue LLC (each along with Plaintiffs a “Debtor” and collectively with Plaintiffs, the “Debtors”) filed voluntary chapter 11 bankruptcy petitions with the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of New Jersey (the “Bankruptcy Cases”). On April 8, 2024, this Court entered an Order providing for the joint administration of the Bankruptcy Cases [ECF No. 35].

This Court has jurisdiction over this adversary proceeding pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1334(b) and 28 U.S.C. § 157(b)(2) and (c), the Debtors’ confirmed Plan and the Confirmation Order (defined, infra), all of which specifically reserved and retained jurisdiction of this Court as to the issues raised in this adversary proceeding. This is a “core proceeding” pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 157(b)(2)(o).

Article XV of the Plan provides that “[a]fter Confirmation of the Plan, the Bankruptcy Court shall retain jurisdiction to the fullest extent legally permissible for all purposes consistent with the Plan and the Code,” including the “determination of all causes of action, controversies, disputes, or conflicts... between the Reorganized Debtors and any other Person, including, but not limited to, any right of the Reorganized Debtors to recover assets.”

This adversary proceeding arises directly from the Everest Capital Loan Commitments (defined, infra), which were approved by this Court and the subject of multiple orders of this Court, including the Order Modifying Order Compelling Debtors’ Performance entered on May 22, 2026 [ECF No. 525] and the Order entered June 12, 2026 [ECF No. 532], each of which incorporated and addressed the Everest Capital Loan Commitments as integral to the Debtors’ Exit Transactions under the confirmed Plan.

Venue is proper in this district pursuant to 28 U.S.C. §§ 1408 and 1409. This adversary proceeding is commenced pursuant to Rules 7001(1), (7) and (9), 7064 and 7065 of the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure and 11 U.S.C. § 105(a).
