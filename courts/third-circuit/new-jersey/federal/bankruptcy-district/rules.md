# U.S. Bankruptcy Court, District of New Jersey (adversary complaint, motion brief): rules

Alias `njb`. Scope: a complaint commencing an adversary proceeding (`adversary-complaint/`) and a memorandum of law / brief supporting a motion, typically a motion to dismiss an adversary complaint (`motion-brief/`), filed by counsel through CM/ECF. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each August 1 (the court's current rules are "Dated: August 1, 2026") and December 1 (FRBP amendment date), and whenever the court posts a new "Local Rules Package"; also the presiding judge's "judicial preferences" (D.N.J. LBR 1001-1, 2025 Comment).

Access note: on 2026-10-17 `www.njb.uscourts.gov` rejected direct requests from this box; the rules PDF was retrieved through the WebFetch tool from the court's own URL and read with `pdftotext`.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (FRBP, FRCP as applied, D.N.J. LBR). **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **Counsel block in the top left corner of page 1** is mandatory on *all* documents: attorney's name, address, telephone and direct e-mail, and the party represented (D.N.J. LBR 9004-1(a)). Filers label it "Caption in Compliance with D.N.J. LBR 9004-1(b)" (EX-SUPOR, EX-ORCA, EX-CUSHING).
2. **Caption shows the chapter, the judge's initials and the hearing date** (D.N.J. LBR 9004-1(b)).
3. **A motion is a set of separate documents**: notice of motion with date, time and place of hearing; certification(s) of facts; memorandum of law "as necessary"; proposed order; Local Form Certification of Service (D.N.J. LBR 9013-1(a)), filed under a single docket entry with the notice as main document (LBR 5005-1(b)(2) and 2015 Comment). The 2025 amendment abolished the "Statement Why No Brief is Necessary".
4. **No typeface, size, spacing, margin or brief-length rule** in the LBR; and "A Local Civil Rule not incorporated into these Rules does not apply" (LBR 1001-1, 2015 Comment), so the district court's L.Civ.R. 7.2 font and page rules do not bind briefs here.

## Requirements: all papers

### Page form

| NJB-FORM-002 | Letter pages, Times/Times New Roman, double-spaced body, 1 inch margins: all four exemplars. Size varies: 12 pt (line pitch about 27-28 pt) in EX-ORCA and EX-GOLDBERG; about 14 pt in EX-SUPOR. | exemplars | C |
| NJB-FORM-003 | The bottom margin carries no text other than the page number: bankruptcy CM/ECF stamps "Case ... Doc ... Filed ... Entered ... Desc Main Document Page x of y" at the foot of each page. | exemplars | C |
| NJB-FORM-004 | Every font embedded and of the template's declared family. | project | T |

## Doc type: motion-brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NJB-LEN-001 | No page or word limit for a memorandum of law in the LBR (a mediation statement is the only document with a page limit, "should not exceed 15 pages", LBR 9019-2(h)). | LBR (no rule found) | M (none) |
| NJB-CONT-010 | A motion consists of separate documents: (1) notice of motion stating the date, time and place of the hearing; (2) certification(s) of facts on personal knowledge (LBR 7007-1); (3) "a memorandum of law stating the legal basis for the relief requested, as necessary"; (4) a proposed order; (5) Local Form Certification of Service. | D.N.J. LBR 9013-1(a) | M |
| NJB-CONT-011 | Facts supporting a motion go in a sworn statement under 28 U.S.C. § 1746, limited to personal knowledge, "may not be combined with any legal argument". | D.N.J. LBR 7007-1 | M |
| NJB-CONT-012 | The motion is filed and served under FRBP 7004 at least 21 days before the hearing date; opposition or cross-motion 7 days before; reply 4 days before. The movant schedules the hearing date. | D.N.J. LBR 9013-2(a)-(b) | M |
| NJB-CERT-001 | Certification of service on the mandatory Local Form. | D.N.J. LBR 9013-1(a)(5); LBR 9009-1 | M |
| NJB-CONT-013 | Structure seen in EX-CUSHING: notice of motion (pp. 1-2) and the combined brief in one PDF; LBR 9004 counsel block; "PRELIMINARY STATEMENT", roman-numbered argument points, "CONCLUSION"; certification of service. One filer: that filer's style. | EX-CUSHING | C (one filer) |
