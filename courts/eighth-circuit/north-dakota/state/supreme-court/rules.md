# North Dakota Supreme Court: briefs, motions, petitions and amicus briefs

Alias `nd`. Scope: the filings a party, movant or amicus files in the Supreme Court of North Dakota: brief on the merits (appellant's principal brief), answering (appellee's) brief, reply brief, motion, petition for supervisory writ, petition for rehearing and amicus curiae brief. Verified against the North Dakota Rules of Appellate Procedure as published on the court's site (Rule 21 effective 9/1/2026; Rule 27 effective 3/1/2022; Rule 28 effective 9/1/2026; Rule 29 effective 7/1/2026; Rule 32 effective 4/1/2024; Rule 40 effective 7/1/2026) on 2026-10-21. The Court of Appeals sheet is `ndctapp`.

The site www.ndcourts.gov returned HTTP 403 to direct `curl` and to WebFetch on 2026-10-21; every rule text below was read through the r.jina.ai reader (HTTP 200) of the same ndcourts.gov URL. Rules 14, 25, 30 and 31 (identity protection, filing and service, record references, filing of briefs) were not retrieved and are not cited.

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ND-AMI-001 | An amicus brief is filed only with leave of court or at its request, limited to issues raised by the parties; a motion for leave may be accompanied by the proposed brief and states the movant's interest and why the brief is desirable and relevant. | NDRAP29(a)(2)-(3) | M |
| ND-AMI-002 | The brief complies with Rules 25 and 32; the cover identifies the party supported, if any, and whether the brief supports affirmance or reversal. | NDRAP29(a)(4) | M |
| ND-AMI-003 | Contents (Rule 28 need not be followed): table of contents with paragraph references; table of authorities with paragraph references; identity and interest of the amicus; statement whether a party's counsel authored the brief, whether a party or counsel funded it, and whether any other person did; argument. | NDRAP29(a)(4)(A)-(D) | M |
