## Requirements

### Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NDB-FORM-001 | Caption identifies the bankruptcy court, case and adversary numbers, and parties, under the federal pleading caption rules. | FRBP 7008; FRCP 10(a) | M |
| NDB-CONT-001 | Complaint states claims and relief under FRCP 8 and uses numbered paragraphs for separate allegations. | FRBP 7008; FRCP 8(a), 10(b) | M |
| NDB-CERT-001 | A nongovernmental corporation requesting relief files a corporate ownership statement with its first pleading or request for relief. | D.N.D. Bankr. L.R. 9013-2; FRBP 7007.1 | M |

### Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NDB-CONT-002 | A motion states its grounds with particularity and the relief sought. | FRBP 9013 | M |
| NDB-CONT-003 | A motion in a bankruptcy case subject to Rule 9013 receives a 14-day response period, unless a different federal rule controls. Failure to respond may be deemed an admission. | D.N.D. Bankr. L.R. 9013-1(A)-(B) | M |
| NDB-CONT-004 | A dispositive adversary motion is filed with its supporting memorandum; that memorandum is at most 40 pages, of which at most 25 pages are argument. | D.N.D. Bankr. L.R. 7007-1(A) | M |
| NDB-CONT-005 | A non-dispositive adversary motion is filed with a supporting memorandum no longer than 20 pages. | D.N.D. Bankr. L.R. 7007-1(B) | M |
| NDB-LEN-001 | Adversary motion responses and replies follow the applicable local page limits: dispositive response 40 pages/reply 10; non-dispositive response 20 pages/reply 7. | D.N.D. Bankr. L.R. 7007-1(A)-(B) | M |
| NDB-ELEC-001 | Attorneys, the U.S. Trustee, and case trustees file through CM/ECF; the electronic case file is the official record. | D.N.D. Bankr. L.R. 5005-1 | M |

## Document-type differences

An adversary complaint follows FRBP 7008 and FRCP pleading rules. An adversary motion uses the separate memorandum requirements and page caps in D.N.D. Bankr. L.R. 7007-1. For motions in the main bankruptcy case, local rule 9013-1 supplies a response period but the adversary page caps do not apply.
