# Eighth Circuit Bankruptcy Appellate Panel briefs: rules

Alias `bap8`. Scope: opening, answering, and reply briefs before the United States Bankruptcy Appellate Panel for the Eighth Circuit. Verified on 2026-10-17 against the official BAP local-rules publication and the current Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure publication. Re-check the Panel's rules page and national rule publication before every template refresh and at least annually; re-check after a rules amendment or standing order.

## Primary sources

| Tag | Source | URL | Version checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP-LR | Local Rules of the United States Bankruptcy Appellate Panel for the Eighth Circuit | https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/newbap/rules/localrules_bapDec2023.pdf | December 2023; linked from https://www.ca8.uscourts.gov/panel-rules-and-publications |
| FRBP | Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure | https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-bankruptcy-procedure | Current rules page checked 2026-10-17; relevant Rules 8014-8016, 8018, 8019, 8022, 8026 |
| FRAP | Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure | https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-appellate-procedure | Current publication checked 2026-10-17; only where FRBP incorporates it |
| EXEMPLARS | Public briefs in each `usage/` directory under the three brief folders | Local files; provenance, source URLs, and SHA-256 are in each folder's `usage/SOURCE.md` | Retrieved dates are recorded per file; visual convention only |

Kinds: **M** = required by rule; **C** = convention; **T** = repository contract; **A** = filer responsibility. The national bankruptcy appellate rules govern generally, supplemented by the BAP's local rules.

## Common form and length

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP8-FORM-001 | Paper is 8 1/2 by 11 inches. | FRBP 8015(a)(1), https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-bankruptcy-procedure, Rule 8015(a)(1), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-FORM-002 | Margins are at least one inch on all sides. | FRBP 8015(a)(2), same official source, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-FORM-003 | Body text is double-spaced; block quotations over two lines and footnotes may be single-spaced. | FRBP 8015(a)(4), same official source, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-FORM-004 | Proportionally spaced text uses a face with serifs, 14 point or larger; headings and captions may use sans-serif. Monospaced type may instead be no larger than 10 1/2 characters per inch. | FRBP 8015(a)(5)(A), same official source, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-FORM-005 | Type is plain roman; italics or boldface may be used for emphasis; case names are italicized or underlined. | FRBP 8015(a)(5)(B), same official source, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-FORM-006 | Cover states the case number, court, case title, proceeding and court below, brief title identifying the filer, and counsel's name, office address, telephone number, and email address. | FRBP 8015(b)(1)-(6), same official source, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-FORM-007 | Each brief is signed by counsel or an unrepresented party. | FRBP 8015(a)(10), same official source, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-LEN-001 | Principal briefs are limited to 13,000 words or 1,300 lines; reply briefs to 6,500 words or 650 lines. | FRBP 8015(a)(7)(A)-(B), Rule 8015(a)(7), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-LEN-002 | Excluded type-volume includes the cover, corporate disclosure, contents, table of authorities, statement on oral argument, addendum, certificates, signature block, and proof of service. | FRBP 8015(f), Rule 8015(f), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-CERT-001 | A principal or reply brief subject to a type-volume limit includes the compliance certificate required by FRBP 8015(h), stating its type-volume and the selected limit. | FRBP 8015(h), Rule 8015(h), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-CERT-002 | Every brief has a certificate of service stating date, method, and address served; CM/ECF serves participants, and a paper copy is served on a nonparticipant. | BAP 8th Cir. L.R. 8014A(c), official BAP-LR, Rule 8014A(c), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-CONT-001 | Each brief cites the record by hyperlink to the docket entry, page, and line, or by docket number, page, and line. | BAP 8th Cir. L.R. 8014A(a), official BAP-LR, Rule 8014A(a), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-CONT-002 | A brief (other than an amicus brief) includes a Certification of Interested Parties and a Certification of Related Cases. | BAP 8th Cir. L.R. 8014A(b)(1)-(2), official BAP-LR, Rule 8014A(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| BAP8-COVER-001 | FRBP 8015 does not impose FRAP paper-cover colors; the BAP local rules do not add a color requirement. | FRBP 8015(a)(8), official source; official BAP-LR, verified 2026-10-17 | M (no color requirement) |
