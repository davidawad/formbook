# Nebraska Court of Appeals: briefs and amicus briefs

Alias `nebctapp`. Scope: the briefs filed in the Court of Appeals: appellant's (opening) brief, appellee's (answering) brief, appellant's reply brief, and brief of amicus curiae. Verified against the Nebraska Court Rules of Appellate Practice as published on the Nebraska Judicial Branch site (§ 2-103 amended November 17, 2021, effective January 1, 2022; § 2-109 amended through June 18, 2025) on 2026-10-21. By § 2-102(A) the Rules of Appellate Practice apply to the Court of Appeals unless otherwise specified; the Supreme Court sheet is `neb`.

## Appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-001 | Cover shows the appellate court case number; the case caption listing the plaintiff first (regardless of who is appellant); the county from which the case was brought; the name of the trial judge; the name, address, city, state, zip code, telephone number, email address and Nebraska attorney identification number of the attorney filing the brief (the firm name may also appear); and the name of the party for whom the brief is filed. | NEB-2-103 § 2-103(C)(1) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-002 | The cover is the title page; no additional title page is contained within the brief. | NEB-2-103 § 2-103(C)(1); NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(a) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-003 | Appellant's brief contains, under appropriate headings and in this order: the cover; a table of contents with page references and an alphabetical table of cases, statutes and other authorities cited with page references; the statement of the basis of jurisdiction; the statement of the case; the assignments of error; the propositions of law; the statement of facts; the summary of the argument; and the argument. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(a)-(i) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-004 | Jurisdictional statement identifies the statute, rule or case law conferring jurisdiction and why the order is appealable, and gives the date of entry of the judgment, any tolling motion and its disposition, the date of the notice of appeal, the docket fee or in forma pauperis date, and for an interlocutory appeal the order language or authority. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(c) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-005 | Statement of the case states, in order: the kind of action or nature of the case; the issues actually tried below; how the issues were decided and the judgment entered; and the scope of the appellate court's review. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(d) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-006 | Each assignment of error is a separate, concise statement, separately numbered and paragraphed. Consideration is limited to errors assigned and discussed in the brief. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(e) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-007 | Propositions of law are in separate numbered paragraphs, stated concisely without argument, each followed by a list of supporting authorities; only propositions discussed in the argument are stated. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(f) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-008 | Statement of facts is in narrative form, and every recitation of fact anywhere in the brief is annotated to the record. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(g) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-009 | Appellant's brief contains a summary of the argument that states the arguments made and does not merely repeat the argument headings. An appellee's brief may contain one. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(h) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-010 | The argument presents each question separately and each proposition of law; authorities relied upon are quoted or otherwise discussed. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(1)(i) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-011 | Appellee's brief contains, in order: table of contents and table of cases cited; jurisdictional statement if the appellant's is not accepted; statement of the case if the appellant's is not accepted; propositions of law; statement of facts if not accepted or amplified; argument. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(2) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-012 | A brief that also presents a cross-appeal notes it on the cover and sets it in a separate section headed "Brief on Cross-Appeal", prepared as an appellant's brief. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(4) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-013 | Reply brief is prepared in the same manner as the appellee's brief; an answer to a cross-appeal is a separate division headed "Answer to Brief on Cross-Appeal" and noted on the cover. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(D)(5) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-014 | Record references: transcript as (T26), supplemental or confidential transcript as (Supp. T) or (Conf. T); bill of exceptions as page and line (156:12); exhibits as (E5, p. 95). | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(C)(1)-(3) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CONT-015 | Every reported case is cited with its title, volume and page, tribunal and year, and a pinpoint page if the opinion is long; Nebraska cases are cited to the Nebraska Reports and/or Nebraska Appellate Reports. Statutes are cited from the last published compilation or supplement. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(C)(4)-(5) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-LEN-001 | Original submission: no more than 15,000 words. For the appellant this is the combined total of appellant's brief, reply brief and answer to the cross-appeal; for the appellee, of the appellee's brief, brief on cross-appeal and reply on the cross-appeal. If the appellee asserts a cross-appeal the limit is 18,000 words. | NEB-2-103 § 2-103(C)(3)(a) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-LEN-002 | All portions of the brief count toward the word limit, including the cover page, table of contents, table of authorities and signature blocks. The certificate does not count. | NEB-2-103 § 2-103(C)(3)(c), (C)(4) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CERT-001 | The final page of every brief carries a certificate that the brief complies with the word limit. It states the name and version of the word-processing software used, that the brief complies with the typeface requirements, and the total number of words. The software's word count may be relied on. | NEB-2-103 § 2-103(C)(4) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-CERT-002 | Proof of service is filed with the brief on or before the due date; service is as provided in Neb. Ct. R. Pldg. §§ 6-1105(b) and 6-1106(c) and Neb. Ct. R. § 2-205. | NEB-2-103 § 2-103(B)(2), (C)(6) | M |
| NEBCTAPP-TIME-001 | Appellant's brief is due 30 days after the transcript is filed (or after the bill of exceptions is due); appellee's brief 30 days after the appellant's brief; reply brief 10 days after the appellee's brief. | NEB-2-109 § 2-109(A)(1)-(3) | A |
