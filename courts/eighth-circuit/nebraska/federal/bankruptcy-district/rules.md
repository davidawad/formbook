## Requirements

### Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEBR-FORM-001 | Complaint caption follows Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7008 and Fed. R. Civ. P. 10(a), stating the bankruptcy court, title, case number, adversary number, and party names. | FRBP 7008; FRCP 10(a); Neb. R. Bankr. P. 7001-1 | M |
| NEBR-CONT-001 | Claims appear in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances; state claims and requested relief. | FRCP 10(b), 8(a); FRBP 7008 | M |
| NEBR-CERT-001 | A nongovernmental corporation requesting relief files a Rule 7007.1 ownership statement with its first pleading, motion, response, or other request. | Neb. R. Bankr. P. 9013-1; FRBP 7007.1 | M |

### Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEBR-CONT-002 | A motion in a contested matter states the requested relief and grounds with particularity. | FRBP 9013 | M |
| NEBR-CONT-003 | A motion is served with a notice stating a specific resistance deadline, at least 21 days after filing unless a federal rule provides otherwise. | Neb. R. Bankr. P. 9013-1(A)-(B) | M |
| NEBR-CONT-004 | An adversary motion follows the adversary-motion service and response requirements; dispositive and nondispositive motions are handled under the applicable local rule and scheduling order. | Neb. R. Bankr. P. 7007-1; FRBP 7007 | M |
| NEBR-ELEC-001 | Electronic filings and service comply with CM/ECF procedures and applicable federal/local rules. | Neb. R. Bankr. P. 5005-1; FRBP 5005 | M |

## Document-type differences

Adversary complaints invoke FRBP 7008/FRCP 10 pleading content. Motion briefs support distinct motions and must be accompanied by the notice and deadline required by Neb. R. Bankr. P. 9013-1; motion-type-specific rules or orders may add requirements. No length or typographic limit unique to these types was located in the cited rules.
