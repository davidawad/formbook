Kinds: **M** = mandated; **C** = convention; **A** = author responsibility. Court civil rules do not prescribe a font, type size, line spacing, or general electronic-document margins for the covered filings. IDs are stable.

## Requirements

### Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NED-FORM-001 | Pleading caption includes the court name, title, file number, and a designation under Fed. R. Civ. P. 7(a); complaint title names all parties. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| NED-CONT-001 | Claims are stated in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| NED-CONT-002 | Caption shows the paper's contents and the party filing it; a jury demand is identified in the caption. | NECivR 10.1(a)(3) | M |
| NED-FORM-002 | A paper filing uses 8½ × 11 inch white paper and leaves a 2-inch top margin on the first page. This paper-copy rule does not set the ECF PDF's layout. | NECivR 10.1(b)(1) | M |
| NED-CERT-001 | An attorney signature includes `s/` and the attorney name, bar number, attorney-for designation, firm, address, telephone, fax, and email. | NECivR 11.1(a)(1)(A) | M |

### Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NED-CONT-003 | A motion raising a substantial issue of law is accompanied by a separate supporting brief stating the reasons and authorities; facts in the brief must be supported as NECivR 7.1(a)(2) requires. | NECivR 7.1(a)(1)(A), (a)(2) | M |
| NED-LEN-001 | A supporting brief is limited to 13,000 words and includes the required certificate of compliance. | NECivR 7.1(d)(1)(A), (d)(3) | M |
| NED-CERT-002 | Attorney signature and contact block follow NED-CERT-001. | NECivR 11.1(a)(1)(A) | M |
