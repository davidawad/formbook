# Missouri Court of Appeals: briefs and writ petitions

Alias `moctapp`. Scope: the briefs filed in the Missouri Court of Appeals (the Western, Eastern and Southern Districts are governed by the same Supreme Court rules; each district has its own folder and rules.md): the appellant's brief, the respondent's brief, the appellant's reply brief, the amicus brief, and a petition for writ. Verified against Missouri Supreme Court Rules 84.03, 84.05 and 84.06 (text as published on the rulesofcivilprocedure.com Missouri pages, rule history on Rule 84.03 showing amendment Mar. 7, 2022, eff. Jan. 1, 2024) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=127234 (the Supreme Court's rules page) returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21; the Court of Appeals local rules and the writ-petition rules (Rules 84.22-84.24) were not read. Rows below cite only the Supreme Court rules that apply to briefs in every appellate court, and each row says so.

## Form (all papers)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOCTAPP-FORM-001 | Documents are legible, formatted for pages 8 1/2 x 11 inches, and every page, including the cover, is consecutively paginated with Arabic numerals at the bottom. | MO-R84-03 (b)(1) | M |
| MOCTAPP-FORM-002 | Margins on the left, right, top and bottom are not less than one inch. Page numbers may appear in the bottom margin; no other text may appear in the margins. | MO-R84-03 (b)(2) | M |
| MOCTAPP-FORM-003 | Line spacing is not less than 1.5. The caption, headings, quotations more than two lines long, footnotes, certificates required by Rule 84.06(c), and the signature block may be single-spaced. | MO-R84-03 (b)(2) | M |
| MOCTAPP-FORM-004 | The font, including footnotes, is not smaller than 13 point, Times New Roman. | MO-R84-03 (b)(3); MO-R84-06 (a) | M |
| MOCTAPP-FORM-005 | Applications, motions, suggestions, briefs and writ petitions with their answers are filed as text-searchable PDF, except by parties ineligible for electronic filing. | MO-R84-03 (c) | M |
| MOCTAPP-FORM-006 | The Rule 84.03(b) format applies "except as provided by local court rules"; the Court of Appeals local rules were not retrieved. | MO-R84-03 (b) lead-in | A |
| MOCTAPP-FORM-007 | Cover layout, caption, heading levels and the section order of the brief follow the filed exemplars, because the Rule 84.04 contents and the Court of Appeals cover rule were not retrieved. Not a rule requirement. | exemplars (usage/SOURCE.md) | C |

## Length and certification (briefs)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOCTAPP-LEN-001 | The appellant's initial brief, and every brief in a cross appeal except the reply, does not exceed 31,000 words. | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPP-LEN-002 | The respondent's brief does not exceed 27,900 words. | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPP-LEN-003 | The appellant's reply brief does not exceed 7,750 words. | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPP-LEN-004 | The cover, any certificate required by Rule 84.06(c), the signature block and the appendix are not counted; all other material is. | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPP-CERT-001 | A brief ends with a certificate of compliance signed by the lawyer or self-represented person. It states that the brief complies with Rule 84.06(b), states the number of words (the word-processing count may be used), says how the brief was served on the opposing party under Rule 43.01(c) or 103.08, and includes the information Rule 55.03 requires. | MO-R84-06 (c)(1)-(4) | M |
