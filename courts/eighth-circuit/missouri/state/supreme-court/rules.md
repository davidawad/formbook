# Supreme Court of Missouri: briefs, writ petitions and related filings

Alias `mo`. Scope: the filings made in the Supreme Court of Missouri: brief on the merits (substitute brief after transfer), respondent's (answering) brief, reply brief, motion with suggestions, petition for writ, and amicus curiae brief. Verified against the Missouri Supreme Court Rules, Rules 83 and 84 as posted on courts.mo.gov (Rule 84.03 and 84.06 readopted March 7, 2023, effective January 1, 2024; Rule 84.04 last amended June 30, 2017, effective January 1, 2018), on 2026-10-21. The Missouri courts share one rules publication; this sheet states the rows that govern this court.

The live courts.mo.gov site returned 403 to curl and WebFetch on 2026-10-21. The rule pages were read from the Internet Archive copies of the same courts.mo.gov pages (web.archive.org/web/2024id_/), which returned HTTP 200. Local rules not retrieved: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199778 (live site), 403; no local rule or administrative order on document form was read, so none is cited.

## Appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MO-CONT-001 | Appellant's brief contains, in order: a detailed table of contents with page references and a table of authorities (cases alphabetically arranged, statutes and other authorities) with the pages where cited; a concise jurisdictional statement; a statement of facts; the points relied on; an argument that substantially follows the order of the points; and a short conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(a) | M |
| MO-CONT-002 | The jurisdictional statement is not a bare recital; it sets out enough factual data to show which provision of article V, section 3 of the Constitution supports jurisdiction. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(b) | M |
| MO-CONT-003 | The statement of facts is fair, concise and free of argument, with specific page references to the record on appeal (legal file, transcript or exhibits; system-generated legal file cites carry the document number, for example D6 p. 7). | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(c) | M |
| MO-CONT-004 | Each point relied on identifies the ruling challenged, states concisely the legal reasons, and explains in summary fashion why those reasons support reversible error, in substantially the form "The trial court erred in [ruling], because [reasons], in that [explanation]." Abstract statements of law alone do not comply. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(d)(1), (4) | M |
| MO-CONT-005 | Immediately after each point relied on, a list of cases (not more than four) and the constitutional, statutory and regulatory provisions or other authority principally relied on. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(d)(5) | M |
| MO-CONT-006 | The argument restates each point relied on at the start of its section, is limited to the points relied on, and for each claim of error states whether the error was preserved, how, and the standard of review; factual assertions carry record page references. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(e) | M |
| MO-CONT-007 | Respondent's brief contains a detailed table of contents, a detailed table of authorities and an argument; its argument headings identify the appellant's points to which each answers. It may add its own jurisdictional statement and statement of facts. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(f) | M |
| MO-CONT-008 | A reply brief does not reargue points covered in the appellant's initial brief. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(g) | M |
| MO-CONT-009 | A party's brief is accompanied by a separate appendix (judgment, text of controlling statutes and rules, instructions) with its own table of contents, numbered A1 onward, filed as a separate document. | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(h) | M |
| MO-LEN-001 | Appellant's initial brief, and every brief in a cross appeal except the reply, is not more than 31,000 words. Respondent's brief is not more than 27,900 words. Appellant's reply brief is not more than 7,750 words. Everything in the brief except the cover, the Rule 84.06(c) certificate, the signature block and the appendix counts. | MOR8406 Rule 84.06(b) | M |
| MO-CERT-001 | A certificate of compliance by the lawyer or self-represented person: includes the information required by Rule 55.03, states how the brief was served on the opposing party, states that the brief complies with the Rule 84.06(b) limitations, and states the number of words (the word-processing count may be relied on). | MOR8406 Rule 84.06(c) | M |
| MO-CERT-002 | Signature block of counsel. Single spacing is permitted. | MOR8403 Rule 84.03(b)(2); MOR8406 Rule 84.06(c) | M |

## Petition for writ

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MO-WRIT-001 | The petition is filed with a one-page writ summary (exclusive of the caption and identity of the parties and attorneys in the underlying action) substantially in conformity with Civil Procedure Form No. 16, suggestions in support, the docket fee or a statement or motion excusing it, and proof of service on the respondent and any party in the underlying action. | MOR8424 Rule 84.24(a) | M |
| MO-WRIT-002 | In an original writ proceeding each point states the relief sought, identifies the action challenged, states concisely the legal reasons for the challenge and explains in summary fashion why they support it; for prohibition, substantially "Relator is entitled to an order prohibiting Respondent from [action], because [reasons], in that [explanation]." | MOR8404 Rule 84.04(d)(3) | M |
| MO-WRIT-003 | No original remedial writ issues where adequate relief can be afforded by appeal or by application to a lower court. | MOR8422 Rule 84.22(a) | M |
| MO-WRIT-004 | If the writ is issued, briefs are filed as on appeals (Rules 84.04 and 84.06 apply), on the schedule set by Rule 84.24(h). | MOR8424 Rule 84.24(h) | M |
| MO-WRIT-005 | The petition and suggestions in support are formatted as Rule 84.03(b) requires. | MOR8403 Rule 84.03(b), (c) | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MO-AMI-001 | A brief may be filed by amicus curiae on the merits only with the consent of all parties or by order of the court, and only if presented within the time allowed for the brief of the party supported. The Attorney General and authorized state entities need no consent. | MOR8405 Rule 84.05(f)(2), (4) | M |
| MO-AMI-002 | If consent is refused, a motion for leave of not more than five pages identifies the parties refusing consent, states the applicant's interest, and sets out facts or questions of law not adequately presented by the parties; the brief is conditionally filed with the motion. | MOR8405 Rule 84.05(f)(3) | M |
| MO-AMI-003 | An amicus brief conforms to Rules 84.03 and 84.06. | MOR8405 Rule 84.05(f)(5) | M |
