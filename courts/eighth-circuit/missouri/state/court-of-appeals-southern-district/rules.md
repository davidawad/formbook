# Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District: briefs

Alias `moctappsd`. Scope: the briefs filed in the Southern District of the Missouri Court of Appeals: the appellant's brief, the respondent's brief and the appellant's reply brief. The Supreme Court rules that apply to briefs in every appellate court are cited below; the district's own local rules were not retrieved. Verified against Missouri Supreme Court Rules 84.03 and 84.06 (text as published on the rulesofcivilprocedure.com Missouri pages, rule history on Rule 84.03 showing amendment Mar. 7, 2022, eff. Jan. 1, 2024) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=127234 (the Supreme Court's rules page) returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-21, so the Southern District's local rules (which Rule 84.03(b) and 84.06(b) defer to "as provided by local court rules") were not read. Word limits below are the Supreme Court rule's limits; the local rule may set different ones.

## Form (all papers)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOCTAPPSD-FORM-001 | Documents are legible, formatted for pages 8 1/2 x 11 inches, and every page, including the cover, is consecutively paginated with Arabic numerals at the bottom. | MO-R84-03 (b)(1) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-FORM-002 | Margins on the left, right, top and bottom are not less than one inch. Page numbers may appear in the bottom margin; no other text may appear in the margins. | MO-R84-03 (b)(2) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-FORM-003 | Line spacing is not less than 1.5. The caption, headings, quotations more than two lines long, footnotes, certificates required by Rule 84.06(c), and the signature block may be single-spaced. | MO-R84-03 (b)(2) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-FORM-004 | The font, including footnotes, is not smaller than 13 point, Times New Roman. | MO-R84-03 (b)(3); MO-R84-06 (a) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-FORM-005 | Briefs are filed as text-searchable PDF, except by parties ineligible for electronic filing. | MO-R84-03 (c) | M |

## Length and certification (briefs)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOCTAPPSD-LEN-001 | The appellant's initial brief does not exceed 31,000 words (Supreme Court limit; a local rule may set a lower limit). | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-LEN-002 | The respondent's brief does not exceed 27,900 words. | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-LEN-003 | The appellant's reply brief does not exceed 7,750 words. | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-LEN-004 | The cover, any certificate required by Rule 84.06(c), the signature block and the appendix are not counted; all other material is. | MO-R84-06 (b) | M |
| MOCTAPPSD-CERT-001 | A brief ends with a certificate of compliance signed by the lawyer or self-represented person. It states that the brief complies with Rule 84.06(b), states the number of words (the word-processing count may be used), says how the brief was served on the opposing party under Rule 43.01(c) or 103.08, and includes the information Rule 55.03 requires. | MO-R84-06 (c)(1)-(4) | M |
