# Exemplar source

Three public filings in the Eastern District of Missouri Bankruptcy Court (`moeb`), from three filers. Each file was downloaded from CourtListener's RECAP archive on 2026-10-14. All three are text-layer PDFs (`pdftotext` reads them) and are used only as structural and visual references. They are public court records.
