# Bankruptcy Court, Western District of Missouri: rules

Alias `mowb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion memoranda in `adversary-complaint/` and `motion-brief/`. Verified 2026-10-17 against the official rules compilation adopted January 5, 2026 and posted court orders. Re-check on every rule/order update and at least annually; apply the current Bankruptcy CM/ECF procedures and assigned judge's orders.

The January 2026 rules compilation is the primary text replacing the older 2012 archive previously cited in the doc-type notes. Confirm current numbering/text when re-checking the pending or later amendments; do not rely on archived 2012 citations for future filings. A separate adversary summons and any affidavit summary/form are outside the complaint or memorandum template. Page size, font and spacing choices not stated above are conventions only.
