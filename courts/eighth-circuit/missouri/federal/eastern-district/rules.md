# Eastern District of Missouri: rules

Alias `moed`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda in `complaint/` and `motion-brief/`. Verified against the court's posted rules on 2026-10-17. Re-check on every local-rules revision and at least annually; before filing, verify the assigned judge's procedures and administrative orders.

| Stable ID | Doc type | Requirement | Source and rule | Verified | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MOED-FORM-001 | both | Filing is typed or legibly written on 8.5 x 11 inch paper; type is at least 12 point and double-spaced. LR additionally requires signer's name, address, telephone, email, and state bar registration number immediately beneath each signature. | E.D. Mo. LR 2.01(A)(1) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MOED-FORM-002 | both | First page preserves clear blank areas of at least 2 x 2 inches in upper left and upper right corners. | E.D. Mo. LR 2.01(A)(2) | 2026-10-17 | M |

| MOED-LEN-001 | motion brief | Motion, memorandum, or brief may not exceed 15 numbered pages without leave; exclusions stated in LR 4.01(D) apply, including contents/authorities, signature page, and attachments. | E.D. Mo. LR 4.01(D) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MOED-CERT-001 | both | Electronic filing ordinarily constitutes service; certificate of service is not required for a paper served through the court's electronic filing system. | FRCP 5(d)(1)(B) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MOED-ELEC-001 | both | Attorneys file electronically in accordance with current CM/ECF procedures. | E.D. Mo. LR 2.08; CM/ECF procedures | 2026-10-17 | M |

Doc-type note: judge-specific requirements may add font sizes, page limits, TOC/TOA, or formatting requirements; check the assigned judge's currently posted procedures. LR 2.01 sets the minimum form stated above; one judge's order is not applied district-wide. Substantive complaint allegations, motion grounds, and selection of requested relief are author responsibilities.
