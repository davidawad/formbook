# Eighth Circuit Court of Appeals briefs: rules

Alias `ca8`. Scope: opening, answering, and reply briefs filed in the United States Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit. Verified against the court's current rules page and the primary rule texts listed below on 2026-10-17. Re-check the official rules page before every template refresh and at least annually; also re-check after a posted amendment or standing order.

## Common form and length (all three brief types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA8-FORM-001 | Pages are 8 1/2 by 11 inches. | FRAP 32(a)(4), FRAP source above, Rule 32(a)(4), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-FORM-002 | Margins are at least one inch on all sides. | FRAP 32(a)(4), Rule 32(a)(4), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-FORM-003 | Body text is double-spaced. Quotations exceeding two lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings, footnotes, and quotations may be single-spaced as the rule permits. | FRAP 32(a)(4), Rule 32(a)(4), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-FORM-004 | Proportionally spaced text uses a face with serifs, 14 point or larger; sans-serif type is allowed in headings and captions. The rule names no font family. | FRAP 32(a)(5)(A), Rule 32(a)(5)(A), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-FORM-005 | Typeface is plain roman; italics or boldface may be used for emphasis; case names must be italicized or underlined. | FRAP 32(a)(6), Rule 32(a)(6), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-FORM-006 | A brief cover includes, in order: case number at top; court name; case title; nature of proceeding and court below; brief title naming the filing party; counsel names, office addresses, and telephone numbers. | FRAP 32(a)(2)(A)-(F), Rule 32(a)(2), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-FORM-007 | Every brief is signed by counsel or an unrepresented party. | FRAP 32(d), Rule 32(d), verified 2026-10-17 | M |

| CA8-LEN-001 | Principal briefs are limited to 13,000 words or 1,300 lines; a reply brief is limited to half the principal brief's applicable type-volume limit. | FRAP 32(a)(7)(B)(i)-(ii), Rule 32(a)(7); 8th Cir. R. 28A(i), CA8-LR pp. 20-21, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-LEN-002 | The word/line count excludes the cover, corporate disclosure, contents, table of authorities, oral-argument statement, addendum of statutes and rules, certificates of counsel, signature block, and proof of service. | FRAP 32(f), Rule 32(f), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CERT-001 | A type-volume certificate states the count and uses the certification required by FRAP 32(g); the circuit's brief certification additionally states that the electronic brief was virus-scanned and is virus-free. | FRAP 32(g)(1); 8th Cir. R. 28A(h)(2), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CERT-002 | A corporate disclosure statement is filed as a separate document within seven days after docketing, when FRAP 26.1 and 8th Cir. | CA8-CONT-002 | Contents and authorities use dot leaders, right-aligned page references, and grouped authority sections where the filer chooses those conventions. | Filed exemplars; SOURCE.md per brief folder, retrieved 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-17 | C |

## Opening brief (appellant or petitioner)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA8-CONT-003 | The brief contains the parts required by FRAP 28(a), in order, including jurisdiction, issues, case, argument summary, argument with standard of review, and precise conclusion. | FRAP 28(a)(1)-(10), Rules 28(a), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CONT-004 | A summary of the case, at most one page, is the first item and states whether oral argument is requested and the time needed. | 8th Cir. R. 28A(i)(1), CA8-LR pp. 20-21, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CONT-005 | For each issue, the statement of issues identifies up to four most apposite cases and the apposite constitutional and statutory provisions. | 8th Cir. R. 28A(i)(2), CA8-LR p. 21, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CONT-006 | Record citations use the circuit format, including `R. Doc. 12, at 3` and, where applicable, `App. 25`. | 8th Cir. R. 28A(j), CA8-LR p. 21, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CONT-007 | The appellant's addendum is filed with the brief; the electronic addendum is a separate document. | 8th Cir. R. 28A(g)(1)-(5), CA8-LR pp. 19-20, verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CONT-008 | The top-level heading hierarchy, page numbering, contents style, and certificate placement follow common practice in the opening-brief exemplars; no additional arrangement is asserted as mandatory. | `opening-brief/exemplars/SOURCE.md`, retrieved 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-17 | C |

## Answering brief (appellee)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA8-CONT-009 | The appellee's brief includes the parts listed in FRAP 28(a)(1)-(8) and (10), except the jurisdictional statement, issues, case statement, and standard of review are omitted unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's treatment. | FRAP 28(b)(1)-(4), Rule 28(b), verified 2026-10-17 | M |
| CA8-CONT-010 | An appellee may file an addendum of no more than 15 pages as a separate electronic document. | 8th Cir. R. 28A(g)(3), (5), CA8-LR pp. 19-20, verified 2026-10-17 | M (if filed) |
| CA8-CONT-011 | Cover color for paper copies is red. Electronic PDF cover remains plain white. | FRAP 32(a)(2), Rule 32(a)(2), verified 2026-10-17 | M (paper copies) |
| CA8-CONT-012 | Heading levels, TOC/TOA grouping, and certificate placement follow the answering-brief exemplars where not fixed by a rule. | `answering-brief/exemplars/SOURCE.md`, retrieved 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-17 | C |

## Author responsibilities

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA8-AUTH-001 | Counsel verifies the accuracy of jurisdictional and record statements, apposite-case lists, oral-argument request, word/line count, and all required certificates. | FRAP 28(a), 32(g); 8th Cir. R. 28A(g)-(j), sources above, verified 2026-10-17 | A |
| CA8-AUTH-002 | The filer redacts personal identifiers and uses the proper redacted/unredacted filing procedure for any confidential material. | FRAP 25(a)(5), Rule 25(a)(5), verified 2026-10-17 | A |
