# Bankruptcy Court, Northern District of Iowa: adversary complaints and motion papers

Alias `ianb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion papers. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules of Bankruptcy Practice and Procedure (https://www.ianb.uscourts.gov/sites/ianb/files/2023%20Local%20Rules%20-%20%28Final%29_0.pdf, "revised, effective January 1, 2024") and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure on 2026-10-20.

Local rules retrieved; Local Rule 9004-1 (General Requirement of Form) states citation and unpublished-decision rules only, and no typeface, point size, margin or spacing rule was found in the retrieved text for papers filed in this court.
