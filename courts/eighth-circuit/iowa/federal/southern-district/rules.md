Alias `iasd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules (https://www.iasd.uscourts.gov/sites/iasd/files/Local%20Rules%20-%20Final%2012142020.pdf, "Final 12/14/2020") and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints and briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IASD-FORM-001 | Documents filed in paper form are on 8 1/2 x 11 inch paper; electronic filings are formatted the same way. | LR 10(a) | M |
| IASD-FORM-002 | Documents are double-spaced and printed on one side of the page. | LR 10(b)(1)-(2) | M |
| IASD-FORM-003 | Top margin is at least one inch on every page. | LR 10(b)(3) | M |
| IASD-FORM-004 | Documents more than one page long are numbered at the bottom of each page. | LR 10(b) | M |
| IASD-CONT-001 | A brief of more than 10 pages has a subject-matter table of contents on its first page. | LR 10(b)(4) | M |
| IASD-CAP-001 | Caption, title, file number and designation; claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited to one set of circumstances. | FRCP-10(a)-(b) | M |
| IASD-CERT-001 | Signed filing states the signer's address, e-mail address and telephone number. | FRCP-11(a) | M |
| IASD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted as FRCP-5.2(a) requires. | FRCP-5.2(a) | M |
| IASD-FORM-005 | The rule states no typeface or point size; the template uses the repository default (12-point or larger serif). | LR 10 (none stated) | T |
