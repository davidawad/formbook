# Court of Appeals of Iowa: briefs and motions

Alias `iowactapp`. Scope: the briefs filed in the Court of Appeals in cases it hears on transfer from the Supreme Court: the appellant's brief, the appellee's brief, the appellant's reply brief, and the brief of amicus curiae. Verified against the Iowa Rules of Appellate Procedure, Chapter 6 (Iowa Court Rules, Appellate Procedure, page footers dated December 2025; PDF dated 07-31-2026) on 2026-10-21. The Court of Appeals follows the same Chapter 6 rules as the Supreme Court; no separate Court of Appeals form rules were found. Motions are covered in the Form table below (6.1007).

## Appellee's brief (answering)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IOWACTAPP-ANS-001 | The appellee files a brief or a statement waiving it. A filed brief conforms to 6.903(2), except that the nature of the case, statement of the facts and jurisdictional statement need not be included unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statements. Each division of the argument begins with whether the appellee agrees with the appellant's statements on error preservation, scope of review and standard of review. | IA-R6 6.903(3) | M |
| IOWACTAPP-ANS-002 | A cross-appellant's brief includes the attachments required by 6.903(2)(b) unless the cross-appeal is from an order attached to the appellant's brief. | IA-R6 6.903(5) | M |

## Amicus curiae brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IOWACTAPP-AMI-001 | An amicus brief is filed only by leave of the court on motion, at the court's request, or with the written consent of all parties. The United States and the Iowa Attorney General may file without leave or consent. | IA-R6 6.906(1)(a) | M |
| IOWACTAPP-AMI-002 | The motion for leave identifies the applicant's interest and states why the brief would assist the court. A brief may be conditionally filed with the motion but is a separate document, not an attachment. | IA-R6 6.906(1)(b)-(c) | M |
| IOWACTAPP-AMI-003 | The amicus brief is filed no later than seven days after the brief of the party it supports (or, if it supports no party, seven days after the appellant's brief), unless the court extends the deadline for good cause. | IA-R6 6.906(1)(d) | M |
| IOWACTAPP-AMI-004 | Amicus length and contents are not separately limited by 6.906; the 6.903(1) form and word limits apply to a brief. | IA-R6 6.906; 6.903(1) | C |
