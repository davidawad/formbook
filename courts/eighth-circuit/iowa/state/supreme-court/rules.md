# Supreme Court of Iowa: briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `iowa`. Scope: the papers filed with the Clerk of the Supreme Court: briefs (appellant's, appellee's, reply, brief on the merits, amicus), petitions for discretionary review, petitions for rehearing, petitions for further review, petitions for review and writ, and motions. Verified against the Iowa Rules of Appellate Procedure, Chapter 6 (Iowa Court Rules, Appellate Procedure, page footers dated December 2025; PDF dated 07-31-2026) on 2026-10-21. Chapter 6 is one rule set for the Supreme Court and the Court of Appeals (see `iowactapp`).

## Briefs on the merits (appellant, appellee, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IOWA-BRF-001 | The appellant's brief contains, in this order: table of contents with page references; table of authorities; statement of the issues, each numbered and stated separately, in argument order; routing statement (retain or transfer to the court of appeals under 6.1101); nature of the case; statement of facts with record references; jurisdictional statement (for an appeal from a final judgment of sentence after a guilty plea); argument; conclusion stating the precise relief sought; request for oral or nonoral submission. | IA-R6 6.903(2)(a)(1)-(10) | M |
| IOWA-BRF-002 | The appellant's brief attaches the orders and judgments being appealed, as 6.903(2)(b) provides. | IA-R6 6.903(2)(b) | M |
| IOWA-BRF-003 | The appellee's brief conforms to 6.903(2), except that the nature of the case, statement of the facts and jurisdictional statement need not be included unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statements. Each division of the argument begins with whether the appellee agrees with the appellant's statements on error preservation, scope of review and standard of review. | IA-R6 6.903(3) | M |
| IOWA-BRF-004 | The reply brief need not contain the sections of 6.903(2)(a)(4)-(7), (8)(1)-(2) and (10); it otherwise complies with 6.903(2)(a). | IA-R6 6.903(4) | M |
| IOWA-BRF-005 | No Chapter 6 rule uses the term "brief on the merits". A merits brief is a brief under 6.903(2) or (3), whichever party files it. | IA-R6 6.903(2)-(3) | C |

## Petitions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IOWA-PET-001 | Petition for discretionary review (district court order): filed with the clerk within 30 days after the order; states with particularity the grounds for review; follows 6.1002(1) and 6.1007; displays any impending deadline beneath the title; pays the filing fee or moves to waive it. | IA-R6 6.106(1)(b), (e)-(f) | M |
| IOWA-PET-002 | Filing a discretionary review application does not stay district court proceedings. | IA-R6 6.106(1)(g) | M |
| IOWA-PET-003 | Application for further review of a court of appeals decision: filed within 20 days after the court of appeals filing (10 days in a chapter 232 or termination of parental rights case); a single document in the 6.903(1) form. | IA-R6 6.1103(1)(a), (c) | M |
| IOWA-PET-004 | Further review application sections, in order: questions presented (first page after the cover, nothing else on that page); table of contents with page references; statement supporting further review (not a mere recitation of the 6.1103(1)(b) grounds; identifies the issue and any conflicting authority); brief with all contentions and authorities (no incorporation by reference); copy of the court of appeals decision with its filing date; other attachments limited to district court record excerpts not exceeding ten pages, district court orders and agency rulings. | IA-R6 6.1103(1)(c)(1)-(6) | M |
| IOWA-PET-005 | The further review application (and any resistance) includes a Form 10 certificate of compliance. | IA-R6 6.1103(5)(b); IA-F10 | M |
| IOWA-PET-006 | Petition for writ of certiorari (original proceeding): filed with the clerk of the supreme court within 30 days after the challenged decision (or after the ruling on a timely Iowa R. Civ. P. 1.904(2) or 1.1007 motion); the caption names the challenging party as plaintiff and the district court, not the judge, as defendant; the petition states whether the issue was raised in district court and the grounds for the writ; it follows 6.1002(1) and 6.1007. | IA-R6 6.107(1)(a)-(b), (e)(1)-(4) | M |
| IOWA-PET-007 | Petition for rehearing in the supreme court: filed within 14 days after the opinion, unless the court shortens or enlarges the time; no rehearing from an order denying further review; states with particularity the points overlooked or misapprehended; in the 6.903(1) form; no longer than one-fifth of the brief length limit without permission; no response unless requested; no oral argument. | IA-R6 6.1205(1)-(4) | M |
| IOWA-PET-008 | Amicus briefs may not be filed in support of or in resistance to a petition for rehearing. | IA-R6 6.906(3) | M |

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IOWA-AMI-001 | An amicus brief is filed only by leave of the court on motion, at the court's request, or with the written consent of all parties. The United States and the Iowa Attorney General may file without leave or consent. | IA-R6 6.906(1)(a) | M |
| IOWA-AMI-002 | The motion for leave identifies the applicant's interest and states why the brief would assist the court. A brief may be conditionally filed with the motion but is a separate document, not an attachment. | IA-R6 6.906(1)(b)-(c) | M |
| IOWA-AMI-003 | The amicus brief is filed no later than seven days after the brief of the party it supports (or, if it supports no party, seven days after the appellant's brief), unless the court extends the deadline for good cause. | IA-R6 6.906(1)(d) | M |
| IOWA-AMI-004 | No amicus brief may be filed in support of or in resistance to an application for further review. After the supreme court grants further review, leave is sought by motion within 30 days of that order. | IA-R6 6.906(2)(a)-(c) | M |

## Length

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IOWA-LEN-001 | A proportionally spaced brief contains no more than 13,000 words. Headings, footnotes and quotations count; the caption, tables of contents and authorities, statement of issues, signature block and certificates do not. | IA-R6 6.903(1)(i)(1) | M |
| IOWA-LEN-002 | A reply brief contains no more than half the type volume of a brief (6,500 words). | IA-R6 6.903(1)(i)(1) | M |
| IOWA-LEN-003 | A monospaced brief is limited to 1,300 lines of text (reply: 650). | IA-R6 6.903(1)(i)(2) | M |
| IOWA-LEN-004 | A petition for rehearing is no longer than one-fifth of the length limit for a required brief, except by permission of the supreme court. | IA-R6 6.1205(2) | M |
| IOWA-LEN-005 | An application for further review, or a resistance to one, is no longer than two-fifths of the brief limit, excluding the court of appeals decision, tables, relevant district court record materials, district court orders and agency decisions. | IA-R6 6.1103(5)(a) | M |
