# Minnesota Court of Appeals: briefs, motions, petitions and amicus briefs

Alias `minnctapp`. Scope: the filings a party or amicus files in the Court of Appeals: appellant's (opening) brief, respondent's (answering) brief, reply brief, motion and memorandum, petition for review of an agency rule (declaratory judgment), petition for writ, petition for review (the Rule 117 petition to the Supreme Court of a Court of Appeals decision), and amicus curiae brief. Verified against the Minnesota Rules of Civil Appellate Procedure as published by the Office of the Revisor of Statutes (Rules 114, 117, 120, 127, 128, 129, 131 and 132, current text with amendments through the dates shown on each rule) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.mncourts.gov/ (403 to curl on 2026-10-21; Court of Appeals internal operating procedures and e-filing manual not cited).

## Appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-001 | Front cover shows the name of the court and the appellate docket number (bold or prominent, one-half inch from the top center), the title of the case, the title of the document (for example Appellant's Brief and Addendum), and the names, addresses and telephone numbers of attorneys for each party, with the preparers' attorney registration license numbers. | MNRCAP 132.01 subd. 2 | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-002 | Appellant's formal brief, in this order: (a) table of contents with page references and an alphabetical table of cases, statutes and other authorities with page references; (b) statement of the legal issues; (c) statement of the case and the facts; (d) argument; (e) short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; (f) optional statement on precedential status; (g) addendum. | MNRCAP 128.02 subd. 1 | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-003 | Each legal issue is followed by how it was raised in the trial court (with record cites), the trial court's ruling, how it was preserved for appeal (with record cites), and the most apposite cases (not more than four) and constitutional and statutory provisions. | MNRCAP 128.02 subd. 1(b) | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-004 | Statement of the case first identifies the trial court and judge and the nature and disposition of the case, then the facts relevant to the grounds urged; each material fact carries a record reference. | MNRCAP 128.02 subd. 1(c) | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-005 | Argument may be preceded by a summary introduction and states the standard of review for each issue; each issue is separately presented. | MNRCAP 128.02 subd. 1(d) | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-006 | Respondent's brief conforms to the appellant's requirements, except a statement of the issues, case or facts is needed only if respondent is dissatisfied with appellant's. | MNRCAP 128.02 subd. 2 | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-007 | Reply brief is confined to new matter raised in the respondent's brief. | MNRCAP 128.02 subd. 3 | M |
| MINNCTAPP-LEN-001 | Principal brief: not more than 45 pages (excluding table of contents, tables of citations and addendum), or not more than 14,000 words. | MNRCAP 132.01 subd. 3, 3(a) | M |
| MINNCTAPP-LEN-002 | Reply brief: not more than 20 pages, or 7,000 words. | MNRCAP 132.01 subd. 3, 3(b) | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CERT-001 | A brief measured by word count carries a certificate of compliance stating the name and version of the word-processing software, that the brief complies with the typeface requirements of the rule, and the number of words. | MNRCAP 132.01 subd. 3 | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CERT-002 | Briefs are served and filed with proof of service. | MNRCAP 131.01; 127 | M |

## Motions and petition for writ

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-008 | Motion states with particularity the grounds and sets forth the order or relief sought; supporting briefs, affidavits or documents are served and filed with it, with proof of service. | MNRCAP 127 | M |
| MINNCTAPP-CONT-009 | Every motion or other document has a caption with the name of the court, the title of the case, the appellate docket number, and a brief descriptive title of the document. | MNRCAP 132.02 subd. 2 | M |

| MINNCTAPP-CONT-011 | Documents and briefs filed in a writ proceeding are in the form specified by Rule 132.02. | MNRCAP 120.04 | M |
