# Minnesota Supreme Court: briefs, motions, petitions and amicus briefs

Alias `minn`. Scope: the filings a party or amicus files in the Minnesota Supreme Court: appellant's principal brief (brief on the merits), respondent's (answering) brief, reply brief, motion and supporting memorandum, petition for review of a Court of Appeals decision, petition for accelerated (discretionary) review, petition for an extraordinary writ, and amicus curiae brief. Verified against the Minnesota Rules of Civil Appellate Procedure as published by the Revisor of Statutes (rule texts and amendment histories shown on each page, latest amendment shown: Rule 129 amended effective April 1, 2025) on 2026-10-21.

The Supreme Court's own rules are the Rules of Civil Appellate Procedure; there is no separate local rules document for form. Rule 133 is the prehearing conference and calendar rule and says nothing about petitions; petitions for review are Rule 117 and petitions for accelerated review are Rule 118. No rule or order was found that sets a form for a petition for rehearing (Rule 140) in this folder: it is BLOCKED in `usage/SOURCE.md` (no exemplar) and is not covered here.

## Principal, respondent's and reply briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MINN-COVER-001 | Front cover: the name of the court and the appellate court docket number in bold or prominent lettering one-half inch from the top center; the title of the case; the title of the document (for example "Appellant's Brief and Addendum"); the names, addresses and telephone numbers of the attorneys for each party, and the attorney registration license numbers of the preparers of the brief. | MNRAP132 Rule 132.01 subd. 2 | M |
| MINN-CONT-001 | Appellant's formal brief, in this order: table of contents with page references and an alphabetical table of cases, statutes and other authorities with page references; statement of the legal issues (each followed by how raised, the ruling, how preserved, and at most four most apposite cases plus constitutional and statutory provisions); statement of the case and the facts; argument; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; the addendum. | MNRAP128 Rule 128.02 subd. 1(a)-(g) | M |
| MINN-CONT-002 | Statement of the case first identifies the trial court and judge and the nature and disposition of the case, then the facts relevant to the grounds urged, stated fairly and concisely, each material fact with a record reference. | MNRAP128 Rule 128.02 subd. 1(c) | M |
| MINN-CONT-003 | The argument states the standard of appellate review for each issue, each issue separately presented; it may be preceded by a summary introduction. | MNRAP128 Rule 128.02 subd. 1(d) | M |
| MINN-CONT-004 | Respondent's brief conforms to Rule 128.02 subd. 1 except that a statement of the issues, case or facts is not needed unless respondent is dissatisfied with appellant's; a notice of related appeal's issues are presented. | MNRAP128 Rule 128.02 subd. 2 | M |
| MINN-CONT-005 | A reply brief is confined to new matter raised in the respondent's brief. | MNRAP128 Rule 128.02 subd. 3 | M |
| MINN-CONT-006 | Record references cite the addendum pages where the record part is reproduced there; otherwise the particular part of the record and specific pages. Document Index Numbers from the Register of Actions are used where available. | MNRAP128 Rule 128.03 | M |
| MINN-CONT-007 | The record is not printed and no appendix is submitted; the appellant files an addendum (separate document if filed electronically) with its table of contents. | MNRAP130 Rules 130.01, 130.02(a) | M |
| MINN-LEN-001 | Principal brief: 45 pages, or 14,000 words; reply brief: 20 pages or 7,000 words; amicus brief: 20 pages or 7,000 words. Tables of contents and citations and the addendum are excluded. | MNRAP132 Rule 132.01 subd. 3 | M |
| MINN-CERT-001 | A brief within the word or line alternative includes a certificate of compliance: it states the name and version of the word processing software, states that the brief complies with the typeface requirements, and states the number of words (or lines of monospaced text). | MNRAP132 Rule 132.01 subd. 3 | M |

## Petitions for review and for accelerated review

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MINN-PET-001 | Petition for review of a Court of Appeals decision: filed within 30 days of the decision, with proof of service. | MNRAP117 Rule 117 subd. 1(a) | M |
| MINN-PET-002 | Petition for review: no more than 2,000 words, excluding the caption, signature block and addendum; contains (a) the legal issues and their disposition by the Court of Appeals, (b) the criteria relied upon (Rule 117 subd. 2) or other substantial and compelling reasons, (c) the statement of the case, and (d) a brief argument. | MNRAP117 Rule 117 subd. 3 | M |
| MINN-PET-003 | The petition and addendum are accompanied by a Certificate of Document Length. The addendum may contain the Court of Appeals decision. | MNRAP117 Rule 117 subd. 3 | M |
| MINN-PET-004 | Petition for accelerated review before a Court of Appeals decision: shows the Rule 117 criteria plus imperative public importance; no more than 4,000 words excluding caption, signature block and addendum; contains (a) the statement of the issues, (b) the statement of the case with all relevant facts and the disposition below, (c) a brief argument; the addendum holds the judgments, orders, findings and conclusions for which review is sought; accompanied by a Certificate of Document Length. | MNRAP118 Rule 118 subds. 1, 2 | M |
| MINN-PET-005 | Petition for an extraordinary writ directed to the Court of Appeals, Tax Court or Workers' Compensation Court of Appeals: specifies the lower court and judge; contains the facts, the issues and relief sought, and the reasons the writ should issue; the order complained of is in an addendum; titled "In re (name of petitioner), Petitioner," followed by the trial court caption. | MNRAP120 Rule 120.01 | M |
| MINN-PET-006 | Writ petition documents are in the form of Rule 132.02, and the petition and proof of service are filed with the clerk of appellate courts. | MNRAP120 Rule 120.04 | M |
| MINN-PET-007 | A response to a petition for review must comply with the requirements for the petition, within 21 days of service. | MNRAP117 Rule 117 subd. 4 | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MINN-AMI-001 | An amicus brief is filed only with leave of the appellate court and only by an attorney admitted to practice before it. | MNRAP129 Rule 129.01(a) | M |
| MINN-AMI-002 | The request for leave is served and filed within 14 days after the filing of the notice of appeal, the initiating petition or the order granting review; it states whether the applicant's interest is public or private, the party supported or whether the brief suggests affirmance or reversal, and why the brief is desirable; at most 1,500 words with a certificate of document length. | MNRAP129 Rule 129.01(b)-(c) | M |
| MINN-AMI-003 | The amicus brief is in the form of a formal brief (Rule 132.01), at most 20 pages or 7,000 words, with a certificate of compliance. | MNRAP132 Rule 132.01 subds. 1-3 | M |
