# Bankruptcy Court, District of Minnesota: rules

Alias `mnb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion packages/memoranda in `adversary-complaint/` and `motion-brief/`. Verified 2026-10-17. The complete local rules are effective September 3, 2025. Re-check on each annual/posted rule update and at least annually; confirm judge orders, current forms, and CM/ECF procedures before filing.

Project implementation selects 12 pt, one-inch margins, and double spacing as layout conventions, not mandates. Every document's substantive allegations and requested relief are author responsibility. Electronic/PDF technical requirements beyond the stated direct-to-PDF rule must be checked in the current filing procedures. The 2025 restyling/renumbering moved motion service and notice to LBR 9013-2; LBR 9013-3 now governs applications.
