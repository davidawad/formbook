# District of Minnesota (civil district): rules

Alias `mnd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda in `complaint/` and `motion-brief/`. Verified 2026-10-17 against the primary sources below. Re-check whenever a cited rule is amended and at least annually; confirm the assigned judge's procedures and current ECF guide before filing.

## Requirements

### Complaint

| Stable ID | Requirement | Primary source and rule | Verified | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MND-FORM-001 | Pleading states grounds for jurisdiction, a short and plain claim, and relief demanded. | FRCP 8(a)(1)-(3) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MND-FORM-002 | Caption gives court name, title, file number, and party names; allegations use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(a)-(b) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MND-FORM-003 | A written jury demand may be included in the complaint; it must be served no later than 14 days after the last pleading directed to the issue. | FRCP 38(b) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MND-FORM-004 | Text is double-spaced and pages are numbered consecutively at the bottom. LR 5.2(a)'s case number and assigned judge initials apply to filed documents after the case-initiating document. | D. Minn. LR 5.2(a) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MND-FORM-005 | Filed document carries the attorney's D. Minn. registration number. | D. Minn. LR 5.2(c) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MND-FORM-006 | Complaint-specific font size is not set by LR 7.1(h), which governs memoranda; no face requirement is asserted. | D. Minn. LR 7.1(h)(1) (scope) | 2026-10-17 | M |

### Motion memorandum

| Stable ID | Requirement | Primary source and rule | Verified | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MND-LEN-001 | Memorandum limit is 12,000 words using a proportional font. | D. Minn. LR 7.1(f)(1)(A) | 2026-10-17 | M |

| MND-FORM-007 | At least 13-point type, including footnotes; double-spaced, except headings and footnotes may be single-spaced and quotations over two lines may be indented and single-spaced. | D. Minn. LR 7.1(h)(1) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MND-FORM-008 | Letter paper; text within 6.5 by 9 inch area, except page numbers. | D. Minn. LR 7.1(h)(1) | 2026-10-17 | M |

| MND-FORM-010 | Attorney registration number appears on filed document. | D. Minn. LR 5.2(c) | 2026-10-17 | M |
| MND-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing must conform to LR 5.1 and the court's current ECF procedures; the local rule defers technical PDF specifications to the guide. | D. Minn. LR 5.1; official guide above | 2026-10-17 | M |

Typeface: the local rules set no specific face; no court-mandated substitute is identified. Complaints remain governed by FRCP 10 and local LR 5.2; the 13-point memorandum rule is not extended to complaints. Judge-specific orders may add requirements. Sample content, including Rule 8 allegations and the precise relief, remains the author's responsibility.
