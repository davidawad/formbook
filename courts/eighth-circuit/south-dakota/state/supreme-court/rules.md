# Supreme Court of South Dakota: briefs and motions

Alias `sd`. Scope: the filings in the Supreme Court that have a filed exemplar: appellant's brief (brief-on-the-merits), appellee's brief (answering-brief), reply brief, and motion with supporting memorandum. Verified against the South Dakota Codified Laws, chapter 15-26A (Appeals to the Supreme Court), as published by the South Dakota Legislature, on 2026-10-21. The Supreme Court's rules of appellate procedure are enacted in this chapter; there is no separate rule book.

## Appellant's brief and appellee's brief (brief-on-the-merits, answering-brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SD-COVER-001 | Cover states: the title of the action, indicating which party is appellant and which is appellee; the court from which the appeal is taken; the name of the judge who tried the action; whether the brief is for the appellant or the appellee; the names and addresses of the attorneys for appellant and appellee; and the date the notice of appeal was filed. | SDCL69(5) | M |
| SD-CONT-001 | Appellant's brief contains, under appropriate headings and in this order: table of contents with page references; table of cases (alphabetical), statutes and other authorities with page references; jurisdictional statement; statement of the legal issues; statement of the case and the facts; argument; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; appendix. | SDCL60(1)-(8) | M |
| SD-CONT-002 | Jurisdictional statement gives the date and form of the judgment or order reviewed, the date the notice of appeal was filed, and shows the order is appealable. | SDCL60(3) | M |
| SD-CONT-003 | Each legal issue is stated as the broad issue presented, followed by how the trial court decided it, and by the most relevant cases (not more than four) and constitutional and statutory provisions. | SDCL60(4) | M |
| SD-CONT-004 | Statement of the case identifies the trial court and judge, the nature of the case and its disposition; the statement of facts follows. Each material fact carries a record reference. | SDCL60(5), SDCL64 | M |
| SD-CONT-005 | Argument presents each issue separately, with the contentions, reasons and citations of authority; needless repetition is avoided. | SDCL60(6) | M |
| SD-CONT-006 | Appendix contains the judgment, order or decision, findings and conclusions, and other items listed in SDCL 15-26A-60(8); its pages are separately numbered and it has its own table of contents. | SDCL60(8) | M |
| SD-CONT-007 | The appellee's brief conforms to SDCL 15-26A-60. | SDCL61 | M |
| SD-CONT-008 | Statutes, ordinances and rules needed to decide the issues are reproduced in the brief or in an appendix at the end. | SDCL65 | M |
| SD-CONT-009 | References to parties use trial-court designations, names, or descriptive terms rather than "appellant" and "appellee" where practicable. | SDCL63 | A |
| SD-CONT-010 | A party in a multi-party appeal may join in a single brief or adopt by reference part of another party's brief. | SDCL67 | A |
| SD-LEN-001 | Appellant and appellee briefs: at most 32 pages in proportionally spaced type, or at most the greater of 10,000 words or 50,000 characters. A brief may exceed the page limit if it meets the type-volume limit. | SDCL66(b), (b)(2) | M |
| SD-LEN-002 | Headings, footnotes and quotations count toward the volume limit; the table of contents, table of cases, jurisdictional statement, statement of legal issues, addendum materials and certificates do not. | SDCL66(b)(3) | M |
