Appellees Crowned Ridge Wind, LLC and South Dakota Public Utilities Commission Staff hereby move for leave to correct clerical error pursuant to SDCL 15-6-60(a). In support of this motion, Appellees state:

1. This appeal originates from a docket before the South Dakota Public Utilities Commission docketed as EL19-003.
2. Throughout its Memorandum Opinion and Order Affirming Decision, the Circuit Court incorrectly listed the docket number as EL18-003.
3. With the exception of the docket number, all other details including the case name correctly refer and relate to the matter now on appeal to this Court.
4. The incorrect docket number was clearly a clerical error.
5. The settled record has been transmitted to the Clerk of the Supreme Court as of the filing of this Joint Motion.

SDCL 15-6-60(a) provides

> Clerical mistakes in judgments, orders, or other parts of the record and errors therein arising from oversight or omission may be corrected by the court at any time of its own initiative or on the motion of any party and after such notice, if any, as the court orders. During the pendency of an appeal, such mistakes may be so corrected before the settled record is transmitted to the clerk of the Supreme Court and thereafter while the appeal is pending may be so corrected with leave of the Supreme Court.

Because the settled record has already be transmitted to the clerk of the Supreme Court, it is necessary to seek leave of the Supreme Court in order to have the clerical error corrected.

WHEREFORE, Appellees jointly respectfully request this Court grant leave for the docket number to be corrected by the Honorable Carmen Means, Circuit Court Judge.
