## Requirements

### Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SDD-FORM-001 | Caption begins with case caption, title of document, and filing party; later papers bear the assigned case number. | D.S.D. Civ. LR 10.1(A) | M |
| SDD-FORM-002 | Papers use 8½ × 11 inch pages, double-spaced text, single-spaced headings and footnotes, at least one-inch margins, and type no smaller than 12 pt. The rule does not specify a typeface or serif. | D.S.D. Civ. LR 83.4(A) | M |
| SDD-CONT-001 | A complaint states a claim for relief and short, plain statement of jurisdiction, claim, and relief sought; claims and defenses are in numbered paragraphs. | FRCP 8(a), 10(b) | M |
| SDD-CERT-001 | Signature block gives signer’s typed or printed name, address, telephone, and email beneath the signature. | D.S.D. Civ. LR 10.1(B) | M |

### Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SDD-CONT-002 | With every opposed motion raising a question of law, the movant serves and files a brief containing legal arguments, supporting authorities, and the Federal Rule relied on. | D.S.D. Civ. LR 7.1(B) | M |
| SDD-LEN-001 | Briefs are limited to 30 pages, excluding contents/authorities, certificate of service, and attachments, absent prior court approval. | D.S.D. Civ. LR 7.1(B)(1) | M |
| SDD-CONT-003 | A response brief is due within 21 calendar days after service; the movant may reply within 14 calendar days after service of the response. | D.S.D. Civ. LR 7.1(B) | M |
| SDD-ELEC-001 | ECF transmission plus the court's Notice of Electronic Filing constitutes filing; the electronic document is the official record. The ECF signature is `/s/` plus the filing user's typed name unless a signature facsimile is used. | D.S.D. Civ. LR 5.1(A)-(B) | M |

## Document-type differences

Complaints use FRCP pleading-content and numbered-paragraph rules. Opposed motion briefs follow the D.S.D. Civ. LR 7.1 brief, page cap, and response schedule. The exemplars settle visual choices not supplied by these rules.
