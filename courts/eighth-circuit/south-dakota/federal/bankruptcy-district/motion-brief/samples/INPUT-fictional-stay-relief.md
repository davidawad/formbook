# MEMORANDUM

## I. Facts

The debtor filed a voluntary petition under Chapter 13 on March 1, 2000. Movant holds a promissory note secured by a 2015 model-year vehicle (the "Collateral").

The debtor's plan proposes to pay the Collateral's value over the life of the plan. The debtor has made no payment on the note since the petition date.

## II. Argument

Under 11 U.S.C. § 362(d)(1), the Court shall grant relief from the stay for cause, including lack of adequate protection of an interest in property.

1. Movant's interest in the Collateral is not adequately protected, because the Collateral depreciates each month the debtor keeps it.
2. The debtor has not offered any payment that would protect Movant's interest.

## III. Conclusion

Movant requests that the Court grant relief from the automatic stay as to the Collateral and waive the fourteen-day stay under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 4001(a)(3).
