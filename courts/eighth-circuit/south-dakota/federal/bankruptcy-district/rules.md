Kinds: **M** = mandated; **C** = convention; **A** = author responsibility. Local rules require one simple font but do not name a face or type size for these papers. Stable IDs are not renumbered.

## Requirements

### Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SDB-FORM-001 | Paper uses white 8½ × 11 pages, at least one-inch margins, one simple font, and is typewritten without erasures. | Bankr. D.S.D. R. 9004-1(a)(1) | M |
| SDB-FORM-002 | Caption includes debtor's full name, full EIN if any, last four of SSN or TIN, and aka/fka names. | Bankr. D.S.D. R. 9004-2; R. 9004-1(a)(3) | M |
| SDB-CONT-001 | Adversary caption includes the bankruptcy caption and complete legal names of all plaintiffs and defendants, conforming to Official Form 416D. | Bankr. D.S.D. R. 7010-2; Official Form 416D | M |
| SDB-CONT-002 | Complaint states claims for relief and requested remedy under the applicable Bankruptcy Rules and FRCP 8. | FRBP 7008; FRCP 8 | M |
| SDB-CERT-001 | Filing attorney signs and dates the document under local filing requirements. | Bankr. D.S.D. R. 9004-1(a)(5) | M |

## Document-type differences

Adversary complaints use the mandatory party and debtor caption fields in SDB-FORM-002 and SDB-CONT-001. Motion memoranda follow FRBP 9013; the cited local rules add separate-document and summary-judgment-specific filing rules. No length limit or special type size for these documents was located in the cited local rules.
