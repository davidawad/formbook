# Arkansas Supreme Court: briefs, motions, petitions and amicus briefs

Alias `ark`. Scope: the filings a party, movant, petitioner or amicus files in the Supreme Court: brief on the merits (appellant's brief), answering (appellee's) brief, reply brief, motion and memorandum, petition for review (this is also the petition for discretionary review: Arkansas has no separate PDR), petition for writ, petition for rehearing, and amicus curiae brief. Verified against the Rules of the Supreme Court and Court of Appeals of the State of Arkansas (current through December 15, 2025) on 2026-10-21. The same rules govern both courts; the Court of Appeals sheet is `arkctapp`.

## Petitions for review (discretionary review)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARK-REV-001 | A petition for review of a Court of Appeals decision is filed electronically within 10 calendar days after the end of the Court of Appeals rehearing period. | ARSUP Rule 2-4(a) | M |
| ARK-REV-002 | The petition is no more than three 8½ × 11 double-spaced pages. It briefly and distinctly states the basis for review and may cite authority, statutes or constitutional provisions. Only a party to the appeal may file it. | ARSUP Rule 2-4(b) | M |
| ARK-REV-003 | No brief accompanies the petition. The petitioner may attach a copy of the petition for rehearing. | ARSUP Rule 2-4(c) | M |
| ARK-REV-004 | The petition alleges one of: a tie vote in the Court of Appeals; a decision in conflict with a prior published holding of the Supreme Court or Court of Appeals; or Court of Appeals error on a Rule 1-2(b) ground. | ARSUP Rule 2-4(d) | M |
| ARK-REV-005 | A response is due within 10 calendar days of the petition's filing and is subject to the same limits; a copy of the response to the petition for rehearing may be attached. | ARSUP Rule 2-4(e) | M |
| ARK-REV-006 | The petition complies with Administrative Order 19 (confidential information) under Ark. R. Civ. P. 5(c)(2)(A)-(B). | ARSUP Rule 2-4(h) | M |
| ARK-REV-007 | Arkansas has no separate petition for discretionary review; the petition for review under Rule 2-4 is that vehicle. An exemplar longer than three pages attaches the Court of Appeals rehearing petition or opinion as permitted by Rule 2-4(c). | ARSUP Rule 2-4; usage/SOURCE.md | A |
