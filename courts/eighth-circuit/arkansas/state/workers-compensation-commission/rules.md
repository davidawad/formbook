# Arkansas Workers' Compensation Commission: briefs and motions to the Full Commission

Alias `arkworkcompcom`. Scope: papers filed with the Commission in a Full Commission appeal from an administrative law judge (ALJ) order: appellant's, appellee's and reply briefs, and motions. Verified against the Rules of the Commission as listed on the Commission's rules page (page last modified 2026-06-19) and read from the Code of Arkansas Rules on 2026-10-21. Commission papers have no page limit, font size or margin stated in the sections read; none is invented here. Appeals from a Full Commission order go to the Arkansas Court of Appeals under its own rules (sheet `arkctapp`).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-001 | Briefs and other legal materials are typed or printed, double-spaced, on 8½ x 11 inch paper. | CAR-25-114(b) | M |
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-002 | Every filing is served on the other known parties and carries a certificate of service. | CAR-25-114(b) | M |
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-003 | A party filing a brief in a Full Commission appeal files an original and two copies. Only one copy of any filing is kept in the claim file; multiple filings are discouraged except for briefs in Full Commission appeals. | CAR-25-114(a), (b) | M |
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-FORM-004 | Typeface, margins, cover layout, heading levels and caption style are not stated in the sections read; follow the conventions of Commission filings. Not a rule requirement. | convention | C |

## Briefs in a Full Commission appeal (appellant, appellee, reply)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-001 | A party who appeals or cross-appeals to the Full Commission from an ALJ or single-commissioner order lists every issue it intends to raise in the notice of appeal or cross-appeal. | CAR-25-120(a) | M |
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-002 | Legal and factual issues should be developed at the hearing before the ALJ or single commissioner; the Commission may refuse to consider issues not raised below. A brief confines its argument to issues so raised and listed. | CAR-25-120(b)(1)-(2) | M |
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-003 | The party appealing a Full Commission compensation order or award pays a $15 processing fee when filing the appeal; the record is not compiled or certified until the fee is received. | CAR-25-114(d) | M |
| ARKWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-004 | Oral argument may be granted on a written request made within a reasonable time after the appeal is filed, and may be required by the Commission whether or not requested. | CAR-25-113(a)-(b) | M |

## Motions
