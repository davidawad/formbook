# Arkansas Court of Appeals: briefs, motions, petitions and amicus briefs

Alias `arkctapp`. Scope: the filings an appellant, appellee, movant or amicus files in the Court of Appeals: opening (appellant's) brief, answering (appellee's) brief, reply brief, motion and memorandum, petition for writ, and amicus curiae brief. Verified against the Rules of the Supreme Court and Court of Appeals of the State of Arkansas (current through December 15, 2025) on 2026-10-21. The same rules govern both courts; the Supreme Court sheet is `ark`.
