# Western District of Arkansas: complaints and briefs

Alias `arwd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules (https://www.arwd.uscourts.gov/sites/arwd/files/ARWD%20Local%20Rules.pdf, Local Rule 5.5 "Pleadings and Filings", amended November 10, 2009 per the document) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints and briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARWD-FORM-001 | Pleadings, motions and papers are typewritten, photocopied, mimeographed or printed, in type not less than elite, double-spaced, on letter-size paper, on one side of the page. | LR 5.5(a) | M |
| ARWD-CAP-001 | Caption, title, file number and designation; claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited to one set of circumstances. | FRCP-10(a)-(b) | M |
| ARWD-CERT-001 | Signed filing states the signer's address, e-mail address and telephone number. | FRCP-11(a) | M |
| ARWD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted as FRCP-5.2(a) requires. | FRCP-5.2(a) | M |
| ARWD-FORM-002 | "Elite" is a monospaced 12-pitch face; the local rule states no point size for proportional type. The template uses the repository default (12-point serif). | LR 5.5(a) | C |
