# District Court of Guam: complaint and motion brief rules

Alias `gud`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs in the District Court of Guam. Verified against the court's primary sources on 2026-10-17. Re-check every six months and before a filing if the court's rules page shows a newer edition.

## Court-wide civil requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule / source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUD-FORM-001 | Paper size is 8 1/2 by 11 inches; paper is white and opaque. | GNLR 5.1(a) | M |
| GUD-FORM-002 | Papers are double-spaced except counsel identification, title of the case, footnotes, quotations, and exhibits. | GNLR 5.1(a) | M |
| GUD-FORM-003 | Pages are numbered consecutively at the bottom. | GNLR 5.1(a) | M |
| GUD-FORM-004 | Court title is centered and begins no less than 3 inches from the top. | GNLR 5.1(b) | M |
| GUD-FORM-005 | Counsel name, address, and telephone are at upper left; the right half of the page is reserved for the clerk's filing stamp. | GNLR 5.1(c)(1) | M |
| GUD-FORM-006 | Action title is below and left of the court title. To its right, show file number, civil designation, document description, and mention any notice of motion or supporting memorandum. | GNLR 5.1(c)(2)-(3) | M |
| GUD-FORM-007 | Names are typed below signatures. | GNLR 5.1(d) | M |
| GUD-ELEC-001 | E-filing, PDF preparation, signatures, and service follow the court's current Administrative Procedures. | GNLR 5.1(f)(1); EFILE | M |
| GUD-FORM-008 | No typeface, type size, or general margin dimension is specified in GNLR 5.1. | GNLR 5.1 | M (rule states no such requirement) |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule / source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUD-CONT-001 | A complaint states a short and plain jurisdictional statement, claim, and demand for relief. | FRCP 8(a) | M |
| GUD-CONT-002 | Claims and defenses use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances; separate claims are stated in separate counts when clarity requires. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| GUD-CONT-003 | Caption states the court, title of action, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| GUD-COVER-001 | A civil cover sheet accompanies a document initiating a civil action. | GNLR 5.1(c)(4) | M |
| GUD-CERT-001 | A certificate of service is not required for a paper served through the court's e-filing system. | FRCP 5(d)(1)(B) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule / source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUD-CONT-004 | A motion is in writing, states its grounds with particularity, and states the relief sought. | FRCP 7(b)(1) | M |
| GUD-CONT-005 | A motion brief's caption includes the file number, civil designation, document description, and mentions the notice of motion or supporting memorandum. | GNLR 5.1(c)(3) | M |
| GUD-CERT-002 | A certificate of service is not required for a paper served through the court's e-filing system. | FRCP 5(d)(1)(B) | M |

## Re-check notes

The current court rules landing page is https://www.gud.uscourts.gov/Local_Rules_of_Practice. At verification, it listed GNLR (2022) and Civil Local Rules (2019) as the current editions. No local general motion page limit or typeface requirement is asserted. The court's 2025 e-filing procedures are the current source for filing mechanics.
