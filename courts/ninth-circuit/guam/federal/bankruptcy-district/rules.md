## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule / source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUB-FORM-001 | Paper size is 8 1/2 by 11 inches; paper is white and opaque. | GNLR 5.1(a), incorporated as appropriate by BKLR 1001-1(d) | M |
| GUB-FORM-002 | Papers are double-spaced except counsel identification, title of the case, footnotes, quotations, and exhibits. | GNLR 5.1(a), BKLR 1001-1(d) | M |
| GUB-FORM-003 | Pages are numbered consecutively at the bottom. | GNLR 5.1(a), BKLR 1001-1(d) | M |
| GUB-FORM-004 | Court title is centered and begins no less than 3 inches from the top. | GNLR 5.1(b), BKLR 1001-1(d) | M |
| GUB-FORM-005 | Counsel name, address, and telephone are at upper left; right of center is reserved for the clerk filing stamp. | GNLR 5.1(c)(1), BKLR 1001-1(d) | M |
| GUB-FORM-006 | Caption includes action title at left and, to its right, file number, bankruptcy or adversary designation, document description, and mention of a notice of motion or supporting memorandum. | GNLR 5.1(c)(2)-(3), BKLR 1001-1(d) | M |
| GUB-FORM-007 | Typed names follow signatures. | GNLR 5.1(d), BKLR 1001-1(d) | M |
| GUB-ELEC-001 | E-filing, PDF preparation, signatures, and service follow the current court Administrative Procedures. | GNLR 5.1(f)(1); EFILE | M |
| GUB-FORM-008 | No typeface, type size, or general margin dimension is specified in GNLR 5.1. | GNLR 5.1 | M (rule states no such requirement) |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule / source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUB-CONT-001 | Adversary proceedings follow FRBP Part VII; an adversary complaint includes the elements and summons process required by FRBP 7003-7004. | BKLR 7001-1(a); FRBP 7003, 7004 | M |
| GUB-CONT-002 | Civil pleadings in an adversary proceeding follow FRCP as incorporated by FRBP 7008; complaint states jurisdictional basis, claim, and relief, with numbered paragraphs and separate counts as applicable. | FRBP 7008; FRCP 8(a), 10(a)-(b) | M |
| GUB-COVER-001 | The caption identifies the bankruptcy case and adversary proceeding and supplies the case and adversary numbers. | FRBP 9004(b); GNLR 5.1(c)(3), BKLR 7001-1 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule / source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUB-CONT-003 | A request for an order is made by written motion; the motion states grounds with particularity and sets out requested relief. | FRBP 9013(a)-(b) | M |
| GUB-CONT-004 | Unless made in open court, a motion includes a supporting memorandum. Motion and memorandum together may not exceed 15 pages, excluding attachments, absent court permission. | BKLR 9013-1(b), as amended by GO 23-0026 | M |
| GUB-CONT-005 | Hearing notice is given no later than 28 days before the hearing and substantially conforms to local form GUB 9073-1; response and reply times follow BKLR 9013-1(c). | BKLR 9013-1(c), as amended by GO 23-0026 | M |
| GUB-CERT-001 | Attorney signs in the attorney's individual name and states address and telephone number. | FRBP 9011(a) | M |

## Re-check notes

The court's current rules landing page is https://www.gud.uscourts.gov/Local_Rules_of_Practice. BKLR 1001-1(d) incorporates the General and Civil Local Rules as appropriate. BKLR 9013-1 was amended by GO 23-0026 effective November 20, 2023; this is the adopted text, not the earlier proposal. Federal and local rules prescribe no typeface in the sources checked.
