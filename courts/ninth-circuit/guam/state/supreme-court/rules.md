# Supreme Court of Guam: briefs and petitions for rehearing

Alias `guam`. Scope: principal briefs (answering brief) and petitions for panel rehearing. Verified against the Guam Rules of Appellate Procedure as posted by the Judiciary of Guam (2014 edition, COL2242014 print, Rules 6, 16, 29, 30) and the Judiciary of Guam Electronic Filing Rules (Promulgation Order No. 15-001-01, Oct. 2, 2015) on 2026-10-22.

## Answering brief (principal brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUAM-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inch. Text is double-spaced; quotations more than two lines long may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | GRAP 16(a)(4) | M |
| GUAM-FORM-002 | Margins are at least one inch on all four sides. Page numbers may be placed in the margins; no other text may appear there. | GRAP 16(a)(4) | M |
| GUAM-FORM-003 | A proportionally spaced face (for example Times New Roman or Arial) must include serifs (sans-serif only in headings and captions) and must be 14-point or larger. The body is set in plain roman type; italics or boldface may be used for emphasis; case names are italicized or underlined. | GRAP 16(a)(5)(A), 16(a)(6) | M |
| GUAM-FORM-004 | If a monospaced face is used instead, it may not contain more than 10½ characters per inch. | GRAP 16(a)(5)(B) | M |
| GUAM-FORM-005 | Reproduction yields a clear black image on opaque, unglazed paper, one side only. | GRAP 16(a)(1)(A) | M |
| GUAM-COVER-001 | The front cover states, in this order: the case number centered at top; the name of the court; the case title; the nature of the proceeding and the court, agency or board below; the brief title identifying the party for whom it is filed; and the name, office address and telephone number of counsel. | GRAP 16(a)(2)(A)-(F) | M |
| GUAM-COVER-002 | Cover color by filer: appellant's brief blue, appellee's brief red, amicus or intervenor green, reply gray, supplemental tan. Unrepresented parties are excepted. | GRAP 16(a)(2) | M |
| GUAM-LEN-001 | A principal brief may not exceed 30 pages, or 14,000 words (a monospaced brief may not exceed 1,300 lines). Headings, footnotes and quotations count; the corporate disclosure statement, table of contents, table of citations, oral-argument statement, any statutory addendum and certificates of counsel do not count. | GRAP 16(a)(7)(A)-(B)(i), (iii) | M |
| GUAM-CERT-001 | A brief submitted under the type-volume limit includes a certificate of compliance by the attorney or unrepresented party stating the number of words (or monospaced lines). The certificate may rely on the word-processing count. Form 3 in the Appendix of Forms is a suggested form. | GRAP 16(a)(7)(D)(i)-(ii) | M |
| GUAM-CERT-002 | Parties may not append or incorporate briefs filed in the Superior Court. | GRAP 16(a)(7)(B)(iv) | M |
| GUAM-CERT-003 | Counsel's certificate as to interested parties (GRAP 13(j)(1)) and the list of Justices who presided or served as counsel (13(j)(2)) attach to the inside of the cover of the initial brief. The exemplar's answering brief carries the same two certificates, a convention. | GRAP 13(j)(1)-(2) | M |
| GUAM-CERT-004 | The statement of related cases appears on the last page of the initial brief, identifying each known related case pending in this court; a statement that there are none is accepted. The answering brief carries the same statement, a convention. | GRAP 13(l) | M |
| GUAM-SIG-001 | Every brief is signed by the party or, if represented, by one of its attorneys. | GRAP 16(c) | M |
| GUAM-ELEC-001 | Documents are filed electronically as PDF, and protected information (SSNs, financial account numbers, dates of birth, names of minor children, individual taxpayer and personal identification numbers, names of sex-crime victims) is omitted or redacted before filing. | EFR 2.1(r) (PDF), 3.6 (electronic filing in PDF), 6.1-6.2 (protected information), 9.2-9.3(a) (Supreme Court cases) | M |

## Petition for panel rehearing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUAM-PET-001 | The petition states with particularity each point of law or fact the Court has overlooked or misapprehended and argues it; an issue not previously briefed may not be raised for the first time. | GRAP 30(a)(2) | M |
| GUAM-LEN-002 | Unless the Supreme Court permits, a petition for panel rehearing may not exceed 15 pages. The principal-brief word limit does not apply to a rehearing petition. | GRAP 30(b); 16(b)(2)(B) | M |
| GUAM-FORM-007 | A petition for rehearing is reproduced in the manner Rule 16 prescribes (paper, spacing, margins, typeface, type style), except that a cover is not required if the caption and signature page carry the cover information. | GRAP 16(b)(2)(A)-(B), 30(b) | M |
| GUAM-COVER-003 | The cover of a petition for rehearing is orange. Unrepresented parties are excepted. Where a cover is used for other papers, Rule 16(b)(2)(A) calls for white; the specific orange rule in Rule 30(c) controls for petitions for rehearing. | GRAP 30(c), 16(b)(2)(A) | M |
| GUAM-TIME-001 | A petition for rehearing is filed within 14 days after entry of judgment, or 45 days where the Government of Guam or one of its agencies or officers is a party, unless an order shortens or extends the time. | GRAP 30(a)(1) | M |
