# Oregon Tax Court: complaints, briefs, motions and amicus briefs

Alias `ortc`. Scope: complaints and pleadings, motions and responses, briefs and memoranda, and amicus briefs filed in the Oregon Tax Court, under the Tax Court Rules of the Magistrate Division (TCR-MD) and the Tax Court Rules of the Regular Division (TCR), with the Uniform Trial Court Rules chapter 21 (UTCR 21) for electronic filing as incorporated by TCR 9 A. No exemplar was supplied; these rows are written from the rules only. Verified against TCR-MD (January 1, 2026 edition) and UTCR chapter 21 (2026 edition, which sets the 50 MB limit under UTCR 21.040(1)) and TCR (Regular Division, January 1, 2022 edition with out-of-cycle amendments) on 2026-10-21. The Regular Division copy retrieved is dated January 2022 and the court's rules page does not state a newer edition; re-check before relying on any TCR row.

## Division note

Most appeals begin in the Magistrate Division; the Magistrate Division's decisions are appealed to the Regular Division. The Magistrate rules say that, where they do not cover a point, the Regular Division rules may be used as a guide, and that citations to "TCR" are to the Regular Division rules (TCR-MD 2026, introductory statement). Rows below are tagged by division.

|---|---|---|---|

## Motions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORTC-MOT-001 | Every motion, except one made during trial (Regular Division) or a court proceeding (Magistrate Division), is in writing, states its grounds with particularity and the relief sought, and is accompanied by the reasons and authorities. | TCR 2022, 14 A; TCR-MD 2026, 7 A. | M |
| ORTC-MOT-002 | Every written motion is accompanied by a memorandum of law or a statement of points and authorities explaining how the authorities support the moving party's contentions. | TCR 2022, 14 D(1). | M |
| ORTC-MOT-003 | Except for dispositive motions, every written motion is accompanied, on a separate page, by a proposed form of order. | TCR 2022, 14 D(1). | M |
| ORTC-MOT-004 | A motion to strike or to make more definite and certain more than two particulars of a pleading attaches a copy of the pleading pages moved against, with the parts to be stricken shown in parentheses and the parts to be made more definite underlined. | TCR 2022, 14 D(2). | M |
| ORTC-MOT-005 | A response to a written motion (other than summary judgment) in the Regular Division is filed within 10 days after service of the motion. | TCR 2022, 14 E. | M |
| ORTC-MOT-006 | A motion under TCR 36 through TCR 46 (discovery) includes a certificate that counsel made a good-faith effort to confer, and the court will deny a discovery motion without it. | TCR 2022, 14 C(1)-(2). | M |
| ORTC-MOT-007 | A response to a written motion in the Magistrate Division, if one is filed, follows the response periods in TCR-MD 7 B-D. | TCR-MD 2026, 7 B-D. | M |
| ORTC-MOT-008 | A motion filed after the initial case management conference that is a request for an order of the court is in writing and states the reasons, authorities and relief requested, with any declarations or documentary evidence. | TCR-MD 2026, 7 A, D. | M |

## Briefs and memoranda (Regular Division)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|

| ORTC-BRF-002 | A brief of more than five pages, or one with more than five citations, has, in order: a title page showing the title of the court, the case number and title, the title of the brief, and the names of counsel; a table of contents with page references; and an alphabetical list of all citations with the pages on which each is cited. | TCR 2022, 61 A(1)-(3). | M |

| ORTC-BRF-004 | Case citations include the pinpoint page where the proposition is discussed.
