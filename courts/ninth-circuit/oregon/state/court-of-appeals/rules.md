# Oregon Court of Appeals: briefs, motions, amicus briefs and petitions for review

Alias `orctapp`. Scope: opening, answering, combined and reply briefs, amicus briefs, motions and responses, and petitions for review of Court of Appeals decisions (filed in the Supreme Court under ORAP 9.05) under the Oregon Rules of Appellate Procedure (ORAP). Verified against the ORAP compilation "ORAP Effective 2019-04-15" (courts.oregon.gov, edition printed 2019-04-15, the most recent full compilation the court site serves) on 2026-10-21. Not verified: amendments adopted after 2019-04-15. A secondary mirror reports a 2025 amendment to Rule 5.05 (word counts unchanged in that mirror), and the court's notice of proposed rulemaking dated 2026-05-19 proposes amendments to ORAP 1.45, 5.05, 5.15, 5.45, 5.50, 7.10, 8.15, 11.05, 16.10 and 16.60 (comments due 2026-07-17, effective 2027-01-01 if adopted). Rows citing those rules must be re-checked against the adopted text before the template is finalised.

## Form of briefs (all briefs, including petitions for review and responses)

ORAP 5.05(3) applies to every "brief", which includes a petition for review or reconsideration and a response to one (ORAP 5.05(3)).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORCTAPP-FORM-001 | All pages are a uniform 8½ × 11 inches. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(a)(i). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-002 | Printed or used area does not exceed 6¼ × 9½ inches, with inside margins of 1¼ inches, outside margins of 1 inch, and top and bottom margins of ¾ inch. (The 2019 text prints the height as "9-12"; the margins above imply 9½ inches.) | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(a)(ii). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-003 | Text is proportionally spaced type in Arial, Times New Roman or Century Schoolbook, not smaller than 14 point for both the text and the footnotes. Reducing or condensing the type to add words is not permitted. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(b)(ii); 5.05(1)(d)(ii). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-004 | Uniformly spaced (typewriter-style) type is not used for briefs, because 5.05 requires proportionally spaced type. | ORAP 2019, 1.45(3) ("Except as otherwise provided in ORAP 5.05"); 5.05(3)(b)(ii). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-005 | Print is legible and black, except hyperlinks. A brief is not prepared entirely or substantially in uppercase. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(b)(i), (iii). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-006 | Briefs are double-spaced, with a double space above and below each paragraph of quotation. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(b)(iv). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-007 | Pages are consecutively numbered at the top within ⅜ inch of the top edge. Excerpt-of-record pages are numbered independently with the prefix "ER-"; appendix pages carry the prefix "App-". | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(c). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-008 | Front cover sets out: the full case title; party designations as in the trial court and on appeal; the case number below and the appellate case number; the party on whose behalf the brief is filed; the court from which the appeal is taken; the name of the judge below; and the litigant contact information under ORAP 1.30. The lower right corner states the month and year of filing. If filed fewer than five business days before oral argument, the caption also reads "ORAL ARGUMENT SCHEDULED FOR [DATE]". | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(d). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-009 | The last page carries the name and signature of the author, the law firm (if any), and the name of the party on whose behalf the brief is filed. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(e). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-010 | Every document carries a caption with the court's name, the case number (if assigned), the title of the document and the party names on the front, plus the name, address and telephone number of the party or attorney. | ORAP 2019, 1.45(1)(a)-(b). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-011 | Any document other than a brief may be uniformly spaced at no more than 10 characters per inch, or proportionally spaced at not less than 13 point (text and footnotes). | ORAP 2019, 1.45(3). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-012 | Documents are printed on recycled paper where it is readily available at a reasonable price, and carry no color highlighting on any part of the text. | ORAP 2019, 1.45(4)(b)-(c). | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-013 | A brief that challenges the constitutionality of an Oregon statute or constitutional provision states that on the cover, names the statute or provision, and is served on the Attorney General when filed. | ORAP 2019, 5.12. | M |
| ORCTAPP-FORM-014 | Briefs are filed through eFiling by an active Oregon State Bar member (unless a listed exception or waiver applies). Each eFiled document is a PDF or PDF/A, text-searchable and copy-paste enabled, and does not exceed the size limit in the rule. | ORAP 2019, 16.60(1)-(2); 16.15(1). The 2019 text sets the size limit at 25 MB; UTCR 21.040(1) raised the trial-court limit to 50 MB, so verify the current ORAP 16.15(1) figure before setting the eFiling check. | M |

## Briefs: opening, answering, combined and reply

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORCTAPP-LEN-001 | An opening brief or an answering brief in the Court of Appeals is 10,000 words or fewer. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(ii)(A)-(B). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-002 | A combined respondent's answering and cross-appellant's opening brief is 16,700 words or fewer, with the answering part limited to 10,000 words. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(ii)(C). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-003 | A combined cross-respondent's answering and appellant's reply brief is 10,000 words or fewer, with the reply part limited to 3,300 words. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(ii)(D). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-004 | A reply brief is 3,300 words or fewer. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(ii)(E). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-005 | The count includes headings, footnotes and quoted material. The front cover, index of contents and appendices, index of authorities, excerpt of record, appendices, certificate of service, other certificates and the signature block are not counted. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(a). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-006 | Each brief ends with a certificate in the Appendix 5.05-2 form stating the word count (the count from the word-processing system may be relied on), or, if the filer has no word-count system, that the brief complies with the page limits of 5.05(1)(c). Supplemental briefs by self-represented parties are exempt. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(d)(i). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-007 | If the filer has no word-processing system with a word count, an opening, answering or combined brief is acceptable at 35 pages or fewer, and a reply brief (or reply part of a combined brief) at 10 pages or fewer. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(c). The page limits are the 2019 text; re-check. | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-008 | A party's appendix does not exceed 25 pages. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(e). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-009 | A supplemental brief does not exceed five pages unless the court orders otherwise. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(f). | M |
| ORCTAPP-LEN-010 | A party exceeding a limit moves for leave with a specific reason, and makes every reasonable effort to file at least seven days before the brief is due. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(2)(a). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-001 | An appellant's combined opening brief and excerpt begins with: an index of contents with a statement of each assignment of error and page references; an index of appendices; and an index of all authorities, classified and alphabetised with full citations. | ORAP 2019, 5.35(1)-(3). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-002 | A reply brief has an index and contains a summary of argument. | ORAP 2019, 5.70(2). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-003 | The opening brief's statement of the case appears first, under separate headings, in the order the rule sets: nature of the action and relief sought; nature of the judgment and whether trial was before the court or a jury; the statutory basis of appellate jurisdiction; the judgment's entry date, the notice-of-appeal dates, and any explanation of timeliness. | ORAP 2019, 5.40(1)-(4). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-004 | Each assignment of error is stated separately under a numbered heading, identifies precisely the ruling challenged, and shows it was preserved; the arrangement follows Appendix 5.45, with record page references. | ORAP 2019, 5.45(1)-(4). Amended text proposed (NPRM 2026). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-005 | The appellant's opening brief includes an excerpt of record (not required of a self-represented party), with the judgment or order on appeal, the rulings addressing the assignments of error, the pleadings and transcript needed for review, and preservation material where preservation is disputed. | ORAP 2019, 5.50(1)-(2). Amended text proposed (NPRM 2026). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-006 | Record references give the page and volume of the transcript or narrative statement, the audio cue or counter number, or the exhibit identification; for electronic records the court prefers the PDF page number. | ORAP 2019, 5.20(1)-(3). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-007 | In the body of a brief, parties are referred to as designated below (not "appellant" or "respondent"); in domestic relations matters, as husband, wife, father, mother or another specific designation; and, in certain offense cases, the victim's full name is not given. | ORAP 2019, 5.15(1)-(2). Amended text proposed (NPRM 2026). | M |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-008 | An appendix is for materials useful to understanding the appeal (for example statutes or unreported cases), and record materials that belong in the excerpt are not placed in it. | ORAP 2019, 5.52. The rule uses "should not" for the record-material point. | A |
| ORCTAPP-ORDER-009 | Appendix pages carry the prefix "App-" (see ORCTAPP-FORM-007) and count toward the appendix limit (ORCTAPP-LEN-008). | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(c), 5.05(1)(e). | M |

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORCTAPP-AMICUS-001 | An application to appear as amicus is accompanied by the brief to be filed, does not argue the resolution of the case, and is submitted by an active Oregon State Bar member. | ORCTAPP-AMICUS-002 | The form of an amicus brief is subject to the same rules as a party's brief, so ORCTAPP-FORM-001 to -014 and the word limit in ORCTAPP-LEN-001 apply. The application must comply with ORAP 7.10(1)-(2). | ORAP 2019, 8.15 (form); 7.10(1)-(2). | M |

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORCTAPP-MOT-001 | A motion has a title that designates the filing party and one of the motion titles in Appendix 7.10-1 (or "Motion–Other" with an accurate title). Each motion in one document is titled. | ORAP 2019, 7.10(1)(a). | M |
| ORCTAPP-MOT-002 | A response to a motion for extension of time is titled as a response to the named motion (for example "Response to Appellant's MOET–File Opening Brief"). | ORAP 2019, 7.10(1)(b)(ii). | M |

| ORCTAPP-MOT-004 | Motions are not subject to the word limits in ORCTAPP-LEN-001 to -004; those limits apply only to opening, answering, combined and reply briefs. No word or page limit for motions appears in ORAP 7.10 (2019 text; ORAP 7.05 not re-read). | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(a); 7.10. | M |

## Petitions for review of a Court of Appeals decision (filed in the Supreme Court)

The Court of Appeals exemplar "petition-for-review" is a petition filed in the Supreme Court under ORAP 9.05 from a Court of Appeals decision. The Court of Appeals does not itself receive it.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORCTAPP-PET-001 | A petition for Supreme Court review of a Court of Appeals decision is filed within 35 days after the date of the decision, subject to the extension and timing rules in ORAP 9.05(2). | ORAP 2019, 9.05(2)(a). | M |
| ORCTAPP-PET-002 | The petition is in the form of a brief under ORAP 5.05 and 5.35 and does not exceed 5,000 words (or 15 pages if the preparer has no word-count system). | ORAP 2019, 9.05(3)(a). | M |
| ORCTAPP-PET-003 | The petition cover identifies: the petitioner on review (and the specific party or parties if there are several on that side); the respondent on review; the date of the Court of Appeals decision; the means of disposition, with the authoring judge and concurring or dissenting members for an opinion, or the members who decided the case for a per curiam, affirmance without opinion or order; and whether the petitioner will file a brief on the merits if review is allowed. | ORAP 2019, 9.05(3)(a)(i)-(v). | M |
| ORCTAPP-PET-004 | A petition or brief that challenges the constitutionality of a statute or constitutional provision is served on the Attorney General and says so on the cover. | ORAP 2019, 5.12. | M |
| ORCTAPP-PET-005 | A response to a petition is due within 14 days after the petition is filed, is in the form of a brief under ORAP 5.05, and does not exceed 5,000 words (or 15 pages). | ORAP 2019, 9.10(2)-(3). | M |
