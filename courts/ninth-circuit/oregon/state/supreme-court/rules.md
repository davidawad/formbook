# Oregon Supreme Court: briefs on the merits, petitions, responses, motions, amicus briefs and petitions for reconsideration

Alias `or`. Scope: briefs on the merits, answering and reply briefs, petitions for review of Court of Appeals decisions, petitions for writ of mandamus, responses, motions, amicus applications and briefs, and petitions for reconsideration filed in the Oregon Supreme Court under the Oregon Rules of Appellate Procedure (ORAP). Verified against the ORAP compilation "ORAP Effective 2019-04-15" (courts.oregon.gov, edition printed 2019-04-15, the most recent full compilation the court site serves) on 2026-10-21. Not verified: amendments adopted after 2019-04-15. The court's notice of proposed rulemaking dated 2026-05-19 proposes amendments to ORAP 1.45, 5.05, 5.15, 5.45, 5.50, 7.10, 8.15, 11.05 and 16.60 (comments due 2026-07-17, effective 2027-01-01 if adopted). Rows citing those rules must be re-checked against the adopted text. A secondary mirror reports a 2025 amendment to Rule 5.05; its word counts match the 2019 text.

## Form of briefs and petitions (all briefs, responses, petitions for writ and reconsideration)

ORAP 5.05(3) applies to every "brief", which includes a petition for review or reconsideration and a response to one (ORAP 5.05(3)). A petition for writ must comply as to form with ORAP 5.05(3) (ORAP 11.05(2)).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-FORM-001 | All pages are a uniform 8½ × 11 inches. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(a)(i). | M |
| OR-FORM-002 | Printed or used area does not exceed 6¼ × 9½ inches, with inside margins of 1¼ inches, outside margins of 1 inch, and top and bottom margins of ¾ inch. (The 2019 text prints the height as "9-12"; the margins above imply 9½ inches.) | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(a)(ii). | M |
| OR-FORM-003 | Text is proportionally spaced type in Arial, Times New Roman or Century Schoolbook, not smaller than 14 point for both the text and the footnotes. Reducing or condensing the type to add words is not permitted. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(b)(ii); 5.05(1)(d)(ii). | M |
| OR-FORM-004 | Uniformly spaced (typewriter-style) type is not used for briefs, because 5.05 requires proportionally spaced type. | ORAP 2019, 1.45(3) ("Except as otherwise provided in ORAP 5.05"); 5.05(3)(b)(ii). | M |
| OR-FORM-005 | Print is legible and black, except hyperlinks. A brief is not prepared entirely or substantially in uppercase. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(b)(i), (iii). | M |
| OR-FORM-006 | Briefs are double-spaced, with a double space above and below each paragraph of quotation. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(3)(b)(iv). | M |

## Length limits (Supreme Court)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-LEN-001 | An opening brief on the merits and an answering brief are 14,000 words or fewer. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(i)(A)-(B). | M |
| OR-LEN-002 | A combined respondent's answering and cross-petitioner's opening brief is 22,000 words or fewer, with the answering part limited to 14,000 words. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(i)(C). | M |
| OR-LEN-003 | A combined cross-respondent's answering and petitioner's reply brief is 12,000 words or fewer, with the reply part limited to 4,000 words. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(i)(D). | M |
| OR-LEN-004 | A reply brief is 4,000 words or fewer. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(b)(i)(E). | M |
| OR-LEN-005 | The count includes headings, footnotes and quoted material. The front cover, index of contents and appendices, index of authorities, excerpt of record, appendices, certificate of service, other certificates and the signature block are not counted. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(a). | M |
| OR-LEN-006 | Each brief ends with a certificate in the Appendix 5.05-2 form stating the word count, or, if the filer has no word-count system, that the brief complies with the page limits in OR-LEN-007. Supplemental briefs by self-represented parties are exempt. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(d)(i). | M |
| OR-LEN-007 | Without a word-count system, an opening, answering or combined brief is acceptable at 50 pages or fewer, and a reply brief at 15 pages or fewer. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(c). | M |
| OR-LEN-008 | A party's appendix does not exceed 25 pages. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(e). | M |
| OR-LEN-009 | A supplemental brief does not exceed five pages unless the court orders otherwise. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(f). | M |
| OR-LEN-010 | A party exceeding a limit moves for leave with a specific reason, and makes every reasonable effort to file at least seven days before the brief is due. | ORAP 2019, 5.05(2)(a). | M |

## Briefs on the merits (petitioner and respondent)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-MER-001 | A petitioner who gave notice of intent to file a brief on the merits files it within 28 days after review is allowed. | ORAP 2019, 9.17(2)(a). | M |
| OR-MER-002 | The petitioner's brief on the merits contains, in order: concise statements of the questions presented and the rule of law proposed (not argumentative, and not raising new questions); a statement of the nature of the action, the relief sought and the judgment below; a narrative statement of all material facts with record references; a summary of the argument; the argument; and a conclusion stating the relief sought with particularity. | ORAP 2019, 9.17(2)(b)(i)-(v). | M |
| OR-MER-003 | The petitioner's brief on the merits conforms to ORAP 5.05, 5.35, 5.95 and 9.05(3). The cross-reference to 9.05(3) is read here as a form cross-reference only; the word limit that applies is OR-LEN-001. The meaning of the cross-reference is an open point and should be confirmed. | ORAP 2019, 9.17(2)(c). | M |
| OR-MER-004 | A respondent's brief on the merits is due 28 days after the petitioner's brief on the merits is filed, or 28 days after review is allowed if the petitioner files no brief. | ORAP 2019, 9.17(3)(a)(i)-(ii). | M |
| OR-MER-005 | A reply brief on the merits is due 14 days after the respondent's brief, and conforms to ORAP 5.05, 5.35 and 5.95. | ORAP 2019, 9.17 (reply); 5.05(1)(b)(i)(E). | M |
| OR-MER-006 | The respondent's brief on the merits conforms to ORAP 5.05, 5.35 and 5.95 (form and length as in OR-FORM-001 to -014 and OR-LEN-001). | ORAP 2019, 9.17(3)(c); 5.05. | M |

## Petitions for review of a Court of Appeals decision

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-PET-001 | A petition for review is filed within 35 days after the date of the Court of Appeals decision, subject to the extension and timing rules in ORAP 9.05(2). | ORAP 2019, 9.05(2)(a). | M |
| OR-PET-002 | The petition is in the form of a brief under ORAP 5.05 and 5.35, and does not exceed 5,000 words (or 15 pages if the preparer has no word-count system). | ORAP 2019, 9.05(3)(a). | M |
| OR-PET-003 | The petition cover identifies: the petitioner on review (and the specific party or parties if several on that side); the respondent on review; the date of the Court of Appeals decision; the means of disposition, with the authoring judge and concurring or dissenting members for an opinion, or the members who decided the case for a per curiam, affirmance without opinion or order; and whether the petitioner will file a brief on the merits if review is allowed. | ORAP 2019, 9.05(3)(a)(i)-(v). | M |
| OR-PET-004 | A juvenile dependency, termination or adoption case expedited under ORAP 10.15 displays the matching expedited caption on the cover. | ORAP 2019, 9.05(3)(a)(vi). | M |
| OR-PET-005 | A response to a petition for review is due within 14 days after the petition, is in the form of a brief under ORAP 5.05 and 5.35, and does not exceed 5,000 words (or 15 pages). | ORAP 2019, 9.10(2)-(3). | M |

## Petitions for reconsideration

The Supreme Court exemplar "petition-for-rehearing" is treated here as a petition for reconsideration under ORAP 9.25; Oregon uses "reconsideration" for this filing.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-REC-001 | A petition for reconsideration of a Supreme Court decision is filed within 14 days after the decision. | ORAP 2019, 9.25(1). | M |
| OR-REC-002 | The petition is in the form of a brief under ORAP 5.05 and 5.95, and is no longer than a petition for review under ORAP 9.05(3)(a) (5,000 words). | ORAP 2019, 9.25(1). | M |
| OR-REC-003 | The petition cover identifies the petitioner, the date of the decision, and the judges who joined any opinion. A copy of the decision is included. | ORAP 2019, 9.25(1). | M |
| OR-REC-004 | A response to a petition for reconsideration is due within seven days after the petition is filed. | ORAP 2019, 9.25(2). | M |

## Petitions for writ of mandamus (original proceedings)

The Supreme Court exemplar "petition-for-writ" is a petition under ORAP 11.05.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-WRIT-001 | A petition for writ of mandamus in the Supreme Court is filed by a petition substantially in the form the rule prescribes, and complies as to form with ORAP 5.05(3). | ORAP 2019, 11.05(1)-(2). | M |
| OR-WRIT-002 | The title page caption is "State ex rel ____, Plaintiff-Relator, v. ____, Defendant" (or the form the rule prescribes for the proceeding), with the relator's litigant contact information under ORAP 1.30 on the title page. The judge or court is not named as a defendant. | ORAP 2019, 11.05(2)(a)-(b). | M |
| OR-WRIT-003 | A memorandum, motion or other document filed in the mandamus proceeding, other than the petition, displays "MANDAMUS PROCEEDING" prominently in its caption. | ORAP 2019, 11.05(4)(a). | M |
| OR-WRIT-004 | The relator serves the adverse party and, in other mandamus proceedings, the Attorney General, and files proof of service. | ORAP 2019, 11.05(2)(b)-(3); 5.12 (for a constitutional challenge). | M |
| OR-WRIT-005 | No word or page limit for a mandamus petition appears in ORAP 11.05 in the 2019 text; the form rules of ORAP 5.05(3) apply. | ORAP 2019, 11.05(2). | A |

## Amicus briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-AMICUS-001 | An application to appear as amicus is accompanied by the brief to be filed, does not argue the resolution of the case, and is submitted by an active Oregon State Bar member. The original and one copy of the application are filed (no filing fee), and it is served on all parties. | ORAP 2019, 8.15. | M |
| OR-AMICUS-002 | The form of an amicus brief is subject to the same rules as a party's brief, so OR-FORM-001 to -014 and the Supreme Court length limits apply (OR-LEN-001 to -004 as the brief type requires). The application complies with ORAP 7.10(1)-(2). | ORAP 2019, 8.15; 7.10(1)-(2). | M |
| OR-AMICUS-003 | An amicus brief supporting a party is due seven days after the date the supported party's brief is due. An amicus brief supporting a petition or response follows the timing in ORAP 8.15 (within 14 days after the petition is filed, or on the date the aligned party's brief is due). | ORAP 2019, 8.15. | M |
| OR-AMICUS-004 | An amicus does not argue orally unless the court orders otherwise. | ORAP 2019, 8.15. | M |
| OR-AMICUS-005 | An eFiled application and the brief it accompanies are filed as separate eFiling transactions, each with the comment that ORAP 16.15(5)(c) requires. | ORAP 2019, 16.15(5)(c). | M |

| OR-MOT-002 | A response to a motion for extension of time is titled as a response to the named motion. | ORAP 2019, 7.10(1)(b)(ii). | M |

| OR-MOT-004 | Motions are not subject to the word limits in OR-LEN-001 to -004, which apply to briefs. No word or page limit for motions appears in ORAP 7.10 (2019 text). | ORAP 2019, 5.05(1)(a); 7.10. | M |
