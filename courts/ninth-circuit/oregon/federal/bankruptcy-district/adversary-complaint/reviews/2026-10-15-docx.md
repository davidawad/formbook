Typst reference defect (not in the docx; for the typst owner): the numbered allegations in `typst/orb-adversary-complaint.typ` (`allegations()`) are emitted as inline content with no paragraph break or block spacing, so consecutive numbered paragraphs run together in the typst PDF (e.g. ¶ 4 begins mid-line after ¶ 3 on typst p.3). FRCP 10(b) wants separate numbered paragraphs. The docx is correct on this point; the typst reference is not a valid comparison for paragraph breaks until it is fixed.
