## Document-type notes

- **Adversary complaint:** FRBP 7001 classifies proceedings requiring an adversary complaint; FRBP 7003 applies FRCP 3; FRBP 7008 applies FRCP 8 and adds bankruptcy-case and consent allegations; FRBP 7010 applies FRCP 10 subject to the Official Form caption. Separate filings include the summons and Official Form 1040 cover sheet.
- **Motion brief:** LBR 9013-1 supplies the notice and objection period; LBR 7007-1 adds the adversary-motion conference certification and 20-page limit. General motions are not subject to that adversary limit unless another rule or order applies.
- **Open convention:** the local rules prescribe minimum type size, not a font family.
