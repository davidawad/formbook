## Document-type notes

- **Complaint:** Fed. R. Civ. P. 8 and 10 apply. A civil cover sheet, summons, Rule 7.1 disclosure statement, and any specialized AO 120/AO 121 notice are separate docket entries where required; they are not cover-page components of the complaint template.
- **Motion brief:** LR 7 requires a combined motion and supporting memorandum. The 11,000-word/35-page alternatives apply to non-discovery motion memoranda; discovery motions have a separate 3,000-word/10-page limit under LR 26-3(b), excluding exhibits. A judge's standing order may govern the assigned case.
- **Open convention:** the court rules cited here do not prescribe a font family. The templates do not represent a particular typeface as mandated.
