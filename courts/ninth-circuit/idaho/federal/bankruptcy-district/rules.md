## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDB-FORM-001 | Paper is 8.5 × 11 inches. | LBR 5005-1(a) | M |
| IDB-FORM-002 | Counsel block begins upper left one inch from top and includes name, Idaho bar number, email, address, telephone, facsimile, and party represented. | LBR 5005-1(c)(1) | M |
| IDB-FORM-003 | Type is 12 to 14 point; the rule specifies no typeface. | LBR 5005-1(c)(2) | M |
| IDB-CONT-001 | Caption states United States Bankruptcy Court, District of Idaho, and includes case number with judge suffix and paper designation. | LBR 5005-1(c)(3); FRBP 9004(b) | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDB-COVER-001 | Complaint is accompanied by Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet, filing fee, and summons. | LBR 7003-1; FRBP 7003-7004 | M |
| IDB-CONT-002 | Complaint follows applicable adversary pleading caption requirements; states the bankruptcy case and whether pleader consents to final orders/judgment where required. | LBR 7003-1; FRBP 7008, 7010, 9004(b) | M |
| IDB-CONT-003 | Allegations use numbered paragraphs. | FRCP 10(b) as applied by FRBP 7010 | M |

## Contested-matter motion

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|

| IDB-CONT-005 | A witness-testimony statement is provided for contested-matter hearings. | LBR 9014-1 | M (author content) |
