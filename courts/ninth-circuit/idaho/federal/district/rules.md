## Court-wide

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDD-FORM-001 | Letter size, plainly typed, double-spaced except quotations and footnotes; consecutive page numbering; margins at least one inch; all text including footnotes at least 12 pt. | Loc. Civ. R. 5.2(a) | M |
| IDD-FORM-002 | Upper-left counsel block includes attorney name, email, bar number, office address, telephone, fax, and party represented. | Loc. Civ. R. 5.2(b) | M |
| IDD-FORM-003 | Caption begins 4 inches below top of first page, except additional counsel-identification space; contains court title, action title, CM/ECF number including division/year/case/judge initials, and paper title. | Loc. Civ. R. 5.2(b)(1)-(5) | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDD-CONT-001 | Complaint states short and plain jurisdiction, claim, and relief. | FRCP 8(a) | M |
| IDD-CONT-002 | Claims use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| IDD-CONT-003 | Caption contains court, action title, case number, and document designation. | FRCP 10(a); Loc. Civ. R. 5.2(b) | M |
| IDD-COVER-001 | A jury demand appears immediately after the pleading title as “Demand For Jury Trial” or equivalent. | Loc. Civ. R. 38.1 | M (if demand made) |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDD-CONT-004 | Motion is accompanied by a separate brief stating all supporting reasons and authorities. | Loc. Civ. R. 7.1(b)(1) | M |
| IDD-CONT-005 | A supporting memorandum is no longer than 20 pages; small fonts or minimal spacing to evade the limit are prohibited. | Loc. Civ. R. 7.1(a)(2) | M |
| IDD-CONT-006 | A supporting document is labeled in its caption with the docket number of the motion. | Loc. Civ. R. 7.1(a)(3) | M |
