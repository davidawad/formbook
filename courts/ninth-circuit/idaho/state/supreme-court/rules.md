# Idaho Supreme Court: appellate filings

Alias `idaho`. Scope: briefs, petitions for review and rehearing, motions and amicus briefs filed in the Idaho Supreme Court.

Verified against the Idaho Appellate Rules (I.A.R. 34, 36, 42, 110 and 118) as published by the Idaho Supreme Court on isc.idaho.gov on 2026-10-21.

Scope note: I.A.R. 36 is written for briefs. It is applied to every brief filed in the Supreme Court, including the brief in support of a petition for review (I.A.R. 118(c)) and the brief in support of a petition for rehearing (I.A.R. 42(b), which adds letter size paper). Petitions and motions have no separate form rule in the fetched text; their rows below are conventions (`C`) or are marked not retrieved.

## Petitions for review and their briefs in support

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDAHO-PET-001 | A petition for review is filed within 21 days after the Court of Appeals opinion or order. | IAR118 (a) | M |
| IDAHO-PET-002 | The brief in support addresses the criteria for review in IAR118(b); discussion is limited to those criteria. | IAR118 (c)(1) | M |
| IDAHO-PET-003 | The brief in support follows the form rules of IDAHO-FORM-001 to -007 and the length rule of IDAHO-LEN-001. | IAR36 (b), IAR34 (b) | M |
