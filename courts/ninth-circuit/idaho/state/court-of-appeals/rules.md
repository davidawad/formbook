# Idaho Court of Appeals: appellate briefs

Alias `idahoctapp`. Scope: opening, answering and reply briefs in appeals assigned to the Court of Appeals.

Verified against the Idaho Appellate Rules (I.A.R. 34 and 36) as published by the Idaho Supreme Court on isc.idaho.gov on 2026-10-21.

## Sources

| Tag | Source | URL |
|---|---|---|
| IAR34 | Idaho Appellate Rules, Rule 34 (briefs: number, length, time for filing) | https://isc.idaho.gov/rules-procedure/print/iar/34 |
| IAR36 | Idaho Appellate Rules, Rule 36 (form of briefs: cover, formatting, typeface, type size) | https://isc.idaho.gov/rules-procedure/print/iar/36 |

Scope note: the fetched text of I.A.R. 34 and 36 does not state its application to the Court of Appeals in a separate sentence; the exemplars in `usage/` are captioned for the Supreme Court (I.A.R. 110, not fetched). Both rules are treated as applying to the briefs here.

## Opening brief (appellant's brief) and answering brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-001 | Briefs are prepared so that, if printed, every page is 8 1/2 x 11 inches. | IAR36 (b)(1) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-002 | Lines of text are double spaced; headings, footnotes and block quotations may be single spaced. | IAR36 (b)(1) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-003 | Margins are at least one inch on all sides. | IAR36 (b)(1) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-004 | Page numbers are required and appear in the bottom margin. | IAR36 (b)(1) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-005 | Typeface is proportionally spaced, Times New Roman or Century Schoolbook, in black; italics or boldface may be used for emphasis. | IAR36 (b)(2) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-006 | Case names are italicized or underlined. | IAR36 (b)(2) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-007 | Type size is 14-point or larger for both text and footnotes. | IAR36 (b)(3) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-LEN-001 | The first brief (opening brief or answering brief) contains no more than 14,000 words. Headings, footnotes and quoted material count; the cover, caption page, tables, signature block, certificates and appendices do not. | IAR34 (b)(1)-(2) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-CERT-001 | The brief includes a certificate of compliance with the word-count limit that states the number of words in the document, placed immediately before the submitter's signature. | IAR34 (b)(4) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-CONT-001 | Cover states the title of the Supreme Court, the title of the action, whether the brief is appellant's or respondent's, the district court or agency appealed from, the trial judge or chairman who presided, and the names and addresses of all counsel of record showing for whom they appear. | IAR36 (a) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-CONT-002 | Table of contents and table of authorities precede the argument. | exemplars (FILED-EXAMPLE-opening-brief, FILED-EXAMPLE-answering-brief) | C |
| IDAHOCTAPP-CERT-002 | Certificate of mailing or service states the date, the documents served, the method, and the recipients, and is signed by the filer. Exemplars from different filers show it. | exemplars (FILED-EXAMPLE-opening-brief, FILED-EXAMPLE-reply-brief) | C |

## Reply brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-101 | The reply brief uses the same page, margin, spacing, typeface, case-name and type-size rules as the opening brief (IDAHOCTAPP-FORM-001 to -007). | IAR36 (b) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-LEN-101 | A reply brief contains no more than 7,000 words, counted and certified as in IDAHOCTAPP-LEN-001 and IDAHOCTAPP-CERT-001. | IAR34 (b)(1), (b)(4) | M |
| IDAHOCTAPP-CONT-101 | Cover follows IDAHOCTAPP-CONT-001. | IAR36 (a) | M |
