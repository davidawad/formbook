# Nevada Supreme Court: briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `nev`. Scope: the briefs (opening, answering, reply, amicus), motions, petitions for rehearing and en banc reconsideration, petitions for review and petitions for extraordinary writs filed in the Nevada Supreme Court. The court's rules are the Nevada Rules of Appellate Procedure (NRAP). Every doc folder under this one takes its numbers from the rows below; its `typst/layout.yaml` cites them by ID.

The Legislature's copy (https://www.leg.state.nv.us/CourtRules/NRAP.html) and nvcourts.gov answer HTTP 403 (Cloudflare challenge) to a script; the text below was read from the Internet Archive's capture of the Legislature's page of 24 February 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250224154012/https://www.leg.state.nv.us/courtrules/nrap.html), which prints NRAP 32 "as amended; effective October 1, 2015" and the other rules with their own amendment notes.
