# Court of Appeals of Nevada: briefs, motions and writ petitions

Alias `nevapp`. Scope: the opening, answering and reply briefs, motions, and petitions for extraordinary writs filed in the Court of Appeals. The Court of Appeals hears the cases the Supreme Court assigns to it (NRAP 17(b)) and applies the same Nevada Rules of Appellate Procedure (NRAP) as the Supreme Court. Petitions for review of Court of Appeals decisions go to the Supreme Court (NRAP 40B) and are covered by the supreme-court folder, not this one. Every doc folder under this one takes its numbers from the rows below; its `typst/` template cites them by ID.

The Legislature's live copy (https://www.leg.state.nv.us/CourtRules/NRAP.html) answers HTTP 403 to a script, so the text below was read from the Internet Archive's capture of the Legislature's NRAP page of 24 February 2025 (https://web.archive.org/web/20250224154012/https://www.leg.state.nv.us/courtrules/nrap.html). The capture prints each rule with its own amendment note. No Court of Appeals local rule was found; the Court of Appeals applies the NRAP. Re-verify against the live Legislature copy before the next revision.

## Cover and caption

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEVAPP-COVER-001 | The cover of a brief: the name of the court and the case number; the title of the case; the nature of the proceeding and the court below; the title of the document (e.g. Appellant's Opening Brief, Respondent's Answering Brief); the names, addresses, telephone numbers and State Bar of Nevada numbers of counsel. | NRAP 32(a)(2)(A)-(E) | M |
| NEVAPP-COVER-002 | Brief covers are colored by party: blue for appellant, red for respondent, green for intervenor or amicus, gray for reply. A cover is required on a brief. | NRAP 32(a)(2) | M |
| NEVAPP-COVER-003 | A motion needs no cover, but a caption with the name of the court and the docket number, the title of the case, and a brief descriptive title indicating the purpose of the motion and identifying the party for whom it is filed. A writ petition or other paper the same caption. | NRAP 27(d)(1)(B); 32(c)(2) | M |

## Briefs: content and order

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEVAPP-ORDER-001 | An opening brief, in order: the NRAP 26.1 disclosure, a table of contents with page references, a table of authorities (cases alphabetically arranged, statutes, other authorities) with the pages where cited, the jurisdictional statement, the routing statement (whether the matter is presumptively retained by the Supreme Court or assigned to the Court of Appeals under NRAP 17, citing the subparagraph), issues, statement of the case, statement of facts, summary of the argument, argument, conclusion, and the attorney's certificate. | NRAP 28(a)(1)-(12); 28.2 | T |
| NEVAPP-ORDER-002 | An answering brief is entitled "Respondent's Answering Brief" and conforms to NRAP 28(a)(1)-(10) and (12), except that the jurisdictional, routing, issues, case, facts and standard-of-review statements need not appear unless the respondent is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement. | NRAP 28(b)(1)-(6) | T |
| NEVAPP-ORDER-003 | A reply brief is entitled "Appellant's Reply Brief", complies with NRAP 28(a)(1)-(2) and (10), and is limited to answering new matter in the opposing brief; no further briefs are filed unless the court permits. | NRAP 28(c) | T |
| NEVAPP-CERT-001 | The certificate of compliance: compliance with the typeface and type style requirements of NRAP 32(a)(4)-(6), naming the typeface and type style, and with the page- or type-volume limit (with the word count when it relies on one). | NRAP 32(a)(9)(A)-(B) | T |
| NEVAPP-CERT-002 | The attorney's certificate (Form 9): the attorney has read the brief; it is not frivolous or interposed for an improper purpose; it complies with the NRAP, including the record references of NRAP 28(e); it complies with NRAP 32(a)(4)-(6) and the page- or type-volume limit. The certificate may be combined with the NRAP 32(a)(9) certificate. | NRAP 28.2(a)(1)-(4); 32(a)(9)(C) | T |
| NEVAPP-CERT-003 | Proof of service with every paper filed: the date and manner of service, the names of the persons served, and their addresses or electronic addresses. | NRAP 25(d)(1)(B) | M |

## Length

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEVAPP-LEN-001 | An opening or answering brief: 30 pages, or 14,000 words; a reply brief: 15 pages or 7,000 words (the disclosure, table of contents, table of authorities, certificates, and addendum excluded). | NRAP 32(a)(7)(A), (C) | M |
| NEVAPP-LEN-002 | Enlargement only on motion made by the brief's due date, showing diligence and good cause, with a declaration and a single copy of the proposed brief. | NRAP 32(a)(7)(D) | M |

## Motions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEVAPP-MOT-001 | A motion or response to a motion: 10 pages; a reply to a response: 5 pages, unless the court permits otherwise. | NRAP 27(d)(2) | M |
| NEVAPP-MOT-002 | A motion states with particularity the grounds, the relief sought and the legal argument; affidavits and papers supporting it are served and filed with it. | NRAP 27(a)(2) | M |
| NEVAPP-MOT-003 | A response is due within 7 days after service of the motion; a reply within 7 days after service of the response. | NRAP 27(a)(3)(A), (a)(4) | M |
| NEVAPP-MOT-004 | A motion to reissue an unpublished order as an opinion (NRAP 36(f)) is filed within 14 days after the order is filed, states on which publication criterion of NRAP 36(c)(1)(A)-(C) it rests, and, if filed by a nonparty, identifies the movant. The form of the motion follows NRAP 27(d). | NRAP 36(f)(1), (3); 27(d) | M |
| NEVAPP-MOT-005 | A motion that relies on a word count carries a certificate of the word count. The Rule 27(d)(2) limit in the capture is a page limit only; no motion word limit is printed there, so this row applies only if a current rule states one. | NRAP 27(d)(2); 32(a)(9)(B) | A |
| NEVAPP-MOT-006 | A caption that names the Court of Appeals of the State of Nevada, the docket number, the case title, and the party on whose behalf the motion is filed. | NRAP 27(d)(1)(B) | M |
| NEVAPP-MOT-007 | The motion's form (typeface, type style, spacing, margins and page numbers) follows NRAP 27(d)(1)(D)-(E), which apply the NRAP 32(a)(4)-(6) requirements, including the 14-point proportional typeface. | NRAP 27(d)(1)(D)-(E); 32(a)(5)(A) | M |
