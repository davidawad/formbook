## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NVB-FORM-001 | Letter paper; double-spaced text; consecutive line numbers in left margin. Exemptions include exhibits, footnotes, quotations, attorney identification, caption, court title, and case name. | LBR 9004(a) | M |
| NVB-FORM-002 | Use proportional font at least 12 point; pages numbered consecutively. | LBR 9004(b) | M |
| NVB-FORM-003 | First-page title begins at least 1.5 inches below top; attorney identification is upper left. | LBR 9004(a), (g) | M |
| NVB-CERT-001 | Signature typed beneath signature space; electronic filing uses `/s/` and filer's typed name. | LBR 9004(c) | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NVB-CONT-001 | Adversary caption includes both bankruptcy case caption and adversary number. | LBR 7010(b) | M |
| NVB-CONT-002 | Adversary complaint substantially conforms to Official Form 416 and uses FRCP 10 caption requirements. | FRBP 7010; LBR 7010 | M |
| NVB-CONT-003 | First pleading states whether pleader consents to final orders or judgment by bankruptcy judge. | FRBP 7008; LBR 7008 | M |
| NVB-COVER-001 | Complaint is accompanied by adversary cover sheet and summons. | FRBP 7003-7004 | M |

## Contested-matter motion

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NVB-CONT-004 | Notice of hearing is a separate filing and states date, time, place, relief, objection deadline, and prescribed warning. | LBR 9014(b)(1) | M |
| NVB-CERT-002 | Proof of service is filed within seven days of motion. | LBR 9014(b)(2) | M |
| NVB-CONT-005 | Motion states supporting facts and includes legal memorandum; no prescribed order of contents. | LBR 9014(c)(1) | M |
| NVB-LEN-001 | Opening brief or points and authorities limited to 20 pages, excluding exhibits, tables, and caption. | LBR 9014(e)(1) | M |
| NVB-CONT-006 | First pleading, motion, or paper in contested matter states whether pleader consents to final orders or judgments by bankruptcy judge. | LBR 7008 | M |
