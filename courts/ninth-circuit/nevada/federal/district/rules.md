## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NVD-FORM-001 | Text is double-spaced; printed lines are consecutively numbered down the left margin, no more than 28 lines per page. Caption, court title, case name, quotations, footnotes, and attorney identification are exempt from double spacing. | LR IA 10-1(a)(1) | M |
| NVD-FORM-002 | Legible, one-sided pages; text at least 12 pt; all pages numbered consecutively; margins at least one inch. | LR IA 10-1(a)(2)-(6) | M |
| NVD-FORM-003 | Paper size is 8.5 × 11 inches. | LR IA 10-1(c) | M |
| NVD-FORM-004 | First page begins at least 1.5 inches below top; counsel identification at top left includes name, Nevada bar number, address, telephone, email, and party represented. | LR IA 10-2 | M |
| NVD-CONT-001 | First-page court title reads “United States District Court / District of Nevada”; caption shows parties at left, case number and paper title at right. | LR IA 10-2 | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NVD-CONT-002 | Caption includes court, title, file number, and document designation; claims use numbered paragraphs. | FRCP 10(a)-(b) | M |
| NVD-COVER-001 | Jury demand is in capital letters on the first page immediately below the pleading title. | LR 38-1 | M (if demanded) |
| NVD-COVER-002 | New civil action includes civil cover sheet; disclosure statement is filed with first appearance/pleading/motion as applicable. | LR 3-1; LR 7.1-1 | M (separate filings) |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NVD-CONT-003 | Motion and supporting points-and-authorities memorandum are combined into one document. | LR 7-2(a) | M |
| NVD-LEN-001 | Motions other than summary judgment are limited to 24 pages, excluding exhibits. | LR 7-3(b) | M |
| NVD-COVER-003 | Notice of hearing is a separate document stating date, time, place, relief description, objection deadline, and prescribed warning. | LR 9014(b)(1) | M |
| NVD-CONT-004 | Motion states facts and includes legal memorandum. | LR 9014(c)(1) | M |
| NVD-LEN-002 | Opening brief or points and authorities in a contested matter is limited to 20 pages, excluding exhibits, tables, and caption. | LR 9014(e)(1) | M |
| NVD-CERT-001 | Proof of service is filed within seven days of the motion. | LR 9014(b)(2) | M |
