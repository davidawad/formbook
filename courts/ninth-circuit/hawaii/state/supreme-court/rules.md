# Supreme Court of Hawaii: briefs, applications, petitions and motions

Alias `haw`. Scope: opening, answering, reply and supplemental briefs on the merits, amicus curiae briefs, motions with supporting memoranda, applications for writ of certiorari (`petition-for-review`), applications for transfer (`petition-for-discretionary-review`), petitions for writs (`petition-for-writ`), and motions for reconsideration (`petition-for-rehearing`). Verified against the Hawaii Rules of Appellate Procedure (HRAP, Judiciary PDF release 06/25, `hrap_ada.pdf`, read from the court's copy) on 2026-10-22. The Supreme Court applies the HRAP; there are no separate Supreme Court form rules in the sources read.

## Form of every paper (HRAP 32(a)-(c))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAW-FORM-001 | Documents are prepared to display, print and copy clearly and legibly, on 8-1/2 x 11 inch pages in portrait orientation. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAW-FORM-002 | Except for the flyleaf, margins are not less than 1 inch on all sides. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAW-FORM-003 | Font size is the equivalent of standard 12 point pica and yields no more than 14 characters to the inch. Twelve point Times New Roman, Courier New or Arial is deemed to satisfy the rule. No attempt is made to reduce or condense print. | HRAP 32(b) | M |
| HAW-FORM-004 | Footnotes and quotations are in the same font and size as the text (12 point). | HRAP 32(b) | M |
| HAW-FORM-005 | Lines are double spaced or one-and-one-half spaced, except in headings, quotations, citations, indexes, footnotes and appendices (those may be single spaced). | HRAP 32(b) | M |
| HAW-FORM-006 | Documents are filed without covers; the flyleaf (HAW-COVER-001) is the first page. Conventionally filed copies are unruled, unglazed, opaque white paper and are not bound, stapled or tabbed. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAW-FORM-007 | Pages are numbered consecutively; the rule does not say where. The exemplars number the pages at the bottom center. | (none) | C |

## Opening brief (HRAP 28(a), (b))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAW-OPEN-001 | The opening brief contains, in this order: (1) a subject index with page references and a table of authorities; (3) a concise statement of the case, with record references; (4) a concise statement of the points of error, separately numbered; (5) a separate section entitled "Standard of Review"; (7) the argument; (8) the relevant constitutional provisions, statutes, ordinances, treaties, regulations or rules, set out verbatim unless they are long; (9) a conclusion specifying the relief sought; (10) an appendix, if any; (11) a statement of related cases. Sections (2) and (6) are reserved. | HRAP 28(b)(1), (3)-(5), (7)-(11) | M |
| HAW-OPEN-002 | Each point of error states the alleged error, where in the record it occurred, and where it was objected to or brought to the court's attention; a point on admission or rejection of evidence, a jury instruction or a finding quotes the ground, instruction or finding as the rule requires. | HRAP 28(b)(4) | M |
| HAW-OPEN-003 | Citations to Hawaii cases since statehood give the state and regional reporters. | HRAP 28(b)(1) | M |
| HAW-OPEN-004 | The statement of related cases is on a separate page entitled "Statement of Related Cases" and is the last page of the brief, after the appendices. | HRAP 28(b)(11) | M |
| HAW-OPEN-005 | A copy of the judgment, decree, findings of fact and conclusions of law, order, opinion or decision relevant to any point on appeal is appended, unless the court orders otherwise. | HRAP 28(b)(3) | M |
| HAW-OPEN-006 | Excluding indexes, appendices and statements of related cases, an opening brief does not exceed 35 pages, except after leave. Not machine-checked (DEF-LEN-001 counts the cover and tables, so no page limit is set). | HRAP 28(a) | M |
| HAW-OPEN-007 | A brief raising ineffective assistance of counsel as a point of error is served on the attorney alleged to have been ineffective. | HRAP 28(a) | M |

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAW-ANS-001 | The answering brief is of like character to the opening brief, except that no statement of points is required; no other section is required unless the section presented in the opening brief is controverted. | HRAP 28(c) | M |
| HAW-ANS-002 | The answering brief is filed within 40 days after service of the opening brief (or receipt of acknowledgment, or docketing, whichever is later). Not machine-checked. | HRAP 28(c) | M |
| HAW-ANS-003 | Excluding indexes, appendices and statements of related cases, an answering brief does not exceed 35 pages, except after leave. Not machine-checked. | HRAP 28(a) | M |
| HAW-REPLY-001 | The reply brief is confined to matters presented in the answering brief and conforms to HRAP 32. It does not exceed 10 pages, exclusive of indexes, appendices and statements of related cases, except after leave. Not machine-checked (DEF-LEN-001 counts the cover and tables). | HRAP 28(a), (d) | M |
| HAW-REPLY-002 | The reply brief's contents are otherwise not enumerated by HRAP 28; the exemplar has a subject index and table of authorities, which the template follows. | HRAP 28(d) | C |

## Motions and supporting memoranda (HRAP 27)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAW-MOT-001 | A motion is made by a written motion, with proof of service on all parties, that states with particularity the grounds and the order or relief sought. Supporting memoranda, affidavits and declarations are served and filed with the motion. | HRAP 27(a) | M |
| HAW-MOT-002 | A motion (other than a motion for reconsideration under HRAP 40) is no more than 3 pages, including an abbreviated caption and signature. Not machine-checked. | HRAP 27(d) | M |
| HAW-MOT-003 | A memorandum in support or opposition is no more than 20 pages, including abbreviated captions and signatures. Only affidavits, declarations and documents necessary to decide the motion are attached. Not machine-checked (the motion-brief folder holds the motion and its memorandum together). | HRAP 27(d) | M |
| HAW-MOT-004 | The motion, its memorandum and any attachments conform to HRAP 32 (HAW-FORM-001 to -006, HAW-SIG-001 and -002). | HRAP 32 | M |

## Application for writ of certiorari (`petition-for-review`) (HRAP 40.1)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAW-CERT-001 | The application for a writ of certiorari is filed with the appellate clerk with proof of service, and is denominated with the petitioner and respondents as the rule describes. | HRAP 40.1(a)-(c) | M |
| HAW-CERT-002 | The application does not exceed 12 pages and contains its sections in the order HRAP 40.1(d) gives, beginning with the questions presented. The decision of the intermediate court of appeals (or other decision) is attached as an appendix. | HRAP 40.1(d) | M |
| HAW-CERT-003 | A response, if filed, does not exceed 12 pages; a reply to the response does not exceed 5 pages. Not machine-checked. | HRAP 40.1(e) | M |

## Petition for writ (`petition-for-writ`) (HRAP 21)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAW-WRIT-001 | A petition for a writ of mandamus or prohibition directed to a judge is filed with the appellate clerk with proof of service on the respondent judge, all parties to the trial-court action and the attorney general. It contains a statement of the facts necessary to an understanding of the issues, a statement of the issues and the relief sought, and a statement of reasons for issuing the writ. Copies of the relevant orders or opinions are attached. | HRAP 21(a) | M |
| HAW-WRIT-002 | The petition conforms to HRAP 32. Page limits are not stated in HRAP 21; the template does not set one. | HRAP 21, 32 | M |

## Motion for reconsideration (`petition-for-rehearing`) (HRAP 40)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAW-RECON-001 | A motion for reconsideration may be filed by a party only within 10 days after the filing of the opinion, dispositional order or ruling, unless additional time is granted by a judge or justice. | HRAP 40(a) | M |
| HAW-RECON-002 | The motion states with particularity the points of law or fact the moving party contends the court has overlooked or misapprehended, with a brief argument on the points raised, and is supported by a declaration of counsel that it is presented in good faith and not for delay. | HRAP 40(b) | M |
| HAW-RECON-003 | Only one motion for reconsideration is permitted. | HRAP 40(e) | M |
| HAW-RECON-004 | The motion conforms to HRAP 32 (HAW-FORM-001 to -006, HAW-SIG-001 and -002). | HRAP 32 | M |
