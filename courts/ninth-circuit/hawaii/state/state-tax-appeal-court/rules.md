# Hawaii State Tax Appeal Court: motions, notices of appeal and briefs

Alias `hawtax`. Scope: motions with supporting memoranda (`motion-brief`), the notice of appeal to the Tax Appeal Court (`petition-for-review`, the exemplar's form), and amicus curiae briefs (`amicus-brief`). The Tax Appeal Court is a trial-level court (HRS 232-16, 232-17); its decisions are appealed to the Intermediate Court of Appeals, so the appellate briefs of the ICA and Supreme Court are not filed here. Verified against the Rules of the Tax Appeal Court of the State of Hawai'i (RTAC, ADA-accessible HTML, September 2024 release) on 2026-10-22. Where RTAC is silent, RTAC 1(b) applies the Hawai'i Rules of Civil Procedure and the Rules of the Circuit Courts, in that order; neither was retrieved from a primary source, so their form rules are not stated below.

## Briefs (RTAC 16)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWTAX-BRIEF-001 | Briefs the court requests contain, in this order: (a) a statement of the nature of the tax and how the proceeding comes before the court; (b) a concise statement of the facts, with references to the transcript pages or exhibits relied on; (c) a concise statement of the points on which the party relies; (d) the argument. | RTAC 16 | M |
| HAWTAX-BRIEF-002 | Unless the court allows otherwise, no brief exceeds 40 pages, including appendices. | RTAC 16 | M |
| HAWTAX-BRIEF-003 | A brief of more than 20 pages has on its front flyleaf a table of contents with page references, and a list of all cases referred to, alphabetically arranged, with the pages where each is cited. | RTAC 16 | M |
