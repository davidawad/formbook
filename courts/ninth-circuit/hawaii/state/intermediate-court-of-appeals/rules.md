## Form of every paper (HRAP 32(a)-(c))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWAPP-FORM-001 | Documents are prepared to display, print and copy clearly and legibly, on 8-1/2 x 11 inch pages in portrait orientation. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-FORM-002 | Except for the flyleaf, margins are not less than 1 inch on all sides. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-FORM-003 | Font size is the equivalent of standard 12 point pica and yields no more than 14 characters to the inch. Twelve point Times New Roman, Courier New or Arial is deemed to satisfy the rule. No attempt is made to reduce or condense print. | HRAP 32(b) | M |
| HAWAPP-FORM-004 | Footnotes and quotations are in the same font and size as the text (12 point). | HRAP 32(b) | M |
| HAWAPP-FORM-005 | Lines are double spaced or one-and-one-half spaced, except in headings, quotations, citations, indexes, footnotes and appendices (those may be single spaced). | HRAP 32(b) | M |
| HAWAPP-FORM-006 | Documents are filed without covers; the flyleaf (HAWAPP-COVER-001) is the first page. Conventionally filed copies are unruled, unglazed, opaque white paper and are not bound, stapled or tabbed. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-FORM-007 | Pages are numbered consecutively; the rule does not say where. The exemplars number the pages at the bottom center. | (none) | C |

## Flyleaf (cover) (HRAP 32(a))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWAPP-COVER-001 | The flyleaf notes, beginning at the top: the case number; the title of the appellate court and the cause, abbreviated or in full; the character of the document; the name or names, telephone numbers, physical and electronic mail addresses and Hawaii bar number of the attorney or attorneys for the party filing; and the names of all judges or hearing officers who participated in the case or matter. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-COVER-002 | The flyleaf has either a 3-inch top margin or a 3-inch-by-3-inch space in the top right corner. | HRAP 32(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-COVER-003 | The cover layout (caption block, title in capitals, counsel block) is the house convention seen in the exemplars; the rule fixes the content, not the arrangement. | HRAP 32(a) | C |

## Opening brief (HRAP 28(a), (b))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWAPP-OPEN-001 | The opening brief contains, in this order: (1) a subject index with page references and a table of authorities (cases alphabetically, textbooks, articles, statutes, treatises, regulations and rules, with the pages where each is cited); (3) a concise statement of the case, with record references; (4) a concise statement of the points of error, separately numbered; (5) a separate section entitled "Standard of Review"; (7) the argument; (8) the relevant constitutional provisions, statutes, ordinances, treaties, regulations or rules, set out verbatim unless they are long; (9) a conclusion specifying the relief sought; (10) an appendix, if any; (11) a statement of related cases. Sections (2) and (6) are reserved. | HRAP 28(b)(1), (3)-(5), (7)-(11) | M |
| HAWAPP-OPEN-002 | Each point of error states the alleged error, where in the record it occurred, and where it was objected to or brought to the court's attention; a point on admission or rejection of evidence, a jury instruction or a finding quotes the ground, instruction or finding as the rule requires. | HRAP 28(b)(4) | M |
| HAWAPP-OPEN-003 | Citations to Hawaii cases since statehood give the state and regional reporters. | HRAP 28(b)(1) | M |
| HAWAPP-OPEN-004 | The statement of related cases is on a separate page entitled "Statement of Related Cases" and is the last page of the brief, after the appendices. If no other case is related, a single statement says so. | HRAP 28(b)(11) | M |
| HAWAPP-OPEN-005 | A copy of the judgment, decree, findings of fact and conclusions of law, order, or opinion relevant to any point on appeal is appended, unless the court orders otherwise. | HRAP 28(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-OPEN-006 | Excluding indexes, appendices and the statement of related cases, an opening brief is no longer than 35 pages, except after leave. Not machine-checked (DEF-LEN-001 counts the cover and tables, so no page limit is set). | HRAP 28(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-OPEN-007 | A brief raising ineffective assistance of counsel as a point of error is served on the attorney alleged to have been ineffective. | HRAP 28(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-OPEN-008 | A concise summary may precede the argument. | HRAP 28(b)(7) | A |

## Answering brief (HRAP 28(c))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWAPP-ANS-001 | The answering brief is of like character to the opening brief, except that no statement of points is required; no other section is required unless the section presented in the opening brief is controverted. | HRAP 28(c) | M |
| HAWAPP-ANS-002 | The answering brief is filed within 40 days after service of the opening brief (or receipt of acknowledgment, or docketing, whichever is later). | HRAP 28(c) | M |
| HAWAPP-ANS-003 | Excluding indexes, appendices and related-case statements, an answering brief is no longer than 35 pages, except after leave. Not machine-checked. | HRAP 28(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-ANS-004 | An appellee who learns of a related case not previously identified files a statement of related cases conforming to HRAP 28(b)(11). | HRAP 28(c) | M |

## Reply brief (HRAP 28(a), (d))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWAPP-REPLY-001 | The reply brief is confined to matters presented in the answering brief. It is filed within 14 days after service of the answering brief (or receipt of acknowledgment, or docketing, whichever is later). | HRAP 28(d) | M |
| HAWAPP-REPLY-002 | Excluding indexes, appendices and related-case statements, a reply brief is no longer than 10 pages, except after leave. Not machine-checked (DEF-LEN-001 counts the cover and tables). | HRAP 28(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-REPLY-003 | The reply brief conforms to HRAP 32. Its contents are otherwise not enumerated by HRAP 28; the exemplar has a subject index and table of authorities, which the template follows. | HRAP 28(a), (d) | C |

## Motions and supporting memoranda (HRAP 27)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWAPP-MOT-001 | A motion is made by a written motion, with proof of service on all parties, that states with particularity the grounds and the order or relief sought, and is accompanied by any matter a specific rule requires. Supporting memoranda, affidavits and declarations are served and filed with the motion. | HRAP 27(a) | M |
| HAWAPP-MOT-002 | A motion (other than a motion for reconsideration under HRAP 40) is no more than 3 pages, including an abbreviated caption and signature. Not machine-checked. | HRAP 27(d) | M |
| HAWAPP-MOT-003 | A memorandum in support or opposition is no more than 20 pages, including abbreviated captions and signatures. Only affidavits, declarations and documents necessary to decide the motion are attached. Not machine-checked (the motion-brief folder holds the motion and its memorandum together). | HRAP 27(d) | M |
| HAWAPP-MOT-004 | The motion, its memorandum and any attachments conform to HRAP 32 (HAWAPP-FORM-001 to -006, HAWAPP-SIG-001 and -002). | HRAP 32 | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs (HRAP 28(g))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAWAPP-AMICUS-001 | An amicus curiae brief may be filed only by leave of the appellate court, and the order granting leave fixes the time for filing. The Attorney General may file without leave in the cases HRAP 28(g) describes. | HRAP 28(g) | M |
| HAWAPP-AMICUS-002 | An amicus brief complies with the applicable provisions of HRAP 28(b) (the sections of an opening brief, as applicable to the amicus's position) and with HRAP 32. | HRAP 28(g) | M |
| HAWAPP-AMICUS-003 | The 35-page limit of HRAP 28(a) is stated for opening and answering briefs; the rule does not state a separate limit for an amicus brief. Not machine-checked. | HRAP 28(a) | A |
| HAWAPP-AMICUS-004 | The amicus brief has a subject index with page references and a table of authorities, as HRAP 28(b)(1) requires for the sections it adopts. | HRAP 28(g), (b)(1) | M |
