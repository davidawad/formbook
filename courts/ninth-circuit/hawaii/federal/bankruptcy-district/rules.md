## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIB-FORM-001 | Paper size is 8.5 × 11 inches with one-inch margins. | LBR 9004-1(a)(2), (b)(3) | M |
| HIB-FORM-002 | Document text is double-spaced except counsel identification, title of case, quotations, footnotes, and exhibits. | LBR 9004-1(b)(2) | M |
| HIB-FORM-003 | All memoranda, including footnotes, use 14-point type. The rule does not name a typeface. | LBR 9004-1(b)(1) | M |
| HIB-FORM-004 | Counsel identification is single-spaced. | LBR 9004-1(b)(2) | M |
| HIB-CONT-001 | Caption identifies court, parties, case number and document designation. | FRBP 9004(b) | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIB-FORM-005 | Complaint caption substantially conforms to the applicable Director's Form 416; parties are named under FRCP 10(a). | FRBP 7010; FRCP 10(a) | M |
| HIB-CONT-002 | Complaint pleads jurisdiction, claim, and relief; jurisdiction allegation identifies bankruptcy case name, number, chapter, district and division, and whether filer consents to final orders or judgment. | FRBP 7008; FRCP 8(a) | M |
| HIB-CONT-003 | Allegations use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b), applied by FRBP 7010 | M |
| HIB-COVER-001 | A complaint initiating an adversary proceeding is accompanied by the Director's Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet. | LBR 7003-1 | M |
| HIB-ELEC-001 | Exhibit pages are separated from pleading text by a page identifying the exhibit; no tabs are used. Exhibits are exempt from LBR 9004-1(b) formatting rules. | LBR 9004-1(a)(4), (b) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIB-FORM-006 | A memorandum is 14-point, double-spaced except stated exemptions, on 8.5 × 11 paper with one-inch margins. | LBR 9004-1(a)(2), (b)(1)-(3) | M |
| HIB-CONT-004 | Motion is written and states grounds with particularity and the requested relief. | FRBP 9013(a)-(b) | M |
| HIB-CONT-005 | Notice of hearing is given at least 28 days before hearing and substantially conforms to local Notice of Hearing form. | LBR 9013-1(c)(1) | M |
| HIB-LEN-001 | Supporting memorandum is limited to 25 pages or, alternatively, 6,250 words with the required certificate. | LBR 9013-2(a)(1), (4) | M |
| HIB-CONT-006 | Briefs and memoranda over 10 pages include a table of contents and table of authorities. | LBR 9013-2(b) | M |
