## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HID-FORM-001 | Use white opaque 8.5 × 11 inch paper, one-inch margins, one-sided, flat and unbound. | LR 10.1; LR 10.2(a)(1) | M |
| HID-FORM-002 | Documents are double-spaced except counsel identification, case title, footnotes, quotations, and exhibits. | LR 10.2(a)(4) | M |
| HID-FORM-003 | Counsel's name, Hawaii bar number, address, telephone, email, and represented party appear at upper left of the first page. | LR 10.2(b) | M |
| HID-FORM-004 | After counsel identification, show court title, action title, case number with judge initials, then document title. | LR 10.2(c)(1)-(4) | M |
| HID-FORM-005 | Memoranda, including footnotes, use exactly 14-point Times New Roman plain style; emphasis may use italics, underlining, or bold. | LR 10.2(a)(2) | M |
| HID-ELEC-001 | Civil complaints are filed electronically under LR 3.1. | LR 3.1 | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HID-COVER-001 | File a civil cover sheet (JS 44) with the complaint. | LR 3.1 | M |
| HID-CONT-001 | Caption states court, action title, file number, and document designation. | FRCP 10(a) | M |
| HID-CONT-002 | Claims use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| HID-CONT-003 | The complaint contains a short and plain statement of jurisdiction, claim, and relief sought. | FRCP 8(a) | M |
| HID-CERT-001 | Complaint counsel identification follows HID-FORM-003; the memorandum-only Times New Roman 14 pt mandate does not extend to complaints. | LR 10.2(a)(2), (b) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| HID-FORM-006 | A memorandum uses 14-point Times New Roman plain style, is double-spaced subject to stated exceptions, on 8.5 × 11 paper with one-inch margins. | LR 10.1; LR 10.2(a)(1), (2), (4) | M |
| HID-CONT-004 | Motion states with particularity its grounds and the relief sought. | FRCP 7(b)(1) | M |
| HID-CONT-005 | A motion caption includes hearing date/time when known, presiding judge, trial date when known, and the related document docket number when applicable. | LR 7.3 | M |
| HID-LEN-001 | Memorandum is at most 25 pages, unless it uses the alternative limit of 6,250 words and includes the required attorney word-count certificate. | LR 7.4(a), (b), (e) | M |
| HID-LEN-002 | Under the word-count alternative, headings, footnotes, and quotations count; the caption, contents, authorities, exhibits, declarations, and certificates are excluded. | LR 7.4(d) | M |
| HID-CONT-006 | A memorandum exceeding 10 pages includes a table of contents and table of authorities. | LR 7.4(f) | M |
| HID-CONT-007 | Counsel confers before filing a motion and includes the required statement that it follows a conference pursuant to LR 7.8. | LR 7.8 | M |
| HID-ELEC-002 | Mandatory chambers copies for a Rule 12 motion include two copies of the related complaint, tabbed and prepared as LR 10.3 requires. | LR 12.1; LR 10.3 | M (separate copy) |
