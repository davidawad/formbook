# Northern Mariana Islands Supreme Court: briefs, motions and rehearing petitions

Verified against the Northern Mariana Islands Supreme Court Rules (2015 edition, effective 2015-09-19) and the CNMI Judiciary pages in Sources on 2026-10-21.

Alias `nmi`. Scope: answering brief only (court-root exemplar). The only exemplar in `usage/` is an appellee's brief; the other doc types below are written from the rule text alone and have no exemplar. Verified against the Northern Mariana Islands Supreme Court Rules, Title I, as adopted by Administrative Order 2015-ADM-0017-RUL (effective September 19, 2015; text from the PDF on cnmilaw.org, fetched 2026-10-21). The judiciary's appellate-rules page (nmijudiciary.gov, fetched 2026-10-21) lists a later edition, "Title I - Rules Effective March 15, 2024", whose text was not retrieved, so every row below must be re-checked against that edition before the template is relied on.

## Motions

No exemplar. Rule text only.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMI-MOT-001 | A motion must be in writing, state with particularity the grounds, the relief sought and the legal argument necessary to support it. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 27(a)(1), 27(a)(3)(A). | M |
| NMI-MOT-002 | A motion seeking substantive relief includes a copy of the trial court's order, judgment or opinion (or agency decision) as a separate exhibit, and a proposed order as a separate document. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 27(a)(3)(B)(ii)-(iii). | M |
| NMI-MOT-003 | A separate brief supporting or responding to a motion must not be filed. A notice of motion is not required. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 27(a)(3)(C). | M |
| NMI-MOT-004 | A motion or response to a motion does not exceed 20 pages, and a reply to a response does not exceed 10 pages, unless the Court permits otherwise. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 27(b)(2). | M |
| NMI-MOT-005 | A motion has a caption with the case number, the name of the court, the title of the case, and a descriptive title identifying the purpose of the motion and the party filing it. No cover is used. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 27(b)(1)(B). | M |
| NMI-MOT-006 | A motion uses 1½ line spacing, 1-inch margins and 8½ by 11 inch paper, and the typeface rules of Rule 32(a)(5)-(6). | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 27(b)(1)(A), 27(b)(1)(D)-(E). | M |
| NMI-MOT-007 | A response to a motion is filed within 8 days after service of the motion unless the Court shortens or extends the time. A reply is filed within 5 days after service of the response. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 27(a)(4)(A), 27(a)(5). | M |

## Reply briefs

No exemplar. Rule text only.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMI-REPLY-001 | The appellant may file a brief in reply to the appellee's brief. Unless the Court permits, no further briefs are filed. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 28(c). | M |
| NMI-REPLY-002 | A reply brief has a table of contents with page references and a table of authorities with the pages of the reply where they are cited. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 28(c). | M |
| NMI-REPLY-003 | A reply brief is filed at least 3 days before argument within 14 days after the appellee's brief is served, unless the Court, for good cause, allows a later filing. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 31(a)(1). | M |

## Amicus briefs

No exemplar. Rule text only.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMI-AMICUS-001 | The United States or the Commonwealth may file an amicus brief without consent or leave. Any other amicus curiae files only by leave of court or where the brief states that all parties consented. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 29(a). | M |
| NMI-AMICUS-002 | A motion for leave is accompanied by the proposed brief and states the movant's interest and why an amicus brief is desirable and relevant. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 29(b). | M |
| NMI-AMICUS-003 | The amicus brief complies with Rule 32. Its cover identifies the party or parties supported and whether the brief supports affirmance or reversal. A corporate amicus includes a disclosure statement like Rule 26.1. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 29(c). | M |
| NMI-AMICUS-004 | The amicus brief includes a table of contents, a table of authorities, a concise statement of the amicus's identity, interest and authority to file, and an argument. It need not follow Rule 28. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 29(c)(1)-(4). | M |
| NMI-AMICUS-005 | An amicus brief is no more than one-half the maximum length for a party's principal brief, unless the Court permits otherwise (17.5 pages under the 35-page cap). | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 29(d); Rule 32(a)(7). | M |
| NMI-AMICUS-006 | An amicus brief is filed no later than 7 days after the principal brief of the party it supports is filed. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 29(e). | M |

## Petitions for rehearing

No exemplar. Rule text only.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMI-REH-001 | A petition for rehearing may be filed within 14 days after entry of judgment, unless the Court shortens or extends the time. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 40(a)(1). | M |
| NMI-REH-002 | The petition states with particularity each point of law or fact the Court overlooked or misapprehended, and argues it. Oral argument is not permitted. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 40(a)(2). | M |
| NMI-REH-003 | No answer to a petition for rehearing is permitted unless the Court requests one. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 40(a)(3). | M |
| NMI-REH-004 | The petition complies in form with Rule 32 and does not exceed 15 pages unless the Court permits otherwise. | NMI SUP. CT. R. (2015) Rule 40(b). | M |
