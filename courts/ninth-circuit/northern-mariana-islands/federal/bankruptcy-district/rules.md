# Bankruptcy Court, Northern Mariana Islands: adversary complaints and motion papers

Alias `nmib`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion papers. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules (https://www.nmid.uscourts.gov/sites/nmid/files/documents/localrules/LBKR20190415.pdf, "Effective April 15, 2019"), which incorporate the District's Local Rules (https://www.nmid.uscourts.gov/sites/nmid/files/documents/localrules/LR20171101.pdf, "Effective November 1, 2017"), and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure on 2026-10-20.

Deviation from the default eval: LR 5.2(a) sets margins of 1.5 in top, 1 in bottom, 0.5 in right and 1 in left, and a 3-inch blank upper-right area on the first page. These differ from the 1-inch default and need an `eval.yaml` when the template is built.
