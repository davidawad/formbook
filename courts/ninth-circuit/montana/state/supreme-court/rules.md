# Montana Supreme Court: briefs, petitions, motions and rehearing petitions

Alias `mont`. Scope: principal and reply briefs, amicus briefs, original-proceeding petitions (writs, supervisory control), motions, and petitions for rehearing filed in the Supreme Court of Montana. Verified against the Montana Rules of Appellate Procedure, Title 25, chapter 21, Montana Code Annotated (Rules 11, 12, 14, 16, 20; text from the legislature's 2015 edition on archive.legmt.gov, history through 2011), and Supreme Court Order AF 07-0016 (Aug. 20, 2019) on 2026-10-21.

| MONT-LEN-002 | A reply brief is 5,000 words or fewer if proportionately spaced, or 14 pages if monospaced. A reply that also answers a cross-appellant's principal brief is held to the reply limit. | MRAP 11(4)(a)-(b); MRAP 12(3)-(4). | M |
| MONT-LEN-003 | The table of contents, table of citations, certificate of service, certificate of compliance and any appendix of statutes or rules are not counted toward the word or page limit. | MRAP 11(4)(d). | M |
| MONT-CERT-001 | The brief carries a certificate of compliance that states the line spacing, the typeface and point size (or monospace characters per inch and page count), and the word count. The certificate is not counted in the length. | MRAP 11(4)(e). | M |
| MONT-COVER-001 | The cover and first page carry: the title of this court; the cause number (blank for a first filing); the title of the case from the lower tribunal with party designations, the tribunal's name and venue, and the judge; counsel's names, mailing addresses, telephone, fax and e-mail for each party, identifying the party represented; and the title of the document. | MRAP 11(6)(b). | M |
| MONT-COVER-002 | Cover color: the appellant's brief is blue; a party filing the opening brief on a certified question is yellow; the appellee's brief is salmon; an intervenor's or amicus's brief is green; a reply brief is gray. Pastel colors only. | MRAP 11(6)(a). | M |
| MONT-ORDER-001 | The brief of the appellant contains, in order: a table of contents with page references; a table of cases and authorities with pages; the statement of the case (procedural background relevant to the issues); the statement of facts with record references; the statement of issues; a summary of the argument (not a repetition of the headings); the argument; the conclusion; and an appendix of the judgment, orders and transcript pages from which the appeal is taken. | MRAP 12(1)(a)-(i). | M |
| MONT-ORDER-002 | An answer brief of the appellee follows the appellant's order; a statement of the issues or case may be omitted unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement. | MRAP 12(2). | M |

## Original proceedings: petitions for writ, supervisory control and review

Montana has no intermediate court and no discretionary petition for review. The original-proceeding petition in this file (the "petition for discretionary review" and "petition for review" exemplars) is a petition under MRAP 14.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MONT-PET-001 | A petition is filed with the clerk of the supreme court, with a signed original and 9 copies, and sets forth the matters required by the rule, with a copy of each judgment, order or document referred to in the petition as exhibits. | MRAP 14(5)(a)-(b), 14(9)(a), 14(5)(b)(iv). | M |
| MONT-PET-002 | The text of a petition and of any response is 4,000 words or fewer if proportionately spaced, or 12 pages if monospaced; the petition conforms to the form rules of MRAP 11. | MRAP 14(9)(b); MRAP 11(2)-(4). | M |
| MONT-PET-003 | A petition that names a district court ruling states the name of the district judge and the judicial district in its title, and the petition and exhibits are served on that judge and all parties. | MRAP 14(6). | M |
| MONT-PET-004 | Exhibits are preceded by a table of contents and separated by colored page separators. | MRAP 14(9)(c). | M |
| MONT-PET-005 | Petitions are not argued orally unless ordered; the court may order a summary response, and a summary response is not a brief and has no reply. | MRAP 14(7)-(8). | M |
