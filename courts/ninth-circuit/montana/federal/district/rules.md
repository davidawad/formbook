## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MTD-FORM-001 | Letter size, one-sided, one-inch margins, no shading. | L.R. 1.5(a)(1) | M |
| MTD-FORM-002 | Typewritten in exactly 14-point font; double-spaced except quoted material and footnotes. The rules name no typeface. | L.R. 1.5(a)(2) | M |
| MTD-FORM-003 | Pages are consecutively numbered at the bottom. | L.R. 1.5(a)(3) | M |
| MTD-CONT-001 | First page caption follows Appendix C Form A: counsel upper left, court and division title, party caption, case number, and document title. | L.R. 1.5(a)(4), Appendix C Form A | M |
| MTD-CERT-001 | Typed name below signature; electronic signature uses /s/ typed name. | L.R. 1.5(b), 11.1 | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MTD-CONT-002 | Complaint states short and plain jurisdiction, claim, and demand for relief. | FRCP 8(a) | M |
| MTD-CONT-003 | Caption names all parties; allegations use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(a)-(b) | M |
| MTD-COVER-001 | Jury demand appears in the title of any pleading carrying a jury demand. | L.R. 38.1 | M (if demanded) |
| MTD-COVER-002 | File complaint with filing fee or IFP motion and civil cover sheet. | L.R. 3.1(a) | M (separate filings) |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MTD-CONT-004 | Supporting brief is filed separately from the motion. | L.R. 7.1(d)(1)(A) | M |
| MTD-LEN-001 | Supporting brief is limited to 6,500 words. | L.R. 7.1(d)(2)(A) | M |
| MTD-CONT-005 | At 4,000 words or more, include table of contents, table of authorities, and exhibit index. | L.R. 7.1(d)(2)(C) | M |
| MTD-CERT-002 | Certificate of compliance states the word count, excluding caption, certificate, TOC/TOA, exhibit index, and certificate of service. | L.R. 7.1(d)(2)(E) | M |
