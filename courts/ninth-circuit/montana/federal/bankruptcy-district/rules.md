## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MTB-FORM-001 | E-filed documents are PDF and one-sided; paper size 8.5 × 11 inches. | LBR 5005-2(a)(1), (6) | M |
| MTB-FORM-002 | Text is legible and at least 12-point. | LBR 5005-2(a)(2) | M |
| MTB-FORM-003 | Margins are at least one inch, excluding page numbers; pages are consecutively numbered at bottom. | LBR 5005-2(a)(3)-(4) | M |
| MTB-FORM-004 | Text is double-spaced except quoted material and footnotes. | LBR 5005-2(a)(5) | M |
| MTB-FORM-005 | Counsel block on first page, upper left, identifies name, office mailing address, telephone, email, state bar ID, and party represented. | LBR 5005-2(b)(1)-(6) | M |
| MTB-CERT-001 | Signature may be cursive or conformed `/s/` with typed name. | LBR 5005-2(c) | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MTB-CONT-001 | Caption states court, case title, case number, adversary number when applicable, and document designation. | FRBP 9004(b) | M |
| MTB-CONT-002 | Jurisdiction allegation states case name/number/chapter/district/division and whether filer consents to final orders or judgment. | FRBP 7008(a)-(b) | M |
| MTB-COVER-001 | Adversary complaint is accompanied by Official Form 1040 cover sheet and summons; these are separate filings. | FRBP 7003-7004 | M |
| MTB-CONT-003 | Number allegations in paragraphs. | FRCP 10(b), applied by FRBP 7010 | M |

## Contested-matter motion

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
