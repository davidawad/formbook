## Why this one

Searched CourtListener's RECAP search (`court=mtb`) for motions and memoranda with an archived copy. Most results were memoranda of decision (court opinions, not filer documents) or were not archived. Kept this trustee's motion because it is a filer's motion in this court with the Montana notice of opportunity to respond, a bankruptcy-specific request (11 U.S.C. § 363, FRBP 6004), numbered paragraphs, a signature block and a certificate of service. Its legal basis is set out in the body, not in a separate memorandum.

Rejected: Thomas, Christopher Gregg, Motion to Disqualify Counsel (17-61231, Doc 44): a pro se debtor's filing, format not comparable. Retz, 04-60247, Doc 139 and 145: court memoranda of decision, not filer documents.
