# Ninth Circuit Bankruptcy Appellate Panel: appellate briefs

Alias `bap9`. Scope: counsel-filed opening, answering, and reply briefs. Verified against the BAP's official rules and court-published Litigant's Manual on 2026-10-17. Re-check at each annual FRBP/local-rule update and at least annually.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by rule/order; **C** = convention; **A** = author responsibility. This rules file covers the three counseled brief types; informal pro se forms are outside scope.

## Requirements

IDs are stable; do not renumber them. The opening-brief rules apply unless a row states a responding-brief difference.

### Form and cover (FRBP 8015)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP9-FORM-001 | Page size is 8 1/2 by 11 inches; margins are at least one inch on all sides. | FRBP 8015(a)(4); MANUAL § VIII.7 | M |
| BAP9-FORM-002 | A proportionally spaced face includes serifs, may use sans-serif in headings and captions, and is 14-point or larger; plain roman type is used, with italics or boldface for emphasis and case names italicized or underlined. | FRBP 8015(a)(5)-(6); MANUAL § VIII.7 | M |
| BAP9-FORM-003 | Text is double-spaced. Headings, footnotes, and quotations of more than two lines may be single-spaced. | FRBP 8015(a)(4); MANUAL § VIII.7 | M |
| BAP9-COVER-001 | The cover states the appeal number, court name, case title, nature of the proceeding and court below, brief title identifying the filing party, and counsel's name, address, telephone number, and email address. | FRBP 8015(a)(2); MANUAL § VIII.7 | M |
| BAP9-COVER-002 | Convention: use a white electronic cover. FRBP 8015 does not impose a cover-color rule; any paper-copy requirement depends on a BAP order. | FRBP 8015(a)(2); BAP Administrative Order Regarding Electronic Filing; MANUAL | C |

### Length and required content

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAP9-LEN-001 | Opening and answering briefs may not exceed 30 pages or 13,000 words. The word limit excludes the cover, tables, certificates, signature block, and other items listed by FRBP 8015(g). | FRBP 8015(a)(7), (g); MANUAL § VIII.8 | M |
| BAP9-LEN-002 | A reply brief may not exceed 15 pages. As the type-volume alternative, it may contain no more than 6,500 words or 650 lines in a monospaced face. | FRBP 8015(a)(7)(A)-(B); MANUAL § VIII.8 | M |
| BAP9-CONT-001 | The opening brief contains, in order: corporate disclosure if required; contents; authorities; jurisdiction; statement of issues and standards of review; statement of the case with record references; summary of argument; argument; and a short conclusion stating the relief sought. | FRBP 8014(a); MANUAL § VIII.5 | M |
| BAP9-CONT-002 | The opening brief identifies each issue, the applicable standard of review, and specific record references. | FRBP 8014(a)(5)-(8); MANUAL §§ VIII.5, VIII.9 | M |
| BAP9-CONT-003 | An answering brief contains the same contents required by FRBP 8014(a), except that it may omit the jurisdictional statement, issues, and case statement if it accepts the appellant's presentation. | FRBP 8014(b) | M |
| BAP9-CONT-004 | A reply brief contains a table of contents, table of authorities, and argument responding to the answering brief; it need not repeat the other opening-brief sections. | FRBP 8014(c) | M |

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |

## Document-type differences

Opening briefs require the related-cases certification in BAP9-CERT-002. Answering briefs use the opening-brief length cap and may adopt the appellant's jurisdictional statement, issue statement, and case statement under BAP9-CONT-003. Reply briefs use the shorter limit in BAP9-LEN-002, omit initial-brief certifications, and respond to the answering brief under BAP9-CONT-004. Interested-party certification applies to each non-governmental party's initial brief, which includes the answering brief when filed by a non-governmental party.
