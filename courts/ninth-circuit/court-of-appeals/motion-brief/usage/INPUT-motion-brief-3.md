# Introduction

Appellee moves under [Fed. R. App. P. 27](toa:frap27) to dismiss this appeal. The order appealed from is not a final decision under [28 U.S.C. § 1291](toa:s1291), and no other statute gives this Court jurisdiction over it.

# Argument

## The Order Is Not a Final Decision

A decision is final when it ends the litigation on the merits and leaves nothing for the court to do but execute the judgment. The district court has not entered judgment, and claims remain pending before it. A party may not manufacture finality to obtain review of an interlocutory order. [*Microsoft Corp. v. Baker*](toa:microsoft), 582 U.S. 23 (2017).

## No Exception Applies

Appellant identifies no collateral-order ground and sought no certification. The appeal should therefore be dismissed.

# Conclusion

The Court should dismiss the appeal for lack of jurisdiction.
