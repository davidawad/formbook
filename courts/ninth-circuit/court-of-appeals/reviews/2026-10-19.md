Rows marked ACCEPTED are open-choice differences from the exemplars or deliberate court-form wording.

Defect found this pass and fixed before the hashes below were taken (LaTeX engine, `latex/template.tex`):
- The footnote rule was `width 30\textwidth` (a typo for 0.3): it ran across the whole page and out to the page edge, and footnote 2 floated mid-page with blank space under it. Fixed to `0.3\textwidth`; footnotes now sit at the foot of their page. Tier 2.
No tier-1 or tier-2 defect was found in the typst PDFs. The typst PDFs were re-rendered byte-differently by `fb check` (typst embeds a timestamp); the rendered pages were compared with the ones viewed and are pixel-identical at 40 dpi and their text is identical. Top-of-page note for LaTeX rows: a level-one heading that opens a page keeps about 1.4 lines of space above it; cosmetic, ACCEPTED.
