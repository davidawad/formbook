# Visual review 2026-10-21

Reviewer: Codex. Method: rendered every page of both current LaTeX PDFs at 75 dpi and viewed all pages in numbered contact sheets (three pages per sheet). The Sweet sample was compared with its filed exemplar, especially the cover, contents and authorities (exemplar pp. 1–4), body layout (pp. 5–12, 22–30), and certificates (pp. 52–54). The fictional sample was compared with the Sweet output, the Ninth Circuit form rules, and the second-filer exemplar (ACA Connects) for cover, contents, and heading conventions. All pages show clear text, consistent margins and page numbers, and no clipping or overlap. The semicolon glyph uses the vendored TeX Gyre Termes face; its appearance is consistent with the surrounding Times New Roman text.
