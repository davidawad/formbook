# Visual review 2026-10-21 (cross-engine layout, fb-595.54)

Reviewer: Claude (agent), fb-595.54. The three engines were moved onto one layout model (docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Cross-engine gate"): no kerning or ligatures, first-fit line breaking, fixed line boxes (29.4 / 17 / 20 pt, baseline at 0.8), spacing in points; every PDF changed, so every page of all six current PDFs (typst, LaTeX, docx; samples sweet and generic) was rendered with `pdftoppm -r 45` and viewed in contact sheets of eight pages, with zooms at 70 and 150 dpi on body pages and the Form 8 check boxes. The pages were compared with the filed exemplar (`usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-opening-brief.pdf`; second filer `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-opening-brief-3.pdf` on the cover) and with each other: `fb diff --engines` finds every line and page break identical in all three, and outside the cover every line's baseline within 0.5 pt of typst's.

Defect found this pass and fixed before the hashes below were taken: the unchecked Form 8 / Form 17 boxes had collapsed to a line in typst and LaTeX (an empty box has no height); all three engines now draw a box of the same height (about 18 pt) around an X or an empty space. Tier 2.
