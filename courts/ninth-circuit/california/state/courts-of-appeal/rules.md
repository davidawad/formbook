# California Courts of Appeal: briefs, motions, petitions for rehearing, writ petitions, amicus briefs

Alias `calctapp`. Scope: papers filed electronically in the Courts of Appeal (districts 1 to 6): appellant's opening brief, respondent's brief, reply brief, amicus curiae brief, motions with memoranda, petitions for rehearing, and petitions for writ of mandate or other extraordinary relief. Verified against the California Rules of Court (2026 edition as posted on courts.ca.gov, rule 8.74 as amended effective July 1, 2026) on 2026-10-21.

Not covered: the Supreme Court's petition for review (rule 8.500) is a different court's paper and is governed by the `supreme-court` folder. Rule 8.1113 was not retrieved (courts.ca.gov returned HTTP 404 for that rule page on 2026-10-21) and no requirement below depends on it.
