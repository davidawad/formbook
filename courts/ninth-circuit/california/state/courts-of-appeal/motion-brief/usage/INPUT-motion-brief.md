# MOTION TO AUGMENT THE RECORD ON APPEAL

Pursuant to rule 8.155(a) of the California Rules of Court, Appellants Franklin Armory, Inc., and California Rifle and Pistol Association, Incorporated, hereby request augmentation of the record on appeal to include the following document:

1\. Reporter’s Transcript of the case management conference held on February 25, 2021, from 9:33 A.M. to 9:44 AM. The court reporter was Cindy Cameron, CSR No. 10315. A true and correct copy is attached to the Declaration of Anna M. Barvir as Exhibit A.

Appellants respectfully request that this transcript be added to the record on appeal because it is necessary for the Court to fully resolve the issues presented. Appellants did not initially designate the transcript of this routine case management conference because, at the time, it appeared unrelated to the substantive rulings on appeal. It was not until Respondents argued in their brief that Appellants could not challenge errors arising from the trial court’s first demurrer order—an issue discussed and decided at the February 25, 2021 case management conference—that the relevance and necessity of the transcript became apparent.

This motion is based on the attached Memorandum of Points & Authorities and the Declaration of Anna M. Barvir attached hereto.

Dated: October 6, 2025

# MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

## I. INTRODUCTION

Appellants respectfully request leave to augment the record on appeal to include the reporter’s transcript of the February 25, 2021, case management conference. Inclusion of this transcript is necessary to ensure a complete and accurate record for the Court’s review.

## II. ARGUMENT

### A. Legal Standard

Under California Rules of Court, rule 8.155(a)(1)(B), a party may move to augment the record at any time to include “[a] certified transcript—or agreed or settled statement—of oral proceedings not designated under rule 8.130.” It is well established that this rule is to be construed liberally so that the appellate court may properly resolve the issues on appeal. (*People v. Brooks* (1980) 26 Cal.3d 471, 484.)

### B. Good Cause Exists to Augment the Record

Good cause exists to include the transcript of the February 25, 2021, case management conference. Appellants did not initially designate this transcript because the proceeding appeared to concern routine scheduling matters unrelated to the substantive rulings challenged on appeal. (Barvir Decl., ¶ 4.) However, after Respondents argued in their brief that Appellants cannot challenge errors arising from the trial court’s first demurrer order—an issue discussed and decided at the February 2021 conference—it became apparent that the transcript is directly relevant and necessary for this Court’s fair consideration of the appeal. (Barvir Decl., ¶ 5.)

Moreover, augmentation will not prejudice any party or delay the proceedings. The transcript is part of the trial court record, and Respondents were present at the hearing and fully aware of the discussion and rulings that took place. Its inclusion merely ensures the Court has an accurate and complete understanding of the procedural and factual history of this appeal. This request is made in good faith, solely to clarify the record for the Court’s consideration, and will not affect the current argument schedule. (Barvir Decl., ¶ 6.)

## III. CONCLUSION

For these reasons, Appellants request that this Court order that the record on appeal be augmented to include the reporter’s transcript of the February 25, 2021, case management conference.
