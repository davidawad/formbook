# California Court of Appeal, Sixth Appellate District: briefs, petitions, and motions

Alias `calctapp6d`. Scope: appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply), amicus curiae briefs, petitions for writ, petitions for rehearing (the Court of Appeal's own reconsideration petition, filed under the petition-for-discretionary-review name), and motions. Verified against the California Rules of Court, Title 8 (Appellate Rules), as posted by the Judicial Branch of California (the 2026 edition shown on each rule page), on 2026-10-22. The Sixth District's local rules were not retrieved (see the last section).

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALCTAPP6D-AMIC-001 | The cover of the application and the proposed amicus brief identifies the party the applicant supports, if any. | CRC 8.200(c)(5) | M |
| CALCTAPP6D-AMIC-002 | An amicus brief is filed only on the presiding justice's permission, on an application made within 14 days after the last appellant's reply brief is filed or could have been filed, unless it falls within rule 8.200 (for example, the Attorney General's brief). | CRC 8.200(a)(4), (c)(1), (c)(7) | M |

The length and format of an amicus brief are not stated in the rule text retrieved above. Its form falls under CALCTAPP6D-FORM-001 to -010 and the cover rows by CRC 8.204 and 8.74, which govern "a brief"; whether 8.204(c)(1) applies to amicus briefs is not verified (see the open items).

## Petitions for rehearing (filed under the petition-for-discretionary-review name)

The Court of Appeal's rehearing petition (CRC 8.268) is the reconsideration procedure for its own decisions. Discretionary review of a Court of Appeal decision is a petition for review in the Supreme Court (CRC 8.500); that is not this folder.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALCTAPP6D-REHR-001 | A party may file a petition for rehearing within 15 days after the filing of the decision. | CRC 8.268(b)(1) | M |
| CALCTAPP6D-REHR-002 | A petition for rehearing produced on a computer does not exceed 7,000 words, including footnotes. | CRC 8.204(c)(5) | M |
| CALCTAPP6D-REHR-003 | The petition for rehearing follows the form rules of CALCTAPP6D-FORM-001 to -009 and the cover rules of CALCTAPP6D-COVER-001 to -003. | CRC 8.204(b); 8.74(b) | M |

## Open items

- **Local rules not retrieved.** The Sixth District's local rules page (`https://www.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/6dca/rules-forms-filing/local-rules-orders`, linked from `https://www.courts.ca.gov/6dca.htm`, HTTP 200) returned HTTP 404 from here on 2026-10-22, so no local rule or court order on format was read. Add them here if they change any requirement above.
- **Amicus length.** The amicus length rule was not verified. Until it is, the amicus template uses CALCTAPP6D-FORM-001 to -009 only.
- **Tier 2 conventions.** Judged against the exemplars in `usage/` (Sixth District filings: Kirk, Owens, Friday, Weseloh, Johnson). Filers differ, so a choice seen across filers is a convention. The petition-for-discretionary-review exemplar is a rehearing petition (see the section above).
- **Exemplar font.** The amicus exemplar is set in a sans-serif face. CALCTAPP6D-FORM-002 requires a serif for the text, so the template keeps its serif face; the exemplar's choice is not followed.
