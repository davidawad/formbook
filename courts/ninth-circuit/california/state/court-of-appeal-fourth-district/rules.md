# California Court of Appeal, Fourth Appellate District: briefs, petitions, and motions

Alias `calctapp4d`. Scope: appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply), amicus curiae briefs, petitions for writ, motions, and petitions for rehearing (the `petition-for-discretionary-review` folder holds the petition for rehearing that this court's exemplar shows; rule 8.268, not a petition for review under rule 8.500). Verified against the California Rules of Court, Title 8 (Appellate Rules), as posted by the Judicial Branch of California, and the Fourth District's local rules page, on 2026-10-22. The statewide rules govern all six Courts of Appeal; the Fourth District local rules page adds no brief-format rule (see the sources).

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALCTAPP4D-AMIC-001 | The cover of the application and the proposed amicus brief identifies the party the applicant supports, if any. | CRC 8.200(c)(5) | M |
| CALCTAPP4D-AMIC-002 | An amicus brief is filed only on the presiding justice's permission, on an application made within 14 days after the last appellant's reply brief, unless it falls within rule 8.200 (for example, the Attorney General's brief). | CRC 8.200(a)(4), (c)(1), (c)(7) | M |

The length and format of an amicus brief are not stated in the rule text retrieved above. Its form falls under CALCTAPP4D-FORM-001 to -009 and the electronic rows by CRC 8.204 and 8.74, which govern "a brief" and "electronic documents"; whether 8.204(c)(1) applies to amicus briefs is not verified (see the open items).

## Open items

- **Certificate of interested entities or persons** (CALCTAPP4D-CIEP-001). Required by CRC 8.208(d)(1) in the principal brief; the templates do not yet place it. Not claimed as passing.
- **Amicus length.** The amicus length rule was not verified. Until it is, the amicus template uses CALCTAPP4D-FORM-001 to -009 and the electronic rows only.
- **Bookmarks.** CALCTAPP4D-EFILE-002 is a stated rule; the default checks do not read PDF outlines. Reviewer checks the bookmark list on each OUTPUT.
- **Tier 2 conventions.** Not yet judged against the exemplars in `usage/` (FILED-EXAMPLE-amicus-brief, -answering-brief, -motion-brief, -opening-brief, -petition-for-discretionary-review, -petition-for-writ, -reply-brief). Add `C` rows once the exemplars are read.
- **Paper-only rules.** Cover color (CRC 8.40(a)), binding and paper weight are not reproduced: the PDF is an electronic filing.
