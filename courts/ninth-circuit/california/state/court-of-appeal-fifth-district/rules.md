# California Court of Appeal, Fifth Appellate District: briefs, petitions, and motions

Alias `calctapp5d`. Scope: appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply), amicus curiae briefs, petitions for writ, and motions. Verified against the California Rules of Court, Title 8 (Appellate Rules), as posted by the Judicial Branch of California, on 2026-10-21. The Fifth District's local rules were not retrieved (see the last section).

## Opening, answering, and reply briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-001 | Every document has a page size of 8½ by 11 inches. | CRC 8.204(b)(1) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-002 | Any conventional font may be used; it may be proportionally spaced or monospaced. | CRC 8.204(b)(2) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-003 | Font size, including footnotes, is not smaller than 13 point. | CRC 8.204(b)(4) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-004 | Font style is roman. Italics, boldface, or underscoring may be used for emphasis. Case names are italicized or underscored. Headings may be uppercase. | CRC 8.204(b)(3) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-005 | Lines of text are unnumbered and at least one-and-a-half-spaced. Headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. Quotations may be block-indented and single-spaced. | CRC 8.204(b)(5) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-006 | Margins are at least 1½ inches on the left and right and at least 1 inch on the top and bottom. | CRC 8.204(b)(6) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-007 | Pages are consecutively numbered in Arabic numerals, starting with the cover as page 1. The number may be suppressed on the cover. | CRC 8.204(b)(7) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-LEN-001 | A brief produced on a computer does not exceed 14,000 words, including footnotes. Tables, the cover information, a certificate under CALCTAPP5D-CERT-001, any signature block, and attachments are excluded. | CRC 8.204(c)(1), (c)(3) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-LEN-002 | A typewritten brief does not exceed 50 pages. | CRC 8.204(c)(2) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-LEN-003 | A longer brief may be allowed on application for good cause. This is not a template requirement. | CRC 8.204(c)(6) | A |
| CALCTAPP5D-CERT-001 | A certificate by appellate counsel or an unrepresented party states the number of words in the brief. The certifier may rely on the word-count program. | CRC 8.204(c)(1) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-CONT-001 | The brief begins with a table of contents and a table of authorities listing cases, constitutions, statutes, court rules, and other authorities cited. | CRC 8.204(a)(1)(A) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-CONT-002 | Each point is stated under a separate heading or subheading that summarizes it. | CRC 8.204(a)(1)(B) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-CONT-003 | Each reference to the record cites the volume and page number of the record. | CRC 8.204(a)(1)(C) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-CONT-004 | An appellant's opening brief states the nature of the action, the relief sought in the trial court, and the judgment or order appealed from; states that the judgment is final or why the order is appealable; and gives a summary of the significant facts limited to the record. | CRC 8.204(a)(2) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-ATT-001 | Attachments of exhibits, record materials, or citable rules and statutes not readily accessible total no more than 10 pages combined. | CRC 8.204(d) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-COVER-001 | A paper brief's cover is in the color prescribed by rule 8.40(a). | CRC 8.204(b)(10), 8.40(a) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-COVER-002 | The cover states the brief's title; the title, trial court number, and Court of Appeal number of the case; the names of the trial court and each participating trial judge; and the party each attorney represents. | CRC 8.204(b)(10)(A)-(D) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-COVER-003 | The cover (or first page) gives the name, mailing address, telephone number, fax number if available, email if available, and State Bar number of each attorney filing or joining in the document, or of the party if unrepresented. One attorney per firm is designated by an asterisk to receive notices. | CRC 8.40(b); 8.74 (electronic documents) | M |

| CALCTAPP5D-FORM-009 | The brief is not required to be signed. | CRC 8.204(b)(9) | A |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALCTAPP5D-AMIC-001 | The cover of the application and the proposed amicus brief identifies the party the applicant supports, if any. | CRC 8.200(c)(5) | M |
| CALCTAPP5D-AMIC-002 | An amicus brief is filed only on the presiding justice's permission, on an application made within 14 days after the last appellant's reply brief, unless it falls within rule 8.200 (for example, the Attorney General's brief). | CRC 8.200(a)(4), (c)(1), (c)(7) | M |

The length and format of an amicus brief are not stated in the rule text retrieved above. Its form falls under CALCTAPP5D-FORM-001 to -007 by CRC 8.204, which governs "a brief"; whether 8.204(c)(1) applies to amicus briefs is not verified (see the open items).

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|

## Motions

No motion-form requirement was retrieved. CRC rule 8.54, the rule cited for motions, returned a page with no operative text on 2026-10-21. Do not infer motion limits from the brief rules; open the rule text again before writing motion rows.
