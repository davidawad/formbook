# Exemplars: California Supreme Court, petition for review

Both are public court records posted by the Supreme Court of California on its website. Both were fetched 2026-10-14 from the document pages linked below, then the PDFs were downloaded directly. Both PDFs have a text layer (`pdftotext` yields text).

## 1. `FILED-EXAMPLE-quach-s275121-petition-for-review.pdf` (primary; used for the sample)

| | |
|---|---|
| Case | *Quach v. California Commerce Club, Inc.* |
| Docket | Cal. Supreme Court **S275121**; Court of Appeal No. **B310458** (Second Appellate District); superior court No. 19STCV42445 (Los Angeles County) |
| Filer | Peter Quach, Plaintiff-Petitioner, by The Vora Law Firm, P.C. (Nilay U. Vora et al.) |
| Document | Petition for Review, 42 pages + Court of Appeal opinion attached (the attachment is not part of the template's output) |
| Date filed | 2022-06-20 (stamped "Electronically FILED on 6/20/2022") |
| Fetched from | https://supreme.courts.ca.gov/document/respondents-petition-review-232 (page lists the PDF as `/sites/default/files/supremecourt/default/documents/1-10-s275121-resp-pet-rev-062022.pdf`; the page's label says "respondents", but the filing is by the petitioner, Mr. Quach) |
| PDF URL | https://supreme.courts.ca.gov/sites/default/files/supremecourt/default/documents/1-10-s275121-resp-pet-rev-062022.pdf |
| Why chosen | It has the rule 8.504(b)(1) opening (a statement of the issues presented, before the introduction), a generated-looking table of contents and table of authorities, a certificate of compliance stating the font and word count, and a proof of service. Typeface 13-point Century Schoolbook (per its own certificate). |

## 2. `FILED-EXAMPLE-hoehn-s277510-petition-for-review.pdf` (second filer; kept for tier-2 comparison, not transcribed)

The template follows the rule, not this exemplar, on that point (see `../README.md`). |

## Search trail

- Supreme Court "document" pages for filed petitions (`supreme.courts.ca.gov/document/...`), found by search for "California Supreme Court petition for review filed pdf". Two were downloaded and checked; both are real filings with text layers.
- Not used: conference lists and order lists on the same site (not petitions).
- Not searched further: CourtListener (its RECAP coverage is federal, not this court).
