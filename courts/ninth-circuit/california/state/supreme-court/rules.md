# California Supreme Court: briefs, petitions, applications and motions

Alias `cal`. Scope: documents filed in the Supreme Court of California (briefs on the merits, petitions for review, petitions for rehearing, original writ petitions, amicus curiae briefs, motions and their memoranda, and petitions for review of State Bar Court decisions). Verified against the California Rules of Court (2026 edition, rule text as posted at courts.ca.gov) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: the Supreme Court's Internal Operating Practices and Procedures and its clerk's office format guidance were not fetched. Rule 8.1113 at https://www.courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index.cfm?title=eight&linkid=rule8_1113 returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-21 and was not used.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |

| CAL-PFR-002 | The petition explains how the case presents a ground for review under rule 8.500(b). | Rule 8.504(b)(2); 8.500(b). | M |
| CAL-PFR-003 | If a petition for rehearing could have been filed in the Court of Appeal, the petition states whether it was and how the court ruled. | Rule 8.504(b)(3). | M |
| CAL-PFR-004 | A copy of the Court of Appeal opinion, showing its filing date and any modification or publication order, is bound at the back of the original and each copy, or attached. | Rule 8.504(b)(4). | M |
| CAL-PFR-005 | A petition that seeks review of an order is accompanied by a copy of that order showing its date; a petition seeking review of a summary writ denial includes the trial court order with its date. | Rule 8.504(b)(5)-(6). | M |
| CAL-PFR-006 | The petition, answer and reply are served and filed within 10 days after the Court of Appeal decision is final; an answer within 20 days after the petition; a reply within 10 days after the answer. | Rule 8.500(e)(1), (4), (5). | M |
| CAL-PFR-007 | The petition is served on the superior court clerk, and on the Court of Appeal clerk when filed in paper; electronic filing constitutes that service. | Rule 8.500(f)(1). | M |
| CAL-PFR-008 | No attachments are permitted except the opinion or order under (b)(4)-(6), exhibits or orders the party considers unusually significant, and citable materials not readily accessible (10 pages combined for the last two). | Rule 8.504(e)(1)-(2). | M |
| CAL-PFR-009 | No incorporation by reference, except a reference to a petition, answer or reply filed by another party in the same case, or in a case with the same issues in which review is pending or granted. | Rule 8.504(e)(3). | M |
| CAL-PFR-010 | The answer to a petition may ask the court to address additional issues if review is granted; the answer's issues statement is concise and nonargumentative. | Rule 8.500(a)(2); 8.504(c). | M |
| CAL-PFR-011 | A filing fee or a fee waiver application accompanies an answer to a petition for review, and any brief filed after review is granted, when the filer is a party other than the petitioner. | Rule 8.25(c)(2)(D)(vi)-(vii), (c)(1). | M |
