# California Superior Court: papers in trial courts (statewide)

Alias `calsuperct`. Scope: papers filed in the trial courts of California, namely complaints and motion papers (notice of motion and motion combined with a memorandum). Verified against the California Rules of Court, Title 2 (rules 2.100-2.119, 2.256) and Title 3 (rules 3.1110, 3.1112, 3.1113), as shown on courts.ca.gov, "California Rules of Court 2026", on 2026-10-21.

Local rules: the Judicial Council has preempted local form-and-format rules (CRC 2.100(a)), so the statewide rules below govern every county. County-specific rules add filing and e-filing practice only; see `san-diego-county/rules.md` for San Diego. No exemplar: no public filed paper from this court's own site was found (search on 2026-10-21, sdcourt.ca.gov and courts.ca.gov domains); see the report for the search note.

## Format of every paper (complaints and motion papers)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-001 | All papers filed are 8½ × 11 inches. Paper not filed electronically is opaque, unglazed, white or unbleached, at least 20-pound weight. | CRC 2.103. | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-002 | Only one side of each page is used when papers are not filed electronically. | CRC 2.102. | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-003 | Font size is not smaller than 12 points, unless a rule states otherwise. | CRC 2.104. | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-004 | Font style is essentially equivalent to Courier, Times New Roman, or Arial. The template uses Times New Roman. | CRC 2.105. | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-005 | Font color is black or blue-black. | CRC 2.106. | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-006 | Left margin is at least 1 inch from the left edge; right margin is at least ½ inch from the right edge. The template uses 1 inch left and ½ inch right, which is the right-margin floor in the folder's `eval.yaml`. | CRC 2.107. | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-007 | Lines are one and one-half or double spaced and numbered consecutively from 1 on each page, at the left margin, with a vertical column of space at least ⅕ inch wide or a single or double vertical rule; at least three line numbers per vertical inch. Quotations, footnotes, and descriptions of real property may be single-spaced. | CRC 2.108(1)-(4). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-008 | Each page is numbered consecutively at the bottom in Arabic numerals, beginning with page 1; the first page's number may be suppressed. | CRC 2.109; CRC 3.1110(c). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-009 | Below the page number, a footer divided from the page by a printed line gives the paper's title or a clear abbreviation, at least 10 points. Exhibits carry no footer. | CRC 2.110(a)-(c). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-010 | Each separately stated cause of action, count, or defense states its number, its nature, the party asserting it if more than one party is represented, and the party against whom it is directed. | CRC 2.112. | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-011 | A paper not filed electronically is firmly bound at the top with original pages and no riders. | CRC 2.113. | M |

## Motion papers

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALSUPERCT-CONT-010 | A notice of motion states in its opening paragraph the nature of the order sought and the grounds. | CRC 3.1110(a). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-CONT-011 | Motion papers are a notice of hearing, the motion, and a supporting memorandum; they may be filed as separate documents or combined if the caption of the combined paper specifies each item separately. | CRC 3.1112(a), (c). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-LEN-001 | An opening or responding memorandum may not exceed 15 pages (20 pages on a summary judgment or summary adjudication motion). A reply may not exceed 10 pages. The caption page, notice, exhibits, declarations, tables of contents and authorities, and proof of service are excluded from the count. | CRC 3.1113(d). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-LEN-002 | A memorandum longer than 10 pages includes a table of contents and a table of authorities; one longer than 15 pages also includes an opening summary of argument. | CRC 3.1113(f). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-LEN-003 | An application to file a longer memorandum is made ex parte, with written notice to the other parties at least 24 hours before the memorandum is due, and states why the argument cannot be made within the limit. | CRC 3.1113(e). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-CONT-012 | A memorandum states the facts, a concise statement of the law, the evidence and argument relied on, and discusses the statutes, cases, and textbooks cited. California case citations give the official report volume and page and the year of decision. | CRC 3.1113(b)-(c). | M |
| CALSUPERCT-FORM-012 | Exhibits: an index of exhibits gives each exhibit's description, number or letter, and page; each paper exhibit is separated by a hard 8½ × 11 sheet with a tab bearing the exhibit designation. | CRC 3.1110(f)(1)-(3). | M |

|---|---|---|---|
