# San Diego County Superior Court: papers in civil actions

Alias `calsuperct-sandiego`. Scope: papers filed in civil actions in the San Diego County Superior Court (complaints and motion papers). Verified against the San Diego County Superior Court Local Rules, Division II (Civil), revised January 1, 2026, and the California Rules of Court as shown on courts.ca.gov, on 2026-10-21.

The format of papers is governed statewide: CRC 2.100(a) preempts local form-and-format rules, so the page, font, margin, line, caption, and length rules in `../rules.md` (CALSUPERCT-FORM, CALSUPERCT-CONT, CALSUPERCT-LEN) apply here. The San Diego Local Rules add filing, fee-notation, and e-filing practice only. Local rule text for Division II was read from the court's own PDF; the San Diego rules page itself returned HTTP 200.

No exemplar in this folder beyond the court-root `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-complaint.pdf`, which is a 2005 press copy and is not used as a tier-2 exemplar (see `complaint/usage/SOURCE.md`).

## Filing and cover notations (Local Rule 2.1.2)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SDCOURT-FORM-001 | A party exempt from filing fees under Gov. Code § 6103 indicates the exemption on the first page of the document, in the upper right-hand corner, above the caption, in an area that will not interfere with the file stamp. The exemption names each exempt party. | SDLR 2.1.2(H). | M |
| SDCOURT-CONT-001 | A paper whose original document the court must keep by statute carries the words "EXEMPT FROM DESTRUCTION" in all caps immediately under the title of the pleading to which it is attached. | SDLR 2.1.2(E). | M |
| SDCOURT-CONT-002 | Papers requesting an extension of masking in an unlawful detainer case carry "extension of masking requested" in the upper right-hand corner, above the caption. (Not applicable to complaints in other civil cases.) | SDLR 2.4.2(H). | M |
| SDCOURT-FORM-002 | Photocopies of Judicial Council and court forms are acceptable only if clear, legible, easily readable, the same color as the original, and on the same paper type. | SDLR 2.1.2(A). | M |

## Electronic filing and exhibits

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SDCOURT-ELEC-001 | E-filed documents go through a court-approved electronic filing service provider and are submitted as PDF, in a format compatible with the selected provider, text searchable (OCR). | SDLR 2.1.4.1(B), (D). | M |
| SDCOURT-ELEC-002 | An e-filed document containing exhibits carries electronic bookmarks linked to the first page of each exhibit, titled with the exhibit number or letter and a brief description. | SDLR 2.1.4.1(C). | M |
| SDCOURT-ELEC-003 | Except for motions, each e-filed document is uploaded as an individual document within the same transaction. For motions, supporting documents may be uploaded as one document under the filing that best describes the motion. | SDLR 2.1.4.1(F). | M |
| SDCOURT-ELEC-004 | Exhibits attached to or filed with a motion or pleading are filed in paper format or by e-filing where available; they must be legible and complete. Audio, video, CD, DVD, and flash-drive exhibits are lodged, not filed, absent leave of court. | SDLR 2.5.6(A). | M |
