# [PROPOSED] ORDER GRANTING MOTION TO SUPPRESS

The motion of defendant Jordan Rivera to suppress evidence under Penal Code section 1538.5 came on for hearing on November 20, 2026, in Department 23 of the above-entitled court.

Having considered the papers, the evidence and argument presented, and the record in this matter, the Court finds and orders:

1\. The motion to suppress is GRANTED.

2\. Evidence obtained as a result of the November 1, 2026 search of the vehicle is suppressed.

3\. The parties shall appear for further proceedings on December 4, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. in Department 23.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
