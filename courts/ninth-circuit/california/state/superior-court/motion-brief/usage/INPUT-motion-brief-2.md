Defendant Jordan Rivera moves under [Penal Code section 1538.5](toa:pc1538) to suppress evidence seized during a warrantless vehicle search on November 1, 2026. The motion is based on this notice, the memorandum below, and the record at hearing.

# MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

# THE SEARCH EXCEEDED THE SCOPE OF CONSENT

The prosecution bears the burden to justify a warrantless search. The record will show that Rivera limited consent to a visual inspection of the passenger compartment and expressly declined a search of the closed center console.

# THE EVIDENCE MUST BE SUPPRESSED

The officer opened the console and seized a phone without a warrant. [People v. Sampson](toa:people_sampson) explains that consent is measured by its objectively reasonable scope. The search therefore exceeded the consent given.

For those reasons, the Court should suppress the phone and any evidence derived from its search.
