# California Court of Appeal, Second Appellate District: briefs, petitions, and motions

Alias `calctapp2d`. Scope: appellate briefs (opening, answering, reply), amicus curiae briefs, petitions for writ, motions, and petitions for rehearing. No `petition-for-discretionary-review` folder is built for this court: its exemplar is a 13,101-word petition for rehearing, over the 7,000-word limit of rule 8.204(c)(5) (see the open items). Verified against the California Rules of Court, Title 8 (Appellate Rules), as posted by the Judicial Branch of California, and the Second District's practices and local rules pages, on 2026-10-22. The statewide rules govern all six Courts of Appeal; this court adds only the practices cited below.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |

| CALCTAPP2D-AMIC-002 | An amicus brief is filed only on the presiding justice's permission, on an application made within 14 days after the last appellant's reply brief, unless it falls within rule 8.200 (for example, the Attorney General's brief). | CRC 8.200(a)(4), (c)(1), (c)(7) | M |

The length and format of an amicus brief are not stated in the rule text retrieved above. Its form falls under CALCTAPP2D-FORM-001 to -009 and the electronic rows by CRC 8.204 and 8.74, which govern "a brief" and "electronic documents"; whether 8.204(c)(1) applies to amicus briefs is not verified (see the open items).

## Petitions for rehearing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALCTAPP2D-PDR-001 | A petition for rehearing is served and filed within 15 days after the filing of the decision (or after a publication, modification, or consent order that restarts finality). | CRC 8.268(b)(1) | M |
| CALCTAPP2D-PDR-002 | The petition for rehearing complies with rule 8.204, including the length limit in 8.204(c)(5): a computer-produced petition does not exceed 7,000 words, including footnotes. | CRC 8.268(b)(3); 8.204(c)(5) | M |
| CALCTAPP2D-PDR-003 | The petition states its word count in a certificate under rule 8.204(c)(1). The certificate is excluded from the limit. | CRC 8.204(c)(1), (c)(3) | M |

The court's exemplar for this doc type (usage/SOURCE.md, the Pico Neighborhood Association respondents' petition for rehearing, 2020) states 13,101 words, about 6,100 words over CALCTAPP2D-PDR-002. A transcription of it would fail the template's word gate by design, so no `petition-for-discretionary-review` folder is built for this court. The First District's exemplar of the same doc type is within the limit and is used there. This is an open item for David (see below).
