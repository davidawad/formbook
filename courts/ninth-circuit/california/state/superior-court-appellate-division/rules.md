# California Superior Court, Appellate Division: briefs and papers

Alias `calappdeptsuper`. Scope: briefs of parties and amici curiae in appeals and writ proceedings heard by the appellate division of a superior court (limited civil, misdemeanor and infraction appeals; CRC 8.800), including the petition for rehearing (rule 8.889). Verified against the California Rules of Court, Title 8 (Appellate Rules), Division 2, Chapter 2 (appellate division), as posted by the Judicial Branch of California (the 2026 edition shown on each rule page), on 2026-10-22. The appellate division's local rules were not retrieved (see the last section).

| CALAPPDEPTSUPER-FORM-009 | If filed in paper form, the brief is bound on the left margin (or at the top if a local rule requires it); a stapled brief has its bound edge covered with tape. | CRC 8.883(c)(9) | M |

| CALAPPDEPTSUPER-CONT-003 | An appellant's opening brief states the nature of the action, the relief sought in the trial court, and the judgment or order appealed from; states that the judgment is final or why the order is appealable; and gives a summary of the significant facts limited to the record. | CRC 8.883(a)(2) | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALAPPDEPTSUPER-AMIC-001 | Any person may apply within 14 days after the appellant's reply brief is filed or was required to be filed, whichever is earlier, for the presiding judge's permission to file an amicus brief. The application states the applicant's interest and identifies the parties and counsel who authored or funded the brief. | CRC 8.882(d)(1)-(3) | M |
| CALAPPDEPTSUPER-AMIC-002 | The proposed brief is served and accompanies the application, and may be combined with it. | CRC 8.882(d)(4) | M |
| CALAPPDEPTSUPER-AMIC-003 | The Attorney General may file an amicus brief without permission, unless submitted on behalf of another state officer or agency. | CRC 8.882(d)(5) | A |

The form and length of an amicus brief fall under CALAPPDEPTSUPER-FORM-001 to -010 and -LEN-001 to -003 by rule 8.883, which governs "a brief"; this is not separately verified for amicus briefs (see the open items).

## Service and filing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CALAPPDEPTSUPER-SERV-001 | Each brief is served as rule 8.817 requires, a copy goes to the trial court clerk for the trial judge, and in a misdemeanor appeal the defendant's appointed counsel serves the brief and sends a copy to the defendant (or files a signed statement that the defendant asked for no copy). | CRC 8.882(e)(1), (3), (5) | M |
| CALAPPDEPTSUPER-SERV-002 | Unless the court orders otherwise, only the original brief, with proof of service, is filed in the appellate division. | CRC 8.882(e)(2) | M |

## Noncomplying briefs

A brief that does not comply may be returned, struck, or disregarded (CRC 8.883(d)). Not a template requirement.
