## Layout

- Numbered lines 1 to 27 (fb-595.68.22), a double rule at the text's left edge and a single rule at its right, footer on one line (as in the complaint folder).
- Counsel from line 1 (may take 18 lines); the court title starts on line 8 or below if counsel needs more lines.
- Caption: party column on the left; on the right "Case No.", the judge (`judge`) and the magistrate judge (`magistrate`), the title in bold, the concurrent-filing note, the hearing date, time and place, and the "Action Filed" date.
- Page numbering: the notice pages are arabic from page 1 (the caption); the tables are roman (i, ii, ...); the memorandum restarts at 1. The exemplar works this way, and the rules do not fix it.
- The table of contents uses Typst's `outline()` over the body's headings, with the memorandum's page numbers; it cannot list a heading that the sample does not contain.
- The table of authorities is printed from the `authorities` frontmatter, one group per category (Cases, Statutes, Other Authorities), each entry with its page references.
- Headings: level 1 centered caps; level 2 bold, left; level 3 bold, left, indented 0.5 inch. Body paragraphs are unnumbered, with a 0.5 inch first-line indent (briefs are not numbered).
- Lists: numbered 1., 2., ..., as in the exemplar.

## Markdown contract (for samples)

- YAML frontmatter, then the body. Blank-line-separated chunks:
  - `[[TOC]]`: the table of contents (starts a new page, roman numbering).
  - `[[TOA]]`: the table of authorities (same numbering run).
  - `[[MEMO]]`: the memorandum title; starts a new page with arabic numbering from 1.
  - `---`: the signature block. Put one `---` at the end of the notice and one at the end of the memorandum. The chunk immediately before a `---` is kept on the page with the signature.
  - `#`, `##`, `###` headings (see Layout).
  - `1.`-style list items.
  - any other paragraph: unnumbered body text with a first-line indent.
- Frontmatter: `case_number`, `judge`, `magistrate`, `counsel` (lines), `caption.parties` (list of `{names, designation}`; "v." is inserted between parties), `brief_title`, `concurrent` (the "Filed concurrently with ..." note; optional), `hearing` (`date`, `time`, `place`; optional), `action_filed` (optional), `footer_title` (one line), `date`, `signature` (`firm`, `signer`, `lines`), `memo_title`, `authorities` (groups of `{cite, pages}`; `cite` may use `*italics*`), `omitted` (a note on what the sample leaves out; not rendered).
- Not supported: footnotes (the filing's footnote 1 is not transcribed), tables in the body, raw typst.

## Known gaps and open choices

1. **One exemplar.** The brief asks for one exemplar per doc type. EVAL-LOOP's tier-2 rule asks for two or three filers. The second candidate is Wilce v. Willow Health Services, Inc., 2:26-cv-02465-DAD-AC, Document 7 (a Rules 12(b)(1), (2), (f) motion with memorandum, 28 pp., George LLP; not saved; see `usage/SOURCE.md`). Tier 2 is therefore **not** judged against two filers yet.
2. **Font.** No E.D. Cal. rule names a typeface or type size. The exemplar is Times New Roman 12 pt; the template now sets vendored Times New Roman 12 pt in typst and docx (fb-595.68.22), so the earlier EB Garamond x-height difference is gone. Not a rule requirement.
3. **Excerpt.** The sample transcribes the notice (pages 1 to 2 of the filing) and its signature (page 3); the full table of contents (page i) and table of authorities (pages ii to iii); memorandum page 1 (introduction and the start of the facts, with the footnote omitted); the argument's Rule 12(e) section (page 17 of the memorandum) and the conclusion (page 18), with signature. Memorandum Parts III.A to III.C (filing pages 4 to 16) are not transcribed, so the sample's argument headings run from D to III. The table of contents lists only the headings in the sample.
4. **Table of authorities page references.** The entries are the filed table's entries, with the filed page numbers. They do not match the sample's pages, because the sample is an excerpt. A complete sample would need its own page numbers, which the template does not compute. Recorded here so no one reads the page numbers as the excerpt's.
5. **Memorandum title.** The memorandum title line comes from `memo_title` through `[[MEMO]]`, in the exemplar's position (page top, centered caps). The exemplar's title sits above the introduction on the same page, as here.
6. **Keep-with-signature.** The chunk before `---` is held with the signature block. In this sample the conclusion moved to memorandum page 3 with its signature, which leaves a gap at the foot of memorandum page 2. That is the intended trade (a signature alone on a page is worse).
7. **Notice signature.** The exemplar signs the notice on its own page 3. The sample signs it at the end of the notice on page 2 (the notice fits on one page), a layout consequence of the excerpt and not a rule.
8. **Checks.** `sh tools/check.sh <this folder>` runs on this folder (`eval.yaml`): typst `RESULT: 9 checks passed, 0 failed`; docx `RESULT: 13 checks passed, 0 failed`; DEF-TOC-001 and DEF-COVER-001 pass on both (fb-595.68.22). No `tools/check-selftest.sh` result is recorded here.
