Alias `caed`. Applies to complaint and motion-brief document types under this court folder. Primary sources verified 2026-10-17 against the Local Rules effective February 23, 2026. ### Complaint

Applies to `complaint/`. FRCP 8, 10 and 38 control content and jury demand. A jury demand must be made within the period in Rule 38(b), when intended.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAED-CONT-006 | Jury demand specifies the issues for jury trial and appears in a pleading or separate writing within the Rule 38 period. | FRCP 38(b) | M |

### Motion brief

Applies to `motion-brief/`. Motion content and supporting declarations are subject to FRCP 7(b)(1), 43(c), local Rule 230, and the assigned judge's standing orders. Exact motion page limits vary by motion/judge; do not hard-code one universal limit.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAED-CONT-007 | Motion identifies the grounds and relief sought; factual contentions are supported as the rules and the judge's procedures require. | FRCP 7(b)(1), 43(c); CAED-LR, L.R. 230 | M |
