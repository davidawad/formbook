Verified against the primary sources cited in this file on 2026-10-17.

### Complaint

Applies to `complaint/`. Rule 8(a), 10(a)-(b), 38(b), and 11-3 control the complaint. Jury demand is included when intended; the rule requires it to be made by a pleading or separate writing within the Rule 38 period.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CACD-CONT-007 | A jury demand, when made, is included in the pleading or separate writing and specifies the issues demanded for jury trial. | FRCP 38(b) | M |

### Motion brief

Applies to `motion-brief/`. Judge-specific standing orders may add requirements; check the assigned judge's current procedures before filing. C.D. Cal. L.R. 7-3 requires a prefiling conference for covered motions, and L.R. 7-5 governs factual support.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CACD-CONT-008 | Motion states with particularity the grounds and relief sought; any required notice and supporting memorandum accompany it under local rules. | FRCP 7(b)(1); CACD-LR, L.R. 7-3, 7-5 | M |
| CACD-CONT-009 | Factual contentions in a motion are supported by declarations/evidence as local rule and FRCP 7(b)(1) require. | CACD-LR, L.R. 7-5; FRCP 7(b)(1) | M |
