### Complaint

Applies to `complaint/`. A jury demand, when intended, must be made in a pleading or separate writing within the Rule 38(b) period.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CASD-CONT-006 | Jury demand states the issues demanded for jury trial. | FRCP 38(b) | M |

### Motion brief

Applies to `motion-brief/`. Notice, memorandum, supporting evidence, filing sequence and length follow CivLR 7.1 and any assigned judge's chambers rules. Confirm those details for the specific motion; do not apply a generic federal default where the local rule or chambers order supplies a different procedure.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CASD-CONT-007 | Notice and memorandum comply with local requirements governing motion contents and supporting papers. | S.D. Cal. CivLR 7.1; FRCP 7(b)(1) | M |
