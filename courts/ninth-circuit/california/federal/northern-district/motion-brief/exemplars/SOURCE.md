## Why this one

Searched for a text-based, public N.D. Cal. motion. Options found: an EFF motion to quash (`Chevron v. Donziger`, 5:12-mc-80237 CRB (NC), 34 pp., Word-generated, text layer present, caption typo "NOTHERN"), and this DOJ motion to dismiss. The DOJ motion is the better match: a motion with notice, relief, issues, a full memorandum, TOC and TOA, and a signature block, in 17 pages within the 25-page limit of Civil L.R. 7-2(b). The filing is 2017, so its layout predates the current local rules text (effective Oct. 2, 2026); the format requirements used by the template come from that current text.
