# NOTICE OF MOTION

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that on Thursday, December 10, 2026, at 1:30 p.m., or as soon thereafter as the matter may be heard, in Courtroom 2, 4th Floor, Defendant Acme Widgets, Inc. will move under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure [12(b)(6)](toa:rule12b6) to dismiss the complaint for failure to state a claim. The motion rests on this notice, the memorandum below, the pleadings and records on file, and any argument the Court hears.

# RELIEF SOUGHT

Defendant asks for an order dismissing the complaint.

# ISSUE TO BE DECIDED

Whether the complaint states a plausible claim for breach of contract.

# MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

## INTRODUCTION

Plaintiff alleges that Defendant breached a supply agreement, but the complaint never identifies the term Defendant is said to have broken. A claim that names no promise does not cross the line from conceivable to plausible.

## STATEMENT OF FACTS

The parties signed a one-year supply agreement in 2025. Plaintiff alleges that some deliveries arrived late. The complaint does not attach the agreement, quote it, or say which delivery dates it set.

## ARGUMENT

### The Complaint Pleads No Breached Term

A complaint must contain enough facts to state a claim to relief that is plausible on its face. [*Twombly*](toa:twombly), 550 U.S. at 570. Labels and conclusions do not suffice, and the Court need not accept legal conclusions couched as facts. [*Iqbal*](toa:iqbal), 556 U.S. at 678. A breach of contract claim needs the contract's terms; without them, the Court cannot infer that any conduct breached one.

### Leave to Amend Should Be Limited

If the Court grants leave to amend, Plaintiff should be directed to plead the term it relies on and the facts of its breach.

# CONCLUSION

The Court should grant the motion and dismiss the complaint.
