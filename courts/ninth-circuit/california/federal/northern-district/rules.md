### Complaint

Applies to `complaint/`. A jury demand, when intended, is made in the pleading or separate writing within the Rule 38(b) period.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAND-CONT-008 | Jury demand specifies the issues demanded for jury trial. | FRCP 38(b) | M |

### Motion brief

Applies to `motion-brief/`. A motion must comply with Civil L.R. 7-2; an accompanying brief follows Civil L.R. 7-4. Standing orders and case-specific orders may alter ordinary requirements.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAND-CONT-009 | Motion states grounds and relief with particularity. | FRCP 7(b)(1) | M |
