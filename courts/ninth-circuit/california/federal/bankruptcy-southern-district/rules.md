# S.D. Cal. bankruptcy filings: rules

Alias `casb`. Applies to adversary complaints and motion briefs. Primary sources verified 2026-10-17 against the court's current rules pages and current rule edition. Proposed amendments published September 3, 2026 were not effective at verification; the court announced a planned December 1, 2026 effective date. Re-check before filing, at each amendment and at least annually.

### Adversary complaint

Applies to `adversary-complaint/`. FRBP 7003, 7008 and 7010 apply. Check current Local Bankruptcy Rule 7003 and adversary cover-sheet form when filing; the court may require a companion form in addition to the complaint.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CASB-CONT-004 | Initiating complaint complies with FRBP 7003 and the local adversary proceeding cover sheet rule/form. | FRBP 7003; CASB-LBR, LBR 7003-1 | M |
| CASB-CONT-005 | Allegations are in numbered paragraphs; separate counts where appropriate. | FRCP 10(b); FRBP 7010 | M |

### Motion brief

Applies to `motion-brief/`. The source document's motion type controls notice and hearing deadlines. Proposed changes to LBR 9013-6 and 9013-7 were announced for December 1, 2026; re-check the effective text before any later filing.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CASB-CONT-006 | Motion or memorandum complies with current LBR 9013-6 and 9013-7 for content, service, hearing notice, briefing and length; no one-size-fits-all notice deadline is asserted. | CASB-LBR, LBR 9013-6, 9013-7; FRBP 9013-9014 | M |
| CASB-CONT-007 | Motion states grounds and requested relief with particularity. | FRBP 9013 | M |
