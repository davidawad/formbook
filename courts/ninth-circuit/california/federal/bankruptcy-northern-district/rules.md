Verified against the primary sources cited in this file on 2026-10-17.

### Motion brief

Applies to `motion-brief/` (a motion with its memorandum of points and authorities, combined as B.L.R. 9013-1(b)
allows). Verified 2026-10-29 against the Bankruptcy Local Rules effective May 4, 2026 (CANB-LBR above); B.L.R.
1001-2(a) applies Civil L.R. 3-4 except 3-4(c)(3) and (e). The notice of hearing is a separate paper.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CANB-MB-FORM-001 | Letter paper with numbered lines. | Civil L.R. 3-4(c)(1) via B.L.R. 1001-2(a) | M |
| CANB-MB-FORM-002 | A standard proportionally spaced font, 12 point or larger, footnotes included. | Civil L.R. 3-4(c)(2)(A)-(B) via B.L.R. 1001-2(a) | M |
| CANB-MB-FORM-003 | Text double-spaced, no more than 28 lines a page; counsel, title of the case, footnotes and quotations excepted. | Civil L.R. 3-4(c)(2) via B.L.R. 1001-2(a) | M |
| CANB-MB-FIRST-001 | Counsel's name, address, telephone, e-mail and bar number at the upper left, with the party represented. | Civil L.R. 3-4(a)(1) via B.L.R. 1001-2(a) | M |
| CANB-MB-FIRST-002 | From the eighth line: the court, the title of the action, the case number, the title of the paper. | Civil L.R. 3-4(a)(3) via B.L.R. 1001-2(a) | M |
| CANB-MB-FIRST-003 | Caption: the bankruptcy case number and any adversary number, the debtor and the parties, the chapter, and the date, time and location of the hearing. | B.L.R. 1005-1(a)-(c) | M |
| CANB-MB-CONT-001 | Motion: a concise statement of the relief sought; memorandum: the issues, the facts, and the argument with authorities; the two may be combined. | B.L.R. 9013-1(b)(1)-(2) | M |
| CANB-MB-CONT-002 | Memorandum of no more than 25 pages of text (reply 15). | B.L.R. 9013-1(c) | M |
| CANB-MB-CONT-003 | A table of contents and a table of authorities for a memorandum of more than 10 pages of text. | B.L.R. 9013-1(c) | M |
| CANB-MB-SIGN-001 | Signed; filed through ECF with the typed name of each signatory. | Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9011(a); B.L.R. 5005-2(b) | M |
| CANB-MB-CONV-001 | Page number at the foot with the paper's title (B.L.R. 1001-2(a) excludes Civil L.R. 3-4(c)(3)). | `motion-brief/usage/SOURCE.md` | C |
