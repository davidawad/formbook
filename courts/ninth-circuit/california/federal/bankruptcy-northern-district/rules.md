Alias `canb`. Applies to adversary complaints. Primary sources verified 2026-10-17 against Bankruptcy Local Rules effective May 4, 2026. Re-check on each amendment and at least annually; judge procedures and standing orders may add requirements.

## Shared requirements

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CANB-FORM-001 | Caption and filed-paper form comply with FRBP 9004 and the current local bankruptcy rules. No fixed font size or family is asserted unless the current rule text or judge order supplies one. | FRBP 9004; CANB-LBR, BLR 9004-1 | M |
| CANB-ELEC-001 | Electronic filing and signatures comply with local rules and FRBP 5005 and 9011; redact personal identifiers under FRBP 9037. | CANB-LBR, BLR 5005-1, 9004-1, 9011-1; FRBP 9037 | M |
| CANB-CONT-001 | Adversary proceeding is commenced by complaint stating claims and relief. | FRBP 7003, 7008; FRCP 8 | M |
| CANB-CONT-002 | Caption identifies the court, parties, case and proceeding; complaint body uses numbered allegations. | FRBP 9004; FRCP 10(a)-(b) via FRBP 7010 | M |
| CANB-CONT-003 | Initiating adversary complaint is accompanied by a completed Clerk-prescribed Adversary Proceeding Cover Sheet. | CANB-LBR, BLR 7003-1 | M |
| CANB-FORM-002 | Caption and visible pleading-paper choices follow patterns seen across the two filed complaint examples when no rule controls them. | `usage/SOURCE.md`, cross-filer comparison | C |

### Adversary complaint

Applies to `adversary-complaint/`. Cover sheet is a separate companion form; the template handles the complaint pleading only.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CANB-CONT-004 | File the Clerk-prescribed adversary proceeding cover sheet with each complaint initiating the proceeding. | CANB-LBR, BLR 7003-1 | M |
| CANB-CONT-005 | Number allegations and use separate counts where helpful to clarity. | FRCP 10(b); FRBP 7010 | M |
| CANB-CONT-006 | State any jury demand as permitted under FRCP 38 and FRBP 9015. | FRCP 38; FRBP 9015 | M |
