# C.D. Cal. bankruptcy filings: rules

Alias `cacb`. Applies to adversary complaints and motion briefs. Primary sources verified 2026-10-17. Re-check upon every Local Bankruptcy Rules revision and at least annually; the court's current rules page controls. Bankruptcy judge standing orders may add requirements.

### Adversary complaint

Applies to `adversary-complaint/`. A complaint to initiate an adversary proceeding must be accompanied by the court-required adversary cover sheet. Any jury demand must satisfy FRCP 38 and applicable bankruptcy rules.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CACB-CONT-005 | File a completed adversary proceeding sheet with every initiating complaint. | CACB-LBR, LBR 7003-1 | M |
| CACB-CONT-006 | Allegations are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b); FRBP 7010 | M |

### Motion brief

Applies to `motion-brief/`. Motions and contested matters must comply with LBR 9013-1. The local rule provides different notice and hearing paths for different motion classes; identify the applicable subsection rather than relying on a universal deadline.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CACB-CONT-007 | Motion states particular grounds and relief, and follows applicable notice, supporting-paper and hearing requirements. | FRBP 9013, 9014; CACB-LBR, LBR 9013-1 | M |
| CACB-CERT-002 | A memorandum longer than ten pages carries indexed contents and authorities; length and certificate requirements follow current LBR 9013-2. | CACB-LBR, LBR 9013-2 | M |
