## Why this one

Found with the CourtListener search API (`type=rd`, `court=azd`, "motion to dismiss" "memorandum", 2025-09 onward). Chosen over the other hits: Steiner v. City of Phoenix (2:26-cv-00280, ECF 10, 8 pages, a short motion without a full memorandum), Fontes (2:26-cv-00066, ECF 25, 21 pages, Attorney General's office), and Fly Vaunt (ECF 14, memorandum split into a separate attachment). The Evancho motion is a single filed document with the notice of motion, the relief sought, a full memorandum with argument headings, and a signature block within the 17-page limit of LRCiv 7.2(e)(1) (the page count the exemplar is measured against).

## Limits

- Only one filer. Tier-2 for this doc type is not judged against 2 or 3 filers (see README.md).
- The filing has no LRCiv 12.1(c) meet-and-confer certification (the rule is in the 2020 master file; see README.md). The exemplar is therefore not a source for that part; the rule governs.
- The filing has a certificate of service with a blank date ("August ____, 2026") and the service list; the sample transcribes it as filed.
