Verified against the primary sources cited in this file on 2026-10-17.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AZD-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inches. Body text is double-spaced, no more than 28 lines per page; footnotes and indented quotations may be single-spaced. | AZD-LCR 7.1(b)(1) | M |
| AZD-FORM-002 | Type is at least 13-point proportional, including footnotes, or fixed-pitch at least 10-pitch. | AZD-LCR 7.1(b)(1) | M |
| AZD-FORM-003 | Pages are numbered; left margin is at least 1½ inches and right margin at least ½ inch. | AZD-LCR 7.1(b)(1) | M |
| AZD-FORM-004 | Papers are signed as provided by Fed. R. Civ. P. 11. | AZD-LCR 7.1(b)(1); FRCP 11 | M |
| AZD-COVER-001 | First page begins at line 1 with counsel's name, address, email, State Bar number, telephone, and represented party, left of center; keep the right half clear for clerk filing marks. | AZD-LCR 7.1(a)(1) | M |
| AZD-COVER-002 | Court title begins on or below line 6. | AZD-LCR 7.1(a)(2) | M |
| AZD-COVER-003 | Action title appears below the court title, left of center; use proper upper and lower case party names. The right side states the case number, document description (including jury demand when made), and any motion notice or supporting memorandum. | AZD-LCR 7.1(a)(3) | M |
| AZD-COVER-004 | Separate parties in an initial or amended pleading by semicolons; for a consolidated action, list affected case numbers below the lead case number. | AZD-LCR 7.1(a)(3) | M |
| AZD-ELEC-001 | ECF documents are PDF. Documents created electronically are converted directly from word-processing source, not from a scanned image. | AZD-LCR 7.1(c) | M |

## Complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AZD-CONT-001 | Include the court name, action title, file number, and Rule 7(a) designation. | AZD-LCR 10.1; FRCP 10(a) | M |
| AZD-CONT-002 | State jurisdiction grounds, a short and plain claim statement, and the relief demanded. | FRCP 8(a) | M |
| AZD-CONT-003 | State claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b) | M |
| AZD-CERT-001 | A jury demand is included in the case title when the complaint makes one. | AZD-LCR 7.1(a)(3)(B) | M |
| AZD-ELEC-002 | A complaint initiating a civil action is accompanied by a completed JS 44 Civil Cover Sheet, except for incarcerated and self-represented filers. | AZD-LCR 3.1(a)-(b) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AZD-CONT-101 | A motion is in writing and, unless the court orders otherwise, is filed with a memorandum stating supporting points and authorities. | AZD-LCR 7.2(a)-(b) | M |
| AZD-LEN-001 | A motion and its supporting memorandum, and a response with memorandum, may not exceed 17 pages exclusive of attachments and any required statement of facts. A reply with memorandum may not exceed 11 pages exclusive of attachments. | AZD-LCR 7.2(e)(1)-(2) | M |
| AZD-CONT-102 | Request oral argument by placing “Oral Argument Requested” immediately below the title of the motion or response. | AZD-LCR 7.2(f) | M |
| AZD-CONT-103 | A Rule 12(b)(6) motion or Rule 12(c) motion includes the required certification that the movant notified the opposing party of asserted issues and the parties could not agree the pleading was curable by permissible amendment. | AZD-LCR 12.1(c); FRCP 12(b)(6), 12(c) | M |
| AZD-CONT-104 | A Rule 12(b)(1) motion on personal or subject-matter jurisdiction follows the Rule 56.1 briefing schedule unless the court orders otherwise. | AZD-LCR 12.1(b) | M |
