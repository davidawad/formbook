## Why this one

Found with the CourtListener search API (`type=rd`, `court=azd`, query "Demand for Jury Trial" complaint, 2025-06 onward). Other D. Ariz. complaints in the same results were scanned images without a text layer (Button v. Kingsley, 2:25-cv-02619, ECF 1), or pro se filings (Cooprider v. Unknown Parties, 2:26-cv-07094, ECF 1, 37 pages, filed 2026-10-01 by an individual). The Harod pleading is counsel-filed, text-based, on numbered pleading paper, with a jury demand in the caption, which matches the Local Rules form (LRCiv 7.1 Appendix C).

## Limits

- Only one filer. EVAL-LOOP's tier-2 rule asks for 2 or 3 filers from different sources; not met (see README.md).
- It is an amended complaint, not an original complaint. The form is the same; the template does not depend on which.
