## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARB-FORM-001 | Papers use 8½ × 11 inch paper, number-lined pleading paper, no more than 28 lines per page, and double spacing except footnotes and indented quotations. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(g) | M |
| ARB-FORM-002 | Text is proportional and at least 13 point; footnotes may be 12 point. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(g) | M |
| ARB-FORM-003 | Left margin is at least 1½ inches and right margin at least ½ inch. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(g) | M |
| ARB-FORM-004 | Each filed document is signed under FRBP 11 or Local Rule 5005-4. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(g) | M |
| ARB-COVER-001 | Caption includes the Court name, debtor, chapter, bankruptcy case number, and a brief description of relief. | ARB-LBR 1005-1(a), 9004-1(a)-(b) | M |
| ARB-COVER-002 | First page starts at line 1, left of center, with filer/law firm name, mailing address, email, responsible counsel and bar number if applicable, telephone, and party represented. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(a) | M |
| ARB-COVER-003 | Include hearing date, time, and place in the caption at right beneath the case number when known. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(e) | M |
| ARB-LEN-001 | Unless the court orders otherwise, a document may not exceed 15 pages exclusive of attachments, except chapter 11 disclosure statements/plans and objections. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(h) | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARB-CONT-001 | Dual-caption adversary filings: debtor in the first caption; plaintiffs and defendants and the adversary number in the second. | ARB-LBR 9004-1(c) | M |
| ARB-CONT-002 | A complaint commences an adversary proceeding. | FRBP 7001 | M |
| ARB-CONT-003 | The complaint states the case name, number, Code chapter, district and division, and whether the pleader consents to final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008(a) | M |
| ARB-CONT-004 | Caption substantially conforms to Official Form 416, using the appropriate adversary version. | FRBP 7010; FORMS | M |
| ARB-CONT-005 | Claims use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010; FRCP 10(b) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARB-CONT-101 | A written motion states its grounds with particularity and specifies the relief or order requested. | FRBP 9013(a)-(b) | M |
| ARB-CONT-102 | A motion includes a supporting memorandum of authorities unless made in open court. | ARB-LBR 9013-1(a) | M |
| ARB-LEN-002 | A motion, response, reply, and their supporting memoranda may not exceed 15 pages exclusive of attachments unless the court orders otherwise. | ARB-LBR 9013-1(b) | M |
| ARB-CERT-001 | For an ex parte or unopposed motion, include the required form of order; when a hearing is accelerated, the separate motion and hearing notice requirements apply. | ARB-LBR 9013-1(e), (i) | M |
| ARB-CERT-002 | Serve the motion and notice of hearing as required and file proof of service. | ARB-LBR 9013-1(k) | M |

The bankruptcy court's official rules use court-specific forms and local-rule provisions; the Federal Rules source governs where a local rule incorporates them. The hearing notice, proof of service, and proposed order are separate author-supplied filings or attachments where required.
