## Form of every paper (ARAP 513.5(b), 212(b))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALASKA-FORM-001 | Documents are on 8-1/2 x 11 inch opaque, unglazed white paper, printed or written on one side only. | ARAP 513.5(b)(1), (b)(6); 212(b) | M |
| ALASKA-FORM-002 | The text is in clear and legible black typeface. | ARAP 513.5(b)(2) | M |
| ALASKA-FORM-003 | The text of the document, including headings and footnotes, is at least 13 point (proportionally spaced) Times New Roman, Bookman Old Style, CG Times, New Century Schoolbook, Palatino Linotype or a substantially similar serifed roman text style. Non-serif text must be at least 12.5 point (ARAP 513.5(c)(1)(B)); the templates use the serif rule. | ARAP 513.5(c)(1)(A) | M |
| ALASKA-FORM-004 | The text is double-spaced. Headings and footnotes are single-spaced. A quotation of more than two lines is single-spaced and indented at least one-half inch on both sides. | ARAP 513.5(b)(3) | M |
| ALASKA-FORM-005 | The left and right margins of a brief are each one inch. | ARAP 212(b) | M |
| ALASKA-FORM-006 | No page carries more than 6-1/2 x 9-1/2 inches of printed or written matter. | ARAP 513.5(b)(5) | M |
| ALASKA-FORM-007 | A document longer than one page is numbered consecutively at the bottom center of each page. | ARAP 513.5(b)(4) | M |
| ALASKA-FORM-008 | Paper copies are two-hole punched at the center of the top of each page (paper filings only). | ARAP 513.5(b)(7) | M |

## Cover and first page (ARAP 212(b), 513.5(e))

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALASKA-COVER-001 | The front cover states (1) the name of the court and the case number; (2) the title of the case; (3) the nature of the proceeding; (4) the name of the court or agency below, the name of the individual who rendered the decision below, and the case number below; (5) the title of the document; (6) the names, addresses, telephone numbers and bar numbers of counsel and the name of the law firm or organization. | ARAP 212(b)(1)-(6) | M |
| ALASKA-COVER-002 | The first page shows the name and address of the law firm or organization and the attorney's bar number (or the bar number is on the signature page). | ARAP 513.5(e) | M |
| ALASKA-COVER-003 | In a criminal case the front cover carries a certificate, in the administrative director's form, stating whether the brief contains information confidential under AS 12.61.100 through 12.61.150. The form itself was not retrieved; the template leaves a slot for it. | ARAP 212(b); 513.5(e) | M |
| ALASKA-COVER-004 | If a party requests relief by a specific date in a petition for review, cross-petition, original application or motion, the date by which a decision is needed appears on the first page. | ARAP 513.5(e) | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALASKA-AMICUS-001 | An amicus curiae brief is in the form prescribed by Rule 212 and is duplicated and served under Rule 212(a)(2). | ARAP 517.2(b) | M |
| ALASKA-AMICUS-002 | An amicus participates only by notice of consent, by motion, or at the court's request, and files within the time allowed the party whose position it supports. | ARAP 517.2(a)-(b) | A |
| ALASKA-AMICUS-003 | The brief is bound with a red cover (paper bound copies). | ARAP 212(a)(2)(B) | A |
| ALASKA-AMICUS-004 | The cover and body follow the brief format in ALASKA-COVER-001 and ALASKA-FORM-001 through -007. The exemplar has a table of contents and a table of authorities after the cover. | ARAP 517.2(b), 212(c) | C |

## Answering (appellee's) briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALASKA-ANS-001 | The brief conforms to Rule 212(c)(1)(A) through (I) (table of contents with page references, table of authorities, the constitutional and statutory provisions, jurisdictional statement, statement of issues, statement of the case, argument with standard of review, short conclusion). A statement of jurisdiction, issues or case need not be repeated unless the appellee is dissatisfied with the appellant's; the list of parties need not be included. | ARAP 212(c)(2) | M |
| ALASKA-ANS-002 | Exclusive of appendices, the appellee's brief may not exceed 50 numbered pages. Numbering begins with the jurisdictional statement. Not machine-checked (DEF-LEN-001 is skipped; its page count includes the cover and tables). | ARAP 212(c)(4) | M |
| ALASKA-ANS-003 | Each major contention is preceded by a heading indicating its subject. | ARAP 212(c)(1)(H) | M |

## Briefs on the merits (appellant's and appellee's principal briefs)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALASKA-MERITS-001 | The brief of appellant contains, in order: a table of contents with page references; a table of cases and other authorities with page references; the provisions principally relied upon; a jurisdictional statement; a list of parties (unless the cover names all parties); the issues presented; the statement of the case; the argument; and a short conclusion. | ARAP 212(c)(1)(A)-(I) | M |
| ALASKA-MERITS-002 | Exclusive of appendices, the appellant's brief may not exceed 50 numbered pages. Not machine-checked (DEF-LEN-001 is skipped). | ARAP 212(c)(4) | M |
| ALASKA-MERITS-003 | The standard of review is identified for each issue, and plain-error argument is included for any issue not raised or ruled on below. | ARAP 212(c)(1)(H) | M |

|---|---|---|---|

## Motions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALASKA-MOT-001 | A motion is made by written motion (a stipulation is not enough) in the form prescribed by Rule 513.5(b), with a brief, complete statement of the reasons, any affidavit needed, the points and authorities relied on, and any statement of prior extensions or of the date by which a decision is needed. | ARAP 503(a)-(b) | M |
| ALASKA-MOT-002 | The motion is filed with the clerk with proof of service, and with the number of copies the rule requires (five for a motion the full supreme court must decide, three for a court of appeals motion). | ARAP 503(c) | M |
| ALASKA-MOT-003 | No page limit is stated for a motion in Rule 503. The template does not impose one. | ARAP 503 | A |
