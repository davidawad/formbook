## Amicus curiae briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-001 | An amicus curiae brief is in the form prescribed by Appellate Rule 212, and the form of a brief is governed by Rule 513.5(b)(1)-(5) and (c) and by Rule 212. | ARAP 517.2(b); 212(b); 513.5(a) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-002 | Documents are on 8-1/2 × 11 inch opaque, unglazed white paper, printed or written on one side only, in clear black type. | ARAP 513.5(b)(1), (b)(2), (b)(6) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-003 | The text of the document, including headings and footnotes, is at least 13 point proportionally spaced Times New Roman (or a substantially similar serifed roman face). | ARAP 513.5(c)(1)(A) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-004 | The text is double-spaced. Headings and footnotes are single-spaced. A quotation of more than two lines is single-spaced and indented at least one-half inch on both sides. | ARAP 513.5(b)(3) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-005 | The left and right margins are each one inch. | ARAP 212(b) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-006 | No page carries more than 6-1/2 × 9-1/2 inches of printed or written matter. | ARAP 513.5(b)(5) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-007 | A document longer than one page is numbered consecutively at the bottom center of each page. | ARAP 513.5(b)(4) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-FORM-008 | Paper copies are two-hole punched at the center of the top of each page. Applies to paper filings. | ARAP 513.5(b)(7) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-COVER-001 | The front cover states the name of the court and the case number; the title of the case; the nature of the proceeding; the name of the court or agency below, the name of the decision-maker below, and the case number below; the title of the document; and the name, address, telephone number and bar number of counsel and the law firm or organization. | ARAP 212(b)(1)-(6); 513.5(e) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-COVER-002 | In a criminal case the cover carries a certificate stating whether the brief contains information confidential under AS 12.61.100 through 12.61.150, in the form the administrative director specifies. | ARAP 212(b) | M |
| ALASKACTAPP-CONT-001 | The brief opens with a table of contents with page references and a table of authorities. The exemplar carries both; this template generates the table of contents and does not yet generate the table of authorities. | ARAP 212(c)(1)(A), exemplar | A |
| ALASKACTAPP-ATT-001 | The amicus curiae may participate only under Rule 517.2(a) (consent notice, motion, or the court's request), and files within the time allowed the party it supports. | ARAP 517.2(a)-(b) | A |
