# District of Alaska: filing form rules

Verified against the primary sources cited in this file on 2026-10-17.

Alias `akd`. Court-wide rules for the complaint and motion-brief folders. Primary local source: [District of Alaska Local Civil Rules, effective October 15, 2025](https://www.akd.uscourts.gov/sites/akd/files/local_rules/Local_Civil_Rules_10-2025.pdf), verified 2026-10-17 against the court's rules page and the PDF text. The Federal Rules were checked against the official U.S. Courts current-rules page on 2026-10-17. Re-check the court rules page and linked primary texts before each release and at least every six months; check earlier when the court announces amendments or a new effective date.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AKD-FORM-001 | Paper is 8.5 × 11 inches; text margins are at least 1 inch on every side, including footers. | AKD-LCR 7.5(a)(1)(B), (a)(2)(A) | M |
| AKD-FORM-002 | Text is double-spaced. Quotations over 50 words or five lines may be single-spaced and indented. | AKD-LCR 7.5(a)(1)(A) | M |
| AKD-FORM-003 | Type is at least 13-point and proportionally spaced, or equivalent. The rule names no typeface. | AKD-LCR 7.5(a)(1)(D) | M |
| AKD-FORM-004 | A document longer than one page has a page number and a footer with the case name and number at the bottom of each page. | AKD-LCR 7.5(a)(1)(C) | M |
| AKD-FORM-005 | Electronic filings are PDF and text-searchable. | AKD-LCR 7.5(a)(3) | M |
| AKD-COVER-001 | The first-page counsel information appears upper left, at least one inch below the top; it includes name, address, telephone, email, and party represented. | AKD-LCR 7.5(b)(1)(A)-(C) | M |
| AKD-COVER-002 | The court title is centered, at least one inch below the top edge and at least one-half inch below the counsel block. | AKD-LCR 7.5(b)(2) | M |
| AKD-COVER-003 | Leave upper right clear for clerk marks; place the case number below the court title at right, including assigned judge initials. | AKD-LCR 7.5(b)(3)(A)-(B) | M |
| AKD-COVER-004 | Show the full action title below the court title, left of center, and the document title centered below the court title and case number. | AKD-LCR 7.5(b)(4)-(5) | M |
| AKD-CERT-001 | Attorney name and Alaska bar number appear beneath the signature, and the attorney identifies the represented party. | AKD-LCR 7.5(c)(1)-(2) | M |
| AKD-ELEC-001 | Filing PDF text remains selectable and searchable; no scanned page image substitutes for text. | AKD-LCR 7.5(a)(3) | M |
| AKD-ELEC-002 | Do not add interlineations; conventional paper filings are one-sided. | AKD-LCR 7.5(a)(1)(E) | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AKD-CONT-101 | A motion states the relief requested and the applicable points and authorities. | AKD-LCR 7.1(a)(1)-(2) | M |
| AKD-CONT-102 | For a motion requesting oral argument, put “Oral Argument Requested” immediately below the motion title. | AKD-LCR 7.1(f) | M |
| AKD-LEN-001 | A Rule 12(b) memorandum is limited to 35 pages or 10,000 words; a reply is limited to 20 pages or 5,700 words. | AKD-LCR 7.4(a)(1) | M |
| AKD-LEN-002 | Exclude caption, signature blocks, tables of contents and authorities, Rand notices, and reproduced statutes from the page and word limits. | AKD-LCR 7.4(a)(4) | M |
| AKD-CONT-103 | Rule 12(b)(1) permits a motion asserting lack of subject-matter jurisdiction. | FRCP 12(b)(1) | M |
| AKD-CONT-104 | A Rule 12 motion does not require a proposed order. | AKD-LCR 7.1(b)(1) | M |

The complaint and motion-brief folders use local rules 7.5 and 10.1. Rule 8.1 is complaint-specific; it is included in the motion-brief README only as a stated-rule citation, not as a form requirement for the memorandum. The notice of motion and proposed order are separate documents.
