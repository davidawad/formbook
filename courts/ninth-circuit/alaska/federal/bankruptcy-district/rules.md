Alias `akb`. Court-wide rules for the adversary-complaint folder. The Federal Rules were checked against the official U.S. Courts current-rules pages on 2026-10-17. Re-check the court's local-rules page and linked primary texts before each release and at least every six months; check earlier when an amendment or new effective date is announced.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AKB-FORM-001 | Paper is 8.5 × 11 inches, white, with margins of at least 1 inch on all sides. | AKB-LBR 9004-1(a)(1)-(2) | M |
| AKB-FORM-002 | Text is double-spaced or 1.5-spaced. Quotations over 50 words are single-spaced and indented. | AKB-LBR 9004-1(a)(4) | M |
| AKB-FORM-003 | Type is 12-point or 10-pitch. No typeface is named. | AKB-LBR 9004-1(a)(7) | M |
| AKB-FORM-004 | Each page is numbered at the bottom. | AKB-LBR 9004-1(a)(5) | M |
| AKB-COVER-001 | The attorney's name, address, telephone, email, and party represented appear upper left, beginning at least 1 inch from the top edge. | AKB-LBR 9004-1(c)(2)(A)-(B) | M |
| AKB-COVER-002 | The caption identifies the court, case title and number, adversary number, and document title; substantially conform to Official Form 416. | FRBP 9004(a), 7010; AKB-LBR 9004-1(c)(1) | M |
| AKB-CERT-001 | Attorney names are typed beneath signatures. | AKB-LBR 9004-1(a)(9) | M |
| AKB-ELEC-001 | The court's ECF filing must be legible and conform to its prescribed electronic filing process. | FRBP 9004(a); AKB-LBR 5005-1 | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AKB-CONT-001 | An adversary proceeding is commenced by complaint. | FRBP 7001 | M |
| AKB-CONT-002 | The caption follows Civil Rule 10 as applied in bankruptcy proceedings and substantially conforms to Official Form 416D. | FRBP 7010; AKB-LBR 9004-1(c)(1) | M |
| AKB-CONT-003 | State the jurisdiction reference, including the bankruptcy case name and number, Code chapter, and district and division. | FRBP 7008(a) | M |
| AKB-CONT-004 | State whether the pleader consents to entry of final orders or judgment by the bankruptcy court. | FRBP 7008(a); AKB-LBR 7012-1 | M |
| AKB-CONT-005 | Claims use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRBP 7010; FRCP 10(b) | M |
| AKB-CERT-002 | File a completed summons for each defendant and pay any required filing fee; conventional filing also requires Form 1040. These are separate items, not part of the complaint. | AKB-LBR 7003-1(a)-(b) | M |

No typeface is specified for adversary complaints. Form 416D supplies a model caption; the rule requires substantial conformity, not exact reproduction. The adversary summons and any conventional-filing cover sheet are separate filings.
