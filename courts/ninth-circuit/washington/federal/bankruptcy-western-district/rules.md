## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAWB-FORM-001 | Pleadings use 8.5 × 11 inch format, a standard embedded font at 11 or 12 pt, and double spacing. No face family is named. | LBR 9004-1(a) | M |
| WAWB-FORM-002 | Except official or clerk-provided forms, pleadings have line numbers in the left margin. | LBR 9004-1(b)(1) | M |
| WAWB-COVER-001 | Judge's name appears at upper right of page 1. A motion and its hearing notice also have the notations required by LBR 9013-1(d). | LBR 9004-1(b)(2) | M |
| WAWB-ELEC-001 | Each page footer places abbreviated paper title and page number at bottom left, and preparer's name, current mailing address and telephone at bottom right. | LBR 9004-1(b)(3) | M |
| WAWB-ELEC-002 | Exhibits and attachments are electronically filed and linked to the related pleading; all are letter size, reduced or mounted on letter paper if needed. | LBR 9004-1(d) | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAWB-CONT-001 | Adversary pleading caption follows FRBP 7010 and substantially conforms to the applicable Form 416. Caption identifies court, case title/number, adversary number, and document nature. | FRBP 7010; FRBP 9004(b) | M |
| WAWB-CONT-002 | Adversary complaint includes a jurisdictional statement and whether the pleader consents to final orders or judgment. | FRBP 7008(a); FRCP 8(a) | M |
| WAWB-CONT-003 | Allegations use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b), applied by FRBP 7010 | M |
| WAWB-CERT-001 | Each party files a separate Notice Regarding Final Adjudication and Consent with its initial adversary pleading, repeating the statements required by FRBP 7008(a) and 7012(b). | LBR 7012-1(a) | M (separate filing) |
| WAWB-CERT-002 | Plaintiff files a certificate of service within 14 days after service of summons and complaint. | LBR 7004-1 | M (separate filing) |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAWB-CONT-004 | A motion's notice may be combined with the motion if caption identifies the combined filing, notice comes first in the text, and each part has its own heading. | LBR 9013-1(c)(3)(A) | M |
| WAWB-CONT-005 | Notice states hearing date/time/place, relief/grounds if not combined, response filing deadline, and warns that the court may grant relief before hearing without further notice if no response is filed. | LBR 9013-1(c)(3)(B) | M |
| WAWB-CONT-006 | Motion includes all supporting reasons and necessary memorandum, plus evidence to be presented. A proposed order is attached separately except for ex parte or stipulated requests. | LBR 9013-1(d)(1)(A), (D) | M |
| WAWB-COVER-002 | Top-right notation includes assigned judge, chapter, hearing location/date/time, and response date. | LBR 9013-1(d)(1)(B) | M |
| WAWB-LEN-001 | Opening and response memoranda are capped at 24 pages for summary judgment or other dispositive motions and 12 pages for other motions; reply brief is limited to half the applicable opening limit, absent prior court approval. | LBR 9013-1(d)(1)(C) | M |
| WAWB-LEN-002 | Motions other than summary judgment and lien avoidance generally have at least 21 days' notice; summary judgment and lien avoidance have at least 28 days. | LBR 9013-1(d)(2)(D), (F) | M |

## Re-check note

The court's 2026 amendments were proposed on August 26, 2026; the comment period ended September 30, 2026, and the court announced final amendments would take effect December 1, 2026. The proposal summary lists changes to LBR 9013-1(d)(3) and (h), not the provisions cited above. Until an adopting order takes effect, the December 1, 2023 text remains the governing source.
