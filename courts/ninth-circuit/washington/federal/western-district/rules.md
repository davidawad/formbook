## Requirements

### Form and caption

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAWD-FORM-001 | Pages are 8½ × 11 inches. | LCR 10(d), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-FORM-002 | Leave at least three inches above the court title on the first page; use one-inch margins; type must be at least 12 point and proportionally spaced. | LCR 10(e)(1), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-FORM-003 | Text is double-spaced or exactly 24 points apart. | LCR 10(e)(1), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-FORM-004 | Put line numbers in the left margin at least one-half inch left of the text. | LCR 10(e)(5), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-FORM-005 | The footer has the abbreviated document title and case number with the page number at left; the preparing attorney's firm, mailing address, and phone at right, subject to the rule's filing-specific exception. | LCR 10(e)(3), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-COVER-001 | The first page states “United States District Court, Western District of Washington” and, below the docket number, the paper's title and the party presenting it. | LCR 10(e)(2), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-FORM-006 | The document is dated and signed as required by FRCP 11 and LCR 11. | LCR 10(e)(4), WAWD-LCR; FRCP 11 | M |

### Contents and length

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAWD-CONT-001 | A complaint has a caption satisfying FRCP 10(a); claims and defenses are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(a)–(b), FRCP | M |
| WAWD-CONT-002 | If a complaint demands a jury trial, the demand appears in the caption and in a demand paragraph. | FRCP 38(b), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-CONT-003 | A motion that requests oral argument states “ORAL ARGUMENT REQUESTED” in the caption. | LCR 7(b)(4), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-LEN-001 | A motion to dismiss and its response may not exceed 8,400 words or 24 pages; a reply may not exceed 4,200 words or 12 pages. | LCR 7(e)(4), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-CONT-004 | A memorandum subject to a word limit includes the rule-required word-count certification in the signature block. | LCR 7(e)(6), WAWD-LCR | M |
| WAWD-CONT-005 | A motion to dismiss is noted no earlier than 28 days after filing unless a rule or order provides otherwise. The filing party supplies the correct noting date. | LCR 7(d)(4), WAWD-LCR | A |

## Document-type notes

- **Complaint:** the civil cover sheet, corporate disclosure statement, and summons are separate filings where required. The jury demand applies only if the party elects one.
- **Motion brief:** the local page and word limits above apply by motion type. A proposed order, declarations, exhibits, and judge-specific copies are separate filings where required.
- The rule prescribes a proportionally spaced typeface and size, not a particular font family.
