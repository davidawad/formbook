## Court-wide requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAEB-FORM-001 | Documents are letter size, one-sided, clearly legible and double-spaced. Quotations may be single-spaced; footnotes remain double-spaced. | LBR 9004-1(a)(1)-(3) | M |
| WAEB-FORM-002 | Body text uses either a proportionally spaced face at least 14 pt or, in a brief, a monospaced face no more than 10.5 characters per inch. Text is upright roman, non-script. | LBR 9004-1(a)(3) | M |
| WAEB-LEN-001 | No pleading, document or brief averages more than 280 words per page, including footnotes and quotations; statutory/rule addenda are excluded. | LBR 9004-1(a)(3) | M |
| WAEB-COVER-001 | Court title appears on or below line 5 of page 1; attorney/firm name, mailing address and telephone appear left of line 5. | LBR 9004-1(a)(4), (6) | M |
| WAEB-CONT-001 | Right of center opposite the caption: case number, related case number above adversary number when applicable, document nature, filing party name/status, and jury demand if made. | LBR 9004-1(a)(5)(A)-(D) | M |
| WAEB-CERT-001 | Pleadings are signed as FRBP 5005(a)(2) and 9011 require; type names beneath signature lines. | LBR 9004-1(a)(4) | M |
| WAEB-ELEC-001 | Footer at bottom left repeats an abbreviated document name and page number. E-filing follows LBR 5005-3 and clerk administrative procedures. | LBR 9004-1(a)(7), (c) | M |

## Adversary complaint

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAEB-COVER-002 | A paper-filed adversary proceeding includes Official Form 1040; the rule does not require that cover sheet for electronic filings. | LBR 7003-1(a) | M (paper filing only) |
| WAEB-CONT-002 | Adversary caption follows FRBP 7010 and substantially conforms to Form 416; complaint names the court, action, file number and pleading designation. | FRBP 7010; FRCP 10(a) | M |
| WAEB-CONT-003 | Allegations use numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to one set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(b), applied by FRBP 7010 | M |
| WAEB-AUTH-001 | Pleader states whether the bankruptcy judge may enter final orders or judgment. The related local consent rule LBR 7008-1 is abrogated; FRBP 7008(a) controls. | FRBP 7008(a); LBR index | A (required content) |
| WAEB-ELEC-002 | Complaint and summons service follows FRBP 7004 and applicable local service rules; the summons form is prepared at clerk request. | FRBP 7004; LBR 7003-1(b), 7004-1 | M |

## Motion brief

| ID | Requirement | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAEB-CONT-004 | In an adversary proceeding, moving party may elect notice and hearing under LBR 2002-1; otherwise obtain a hearing date through the court website and allow the applicable objection period. | LBR 9013-1(a)(1)-(2) | M |
| WAEB-CONT-005 | A motion may be supported by a brief memorandum of reasons and authorities; if the court requires it, file and serve the memorandum with the motion under LBR 5005-1(c). Washington, other-state, federal, and bankruptcy case reporter citations follow LBR 9013-1(d)(2). | LBR 9013-1(d)(1)-(2) | M |
| WAEB-CONT-006 | A notice after notice and hearing states purpose, how and when to object, and that the action may occur without a hearing if no timely objection is filed. | LBR 2002-1(a)(1)-(2) | M (when applicable) |
| WAEB-LEN-002 | Under LBR 2002-1(c)(1), provide at least 21 days plus three additional days if FRBP 9006(f) applies. | LBR 2002-1(c)(1) | M (when applicable) |

## Re-check note

GO 26-01, effective July 17, 2026, amended only LBR 3022-1 and 7055-1; it does not change the requirements above. The court compilation was checked through that date. The court states LBR 7008-1 is abrogated, so the federal adversary pleading consent statement is retained as author-supplied required content rather than treated as a local layout rule.
