## Requirements

### Form

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAED-FORM-001 | Pages are 8½ × 11 inches. Documents have line numbers in the left margin. | LCivR 10(d), WAED-LCR | M |
| WAED-FORM-002 | Use a serif or traditional Roman typeface, proportionally spaced at 14 points or larger. | LCivR 10(d), WAED-LCR | M |
| WAED-FORM-003 | Text is double-spaced. Quotations, headings, captions, signature blocks, and certifications may be single-spaced. | LCivR 10(d), WAED-LCR | M |
| WAED-FORM-004 | Every page has a footer with the title or abbreviated title followed by the page number, in sequential order. | LCivR 10(a)(1)(E), 10(a)(2), WAED-LCR | M |

| WAED-COVER-002 | Caption appears at left below the court title; case number and pleading title appear at right. | LCivR 10(a)(1)(C)–(D), WAED-LCR | M |

### Contents and length

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAED-CONT-001 | A complaint has the caption required by FRCP 10(a); claims and defenses are in numbered paragraphs, each limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances. | FRCP 10(a)–(b), FRCP | M |
| WAED-CONT-002 | A motion brief states its requested relief and supporting grounds; motion and briefing conventions in LCivR 7 apply. | LCivR 7(b)(1), WAED-LCR | M |
| WAED-LEN-001 | A dispositive motion, including a motion to dismiss, may not exceed 20 pages, excluding caption, signature, and certificates. | LCivR 7(f)(1), WAED-LCR | M |
| WAED-CONT-003 | For a dispositive motion, the filer sets a hearing date at least 50 days after filing or follows an applicable exception; the template cannot determine the proper date for a case. | LCivR 7(i)(2)(B), WAED-LCR | A |

## Document-type notes

- **Complaint:** the local disclosure statement rule is reserved; FRCP 7.1 governs corporate disclosure when applicable. Civil cover sheet and summons are separate filings.
- **Motion brief:** LCivR 7(b)(1) requires motion and supporting materials in one filing. A motion is noted for hearing under LCivR 7(i); dispositive motions have the 50-day minimum and 20-page cap above.
- The local form rule specifies “serif or traditional Roman” and proportional spacing, but does not name a typeface family.
