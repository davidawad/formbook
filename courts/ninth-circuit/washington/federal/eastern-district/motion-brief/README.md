## Layout

- Caption page: counsel (lines 1 to 6 here, single spaced), court title on lines 7 to 9 (on or below line 5), caption with the hearing notation at the right, body starts after the caption.
- Table of contents: generated by the template from the body's headings (`outline`, depth 2), so its page numbers are the render's own; on its own page after the caption.
- Body: 21 numbered lines at a 32 pt pitch (double spacing at 14 pt; the exemplar measures 32.2 pt for its 14 pt Times), paragraphs with a 0.5 in first-line indent, headings bold and left aligned.
- Signature block after the conclusion ("DATED this 6th day of July, 2026", "By s/" the signer), then the certificate of service on its own page.

## Markdown contract (for samples)

- YAML frontmatter, then the body. Body chunks are separated by blank lines: `#` (level 1, bold caps), `##` (level 2, bold), `###` (bold italic); a list (`1.` items, lettered A., B. by the template); or a paragraph (not numbered).
- A heading must fit on one numbered line (`heading-line` asserts this); a long sub-heading is split over two lines in the sample (`## B. Plaintiff Fails to State an Actionable Retaliation Claim.` fits on one line at 14 pt).
- The body section `# CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE` is split off by the template and rendered after the signature block on its own page.
- Frontmatter fields: `case_number`, `caption.parties` (`{names, designation}`; "v." is inserted between parties, so do not add it), `brief_title`, `footer_title` (abbreviated, see below), `counsel`, `hearing` (`date`, `type`, optional `judge`), `division`, `jury_demand`, `toc` (true to generate the table of contents), `date`, `signature` (`firm`, `signer`, `lines`).
- Not supported: tables, footnotes, raw typst. The table of authorities is not generated (gap 2).

## Known gaps and open choices

1. **The exemplar's type size is below the rule.** The filed motion's body is about 12 pt Times New Roman (its font size measures 12 pt, not 14 pt). LCivR 10(d) asks for 14 pt. The template follows the rule. This exemplar is the only motion-to-dismiss exemplar saved for this doc type; no second filer's motion was found in the search (CourtListener RECAP, `type=r&court=waed`, motions and orders listed). Tier 2 is therefore judged against one filer's document, which does not meet the EVAL-LOOP's 2-to-3-filer rule.
2. **Table of authorities not generated.** The exemplar's table of authorities (pages iii and iv) is not produced; the sample does not transcribe it. A TOA would need a citation index the markdown contract does not carry.
3. **Excerpt.** The sample is an excerpt (11 pages rendered). Sections IV.C to IV.E, the July to November 2024 facts, and the footnotes are not transcribed. The text layer of the exemplar is jumbled in places (its floating text runs out of order), so some sentences are reassembled in reading order; the frontmatter comment says so.
4. **Page numbers.** Numbering runs continuously (1, 2, …) through the caption, TOC and certificate. The exemplar's TOC uses roman numerals and starts the memorandum at page 1; LCivR 10(a)(2) asks for sequential pagination only, so the continuous numbering satisfies it. Recorded as an ACCEPTED choice.
5. **Footer title.** The footer uses the abbreviated title "BATTELLE'S MOTION TO DISMISS - n" (LCivR 10(a)(1)(E) allows an abbreviated title). The full title wraps to two lines at 14 pt in the 6.4 in text width.
6. **Hearing.** The caption shows the date and "Without Oral Argument" (the exemplar's form). LCivR 7(i)(1) also asks for a time and place where argument is set; none is shown because none is set.
7. **Page limit and timing.** LCivR 7(f)(1) (20 pages for a dispositive motion) and LCivR 7(i)(2)(B) (50 days) are not checked by the template. Author responsibility.
8. **Typeface.** LCivR 10(d) names no face (serif or traditional Roman, proportionate at 14 pt or more), so EB Garamond stays; no `docs/FONTS.md` substitute applies. EB Garamond at 14 pt has a smaller x-height than Times at 14 pt (see the complaint README, gap 2).
9. **Check scripts not run.** `tools/check.sh` expects a court folder with `court.json` and `rules.md`; this doc-type folder is not that shape, and the brief forbids touching `tools/`. Render, overlay and the page review above are the checks for this doc type.

## Engines

### docx (`docx/`)

Build and render: `sh docx/build-reference.sh docx/reference.docx` (the reference is generated), then `sh tools/render.sh --engine docx <this folder> usage/INPUT-waed-motion-brief-hartsockmotiontodismiss.md`, which writes `usage/OUTPUT-waed-motion-brief-hartsockmotiontodismiss.docx` and `usage/OUTPUT-waed-motion-brief-hartsockmotiontodismiss.docx.pdf` (LibreOffice 26.8 conversion; EB Garamond embedded). `docx/motion-brief.lua` lays out the caption (with the hearing notation), the table of contents, the memorandum and the signature block; `docx/pleading.lua` holds the shared helpers. `docx/render.sh` runs two passes: pass 1 writes the headings it finds and their pages come from its PDF; pass 2 writes the table of contents with those pages and must paginate like pass 1.

What matches the typst output (checked on the LibreOffice PDF, 11 pages, the same first line on every page, and the same TOC pages: I 3, II 4, III 6, IV 7, A 7, B 7, V 10, certificate 11):

- Letter; 1.1 in left and 1 in right margin; 72 pt top margin; 14 pt EB Garamond; 21 numbered lines on a 32 pt grid (header text box); exact 32 pt body lines and 16 pt single lines for counsel, caption, signature and TOC.
- Counsel on lines 1 to 6 or more (padded to whole lines, at least 4 numbered lines), court title, caption on line 11 with the right and bottom rules, case number and bold title at right, "Hearing date" and "Without Oral Argument".
- Table of contents on its own page (TOC field, cached result, dot leaders, level 2 indented 0.5 in); the memorandum starts on a new page.
- Headings flush left (level 1 bold caps), paragraphs with a 0.5 in first-line indent, not numbered; the signature block after the conclusion; the certificate of service on its own page after the signature.
- Double rule at the left, single at the right (page borders); footer `TITLE - n` on every page, sequential numbering.
- Widow and orphan control on, as in the typst output (the page breaks of the body match on all 11 pages).

What differs (not a rule; tier 2 or 3):

- The table of contents is a Word TOC field with its cached entries; Word shows the cached page numbers until the field is updated (F9). The page numbers are the LibreOffice pagination, and Word may paginate differently; update the field in Word if the pages move.
- Word numbers the lines with the header text box, not with `w:lnNumType` (see the complaint's Engines note).
- Word and LibreOffice may break a line differently from the typst output; the page starts above are the LibreOffice PDF's.
- Table of authorities: not generated, as in the typst output (gap 2 above).

Checks: `tools/check.sh` cannot run on a doc-type folder: it reads `<court>/court.json` and `<court>/checks.yaml`, and this folder has neither. The docx PDF was checked by hand: every word of the sample is in the PDF (the two exceptions are em-dash joins that `pdftotext` splits), all 11 pages were viewed as PNG (`reviews/docx-review.md`).
