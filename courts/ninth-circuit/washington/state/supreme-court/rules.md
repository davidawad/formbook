# Washington Supreme Court: briefs on the merits, motions, petitions for review, original actions, and reconsideration

Alias `wash`. Scope: briefs of petitioner and respondent (including briefs on the merits and answering briefs), reply briefs, amicus briefs, supplemental briefs, motions (including motions to strike and motions for discretionary review under RAP 13.5), petitions for review and answers and replies (RAP 13.4), original-action petitions against a state officer (RAP 16.2), personal restraint petitions (RAP 16.7), motions for reconsideration and amicus memoranda on reconsideration (RAP 12.4), objections to a commissioner's or clerk's ruling (RAP 17.7), and statements of additional grounds (RAP 10.10), filed in the Supreme Court of the State of Washington. Verified against the Washington Rules of Appellate Procedure as printed on courts.wa.gov (RAP 10.3, 10.4, 10.7, 10.10, 12.4, 13.4, 13.5, 13.7, 16.2, 16.7, 17.4, 17.7, 18.17 and RAP Form 5; the amendment date printed on each rule is in the Sources table), GR 14 and GR 30 as printed on courts.wa.gov, and the Washington State Appellate Courts' Portal electronic filing instructions dated April 13, 2017, on 2026-10-21. RAP 18.17 and most rules that cite it are phrased "should". This file treats those directives as the requirement for tier 1 and cites them as M, quoting the operative word where it matters.

Open conflict: GR 14(a) sets a 3-inch minimum top margin on the first page of a paper filed in any Washington court, while RAP 18.17(a)(1) sets 1½-inch top margins for appellate filings (see WASH-FORM-003).

Open question: RAP 13.5(a) places a motion for discretionary review in the Court of Appeals, but the Supreme Court exemplar of a motion for discretionary review was filed in the Supreme Court. This file does not decide the filing court (see WASH-PET-001).
