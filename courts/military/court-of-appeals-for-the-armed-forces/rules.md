# U.S. Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces (opening brief, petition supplement): rules

Alias `armfor`. Scope: the brief on behalf of an appellant in a granted, certified or mandatory-review case (`opening-brief/`, CAAF Rule 24) and the supplement to a petition for grant of review (`petition-supplement/`, CAAF Rule 21), filed by counsel electronically. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each October 1 (the court's rule editions take effect October 1: the current "Rules of Practice and Procedure" are effective October 1, 2026, superseding the edition amended through September 26, 2025) and whenever the court posts an amendment notice on its Rules page.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a CAAF rule. **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **14-point proportional type, footnotes included**: "All printed matter, including footnotes, must appear in proportional type, e.g., Times New Roman, in fourteen-point type" (Rule 37(a)). Rule 37(a) speaks of papers "not filed electronically", but Rule 24(a) makes "All briefs ... conform to the printing, copying, and style requirements of Rule 37" and Rule 21(b) makes the supplement "conform to the provisions of Rules 35A and 37", so both doc types carry it.
2. **Word limits**: brief and answer 13,000 words, reply 6,500 (Rule 24(b)); supplement and answer 9,000, reply 4,500 (Rule 21(b)). Each carries a word-count certificate.
3. **Caption is prescribed** (Rule 24(a) form): "IN THE UNITED STATES COURT OF APPEALS FOR THE ARMED FORCES", UNITED STATES with its role, "v.", the accused's full name, grade and service with role, the brief title "BRIEF ON BEHALF OF (APPELLANT ...)", "Crim. App. Dkt. No." and "USCA Dkt. No.".
4. **Section list is prescribed** for briefs (Rule 24(a)) and for the supplement (Rule 21(b)); a subject index and table of authorities are required for any pleading of ten or more pages (Rule 37(c)(1)).
5. **Electronic filing by e-mail** to efiling@armfor.uscourts.gov, with opposing counsel copied (Rule 36(b)).
