# U.S. Air Force Court of Criminal Appeals (opening brief, answering brief): rules

Alias `afcca`. Scope: the brief on behalf of an appellant with assignments of error (`opening-brief/`) and the United States' answer to assignments of error (`answering-brief/`), filed electronically by appellate counsel. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 and whenever the court posts a new edition of its Rules of Practice and Procedure (current edition "Effective 29 June 2026", published together with the Joint Rules of Appellate Procedure for Courts of Criminal Appeals, JRAP).

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a rule (AFCCA Rules or JRAP). **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **12-point proportional type, Times New Roman or Century Schoolbook named as examples** (Rule 17.1(a)); not FRAP's 14 point. The Air Force court differs from CAAF (14 point).
2. **Page limit, not word limit**: filings "shall not exceed 50 pages, excluding indices, attachments, and appendices", and "Headings, footnotes, and quotations count toward the page limit" (Rule 17.3). No word-count certificate is required.
3. **Index and table of authorities only above 30 pages** (Rule 18.1).
4. **No images in briefs** (Rule 17.1(a)); the PDF file name and e-mail subject line follow fixed patterns (Rule 17.1(b)-(d)).
5. **Caption and section list are prescribed** by Appendix A: ")" column caption with "BRIEF ON BEHALF OF APPELLANT", "Before Panel No. X", "No. ACM XXXXX", "[Date Filed]"; "TO THE HONORABLE, THE JUDGES OF THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE COURT OF CRIMINAL APPEALS:"; Assignment(s) of Error in bold, roman-numbered; Statement of the Case; Statement of Facts; Argument with a standard of review and a prayer for relief after each error.
6. **Answers follow the same format** and respond separately to each assigned error (Rule 18.3).

## Doc type: opening-brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| AFCCA-CONT-010 | Assignments of error are filed in compliance with Appendix A: "Assignment(s) of Error" (each in bold type; consecutive roman numerals if more than one); "Statement of the Case" (chronology: trial dates, nature of charges, pleas, findings, sentence, convening authority action); "Statement of Facts" (record page numbers and exhibit designations); "Argument" (each assignment of error in bold, separate argument, applicable standard of review, followed by a prayer for the specified relief); optional "Appendix"; signature; certificate of filing and service. | Rule 18.1; APP-A | M |
