# U.S. Court of Military Commission Review (opening brief, answering brief): rules

Alias `mc`. Scope: the brief on behalf of appellant (`opening-brief/`) and the brief on behalf of appellee answering it (`answering-brief/`) in the United States Court of Military Commission Review (CMCR), filed by counsel, usually by e-mail. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each December 1 and whenever the Office of Military Commissions posts revised CMCR Rules of Practice (the edition read here is the February 3, 2016 revision, "Email Addresses Updated on December 13, 2022").

Access note: `www.mc.mil` returns HTTP 403 to this box. The rules and the new exemplars were read from Internet Archive captures of the Office of Military Commissions' own URLs. The CDX index of `mc.mil/Portals/0/` holds no CMCR rules file newer than the December 13, 2022 update (captured 2024-12-04); a later edition is not verified.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a CMCR rule. **C** = convention (from the exemplars). **T** = project/tooling rule. **A** = author responsibility.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **Times New Roman 12 point is named** for the original: "typed double-spaced in Times New Roman, 12 point" (Rule 15(e)). The certificate form itself is garbled: it calls for "12-point font ... with monospaced typeface, such as Times New Roman or Courier New" and a characters-per-inch statement (Rule 15(i); APP-1). Filers adapt it (EX-QOSI states a word count only).
2. **Both a page and a word limit apply**: appellant's brief and appellee's answer 30 pages (Rule 15(f)) and 14,000 words or 1,300 monospaced lines (Rule 15(g)); reply 15 pages and half the type volume.
3. **TOC and table of authorities above ten pages** (APP-1), not 30.
4. **Appendix 1 is copied from the Army court's rules** and cites "Rule 14(i)" and "Rule 14(e)" in its certificate form, while the operative rules are 15(g)-(i) and 15(e). EX-HICKS-OB and the Khadr brief title the certificate "Rule 14(i)"; EX-QOSI uses "Rule 15". The rule numbers in the body control.
5. **Filings are public**: unclassified filings are released on the Office of Military Commissions website (Rule 30), and the posted copies carry "UNCLASSIFIED//FOR PUBLIC RELEASE" markings (EX-NASHIRI, EX-QOSI).

## Doc type: opening-brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MC-LEN-001 | "Briefs of the appellants shall not exceed 30 pages", not including appendices, unless authorized. | Rule 15(f)(1) | M |
| MC-LEN-002 | A brief of the appellants is acceptable "if it contains no more than 14,000 words; or no more than 1,300 lines of monospaced text"; a reply half that. Headings, footnotes and quotations count; the index, table of authorities, appendix and certificates do not. | Rule 15(g)(1)-(3) | M |
| MC-CONT-001 | Contents in the APP-1 order: Table of Contents and table of cited authorities (required if the brief exceeds ten pages); Issue(s) Presented (in upper case); Statement of Statutory Jurisdiction; Statement of the Case; Statement of Facts (with record page references; may be folded into each error in distinct paragraphs); Errors and Argument (each assignment of error in upper case, separate argument, standard of review, prayer for the specific relief); Appendix (optional); signature; Certificate of Compliance; Certificate of Service. | Rule 15(a), (e)(2); APP-1 | M |
| MC-CONT-002 | An appendix may carry unpublished or not readily available authorities, record extracts, statutes, rules or regulations; other matter needs a motion. | Rule 15(d) | M |
| MC-CONT-003 | Issues personally raised by an appellant go in a footnote or an appendix to the brief. | Rule 16(b) | M (conditional) |
| MC-CONT-004 | Timing: in cases referred under R.M.C. 1111, the defense brief within 30 days after detailed defense counsel receives the authenticated record; in government appeals under R.M.C. 908, the government brief within ten days of the notice of appeal. | Rule 15(c)(1)-(2) | A |
| MC-CONT-005 | Section sequence seen: EX-NASHIRI: Table of Contents, Table of Authorities, Issues Presented, Statement of Statutory Jurisdiction, Statement of the Case, Statement of Facts, Argument, Conclusion, Certificate of Compliance, Certificate of Service; EX-QOSI: Table of Contents, Table of Authorities, Issues Presented, Statement of Jurisdiction, Statement of the Case and Facts, Errors and Argument, signature, Certificate of Compliance with Rule 15, Certificate of Service. All three filers follow APP-1 closely. | EX-NASHIRI, EX-QOSI, EX-HICKS-OB | C |
