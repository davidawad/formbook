# Navy-Marine Corps Court of Criminal Appeals: briefs

Alias `nmcca`. Scope: opening and answering briefs. Verified against the NMCCA Rules of Appellate Procedure (2022, "Effective 1 June 2022", https://www.jag.navy.mil/documents/4691/2022_NMCCA_Rules_of_App_Proc.pdf), Rule 17, on 2026-10-20.

Local rules not retrieved: the Service court rules that Rule 17 defers to for length limits were not located (https://www.jag.navy.mil/about/organization/ojag/code-05/nmcca/ returned HTTP 200 with links to the NMCCA rules only).

| NMCCA-FORM-004 | All printed matter, including footnotes, is 14-point proportional type (for example Times New Roman or Century Schoolbook). Courier and other non-proportional fonts are not acceptable. This is larger than the 12-point default. | NMCCA-R Rule 17(c) | M |
| NMCCA-FORM-005 | Text is double-spaced; the caption, headers and signature blocks are excepted. | NMCCA-R Rule 17(c) | M |
| NMCCA-FORM-006 | Every page except the first is numbered. | NMCCA-R Rule 17(c) | M |
