# Bankruptcy Court, District of South Carolina: adversary complaints and motion papers

Alias `scb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion papers. Verified against the court-posted South Carolina Local Bankruptcy Rules (https://www.scb.uscourts.gov/pdf/Local_Rules/2026LocalRulesAugAmendments.pdf, "Effective as of 08/17/2026") and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure on 2026-10-20.

Local rules retrieved; the extracted text has no typeface, point size, margin or spacing rule for papers (LR 5005-4 governs electronic filing only). Court-wide form requirements were not located.
