# South Carolina "Court of Chancery": form of papers

Alias `ctchansc`. Scope: papers in a South Carolina court styled "Court of Chancery". No court of that name appears in the South Carolina Judicial Branch court-rules index (categories checked on 2026-10-21: ADR, Appellate, Civil, Criminal, E-filing, Evidence, Family, Magistrate, Probate). Equity matters are heard in the circuit courts, and their appeals go to the Court of Appeals or the Supreme Court (see `usage/SOURCE.md`). No Chancery rules exist to cite. The only published form rule for papers in this court family is SCACR 267, which binds papers filed in the Supreme Court and the Court of Appeals. Its M rows below apply to papers filed in those courts. Nothing here is applied to a "Court of Chancery" filing until David decides whether this entry should stay (see the BLOCKED note in the PR).

No court-root FILED-EXAMPLE exists and no public filed brief was found (see `usage/SOURCE.md`), so this file is rules only.

| SCCHANCERY-FORM-001 | Paper and type size: typewritten or machine duplicated; type size standard 12-point or larger; double spaced; white bond paper not less than 20-pound weight; 8 1/2 by 11 inches. No photographic reduction that makes the alphabet smaller than pica type. | SCACR 267(c). | M |

| SCCHANCERY-CAP-001 | Caption: the name of the appellate court (Supreme Court or Court of Appeals); for review of a lower court, the county, the judge and his title; the case title with the party who commenced the action in the lower court first; the title of the document; and the name, address and telephone of counsel. In an appeal from an administrative tribunal, the tribunal and the administrative judge replace the county and judge. | SCACR 267(a). | M |
