# Supreme Court of South Carolina: papers filed in the Supreme Court

Alias `sc`. Scope: papers filed in the Supreme Court of South Carolina: briefs (principal, respondent, reply, final brief on the merits), amicus curiae briefs, motions and petitions (including petitions for rehearing and petitions for extraordinary writs under Rule 245). Verified against the Supreme Court's Appellate Court Rules (SCACR) as posted on sccourts.org, with the rule-text amendment dates shown below, on 2026-10-21. The formatting order under Rule 267(f) and the electronic-filing order under Rule 262 are part of the current requirements.

## Common form (every paper filed in the Supreme Court)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-FORM-001 | Papers are typewritten on white bond paper of at least 20 lb weight, 8 1/2 by 11 inches, double-spaced, in type of 12 point or larger. Photographic reduction that makes the type smaller than pica is not allowed. | SCACR 267(c). | M |
| SC-FORM-002 | Blank margin of one inch on all sides of every page. | SCACR 267(d). | M |
| SC-FORM-003 | The rules set no typeface. The template declares Times New Roman (metric twin Liberation Serif) as the face. | SCACR 267(c) (size only). | A |
| SC-FORM-004 | The front cover contains only the caption. | SCACR 267(e). | M |
| SC-FORM-005 | Caption: the name of the court ("In the Supreme Court"); for an appeal from a lower court, the county and the judge with title; the case title (the party who commenced the action in the lower court is named first); the document title; and the name, address and telephone number of counsel submitting the document. | SCACR 267(a). | M |
| SC-FORM-006 | Papers are signed by the lawyer or self-represented litigant filing them. A typed "s/ name" signature is permitted. The signature certifies the paper has been read, has good ground, and is not interposed for delay. | SCACR 267(b). | M |
| SC-FORM-007 | On motions and petitions, counsel's name, address and telephone number are placed at the end of the document with the signature. | SCACR 267(a). | M |
| SC-FORM-008 | Covers may be white (the color scheme in SCACR 267(e) applies only where additional paper copies are requested). | SC-FMT (b), (d). | A |
| SC-FORM-009 | A paper copy of a final brief is filed unbound; briefs are not stapled. Electronic filing is in Adobe PDF (.pdf) by OneDrive for Business or by e-mail to supctfilings@sccourts.org. | SCACR 211(a), 267(d); SC-FMT (b); SC-EFILE (b)(1)-(2). | M |
| SC-FORM-010 | Motions and petitions carry a certificate or affidavit of service that reflects service on all parties; the original is filed with the original paper. | SCACR 240(c)(1). | M |
| SC-FORM-011 | Pages of a motion or petition and of all supporting documents are consecutively numbered. | SCACR 240(c). | M |

## Respondent's brief (answering brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-ANS-001 | The brief of respondent conforms to the content requirements for the brief of appellant, in order: table of contents and table of cases with page references; statement of the issues (may be omitted if respondent is satisfied with appellant's statement); statement of the case (may be omitted for the same reason); standard of review (may be omitted for the same reason); argument; conclusion stating the precise relief requested. | SCACR 208(b)(2), 208(b)(1)(A)-(F). | M |
| SC-ANS-002 | A principal brief does not exceed fifty (50) pages. | SCACR 208(b)(5). | M |
| SC-ANS-003 | The respondent's brief is served and filed within thirty (30) days after service of appellant's brief. | SCACR 208(a)(2). | M |
| SC-ANS-004 | Record references are to the page and line of the transcript or the page of the record material, and are revised to the Record on Appeal when the final brief is filed. | SCACR 208(b)(4); 211(b)(1). | M |
| SC-ANS-005 | Section headings and the argument headings are distinct; each argument part states the issue in distinctive type at its head. | SCACR 208(b)(1)(E). | M |

|---|---|---|---|

| SC-REP-002 | A reply brief does not exceed twenty-five (25) pages. | SCACR 208(b)(5). | M |

## Brief on the merits (final brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-MER-001 | The final brief is identical to the initial brief served under Rule 208, except that record references are revised to the Record on Appeal and obvious typographical errors and misspellings may be corrected. No other change may be made. | SCACR 211(b)(1)-(2). | M |
| SC-MER-002 | The final brief is served and filed within twenty (20) days after service of the Record on Appeal, and it is signed. The signature certifies compliance with Rule 211(b). | SCACR 211(a). | M |
| SC-MER-003 | A principal brief does not exceed fifty (50) pages. | SCACR 208(b)(5). | M |
| SC-MER-004 | Content, in order: table of contents and table of cases; statement of the issues on appeal; statement of the case; standard of review; argument; conclusion. | SCACR 208(b)(1)(A)-(F). | M |
| SC-MER-005 | The front cover contains only the caption. | SCACR 267(e). | M |

## Motion and supporting memorandum

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-MOT-001 | A motion or petition is in writing, states its grounds, and complies with Rule 267. | SCACR 240(c). | M |
| SC-MOT-002 | A motion or petition includes a memorandum with citation of authorities in support. | SCACR 240(c)(2). | M |
| SC-MOT-003 | A motion to dismiss an appeal or a motion to relieve counsel automatically stays the time limits for perfecting the appeal until the motion is decided. | SCACR 240(b). | M |
| SC-MOT-004 | Any party opposing a motion has ten (10) days from service to file a return; the movant may reply within five (5) days after service of the return. | SCACR 240(e)-(f). | M |
| SC-MOT-005 | Failure of the moving party to perform a required act may be deemed abandonment of the motion. | SCACR 240(g). | A |

## Petition for rehearing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-REH-001 | A petition for rehearing must be actually received by the appellate court within fifteen (15) days after the filing of the opinion, order, judgment or decree. | SCACR 221(a). | M |
| SC-REH-002 | A petition for rehearing is in accordance with Rule 240 and states with particularity the points supposed to have been overlooked or misapprehended by the court. | SCACR 221(a), 240. | M |
| SC-REH-003 | A petition for rehearing does not exceed fifteen (15) pages. | SCACR 221(a). | M |
| SC-REH-004 | No petition for rehearing is allowed from an order denying a petition for writ of certiorari under Rule 242, declining to entertain a matter under Rule 245, or denying a motion to reinstate under Rule 260. | SCACR 221(a). | M |

## Petition for writ (original jurisdiction)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-WRIT-001 | A petition for an extraordinary writ in the original jurisdiction is served and filed, and complies with the requirements of Rule 240. | SCACR 245(b), 240. | M |
| SC-WRIT-002 | The petition states the facts showing why the Supreme Court should exercise original jurisdiction, such as public interest or special grounds of emergency. | SCACR 245(a). | M |
| SC-WRIT-003 | An action in original jurisdiction (rather than an extraordinary writ) is brought by a petition, a complaint under SCRCP Rule 8, and a notice to each respondent that a return is due in twenty (20) days; service is as for a summons under SCRCP Rule 4. | SCACR 245(c). | M |

## Amicus curiae brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-AMI-001 | An amicus brief may be filed only by leave of the court granted on motion, or at the court's request. It may be conditionally filed with the motion for leave. | SCACR 213. | M |
| SC-AMI-002 | The motion for leave identifies the applicant's interest and states why an amicus brief is desirable. | SCACR 213. | M |

| SC-AMI-004 | The amicus brief cover is green under the color scheme of SCACR 267(e), unless white covers are used under the Rule 267(f) order. | SCACR 267(e); SC-FMT (b). | A |
