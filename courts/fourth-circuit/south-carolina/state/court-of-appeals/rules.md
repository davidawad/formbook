# Court of Appeals of South Carolina: briefs and motions

Alias `scctapp`. Scope: principal briefs (opening/appellant, answering/respondent, reply), amicus curiae briefs, and motions with supporting memoranda, filed in the Court of Appeals. The court's rules are the South Carolina Appellate Court Rules (SCACR), which apply to the Court of Appeals and the Supreme Court alike; the Court of Appeals has no separate formatting rules. Verified against the SCACR as posted on sccourts.org (rule text shows "Last amended by Order dated" April 30, 2024 for Rules 211, 240, 267; May 1, 2018 for Rule 208; April 24, 2024 for Rule 262; September 20, 2017 for Rule 268) and the Supreme Court's e-filing order of April 24, 2024, on 2026-10-21.
