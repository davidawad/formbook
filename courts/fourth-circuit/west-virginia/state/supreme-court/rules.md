# Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia: briefs, petitions, motions, amicus briefs

Alias `wva`. Scope: briefs and summary responses, the original-jurisdiction petition, the petition for rehearing, motions, and amicus briefs filed in the Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia. The Rules of Appellate Procedure (RAP) govern every filing in that court. Verified against the Rules of Appellate Procedure as posted on the West Virginia Judiciary site (the page carries a 2026 copyright line and the current rule text, including Rule 38A/38B e-filing) on 2026-10-21.

## Briefs and summary responses (Rule 10; Rule 14 for PSC appeals; Rule 17 for certified questions)

These rows cover the opening (petitioner's), respondent's, and reply briefs, and the petition for review of a Public Service Commission order, which Rule 14(d) and (i) and (j) require in the same format as Rule 10. The petition-for-discretionary-review exemplar is a petitioner's brief in an appeal from the Intermediate Court of Appeals under Rule 10, since West Virginia has no separate discretionary-review petition.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WVA-FORM-001 | Paper is 8½ × 11 inches, printed or typed, and reproduced to produce a clear black image on white paper. | RAP 38(a) (clear black image on white background); RAP 38B(b) (paper size 8½ × 11 inches) | M |
| WVA-FORM-002 | Text is double-spaced. Footnotes and indented quotations may be single-spaced. | RAP 38(a) | M |
| WVA-FORM-003 | Text is no smaller than 12-point proportionally spaced type, or 11-point nonproportionally spaced type. | RAP 38(a) | M |
| WVA-FORM-004 | Footnote text is no smaller than 11-point proportionally spaced type (10-point nonproportionally spaced). | RAP 38(a) | M |
| WVA-FORM-005 | Margins are no less than one inch on all sides. | RAP 38(a) | M |
| WVA-COVER-001 | The cover page contains: (1) "In the [Intermediate or Supreme] Court of Appeals of West Virginia"; (2) the docket number, if assigned; (3) the official caption as set forth in the scheduling order, if one has issued; (4) a brief descriptive title; and (5) name, address, telephone number, West Virginia State Bar identification number, and e-mail address of counsel or the unrepresented party. | RAP 38(b); RAP 10(a) | M |
| WVA-COVER-002 | A cross-assignment of error on a respondent's brief is shown on its cover page. | RAP 10(f) | M |
| WVA-CONT-001 | Petitioner's brief sections appear in this order, immediately after the cover page: table of contents; table of authorities; assignments of error; statement of the case; summary of argument; statement regarding oral argument and decision; argument; conclusion; certificate of service. | RAP 10(c)(1)-(9) | M |
| WVA-CONT-002 | A table of contents, with page references to the sections and argument headings, is required if the brief exceeds five pages. The table of contents does not count toward the page limit. | RAP 10(c)(1) | M |
| WVA-CONT-003 | A table of authorities, alphabetical, listing cases, statutes and other authorities with the pages where each is cited, is required if the brief exceeds five pages. It does not count toward the page limit. | RAP 10(c)(2) | M |
| WVA-CONT-004 | The brief opens with the assignments of error, in terms and circumstances of the case. | RAP 10(c)(3) | M |
| WVA-CONT-005 | The statement of the case has supported, specific appendix or record references and states the procedural history and relevant facts. | RAP 10(c)(4) | M |
| WVA-CONT-006 | The summary of argument is a concise condensation of the argument; it may not repeat the argument headings. | RAP 10(c)(5) | M |
| WVA-CONT-007 | The brief states whether oral argument is necessary under Rule 18(a), and if so, whether a Rule 19 or Rule 20 argument is requested, and why. | RAP 10(c)(6) | M |
| WVA-CONT-008 | The argument states the points of fact and law, the standard of review, and cites authorities, under headings corresponding to the assignments of error. | RAP 10(c)(7) | M |
| WVA-CONT-009 | The brief ends with a conclusion specifying the relief sought. | RAP 10(c)(8) | M |
| WVA-CONT-010 | The certificate of service required by Rule 37 is attached at the end of the brief. It does not need a page number and does not count toward the page limit. | RAP 10(c)(9); RAP 37(b) | M |
| WVA-CONT-011 | A respondent's brief follows Rule 10(c), omits a restated statement of the case beyond what is needed to correct the petitioner's, need not restate the assignments of error, and responds to each assignment of error. | RAP 10(d) | M |
| WVA-CONT-012 | A reply brief follows the respondent's-brief parts that apply, need not contain a summary of argument, and is divided by topical headings. | RAP 10(g) | M |
| WVA-LEN-001 | Petitioner's and respondent's briefs may not exceed 40 pages (excluding cover page, table of contents, table of authorities, and certificate of service). | RAP 38(c) | M |
| WVA-LEN-002 | A reply brief may not exceed 20 pages (same exclusions). If a respondent's brief asserts cross-assignments of error, the reply limit is 40 pages. | RAP 38(c); RAP 10(g) | M |
| WVA-LEN-003 | A summary response may not exceed 15 pages (same exclusions). | RAP 38(c); RAP 10(e) | M |
| WVA-CERT-001 | Signed filing carries the attorney's name, address, telephone, e-mail, and West Virginia State Bar identification number, and the typographical signature "/s/ name". | RAP 38A (signature and identification of the filer); RAP 38(b)(5) | M |
| WVA-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers (social security, taxpayer, driver's license, financial account, card, and PIN numbers) are redacted from e-filed documents, and counsel avoids unnecessary personal identifiers. | RAP 38A (personal identifiers); RAP 10(a); RAP 40(e) | M |
| WVA-ELEC-002 | The e-filed document is titled with its document type, such as "Petitioner's Brief". | RAP 38A (titling) | M |

## Original-jurisdiction petitions (Rule 16)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WVA-WRIT-001 | The petition is captioned "State of West Virginia ex rel. [petitioner] v. [respondent(s)]" and names the presiding judicial officer and real parties in interest for mandamus or prohibition. | RAP 16(d) | M |
| WVA-WRIT-002 | The petition follows Rule 10 format and Rule 40(e), and opens with the questions presented. Sections then follow in order: table of contents; table of authorities; questions presented; statement of the case; summary of argument; statement regarding oral argument and decision; argument; conclusion. | RAP 16(d)(1)-(8) | M |
| WVA-WRIT-003 | A table of contents and table of authorities are required if the petition exceeds five pages; neither needs a page number nor counts toward the limit. | RAP 16(d)(1)-(2) | M |
| WVA-WRIT-004 | The petition may not exceed 40 pages (same exclusions). | RAP 38(c) (original jurisdiction petition and response) | M |
| WVA-WRIT-005 | The petition is served under Rule 37, and on the Attorney General where a State official is a respondent or the petition raises a constitutional challenge. | RAP 16(c) | M |

## Petitions for rehearing (Rule 25)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WVA-REH-001 | A petition for rehearing is filed within thirty days of release of the memorandum decision or opinion, unless the time is changed by order. It must state with particularity the points overlooked or misapprehended and may not repeat argument already presented. | RAP 25(a), (b) | M |
| WVA-REH-002 | The petition for rehearing complies with the page limit in Rule 38, which is 15 pages for a petition for rehearing and response (RAP 38(c)). | RAP 25(b); RAP 38(c) | M |

## Amicus curiae briefs (Rule 30)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WVA-AMI-001 | An amicus brief need not strictly comply with Rule 10, but must include, in order: a cover page with the caption, the party supported, and whether it supports affirmance or reversal; a table of contents; a table of authorities; a concise statement of the amicus's identity, interest, and source of authority to file; a statement whether counsel for a party authored the brief in whole or in part and who made a monetary contribution (except for a State, county, or municipal amicus); and the argument. | RAP 30(e)(1)-(6) | M |
| WVA-AMI-002 | An amicus brief may not exceed 25 pages (same exclusions). | RAP 38(c) (amicus curiae briefs, Rule 30) | M |
| WVA-AMI-003 | An amicus brief is filed within the time allowed the party whose position it supports, unless leave is granted. Non-State amici file with leave or a statement that all parties consented. | RAP 30(a), (d) | M |
| WVA-AMI-004 | Counsel of record for all parties receive notice at least five days before the amicus brief's due date. | RAP 30(b) | M |
