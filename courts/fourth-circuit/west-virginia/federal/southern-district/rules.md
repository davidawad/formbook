# Southern District of West Virginia: complaints and memoranda

Alias `wvsd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion memoranda. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules of Procedure (https://www.wvsd.uscourts.gov/sites/wvsd/files/LocalRulesofProcedure-June2017.pdf, file dated June 2017) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WVSD-CAP-001 | Caption, title, file number and designation; claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited to one set of circumstances. | FRCP-10(a)-(b) | M |
| WVSD-CERT-001 | Signed filing states the signer's address, e-mail address and telephone number. | FRCP-11(a) | M |
| WVSD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted as FRCP-5.2(a) requires. | FRCP-5.2(a) | M |

## Memoranda

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| WVSD-FORM-001 | Paper is 8 1/2 x 11 inch. Text is double-spaced; quotations more than two lines may be indented and single-spaced; headings and footnotes may be single-spaced. | LR 7.1, paragraph (3) | M |
| WVSD-FORM-002 | Margins are at least one inch on all four sides. Page numbers may be in the margins; no other text may appear there. | LR 7.1, paragraph (3) | M |
| WVSD-FORM-003 | Typeface is proportionally spaced (12-point or larger) or monospaced (no more than 102 characters per inch). | LR 7.1, paragraph (4) | M |
| WVSD-LEN-001 | A memorandum is 20 pages or fewer. Motions to exceed are denied absent good cause; response and reply memoranda keep the same limit. | LR 7.1, paragraph (2) | M |
