## Caveats

- **No separate memorandum of law from this court was found.** Both exemplars put the argument inside the motion. The template follows that form. A separately filed "memorandum in support" (the form the brief names) was searched for and not found in this court's public copies.
- **Exemplar 2 is short.** It shows the emergency-motion form, with no legal argument section. It is used only for the caption and title conventions.
- Both exemplars are from the same case, so they share a caption and counsel's conventions. Two different filers (committee counsel and debtors' counsel) are the cross-filer check the tier-2 rule asks for, within the limit of what the court's public record offers.
- The ECF header (case number, filed/entered stamp) on each page is generated by the court system and is not reproduced by the template.
