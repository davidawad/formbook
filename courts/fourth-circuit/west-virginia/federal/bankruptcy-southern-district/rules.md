# Bankruptcy Court, Southern District of West Virginia: adversary complaints and motion papers

Alias `wvsb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion papers. Verified against the court-posted Current Local Bankruptcy Rules (https://www.wvsb.uscourts.gov/sites/wvsb/files/localrules.pdf, effective 1/2/2013) and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure on 2026-10-20.

Local rules retrieved; the rule text in this document states no typeface, point size, margin or spacing rule for papers. The contents list names LBR 9004-2 (Caption, Papers, General) and LBR 5005-1 (Filing Papers and Magnetic Media, Requirements), but their body text was not located in this pass.
