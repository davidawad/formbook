## Caveats

- **Not an adversary-numbered docket.** The document is captioned as an adversary complaint and is titled "Adversary Proceeding Complaint," but it is docketed in the main case (Doc. 334) with the adversary number blank. It is the only adversary-style complaint from this court found in the searches below. It is the closest exemplar available; the template follows its caption and body form, not an adversary docket number.
- **Filing is a class action.** The exemplar is a wage/ERISA/WARN class complaint, not an individual one. Only the form is used; the case content is not carried into the template.
- **No consent statement.** The exemplar does not state whether the plaintiffs consent to final orders by the bankruptcy court (FRBP 7008(a)). See `../README.md`, WVSB-CMP-004.
- Public court record; the Kroll copy is a public download of an ECF document, not a PACER-certified copy.
