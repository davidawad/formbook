# Supreme Court of Virginia: briefs, petitions and motions

Alias `va`. Scope: filings in the Supreme Court of Virginia (appeals, petitions for appeal and review, original-jurisdiction petitions, briefs, amicus briefs, motions). Verified against the Rules of the Supreme Court of Virginia (consolidated PDF, amendments through the June 18, 2025 order for Rule 5:30, effective August 17, 2025) and the Court's List of Acceptable Fonts, on 2026-10-21.

## Petitions for appeal (discretionary review under Rule 5:17)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VA-PDR-001 | Assignments of error under a separate heading, listed clearly without extraneous argument, each with exact reference to the transcript or record page where the error was preserved. | RULES 5:17(c)(1), (c)(1)(iv) | M |
| VA-PDR-002 | Contents: table of contents and table of authorities (year of each citation); nature of the case and material proceedings below; statement of facts with record references; authorities and argument per assignment in one place; conclusion stating the precise relief sought. | RULES 5:17(c)(2)-(6) | M |
| VA-PDR-003 | Length: the longer of 35 pages or 6,125 words. | RULES 5:17(f) | M |
| VA-PDR-004 | Certificate in the petition: names of all appellants and appellees; counsel's VSB number, address, telephone, fax (if any), e-mail; statement of service on opposing counsel with date and manner; word count if used; in criminal, habeas, and related appeals, whether counsel was appointed or privately retained; whether appellant wants to argue orally. | RULES 5:17(i)(1)-(5) | M |
| VA-PDR-005 | Filing fee accompanies the petition. | RULES 5:17(d) | M |
