# Court of Appeals of Virginia: briefs, amicus briefs and motions

Alias `vactapp`. Scope: opening brief of appellant, brief of appellee, reply brief, brief amicus curiae, and motions. Verified against the Rules of the Supreme Court of Virginia, Part Five A (The Court of Appeals), on vacourts.gov (file last modified 2026-09-15; Rule 5A:4 last amended by Order dated November 1, 2021, effective January 1, 2022; Rule 5A:19 last amended June 21, 2024; Rule 5A:23 last amended June 18, 2025), and the Supreme Court's list of acceptable fonts, on 2026-10-21. The old courts.state.va.us host now redirects to vacourts.gov and was not used.

Petitions for appeal (Rule 5A:12), petitions for rehearing (5A:35), appendices (5A:25) and the record are out of scope here; no template covers them.

## Form for all briefs and motions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VACTAPP-FORM-001 | Pages are 8-1/2 x 11 inch, printed on one side only, with a clear black image on white. | Rule 5A:4(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-FORM-002 | Briefs and motions, including footnotes, use a font style on the Court's list (Arial, Cambria, Century, Century School Book, Constantia, Courier New, Franklin Gothic Book, Georgia, Palatino Linotype, Tahoma, Times New Roman, Verdana), in at least 14-point type. Text may not be reduced. The template uses Times New Roman (metric twin Liberation Serif) at 14 pt, with footnotes also at 14 pt. | Rule 5A:4(a); FONTS list | M |
| VACTAPP-FORM-003 | Text is double-spaced, except headings, assignments of error, quotations that exceed 49 words, and footnotes, which may be single-spaced. | Rule 5A:4(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-FORM-004 | Margins are at least one inch on all four sides of each page. Page numbers, if placed in the bottom margin, are the only text permitted there. | Rule 5A:4(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-FORM-005 | Condensed or multi-page transcripts are prohibited; text may not be reduced to fit a limit. | Rule 5A:4(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-FORM-006 | The front cover shows the style of the case with the appellant or petitioner named first, the record number, and the name, Virginia State Bar number, mailing address, telephone number (with any extension), facsimile number (if any) and e-mail address of counsel submitting the document. | Rule 5A:4(b) | M |
| VACTAPP-FILE-001 | Documents are filed electronically in PDF through VACES under Rule 1:17, and electronically served on opposing counsel. A pleading that is filed electronically has no paper copies. | Rule 5A:1(c)(1)-(2) | M |
| VACTAPP-CERT-001 | Each brief, motion, petition or other document with a word limit carries a certificate by counsel (or the unrepresented party) that it complies with the word limit, stating the number of words it contains, excluding the parts the Rules exempt. The person preparing the certificate may rely on the word-processing count. | Rule 5A:4(d) | M |
| VACTAPP-CERT-002 | Each filing carries a certificate of service or acceptance of service showing the date and manner of transmittal to all counsel, and, where a page or word limit applies, the number of pages or words. | Rule 5A:1(c)(4) | M |
| VACTAPP-CERT-003 | Signatures: the document is signed by counsel for the filing party, or personally by a pro se party. Electronic signatures are permitted. | Rule 5A:1(c)(3) | M |
| VACTAPP-COLOR-001 | Non-electronic covers are colored: Brief of the Appellant white, Brief of the Appellee blue, Reply Brief of the Appellant green, Brief Amicus Curiae gray. Applies only to documents filed non-electronically (Rule 5A:1(c)(1)); no error results from non-compliance. | Rule 5A:24(a)-(b) | A |
| VACTAPP-CITE-001 | Briefs cite the record by record-page number ("R. 1") for an electronic record, or by appendix-page number ("J.A. 1") for a paper record. Arguments may not be incorporated by reference to filings below. | Rule 5A:19(e)-(f) | M |
| VACTAPP-SUPP-001 | Pertinent authority that comes to a party's attention after filing is brought to the clerk by letter (350 words or fewer), copied to all parties. | Rule 5A:4A | A |

## Opening brief of appellant

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VACTAPP-OPEN-001 | Contents, in order: table of contents and table of authorities (cases alphabetical, year of each citation); statement of the nature of the case and of the material proceedings below; "Assignments of Error" under its own heading, each with an exact reference to the record page where the error was preserved; statement of facts with record references; standard of review and argument for each assignment of error, stated in one place; short conclusion stating the precise relief sought; signature block; certificate. | Rule 5A:20(a)-(h) | M |
| VACTAPP-OPEN-002 | Length: the opening brief may not exceed the longer of 50 pages or 12,300 words. Cover, table of contents, table of authorities, signature blocks and certificate are excluded. Headings, footnotes and quotations are included. | Rule 5A:19(a); Rule 5A:4(d) | M |
| VACTAPP-OPEN-003 | The signature block gives the signing counsel's name, Virginia State Bar number, address, telephone, facsimile (if any) and e-mail. | Rule 5A:20(g) | M |
| VACTAPP-OPEN-004 | The certificate states that the brief was served on opposing counsel, whether counsel desires to waive oral argument, and the word count. | Rule 5A:20(h); Rule 5A:4(d) | M |
| VACTAPP-OPEN-005 | A table of authorities lists cases alphabetically, with the year of each citation. | Rule 5A:20(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-OPEN-006 | Filing time: 40 days after the record is filed (appeal of right) or 40 days after the certificate of appeal (discretionary appeal). | Rule 5A:19(b)(2), (c)(1) | A |

## Brief of appellee

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VACTAPP-APPE-001 | Contents, in order: table of contents and table of authorities; statement of the case if the appellee disagrees with the appellant's; any additional assignments of error with exact record references; statement of facts necessary to correct or amplify the appellant's; standard of review and argument for each assignment, stated in one place; cross-error, if any, with record references, standard, argument and authority; precise relief sought; signature block; certificate. | Rule 5A:21(a)-(h) | M |
| VACTAPP-APPE-002 | Length: the brief of appellee may not exceed the longer of 50 pages or 12,300 words, with the same exclusions as the opening brief. | Rule 5A:19(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-APPE-003 | The certificate states service, the oral-argument waiver position, and the word count. | Rule 5A:21(h); Rule 5A:4(d) | M |

## Reply brief

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VACTAPP-REPL-001 | The reply brief contains argument only in reply to the brief of appellee; no reply is needed where the opening brief already answers the contention. No table of contents or table of authorities is mandated by Rule 5A:22; the exemplars include both, and the template follows the exemplars (tier 2). | Rule 5A:22(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-REPL-002 | Length: the reply brief may not exceed the longer of 20 pages or 3,500 words, with the same exclusions. | Rule 5A:19(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-REPL-003 | The reply brief's certificate states service, the oral-argument waiver position, and the word count. | Rule 5A:22(c); Rule 5A:4(d) | M |

## Brief amicus curiae

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VACTAPP-AMIC-001 | An amicus brief must comply with the rules applicable to the brief of the party supported, and its cover identifies the party or parties supported. | Rule 5A:23(e)(1) | M |
| VACTAPP-AMIC-002 | An amicus brief states whether a party's counsel authored the brief in whole or part, whether a party or counsel contributed money to fund it, and whether any other person contributed money (identifying each). Commonwealth and United States amici are exempt from this statement. | Rule 5A:23(e)(2) | M |
| VACTAPP-AMIC-003 | Word limits: an amicus brief complies with the word limits that apply to the brief of the party being supported. | Rule 5A:19(a) | M |
| VACTAPP-AMIC-004 | Timing: 7 days after the principal brief or filing of the party supported. An amicus brief may not file a reply brief without the Court's permission. Leave of Court by motion is needed except for the United States and the Commonwealth. | Rule 5A:23(c)-(d) | A |

## Motions

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VACTAPP-MOT-001 | Motions are in writing and filed with the clerk; they carry a statement that the other parties were informed of the intended filing, and, where all parties are represented, whether the other parties consent or intend to oppose. | Rule 5A:2(a)(1) | M |
| VACTAPP-MOT-002 | Form: a motion is a document under Rule 5A:4(a) (14-point font on the Court's list, double-spaced, one-inch margins, one side). No word or page limit is set for motions in Part Five A. | Rule 5A:4(a); Rule 5A:2 | M |
| VACTAPP-MOT-003 | The cover requirements of Rule 5A:4(b) govern "petitions for appeal, briefs, appendices, petitions for rehearing, and petitions for rehearing en banc"; a motion is not in that list, so its caption layout is tier 2 (the exemplars use the Court's style with counsel block and date). | Rule 5A:4(b) | C |
