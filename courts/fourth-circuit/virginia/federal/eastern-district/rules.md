# Eastern District of Virginia: complaints and briefs

Alias `vaed`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules (https://www.vaed.uscourts.gov/sites/vaed/files/Local%20Rules%20EDVA%20December%201%202024.pdf, effective December 1, 2024) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VAED-FORM-002 | Briefs, including footnotes, are written in 12-point Roman style or 10-pitch Courier, with one-inch margins. | LR 7(F)(3) | M |
| VAED-LEN-001 | Opening briefs are 30 pages or fewer, and rebuttal briefs 20 pages or fewer, of 8 1/2 x 11 inch double-spaced pages, except for good cause shown in advance of filing. | LR 7(F)(3) | M |
