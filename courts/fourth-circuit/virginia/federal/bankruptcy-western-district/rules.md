# Bankruptcy Court, Western District of Virginia: adversary complaints and motion papers

Alias `vawb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion papers. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules (https://www.vawb.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/local_rules/Local%20Rules%20-%202025.pdf, 2025 edition) and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints and motion papers

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| VAWB-FORM-001 | Papers are legibly typed, printed or reproduced on one side of the paper only. | LBR form-of-papers, item A | M |
| VAWB-CAP-001 | Caption and form follow the Bankruptcy Rules, Official Forms and Local Rules; each paper bears the case number. | FRBP-9004(b); LBR form-of-papers, item B | M |
| VAWB-FORM-002 | Papers are standard weight, letter size (8 1/2 x 11 inches), with a top margin of at least 1 1/2 inches. Multi-page documents are fastened at the top. | LBR form-of-papers, item C | M |
| VAWB-SIG-001 | The lower left of the signature page states the name, address, telephone number and e-mail address of the attorney or pro se party. | LBR 7001-1, item 4 | M |
| VAWB-FORM-003 | A pro se debtor's list of creditors is on white bond or standard copy paper, 8 1/2 x 11 inches, with margins of at least 1 inch top, bottom and left, in a font no smaller than 12-point. | LBR (petition filing format, paragraph F) | M |
