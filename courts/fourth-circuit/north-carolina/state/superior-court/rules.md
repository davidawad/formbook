# Superior Court of North Carolina: briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `ncsuperct`. Scope: papers filed in the Superior Court of North Carolina (the trial court of general jurisdiction), including briefs and memoranda, motions, amicus briefs, and petitions for judicial review of agency decisions (N.C.G.S. 150B-43) and petitions for writ, as shown by the exemplars in `usage/`. Verified against the exemplars in `usage/` (filed papers, retrieved 2026-10-17; the URLs of the seven exemplars answered HTTP 200 on 2026-10-21) on 2026-10-21; no North Carolina rule text was retrieved, so every row below is a convention or advisory row and none is mandatory (`M`).

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/local-rules/general-rules-of-practice-for-the-superior-and-district-courts (HTTP 403); https://www.nccourts.gov/assets/inline-files/General-Rules-of-Practice-for-the-Superior-and-District-Courts-Codified-1-September-2021.pdf (HTTP 403); https://nccourts.gov/courts/supreme-court/court-rules/general-rules-of-practice-for-superior-and-district-courts (HTTP 403); https://www.nccourts.gov/rules (HTTP 403); https://www.nccourts.gov/ (HTTP 403); https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByChapter/Chapter_1A.html (N.C. Rules of Civil Procedure, HTTP 403); https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_150B/GS_150B-43.html (judicial review, HTTP 403); https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_7A/GS_7A-31.html (HTTP 403). The WebFetch tool was also refused (HTTP 403) on the General Rules PDF and on a third-party copy of the General Rules (https://ruledex.com/north-carolina/general-rules-of-practice-for-the-superior-and-district-courts/). On 2026-10-21 the Supreme Court's 2019 codification (https://www.nccourts.gov/assets/inline-files/General-Rules-of-Practice-for-the-Superior-and-District-Courts-Codified-5-April-2019.pdf) and its rules page (https://nccourts.gov/courts/supreme-court/court-rules/general-rules-of-practice-for-superior-and-district-courts) both answered HTTP 403 to curl and to WebFetch. Until the General Rules of Practice and the Rules of Civil Procedure are read from an official source, the paper-size, margin, typeface, spacing, page-limit, certificate and e-filing requirements are NOT set here; the default eval checks apply. Retried 2026-10-22: the nccourts.gov General Rules PDF and the ncleg.gov § 150B-43 page still answered HTTP 403 to curl (one request each, 2 s apart), so no rule text is cited here.
