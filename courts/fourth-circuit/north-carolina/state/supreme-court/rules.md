# Supreme Court of North Carolina: briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `nc`. Scope: papers filed in the Supreme Court of North Carolina: briefs (principal, new brief, reply, amicus curiae), motions with supporting memoranda, petitions for discretionary review, petitions for review, petitions for writ (prohibition, certiorari, supersedeas), and petitions for rehearing. Verified against the N.C. Rules of Appellate Procedure as summarized by the North Carolina Bar Association's Appellate Rules Committee (2017 memorandum on the amended rules, and the Style Manual for the North Carolina Rules of Appellate Procedure, latest revision April 2017) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.nccourts.gov/courts/supreme-court/court-rules/north-carolina-rules-of-appellate-procedure (HTTP 403 on every attempt, as was the root https://www.nccourts.gov/ and the Appellate Division PDFs on https://appellate.nccourts.org/, all HTTP 403 from this host on 2026-10-21). The verbatim text of N.C. R. App. P. 15, 16, 21, 26, 28, 30, 33 and 37 was therefore not read. Every row below rests on the Bar Association's summary of the rule (tagged in the Source column) or on the exemplars; rows whose source is a rule number that was not read are marked as such and must be checked against the official text before the template is relied on for a filing.
