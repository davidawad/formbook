# Court of Appeals of North Carolina: briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `ncctapp`. Scope: papers filed in the North Carolina Court of Appeals: opening (appellant's principal) briefs, answering (appellee's principal) briefs, reply briefs, motions and supporting memoranda, petitions for review, petitions for writ, and amicus curiae briefs. Verified against the North Carolina Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 26(g)(1) and 28(j), as quoted in the North Carolina Bar Association's summary (secondary; Rules 26, 28, 30 and 33 were not read in primary text) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.nccourts.gov/assets/inline-files/North-Carolina-Rules-of-Appellate-Procedure-Codified-26-August-2020.pdf (HTTP 403); https://www.nccourts.gov/courts/court-of-appeals (HTTP 403); https://www.nccourts.gov/assets/inline-files/Order-Amending-Rules-28-29-and-33.1.2018-03-01.pdf (HTTP 403, repeated). Rows marked NCBAR rest on that secondary summary and must be checked against the primary Rules text before the PR is merged.

## Papers (all doc types)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCCTAPP-FORM-001 | Printed matter is in a font no smaller than 12-point and no larger than 14-point, proportionally spaced, with serifs (Rule 26(g)(1), as quoted). | NCBAR | M |
| NCCTAPP-FORM-002 | Page size is US letter (8 1/2 x 11 in). Margins are not set by any retrieved text; the template uses 1 in on all sides, as the exemplars do. | exemplars; default | C |
| NCCTAPP-FORM-003 | Body text is double-spaced, with headings, block quotations, footnotes, captions and counsel blocks single-spaced, as the exemplars show. | exemplars | C |
| NCCTAPP-CONT-001 | The cover carries the appellate docket number (`No. COA__`), the county of the trial court, the caption naming the parties and their designations, and the title of the paper. | exemplars (opening-brief, answering-brief, reply-brief, motion-brief, petition-for-writ) | C |
| NCCTAPP-CERT-001 | A word-count certification that the brief complies with the word limit goes immediately before the certificate of service; footnotes and citations count toward the limit, and covers, captions, indexes, tables of authorities, certificates, signature blocks and appendices are excluded (Rule 28(j), as quoted). | NCBAR | M |
| NCCTAPP-CERT-002 | A certificate of service follows the signature block. | exemplars (all doc types) | C |
| NCCTAPP-CONT-002 | A template contains no case-specific text, measured coordinates or forced line breaks. | (template) | T |

## Briefs: principal (opening-brief, answering-brief)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCCTAPP-LEN-001 | A principal brief does not exceed 8,750 words (Rule 28(j), as quoted). | NCBAR | M |
