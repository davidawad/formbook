# North Carolina Industrial Commission: workers' compensation filings

Alias `ncworkcompcom`. Scope: documents filed with the Industrial Commission in workers' compensation cases (requests for hearing, motions and responses, applications, correspondence). No court-root FILED-EXAMPLE exists and no two public filed documents were found (see `usage/SOURCE.md`), so this file is rules only; no doc-type folder is built.

## Filings generally

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-ELEC-001 | All documents filed with the Commission in workers' compensation cases are submitted electronically through the Electronic Document Filing Portal (EDFP); a document sent in another manner is not accepted for filing. Unrepresented employees and non-insured employers may file by EDFP, e-mail to dockets@ic.nc.gov, fax, U.S. mail, courier or hand delivery. Emergency temporary waivers are by application. | NCAC .0108(a), (b), (f); 11 NCAC 23E .0203 (as cited in .0108(a)). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-ELEC-002 | Motions and responses set out in the body of e-mail correspondence, or contained in a brief, are not accepted for filing. | NCAC .0609(e). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-ELEC-003 | Form 19 first report of injury is filed by electronic data interchange (EDI) except for the categories listed in .0108(d); fraud complaints, employee misclassification and safety rule filings go to the e-mail addresses named in .0108(e). | NCAC .0108(d), (e). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-ELEC-004 | Discovery requests and responses are not filed with the Commission, except notices of deposition, discovery pertinent to a pending motion, responses after a motion or order to compel, and post-hearing discovery; those are filed and also served. | NCAC .0605(8). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-SERV-001 | A written communication to the Commission is sent by the same method, where possible, to opposing party or opposing counsel at the same time. | NCAC .0620(b). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-SERV-002 | Written communications to the Commission do not introduce new evidence or argue the merits. | NCAC .0620(c). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-TIME-001 | Periods are computed under .0107(a); when service is by mail, three days are added. Notice by e-mail is complete one hour after it is sent (Commission notice). | NCAC .0107(a), (b). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-001 | Each attorney of record maintains current telephone, facsimile, e-mail and mailing contact information through EDFP. Unrepresented parties give written notice of changes by the methods in .0109(c). | NCAC .0109(a)-(c), (e). | M |

## Requests for hearing

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-002 | A request for hearing states the basis of disagreement and the issues; the date of injury; the body part injured; the city and county of injury; the names and addresses of doctors and experts; the names of lay witnesses; a time estimate; and the telephone, e-mail and mailing address of the requesting party and its counsel. A completed Form 33 Request that Claim be Assigned for Hearing satisfies this rule. The copy goes to opposing counsel or unrepresented parties. | NCAC .0602(a), (b). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-003 | Application forms that set a figure carry it: a Form 24 application to terminate or suspend compensation specifies the number of pages of attached documents; a Form 23 application to reinstate compensation does likewise. | NCAC .0404(d), .0405. | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-004 | Minors and incompetent persons who may bring or receive benefits act through a guardian ad litem appointed on a Form 42 application. | NCAC .0604(a), (i). | M |

## Motions and responses

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CAP-001 | Every motion and response, including requests for extensions and requests to withdraw a motion, has a caption with the Industrial Commission file number(s), the party names, and a title naming the motion or response. Parties without legal representation are excepted. | NCAC .0609(e). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-005 | A motion states with particularity the grounds, the relief sought, the opposing party's position if known, and the effort to resolve the dispute before filing; it is served on all opposing counsel or unrepresented parties. | NCAC .0609(f). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-006 | Every written motion and every response includes a proposed Order in Microsoft Word format containing: the file number(s); the case caption; the subject of the order; the procedural posture; and the appearances, with attorney and firm name, e-mail, telephone and fax for represented parties, or the e-mail, telephone and fax (if available) of unrepresented parties. | NCAC .0609(k), .0609(k)(1)-(5). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-TIME-002 | The responding party has 10 days after service of a motion to respond. The Commission may shorten or extend that time; parties may stipulate to one extension not exceeding 30 days, except for medical motions under .0609A. | NCAC .0609(i). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-007 | A motion to continue or remove a case from the hearing calendar is made as far in advance as possible, states the basis, and states the other parties' position or a reasonable attempt to ascertain it. | NCAC .0609(g). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-008 | A motion to compel discovery represents that informal means of resolving the dispute were attempted in good faith and states the opposing party's position or a reasonable attempt to contact it. | NCAC .0605(9), .0607. | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-009 | Interrogatories are limited to 30 in number, including subparts. | NCAC .0605(1). | M |
| NCWORKCOMPCOM-CONT-010 | A motion to withdraw as counsel includes a proposed order in Word format and, for an unrepresented client, the client's last known address, after reasonable efforts to ascertain it. | NCAC .0617, .0609(k). | M |
