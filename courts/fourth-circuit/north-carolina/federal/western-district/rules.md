# Western District of North Carolina: complaints and briefs

Alias `ncwd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Civil Rules (https://www.ncwd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/local_rules/Revised%20Local%20Rules%20120122.pdf, effective December 1, 2022) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCWD-CONT-001 | Caption, title, file number and designation; claims in numbered paragraphs, each limited to one set of circumstances. | FRCP-10(a)-(b) | M |
| NCWD-CERT-001 | Signed filing states the signer's address, e-mail address and telephone number. | FRCP-11(a) | M |
| NCWD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted as FRCP-5.2(a) requires. | FRCP-5.2(a) | M |

## Motion briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCWD-FORM-001 | Absent a Standing Civil Order or Case Management Order, font is a minimum of 12-point, lines are double-spaced, margins are one inch, and each page is numbered. A judge's order controls when it sets different terms. | LCvR 7.1(d) | M |
| NCWD-LEN-001 | Absent a Standing Civil Order or Case Management Order, a brief is 25 pages or fewer. | LCvR 7.1(d) | M |
| NCWD-EXH-001 | Exhibits, and all pages within an exhibit, are numbered before filing; each is cited by exhibit number and page. | LCvR 7.1 | M |
