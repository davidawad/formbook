# Bankruptcy Court, Western District of North Carolina: adversary complaints and briefs

Alias `ncwb`. Scope: adversary complaints and briefs in adversary proceedings and contested matters. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules (https://www.ncwb.uscourts.gov/sites/ncwb/files/WDNC%20Bankruptcy%20Local%20Rules%20Sept%202021.pdf, revised effective September 1, 2021) and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints and briefs

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCWB-CAP-001 | Caption sets out the court's name, the case title, the case number and adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document. | FRBP-9004(b) | M |
| NCWB-FORM-001 | Briefs: font is a minimum of 12-point, text is double-spaced, margins are one inch on all sides, and each page is numbered, unless the court orders otherwise. | LBR 9013-2(a) | M |
| NCWB-LEN-001 | A brief is 25 pages or fewer, unless the court orders otherwise. | LBR 9013-2(a) | M |
| NCWB-CONT-001 | A brief includes a concise statement of the facts of the case (first item of the required contents). | LBR 9013-2(a)(1) | M |
