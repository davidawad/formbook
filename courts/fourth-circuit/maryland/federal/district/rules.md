Alias `mdd`. Scope: civil complaints and motion briefs. Verified against the court-posted Local Rules (https://www.mdd.uscourts.gov/sites/mdd/files/LocalRules.pdf, edition as posted; no edition date on the document) and the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Complaints

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MDD-CAP-001 | Caption of court documents shows only the short title (first plaintiff and first defendant) and the civil action number. The original complaint is the exception: it names all parties and their addresses and the county of residence of any Maryland party. | LR 102.2(a); FRCP-10(a) | M |
| MDD-FORM-001 | Pages are 8 1/2 x 11 inch, with a one-inch margin on all sides. Lines are double-spaced except quotations and footnotes. Pages are numbered at the bottom of every page after the first. | LR 102.2(b) | M |
| MDD-FORM-002 | Text is at least 12-point and footnotes at least 10-point, in Times New Roman or a larger typeface. | LR 102.2(b) | M |
| MDD-FORM-003 | Documents may not be handwritten (the rule's exception is for a filer without the ability to type or print). | LR 102.2(b) | M |
| MDD-CONT-001 | Claims are in numbered paragraphs, each limited to one set of circumstances; caption, title and designation as FRCP-10(a) requires. | FRCP-10(a)-(b) | M |
| MDD-CERT-001 | Signed filing states the signer's address, e-mail address and telephone number. | FRCP-11(a) | M |
| MDD-ELEC-001 | Personal identifiers are redacted (SSN and taxpayer ID to last four digits; birth year; minor's initials; financial account to last four digits). | FRCP-5.2(a) | M |
