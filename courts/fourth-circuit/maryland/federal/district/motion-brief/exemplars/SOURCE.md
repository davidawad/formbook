## Notes on the exemplar's form

- The filed signature block has no fax line. LR-102.1.b.i requires a fax number; the sample states "None" in that position, and the README records the gap.
- The filed memorandum carries a page number on page 1. LR-102.2.b says pages are numbered after the first page; the template follows the rule, not the exemplar.
