# Bankruptcy Court, District of Maryland: adversary complaints and motion papers

Alias `mdb`. Scope: adversary complaints and motion papers. Verified against the court-posted Local Bankruptcy Rules (https://www.mdb.uscourts.gov/files/localrules.pdf, "Revised December 1, 2025, effective as of December 1, 2025") and the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure on 2026-10-20.

## Adversary complaints and motion papers

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| MDB-FORM-001 | Petitions, pleadings, schedules and other papers filed by an attorney are legible, have at least a one-inch margin on each side, use 12-point or larger type for text and 10-point or larger type for footnotes. | LBR 9004-1 | M |
| MDB-FORM-002 | Papers comply with the Court's Electronic Case Filing Procedures (Appendix H). | LBR 9004-1 | M |
| MDB-FORM-003 | Papers are legible and use commonly used English abbreviations only. | FRBP-9004(a) | M |
| MDB-CAP-001 | Caption sets out the court's name, the case title, the case number and, if any, the adversary-proceeding number, and a brief designation of the document. | FRBP-9004(b) | M |
| MDB-FORM-004 | The typeface is not named in the local rule (size and footnote size are); the template uses the repository default serif face. | LBR 9004-1 (none stated) | T |
