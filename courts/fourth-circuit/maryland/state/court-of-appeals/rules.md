# Court of Appeals of Maryland (Supreme Court of Maryland): briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `md`. Scope: papers filed in the Court of Appeals (the Supreme Court of Maryland, the court's name since the 2022 renaming): principal and reply briefs, amicus briefs, briefs on the merits, motions, petitions for certiorari, petitions for writ, and petitions for rehearing. Verified against the Supreme Court of Maryland's "Filing Briefs" page (Rule 8-112, 8-503 and 8-504 summaries, current as posted), the Administrative Order of September 27, 2023 on approved fonts, and the Rules Order of November 13, 2024 (Title 1, Rule 1-301) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.mdcourts.gov/rules (HTTP 200, index page only; the Title 8 PDF URL https://mdcourts.gov/sites/default/files/import/rules/title8.pdf returned 404; state-rules.com and courtrules.net returned 403). The verbatim text of Rules 8-112, 8-302, 8-303, 8-504 and 8-605 was therefore not read; rows that rest on them cite the Court's own summary of the rule.
