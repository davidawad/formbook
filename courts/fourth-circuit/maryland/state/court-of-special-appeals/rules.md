# Appellate Court of Maryland (formerly the Court of Special Appeals): briefs, motions and petitions

Alias `mdctspecapp`. Scope: papers filed in the Appellate Court of Maryland (the intermediate appellate court, renamed from the Court of Special Appeals in 2022): principal and reply briefs, amicus curiae briefs, motions, applications for leave to appeal (petitions for discretionary review under Rule 8-204) and the other papers that reach this court under Title 8. Verified against Title 8, Chapter 500 as amended by the Rules Orders of April 21, 2023 (Rule 8-112, 8-503, 8-504, 8-511), April 5, 2024 (Rule 8-511(b)), November 13, 2024 (Rule 1-301(c), margins), February 19, 2026 (Rules 8-502, 8-503, 8-504; effective July 1, 2026), the Judiciary's "Filing Briefs with the Appellate Court of Maryland" page, the Administrative Order of September 27, 2023 on approved fonts, and the Rules Order of June 4, 2026 (checked: no change to Rules 8-112, 8-503, 8-504 or 8-511) on 2026-10-21.

Local rules not retrieved: https://www.mdcourts.gov/sites/default/files/import/rules/title8.pdf (HTTP 404); the full Title 8 text is therefore not read. Rules 8-112(c), 8-503, 8-504(a), 8-511(b) and (e) are quoted from the Rules Orders that amend them (verbatim, as amended); Rules 8-511(a), (c) and (d), 8-204 and 8-605 were not retrieved and no row rests on them. The govt.westlaw.com/mdc official text returns a Cloudflare challenge.
