# Fourth Circuit: briefs (opening, answering, and reply)

Alias `ca4`. Scope: counseled formal briefs in appeals, filed electronically. Verified against the court's current Local Rules and Appellate Procedure Guide on 2026-10-17. Re-check annually and whenever the court posts a rules or guide update. Case-specific briefing orders control when they vary a generally applicable deadline or limit.

## Document-type differences

- **Opening brief**: use CA4-CONT-002 and CA4-CONT-003; include the disclosure statement if Local Rule 26.1 requires one. Standard length is 13,000 words/30 pages (or 1,300 monospaced lines).
- **Answering brief**: use CA4-CONT-004 and CA4-CONT-006; the statement of the case and standard of review are omitted only when the answering party accepts the corresponding opening-brief statements. Standard length is 13,000 words/30 pages (or 1,300 monospaced lines).
- **Reply brief**: use CA4-CONT-005; it is limited to responding to the answering brief and requires permission for further briefs. Length is 6,500 words/15 pages (or 650 monospaced lines). Disclosure statements belong in the party's initial brief, not a later reply.

Stable IDs are not renumbered; retire one by marking it `(withdrawn)`.
