# United States Court of International Trade (cit): motion-brief

- Court: `cit`, United States Court of International Trade (`courts/federal-circuit/court-of-international-trade/`)
- Doc type: `motion-brief`. A motion for judgment on the agency record (USCIT Rule 56.2) with the memorandum in support, submitted as one document. Covers the Rule 56.2 motion, its brief, TOC and TOA, the signature block and the Chambers word-count certificate.
- Exemplar: `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-motion-brief.pdf` (*BGH Edelstahl Siegen GmbH v. United States*, Ct. No. 26-01186, ECF No. 26, filed 2026-09-18, 58 pages; provenance in `usage/SOURCE.md`).
- Sample: `usage/INPUT-motion-brief.md`, transcribed from that filing: caption, motion, memorandum cover, Rule 56.2 statement, parts of the statement of facts, summary of argument, standard of review, conclusion and prayer, signature, and the word-count certificate (regenerated by the template). Sample render: 13 pages (typst and docx alike).
- Status: template built; engines typst and docx on the shared cross-engine layout model (fb-595.54.6), the docx breaking every line and page as typst (XENG-001..003 PASS, `params.cross_engine: gate` in `eval.yaml`); render, overlay and page review done (below), re-run 2026-10-15 with typst 0.15.0 from a fresh conda-forge toolchain. Not reviewed by a human; `court.json` untouched.
- Exemplar present: `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-motion-brief.pdf`, re-downloaded from the URL in `usage/SOURCE.md` (58 pages, sha256 `43d48a199313a3d7a0cda550fbd6fda7767da50e2e0639457e25897cf956f001`).

## Markdown contract (frontmatter)

| Key | Required | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `court` | yes | court-name lines, printed in capitals on each cover |
| `court_no` | yes | court number, printed as `Ct. No.` at the right of the caption |
| `before` | no | assigned judge, printed under the court number (Rule 81(g)) |
| `caption` | yes | sides; each has `names`, `designation`, and `between` (the word joining it to the next side, default `v.`) |
| `party` | yes | party name printed under the caption on both covers |
| `brief_title` | yes | title of the motion, e.g. `Motion for Judgment Upon the Agency Record` |
| `memo_title` | yes | title of the memorandum cover |
| `motion` | yes | markdown: the motion's paragraphs, relief list and WHEREFORE paragraph |
| `counsel` | yes | counsel block on the memorandum cover |
| `date` | yes | date line of both signature blocks |
| `signature` | yes | `signer` and `lines` (Rule 11(a): address, e-mail, telephone) |
| `authorities` | yes if any `toa:` link is used | map of key to `kind` (`case`, `statute`, `constitution`, `regulation`, `rule`, `other`), `name` (cases), `cite`, `sort` |

The body is markdown from the first heading on. Headings carry their own numerals (`I.`, `A.`, ...), so the text is exactly the author's. Each citation that the table of authorities should list is a link `[text](toa:key)`; a `toa:` key missing from `authorities` fails the build. Footnotes are markdown footnotes. The word count is taken from the body by the rules in Chambers 2(B)(1)(c), and the build fails above 14,000 words.

## Governing rules

Primary source: United States Court of International Trade, Rules of the Court, "As amended August 21, 2026, eff. August 21, 2026" (the complete rules and forms PDF, 487 pages), <https://www.cit.uscourts.gov/sites/cit/files/COMPLETE%20RULES%20AND%20FORMS.pdf>, including the Standard Chambers Procedures (PDF pp. 422-423 of that file). Downloaded and read on 2026-10-14. The Chambers Procedures are part of the same compilation and are cited by their section numbers.

| Requirement ID | Rule (quoted in part) | Where the template uses it |
|---|---|---|
| CIT-MOT-001 | Rule 56.2(c)(1): "The motion for judgment on the agency record and the brief in support thereof must be submitted in a single document." Chambers 2(A): same. | one document; the motion and the memorandum are in one file |
| CIT-MOT-002 | Rule 56.2(c)(1)(A)-(C): the brief "must include a statement setting out in numbered paragraphs" the administrative determination (with Federal Register reference), the issues of law with reasons, and "the request for court order and relief sought" | sample Part I (Rule 56.2 statement), subparts A and B; relief in the motion |
| CIT-MOT-003 | Rule 56.2(c)(2): "The brief must include the authorities relied on ... The brief also must include a table of contents and a table of authorities." | `toc` (outline of headings) and `toa-section` (from `toa:` links) |
| CIT-MOT-004 | Rule 7(b)(1)(B): "state with particularity in a single document the grounds for seeking the order and the legal argument necessary to support it. A separate brief ... must not be filed unless specifically required by the Court." | the motion's grounds plus the memorandum in one file |
| CIT-MOT-005 | Rule 7(b)(1)(D): "state the relief sought" | the motion's relief list and WHEREFORE paragraph |
| CIT-MOT-006 | Rule 7(b)(1)(E): "be accompanied by a proposed order" | the motion says a proposed order is submitted; the order is a separate document and is not in this template (see Known gaps) |
| CIT-WORD-001 | Chambers 2(B)(1)(a): "Movant's and respondent's briefs must not exceed 14,000 words" | `word-limit = 14000`; `count-words()` panics above it |
| CIT-WORD-002 | Chambers 2(B)(1)(c): "Headings, footnotes, and quotations count toward the word limitations. The corporate disclosure statement, table of contents, table of authorities, any addendum ..., any certificates of counsel, and counsel's signature block do not count." | `count-words()` counts the body (headings, footnotes, quotations) and nothing else |
| CIT-WORD-003 | Chambers 2(B)(2): "Any brief must include a certificate ... The certificate must state the number of words in the brief and be signed by the person preparing the certificate." | `certificate-of-compliance()`: the count is printed from `count-words()`; signature block |
| CIT-CAP-001 | Rule 10(a): "Every pleading must have a caption with the court's name, a title, a court number, and a Rule 7(a) designation." | `caption()`; `cover-page()` |
| CIT-SIG-001 | Rule 11(a): "Each paper must state the signer's address, e-mail address, and telephone number." | `signature-block()` lines |
| CIT-STAT-001 | Rule 81(g): papers must identify "the court number assigned to the case" and, if assigned, the judge | `court_no`, `before` |
| CIT-PAPER-001 | Rule 81(f): "must be 8½ by 11 inches in size" | `paper: "us-letter"` |
| CIT-PAPER-002 | Rule 81(f): "typed matter not exceeding 6½ by 9½ inches" | 1 inch margins: 6½ × 9 in typed area |
| CIT-TYPE-001 | Rule 81(f): "type size of 12 points or larger, including type used in footnotes" | `type-size = 12pt`; footnote entries at 12 pt |
| CIT-SPACE-001 | Rule 81(f): "Typed matter must be double spaced except footnotes ..., quoted material, which may be indented and single spaced, and titles, schedules, tables ..." | `pitch` (24 pt line pitch); single spacing for block quotes, footnotes, headings, TOC and TOA, caption, counsel and signature lines |
| CIT-NUM-001 | Rule 81(d): pages "numbered consecutively, commencing with the number 1" | one arabic sequence from the cover |
| CIT-NUM-002 | Rule 81(f): "Pages must be numbered on the bottom." | footer |
| CIT-PDF-001 | Rule 81(b): PDF, "text-searchable to the maximum extent possible" | typst output, embedded fonts (Times New Roman only, checked with `pdffonts`), extractable text |

Not used by this doc type: Rule 7(f) consultation (a Rule 56.2 motion is not one of the listed motions); Rule 56.2(c)(3) and Chambers 2(C) joint appendix (a separate filing); Rule 81(h) confidential-information sets (none in the sample).

## Known gaps

- **Exemplar: one filer.** Only the DeKieffer & Horgan filing is in `usage/`. Tier 2 needs 2 or 3 filers; the cover layout, TOC/TOA layout and caption are open until a second filer's Rule 56.2 motion is checked. Not judged against one filer.
- **Single-document rule.** Rule 56.2(c)(1) requires one document; the exemplar is one ECF document with the motion and the memorandum under separate covers. The template follows the rule (one file, two covers).
- **Excerpt, not the whole brief.** The sample omits the filed statement of facts parts II.B and the rest of II.A after p.13, the argument parts IV.B and IV.C, and the summary's later paragraphs. The certificate's count (1,911) is the excerpt's, not the filed count (13,834). The excerpt is 13 pages; the filed memorandum is 47 pages.
- **Omitted filed text.** The footnote on counsel admission (Virginia bar, D.C. Bar Rule 49(c)(2)) is not transcribed; the `*` after counsel's names is dropped from the signature lines.
- **Italics are by convention.** Case names and "Id." are italic in the markdown; the filed PDF's italic runs were not checked word by word against its font. Titles of documents ("Petition Amendment") are plain.
- **Proposed order.** Rule 7(b)(1)(E) requires a proposed order; the order is a separate document and is not templated here.
- **Overlay pairing** is by word overlap; the TOC and TOA pairings are weak (30-44%) because the layouts differ, not because the content differs.
- **Certificate on an excerpt.** The certificate prints 1,911 words, the count of this excerpt; the filed count is 13,834. For a filed brief the number must be the count of the full brief, which the template takes from the full markdown body (the body count is `count-words()`, not a word-processor count; see Chambers 2(B)(1)(c)).
- **Engines.** Typst and docx (see Engines). No `latex/` engine for this doc type.
