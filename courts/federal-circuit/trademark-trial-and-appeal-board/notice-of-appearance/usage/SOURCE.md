Four filers, two sides (three applicants, one opposer), 2015 to 2026.

What each shows (tier 2, convention across filers):

- All four: the two-line Board heading; a caption with the parties and their roles on the left and the proceeding number and the application (and, in Happi, the mark) on the right; a centered bold title; a short body citing 37 C.F.R. § 2.17 and TBMP § 114.03; a signature block with the attorney's name, firm, address, telephone and email; a certificate of service by email (GNC: email and first-class mail) on its own page naming the recipient and email addresses.
- Happi (2026) and Creatively Disruptive (2022): the statement that counsel "is an attorney as defined in 37 CFR § 11.1"; a correspondence block set off and single-spaced.
- Happi: the power-of-attorney variant: the party appoints counsel "with full power of substitution and revocation, and to transact all business in the United States Patent and Trademark Office in connection therewith" (the TBMP § 114.03 wording); a second signature block for the party (signed "/s/ Lisa Hurwitz" for Happi Co.) above counsel's. Its revocation sentence cites "37 CFR § 116.01(a)(4)", which does not exist; the rule is 37 C.F.R. § 2.19(a)(4) (TBMP § 116.01). The template cites § 2.19(a)(4).
- BCBSMI: the change-of-correspondence variant without a power of attorney, three appearing attorneys, primary and secondary email addresses.
- GNC: the shortest form (one paragraph citing "TBMP § 114.03 and 37 CFR § 2.17(c)"), the opposer's side, no correspondence change.
- Signature forms vary: "/John W. McIlvaine/" (GNC, the 37 C.F.R. § 2.193(c)(1) form), "/s/ Name" (Happi), a blank line (Creatively Disruptive, BCBSMI). The template prints the `/Name/` form the filer types (rules.md, Finding 5).
- Filer errors not reproduced: Creatively Disruptive's signature reads "Counsel for Opposer" for an applicant's counsel; GNC's certificate is signed "Attorney for Petitioner" in an opposition.
