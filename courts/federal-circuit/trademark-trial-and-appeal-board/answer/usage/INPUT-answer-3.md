# Affirmative Defenses

## A. Respondent's use of HARBORLIGHT is prior to Petitioner's

Respondent pleads the following, each in the alternative:

1. The Petition alleges that Petitioner has used its mark since 2021. Respondent's first use of HARBORLIGHT in commerce predates that date. The registration's certificate, quoted below, states the date.

    That date is before every date on which the Petition relies.

> Registration No. 9,999,001 recites a date of first use in commerce of June 1, 2018.

2. Respondent will rely on the following in support of this defense:

    - the dated invoices for HARBORLIGHT lanterns sold in 2018;
    - the dated catalog pages for the 2018 and 2019 seasons; and
    - the certificate of registration.

### Alternative pleading

3. If the Board finds that the goods recited in the registration are broader than Respondent's use, Respondent is entitled to retain the registration for the goods on which the mark was used before Petitioner's claimed date.
