What each shows (tier 2, convention across filers):

- Both: heading "IN THE UNITED STATES PATENT AND TRADEMARK OFFICE BEFORE THE TRADEMARK TRIAL AND APPEAL BOARD"; a caption naming the application, the mark, the parties with their roles, and the proceeding number; a centered title; numbered responses that track the Notice; a signature block naming counsel and the party; a certificate of service giving date, manner (email, plus first-class mail in the 2010 paper) and the recipient's name, address and email.
- Sage: "In the Matter of Application Serial No.", "For the mark:", "Filed:", "Published:" lines (the form the matter's instruction asks for); a separate "Affirmative Defenses" part with numbered defenses; counterclaims under their own caption; the certificate of service on its own page under a repeated caption.
- Embarcadero: the shortest acceptable answer (paragraph-by-paragraph admit, deny, lack of knowledge; one-line prayer).

Substantive lesson recorded for the matter (not formatting): in *Sage*, the Section 18 counterclaim challenged four of the six registrations the opposer pleaded, and the Board sustained the opposition on registrations the counterclaim did not reach (TBMP § 309.03(d) n.7, June 2026, citing Sage Therapeutics, 2024 TTAB LEXIS 139, at *42-43). A restriction counterclaim must cover every pleaded registration the opposition could be sustained on.
