# Trademark Trial and Appeal Board (USPTO): rules

Alias `ttab`. Scope: papers filed electronically in TTAB inter partes proceedings (oppositions and cancellations), for two doc types: the notice of opposition (`notice-of-opposition/`) and the brief on the case at final hearing (`trial-brief/`, which covers the main, defendant's and reply/rebuttal briefs). Ex parte appeal briefs (37 C.F.R. § 2.142) and motion briefs (§ 2.127) are out of scope. The TTAB is an administrative board, not on CourtListener; its rules are 37 C.F.R. Part 2 and its practice manual is the TBMP.

Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each July (the TBMP is revised every June; the June 2026 edition is current) and whenever the USPTO publishes a Federal Register rule amending 37 C.F.R. Part 2, Subpart (inter partes) §§ 2.101-2.136 or §§ 2.193-2.194. Also re-check the TTAB Center page: filing is moving from ESTTA to TTAB Center (TBMP § 110.01, June 2026).

## Findings

1. **The only typographic rule is § 2.126(a)(1):** "Text in an electronic submission must be filed in at least 11-point type and double-spaced." No typeface, margin, paper-size, line-numbering or page-numbering rule applies to electronic submissions. Paper size, numbered pages and one-sided text in § 2.126(b) govern paper submissions only (permitted only when ESTTA/TTAB Center is unavailable).
2. **The rule names no exception to double spacing** (no carve-out for footnotes, block quotations, headings, captions or signature blocks). TBMP § 801.03 ("Extensive single-spaced footnotes may not be used as a subterfuge to avoid the page limit") shows the Board tolerates single-spaced footnotes; single spacing elsewhere is convention, recorded as C.
3. **Letter size and 1-inch margins are guidance, not rules.** TBMP § 110.03(b): PDF attachments "should be formatted in letter size (8.5" x 11")"; TBMP § 106.03: "Business correspondence margins of one inch are considered standard" (said of paper submissions).
4. **TBMP edition.** The doc-type READMEs cite TBMP June 2025; the current edition is June 2026. The sections relied on (106.03, 309.02, 801.03) were re-read in the June 2026 text.
5. **Electronic signature form.** § 2.193(c)(1) requires the signature to be "placed between two forward slash ("/") symbols" (e.g. `/Jane Doe/`). The Brink notice does this. The TreeFrog ("s/Name") and Sakar ("/s/ Name") briefs do not; the Board treats the filing-form signature as covering attachments (TBMP § 309.02(b), for complaints). The template should print `/Name/`; the trial-brief README's "s/Name" is that one filer's style and is not rule-compliant on its face.
6. **No certificate of service on a notice of opposition** (§ 2.119(a) excepts it; the Board serves it with the notice of institution, § 2.105(a)). Every other paper, including every brief, needs proof of service (§ 2.119(a)).
7. **Page limits count the whole brief** ("in its entirety", § 2.128(b)), so the caption page and the table of authorities count. The rule names only a "main brief" (55 pages) and a "reply brief" (25 pages); treating the defendant's brief as a main brief is an interpretation (TTABVUE dockets it as "D MAIN BRIEF: TM RULE 2.128", e.g. Opposition No. 91232860 entry 37).

## Doc type: notice-of-opposition

Required order (Board guidance, TBMP § 309.02(a)): heading; identification of the application ("Serial No."); name of proceeding and "Opposition No."; title; opposer's name, entity type and address; the pleading (entitlement and grounds); signature with capacity. The filing form (ESTTA/TTAB Center cover sheet) is part of the pleading but is generated by the system, not by the template.
