# Trademark Trial and Appeal Board (USPTO): rules

Alias `ttab`. Scope: papers filed electronically in TTAB inter partes proceedings (oppositions and cancellations), for four doc types: the notice of opposition (`notice-of-opposition/`), the notice of appearance of counsel, with or without a power of attorney and a change of correspondence address (`notice-of-appearance/`), the answer to a notice of opposition or petition to cancel, alone or with a counterclaim (`answer/`), and the brief on the case at final hearing (`trial-brief/`, which covers the main, defendant's and reply/rebuttal briefs). Ex parte appeal briefs (37 C.F.R. § 2.142) and motion briefs (§ 2.127) are out of scope. The TTAB is an administrative board, not on CourtListener; its rules are 37 C.F.R. Part 2 and its practice manual is the TBMP.

Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

The answer doc type (`answer/`) was verified against primary sources on 2026-10-21: 37 C.F.R. Part 2 (eCFR as of 2026-10-19), TBMP June 2026, and the TTAB Center page (updated 2026-09-08).

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each July (the TBMP is revised every June; the June 2026 edition is current) and whenever the USPTO publishes a Federal Register rule amending 37 C.F.R. Part 2, Subpart (inter partes) §§ 2.101-2.136 or §§ 2.193-2.194. Also re-check the TTAB Center page: filing is moving from ESTTA to TTAB Center (TBMP § 110.01, June 2026).

## Findings

1. **The only typographic rule is § 2.126(a)(1):** "Text in an electronic submission must be filed in at least 11-point type and double-spaced." No typeface, margin, paper-size, line-numbering or page-numbering rule applies to electronic submissions. Paper size, numbered pages and one-sided text in § 2.126(b) govern paper submissions only (permitted only when ESTTA/TTAB Center is unavailable).
2. **The rule names no exception to double spacing** (no carve-out for footnotes, block quotations, headings, captions or signature blocks). TBMP § 801.03 ("Extensive single-spaced footnotes may not be used as a subterfuge to avoid the page limit") shows the Board tolerates single-spaced footnotes; single spacing elsewhere is convention, recorded as C.
3. **Letter size and 1-inch margins are guidance, not rules.** TBMP § 110.03(b): PDF attachments "should be formatted in letter size (8.5" x 11")"; TBMP § 106.03: "Business correspondence margins of one inch are considered standard" (said of paper submissions).
4. **TBMP edition.** The doc-type READMEs cite TBMP June 2025; the current edition is June 2026. The sections relied on (106.03, 309.02, 801.03) were re-read in the June 2026 text.
5. **Electronic signature form.** § 2.193(c)(1) requires the signature to be "placed between two forward slash ("/") symbols" (e.g. `/Jane Doe/`). The Brink notice does this. The TreeFrog ("s/Name") and Sakar ("/s/ Name") briefs do not; the Board treats the filing-form signature as covering attachments (TBMP § 309.02(b), for complaints). The template should print `/Name/`; the trial-brief README's "s/Name" is that one filer's style and is not rule-compliant on its face.
6. **No certificate of service on a notice of opposition** (§ 2.119(a) excepts it; the Board serves it with the notice of institution, § 2.105(a)). Every other paper, including every brief, needs proof of service (§ 2.119(a)).
7. **Page limits count the whole brief** ("in its entirety", § 2.128(b)), so the caption page and the table of authorities count. The rule names only a "main brief" (55 pages) and a "reply brief" (25 pages); treating the defendant's brief as a main brief is an interpretation (TTABVUE dockets it as "D MAIN BRIEF: TM RULE 2.128", e.g. Opposition No. 91232860 entry 37).
8. **Filing system for an answer (answer doc type).** The rule text still says ESTTA (CFR-2.106 (b)(1), CFR-2.114 (b)(1), CFR-2.126 (a)). TBMP § 110.01 (June 2026) says TTAB Center is public for notices of opposition, petitions to cancel, trial testimony, evidence and briefs, and that ESTTA redirects to it as capability arrives. The TTAB Center page (updated 2026-09-08, read 2026-10-21) lists motions, attorney appearances and correspondence updates as available and says the next release will let customers "File answers, counterclaims, amended pleadings and related motions." No date is given. Until that release an answer and a counterclaim go through ESTTA; check the page on the day of filing.
9. **Answer time.** The Board's notice of institution sets it, "not less than thirty days" from the mailing date (CFR-2.105 (a)). TBMP § 310.03(a) says the long-standing 40 days "is changing" to 60 days. Calendar from the order, never from the manual.
10. **A restriction of a plaintiff's pleaded registration needs a counterclaim** (CFR-2.133 (d)), but a Section 18 restriction claim is not an attack on validity, so the five-year limit of Section 14 does not bar it (TBMP § 309.03(d), notes 10 and 11).
11. **The Board will not restrict a registration the counterclaim does not name,** and a main claim can be sustained on a pleaded registration the counterclaim did not reach (TBMP § 309.03(d) n.7; the SageForth exemplar). A counterclaim must name every pleaded registration the opposition could be sustained on.
12. **No reply to an affirmative defense** (CFR-2.106 (b)(2)). An attack on a pleaded registration's validity, including abandonment, is not an affirmative defense and is heard only as a counterclaim or separate petition (CFR-2.106 (b)(3)(ii); TBMP § 311.02(d)).

## Doc type: answer

The answer to a notice of opposition (37 C.F.R. § 2.106) or to a petition to cancel (§ 2.114), alone or with a counterclaim (§§ 2.106(b)(3), 2.111-2.115, 2.133(d)). The same caption, signature and certificate blocks serve other inter partes papers (motions, stipulations); their own content rules are not covered here. The court-wide form, cover, signature, service and electronic-filing rules above apply to the answer unchanged (the map below lists the answer rules dropped as duplicates). Answer-specific points follow. The answer template sets 12-point type with a body baseline pitch of 27.6 pt (Word's "double" for Times New Roman at 12 pt), which satisfies the operational definition in TTAB-FORM-002 (at least twice the type size), in Liberation Serif (Times New Roman metrics), a template choice under TTAB-FORM-006.

## Doc type: notice-of-appearance

The notice by which an attorney appears for a party in an inter partes proceeding (37 C.F.R. § 2.17(b)), with or without a power of attorney signed by the party (§ 2.17(b)(1)(i), (c)) and a request to change the correspondence address (§ 2.18). The court-wide form, cover, signature, service and electronic-filing rules apply unchanged. The template uses the answer's measurements (12-point Times New Roman or Liberation Serif, 27.6 pt body pitch, 13.8 pt single-spaced blocks) and generates the appearance, attorney, power-of-attorney and correspondence paragraphs from the frontmatter, with the citations below; the body may add paragraphs, or (`generate: false`) replace the generated wording. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-23 (eCFR title 37 up to date as of 2026-10-21; TBMP June 2026, sha256 as above; TTAB Center page updated 2026-09-08).
