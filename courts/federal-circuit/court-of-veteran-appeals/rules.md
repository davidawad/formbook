# U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (opening brief, answering brief): rules

Alias `cavc`. Scope: the appellant's principal brief (`opening-brief/`, Vet. App. R. 28(a)) and the Secretary's principal brief (`answering-brief/`, Vet. App. R. 28(b)), filed by counsel through the court's electronic filing system (CM/ECF, and from October 1, 2026, ACMS for new cases). Informal briefs of self-represented parties (Rule 28(e)) are out of scope. Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-17.

Re-check cadence: re-check annually each October 1 (the court's current rules and E-Rules took effect October 1, 2026, by Misc. Order 08-26) and whenever the court posts a new miscellaneous order on its Rules or E-Filing Rules page; ACMS-specific rules are announced as "forthcoming" in the E-Rules preface, so re-check when they appear.

Kinds: **M** = mandated by a court rule (Vet. App. R. or E-Rule). **C** = convention (the rules leave it open; taken from exemplars of different filers). **T** = project/tooling rule of this repo. **A** = author responsibility, not machine-checkable.

FRAP does not apply in this court: Rule 1(a) makes the court's own Rules and E-Rules govern practice here, and FRAP 1(a) limits FRAP to the United States courts of appeals. Nothing below is taken from FRAP.

Findings that differ from what many templates assume:

1. **13-point floor, no serif requirement.** Rule 32(b): "If a proportionally spaced typeface is used, it shall be 13-point or larger." The rule says nothing about serifs; all three Secretary's briefs are set in Arial.
2. **Page limit, not a word limit.** Rule 32(e): principal briefs "may not exceed 30 pages" (reply 15), not counting the TOC, TOA, superseded-authorities appendix and certificate of service. There is no word count and no certificate of compliance.
3. **Caption page, no cover.** Rule 32(c): "Briefs must begin with a separate caption page; a cover is not required. See Form 2." No cover colors.
4. **Proof of service is generated by the system between CM/ECF users.** Rule 25(a) asks for proof of service, but Rule 25(c)(1) and E-Rule 6(a) say the notice of docket activity "shall constitute both service and proof of service" for CM/ECF users, and E-Rule 6(b) makes the Secretary always a CM/ECF user. A certificate of service is needed only for service on a non-CM/ECF user (Rule 25(c)(2)). None of the six exemplars has one. (The current templates print one unconditionally; that is a template question, not recorded here as a rule.)
5. **Record citations are part of the TOA.** Rule 28(a)(2) adds "pages of the record before the agency cited (including a title or description for each document) in numerical order by record citation" to the table of authorities (Form 18), and Rule 28(a)(4)(ii) fixes the record-cite form "R. at 44 (43-45)".
6. **Roman numerals for the certificate of service.** Rule 32(d) puts the TOC, TOA, certificate of service and superseded-authorities appendix in lower-case roman, the pages subject to the limit in arabic, all "in the center of the bottom margin".

The appellant's principal brief. Required order (Rule 28(a)): caption page; (1) table of contents; (2) table of authorities with record citations; (3) statement of the issues; (4) statement of the case; (5) argument, beginning with a summary; (6) conclusion; signature.

## Doc type: answering-brief

The Secretary's principal brief. Rule 28(b)(1): it "conforms to the requirements of subsection (a), but a statement of the issues or of the case need not be made unless the Secretary is dissatisfied with the appellant's statement". All three exemplars are VA Office of General Counsel filings (the only possible filer for this doc type: the Secretary is the appellee in every case); they differ in case, appellate attorney and Deputy Chief Counsel. Shared traits below are therefore recorded as OGC practice, not as a cross-party convention.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAVC-CONT-201 | Table of contents and table of authorities (with record citations) as in CAVC-CONT-001/002. | VR Rule 28(b)(1), 28(a)(1)-(2) | M |
| CAVC-CONT-202 | Statement of the issues and statement of the case are optional (required only if the Secretary is dissatisfied with the appellant's). All three exemplars include them anyway: "Issue(s) Presented" and "Statement of the Case" (EX-FULTON, EX-PENNEY, EX-RUNDELL). | VR Rule 28(b)(1); exemplars | M (optional); C (included, OGC practice) |

## Not verified / open

- **No public answering brief from a party other than the Secretary exists** (the Secretary is the appellee in every CAVC appeal). Tier-2 judgement for `answering-brief` rests on three VA OGC briefs by different attorneys; what they share may be office house style.
- **The court's ACMS-specific rules** are announced as "forthcoming" (E-Rules preface, effective October 1, 2026). Re-check when issued; they may add PDF requirements (searchability, size limits) not present today.
- The rules PDF has no edition date or page numbers of its own; its currency rests on the Rules page ("Posted 10/01/2026") and MO-08-26.
- Exemplar font sizes are approximate (`pdftohtml` font metrics), not measured from glyph heights.
