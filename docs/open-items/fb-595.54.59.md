Batch fb-595.54.59 of the generated-engines fan-out (docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Generated engines"). This file records what
the batch could not finish inside its folders: features or fixes the shared model (lib/typst/xdoc.typ,

file was edited by this batch.

## Shared-model requests

Each: the feature or defect, the folders it blocks, the evidence (engine, page, line of the committed renders).

1. **LaTeX splits a kept multi-line heading at a page end; typst and Word keep it whole.** lib/latex/xdoc.lua `par`
   writes `\nobreak` after a `keep` paragraph but leaves `\interlinepenalty` at 0 inside it, so a five-line level-2
   heading breaks after two lines; typst (a sticky block) and Word (`keepNext` + `keepLines`) move it whole. Fix: set
   `\interlinepenalty=10000` inside a `keep` paragraph (as `\xd@row` does). Blocks:
   `eighth-circuit/missouri/state/supreme-court/brief-on-the-merits` latex (p14: typst starts p15 with "POINTS RELIED
   ON", LaTeX sets it and two heading lines on p14; 22 of 38 page ends then differ).
2. **A doubled section or pilcrow sign ("¶¶ 22–26", "§§ 552.020.8") breaks differently in every engine.**
   lib/typst/xengine.typ boxes `[§¶] \S+`, which leaves the first of two signs free: typst breaks between "¶" and
   "¶ 22–26". lib/pandoc/xengine.lua `bind_signs` binds a single sign only, so Word sets "¶¶ 22–" and breaks after
   the en dash. Fix: bind `[§¶]+ ` to the next word in all three engines (the Georgia folders bound `[§¶]{2,} \S+`
   in their own templates before this port). Blocks: `missouri/.../brief-on-the-merits` docx (p10 lines 3-8, p11
   line 17), `missouri/.../petition-for-writ` latex and docx (p10 lines 26-27).
3. **typst keeps a footnote paragraph's last two lines together; LaTeX and Word set a widow.** When a paragraph's last
   line carries a note that does not fit, typst carries the line before it over too (its default widow cost); the
   LaTeX and Word filters turn widow control off for such a paragraph (lib/latex/xdoc.lua line 238,
   tools/fb/src/enginegen/word.rs), so one line moves. Fix: one rule in the three engines (xengine
   `footnote-widows: false` in xdoc.typ, or widow control kept in LaTeX and Word). Blocks:
   `missouri/.../petition-for-writ` latex and docx (p5/p6: typst moves "7-11 store ... deadly" with "weapon.
   (E1-2).³").
4. **A spaced ellipsis (". . .") after a word.** typst does not break before " ." (UAX 14: no break before an infix
   separator), LaTeX and Word do: "whatsoever . . ." moves whole in typst, Word and LaTeX break before the dots.
   Blocks: `missouri/.../petition-for-writ` latex and docx (p9 lines 12-14).
5. **A typst authority mark on the last word of a page counts on the next page.** "§40(30)." ends p17 of the reply
   brief (typst and Word alike); typst's table of authorities lists p18 for it, Word's (docxpages) p17. Blocks:
   `missouri/.../reply-brief` docx (p4 line 5, the table of authorities row "Mo. Const. art. III, §§40(30)").
6. **A slash inside a word ("annex/incorporate").** LaTeX breaks after the slash where typst does not (or the
   reverse). Blocks: `missouri/.../reply-brief` latex (p11 lines 15-21), `missouri/.../petition-for-writ` latex and
   docx (p17 line 8, "treatment/").
7. **Two authorities with the same `find` give Word identical page needles.** docxpages finds the second needle at
   the next occurrence of the text, every later needle is then searched past its place and the render fails ("not
   found in the PDF"). Fix: needles at the same place resolve to the same place (or the generator merges them).
   Worked around in `missouri/.../petition-for-writ` (the Amend. XIV entry now finds "Fourteenth", the same pages).
8. **An empty string in a list field ("" in `lines`).** typst and LaTeX set no paragraph for it, Word an empty line.
   Worked around in the Missouri samples (the "" entries, blank lines used as spacing, removed from brief-on-the-merits,
   reply-brief and petition-for-writ). The model should either drop it in Word too or set an empty line everywhere.
9. **pandoc reads more lists and fewer headings than cmarker.** A paragraph starting "(A) " or "(1) " (also inside a
   quotation) is a list to pandoc's markdown reader (`fancy_lists`) and the latex and docx filters reject it ("a
   quotation holds paragraphs only") or drop its label; an ATX heading with no blank line before it is a paragraph to
   pandoc (`blank_before_header`) and Word lost the certificate it opened. Fix: read the samples with pandoc's
   commonmark reader or disable both extensions in the generated engines. Worked around in the samples (`\(A)`, a blank
   line before the heading).
10. **`finds` (several strings for one authority).** The Missouri amicus sample cites Rule 4-1.1 in four forms
    (`finds: ["4-1.1,", "4-1.1.", "4-1.1 (", "4-1-1"]`); the model reads one `find` and "4-1.1" alone also marks
    "4-1.18". Blocks the port of `missouri/.../amicus-brief` (not ported: its table of authorities would lose pages).
11. **Blocks after the tail.** The Missouri respondent's brief signs its certificate of compliance again after the
    certificates (`cert_signature`); the model sets the tail last. Blocks the port of `missouri/.../answering-brief`
    (not ported: the certificate's signature would be lost).
12. **The tables in the table of contents, a vertical rule between columns, space that fills a page.** Not needed for
    tier 1 and given up in the ports (tier 2): the old Missouri and Georgia contents listed the table of authorities;
    the petition-for-writ cover had a rule between the caption and the cause number; the brief-on-the-merits cover
    set counsel at the foot of the page (`v(1fr)`, now 72 pt below the title).
