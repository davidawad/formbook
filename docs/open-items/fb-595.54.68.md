# fb-595.54.68: open items

Bead fb-595.54.68 ports the Texas Court of Criminal Appeals and Massachusetts Appeals Court folders onto the shared
document model (docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Generated engines"). This file records what the batch could not finish within its
scope (its court folders only; no edits to lib/, tools/, data/ or docs/ beyond this file), for the serial lane

## Shared-model requests

### 1. Widow and orphan control in a paragraph that anchors a footnote

- **Feature:** typst keeps its default widow and orphan control (`text.costs`) in every paragraph, including one
  that anchors a footnote. lib/latex/xdoc.lua `para()` sets `\widowpenalty=0\clubpenalty=0` for such a paragraph,
  and lib/docx/xdoc.lua drops widow control for it ("Body Text Note"). So when a footnote paragraph starts on the
  last line of a page, or would leave its last line alone on the next one, typst moves the line(s) and LaTeX and
  Word do not.
- **Folders:** `courts/fifth-circuit/texas/state/court-of-criminal-appeals/motion-brief` (ported, `cross_engine:
  report`); `courts/first-circuit/massachusetts/state/appeals-court/amicus-brief` (ported, `cross_engine: report`:
  docx XENG-003 839/866 lines, the first page difference p23 to p24, where the paragraph that anchors note 3 keeps its
  last two lines together in typst and leaves one in Word; the later page lists of the authorities follow from it).
- **Evidence:** `check.sh --engine latex` and `--engine docx`, XENG-003 976/1020 and 980/1020 lines. First difference
  p8 to p9: the paragraph "An evidentiary hearing in the 03 writ proceeding ..." (it anchors note 5) starts on the
  last line of p8 in LaTeX and Word, while typst moves it whole to p9 (orphan). Keeping `\clubpenalty` (only
  `\widowpenalty=0`) in a local, uncommitted run brought LaTeX to 1011/1020. The next difference is p12 to p13:
  the paragraph that anchors note 6 ends "... transcripts contain ... / examiner's views ..."; typst carries both last
  lines to p13 (widow) and LaTeX leaves one. So typst sets neither widows nor orphans here, and the zero penalties
  that fb-595.54.6 added for the opposite case (typst setting a widow beside its note) do not hold in general. The
  rule needs one definition shared by the three engines.

### 2. Break opportunities inside a long URL

- **Feature:** a URL longer than the line (a bare URL in a footnote) breaks at a different place in each engine:
  typst after "%" or "/" by its UAX 14 rules, Word at the last character that fits, LaTeX only at a hyphen, so it runs
  into the right margin. The model needs one set of break points in a URL (or in any word wider than the line) for
  the three engines.
- **Folders:** `courts/first-circuit/massachusetts/state/appeals-court/amicus-brief`: latex BLOCKED (not
  committed: no `latex/`, so `fb engines gen --check` reports its two files missing).
- **Evidence:** `check.sh --engine latex`, DEF-FORM-002 (tier 1): "right edge 618.99 > 540 at page 40" on note 8
  (`https://www.mass.gov/news/letter-from-the-seven-justices-...#:~:text=As%20judges%2C...`). typst breaks it
  after "As%", Word after "As%2".

### 3. Smart quotes: one rule for the direction of a straight quote

- **Feature:** cmarker (typst) and pandoc decide the direction of a straight `"` differently. `complying with" the`
  (the closing quote of a quotation that opened earlier on the line) is “ in typst and ” in pandoc, so the line
  differs in the text the XENG checks compare.
- **Folders:** `courts/first-circuit/massachusetts/state/appeals-court/motion-brief`. Worked around in the sample: the
  INPUT now has the curly ” the filing means, and XENG passes; any other sample with the same pattern fails the same
  way.
