## Shared-model requests (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82)

1. **"Page X of N" page numbers.** Feature: a `page_number` format with the total page count (typst `counter(page).final()`, LaTeX `\pageref{LastPage}` or the `.aux`, Word `PAGE`/`NUMPAGES` fields). Folders: all six Connecticut doc folders. Evidence: Practice Book Sec. 67-2A(c) (CONNAPPCT-FORM-008, mandatory): "the page number is centered at the bottom of each page and written 'Page X of XX'"; Sec. 66-2 and 77-1(b) the same for motions and petitions (CONNAPPCT-MOT-002, -PET-003). The model sets only a bare number, so moving these folders would fail a tier-1 rule.
2. **Front matter between the tables.** Feature: blocks (or a frontmatter markdown field) set between `contents` and `authorities`, each on its own page. Folders: Connecticut opening-brief and answering-brief (and the amicus/reply templates, which share the code). Evidence: Practice Book Sec. 67-4(i) and 67-5 (CONNAPPCT-SEC-001, -009, mandatory): table of contents; statement of issues; table of authorities; then the facts. The templates set the frontmatter's `issues` there. The Connecticut INPUTs also mark authorities as `[text](toa:key)` links with a `category`; they would move to the model's `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` (an INPUT change, no model change).
3. **LaTeX: a heading split across pages.** `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` `heading` keeps a heading with what follows (`\nobreak` after it) but not its own lines together (no `\interlinepenalty=10000` inside it); typst and Word never split a heading. Folder: Illinois opening-brief, latex p13/p14: the 12-line level-2 heading "I. THE RENEWED PROSECUTION ..." runs "... PREJUDICIAL AND" at the foot of p13 and "REQUIRES REVERSAL ..." at the top of p14; typst moves the whole heading (with ARGUMENT) to p14.
4. **A paragraph that anchors a footnote: typst keeps its first line off the page foot, LaTeX and Word do not.** `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` `para` sets `\clubpenalty=0` (and `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` Body Text Note has no widow control) for such a paragraph, but typst's own orphan rule still applies there, so typst carries the paragraph's first line (and a heading kept with it) to the next page where LaTeX and Word leave it alone at the foot. Folders and first differing pages: Illinois opening-brief docx p19 ("The consideration for the agreement ..." ends p18 in docx, starts p19 in typst); reply-brief latex and docx p7 ("Additionally, in the present case ..."); amicus-brief latex and docx p11 (ARGUMENT and "Intimate partner violence[^1] ..." end p10 in latex and docx, start p11 in typst).
5. **A spaced ellipsis (". . .").** typst does not break before a period, even after a space (UAX #14 LB13), so "change . . . the" holds together; LaTeX and Word break at any of the spaces ("change . ." | ". the"). A no-break space does not help: `lib/latex/xengine.lua` turns a no-break space after a period back into a space (pandoc's abbreviation rule). Folders and first differing lines: Illinois answering-brief p3 line 18 and p12 line 13 (latex), p12 line 13 (docx); amicus-brief, opening-brief and reply-brief carry such ellipses too (7, 2 and 1).
6. **A URL broken after a period.** typst breaks "https://www.cnn." | "com/2019/03/27/..." ; LaTeX and Word do not break inside the URL. Folder: Illinois reply-brief p12 lines 30-31 (latex and docx).
7. **docx: a cover without tables.** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` writes `spec.cover` only when the layout has `contents` or `authorities`: without them the cover is dropped in Word. Worked around in the Illinois motion-brief and petition-for-review (numbered from the cover, no tables): their cover is `front` ending in `{break: true}`, which every engine sets alike.
8. **docx: a lettered list numbered 1, 2, 3.** pandoc reads "a. ..." as a lower-alpha list; LaTeX keeps the letters, the docx list writes decimal numbers. Illinois motion-brief p4 ("a. Motion to Dismiss Double Jeopardy"): worked around by escaping the markers in the INPUT (`a\.`), which typst always read as text.
9. **An empty list item.** A `""` item of a `{field}` list gives no line in typst and LaTeX, an empty line in Word. Illinois opening-brief and answering-brief `trial_court` had a `""` spacer: removed from the INPUTs.

## Shared-model requests, second pass (for the next serial lane)

A. **LaTeX loses a footnote inside a section or pilcrow sign's token.** `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` `texify` puts the token
   after "§" or "¶" in an `\mbox` (`xd.hold_sign_tokens` collects the inlines up to the next space), and a
   `\footnote` inside an `\mbox` is dropped: the mark prints, the note does not, and every page after it shifts.
   The token should end before a `Note` (or the note be moved after the box). Evidence: Illinois opening-brief
   "¶¶ 38-49.[^2]" (latex p18, note 2 missing; pp. 19 to 78 shift); Connecticut reply-brief
   "[Practice Book § 4007](toa:pb-4007),[^2]" (latex p13). check.sh Gate 0 does not catch it on latex (the content
   check passes with the note missing).
B. **docx page lookup: a citation over a page end on a page that ends with footnotes.** `tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs`
   finds a body needle's head at the end of a page's text, but a page with footnotes ends with the notes, so a
   citation split across the page break is not found there; an identical later citation is taken for it, and every
   needle before that one is then "not found" and the render stops. Evidence: Connecticut reply-brief, needle a2.1
   (*Demello v. Lakeside Club*, "Demello v." at the foot of p6 above note 1, the rest on p7; its second use, a2.2, on
   p7 has the same text): `fb render: not found in the PDF: a8.1, a10.1, a7.1, a3.1, a2.2`.
C. **LibreOffice sets a note that does not fit under its anchor's line on the next page** (already open from
   fb-595.54.13): Connecticut answering-brief p13 ("MRI.7"), Illinois opening-brief p44 ("(R 432).9").
D. **Word's footnote area** (open in fb-595.54.82, "Word's footnote area"): Illinois amicus-brief p10, Word sets
   two body lines above a 7-line note where typst and LaTeX set one.
E. **typst: a keyed `toa:` link in a heading marks the contents page.** `lib/typst/xdoc.typ` `contents-pages` sets
   `h.body`, and the keyed-link show rule marks it again there, so the table of authorities lists the contents page
   (LaTeX and Word do not). Worked around in the Connecticut opening-brief and amicus-brief INPUTs by unlinking the
   six citations in headings (the heading's page is then not listed unless the body cites it there: *Upjohn* loses
   p8 in the opening brief); relink them when fixed.
