## Shared-model requests (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82)

1. **Line breaks inside a bare URL** (as fb-595.54.62 request 2). `amicus-brief`, notes 1 and 6: typst breaks
   `https://ihaonline.org/wp-content/` | `uploads/2026/03/26Mission-Essential-2.pdf` (p5 lines 20-21, p22 lines
   20-21); pdflatex does not break the URL (XENG-003 latex p5 line 20), Word breaks it elsewhere (docx p22 line 20).
2. **xengine binds only the last sign of `§§`.** lib/typst/xengine.typ boxes `[§¶] \S+`; in "Iowa Code §§ 668.1(2)"
   that boxes the second `§` with the number and leaves a break between the two signs: typst ends `amicus-brief` p11
   line 8 with "Iowa Code §" and starts line 9 with "§ 668.1(2)"; LaTeX keeps "§§" on line 8, Word carries
   "§§ 668.1(2)" to line 9 (XENG-003 p11 lines 8-9, both engines). Wanted: `[§¶]+ \S+` (and the same in the
   pandoc side, which binds double signs already: `bind_double_signs`).
3. **LaTeX lets the first line of a hard-broken paragraph end a page.** A paragraph of lines joined by `\`
   (an address block in a certificate of service, "/s/ name\ Counsel for ..."): typst and Word move its first line
   with the rest (no orphan), pdflatex leaves it alone at the foot of the page. `amicus-brief` p25 (latex sets
   "H. Daniel Holm, Jr." on p25, typst on p26; XENG-002 latex p26, p27), `petition-for-rehearing` p17 (latex one
   line more on p17, XENG-002 latex p18). xengine.sty sets `\clubpenalty=10000`, but the line ends at pandoc's `\\`.
4. **Spaced ellipsis after a word.** `brief-on-the-merits` p29 line 21: "misleading the jury . . .”" -- typst
   does not break before a period (UAX 14), so "jury . . .”" moves whole to p30; LaTeX and Word break after "jury"
   and also between the dots (". ." | ".”"). XENG-002/003 latex and docx from p29 line 21 (p30 start). Wanted: one
   rule binding a spaced ellipsis to the word before it (and its dots together) in all three engines. Non-breaking
   spaces in the INPUT were tried: LaTeX ignored them (still broke between the dots), so the INPUT is unchanged.
5. **Word fits a line typst and LaTeX break when it ends within 0.05 pt of the margin.**
   `petition-for-discretionary-review` p12 line 3: "Unlike in 2018, neither *Casey* nor PPH 2018 loom as
   impediments to finding" ends at 539.956 pt in the docx (margin 540); typst and LaTeX carry "finding" to line 4
   (XENG-003 docx p12 lines 3-6; XENG-001/002 pass). The model's first fit needs the same tolerance in LibreOffice.
   Also for this folder (tier 2): the filed application sets the table of contents on page 1 under the
   introductory paragraph; the model's `contents` always opens a page (`table-title`), so the contents are on p2.
   Wanted: a `contents: {break: false}` (contents on the page of the cover).
6. **docx drops the cover when the document has no tables.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits `spec.cover` only inside
   `if paged` (contents, or authorities with frontmatter `authorities`); `motion-brief` (no tables, 6.1007) lost its
   whole caption page in Word (XENG-001 typst 10 pages, docx 9). Worked around: the motion's caption page is the
   layout's `front` followed by `{break: true}`. The cover should be emitted unconditionally.
7. **An empty item of a list field.** A signature's `lines` with `""` items (blank lines between signers, as filed):
   typst and LaTeX set no line for them, Word sets an empty paragraph of one line. `motion-brief` p9 and
   `petition-for-discretionary-review` p22 (the signature page) differ only in the blank lines (one page, no XENG effect);
   `petition-for-rehearing` p16: the Word signature runs one page longer (XENG-001 docx 19 pages, typst 18). Wanted:
   one rule (an empty item is an empty line in all three engines, or none).
8. **Body sections before the tables.** `petition-for-review`: Iowa R. App. P. 6.1103(1)(c)(1) (IOWA-PET-004, tier 1)
   puts the question presented "on the first page after the cover, nothing else on that page", before the table
   of contents; the current template does it with the frontmatter's `before_toc: 1` (the first N level-1 sections
   of the body set on their own pages ahead of the tables). The model's tables always follow the cover, and `front`
   comes after them, so porting would break a tier-1 rule; the folder is left as it was. The same feature serves
   `brief-on-the-merits` (`before_toc: 1`, the filer's certificate of compliance ahead of the contents; ported with
   the certificate as the first body section after the tables, tier 2, and the contents list it). Also wanted for
   `petition-for-review`: the notice line ("Iowa Court of Appeals Decision: ...") under the title, and a signature
   without a `signer` (both expressible now: `optional`).
9. **Checker: a standalone `....` line after a sentence ending in a period** (not a model item; tools/check,
   fb-595 lane). `answering-brief` quotes the record with paragraphs of `....` between them (as filed).
   gate0.awk `leader()` drops every period after four in a row from the PDF stream (table leaders), so "quickly."
   followed by the line "…." leaves four periods where the markdown has five, and DEF-CONT-001 then misses 25
   blocks from Q block 26 on -- on the committed render as well as on the ported one (the committed
   `.check-typst.txt` predates the current gate0.awk). Until the checker counts a leader only inside a row (or a
   run of its own longer than 4), no change to the folder can pass `fb check-changed`. Its port is otherwise
   ready to repeat: the shared Iowa layout (as `reply-brief`) with `between: [{text: "{caption_sep}"}]` and a
   level-4 heading `{align: center, bold: true, above: 14, below: 11.2}`; the statement-of-issues paragraphs need
   `I\.`, `II\.`, `III\.` (pandoc reads "I. WHETHER" as a roman list, fb-595.54.62 request 4); the docx and latex
   renders then still differ from p8 (a footnote's fit: typst carries one body line less on p8) and p44 on.
