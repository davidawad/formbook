## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

### 1. Two authorities that mark the same word: the docx page lookup loses its place (ND opening and answering)

- Folders: `north-dakota/state/court-of-appeals/opening-brief` and `answering-brief`.
- Evidence: the authorities list both "N.D.C.C. § 39-08-01" (`find: "39-08-01"`) and "N.D.C.C. § 39-08-01.2"
  (`find: "39-08-01.2"`). Every "39-08-01.2" in the body is marked for both entries. This happens in typst
  (`with-marks` matches the whole word around the find) and in `lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua` (plain find). `lib/docx/xdoc.lua`
  then writes two body needles with the same text, one after the other (`a13.1` and `a14.1`:
  `3908012tosmithwhowasconvicted...`). `tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs` `lookup` searches the second needle from one past
  the first needle's position. It misses, and the fallback ("3908012") is then found at a *later* citation. That
  moves `cur` past the next heading, and every later needle is "not found in the PDF" (h2 onwards in the opening
  brief, h4 onwards in the answering brief). `fb render --engine docx` fails.
- Needed (either or both):
  - (a) docxpages.rs: a body needle equal to the one before it is found at the same position (search from `cur - 1`
    or keep the previous match).
  - (b) The model marks a `find` as a word of its own, so "39-08-01" does not also mark "39-08-01.2". The tables of
    authorities in typst and LaTeX now list 39-08-01 on every page that cites only 39-08-01.2. This tier-2 inaccuracy
    was already in the old template.
- Until then, the two folders have no `docx/` (the generator would write it again; `fb engines gen --check` reports it
  as missing). Their docx gate is `report`.

### 2. TeX Gyre Schola (Century Schoolbook) in LaTeX: both Alabama courts

- Folders: `alabama/state/court-of-civil-appeals/{opening-brief,answering-brief,reply-brief}` and
  `alabama/state/court-of-criminal-appeals/{amicus-brief,answering-brief,petition-for-writ}`.
- Rule: ARAP 32(a)(7) requires "Century Schoolbook 14 point", footnotes included (court eval.yaml, rules.md). The
  templates set the vendored TeX Gyre Schola. `fb engines gen` refuses the face: "the shared document model does not
  set Schola in LaTeX yet (lib/latex/xeschola.lua)".
- These folders also need, from the same templates:
  - Headings numbered by the template: level 2 "I.", level 3 "A.", level 4 "1.", level 5 "a." (crim). The body is
    typed without numbers.
  - A word count the template computes for the ARAP 32(d) certificate of compliance. It counts from the Statement of
    the Case through the conclusion (`counted-words` / `count-words`). The frontmatter gives no count.
  - A fixed certificate of compliance and a certificate of service generated after the signature, with the first
    signer's block repeated under each.
  - Space that fills the cover (`v(1fr)` around the title and before counsel).
  - Criminal appeals, petition-for-writ: `caption_v: false` drops the "v." (a `when` on a false value; see request 4).
- None of the six folders was moved. Each keeps its own typst template, and `params.cross_engine` stays unset
  (report).

### 3. Texas Special Court of Review: numbered headings, the § caption, the authorities' own schema

- Folders: `texas/state/special-court-of-review/{answering-brief,motion-brief,reply-brief}`. All three share one
  template.
- Headings numbered by the template: level 2 "I." (centred), levels 3 to 5 "A.", "1." and "a." in a hanging 0.5 in
  column. The samples' headings carry no numbers.
- answering-brief (`memo: true`): no cover. Page 1 has the examiners' memorandum caption, a three-column grid of
  `caption-left`, a column of "§" and `caption-right`, row for row (the "column of ')' beside a caption" gap, with "§").
- The authorities use the court's own frontmatter (`authorities: [{group, items: [{name, cite, italic, find}]}]`), not
  the model's `{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}`. A missing `find` falls back to the name, and an uncited entry prints
  "not cited" in bold. The checklist forbids new frontmatter fields, so this needs either the model to read this schema
  or a decision to rewrite the three samples.
- The body is split at a `{{contents}}` line: front matter on lower-roman pages before the tables, then the body from
  1.
- The cover sets counsel in two columns per row (`columns: {each, per_row: 2}` would cover that). Lines shorter than 50
  characters get no first-line indent, and a paragraph ending in ":" is kept with the next. That is a typst `show par`
  rule the model does not have.
- Not moved. Each folder keeps its own typst template, and `params.cross_engine` stays unset (report).
