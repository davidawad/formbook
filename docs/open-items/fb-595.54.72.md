# fb-595.54.72: open items (generated engines fan-out)

Folders of this batch: the Tennessee Court of Criminal Appeals, the Utah Court of Appeals and the New Jersey Supreme
Court, each court root and its doc folders. What the shared model (lib/typst/xdoc.typ, lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua,
lib/latex/xdoc.lua, lib/docx/xdoc.lua, tools/fb/src/enginegen) lacks or gets wrong for them, for the serial lane
fb-595.54.82. Nothing here was changed in shared code by this batch.

## Shared-model requests

### docx: a cover that opens with a `columns` block has no "start" needle

- Feature: `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` takes the page-1 needle ("start") from the text of the first node of the cover's tree;
  a cover whose first block is `columns` gives an empty needle and `fb render --engine docx` fails with
  "not found in the PDF: start".
- Folders: none left blocked, by a change of layout: the Utah cover now opens with the centered court name (as the
  opening and reply exemplars do), the New Jersey amicus letterhead is stacked instead of two columns. Any cover that
  starts with a caption grid or a two-column letterhead hits it.
- Evidence: Utah opening-brief with the court name and address beside the case number as its first block.

### docx: a first body heading longer than the 48-character needle is found in the table of contents

- Feature: the needle of a heading is its text and what follows, cut to 48 reduced characters (`NEEDLE`); when the
  heading alone is longer, the needle of the first body heading matches its own contents row (the contents page comes
  first), so the body's decimal numbering starts on the contents page and every docx page label (contents and
  authorities) is three pages early. The search after the `front` needle should start past the tables.
- Folders: `courts/tenth-circuit/utah/state/court-of-appeals/amicus-brief` (docx; `cross_engine: report`,
  `cross_engine_latex: gate`).
- Evidence: amicus-brief docx p2 "Statement of the Issues .... 4" where typst and LaTeX print 1 (XENG-003, p2 line 4;
  first heading "Identification of Amicus Curiae and Statement of Interest in Issue Presented").

### docx: LibreOffice fits a line typst overflows (advance drift)

- Feature: LibreOffice's word positions run up to about 0.13 pt short of typst's over a 468 pt line (twip-rounded
  advances); a line typst overflows by less than that keeps its last word in Word. LaTeX has `latex_bias` for newtx;
  docx has none.
- Folders: `courts/tenth-circuit/utah/state/court-of-appeals/opening-brief` (docx; `cross_engine: report`,
  `cross_engine_latex: gate`).
- Evidence: opening-brief p11 line 15: typst ends after "had" ("moved" would end at 540.02 pt, 0.02 pt over the
  column), docx sets "moved" ending at 539.89 pt (XENG-003, 1157/1160 lines).

### typst vs LaTeX/Word: a footnote paragraph's last two lines

- Feature: typst keeps the last two lines of a paragraph together (its widow control) when the last line carries a
  footnote; LaTeX and Word, whose filters turn widow control off for a paragraph that anchors a footnote, leave the
  second-to-last line on the page. xdoc does not pass xengine's `footnote-widows` option and offers no way to match.
- Folders: `courts/tenth-circuit/utah/state/court-of-appeals/answering-brief` (latex and docx; `cross_engine:
  report`).
- Evidence: answering-brief p13/p14: typst p14 starts "ordered Harper's release ...", LaTeX and docx start
  "investigation (PSI) report. R938:604-05.4" (XENG-002, 21 of 65 page ends on LaTeX, 19 on docx; both other engines
  agree with each other).

### docx: an alphabetic list label prints as a number

- Feature: pandoc (commonmark_x, fancy lists) reads a paragraph that starts "(A) " as an upper-alpha list where
  cmarker reads text; the docx filter then labels it "(1)". Fixed in this batch's sample by escaping the parenthesis
  (`\(A)`, the same text); a sample that means such a list would still print the wrong label in docx.
- Folders: none left blocked (`courts/tenth-circuit/utah/state/court-of-appeals/amicus-brief`, INPUT line 223).
- Evidence: DEF-CONT-001 on docx, "block 74 ... (A) in any proceeding".

### typst breaks a URL link inside a word; LaTeX and Word do not

- Feature: cmarker makes a bare URL a typst link, which typst may break after "_" and other URL characters; the
  pandoc engines set it as one word. The shared model has no URL break rule (ca8's folder filters had one:
  break only after "?", "=", "&", a hyphen or a slash, never after the scheme's "//", in all three engines).
- Folders: `courts/third-circuit/new-jersey/state/supreme-court/amicus-brief` (latex and docx; `cross_engine:
  report`; every other line matches, 314/324 lines, all page ends equal).
- Evidence: amicus-brief p11 line 21, footnote 2: typst "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_" / "Wire (last visited
  ...", LaTeX and docx move the whole URL to the next line (XENG-003).

### LaTeX: a level-1 heading followed by a numbered list can end a page

- Feature: lib/latex/xdoc.lua keeps a heading with a following paragraph but not with a following list; typst and Word
  keep it with the list's first item.
- Folders: `courts/third-circuit/new-jersey/state/supreme-court/petition-for-writ` (latex; `cross_engine: report`,
  `cross_engine_docx: gate`).
- Evidence: petition-for-writ p6: LaTeX sets "ESTABLISHMENT OF NJRC" as the page's last line, typst and docx start
  p7 with it (XENG-002, 13 of 20 page ends from p7).

### Keyed authorities with explicit marks, kinds, sorting and "passim" (NJ formal briefs)

- Feature: the New Jersey formal-brief samples give `authorities` as a map of keys to `{kind, name, cite, sort}` and
  mark each citation in the body (and in footnotes) by a link `[text](toa:key)`; the template groups the entries by
  kind (cases, statutes, constitutional provisions, rules, other authorities), sorts them by `sort` or `name`, sets
  the case name in italics and prints "passim" for seven pages or more. The shared model reads only
  `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` marked by `find` text: a `find` cannot tell apart two entries
  cited by the same short form (State v. Adams, 2008 and 2006) and would mark every unlinked mention too. Not
  converted: the sample's frontmatter is read as written.
- Folders: `courts/third-circuit/new-jersey/state/supreme-court/answering-brief`,
  `courts/third-circuit/new-jersey/state/supreme-court/brief-on-the-merits` (not moved onto the model; typst
  template unchanged, no latex or docx; ungated).
- Evidence: answering-brief INPUT `authorities: {adams194: {kind: case, ...}, adams06: ...}` and 100 `(toa:` links;
  brief-on-the-merits 86. Their covers also fit counsel stacked or in two balanced columns by measuring the page
  (`layout()` + `measure`), which the model cannot do (one or the other is fixed per folder).

### Tennessee: TeX Gyre Schola, a cover that fills its page, numbered headings, a computed word count

- Feature (each missing from the model, "Not yet in the model" in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md):
  1. TeX Gyre Schola in LaTeX: `fb engines gen` refuses the face (gen.rs: lib/latex/xeschola.lua is not run by
     lib/latex/xdoc.lua). Rule 46 section 3.02(3)(a) asks for a Century-family face (TENNCRIMAPP-FORM-004), so no
     other vendored face may stand in.
  2. Space that fills a page: the cover centres the title between the caption and counsel with `v(1fr)`.
  3. Headings numbered by the template: level 2 "I.", level 3 "A.", level 4 "1.", level 5 "a." (the INPUT headings
     carry no numbers), in the body and the table of contents.
  4. A word count the template computes from the body for the certificate of compliance (Tenn. R. App. P. 30(e)); the
     frontmatter gives none.
- Folders: `courts/sixth-circuit/tennessee/state/court-of-criminal-appeals/amicus-brief`, `.../answering-brief`,
  `.../motion-brief`, `.../opening-brief` (not moved onto the model; typst template unchanged; ungated, no latex or
  docx).
- Evidence: tenncrimapp-*-brief.typ lines 15 (face), 58-66 (heading-number), 133-138 (count-words), the cover's two
  `v(1fr)`.

## Tier-2 changes made by the port (for review)

- Utah: the ruled case-number box of the old cover is gone (no exemplar has one; the model has no box): the case
  number sits beside the caption, between rules. The certificate of compliance moved from the first body page to after
  the signature (three of the four exemplars; rules.md UTAHCTAPP-CERT-004). The certificates are no longer rows of the
  table of contents (they are closing blocks, not headings).
- New Jersey amicus letter brief: the letterhead's two columns are stacked (the organisation's lines, then the
  signer's lines set right): a cover that opens with `columns` cannot be paged in docx (above). The table of contents
  moves from the foot of the letter page (exemplar page i) to a page of its own (i); the letter page is unnumbered.
  The point headings carry their numbers in the INPUT ("I.", "II.", as filed) instead of the template numbering them.
- New Jersey petition: the caption is no longer a two-column grid with a dashed rule (it ran over two pages; a model
  column never breaks): the Court and docket number at the right, the parties full width, the title between rules.
  The numbered paragraphs hang their text after the number at the margin. Exhibit slip sheets set the heading 3 in.
  down a page of its own (the model cannot centre on a page).
