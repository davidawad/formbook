  `columns` ("V." on the left, `{cause_number}` on the right), followed by the title between two `rule`s. The

### 2. URL line breaks (the same break opportunities in every engine)

- Folder: `ninth-circuit/alaska/state/court-of-appeals/amicus-brief`, latex and docx.
- First difference: page 9, line 24 (typst page numbering), footnote 19. Typst breaks
  `https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2022/dec/` | `27/pamela-moses-...` (after a slash, before a digit). LaTeX
  breaks `https://www.theguardian.com/us-` | `news/2022/...` (after a hyphen). Word moves the whole URL to its own
  line and breaks `...mistake-` | `jail.`. Footnotes 20, 21, 50, 51 and 54 differ the same way. The page ends
  then drift from page 11 on.
- What the model lacks: lib/docx/xdoc.lua `breaks` adds a break only after a slash inside a word *before a
  letter*. Typst also breaks after a slash before a digit. pdflatex breaks after an explicit hyphen inside a URL,
  and Word/LibreOffice do not. No rule makes the three agree for URLs.
- Needed: one URL rule in all three engines. For example: break after every `/` and nowhere else inside a URL
  (typst: ZWSP or `box`es per segment; LaTeX: `\hyphenchar` off and `\allowbreak` after `/`; Word: ZWSP after `/`
  and a non-breaking hyphen for `-`).
- The folder passes tier 1 on all three engines. `params.cross_engine: report` until this rule lands.
