## Shared-model requests (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82)

1. **Bare URLs.** Folders: amicus-brief, answering-brief, motion-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review,
   petition-for-writ (the same gap as fb-595.54.62 request 2). typst breaks a URL inside the word wherever the line
   fills (PDR p19 line 11: `https://www.baltimo` / `recityschools.org/district-overview`; escaping the autolink in
   the INPUT, `https\://www\.`, changes nothing, so it is typst's own URL breaking, not cmarker's autolink);
   LibreOffice breaks after a slash (amicus p11 note 2: `.../reports/` / `AOB_Report_Revised_Redacted_Interim.pdf`)
   or moves a URL that fits whole; pdflatex never breaks and runs past the margin (answering p18: right edge
   573.43 pt > 540, `https://www.elections.maryland.gov/press_room/documents/ThreeElectionsDeadlinesExt...`;
   DEF-FORM-002 fails). Wanted: one break rule for URLs in all three engines.
2. **A cover without tables is dropped by the docx engine.** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` emits `spec.cover` only inside
   `if paged` (contents or authorities present). Worked around in motion-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review and
   petition-for-rehearing: the caption page is written as `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}` (the same page 1 in
   every engine; numbered with `page_number.from: 2` in the petitions: the caption page counts but prints no number;
   before the move it was unnumbered and the body began at 1).
3. **A heading that starts with an authority's `find` shifts every later docx page reference.** reply-brief, before
   its INPUT was fixed: the heading `8-103 OF THE ELECTION ARTICLE IS NOT UNCONSTITUTIONAL` (find `8-103`) wrote a
   heading needle and an authority needle at the same text position; docxpages.rs searches each body needle from one
   past the previous match, so the authority needle missed, its fallback (`8103`) matched the next occurrence, and
   every following needle landed one occurrence late until some were not found at all (`fb render: not found in the
   PDF: a1.9, a4.2, ...`, the docx render fails). Worked around: the heading was one heading split in two by the text
   layer; joined (`## I. APPELLEE PROVIDES ... THAT 8-103 OF ...`) the find is no longer at the heading's start.
4. **typst splits a doubled section or paragraph sign from its number.** `lib/typst/xengine.typ` boxes `[§¶] \S+`,
   which binds the second sign of `§§ 9-209` but leaves a break between the two signs; `lib/docx/xdoc.lua`
   (`bind_double_signs`) and LaTeX keep `§§ 9-209` together (motion p2 line 13, PDR p12 line 9: typst ends the line
   with `§`). Worked around in the motion and PDR INPUTs with a no-break space after `§§` / `¶¶`; the typst rule should
   box `[§¶]{1,2} \S+`.
5. **LaTeX leaves a heading at the foot of a page when a list follows.** answering-brief p6: `QUESTIONS PRESENTED`
   is the last line of p6 in latex, its numbered list on p7; typst and Word carry the heading over with the list.
6. **LaTeX does not break inside `d/b/a`.** amicus-brief p1 line 4 (cover caption): typst and Word break after
   `d/` (the slash rule inside a word), pdflatex moves the whole word to the next line.
7. **A paragraph that anchors a footnote keeps its last two lines together in typst only.** amicus-brief p18/p19:
   the paragraph holding note 5 ends `... repose.” 54` / `C.J.S. Limitations of Actions § 132.`; typst moves both
   lines to p19 (widow control), Word leaves the first on p18 (Body Text Note has no widow control), and every later
   page start differs. The footnote-widow option of xengine (`footnote-widows: false`) is not reachable from
   layout.yaml.
8. **A keyed table of authorities with marks as links.** brief-on-the-merits: the frontmatter's `authorities` is a
   map `key: {kind, name, cite, sort}` and the body marks each citation as a link `[text](toa:key)`; the table groups
   by kind (cases, statutes, other, rules, constitutional provisions), sorts by `sort`/`name`, prints "passim" at 7 or
   more pages, and italicizes case names. The model reads only `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`
   with text matches; converting the INPUT would change the sample's contract and lose the exact marks.
9. **A body part placed between closing blocks.** brief-on-the-merits sets, after the signature, the certification
   of word count, then the body's `# CITATION AND VERBATIM TEXT OF ALL PERTINENT LAW` appendix, then the certificate
   of service. `tail` sets the body part after the whole closing; the order needs a closing split around the tail.
10. **Pipe tables in the body.** petition-for-review (`| District | Reock Score | Polsby-Popper Score |`, in
    section 6.4): `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` and `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` stop with `xdoc: unsupported block Table`.

    `?fbclid=IwAR0_M2BhVxD42BjIfTh_bYVwSfg6nH68cwLArtGt7GPpl58FqpE4g_Bnfgo.` contains no slash, no dot before a letter,

    emergency break rule for a piece that cannot fit on a line, the same in every engine.
12. **LibreOffice ignores the zero-width space after a hyphen when a no-break hyphen follows in the word.**
    amicus-brief note 15 (p29). The docx holds `INVESTIGATING-<ZWSP>GRAND-<ZWSP>JURY-<ZWSP>REPORT-<ZWSP>NO.`,

    LibreOffice sets `...REPORT-NO.-1_FINAL_May-2023_Redact` and moves `ed.pdf.` to the next line. Pages 30 to 33

    typst on the same note.
13. **The sign binding does not reach inside a keyed authority link.** brief-on-the-merits has
    `[§ 11-308(a)](toa:s-11-308)` in the body. typst keeps `§ 11-308(a)` together; pdfLaTeX and LibreOffice end the
    line after `§` (p7 lines 3, 7, 12). Worked around in the INPUT with a no-break space after a `§` that opens a
    link (27 links), as for request 4.
14. **A keyed group in italics drops the comma.** `kinds: [{italic: true}]` sets `*name* cite`; the Court's tables
