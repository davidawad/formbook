# fb-595.54.32: open items (Kentucky Supreme Court on the shared model)

None of the bead's eight doc folders could be gated, for the reasons below. They are left on their current typst
templates (no `layout.yaml`, no generated latex/docx, `cross_engine` not set, so `report`) for the serial lane
fb-595.54.82. Each blocker was hit by an actual port (layout.yaml written, typst moved onto lib/typst/xdoc.typ,
`fb engines gen`, latex and docx rendered, `fb diff --engines`) or follows from what lib/typst/xdoc.typ has; the ports
were then reverted.

## Shared-model requests

### A colored cover page

- Feature: a fill for the cover page (layout.yaml e.g. `cover_fill: "#d9c4a0"`), set behind the cover in all three
  engines (typst `page(background)`, LaTeX a full-page `\pagecolor`/picture on that page only, Word a shape or page
  background on the first section).
- Folders: `courts/sixth-circuit/kentucky/state/supreme-court/amicus-brief` (tan), `answering-brief` (blue),
  `brief-on-the-merits` (blue), `reply-brief` (yellow), `petition-for-rehearing` (green).
- Evidence: RAP 31(C)(2), 43(B)(4), 34(B)(4) (rules.md KY-COVER-002, -003, -004, kind M) mandate the cover colors; the
  current templates fill the cover page (`cover-color`). lib/typst/xdoc.typ has no page fill or background, so a port
  would drop a tier-1 requirement.

### Statement of points and authorities from the Kentucky frontmatter (link-keyed authorities, *passim*)

- Feature: an authorities table read from `authorities: {KEY: {kind, name, cite, sort}}` (grouped by `kind` under fixed
  titles CASES, STATUTES, CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS, RULES, OTHER AUTHORITIES; a case name in italics), its pages
  from body links `[text](toa:KEY)` (the link text printed, the page marked) rather than `find` text; a page list of 7
  or more set as *passim* (see also the *passim* request in fb-595.54.71.md). The contents and the authorities share
  one title (STATEMENT OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES, RAP 32(A)(2)) and are numbered i, ii.
- Folders: `amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`, `reply-brief` (21, 26, 21 and 23 `toa:` links in
  their INPUTs).
- Evidence: the layout.yaml grammar reads only `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`, and the checklist
  forbids new frontmatter fields; rewriting the four INPUTs to the model's shape would change the samples, not the
  layout.

### The ʻokina (U+02BB) in LaTeX

- Feature: pdflatex (T1, newtx) has no mapping for U+02BB; `fb render --engine latex` stops with "Unicode character ʻ
  (U+02BB) not set up for use with LaTeX". lib/latex needs a `\DeclareUnicodeCharacter{02BB}` (the face's glyph, or a
  turned comma at the typst advance) shared by every folder (nine Hawaii INPUTs carry it as well).
- Folder: `motion-brief` ("PLANNED PARENTHOOD GREAT NORTHWEST, HAWAIʻI, ALASKA, ..."; the filed motion has the ʻokina,
  so the INPUT is right). docx also differs on those lines (p1 lines 6 and 17, p10 lines 10 and 20 of the ported render).

### Breaking around a spaced ellipsis (". . .")

- Feature: one rule for line breaks inside and before a spaced ellipsis. typst (UAX #14, LB13: no break before ".")
  keeps "issue . . ." together; Word breaks after "issue ." and LaTeX at any space.
- Folders: `motion-brief` (3 in the INPUT), `petition-for-writ` (7), `petition-for-rehearing` (2), `answering-brief` (1).
- Evidence: motion-brief ported, docx: 262/268 lines; first differing line not explained by the ʻokina is p8 line 1,
  typst "... the constitutionality of the laws at" / "issue . . . the state's interest", Word "... laws at issue ." /
  ". . the state's interest".

### Pandoc reads lettered or parenthesized paragraphs as lists (cmarker does not)

- Feature: the pandoc reader of lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua should parse markdown as cmarker (CommonMark) does: pandoc's
  `fancy_lists` turns "a. Take *DeGrella*." and "(1) To retaliate ..." into ordered lists, typst sets paragraphs (a
  `-fancy_lists` reader, or `commonmark_x`).
- Folders: `petition-for-writ` (paragraphs a.-d., INPUT lines 273-289: latex p19 line 21 "a. Take DeGrella" -36 pt
  indent, page ends differ from p20 on, 36 vs 35 pages), `petition-for-discretionary-review` (a block quotation of KRS
  344.280 whose clauses open "(1)", "(2)": both pandoc engines stop with "xdoc: a quotation holds paragraphs only",
  the "list inside a quotation" request of fb-595.54.71.md).

### URLs in footnotes

- `petition-for-writ`: typst breaks inside the URLs of notes 1 and 6 (p5 line 23 ".../" "record/19rs/sb9.html", p14
  line 23), LaTeX and Word do not: the URL request of fb-595.54.71.md.

### A frontmatter value that is only underscores

- The model sets every frontmatter string as markdown inlines, so a signature line "_______________________________"
  (motion-brief `signature[0].lines[0]`, `service.signer[0]`) is a thematic break: typst warns "block may not occur
  inside of a paragraph" and drops it. Escaping it in the INPUT (`"\\_\\_..."`) works in typst; the shared fix is to
  set a value that is a thematic break as literal text in all three engines.

## Tier 2, for the port

- Pitch: the current templates use `leading: 0.9em` (a line pitch of 19.1 pt, measured on the motion render, p2),
  under Word's double (27.6 pt at 12 pt). On the model's 27.6 pt the motion runs 11 pages (was 8) and the writ 35 (was 25; RAP 60(F) is 14,000 words or
  30 pages, word-controlled). The double space of RAP 5(B)(4) is the reason; the page counts change with it.
- The motion's closing (date, "Respectfully submitted," six signature blocks two to a row) does not fit after the
  conclusion and takes page 10 alone: "the last paragraph kept with the closing" is not in the model.

then the certificate of service and, when given, the RAP 15(E) word-count certificate on their own pages); the
discretionary review and the writ set their single signature at `left: 216` instead of two to a row:
