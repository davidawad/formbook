## Shared-model requests

1. **A spaced ellipsis (". . .") after a word.** Already filed by fb-595.54.32, .44, .53, .56 and .59. typst does not
   break before " ." (UAX 14 LB13), LaTeX and Word do. Folder: hawtax `motion-brief`, latex and docx, p12 lines 2-3
   ("the tax appeal court . . . in the person's own name": typst moves "court" on, LaTeX and Word end the line after
   "court . ."), 374 of 376 lines identical, pages and page ends equal. `cross_engine: report` until the engines share
   one rule.
2. **LaTeX splits a heading of several lines at a page foot.** Already filed by fb-595.54.35 (request 5) and .47
   (request 3). Folder: idahoctapp `opening-brief`, latex: heading C of the argument (seven lines) starts at the foot of
   p11 with two lines; typst and Word move it whole to p12 with the quotation after it, and p12 to p18 shift (XENG-002,
   XENG-003). `cross_engine_latex: report`.
3. **An authority's `find` that matches a whole word only.** `with-marks` (typst) and `xd.mark` (pandoc) match `find`
   inside a longer word: "232-1" marks every "232-14.5", and two authorities marked at one place make the docx page
   lookup (docxpages.rs) miss every later needle ("not found in the PDF: h2, a13.1, ..."), the defect fb-595.54.19
   worked around by hand. Folder: hawtax `motion-brief` (HRS § 232-1 and § 232-14.5). Worked around in the INPUT: the
   § 232-1 entry has no `find` and carries its pages ("12, 13", read off the render) as the model's `pages`.
4. **A cover that opens with columns breaks the docx page lookup.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes the `start` needle from
   the first block of `cover` (or `front`) only when it is a text block; a cover whose first block is `columns` writes
   an empty needle, which is never found ("not found in the PDF: start"). Folder: hawtax `motion-brief`, whose flyleaf
   sets counsel in a 3.5-inch column beside a 3-inch band left clear for the clerk (as the amicus and notice folders
   do in `front`, which has no tables and so no needles). Worked around: the motion's cover opens with counsel as text
   blocks with a right indent, and the band is not kept when counsel is short (the motion's counsel runs past it).
5. **A blank band of fixed height.** The hawtax flyleaf keeps the top right 3 inches clear for the clerk (RCCH 3(c),
   via HAWTAX-FORM-002); the model has no empty block of a given height, so the amicus and notice folders close the
   band with a one-line no-break space under 202.2 pt of space above. A `{space: <pt>}` block would say it plainly.
6. **LibreOffice footnote placement.** Already filed by fb-595.54.13 (Pennsylvania amicus and King's Bench): LibreOffice
   sets a note that does not fit under its anchor's line on the next page and keeps the line; typst and LaTeX move the
   line on with its note. Folders: ri `amicus-brief` docx (p6, "on February 11, 2020.2"; 458 of 461 lines identical),
   ri `reply-brief` docx (p10, footnote 2, nine lines; pages 11 to 22 shift). The spaced ellipsis of request 1 also
   blocks ri `reply-brief` latex (p27 "matters . . . arising", p30 "a way . . . to render"; 708 of 713 lines).

## Input changes made for the model (documented here, not new fields)

- hawtax `motion-brief`: `authorities` changed from a keyed map (`key: {kind, name, cite}`) to
  `[{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` (Cases:, Statutes:, Rules:, each sorted as the old table sorted them); the
  body's `[text](toa:key)` links became their text. Citations by "*Id.*" alone and "HRCP Rule 12(h)(3)" are no longer
  marked (a `find` is one string); "passim" is gone (the model lists every page).
- hawtax `amicus-brief`, `petition-for-review`: the templates printed no table of authorities (`tables` false); the
  unused keyed `authorities` map was removed and the `toa:` links became their text.
- ri `amicus-brief`: a footnote definition that followed a paragraph without a blank line (pandoc reads it as text;
  CommonMark as a definition) got its blank line. The same fix in nyappterm `amicus-brief`.
