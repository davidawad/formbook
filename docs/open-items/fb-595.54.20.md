### Still missing (for a second serial lane)

**A. The ʻokina passes DEF-CONT-001 in LaTeX and Word** (5 folders). The check reads the PDF text through
`pdftohtml -xml` (tools/fb/src/check/prepare.rs, `runs.tsv`), which ignores LaTeX's `/ActualText`: the newtx glyph the
model sets for U+02BB reads as ‘ and normalizes to `'`, so every block holding an ʻokina fails ("the markdown has
'ʻi403,…' where the PDF has ''i403,…'", `amicus-brief`, 8 blocks; `petition-for-writ` likewise). `pdftotext` (and so
XENG) reads it right. In Word LibreOffice takes ʻ from a fallback face and the PDF text sets it after the word
("Hawai i" in `pdftohtml`, "iʻ403" in DEF-CONT-001). Either the check treats ʻ and ‘ alike and joins the split word as
the cross-engine diff does, or the engines set U+02BB from a glyph whose ToUnicode is U+02BB (a docx run in a face that
has it; a LaTeX virtual glyph with a `/ToUnicode` entry).

**B. A table inside a frontmatter markdown value** (`petition-for-rehearing`, HAW-RECON-002 tier 1). Its certificate
of service (`certificates[1].text`) lists the persons served as a pipe table; `{markdown: "{text}"}` joins the table's
lines into one paragraph of pipes. Needs tables in the body or in a `{markdown}` value (the "tables in the body"
request, 5 folders). The declaration (paragraphs "1\." to "3\.") would set with `{markdown}` as it is.

**C. docx: pages of `before_tables` numbered from the cover** (`petition-for-writ`, the first folder to use the key).
typst and LaTeX number the counsel page i and the contents ii; the Word render prints iii and iv (XENG-002 p2, p3;
`fb diff --engines`: "p2: typst runs 'DONNA' .. 'i', this engine 'DONNA' .. 'iii'").

**D. LaTeX: a footnote right after a section sign's token is lost** (`amicus-brief` note 6, "HRS § 602-4.[^6]";
`petition-for-review` note 8, "HAR § 18-237-29.53-10(a).[^8]"). lib/latex/xdoc.lua `texify` boxes the token after "§"
up to the next space in an `\mbox` (xd.hold_sign_tokens), and the note mark is part of the token: a `\footnote`
inside an `\mbox` sets its mark and drops the note text (no note on the page, DEF-CONT-001 and XENG-003 differ).
The hold should end the token before a Note (typst and Word keep the note).

**E. LaTeX: no break after a slash inside a word** (`petition-for-review` p5: typst and Word break "and/ or", LaTeX
moves "and/or" to the next line). The URL rule breaks after a slash in a URL only; typst and LibreOffice also break
after a slash between two words.

**F. typst: a list in a quotation is narrower than in LaTeX and Word** (`answering-brief` p8 line 18 and p26: typst
breaks "convert | wind energy" where LaTeX and Word set "wind" on the line, its right edge at 497.3 pt inside the
quotation's 504; the widest typst line of such a list ends at 490.6). One measure for a quotation's list in all three.

**G. docx: a cover without tables 2.4 pt higher** (`petition-for-discretionary-review` p1: every flyleaf line 2.4 pt
above typst's, first line at 216.3 to typst's 218.7); the caption's two columns then read in another order (XENG-003
p1 lines 9-15).

Also seen, already listed as not implemented by fb-595.54.82: typst setting a note on another page than its mark
(`petition-for-discretionary-review` p9: notes 5 and 6 on one line, typst splits them over pages 9 and 10, LaTeX moves
the line); LibreOffice keeping a line whose note goes to the next page (`reply-brief` p9, `petition-for-writ` p8: docx
reported); the line-fit tolerance (`amicus-brief` docx p8: typst leaves 0.29 pt, Word fits "Police";
`petition-for-discretionary-review` docx p8).

## Shared-model requests of pass 1 (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82; status above)

### 1. The ʻokina (U+02BB MODIFIER LETTER TURNED COMMA) in LaTeX and Word

- Feature: set U+02BB in the generated LaTeX preamble (pdflatex with `fontenc` T1 has no glyph for it:
  `! LaTeX Error: Unicode character ʻ (U+02BB) not set up for use with LaTeX`, no PDF) and keep it in place in the
  Word render (LibreOffice sets it from a fallback face and `pdftotext` reads it out of order: "Hawaiʻi" comes out
  as "Hawaii" with the ʻ a word later, so DEF-CONT-001 fails and XENG-003 differs on every line that carries it).
  Probably a `\DeclareUnicodeCharacter{02BB}` to a glyph of the face (e.g. the left single quote, with an
  `/ActualText` span so the PDF text keeps U+02BB, as `\xd@ellipsis` does) and the same advance in xengine's width
  model, and a docx run in the body face.
- Folders: `amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `petition-for-discretionary-review`, `petition-for-review`,
  `petition-for-writ` (their INPUTs, transcribed as filed, spell Hawaiʻi with the ʻokina).
- Evidence: `fb render --engine latex` on `amicus-brief` stops at the flyleaf's court line; the docx render of the
  same sample fails DEF-CONT-001 with 19 of 43 blocks altered ("the markdown has 'ʻi403,…' where the PDF has
  'iʻ403,…'"). A diagnostic render of the sample with U+02BB replaced by U+2018 (not committed) gets past it.

### 2. A paragraph that anchors a footnote: its first line alone at the foot of a page

- Feature: typst keeps orphan control on a paragraph that carries a footnote; the LaTeX and Word filters turn
  widow control off for such a paragraph (Body Text Note), so they set its first line alone at the foot of the
  page where typst moves it on. One rule for all three.
- Folder: `amicus-brief` (diagnostic render, U+02BB replaced): page 12 (printed 10), typst moves "In five
  states—Massachusetts, …" to page 13; LaTeX and Word both keep it on page 12 (they agree with each other; 4 of 16
  pages end on another word from there).
- Folder: `reply-brief`, page 17 (printed 14): Word sets heading C and the first line of the paragraph that
  anchors note 9 at the page foot; typst moves both to page 18.

### 3. docx contents page numbers when the tables are not numbered apart

- Feature: lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes a heading's needle as its text and the text after it, cut at 48 reduced
  characters, and searches it from page 1 when `page_number.front` is not set; a heading longer than that is found in
  its own row of the table of contents, so the row prints the contents page. Search from the end of the tables.
- Folders: none left on it (`brief-on-the-merits` showed it with `page_number: {from: 2}`: rows I-III printed 2;
  its exemplar numbers the tables in lower roman, which the layout now does, and with `front` the search starts
  after the tables).

### 4. Space that fills a page on the flyleaf (`v(1fr)`)

- The old templates of `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`, `reply-brief`, `petition-for-review`,
  `petition-for-discretionary-review` and `petition-for-rehearing` spread the flyleaf with `v(1fr)` (title midway,
  counsel at the foot). The layouts use fixed space instead (HAW-COVER-003: the arrangement is convention, the
  rule fixes the content); a fill would match the exemplars more closely. Already listed under "Not yet in the
  model" in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md.

### 5. A long note split over a page in LaTeX

- Feature: typst splits a long footnote over two pages and keeps its anchor line on the first; the LaTeX engine
  moves the anchor line and the note to the next page. Word splits it where typst does.
- Folder: `reply-brief`, page 7 (printed 4): typst ends the page on "Hawai’i’s appellate review … error.4 Rather,
  this Honorable" with note 4 continued on page 8; LaTeX ends it a line earlier; 17 of 23 pages differ from there.

### 6. The last paragraph kept with the closing (signature page, HAW-SIG-002)

- HAW-SIG-002 wants at least 2 lines of text on the page that carries the signature (or a notation at its foot).
  The model keeps the closing whole but not with the last paragraph of the body, so a closing can open a page
  alone (the Word render of `reply-brief`: date and signature alone on page 23). Already listed under "Not yet in
  the model" in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md; the old `answering-brief` template placed its `notation` at the page foot,
  which needs the same feature (or a fill, request 4).

### 7. docx: a cover without tables is dropped

- Feature: lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits `cover` only inside its `if paged` branch (a layout with `contents` or
  `authorities`), so a folder with a cover and no tables loses its cover in Word (the typst and LaTeX renders keep
  it). Emit the cover (and its section when `page_number.front` is set) in every case.
- Folder: `motion-brief` (first docx render: 7 pages to typst's 8, page 1 the motion). Its flyleaf is now the
  `front` of page 1 followed by `{break: true}`, with `page_number: {from: 2}` (the flyleaf counted, unnumbered);
  the same layout as before in all three engines.

### 8. `footnote.gap` on a page with one note

- typst counts the gap between notes when it decides whether a line fits above a page's only note; Word does not
  (the note is set at the same place in both). `motion-brief`, page 7: with `gap: 7` typst moves the last 2 lines
  of the conclusion to page 8, Word keeps them; with `gap: 0` or `3` the two agree. (In `brief-on-the-merits` the
  opposite: `gap: 3` let Word move a line on, `gap: 7` agrees.) The layouts use the value at which every engine
  agrees; the model should make the gap mean the same in all three.

### 9. U+00B4 (´) in LaTeX

- pdflatex sets the acute accent (`Hawai´i`, typed so in the filed motion) from a Type 3 bitmap font (`F48`), so
  DEF-FONT-002 fails (tier 1). Map it to the face's glyph in the generated preamble.
- Folder: `motion-brief` (pages 2-5). Its `latex/` is not committed.

### 10. A hard line break in a body paragraph in LaTeX

- A markdown hard break (`\` at a line end) in a body paragraph: typst and Word start the next line at the margin;
  the LaTeX engine indents it by the first-line indent (+36 pt) and breaks the long line after it earlier.
- Folder: `motion-brief`, page 3 (the motion's own signature lines, "MOANA M. LUTEY\ Corporation Counsel\ …"):
  diagnostic render with U+00B4 replaced: 202/203 lines, first differing p3 line 4.

### 11. An empty item of a list field

- `"{field}"` with a list gives a paragraph per item; typst sets an empty item ("") as no line at all, where the
  old templates (and a Word or LaTeX empty paragraph) leave a blank line. The flyleaf lists of
  `petition-for-discretionary-review` (`caption_right`: "" between the proceedings) and the signature lines of
  `amicus-brief` lose their gaps in typst; the other engines are blocked on request 1 there, so the difference is
  not measured yet. The model should give an empty item one blank line in all three (or document "" as nothing).

### 12. Frontmatter fields set as markdown blocks (certificates)

- Feature: a block that sets a frontmatter string as markdown blocks (paragraphs, numbered paragraphs, a pipe
  table), with its own title, date and signature: `each: certificates` with `{markdown: "{text}"}`, say.
- Folder: `petition-for-rehearing`: the declaration of counsel (HAW-RECON-002, tier 1) and the certificate of
  service are the frontmatter's `certificates: [{title, text, date, signature}]`, `text` holding paragraphs and a
  table of the persons served. A `text` block sets one paragraph, so the port would lose the declaration's
  paragraphs and the service table. Not ported: its typst template is the old one, no `layout.yaml`.

### 13. A flyleaf of two pages (a numbered page before the tables)

- Feature: a cover page, then a page of counsel and judges numbered "i" before the table of contents (the exemplar's
  flyleaf continues on a second page). The model's cover is unnumbered throughout: a `{break: true}` inside it
  gives two unnumbered pages and DEF-COVER-001 fails (tier 1); the counsel of four counties (about 50 lines) do not
  fit on the flyleaf under its 3-inch top margin.
- Folder: `petition-for-writ` (also needs request 1). Not ported: its typst template is the old one.
