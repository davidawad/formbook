## Shared-model requests

1. **An authority with several `find` strings.** `lib/typst/xdoc.typ` `with-marks` calls `e.find.replace(..)`: a list
   crashes the render. `answering-brief` cites two authorities under forms with no common string ("Comm. for Pub.
   Couns." and "C.P.C.S."; "art. 26" and "Article 26"); one string loses pages from the table of authorities, which
   MRAP 16(a)(4) (MASS-FORM-008) requires for every page cited: a tier-1 loss, so the folder was not ported. (In
   `brief-on-the-merits` every list had a common string; the INPUT now holds it, page lists unchanged.) "passim"
   (the old templates printed it for more than five pages) is also not in the model.
2. **Blocks after the tail.** MRAP 16(a)(12)-(15) (MASS-OPEN-001, MASS-FORM-010): signature, addendum, certificate of
   compliance (on the last page), certificate of service. `answering-brief` carries its addendum in the body (`#
   ADDENDUM`, the tail) and its certificates in the frontmatter; the model sets the tail after every closing block, so
   the certificates came before the addendum (tried: compliance p56, service p57, addendum p58 of 58), against the
   mandated order. Needed: closing blocks after the tail (or a closing split around it). Same as fb-595.54.63.
3. **Authorities as a flat keyed list with link marks.** `reply-brief` and `petition-for-discretionary-review` give
   `authorities: [{key, category, text}]` and mark each citation in the body as `[text](toa:<key>)`; the model reads
   only `[{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` (`authority-entries` reads `g.entries` and panics), and no engine strips
   the `toa:` links. Converting the INPUTs is a schema change, not a transcription fix: decision for David (convert the
   INPUTs, or teach the model the keyed form). Same request as fb-595.54.21 and fb-595.54.24.
4. **A footnote longer than a page.** `petition-for-writ` note 30 runs past a full page at the mandated 14 pt single
   spaced (the filed copy runs it from p10 onto p11). The model sets every note unbreakable: typst clipped it (p14;
   DEF-CONT-001 and DEF-FORM-002 failed on the trial port). A note that cannot fit on any page must split, in typst as
   LaTeX and Word do.
5. **Line breaks in URLs and at quotes** (from the `petition-for-writ` trial port): typst breaks after "?" in a URL,
   LibreOffice after a hyphen before a digit ("covid-|19", p5) and between digits ("%2003|2020", p10); typst keeps a
   footnote's anchor line and moves only the note when the note does not fit (p26), LaTeX and LibreOffice move the line;
   LibreOffice keeps widow control on a block quote that holds a note (p7 line 17).
6. **LibreOffice lets a line overflow the measure by a fraction of a point.** `amicus-brief`, docx, p24 (note 6):
   LibreOffice sets "https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/" on one line ending at x = 540.024 on a 540 pt
   measure; typst breaks after "technology/" (556/558 lines; pages and page ends equal). `cross_engine_docx: report`.
7. **pdfTeX breaks between a closing quote and an em dash.** `brief-on-the-merits`, latex, p11 line 12 ("screening
   testing” / —is"); typst and LibreOffice keep `”—` together (Unicode LB). 1231/1243 lines, the end of p12 differs.
   `cross_engine_latex: report`.
8. **The Word page lookup misses a mark in a paragraph that crosses a page.** `brief-on-the-merits`, docx, p7 (table
   of authorities): Turner v. Safley lists "49, 50" where typst and LaTeX list "49, 50, 51"; the mark "Turner, 482 U.S.
   at 89" is on p51 line 3 in a paragraph that starts on p50 (tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs). The docx table of authorities
   is wrong for that entry: `cross_engine_docx: report` (1242/1243 lines).
9. **LaTeX splits a multi-line heading.** `answering-brief` trial port, latex, p48: a five-line level-2 heading set three
   lines at the foot of p48; typst and Word move it whole (fb-595.54.23 has the same).
10. **Two smaller gaps.** `petition-for-review`: `when:` is true for a field set to `false` (`signed: false`), so every
    signer gets a signature rule; `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` writes the `cover` only when there is a table, so the folder sets
    its caption page as `front` blocks. `petition-for-rehearing`: a list item that holds more than one paragraph fails in
    docx (`lib/docx/xdoc.lua:371`); the INPUT's lettered sub-items are body paragraphs now. `motion-brief`: typst splits a
    two-line list item over a page (1 + 1), LaTeX and Word keep it whole (avoided here by the layout's spacing).
