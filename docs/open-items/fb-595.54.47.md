## Shared-model requests (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82)

1. **A footnote whose reference ends a page.** Folder: `courts/ninth-circuit/idaho/state/supreme-court/motion-brief`.
   Evidence: p8 ends with a block quotation whose last line carries note 5 ("... of districts with low organizational
   potential.5", typst y 605-618 pt); typst sets that line on p8 and the whole of note 5 at the foot of p9 (with
   note 6), while LaTeX and Word move the line to p9 with its note (latex XENG-003 first differing p8 line 27, docx
   p8 line 26; docx then runs 19 pages to typst's 18). A sibling of fb-595.54.51 request 3 (a note typst splits):
   the model should keep a note's first line on its reference's page in every engine (typst: no deferred entries),
   or let LaTeX and Word defer the note as typst does.

2. **A paragraph that anchors a note, starting at a page foot.** Folder:
   `courts/ninth-circuit/idaho/state/supreme-court/petition-for-rehearing`. Evidence: the paragraph "Ada County argued
   in its initial brief ..." has notes 7 and 8 on its second line; LaTeX and Word (widow control off for a paragraph
   that anchors a note, lib/typst/xengine.typ) set its first line alone at the foot of p5, typst (orphan control on)
   starts it on p6 (latex XENG-003 first differing p6 line 1, 207/213 lines; docx 212/213). The engines should agree
   on orphans in such a paragraph (typst without orphan control there, or LaTeX and Word with it).

3. **A multi-line heading after a heading at a page foot (LaTeX).** Folder:
   `courts/ninth-circuit/idaho/state/supreme-court/petition-for-writ`. Evidence: p15 ends with "CLAIMS FOR RELIEF"
   (level 1) followed by a three-line level-2 heading; typst and Word carry both to p16, LaTeX sets the level-1
   heading and two lines of the level-2 heading on p15 and its third line on p16 (XENG-003 first differing p15 line
   17). LaTeX should keep the lines of a heading together and with the next block, as typst does. The folder gates
   docx (`cross_engine_latex: report`).

4. **An empty paragraph.** Folder: `courts/ninth-circuit/idaho/state/supreme-court/petition-for-discretionary-review`.
   A `""` item of a `{text: "{list}"}` block gives a zero-height paragraph in typst and LaTeX and a line of the pitch
   in Word (the empty row the sample put before the title in `caption_right`; docx XENG-003 p1 line 15). The INPUT no
   longer carries the empty item; the model should treat an empty paragraph alike in every engine.

5. **An authority mark after the last word of a page.** Folder: `courts/ninth-circuit/idaho/state/supreme-court/reply-brief`
   (worked around). `find: "Appellate Rule 40"` matched "Rule 40." at the end of the paragraph that ends p10; typst
   listed p11, LaTeX and Word p10 (the mark after the word lands past the page break). The INPUT now finds "Idaho
   Appellate Rule" (mid-line).

6. **A page list wider than the page column of a table of authorities.** Folders:
   `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/court-of-appeals/answering-brief`, `.../petition-for-review`. Evidence: the
   model sets an entry's page list on one line in a column as wide as the list (lib/typst/xdoc.typ
   `authorities-pages`, `calc.max(col, measure(list).width)`); on Washington's 5-inch measure (RAP 18.17(a)(1)) a list
   of 15 or more pages leaves the cite a column one word wide: answering brief, *State v. Petrich* (find "Petrich",
   17 pages, typst p10), petition for review, *O'Leary* (19 pages) and RCW 82.04.4281 (30 pages, the list then runs
   109 pt into the right margin); DEF-TOC-001 and DEF-FORM-002 fail on typst. Wanted: page ranges ("50-55", as the
   filed answering brief prints) and a page list that wraps under the leader in a column of fixed width (the old
   templates' 2.3 in column), in all three engines, or "passim" past a count. Both folders keep their old typst
   templates (a layout.yaml was written and rendered; every other tier-1 check passed on typst once the cover's
   counsel went into one column).

7. **More than one `find` per authority.** Folder: `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/court-of-appeals/opening-brief`.
   Evidence: the INPUT gives each authority `finds: [..]` (67 entries), four of them with two strings
   (`["art. I, § 3", "art. I, §§ 3"]`, `["art. I, § 9", "I, section 9"]`, `["art. I, § 21", "3, 21"]`,
   `["art. I, § 22", "I, section 22"]`); the model reads one `find`. Rewriting the single-string entries as `find` is
   mechanical, the four would lose pages. Wanted: `finds` (a list) in lib/typst/xdoc.typ and lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua. The
   brief's appendix fits the model's `tail` (`at: "APPENDIX"`).

8. **Blocks after the tail.** Folder: `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/court-of-appeals/petition-for-writ`
   (personal restraint petition). Evidence: the old template sets the body to the conclusion, then the attorney's date
   and signature, then the "REQUEST FOR RELIEF" section of the body, then the petitioner's oath from the frontmatter
   (`oath: {date, signer}`, RAP 16.7(a)(6)). `tail` puts the request after the closing, but nothing can follow the
   tail. Wanted: a `tail_closing` (blocks after the tail), or a closing block that sets a named body section.

9. **A line Word fits past the measure.** Folder: `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/court-of-appeals/motion-brief`.
   Evidence: p24 line 6, "13.40.020(19); RCW 13.40.160(2). In B.O.J., the defendant was": typst and LaTeX break
   before "was" (the line would end at 504.06 pt, 0.06 pt past the 504 pt measure), LibreOffice keeps it (docx
   XENG-003 first differing p24 line 6). The model's widths need a tolerance LibreOffice honours, or a shared slack.

10. **A note holding a long URL at a page foot.** Same folder, p33: note 4 is a bare URL
    ("https://usatoday30.usatoday.com/news/nation/2008-03-02-..."); LaTeX and Word keep the next body line on p33,
    typst moves it to p34 (latex XENG-003 first differing p33 line 15). Related to fb-595.54.62 request 2 (break points
    inside a long word): the engines break the URL differently, so the note's height differs.

11. **Doubled section signs and pilcrows.** Folder:
    `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/court-of-appeals/petition-for-discretionary-review`. Evidence: p17 line 13,
    "id. ¶¶ 180-82": lib/typst/xengine.typ binds `[§¶] \S+`, i.e. the second pilcrow to the number, so typst breaks
    between the two pilcrows; LaTeX and Word bind "¶¶" to the number and move it whole (XENG-003, latex 669/681, docx
    670/681 lines). The typst rule should be `[§¶]+ \S+`.

12. **No break after a slash inside a word (LaTeX).** Folder:
    `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/court-of-appeals/reply-brief`. Evidence: p18 line 5 ("affixed/attached"):
    typst and Word break after the slash, LaTeX does not (XENG-003 326/332 lines). Also seen there: an underlined
    signature line ("/s/ *{signer}*") never wraps in LaTeX (it ran 10 pt into the right margin at a 2-inch signature
    indent; the Washington layouts set the signature 1.5 in in so it fits). The folder gates docx
    (`cross_engine_latex: report`).

## Worked around in the INPUTs and layouts

- pandoc reads a paragraph that starts with `(1)`, `(a)` or `A.` as a list (fb-595.54.62 request 4); the INPUTs of
  these folders escape the marker (`\(1)`, `A\.`), which cmarker and pandoc both print as written.
- A `"` that typst and pandoc pair differently (after a space, or in a quotation that runs over paragraphs) is
  written as the typographic quote in the INPUT (brief-on-the-merits p11, petition-for-review p10).
- lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits no `cover` in a layout without `contents` or `authorities` (fb-595.54.51 request 2): the
  Idaho petitions for review, rehearing and writ set their cover as `front` blocks closed by `{break: true}` (the
  cover counted and numbered as page 1, as the old templates did).
