# fb-595.54.65: California courts of appeal and Supreme Court on the shared document model

Bead fb-595.54.65 ports twelve folders onto the shared document model (docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Generated engines",
fb-595.54.12). This batch could not edit `lib/` or `tools/` (65 batches ran in parallel), so a feature the model
lacks is recorded here for the serial lane fb-595.54.82, and the folder is left as it was: no `layout.yaml`, its
typst template unchanged, `params.cross_engine` unchanged.

Result: **no folder ported**. Each of the ten doc folders needs at least one feature the model lacks (below). The two
court roots have no template to port.

## Shared-model requests

Each request lists the folders that need it and where the current template does it. Line numbers are in
`typst/<name>.typ` of each folder as of fec1eac23. `coa/` is `courts/ninth-circuit/california/state/courts-of-appeal/`,
`sc/` is `courts/ninth-circuit/california/state/supreme-court/`.

### R1. A word count the template computes (tier 1)

Rule 8.204(c)(1), 8.504(d)(1), 8.520(c)(1) and 8.268(b)(3) require a certificate of the brief's word count
(`coa/rules.md` CALCTAPP-CERT-001, `sc/rules.md` CAL-LEN-007, both marked M). Every California brief template counts
the words of its own markdown (`count-words`: body, headings and footnotes; list markers, footnote marks, link
targets and markup not counted), prints the figure in the certificate, and fails the render over the rule's limit
(14,000, 8,400 or 7,000 words, per template). The model prints only a count that the frontmatter gives (`{word_count}`). None of
these INPUTs has one: adding it would mean a new field and a figure typed by hand, which these templates were written
to avoid.
Needed: a `{word_count}` the model computes from the body (the same normal form in typst and `lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua`),
an optional `word_limit` in `layout.yaml` that fails the render, and digit grouping ("11,942").

### R2. Headings numbered by the template

The INPUT headings carry no labels ("# Introduction", "## Teresa Brown observes a religious Sabbath."); the template
numbers them: levels 2 to 5 as I. / A. / 1. / a. (sc/petition-for-rehearing and sc/petition-for-review: levels 1 to 5
as I. / A. / 1. / a. / (1)), the label in a half-inch box and the title's later lines hung under the title, the same
label in the contents row. Unnumbered headings (the statements, the conclusion) stay unnumbered. This is already in
the "Not yet in the model" list of docs/EVAL-LOOP.md.
Needed: `heading: [{number: "I.", label_box: 36, ..}]` per level, applied in the body and the contents in all three
engines.
Folders: all nine of R1 (`heading-label` / `heading-number`: coa/answering-brief 134, coa/opening-brief 190,
coa/petition-for-discretionary-review 118, coa/reply-brief 190, sc/answering-brief 118,
sc/opening-brief-on-the-merits 118, sc/petition-for-rehearing 119-131, sc/petition-for-review 166-178,
sc/reply-brief 118).

### R3. Authorities marked by links in the body, grouped by kind, with *passim*

The model reads `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` and marks every `find` string. The California
INPUTs (the README's "Markdown contract") mark each citation with a link, `[text](toa:<key>)`, and give
`authorities: {<key>: {kind, name, cite, sort?}}`; the template groups the entries by `kind` in a fixed order (Cases,
Constitutional Provisions, Statutes, Regulations, Rules, Other Authorities), sorts them by name, sets a case's name in
italics before its citation, puts "Page(s)" on the first group's line, and prints *passim* for an authority cited on
seven pages or more. A link with no entry fails the render. Converting the INPUTs to `find` strings would change the
markdown contract of every California folder, including the district folders outside this bead that share it.
Needed: `authorities: {source: links, groups: [{kind, title}], passim: 7, case_name: italic}` (or an equivalent), in
all three engines (LaTeX `\xd@amark` and Word's needles at the link instead of at a `find` match).
Folders: every folder of R1 whose INPUT links authorities: coa/answering-brief (14 links), coa/opening-brief (14),
coa/petition-for-discretionary-review (32), coa/reply-brief (9), sc/opening-brief-on-the-merits (18),
sc/petition-for-review (13). sc/answering-brief, sc/petition-for-rehearing and sc/reply-brief build the same table
(`toa-section`) but their INPUTs have no links yet.

### R4. TeX Gyre Schola in LaTeX (already listed in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md)

`fb engines gen` refuses the face (tools/fb/src/enginegen/gen.rs, `latex_face`). Century Schoolbook is the face rule
8.74(b)(1) prefers, and it is the face of these folders' exemplars:
coa/motion-brief (FILED-EXAMPLE-motion-brief: CenturySchoolbook), coa/answering-brief (CenturySchoolbook),
sc/opening-brief-on-the-merits (CenturySchoolbook, both exemplars). sc/answering-brief and sc/reply-brief also set
Schola (their exemplars are Garamond and Times New Roman; the face is open under the rule, so these two could move to
Times New Roman, but that is a template decision, not a port).

### R5. A cover whose spacing fills or fits the page

coa/motion-brief: the title and the counsel block sit at `v(1fr)` (lines 70, 73), i.e. the cover fills its page
(listed in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md). coa/answering-brief (359), coa/petition-for-discretionary-review (307),
sc/answering-brief (319), sc/opening-brief-on-the-merits (319), sc/petition-for-rehearing (341), sc/reply-brief (319):
the cover tries the gaps 1.0, 0.8, .. 0 em and keeps the largest at which it fits one page (`fits(g)`). Fixed gaps in
`layout.yaml` would do for one sample but a longer caption or counsel list would push the cover onto a second page.
Needed: a cover `fill: true` (space between blocks shares out what the page leaves) in all three engines.

### R6. A caption column ruled at its right and foot

coa/opening-brief and coa/reply-brief (line 314): the parties sit in a 3.4 in column ruled on its right and at its
foot, and the case number beside it. The model has `{rule}` blocks (full-width) and `columns` without borders.
Needed: `columns: [{width, border: {right, bottom}, pad}]`.

### R7. The last paragraph kept with the closing (already listed in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md)

coa/answering-brief 557, coa/opening-brief 462, coa/petition-for-discretionary-review 437, coa/reply-brief 463,
sc/answering-brief 445, sc/opening-brief-on-the-merits 445, sc/reply-brief 445: the signature block never stands
alone on a page; the body's last paragraph (when it holds no footnote reference) is kept with it.

### R8. A right-hand column as wide as its widest line

sc/petition-for-rehearing (275, `right-column`): the cover's counsel block and the signature block are set against
the right margin, as wide as their widest line (at least half the text width). The model's columns have fixed or
`1fr` widths.
