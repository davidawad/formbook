## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **Authorities keyed by kind.** Feature: a table of authorities from a frontmatter map
   `authorities: {<key>: {kind, name, cite[, sort]}}`, grouped by `kind` under fixed court headings (Cases,
   Constitutional Provisions, Statutes, Regulations, Rules, Other Authorities), alphabetical within a group, a case
   name in italics. The model reads only `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`, and
   `authority-entries` (lib/typst/xdoc.typ) and `xd.authority_entries` (lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua) iterate the field
   whenever it is present (typst reads each map entry as a key-value pair and fails on `.entries`), so this
   frontmatter fails the typst render even when layout.yaml has no `authorities` key.
   Folders: amicus-brief, answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits, opening-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review,
   petition-for-rehearing, reply-brief (every INPUT has the map; README "Markdown contract"). Rewriting the INPUTs
   into the model's shape would change the court's markdown contract (a decision, not a port).
2. **Word count computed by the template.** Feature: the certificate of compliance (22 NYCRR 500.13(c)(1),
   NY-LEN-001) prints the body's word count, which each template computes (`count-words`, with the questions
   presented excluded in opening-brief and brief-on-the-merits). The model takes only a count written in the
   frontmatter, and these INPUTs have none. Folders: amicus-brief, answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits, opening-brief,
   petition-for-discretionary-review, petition-for-rehearing, reply-brief.
3. **A numbered page between the cover and the tables.** Feature: the disclosure statement and the statement of
   related litigation go *before* the table of contents (500.13(a), NY-CONT-001, -002) and are numbered with the
   tables (ii, ..). The model has a cover (unnumbered) and then the tables. Folders: amicus-brief (`disclosure`),
   brief-on-the-merits and opening-brief (`disclosure`, `related_litigation`).
4. **A cover that fills or fits the page.** Feature: counsel at the foot of the cover (`v(1fr)` in the typst-only
   templates; answering-brief and opening-brief choose the largest gap at which the cover fits one page). The model
   has fixed space only. Folders: all seven above. (motion-brief sets a fixed inch instead; its filed exemplar has no
   cover, so nothing there is lost.)
5. **Template-generated certificates and affidavit.** Feature: a certificate of service from `service.method` and
   `service.unregistered`, and the affidavit of service typed with blanks and a venue column of ")" (opening-brief,
   brief-on-the-merits; the "column of ')'" gap already listed in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md). The model sets certificates only
   from the body (`tail`). Folders: amicus-brief, answering-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review,
   petition-for-rehearing, reply-brief (certificate of service when `service` is given); opening-brief and
   brief-on-the-merits (affidavit).
6. **Contents heading labels and the addendum.** opening-brief and brief-on-the-merits set heading labels in a
   0.5 in box at each level's indent and list an addendum in the contents; the docx of opening-brief stops at an
   addendum (README BLOCKED). Folders: opening-brief, brief-on-the-merits.

## Model defects found (worked around in motion-brief's layout.yaml)

- **`cover` without tables in docx.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or
  authorities present): a folder with a cover and no tables loses its cover in docx (motion-brief: docx 6 pages,
  typst 7; XENG-001 failed). Workaround: the cover as `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}`.
- **`page_number.front` without tables in latex.** With `front: lower-roman` and no contents or authorities, the
  latex body keeps roman numbers (motion-brief: pages "i".."vi" where typst prints "1".."6"; XENG-003 failed on every
  page-number line). Workaround: `page_number: {from: 2}` (pages numbered consecutively, the cover page 1 and blank,
  as 22 NYCRR 500.1(l) allows).

### Input changes made for the model (not new fields)

- Every keyed `authorities` entry of a case has its name in markdown italics (`name: "*People v Dietze*"`) and its
  group is not `italic`: the model's italic group sets "*name* cite" without the comma New York's official style
  (and the old templates) put after the name. An entry whose `cite` was "" has its text moved to `cite` and an empty
  `name` (the model would print "name, ").
- `[text](toa:key)` links added to the bodies of reply, amicus, answering, petition-for-discretionary-review and
  petition-for-rehearing (brief-on-the-merits and opening-brief had them): each full case name, each short form
  before a reporter cite ("*Gertz*, 418 US"), italic short names in amicus. An entry the transcribed pages never
  cite drops out of the table (answering: *Love*, *Rivera*). No link in a heading.
- reply: the link to Penal Law § 240.25(2) covers "Penal Law" only (see 6). amicus: *US v Playboy* carries
  `sort: "*U.S. v Playboy ...*"` (see 7).

## Shared-model requests, still open (for a second serial lane)

1. **A break after a slash inside a word in LaTeX.** typst and LibreOffice may end a line after "/" in "A.B. 7228/S.B.
   2180"; pdfLaTeX does not (the model breaks at a slash only inside a URL). Evidence:
   petition-for-discretionary-review, INPUT page 16 line 20 (XENG-003, latex only). Folder: that one.
2. **LibreOffice's note placement** (as fb-595.54.13's Pennsylvania amicus): a long note that does not fit under its
   anchor's line goes to the next page while LibreOffice keeps the line; typst and LaTeX move the line. Evidence:
   petition-for-rehearing body page 6 ("unlawful imprisonment.4"). Folder: that one (docx).
3. **A footnote lost in LaTeX inside a sign box.** lib/pandoc's binding of "¶ 7])." puts the `\footnote` that follows
   inside the `\mbox` (`¶~\mbox{7{]}).\footnote{...}}`), and LaTeX drops a note set in a box. Evidence:
   brief-on-the-merits note f1 (body page 4), every later page shifts. The mark should close the box before the note.
4. **tools/check/gate0.awk cuts a run of dots across a block boundary** (DEF-CONT-001, every engine, failing on master
   before this port too): a paragraph that ends in dots (". . . .", "government.") and a quotation paragraph that
   opens with "…." read as one dot leader of four, so the second block is never found (brief-on-the-merits blocks
   51 and 70). A tools fix; the INPUT is the filed text and was not bent to the checker.
5. **Word's line fit** (the open request): brief-on-the-merits docx, a URL line of note f1 that typst fills to the
   measure and a line on body page 16.
6. **A keyed link split across a page** (docx page lookup): a link whose text runs from one page onto the next marks
   typst's page (its first word) and LaTeX's, Word's needle the page of the words after it (reply: "[Penal Law §
   240.25(2)](toa:..)" over body pages 15-16). And a link that starts with "§" loses the sign binding in LaTeX and
   Word ("[§ 240.25(2)]" broke after "§"). Worked around in the INPUT.
7. **typst's authority sort key keeps its punctuation.** lib/typst/xdoc.typ `sort-key` writes `.replace(regex(..))`
   on the line after `lower(..)` in a `#let`, where it is markup, not a method call: typst sorts "*u.s. v reeves*"
   before "*us v playboy*", lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua (stripped) after. Evidence: amicus-brief Table of Authorities
   (XENG-003 before the `sort` work-around). Any folder with "U.S." and "US" names, or names that differ only in
   punctuation, can sort differently in typst.
8. **An optional page before the tables.** `before_tables` ends in a hard `pagebreak()`, so a `when` block that is
   not set leaves a blank page in typst (LaTeX and Word set none). The disclosure statement the old templates printed
   "only when given" (500.1(f), NY-CONT-001) is therefore not set in reply-brief, answering-brief,
   petition-for-discretionary-review and petition-for-rehearing (none of their INPUTs gives one; amicus-brief, whose
   INPUT does, sets it). Set at the end of the cover instead, the cover runs to two pages (DEF-COVER-001).
9. **An addendum** (500.1(h): its own contents, "Add-" page numbers), which the old opening-brief and
   brief-on-the-merits templates set after `# Addendum` (request 6 above, its contents labels now in the model). No
   INPUT has one; an INPUT that does now gets an ordinary section.
10. **Footnotes at 14 point** (the open "Word's footnote area" request): brief-on-the-merits set its notes at the body
    size as the filed brief does; at 14 point the latex and docx renders placed a note differently (body page 4).
    The layout sets 12 point, the rule's floor.
11. **A table of authorities row "name," then the citation on the next line** (the old merits and opening templates);
    the model sets "name, citation" on one row. Tier 3; not chased.
