## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **Authorities keyed by kind.** Feature: a table of authorities from a frontmatter map
   `authorities: {<key>: {kind, name, cite[, sort]}}`, grouped by `kind` under fixed court headings (Cases,
   Constitutional Provisions, Statutes, Regulations, Rules, Other Authorities), alphabetical within a group, a case
   name in italics. The model reads only `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`, and
   `authority-entries` (lib/typst/xdoc.typ) and `xd.authority_entries` (lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua) iterate the field
   whenever it is present (typst reads each map entry as a key-value pair and fails on `.entries`), so this
   frontmatter fails the typst render even when layout.yaml has no `authorities` key.
   Folders: amicus-brief, answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits, opening-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review,
   petition-for-rehearing, reply-brief (every INPUT has the map; README "Markdown contract"). Rewriting the INPUTs
   into the model's shape would change the court's markdown contract (a decision, not a port).
2. **Word count computed by the template.** Feature: the certificate of compliance (22 NYCRR 500.13(c)(1),
   NY-LEN-001) prints the body's word count, which each template computes (`count-words`, with the questions
   presented excluded in opening-brief and brief-on-the-merits). The model takes only a count written in the
   frontmatter, and these INPUTs have none. Folders: amicus-brief, answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits, opening-brief,
   petition-for-discretionary-review, petition-for-rehearing, reply-brief.
3. **A numbered page between the cover and the tables.** Feature: the disclosure statement and the statement of
   related litigation go *before* the table of contents (500.13(a), NY-CONT-001, -002) and are numbered with the
   tables (ii, ..). The model has a cover (unnumbered) and then the tables. Folders: amicus-brief (`disclosure`),
   brief-on-the-merits and opening-brief (`disclosure`, `related_litigation`).
4. **A cover that fills or fits the page.** Feature: counsel at the foot of the cover (`v(1fr)` in the typst-only
   templates; answering-brief and opening-brief choose the largest gap at which the cover fits one page). The model
   has fixed space only. Folders: all seven above. (motion-brief sets a fixed inch instead; its filed exemplar has no
   cover, so nothing there is lost.)
5. **Template-generated certificates and affidavit.** Feature: a certificate of service from `service.method` and
   `service.unregistered`, and the affidavit of service typed with blanks and a venue column of ")" (opening-brief,
   brief-on-the-merits; the "column of ')'" gap already listed in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md). The model sets certificates only
   from the body (`tail`). Folders: amicus-brief, answering-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review,
   petition-for-rehearing, reply-brief (certificate of service when `service` is given); opening-brief and
   brief-on-the-merits (affidavit).
6. **Contents heading labels and the addendum.** opening-brief and brief-on-the-merits set heading labels in a
   0.5 in box at each level's indent and list an addendum in the contents; the docx of opening-brief stops at an
   addendum (README BLOCKED). Folders: opening-brief, brief-on-the-merits.

## Model defects found (worked around in motion-brief's layout.yaml)

- **`cover` without tables in docx.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or
  authorities present): a folder with a cover and no tables loses its cover in docx (motion-brief: docx 6 pages,
  typst 7; XENG-001 failed). Workaround: the cover as `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}`.
- **`page_number.front` without tables in latex.** With `front: lower-roman` and no contents or authorities, the
  latex body keeps roman numbers (motion-brief: pages "i".."vi" where typst prints "1".."6"; XENG-003 failed on every
  page-number line). Workaround: `page_number: {from: 2}` (pages numbered consecutively, the cover page 1 and blank,
  as 22 NYCRR 500.1(l) allows).
