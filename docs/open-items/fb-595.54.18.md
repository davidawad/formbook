# fb-595.54.18: Nevada Supreme Court (nev) on the shared document model, open items

Folders: `courts/ninth-circuit/nevada/state/supreme-court` and its nine doc folders. For the serial lane fb-595.54.82.

The court root has no template: its `typst/` and `latex/` hold only `.gitkeep`, and its `usage/` only FILED-EXAMPLEs. There is nothing to port there. It now has `rules.md`, which every doc folder's `layout.yaml` cites. The NRAP text was read from the Internet Archive's capture of the Legislature's copy, because leg.state.nv.us and nvcourts.gov answer HTTP 403 to a script.

## Shared-model requests

1. **A numbered front page before the tables** (NRAP 26.1(b): the disclosure statement comes before the table of contents; NRAP 32(a)(4), NEV-FORM-006: those pages are numbered in lowercase Roman).
   - Folders: amicus-brief, brief-on-the-merits, petition-for-writ.
   - Today the disclosure is set as a second cover page after `{break: true}` (`params.cover_pages: 2`), so it prints no number, where the rule wants i. This is a recorded tier-1 deviation in every engine.
   - LaTeX also numbers that second cover page: `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` (about line 284) sets `\thispagestyle{empty}` on the first cover page only. That causes DEF-COVER-001 FAIL on latex (p2 prints "2") in the same three folders.
2. **Arabic numbering from a named body heading** (NRAP 32(a)(4), (a)(7)(C): Arabic from the statement of the case).
   - Folder: brief-on-the-merits. The frontmatter field `arabic_from` names the heading.
   - The model starts Arabic numbering at the first body heading, so the jurisdictional and routing statement and the issues are pages 1-2, where the old template made them v-vi. This is a tier-1 deviation in all engines.
3. **Break points in URLs and after a slash inside a word, in LaTeX.**
   - Typst and Word break after "/" ("https://www.eac.gov/" | "sites/...", "Coroner/" | "Medical"); LaTeX does not. A long URL then overflows the LaTeX margin (DEF-FORM-002) or moves lines and pages.
   - Typst also breaks at a "." inside a URL ("VVSG." | "1.1...", "MediaPlayer." | "php"), where Word does not.
   - Evidence:
     - answering-brief p11-p12, notes 25-26;
     - brief-on-the-merits p12, p13, p22 (x = 612.9), p25;
     - petition-for-review p19 (x = 589.9);
     - petition-for-writ p11 note 6, p21 note 47 (x = 614.9);
     - motion-brief p7 note 11 and "Corporation/Democratic" on p13, p15, p16;
     - petition-for-rehearing p14.
   - ca8 has per-court URL rules (fb-595.54.4) that could move into lib.
   - Worked around in the landed INPUTs with a zero-width space after an inner slash (Status, above); with that, petition-for-review gates and the DEF-FORM-002 overflows are gone. Typst's break at "." inside a URL still differs from Word in the restored brief-on-the-merits.
4. **Orphan control around a paragraph that anchors a footnote.** `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` and `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` drop the club/widow penalty for such a paragraph. Typst keeps it: when the paragraph's second line carries a note that does not fit, typst carries the first line over too.
   - answering-brief p16-p17: "PLAN contends ... hand counting of".
   - reply-brief p6-p7: the "II. ARGUMENT" heading, II.A and one line end body page 2 in LaTeX and Word.
   - petition-for-discretionary-review body p3 (latex) and p5-p6 (typst). Here typst moves two lines of "After further proceedings ..." to p6 and leaves note 17 on p5, away from its call: a typst defect in the reference render.
   - petition-for-writ p16-p18 and p20-p21.
5. **Docx table of authorities misses a page cited only in a footnote** (`tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs`). Typst and LaTeX list the page.
   - amicus-brief: *Brady v. Maryland* and *Thomas* in note 1 on p7 ("3, 6" for "3, 6, 7").
   - petition-for-writ: NRS 34.170 in note 39 (p18) and NRS 293.3606 in note 44 (p20).
   - Likely cause, not confirmed: the needles follow the notes at their reference, while the PDF text has them at the page foot.
6. **Word splits a long footnote across pages** where typst and LaTeX keep it whole: reply-brief p8-p9, note 3.
7. **A spaced ellipsis "[. . .]"**: typst never breaks before a period that follows a space; LaTeX and Word do.
   - petition-for-rehearing p4 line 1 ends "confidentiality [.".
   - A no-break space in the INPUT does not help: a period followed by a no-break space is turned back into a breakable space.
8. **The authority mark of the body's last word lands on the next page in typst** when the closing group breaks to a new page.
   - petition-for-writ: the TOA says "NRS 293.277 ... 25", where the body ends on p24. LaTeX and Word say 24, which is correct.
9. **Keep the last paragraph with the closing.** The date and signature stand alone at the top of a page:
   - motion-brief p11;
   - petition-for-writ p25;
   - reply-brief p20 (docx);
   - brief-on-the-merits p26 (latex).
10. **Closing blocks in the table of contents.** The certificates (compliance, NRAP 27(e), service, verification) are closing blocks, so they get no contents row. The old templates and the filed exemplars list them. Folders: all except motion-brief.
11. **`v(1fr)` on a cover.** The old covers pushed the title and counsel down with flexible space; the model has fixed space only.
    - Folders: answering-brief, reply-brief, brief-on-the-merits, petition-for-rehearing, petition-for-review, petition-for-writ.
    - Tier 2 only: the lower cover is emptier.
12. **Docx drops a cover with no tables after it.** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` writes `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or authorities present). Folders without tables set the caption page as `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}` instead.
    - Folders: motion-brief, petition-for-review.
13. **Word fits one body line less than typst above four single-spaced 14 pt notes**: petition-for-writ body p7 (physical p13).
14. **An empty item in a `{field}` list.** Word prints it as an empty paragraph; typst and LaTeX drop it. The empty item was removed from the petition-for-discretionary-review INPUT.
15. **A ")" column beside a caption** is not in the model. petition-for-discretionary-review uses two columns with a gutter instead (the filed exemplar uses a rule).

## Checker defects

- **C1, DEF-CONT-001 false positive** (`tools/check/gate0.awk`, `fn_find`).
  - What happens: a footnote is looked for from the start of the PDF text, so its normal form can match inside the table of authorities. The previous row's page number has to end in the note's number, followed by a cite equal to the note's text.
  - answering-brief: note 7 "NRS 293B.033." matches "…NRS 293.3677(2) ….17" + "NRS 293B.033" + the leader on p iii. The old render passed only because that row's page was 16.
  - The body paragraph then fails as "missing" on every engine. A fix: search for the note from the body's first page, or require it at a page foot.
- **C2, DEF-CONT-001 false negative.** petition-for-discretionary-review typst: note 17 is on body p5 and its call on p6, and the "footnote on its reference's page" check did not report it.
