## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **Bare URLs break differently.** typst breaks a URL after a period or slash ("http://www.ose." / "state.nm.us/...",
   amicus p9 footnote 2; "https://www.nmlegis." / "gov/Legislation/...", motion p9 footnote 1); LaTeX and Word do not
   break it at all, so the note has a line more or less and every later page shifts. In LaTeX the motion's URL does not
   fit the measure and runs 72 pt into the right margin (DEF-FORM-002 fails), so the motion ships without its latex
   engine (`fb engines gen` writes it again; `fb engines gen --check` reports its two files STALE until then). Needs one URL rule in all three engines
   (break opportunities after `/` and `.` inside a URL, as typst has, in lib/pandoc/xengine.lua and lib/docx/xdoc.lua,
   or none anywhere). Folders: nmctapp amicus-brief, motion-brief (and answering-brief once ported: it
   cites one).
2. **A doubled pilcrow or section sign and the number after it.** lib/typst/xengine.typ boxes `[§¶] \S+`, which in
   "¶¶ 11–12" starts at the second pilcrow: typst may break between the two signs and never inside "11–12". LaTeX and
   Word bind "¶¶ " to the number with a no-break space and still break after the en dash. reply-brief p5 line 12:
   typst "… 12 RP 2747–48 ¶" / "¶ 11–12 (considers …", LaTeX and Word "… 2747–48 ¶¶ 11–" / "12 (considers …"; every
   later page shifts. Needs `[§¶]+ \S+` in typst and no break inside the token after the sign in the pandoc engines (or
   the reverse). Folder: nmctapp reply-brief; any "§§"/"¶¶" with a ranged number can hit it.
3. **Footnotes split across pages.** typst and Word carry a footnote that does not fit to the next page; the LaTeX model
   never splits one (lib/latex/xengine.sty `\interfootnotelinepenalty=10000`) and moves the anchor line instead.
   petition-for-writ p4: typst and docx keep "court issued separate letter rulings denying each.²" on p4 with three lines
   of note 2 and continue it on p5; LaTeX moves that line to p5. Folder: nmctapp petition-for-writ (latex only).
4. **A cover with no tables in docx.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or
   authorities present); a folder with a cover and no tables loses its cover in the docx without an error (petition:
   12 pages instead of 14). The petition sets its cover as `front` blocks and a `{break: true}` instead (page numbers
   from 2), which every engine sets alike.
5. **Blocks between the table of contents and the table of authorities.** NMRA 12-318(A)(1)(c) (NMCTAPP-CERT-001, M):
   the table of contents includes the statement of compliance; the opening and answering exemplars and samples put it
   on a page after the contents (`compliance.position: after-toc`). The model has no slot between `contents` and
   `authorities` (and only `when: <field>`, no test of a value, so `position` cannot choose). Folders: nmctapp
   opening-brief, answering-brief: left on their own typst template.
6. **TeX Gyre Schola in LaTeX.** Tenn. Sup. Ct. R. 46 § 3.02.3(a) (TENN-FORM-004) requires a Century-family face; the
   templates set TeX Gyre Schola, which `fb engines gen` refuses (lib/latex/xeschola.lua is not run by
   lib/latex/xdoc.lua). Folders: all six Tennessee doc folders.
7. **Headings numbered by the template.** The Tennessee templates label level 2..5 headings "I.", "A.", "1.", "a."
   (the INPUT headings carry no numbers) and repeat the labels in the contents. Folders: all six Tennessee doc folders.
8. **A word count the template computes.** The Tennessee certificate of compliance (Rule 46 § 3.02.7(c), Tenn. R. App.
   P. 30(e); TENN-FORM-011) states the brief's word count, which the template counts from the body and checks against
   `word_limit`; the frontmatter has no count. Folders: Tennessee amicus-brief, answering-brief,
   petition-for-discretionary-review, petition-for-review, reply-brief (motion-brief only with a `word_limit`).
   Also: the Tennessee cover shrinks its gaps until it fits one page (no fixed layout), and the brief's last paragraph
   is kept with the signature block (both listed under "Not yet in the model").

### Shared-model requests, second serial lane

- **B1. A footnote inside the token after "§" or "¶" (LaTeX loses it, Word joins it).** `xd.hold_sign_tokens`
  (lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua:615) collects every inline up to the next space after the sign, a `Note` included:
  "§ 28.[^4]" holds note 4, "¶ 47][^8]" note 8. lib/latex/xdoc.lua wraps the pieces in `\mbox{..}`, and LaTeX drops a
  `\footnote` set inside a box: the note's text is missing from the PDF (nmctapp amicus p9 note 4, reply p16 note 8,
  petition-for-writ note 5, motion p9 note 1; DEF-CONT-001 does not see it). lib/docx/xdoc.lua puts a word joiner
  after every slash and hyphen of the pieces, the note's text included, so the motion's note URL no longer breaks
  after "http://" (p9). Fix: end the held token at a `Note` (and leave the note outside the box). Until then the four
  folders keep their committed latex renders (pass 1, which have the notes) and report latex.
- **B2. A break after an em dash before an opening quote (LaTeX).** typst and LibreOffice do not break between "—"
  and a following "‘" or "“" (UAX #14), and break before the dash instead; the LaTeX model allows a break after the
  dash whatever follows. tenn answering p12 ("pain—‘superadd[ing]’"), reply p8 ("Plaintiffs—“that"),
  petition-for-discretionary-review p9 ("(b)(2)—and": the reverse, typst breaks after it).
- **B3. A slash inside a word (LaTeX).** typst and Word break after "/" inside a word ("Baze/" "Glossip",
  "supplier/" "compounder"); LaTeX does not. tenn answering p38, p43.
- **B4. URLs in LaTeX still break at other pieces.** After "https://www." typst and Word break before the host
  ("https://www." / "tn.gov/..."); LaTeX keeps the URL whole on the next line and stretches the justified line
  before it (tenn motion p4, p5; petition-for-review p17, "…/story/" / "news/local/…"). The pieces of
  `url_pieces` seem not to reach pdfTeX as break points in a justified paragraph.
- **B5. TeX Live's bold Schola spaces.** In a bold heading the spaces of tgschola bold are 0.13 pt wider than the
  vendored TeX Gyre Schola bold's (tenn petition-for-discretionary-review p8: "… standard set forth" fits in typst,
  "forth" moves down in LaTeX). The regular face matches.
- **B6. The line-fit tolerance with Schola in Word** (the open item of fb-595.54.82): lines typst fills to within a
  point of the 468 pt measure fit in LibreOffice's twip-rounded advances (tenn amicus p10 at 467.83 pt, reply p4,
  petition-for-review p1 and the contents, petition-for-discretionary-review p2, p4; nmctapp opening p15 with Times).
- **B7. The docx heading needle with `keep_with_closing`.** When the body's last paragraph is split off to be kept
  with the closing, the heading before it gets a needle with no following text (lib/docx/xdoc.lua `needles_of`,
  `nxt` empty), which finds the heading's word earlier in the body: tenn petition-for-discretionary-review's docx
  contents printed CONCLUSION at 10 for a heading on 13 (the word "conclusion" in a p10 sentence). That folder sets
  no `keep_with_closing` (its signature block opens p13 alone, tier 2 open) until the needle carries the kept
  paragraph's text.
