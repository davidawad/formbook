folder now has `typst/layout.yaml` and the three-line typst template, generated `latex/` and `docx/`, renders on all

## Shared-model requests

Each is a difference between the engines that the shared code (lib/typst/xdoc.typ, lib/typst/xengine.typ,
lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua, lib/latex/*, lib/docx/*) makes, not the folder's layout; the folders below stay
`cross_engine: report` until it is fixed once, in the shared code.

1. **A footnote typst splits across pages.** lib/latex/xengine.sty sets `\interfootnotelinepenalty=10000` (a footnote
   is never split); typst splits a long one, and LibreOffice splits it too but a line apart. Folder:
   `missouri/.../court-of-appeals-western-district/opening-brief` (footnote 3, typst p34-35; latex keeps the note whole
   and every page from p36 ends elsewhere; docx p35-37). Evidence: `fb diff --engines` latex "page ends: 10 of 46 shared
   pages differ", first p36; docx 3 of 46, first p35.
2. **Widow and orphan control in a paragraph that anchors a footnote.** The LaTeX and Word filters turn widow control
   off for such a paragraph (lib/typst/xengine.typ, comment on `footnote-widows`); typst's own two-line rule still
   holds, so typst carries two lines over where LaTeX and Word carry one (a widow), or carries a paragraph's first line
   over where they leave it (an orphan). `xdoc` calls `xengine` without `footnote-widows: false`. Folder:
   `.../answering-brief` (p6-7: "constitutional challenges ... / L.F. 549-59, 655-74, 700-716.[^1]"; p14-15: "As
   Appellants recognize, ... governed by" with footnote 3); latex 18 and docx 20 of 35 page ends differ from p7.
3. **A spaced ellipsis ". . ." breaks in LaTeX.** typst and Word keep "employer . . ." together (Unicode line breaking:
   no break before a full stop after a space, LB13); LaTeX breaks between the dots. Folder: `.../amicus-brief` (footnote
   3, p7 line 23: typst "... [f]or an" / "employer . . . to discriminate", latex "... [f]or an employer ." / ". . to").
4. **A URL breaks after a "." or "//" in typst only.** typst ends a line after "http://www.apa." or "https://";
   LaTeX and LibreOffice move the whole URL to the next line. Folder: `.../amicus-brief` (docx p10 line 30, p16
   line 27, p17 line 11; latex p10 line 30). The ca8 folders keep their own URL rules (fb-595.54.4);
   the shared model has none.
5. **A token around ")(" is boxed whole in typst, joined only at ")(" in LaTeX and Word.** lib/typst/xengine.typ
   `show regex("\\S*\\)\\(\\S*"): box` makes "§1052-a(1)(c).)" one unbreakable word; lib/docx/xdoc.lua (and the LaTeX
   filter) put a word joiner between ")" and "(" only, so LaTeX and Word break after "§1052-". Folder: `.../reply-brief`
   (p13 line 2; the only line of 578 that differs, in both latex and docx).
6. **An empty item of a line list.** A `""` in a list the layout prints one per line (`{text: "{lines}"}`) gives no
   line in typst and LaTeX, an empty paragraph of a full line in Word. Folders: `.../opening-brief` (cover counsel
   columns: "Respectfully submitted,", "", ...; docx p1 lines 14-20 differ), `.../petition-for-writ` (caption: "Relators,",
   "", "v."; docx p1 line 6, the case number centred against a taller caption), `.../amicus-brief` (signature lines;
   baselines only). The filer's blank line is meant (it aligns two counsel columns): typst and LaTeX should keep it.
7. **A full line at the right margin in Word.** LibreOffice fits a word that ends within about 0.25 pt of the right
   margin where typst does not (`.../petition-for-writ` footnote 1, p3: "signatures." ends at 539.77 pt in Word; typst's
   start is 0.08 pt to the left and its line would run 0.05 pt past 540). Same kind: a bold level-4 heading of
   `.../amicus-brief` (p14 line 13: LaTeX fits "men", typst does not). Folders: `.../petition-for-writ` (docx
   453/475 lines, page ends p9-11), `.../amicus-brief` (latex).
8. **A list as the PDF title.** `xdoc` sets `document(title: str(meta.brief_title))`, which fails when `brief_title` is
   a list of cover lines (`.../opening-brief`). The folder's template passes `title:` joined from the list; the model
   could join a list itself.
9. **TeX Gyre Schola in LaTeX** (already listed in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Not yet in the model"). The Nebraska Supreme
   Court folders (`amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`, `petition-for-writ`, `reply-brief`) set
   Century Schoolbook (Neb. Ct. R. App. P. § 2-103(A)(4), NEB-FORM-006: "Preferred fonts are Century or Century
   Schoolbook"; TeX Gyre Schola is the vendored clone). `fb engines gen` refuses the face ("the shared document model
   does not set Schola in LaTeX yet (lib/latex/xeschola.lua)"), so no latex or docx can be generated for them. They also
   need, from the same list, **space that fills a page** (the cover's counsel at the foot of the page, typst `v(1fr)`)
   and **a page list's width for a face other than Times New Roman** (lib/docx/xdoc.lua `list_width`), and two features
   the model has no block for:
   - **blocks after the tail**: every Nebraska brief sets its certificate of compliance (NEB-CERT-001, "the final page")
     after the signature block and then signs it (`cert_signature`); the model ends the document with the tail, so
     `closing` cannot follow it (folders `amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`, `reply-brief`);
   - **a document of parts, each opening with its own caption and title**: `petition-for-writ` is the application for
     leave (Neb. Ct. R. App. P. § 2-115(A)(1), NEB-ORIG-001) and the verified petition filed as its attachment, split at `<!-- part -->`,
     each part with the caption and its `part_titles` entry beside `case_ref`; the model has one `front` per document.

Decision for David (Nebraska): § 2-103(A)(4) allows Times New Roman too ("Other allowed fonts are Times New Roman,
Baskerville Old Face, Book Antiqua, or Palatino"). Moving the five folders to Times New Roman would put them on the
model today for the face (the two block features above would still be needed); keeping the court's preferred Century
Schoolbook waits for request 9. They were left on Schola, their templates, samples and renders unchanged: with no
latex or docx to compare, moving typst onto the model alone would change five passing renders for no gate.
