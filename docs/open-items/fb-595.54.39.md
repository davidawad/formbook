## Shared-model requests (for a second serial lane)

1. **LaTeX: a break after a slash inside a word.**
   - Folders: `petition-for-review` (p12, p13: "calls/phone" in two quotations),
     `opening-brief` (p10: "employment/partnership"; pages 20-27 then end apart), `answering-brief` (p9:
     "Plaintiff/Counterdefendant").
   - Evidence: typst and LibreOffice end a line after the slash (lib/docx/xdoc.lua `breaks` writes a zero-width
     space for `[^%s/]/%a`); lib/latex/xengine.lua has no such rule outside URLs, so pdfLaTeX carries the whole word
     to the next line (`fb diff --engines`: "ours does not carry the line ... calls/").
2. **Word page lookup: an authority whose find starts a heading.**
   - Folder: `answering-brief` (`### Applegate requires affirmance when the appellant provides no record of the
     trial.`, the find "Applegate").
   - Evidence: tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs searches an authority needle after a heading from the heading's position + 1
     ("an authority cited in the heading is searched from the next character"), so a find at the heading's first
     character falls back to its next occurrence two paragraphs on; every later needle is then searched past its own
     place and `fb render --engine docx` stops: "not found in the PDF: a14.2, a6.1, ... h8, ...". The folder ships
     typst and latex only (no `docx/`), recorded in its eval.yaml.
3. **Footnote clearance for TeX Gyre Bonum at 32.9 pt.**
   - Folders: `opening-brief` (p7), and the other Bonum briefs that set the same value (`amicus-brief`,
     `answering-brief`, `reply-brief`).
   - Evidence: LibreOffice keeps a body line 15.25 pt above the first note's line box (Bonum, body 32.9, note 16.45),
     where the model's default (gap 7 + clearance 9.1 = 16.1, measured on Times and Arial) moved the line on in typst
     and LaTeX (docx 18 body lines on p7, typst 17; every later page ended apart). typst keeps the line at a clearance
     of 7.9 or less; the folders set 7.5 (`footnote.clearance`, carried to LaTeX's `\skip\footins`), after which every
     docx page of the opening brief ends as typst's. The default belongs to the face (gen.rs) rather than each layout.
4. **typst: a list inside a quotation narrower than LaTeX's and Word's.**
   - Folder: `amicus-brief` (p11, `> - ...` bullets in a block quotation, quote left/right 36).
   - Evidence: LaTeX and Word fill the list lines to 503 pt on the 504 pt measure ("the seriousness of the risk"
     ends at 500.2 pt in both); typst breaks before "risk" on every such line, then the pages after p11 end apart.
5. **Word: TeX Gyre Bonum advances.**
   - Folder: `opening-brief` (p20 line 8).
   - Evidence: LibreOffice sets "different" 57.93 pt wide, typst and LaTeX 57.12 (no kerning or ligatures in any
     engine; the other words of the line within 0.1 pt), so "homes," that typst ends at 539.32 of 540 pt goes to the
     next line in Word (597/603 lines, pages equal).
6. **The line-fit tolerance (already open in fb-595.54.82, "Not implemented"), with this bead's lines:**
   `reply-brief` p9 and p22 (typst fills to 539.88 of 540 pt; Word breaks a word earlier), `amicus-brief` p5 (a note
   line at 539.73).
7. **Word page lookup: a heading followed by the paragraph `keep_with_closing` holds.**
   - Folder: `reply-brief` (tried, then dropped from the layout).
   - Evidence: with `keep_with_closing: true` the heading "CONCLUSION" gets a needle of its own text only
     ("conclusion"), which the lookup finds in the body a page earlier ("reached the same conclusion"): the docx
     contents gave the conclusion page 25, typst 26. Without the key the needle carries the next paragraph and the
     pages agree; the cost is two lines of the conclusion above the signature on p26 (tier 2).
