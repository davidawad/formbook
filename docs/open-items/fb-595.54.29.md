# fb-595.54.29: open items (New Mexico Supreme Court on the shared model)

Folders of the bead not gated, with the reason, for the serial lane fb-595.54.82. One folder is ported and gated:
`courts/tenth-circuit/new-mexico/state/supreme-court/motion-brief` (typst 8 passed, latex 11 passed, docx 12 passed,
0 failed; XENG-001..003 PASS, 104/104 lines in both engines). Every other folder is left on its current typst template
(no layout.yaml, no latex or docx; cross_engine not set: report).

The eight doc types share one template in two variants (the cover at a fixed 14 pt in amicus-brief, motion-brief and
petition-for-writ; a cover that steps down from 14 to 11 pt to fit one page in the other five). The layout written
for them (cover: court, parties beside the docket number, cover lines, lower court, counsel two to a row, the title
between rules; contents, authorities, roman tables; closing: date, signature, certificate of compliance and
certificate of service each on a page of its own) is the motion-brief's `typst/layout.yaml` with `cover:` instead of
`front:`, `page_number: {align: center, front: lower-roman}`, and the `contents`/`authorities` blocks; it rendered
all eight INPUTs. The tests below were run with it.

## Shared-model requests

### A cover that steps its type size down to fit one page

- Feature: a cover whose size is the largest of a list (here 14, 13, 12, 11 pt, then 11 pt with counsel three to a
  row) at which it fits one page, in all three engines. The current templates measure the cover in typst (`layout`
  and `measure`) and pick the size.
- Folders: `brief-on-the-merits` (the INPUT's cover is set at 11 pt), `petition-for-discretionary-review` (11 pt),
  `reply-brief` (12 pt); also `answering-brief` and `petition-for-review`, whose covers fit at 14 pt on the old
  template's leading but take two pages at the model's single pitch (1.15 x 14 = 16.1 pt): DEF-COVER-001 fails in
  typst ("cover pages 1-2 (2 pages); page 3 prints i" / "1").
- Evidence: NMRA 12-305(D)(1) (rules.md NM-FONT-003, NM-BRIEF-002): the cover may be 11 point "if needed to fit all
  required cover information on one page"; a fixed smaller size would set it when not needed, and a fixed 14 pt
  breaks DEF-COVER-001 for these covers. Not ported.

### URLs: typst breaks inside them, LaTeX and Word do not

- Same request as fb-595.54.71 ("URLs: typst breaks inside them").
- Folders and first differing line (typst vs latex and docx, which agree with each other):
  - `amicus-brief`: p4 line 23, the table of authorities row "FiveThirtyEight (July 19, 2023, 3:50 PM),
    https://projects.fivethirtyei" / "ght.com/..."; then p20 lines 11-16 (body). latex 473/487 lines, docx 473/487,
    23 pages each. Everything else matched (tier 1 0 failed in typst).
  - `answering-brief`: p11 line 2 "... (Dec. 30, 2021), https://bit.ly/3" / "GEviFX ..."; the pages after it move, so
    the first differing line is p4 line 18, a page reference of the table of authorities (latex 597/621 lines; plus
    the cover above).
  - `petition-for-writ`: p2 line 19, footnote "Voter Fraud Concerns: https://www.alamogordonews.com/story/
    community/2022/" / "06/13/..."; latex 358/371 lines.
  - `brief-on-the-merits` and `reply-brief` carry bare URLs too (10 and 4) and would need this after the cover.

### A cover in the docx engine when the folder has no tables

- lib/docx/xdoc.lua sets `spec.cover` only inside the `paged` branch (contents, or authorities with the frontmatter's
  `authorities`): a folder with a cover and no tables loses its cover in Word (motion-brief: docx 4 pages against 5,
  XENG-001 failed; petition-for-writ: docx 18 against 19). typst and LaTeX set it.
- Worked around in motion-brief: the cover is `front:` blocks ending in `{break: true}`, numbered from page 1 (the filed
  motion prints 1 on its cover; NM-FORM-002). The shared fix is to emit the cover (and its section) whenever
  `spec.cover` is set.

### A doubled pilcrow (¶¶) breaks differently

- Folder: `petition-for-review`, p20 line 3: typst ends the line after "Exhibit 2, ¶" and sets "¶ 90-102." on the
  next (it binds only the second sign to the number); LaTeX keeps "¶¶" together and breaks before "90-102". The docx
  filter binds doubled signs (`bind_double_signs`); typst's xengine.typ and LaTeX each do it another way.
- Same folder, p8 line 20: typst leaves the page one line short and starts "The majority then held ..." (a paragraph
  that carries a footnote) on p9; LaTeX starts it on p8 (latex 592/595 lines, XENG-002 2 of 30 pages). A footnote
  placement difference in the shared model, not the folder's.
- Also the cover (two pages at the model pitch, above). Not ported.

### A group inside a column takes the full text width in LaTeX

- lib/latex/xdoc.lua `group()` sets `vtop(TEXTW, ..)`: a `{group: [..]}` inside a column is as wide as the page, so a
  party's lines ran under the docket-number column (amicus-brief p1 line 2, "No. S-1-S" cut at the margin;
  DEF-FORM-002 failed in latex). The NM layout sets the parties as plain blocks instead; the shared fix is the
  column's width (`\hsize`) for a group in a column.
- Tier-2 consequence: a party with no designation (motion-brief's "In the Matter of ..." line; answering-brief's
  "HONORABLE FRED VAN SOELEN,") has no space above it, since the space is on the designation. Accepted for
  motion-brief.

### The tables as contents rows

- The old templates list the table of authorities and the certificate pages in the table of contents (as the filed
  answering brief and brief-in-chief do); xdoc's `table-title` is not a heading and the closing blocks are not
  headings, so the ported contents lists body headings only. Same request as fb-595.54.71 ("The tables as PDF
  bookmarks"). The certificate of compliance moves from the front matter (after the contents) to after the
  signature, where the filed petition-for-discretionary-review prints it; the filed briefs vary (after the contents,
  after the authorities, at the end), NM-CERT-003 sets no place.
