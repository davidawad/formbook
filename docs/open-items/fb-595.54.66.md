## Shared-model requests

### 1. Widow and orphan control on a paragraph that anchors a footnote
- Feature: typst keeps its widow/orphan prevention on a paragraph that holds a footnote reference, while the LaTeX and
  Word filters turn widow control off for that paragraph (xengine `footnote-widows: true`, the model's default). The
  engines then break the page in different places.
- Folders and evidence:
  - `fourth-circuit/west-virginia/state/intermediate-court-of-appeals/amicus-brief`: p7, the paragraph "The Supreme
    Court of Appeals recognized long ago in Paul v. National Life" (footnote 11): typst carries two lines to p8
    (no widow), LaTeX and Word set "alcoholic beverages who overserves ..." on p7 and one line on p8. XENG-002/003, latex
    and docx.
  - `fourth-circuit/west-virginia/state/intermediate-court-of-appeals/opening-brief`: p10/p11, "Under the Supremacy
    Clause[^3] neither ...": typst sets footnote 3 on p10 and moves the paragraph's first line (an orphan) to p11, so
    the note stands a page before its mark; LaTeX moves line and note to p11; Word keeps the line on p10 with the note.
    XENG-002/003, latex and docx.
  - `fourth-circuit/west-virginia/state/intermediate-court-of-appeals/reply-brief` (docx): p15, the last line of the
    conclusion "underlying the Subject Property.[^1]": Word sets line and note on p15, typst and LaTeX move the line to
    p16 (with `footnote.clearance: 4` typst keeps it on p15 too, so typst's footnote area is a few points taller than
    Word's for one note; a smaller clearance was tried for all five WV folders and broke the amicus brief, so it is not a
    folder fix). XENG-002/003, docx.
- Request: one widow/orphan policy for footnote-anchoring paragraphs in all three engines (and the footnote-area height
  Word uses), in lib/typst/xengine.typ, lib/latex/xengine.sty, lib/docx/xengine.lua.
