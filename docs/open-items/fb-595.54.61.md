## Shared-model requests

1. **Cover without tables (docx).** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` emits `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or
   authorities present); a layout with a `cover` and no tables loses the cover in Word (latex keeps it).
   Folder: `oklahoma/.../petition-for-rehearing` (worked around: the cover is `front` blocks closed by `{break: true}`).
   Evidence: docx render of INPUT-petition-for-rehearing had 25 pages against typst's 26, page 1 the body.
2. **No break before a spaced ellipsis.** typst (UAX 14: "." is class IS, no break before it, even after a space)
   keeps "means . . . the" together; LaTeX and Word break between "means" and ". . .". Folders:
   `oklahoma/.../petition-for-rehearing` (p12 line 21: typst ends after "jurisdiction", latex and docx after
   "means"; XENG-003 534/547 latex, 533/547 docx), left at `cross_engine: report`. The model should bind the space
   before an IS-class word (a no-break space in lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua, or allow the break in typst).
3. **Reader dialects.** The body goes through cmarker (CommonMark) in typst but pandoc `markdown+smart` in latex and
   docx: a paragraph starting "(1) " or "(405) 521-3921" becomes an ordered list in pandoc (fancy_lists; the second
   item renumbered "(406)"), and "$, 200$" becomes TeX math (tex_math_dollars). Worked around in the INPUT with
   CommonMark escapes (`\(`, `\$`) that both readers print as the character; the model should read the body with
   `commonmark_x+smart` (as lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua already reads fields) or disable those extensions.
