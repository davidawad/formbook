## Shared-model requests

1. **Cover without tables (docx).** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` emits `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or
   authorities present): a layout with a `cover` and no tables loses the cover in Word (LaTeX keeps it). Folder:
   oklahoma/petition-for-rehearing (worked around: the cover is `front` blocks closed by `{break: true}`). Evidence:
   the docx render had 25 pages against typst's 26, page 1 the body.
2. **No break before a spaced ellipsis.** typst (UAX 14: "." is class IS, no break before it, even after a space)
   keeps "means . . . the" together; LaTeX and Word break between "means" and ". . .". Folder:
   oklahoma/petition-for-rehearing, p12 line 21 (typst ends the line after "jurisdiction", latex and docx after
   "means"; XENG-003 534/547 latex, 533/547 docx). The pandoc side could bind the space before an IS-class word.
3. **Reader dialects.** The body goes through cmarker (CommonMark) in typst but pandoc `markdown+smart` in latex and
   docx (tools/fb/src/engines.rs). Where they part: a paragraph opening "(1) ", "(b) ", "a. ", "v. ", "ii. " or
   "1) " is an ordered list in pandoc (fancy_lists; "(405) 521-3921" then "(406) ..."), "$...$" is TeX math,
   "~~" is strikeout. Worked around in the INPUTs with CommonMark escapes both readers print as the character
   (`\(`, `a\.`, `1\)`, `\$`, `\~`). The model should read the body with `commonmark_x+smart` (as
   lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua already reads fields) or turn those extensions off.
4. **Break after "//" in a URL.** typst breaks "http://www.example.com/..." after "http://"; lib/docx/xdoc.lua and
   LaTeX break only after a single slash inside a word before a letter (`([^%s/])/(%a)`). Folders:
   oklahoma/amicus-brief p6 line 12 (XENG-003 469/495 latex, 473/495 docx), oklahoma/answering-brief p29 line 7.
5. **"§§" and "¶¶" split in typst.** typst breaks a line between the two signs ("18 U.S.C. §" | "§ 1152-53";
   "¶" | "¶ 2, 6"); LaTeX and Word keep them (bind_double_signs). Folder: oklahoma/answering-brief p12 line 11,
   p41 line 1.
6. **Slash before a digit.** typst breaks after "/" before a digit in a URL ("DocketPDF/" | "17/17-1107/..."), the
   pandoc engines do not: LaTeX ran the URL 32 pt into the right margin (DEF-FORM-002 FAIL on latex, p65). Worked
   around: a U+200B after "DocketPDF/" in oklahoma/answering-brief's INPUT.
7. **Thematic break.** lib/latex/xdoc.lua and lib/docx/xdoc.lua stop on a HorizontalRule ("xdoc: unsupported block
   HorizontalRule"). oklahoma/answering-brief has a "***" section break and a "___" signature line: escaped
   (`\*\*\*`, `\___`), printed as text.
8. **Short lines flush left.** The old oklahoma/answering-brief template set a paragraph under 50 characters
   without its first-line indent (the transcribed signature and certificate lines); the model indents every body
   paragraph. Tier 2 only; recorded for the vocabulary.
9. **LaTeX splits a long heading.** oklahoma/brief-on-the-merits: a level-2 heading of seven lines starts at the foot
   of p55 in LaTeX (three lines there, four on p56); typst and Word move it whole to p56. The heading paragraph needs
   no break inside it in lib/latex/xdoc (docx passes 1751/1751 lines).
10. **Smart quotes.** cmarker and pandoc pair unbalanced straight quotes differently (`minute." (Tr.` is “ in typst,
    ” in pandoc). Worked around: oklahoma/brief-on-the-merits' INPUT quotes made typographic (opening after a space,
    bracket or dash, closing elsewhere; the content check ignores quote style).
11. **Column of ")" beside a caption.** oklahoma/motion-brief sets one ")" as a vertically centred column (valign:
    center) between the parties and the docket number, as the old template did; a ")" per caption line, as filed
    captions have, is not in the model.
12. **Computed word count.** Every delaware/ doc folder prints a certificate of compliance stating the word count the
    template computes (rules.md DEL-CERT-001, DEL-CERT-101, DEL-FORM-204 / R. 14(d)(ii), 28(d), 30(d); kind M, tier 1)
    and refuses a sample over the word limit (R. 14(d)(i): 10,000 / 14,000 on cross-appeal; reply 5,500; amicus
    5,000; motion 1,200). The model has a count given in the frontmatter only, and the samples have no such field
    (no new fields allowed in this batch). Not ported: the typst templates are unchanged and ungated. Needed: a
    `{word_count}` the model computes (with the folder's exclusions: cover, tables, signature block; footnotes,
    headings and quotations counted) in all three engines, and a limit check.
13. **Authorities marked by link.** delaware/answering-brief and delaware/reply-brief mark each citation in the body
    as a link `[text](toa:key)` and give `authorities:` as a map `key: {kind, name, cite, sort}`; the table groups by
    kind (cases, statutes, constitutional provisions, rules, other), sorts, and prints "passim" from seven pages. The
    model reads only `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` by `find` text.

    run: when any paragraph in it calls a footnote, every paragraph of the quotation loses widow and orphan control

    Folder: delaware/motion-brief, p7: typst sets the first line of the quoted paragraph "Here, the State presented
    ..." alone at the foot of the page, LaTeX and Word move it to p8 (XENG-002 p8; XENG-003 320/325 latex, 323/325

    latex pages and page ends equal (only request 15 left). The split should go into the quotation's paragraphs.
15. **"* * * *" in LaTeX's text.** pdfTeX sets the spaced asterisks of a quotation's section break ("\* \* \* \*")
    at typst's positions but without space glyphs, so pdftotext reads one word "****" (typst and Word: four).
    Folder: delaware/motion-brief p7 lines 21, 27, p8 line 6 ("ours does not carry the line '* * * *'"). Either the
    LaTeX side writes real spaces there or the cross-engine diff joins the pieces of a word made of one repeated mark.

    amicus 8, 11, 12, 31). The model has no page break before a heading (a heading style `break: true`, typst
    `pagebreak(weak: true)`, LaTeX `\clearpage`, Word page-break-before, the contents' pages following). Folders:
    delaware/amicus-brief, answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits, reply-brief: not ported (their old typst templates,
    which lack it too, stay; no latex or docx engine). A trial port of delaware/answering-brief (uncommitted) found
    nothing else missing for tier 1 but these, for the serial lane:
    - the cover with three counsel blocks two to a row runs to a second page (DEF-COVER-001); the old template fits one
      page only by overlapping its stacked 14-pt lines. The layout needs the counsel set tighter (or request "a cover
      whose type size is chosen to fit", fb-595.54.82's open list);
