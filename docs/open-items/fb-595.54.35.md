## Input changes (in the folders' own `usage/`)

The Virginia INPUTs listed their authorities as a map (`authorities: {key: {kind, name, cite}}`) and marked each
citation in the body with a link `[text](toa:key)`; the model reads `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite,
find}]}]` and marks every occurrence of `find`. The ported folders' INPUTs were rewritten to the model's form: the
groups in the old template's order (CASES, STATUTES, CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS, RULES, OTHER AUTHORITIES), the entries
sorted as before, each cite as the old template printed it; the `toa:` links became their plain text (the words are
unchanged, DEF-CONT-001 passes). Each `find` is a string of the citation inside one style run (a reporter volume, a
section number, a docket or WL number) chosen to hit the link texts of that authority and no other's. Where one
authority was cited in forms with nothing in common (an "Id.", a short name and a reporter cite), the page of the
other form is no longer listed: the table can miss a page the old `toa:` link listed. `passim` (seven or more pages)
is not in the model: those entries list every page.

`answering-brief`: the filer's PDF has "487−88" with U+2212 MINUS SIGN; pdflatex (`lib/latex/xdoc.lua`) has no glyph
for it and stops, so the INPUT sets the en dash the pin cite means.

## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **A footnote too long for the rest of the page.** typst splits it (part on this page, the rest on the next);
   `lib/latex/xengine.sty` sets `\interfootnotelinepenalty=10000` (never split) and LibreOffice splits it elsewhere.
   Evidence: `answering-brief` typst p33 (body page 27) carries the first four lines of note 5 and the rest on p34;
   LaTeX moves the line with the mark ("the impending revisions ... program.5 The General") and the whole note to p34;
   docx splits note 2 over p2-p3 where typst keeps it on p2 (XENG-002 from p35 on). The engines must agree: split
   at the same line in all three, or never.
2. **URLs.** typst breaks a URL at "/" and inside a word ("https://tinyurl.com/3" | "ffa798x."; ".../legp604." |
   "exe?202+..."); LaTeX does not break it (the whole URL moves to the next line, or overfills a table row: "...cgibin/
   legp604.exe?202+ful+CHAP0050+pdf" on one line of the authorities); Word breaks after "/" only. Evidence:
   `answering-brief` typst p14 note 3 (the first differing line of XENG-003 after the footnote), `motion-brief` p4
   (table of authorities, OTHER AUTHORITIES row 1) and p8 lines 11-12.
3. **A break before an em dash.** `answering-brief` p18 line 1: typst "... in violation of Code" | "§ 18.2-48.1—
   enumerated ...", LaTeX "... Code § 18.2-48.1" | "—enumerated ..." (LaTeX breaks before "—", typst does not).
4. **"§§".** typst breaks between the two signs: `motion-brief` p8 lines 4-5, typst "... 1995, Code §" | "§ 53.1-202.2
   through ...", LaTeX and Word "... Code §§" | "53.1-202.2 ...". The pair is one word in LaTeX and Word.
5. **A multi-line heading at the foot of a page.** `motion-brief` LaTeX p13 sets the first two of the three lines of
   the level-2 heading "II. The inclusion of specific “stand-alone” inchoate offenses in Code § 53.1-202.3(A) ..." at
   the foot of p13 and the rest on p14; typst and Word move the whole heading to p14 (`keep`). The heading must not
   split in LaTeX.
6. **A blank for a missing field.** The old Virginia template printed "____________" for a date of the certificate of
   filing and service the filer has not given (Rule 5:1B(c) requires both dates; the filer completes them), and
   left room for the recipients. The model either panics on a missing field or drops the paragraph (`optional`).
   Needed: a default for a field (`{service.filed_date|____________}` or a `blank:` style). Folders:
   `brief-on-the-merits`, `reply-brief` (no `service.filed_date`; the filed exemplars' certificates state only the
   service date), `petition-for-review` (no `service`).
7. **A list item by index, or an answer under each question.** Rule 5:30(e)(2) (VA-AMI-004): the amicus brief states
   three answers; the old template sets the three questions (court constants) with the frontmatter's
   `amicus_statement[i]` after each, or a ruled blank when not given, on a page of its own in the roman-numbered
   front matter. The model cannot pair a constant with the i-th item of a list, has no blank (request 6) and sets no
   page between the tables and the body. Folder: `amicus-brief`.
8. **An empty string as absent.** `petition-for-review` has `record_number: ""` (not yet assigned); the old template
   omits "RECORD NO." then. The model prints "RECORD NO. " (`optional` and `when` test presence, not emptiness).
9. **A numbered cover.** `petition-for-writ` (VA-WRIT-004, rules.md): the caption styles every respondent and runs
   over pages i to iv, the filed petition numbers every page from i, the title on v, the body from 1. The model's
   `page_number.front` leaves the cover unnumbered (DEF-COVER-001 would then fail on the caption's continuation
   pages). Needed: `page_number.front` with the cover numbered in the front numbering.
10. **One line of a paragraph at the foot of a page.** `petition-for-discretionary-review` body p15 (PDF p20): LaTeX and
   Word set the first line of the paragraph "“This Court has long recognized ..." alone at the foot of the page; typst
   moves it to p16. The three engines need one orphan rule (XENG-002 from p21 on).
11. **A page list wider than the column.** `petition-for-discretionary-review` table of authorities, the row of *Neal v.
   Fairfax Cty. Police Dep’t* (13 pages): typst ends the cite's first line after "295", LaTeX and Word after "295 Va."
   (`lib/docx/xdoc.lua` `list_width` and LaTeX measure the page list wider or narrower than typst).
