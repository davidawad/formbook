## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **A cover without tables in the docx engine.** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` (the `Pandoc` filter, `local paged = spec.contents
   or (spec.authorities and meta.authorities)`) writes `spec.cover` only inside `if paged`: a layout with a `cover` and
   no contents or authorities table loses its cover in Word (typst and LaTeX set it). Evidence: Utah motion-brief,
   petition-for-rehearing and petition-for-writ with `cover:` and no `contents:` rendered the docx without page 1
   (motion: 8 pages against typst's 9, XENG-002 from p1 line 1). Workaround in these three folders: the cover is the
   `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}`, `page_number: {from: 2}` (the cover counted as page 1, unnumbered; the
   tables-apart numbering needs `page_number.front`, which needs the cover). Needed: the cover in its own section
   whether or not the tables exist.
2. **A footnote typst sets on another page than its mark.** typst lets the line that carries a footnote mark and the
   note itself fall on different pages (the note deferred to the next page, or the line moved on and the note left
   behind); LaTeX and Word move the line with the note. Already listed for Michigan court-of-appeals motion-brief
   (fb-595.54.63) and Mississippi (fb-595.54.51). Folders here:
   - Utah brief-on-the-merits: typst body p8 ends a line early; the line with mark 44 ("After some procedural
     wrangling,⁴⁴") starts p9 while note 44 stays on p8 (XENG-003 latex and docx 659/774 lines; in the body the first differing line is p11
     line 1; the contents then give pages one higher from 19 on).
   - Utah petition-for-rehearing: typst sets mark 4 on the last line of p2 and note 4 on p3; LaTeX moves the line to p3
     (latex 371/477, docx 402/477, first differing p2 line 27).
   - Utah reply-brief: note 27 (latex and docx list *Patterson* on pages "8, 9", typst "9"; 311/337 and 315/337, first
     differing p3 line 5, the authorities table).
3. **Line breaks inside a long URL** (fb-595.54.73 request 5). Utah amicus-brief, footnote 2
   (`https://corpus.byu.edu/coca/?c=coca&q=73188125`; latex 355/371, docx 338/371, first differing p7 line 17); Utah
   petition-for-writ, a footnote on p21 (`le.utah.gov/~2021s2/bills/static/HB2004.html`; 620/635 latex and docx).
   Pennsylvania motion-brief, body p3 (`https://www.pasenategop.com/blog/intergovernmental-operations-...`; latex 242/247,
   docx 243/247, first differing p3 line 17); petition-for-review, footnote on p10 (`https://www.census.gov/newsroom/...`);
   amicus-brief, body p12 (docx 367/372) and p14, where pdflatex does not break
   `https://www.paauditor.gov/Media/Default/Reports/Department%20of%20State_SURE%20Audit%20Report%2012-19-19.pdf` at
   all and the line runs to 612.51 pt (DEF-FORM-002, right edge > 540): the amicus latex engine is BLOCKED and its
   generated `latex/` is not committed (as kansas court-of-appeals amicus-brief, fb-595.54.62 request 2).
4. **A heading kept with a numbered list in LaTeX.** A level-1 or level-2 heading followed by a numbered paragraph (a
   markdown ordered list) is kept with it in typst and Word, but LaTeX leaves the heading at the foot of the page.
   Pennsylvania petition-for-review p8 ("II. The 2020 Census is complete." under item 17; latex 515/525, first
   differing p8 line 19) and p17; petition-for-writ p5 ("BACKGROUND" under item 24; latex 554/589, first differing p5
   line 18). Needed: lib/latex/xdoc.lua keeps a heading with a following list item as with a paragraph.
5. **Underlined text that does not fit a line in LaTeX.** `\underline` sets its text in a box, so an underlined block
   longer than its measure never breaks: the Pennsylvania writ's "/s/ Thomas W. King, III, PA. I.D. No. 21580" (at
   `left: 230.4`) ran to 553.2 pt in LaTeX where typst and Word wrap "21580" (XENG-003 p2 line 7). Workaround: the
   Pennsylvania "/s/" lines are not underlined (no rule asks for it). Needed: an underline that breaks with its text.
6. **A break after a slash inside an italic word.** typst and Word break *Project Vote/Voting for Am.* after "Vote/";
   LaTeX does not (the slash rule seems not to reach text inside emphasis): Pennsylvania amicus-brief p8 line 5.
7. **An authority mark on the last line of a page.** Pennsylvania amicus-brief: "Pa.R.A.P. 531(a)." ends the last line
   of body p1; typst lists the authority on page 2, LaTeX and Word on page 1 (XENG-003 p4 line 11, the authorities
   table). Related to fb-595.54.62 request 3 (a find across a line or page break), but this find is on one line.
8. **Headings numbered by the template** (already "Not yet in the model"; fb-595.54.73 request 3). Pennsylvania
   opening-brief, answering-brief, reply-brief: the template numbers level 2 "I.", level 3 "A.", level 4 "1." with a
   0.5 in gap, and the samples' headings carry no numerals (`## Introduction`). Porting would print the headings
   unnumbered (or needs the numerals typed into the samples, a change of the samples' contract: a decision). These
   three folders are untouched (typst only, as before). The amicus-brief sample already types its numerals (`# I.
   INTEREST OF AMICUS CURIAE`, `## A. Federal Law ...`): the old template numbered them again ("I. A. Federal Law"),

### Shared-model requests, second serial lane

A. **LaTeX drops a footnote whose mark ends a held "§" token** (a bug of fb-595.54.82's "the token after § or ¶ never
   broken"). lib/latex/xdoc.lua `texify` wraps the token after the sign in `\mbox{..}` (xd.hold_sign_tokens), and a
   `\footnote` inside an `\mbox` sets its mark but loses its text. Evidence: Utah petition-for-rehearing,
   `§ 78B-5-302(3)[^1]; § 78B-5-305(1)[^2]; § 78B-5-201(2)[^3], § 78B-5-202(7)(c)[^4]` (body p2): the latex render
   prints marks 1-4 and none of the four notes; brief-on-the-merits `§ 78B-6-301(4)–(5).[^17]`, note 17 missing. Word
   keeps the notes. 101 INPUTs in the repo have a note mark inside such a token (`grep -rlP '[§¶] [^\s]*\[\^' courts`),
   so every folder gated before this lane renders them without those notes when re-rendered. DEF-CONT-001 passed on
   the latex render that lost them: the content gate does not see a missing note text (a checker gap). Needed: the note
   outside the box (`\mbox{..}` around the text before the note, the note after it, as typst's box keeps the
   note's mark in the line).
B. **typst sets a list item's first line alone at the foot of a page** (no orphan control in a list item), where
   LaTeX and Word move the line on. lib/typst/xdoc.typ `list-items` sets each item as `block(pad(par(..)))`. Evidence:
   Pennsylvania petition-for-review p10 line 16 (item 24, "2019 Census Bureau data make clear ..."; latex and docx
   517/525), petition-for-writ p10 line 21 (item 40, "Plaintiffs have obtained a sworn Affidavit ..."; latex 562/589,
   docx 566/589). Neither item anchors a note.
C. **A URL without a scheme** (request 3's rule reads only `http://`, `https://`, `www.`): Utah petition-for-writ,
   footnote on p21, `le.utah.gov/~2021s2/bills/static/HB2004.html` (typst breaks after "gov/", LaTeX and Word do not;
   620/635 latex and docx, first differing p21 line 10).
D. **A list item inside a quotation wraps a word early in typst.** Pennsylvania opening-brief p6, the quoted order's
   item 4 ("4. 182 ballots with a mismatched address on the outer envelope; and"): "outer" ends at 466.97 pt in LaTeX
   and Word, inside the quotation's right edge (468); typst moves it to the next line, though a quotation paragraph
   reaches 467.89 on the same page. Reproduced alone with `xdoc` (a one-item list in a `>` quotation), not with plain
   typst `par(hanging-indent)` in a `pad` of the same width: something in the model's quotation list costs typst about
   a point. latex 744/748, docx 746/748.
E. **Request 6, wider: LaTeX never breaks after a slash inside a word.** Word gets a zero-width space there
   (lib/docx/xdoc.lua, "CM/ECF"), typst breaks by Unicode line breaking, lib/latex has no such rule (not only inside
   emphasis): Pennsylvania opening-brief p14 "(MM/DD/YYYY)" in a quotation (docx breaks after "DD/" as typst does),
   amicus-brief p8 *Project Vote/Voting* (unchanged).
F. **`keep_with_closing` when the last paragraph and the closing exceed a page.** Pennsylvania reply-brief (a 30-line
   signature block): typst leaves the paragraph and sets the closing on the next page; LaTeX kept them and ran the
   closing past the bottom margin (its last lines not set), XENG-001 46 pages against 47. Workaround: the reply
   layout does not set `keep_with_closing`. Needed: LaTeX gives the keep up as typst does when the two do not fit.
G. **A list item of two paragraphs** (already "not yet in the model"): typst joined them with no space
   ("election?Suggested Answer: Yes.", Pennsylvania answering-brief, Statement of Questions). Workaround in the INPUT:
   a markdown hard line break inside the item (the filed copy sets "Suggested Answer" on its own line).
