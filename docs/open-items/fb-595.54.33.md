# fb-595.54.33: open items (Illinois Supreme Court on the shared model)

Bead fb-595.54.33 lists the Illinois Supreme Court root and its eight doc-type folders. The root has no template
(`typst/` and `latex/` are empty, no INPUT; `check.sh` gives `RESULT: 0 checks passed, 0 failed` on every engine), so
there is nothing to port there. The eight doc-type folders share one typst template
(`<doc>/typst/ill-<doc>.typ`, 317 lines; the copies differ only in line 24, the cover colour). Every one of them
needs at least one feature that lib/typst/xdoc.typ (and its latex and docx twins) does not have. None was ported, so
there is no `layout.yaml`, no generated latex or docx and no `params.cross_engine` in any of them (report). Nothing in
the court folders was changed. Checked on this tree (2026-10-22). The current typst renders pass tier 1:
`sh tools/check.sh <doc> --engine typst` gives 0 failed for all eight. latex and docx fail DEF-RENDER-001 only
because there is no `latex/template.tex` or docx engine yet.

## Shared-model requests

### 1. A cover page colour (tier 1)

- Feature: a fill colour for the cover page, e.g. `cover_fill: "#BDD7EE"` in layout.yaml (typst `page(fill:)`; LaTeX
  `\pagecolor` for the cover page only; Word a page background, or a cover section with a full-page shape behind the
  text, since Word's page colour applies to the whole document).
- Folders: `amicus-brief` (light blue, `#BDD7EE`), `answering-brief` (light blue), `reply-brief` (light yellow,
  `#FFF9B0`). `brief-on-the-merits`, `petition-for-discretionary-review` and `petition-for-review` are white today,
  but they take the same cover block.
- Evidence: rules.md ILL-FORM-008 (Ill. S. Ct. R. 341(d), 345(b), mandatory) sets the cover colour by document type.
  The template sets it at `ill-<doc>.typ:24` and `:123` (`page(fill: cover-fill)`). lib/typst/xdoc.typ has no fill.
  Porting without it would drop a tier-1 requirement.

### 2. Points and Authorities: the authorities under each point of the Argument (tier 1)

- Feature: a table that lists, under each level-2 and level-3 heading after the level-1 heading "Argument", that
  heading with its page, then each authority cited between it and the next heading of the same or a higher level,
  with the pages of those citations. It also needs *passim* at 6 or more pages (as in fb-595.54.71's *passim*
  request). The frontmatter is a registry `authorities: {<key>: {kind, name?, cite}}`, and the body cites with
  markdown links `[..](toa:<key>)`. The model reads `authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` and finds
  strings, so these INPUTs cannot be read "as they are" (no new fields).
- Folders: `amicus-brief` (105 `toa:` links), `answering-brief` (33), `brief-on-the-merits` (113),
  `petition-for-discretionary-review` (50), `reply-brief` (57). `petition-for-review` has `toc: false` and an empty
  registry, but the same template.
- Evidence: rules.md ILL-CONT-002(1) and ILL-CONT-003 (Rule 341(h)(1), 341(i), mandatory): "table of contents and
  'Points and Authorities' (headings of the points and subpoints with the authorities under each, and page
  references)". The template has `points-and-authorities` at `ill-<doc>.typ:197`, links marked at `:291`, and passim
  at `:25`. The model's flat `authorities` table is not this table.

### 3. A caption with a column of ")" and party designations indented under the names (tier 2)

- Feature: the caption rows `caption: [{left, right, indent?}]`, set as a left column, a column of ")" and a right
  column. A row with `indent: true` sets its left text 1.1 in further right. This is "a column of ')' beside a
  caption", already in the EVAL-LOOP list of features not in the model. It also needs a conditional with an else
  branch (or a style taken from an item field): `when` only tests that a field is present, so `{each: caption, ..}`
  cannot set one row indented and the next not.
- Folders: all eight (in the front matter of the cover, or at the top of page 1 when `cover: false`: `motion-brief`,
  `petition-for-writ`).
- Evidence: `ill-<doc>.typ:85-86`. The filed motion (FILED-EXAMPLE-motion-brief.pdf, Attorney General) and the filed
  writ motion (FILED-EXAMPLE-petition-for-writ.pdf, State Appellate Defender), from different filers, both indent
  "Plaintiff-Petitioner," / "Movant," / "v." / "Respondents." under the party names, with a ")" on every caption line.

### 4. A title broken where the frontmatter breaks it (tier 2)

- Feature: a newline inside a field value starts a line, as `<br>` does in `expand`. `brief_title` holds a `\n` in
  six of the eight INPUTs (motion: `"MOTION TO CONTINUE STAY OF MANDATE\nTHROUGH DISPOSITION OF APPEAL"`), and
  cmarker reads it as a space. The template splits on it (`ill-<doc>.typ:104`). Both exemplars above set the title
  on two lines.
- Folders: all but `petition-for-review` and `reply-brief` (their titles have no `\n`).

### 5. A cover whose counsel block sits at the foot of the page

- Feature: space that fills a page (`v(1fr)` on a cover), already in the EVAL-LOOP list of features not in the
  model.
- Folders: the six with `cover: true` (`amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`,
  `petition-for-discretionary-review`, `petition-for-review`, `reply-brief`). Evidence: `ill-<doc>.typ:125`.

### 6. Certificates chosen by type

- Feature: `when` on a value (`{when: type, is: compliance, ..}`), so that `{each: certificates, ..}` can set the
  Rule 341(c) compliance text (a court constant around `{text}`) for `type: compliance` and the `{heading}` and
  `{text}` of the others, each on a new page and followed by the signature block. Today the compliance item has no
  `heading` and would need the presence test inverted (an else branch, as in request 3).
- Folders: those whose INPUT has a compliance certificate (`amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`,
  `petition-for-discretionary-review`, `petition-for-review`, `reply-brief`). Evidence: `ill-<doc>.typ:150-170`,
  rules.md ILL-CONT-007 (Rule 341(c), mandatory).

`motion-brief` and `petition-for-writ` are the closest. Once the model has request 3 (the ")" column with indented
rows) and request 4 (a line break from `\n`), they can be ported with a `front` of expedited caption, "No.

with `{signature.lines}` at 2.9 in, and each certificate would follow on a new page with the signature again.
