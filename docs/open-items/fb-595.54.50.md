## Notes

- The court-root folders (courts/fourth-circuit/north-carolina/state/court-of-appeals,
  courts/first-circuit/maine/state/supreme-judicial-court) hold no template and no INPUT (only FILED-EXAMPLEs of their
  doc types and empty `typst/`, `latex/` folders): there is nothing to generate or render there.
- petition-for-review INPUT: the six grounds of N.C. Gen. Stat. § 62-94(b) quoted as "(1) …" lines read in pandoc as a
  numbered list inside the quotation (the model's quotations hold paragraphs only); they are now one quoted paragraph
  each with the "(1)" escaped, as typst rendered them before as one run-on paragraph.
- amicus-brief INPUT: the Hunter article's `find` is "Prologue" (was "Past as Prologue", which ran over a page end:
  Word's needle took the page of its first word, typst the page of its last).
- Maine SJC amicus-brief, answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits INPUTs: their tables of authorities were keyed links
  (`[text](toa:key)` with `authorities: {key: {kind, name, cite}}`), which the shared model does not read. The INPUTs now
  use the shared schema (`authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`, the groups in the old template's order and
  titles, the entries in its sort order, the cite as it printed them); each `find` is a word or two of the authority's
  link texts that no other authority's link carries (most cover every citation; a few cover fewer, e.g. Bell I 2 of 3,
  where the filer's short cites differ). The body text is unchanged but for the links unwrapped. An authority that no
  link text singles out (1 M.R.S. § 5, an unused entry) prints "--"; an authority on 7 or more pages prints "passim"
  (request 15).
- Maine SJC amicus-brief INPUT: the elision line "…." inside the Coastal Act quotation is now "…": DEF-CONT-001
  (tools/check/gate0.awk `leader()`) cuts a run of four dots after a sentence's period to four, so the line's fourth
  dot was never found and every block after it failed (it failed on the template before this change too).
- Maine SJC motion-brief INPUT: "> (1) it will suffer …" escaped as "\(1)": pandoc read it as a numbered list inside
  the quotation.
- Maine SJC petition-for-writ was pilot 2 (fb-595.54.12): unchanged, still gated and green.
- Tier 2, ACCEPTED (all engines): certificate-of-service signature blocks that fall to a page of their own (Maine
  reply-brief p41, brief-on-the-merits p87; NC amicus-brief p16), request 17.
