## Shared-model requests

1. **A footnote longer than a page.** amicus-brief footnote 2 (a string of legislative-history citations, about 40
   lines) is longer than a page at the double spacing Rule 4-1(b) requires for footnote lines (ARK-FORM-003, tier 1).
   lib/typst/xdoc.typ sets every note unbreakable; typst then puts the whole note on p16, past the page number into the
   bottom margin, and leaves an empty note rule on p15; LaTeX never splits a note and Word splits it at another line
   (pages differ from p14; DEF-CONT-001 fails on the typst render). Needed: a note taller than the page area splits,
   the same way in every engine (related: "footnotes split across pages" in fb-595.54.55 S3, fb-595.54.57). The current
   template sets notes single spaced, against the rule, and fits. Left on its current typst template, no latex/docx.
2. **The table of authorities after a body section.** Rule 4-2(a)(2)-(4) (ARK-CONT-002, tier 1): cover, contents,
   points on appeal, table of authorities, jurisdictional statement ... brief-on-the-merits opens with
   `# Points on Appeal` and its template sets the authorities after it; the model sets `authorities` before the body
   only. Same request as fb-595.54.71 (`authorities: {after: "Points on Appeal"}`). Left on its current typst template.
3. **An exact fit.** answering-brief: the closing group (the signature, 7 lines) ends at 720.0 pt, the bottom margin,
   in LaTeX and Word (last line box 703.3 to 719.4 pt); typst moves it to p25 alone, so typst has 27 pages, the others
   26 (every line before it is the same). typst's fit test needs a tolerance (a twip) or the others none; not tuned per
   folder (a 0.2 pt change of `closing.above` would hide it for this sample only).
4. **An authority `find` split over a page break.** answering-brief "Tucker v." with "Tucker" the last word of p12
   and "v." the first of p13: Word's needle counts p12, typst and LaTeX mark after the match's last word, p13 (the
   authorities row reads 12 in Word, 13 in the others). The model should count the page where the match starts.
5. **A typst authority mark on the last word of a page.** reply-brief: "sec. 12." ends the last line of p9; typst
   counts its mark on p10 ("6, 10"), LaTeX and Word on p9 ("6, 9"). Same as fb-595.54.45 item 7 and fb-595.54.59
   item 5.
6. **A cover without tables in docx.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or
   authorities): a folder with a cover and neither table loses its cover in Word (petition-for-review and
   petition-for-discretionary-review: Word started p1 with the body). Worked around here: those two set their cover as
   `front` blocks and a `{break: true}` (the same pages in every engine, numbered from the cover, Rule 4-1(e)). The
   fix belongs in the shared code.
7. **A hard line break in the tail (LaTeX).** The certificates' `/s/ name\` + `name` (a markdown hard break): LaTeX
   indents the line after the break by the tail's first-line indent (36 pt), typst and Word do not (motion-brief p15,
   petition-for-writ p12, petition-for-discretionary-review p6, answering-brief p26). Diagnostic only (the words and
   breaks are the same; XENG passes).
8. **An empty string in a text list.** petition-for-review `signature.firm` holds `""` between two signers: Word sets a
   blank line (+16.1 pt from there), typst and LaTeX nothing. Same as fb-595.54.53. XENG passes (diagnostic only).
