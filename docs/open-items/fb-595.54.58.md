## Shared-model requests

1. **Counsel in balanced flowing columns** (also fb-595.54.60 request 2). The lactapp `petition-for-discretionary-review`
   cover lists three counsel groups (7, 8 and 12 lines at 14 pt); `columns: {each: counsel, per_row: 2}` puts the third
   group in a second row that does not fit under the caption, title and proceeding (the row moves whole to page 2;
   DEF-COVER-001 does not see it, page 2 is numbered). `per_row: 3` wraps every line at a third of the measure. The old
   template split the groups into two columns of least height (1 and 2 left, 3 right). The port was reverted.
2. **A slash inside a word.** typst (xengine) breaks after "/" before a letter ("Tier 2/section", "and/or",
   "Plaintiffs/Respondents"), pdfTeX does not (LibreOffice did in every case seen). First lines: lactapp
   `opening-brief` latex p25 line 11 ("Tier 2/"); mdctspecapp `motion-brief` latex p2 line 19 ("and/").
3. **A hyphen before "(".** "19:2.2(B)(4)-(7)": typst keeps it whole, pdfTeX and LibreOffice break after "-". lactapp
   `answering-brief` p26 line 2, latex and docx.
4. **docx page lookup of an authority.** (a) lactapp `opening-brief`: "La. Stat. Ann. § 1:4" (find "§ 1:4", cited once,
   on p33 in all three renders) reads page 32 in the docx table (p4 line 11); the page list of "§ 89-25(h)" there sets
   its leader up to the number (p6 line 3, the list wider than the column: `list_width`). (b) mdctspecapp
   `opening-brief`: fb render fails, "not found in the PDF: a47.1", the needle of "Article III §§ 32 and 52" (the
   doubled section sign bound to its number); typst and LaTeX find it (latex XENG PASS 1216/1216). A find of "32 and
   52" instead fails in typst (the "§§ 32" box splits it). The folder ships typst and latex only.
5. **A cover without tables in docx.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits `cover` only on its paged path (when the folder has
   `contents` or the sample has `authorities`); without them the docx render dropped the cover (lactapp `motion-brief`,
   4 pages for typst's 6). Both motion folders set their cover as `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}` instead,
   which every engine sets alike; a motion with authorities would need `cover` back.
6. **An authority mark at a paragraph's end on a page's last line.** lactapp `petition-for-writ`: "...Uniform Rule
   4-3." ends a paragraph on the last line of p6; LaTeX and Word list page 6, typst page 7 (table of authorities, p5
   line 13).
7. **The spaced ellipsis ". . . ."** breaks between its periods in pdfTeX (and LibreOffice in lactapp
   `petition-for-writ`), not in typst: lactapp `petition-for-writ` p13 line 16 (latex, docx), mdctspecapp
   `answering-brief` p15 line 12 (latex; docx agrees with typst there).
8. **URLs in footnotes.** LibreOffice (lactapp `petition-for-writ` p13 line 23, then pages 13 to 18 start a line
   earlier) and pdfTeX (mdctspecapp `reply-brief` p32 line 16, `amicus-brief` p11 line 23) break a long URL elsewhere
   than typst.
9. **"¶ n" after a number.** typst boxes the sign with its number and breaks before "¶ 5" in "Ex. 6 ¶ 5"; LibreOffice
   fits the line (mdctspecapp `petition-for-discretionary-review` docx p16 line 13).
10. **An en dash and "¶¶ n–".** typst breaks after an en dash ("1688–" / "1766", "Success –"), pdfTeX does not
    (mdctspecapp `amicus-brief` latex p3 line 21, p4 line 9); pdfTeX breaks inside "¶¶ 40–41" after "40–", typst
    before "¶¶" (mdctspecapp `reply-brief` latex p11 line 11).
11. **docx page drift with long notes, cause not isolated.** mdctspecapp `reply-brief` pages 30 to 41 and `amicus-brief`
    pages 13 to 22 start on other words in the docx render (page counts equal; the tables' page numbers follow).
12. **U+2012 FIGURE DASH** is not in Times New Roman: typst and Word fell back to Liberation Serif, LaTeX printed an en
    dash, and the docx content check (DEF-CONT-001) lost the note that held it (lactapp `answering-brief` footnote 3).
    The INPUT now sets en dashes (the filed brief's role for them); the engines still differ for the character.
