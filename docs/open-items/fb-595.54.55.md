## Shared-model requests (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82)

- **M1. Markdown sections before the tables.** Kentucky RAP 32(A)(1)-(2) (KYCTAPP-CONT-001, -003): the introduction
  and the statement concerning oral argument come before the statement of points and authorities, numbered in roman
  with it; the amicus brief's introduction and statement of interest likewise. The model sets only frontmatter
  blocks (`cover`) before the tables and the whole markdown body after them. The samples mark the split with
  `body_start` (the first heading of the body proper). Folders: kentucky amicus-brief, answering-brief,
  opening-brief. Evidence: their old typst templates (`split-body`), the three INPUTs (`body_start`).
- **M2. The contents and the authorities on one page, one title.** Kentucky's "statement of points and authorities"
  (KYCTAPP-CONT-001): the points (headings) with the authorities under them. `table-title` opens a page for each
  table. Folders: the three above, and the reply (which now sets only "STATEMENT OF AUTHORITIES"). DEF-TOC-001 does
  not recognise that title, so it does not inspect the page (it passes vacuously; as before the move).
- **M3. Row-aligned caption grids.** Kentucky covers set the appeal-from lines in a middle column beside the first
  party and the designations right-aligned on the party rows; Ohio motion and petition captions have a vertical rule
  between the parties and the case column. The model has independent columns and no vertical rule.

## Shared-model differences that keep XENG from passing (folder cannot fix)

- **S1. Hyphen before a digit or letter inside a token.** typst does not break "Section 807-1-A4(2)(b)" after
  "807-1-" (nor "2022-Ohio-448" after "2022-"); LaTeX and Word do. Ohio opening p7 line 1, answering p5 line 12.
- **S2. A "." after a space.** typst (UAX 14 LB13) will not break before ".7705(2)" in "KRS 311.772(4)(a), .7705(2),
  .7706(2)"; LaTeX and Word do. Kentucky motion p2 line 18.
- **S3. A footnote split across pages.** typst splits a long note and continues it on the next page; LaTeX moves the
  referencing line and the whole note to the next page; Word splits a line later. Ohio opening p12-13 (note 4),
  amicus p10-13, reply p6-7; Kentucky petition p10 (note 3).
- **S4. URL breaks.** In footnotes and body typst breaks long URLs at other points than LaTeX and Word (Ohio amicus p6
  lines 18-19, motion p20-21, Kentucky petition p10). In table-of-authorities rows LaTeX does not break a URL at all:
  `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` `authorities()` sets `tex(xd.inlines(e.cite))` without the `xe.inlines` walk body paragraphs
  get. Ohio answering: an unbreakable URL put "27" 3.2 pt into the margin (DEF-FORM-002) until the folder narrowed its
  page column to 36 pt; one browser text fragment ("#:~:text=...") was cut from the INPUT for the same reason.
- **S5. Word: the first contents row reads page 2.** Ohio answering p2 line 2: "STATEMENT OF ASSIGNMENTS OF ERROR"
  gets page 2 (the contents page) in the docx render, 8 in typst and LaTeX; the page needle seems to match the
  contents row itself.
- **S6. "\* \* \*" in LaTeX** has no space characters between the stars: `pdftotext` reads "***" (Kentucky motion p4
  line 10), typst and Word "* * *".
- **S7. Word drops the cover of a folder without tables.** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` emits `spec.cover` only inside
  `if paged` (contents or authorities). Ohio reply-brief (no tables): the docx starts with the body, one page short
  (XENG-001 differs, 10 pages against 11).
- **S8. Runs of section signs.** typst's `show regex("[§¶] \\S+")` boxes "¶ ¶" and lets "8–9" break away; pandoc's
  `bind_signs` binds every sign to what follows. Ohio petition p6 line 22, p8 line 5 ("Ex. D, ¶ ¶ 8–9").
- **S9. Underlined text cannot wrap in LaTeX.** `lib/latex/xdoc.sty` `\xd@ul` sets underlined text in one `\hbox`;
  an underlined heading longer than the line runs into the margin. Ohio motion sets its level-1 and level-4 headings
  without underline (the filing underlines them). Also: a heading style's `bold: false` is ignored by typst (bold)
  but honoured by LaTeX and Word.
- **S10. An empty string in a list value** (`lines: ["a", "", "b"]`) gives an empty line in LaTeX and Word, nothing in
  typst. The Ohio INPUTs drop the empty items.
- **S11. pandoc reads "(1) ", "(a) ", "i. " opening a paragraph as a list** (commonmark_x fancy lists), cmarker as text;
  latex fails "a quotation holds paragraphs only" inside a quotation. The INPUTs escape them ("\(1)", "i\.").
- **S12. Pipe tables** are rejected by the pandoc engines ("unsupported block Table"): the Ohio petition's index of
  exhibits is now one paragraph per exhibit.

## Decisions

- **D1. Ohio motion-brief: one document.** The filing (and the old template) set the motion with its caption and
  signature, then the memorandum on a new page with a second caption, page numbers from 1 again, a second signature
  and the certificate. The model has no second caption or signature, no blocks after the tail and no page restart, so
  the folder now sets one caption, the motion, the memorandum under its own level-1 heading, one signature and the
  certificate of service on its own page (pages 1-28). Tier 1 holds (caption, signature, certificate). Accept, or
  wait for the model to carry a second part?
- **D2. Kentucky motion "V." and Ohio petition "Case No. ______".** A court constant stands where the old templates
  read an optional field (`connector`; a missing `case_number`): the model cannot print a default for a missing
  field.
