# fb-595.54.17: Washington Supreme Court on the shared document model

Folders: `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/supreme-court` and its nine doc types, which had one identical
typst template. Ported and committed: petition-for-rehearing (gated: XENG passes on latex and docx) and motion-brief
(latex passes XENG; docx differs on one line, request 3, so `cross_engine: report`). Each has a `typst/layout.yaml`
(RAP 18.17, RAP 10.3, RAP Form 5, cited in the court's `rules.md` as WASH-*), its typst template on
`lib/typst/xdoc.typ`, and generated `latex/` and `docx/`. The seven doc types with tables are not ported (see
"Folders that cannot match"). The court root has no template (empty `typst/` and `latex/`): nothing to generate.

Changes to the samples (usage/INPUT-*.md, body only; committed for motion-brief, the one folder of the two that had
any): pdftohtml left a space before `,` `;` `:` `)` and a closing
`.`, and after an opening quote or parenthesis ("Black’s , supra", "“ [a]nything"); the filed briefs have none, and
LibreOffice breaks at that space where typst does not, so the spaces are removed.

Changes to the layout against the old template (tier 2): the caption's column of ")" is gone (the model has no such
column; RAP Form 5 does not ask for it); the counsel block sits a fixed 64.4 pt below the caption instead of at the
foot of the page (no `v(1fr)` in the model); the `/s/` line is not underlined (request 2 below); the
signature block starts 2 in from the left margin.

## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **docx: a cover without tables is dropped.** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` emits `spec.cover` only inside `if paged`
   (contents or authorities present); a layout with a cover and no tables loses its title page in Word (typst and
   LaTeX set it). Folders: motion-brief, petition-for-rehearing (worked around: the title page is `front` blocks
   ending in `{break: true}`, page numbers from 2, which DEF-COVER-001 accepts as a counted cover).
2. **LaTeX: underlined text cannot break.** `underline: true` sets the run in one box: a `/s/ name, WSBA no.` line
   longer than the column ran 25 pt into the right margin (petition-for-rehearing p4, motion-brief p14) where
   typst and Word wrap it. Folders: all nine (worked around: no underline).
3. **Word lines run about 0.1 pt shorter than typst's.** A typst line that overflows the measure by less than
   ~0.1 pt keeps its last word in Word: motion-brief docx p8 line 10, "Br. at 31-33." (typst: "at" at 454.25 pt,
   the word ends at 504.01 against a 504 pt margin; Word: "at" at 454.13, ends at 503.9). The longer the brief,
   the likelier such a line. Folder: motion-brief (docx).
4. **A page list wider than the line.** `authorities-pages` (lib/typst/xdoc.typ) and its LaTeX and Word twins make
   the page column as wide as the list: an authority cited on twenty pages ("*D.C. v. Heller*, 554 U.S. 570
   (2008) .... 16, 17, 18, 26, 31, 35, ...") leaves its citation a column one word wide, the list runs into the
   right margin when it is wider than the line, and each engine wraps the narrow column differently. The old
   template set such a citation first and the leaders and list on a line of their own (more than five pages). Needs:
   a list that does not fit beside the citation goes on its own line(s), right-aligned after a leader, in all three
   engines. Typst tier 1 fails: DEF-TOC-001 (amicus-brief p6, answering-brief p4, brief-on-the-merits p6 and p8,
   petition-for-discretionary-review p4, petition-for-review p5) and DEF-FORM-002 (answering-brief,
   brief-on-the-merits: the list in the margin); reply-brief p6 and petition-for-writ p4 pass tier 1 but set a
   citation one word to a line.
5. **Nested `find`s lose every docx page.** petition-for-writ lists "RCW 10.77" (`find: "10.77"`) and "RCW 10.77.010"
   (`find: "10.77.010"`): both mark the same word, and docxpages.rs finds body needles in order from the one
   before, so after the first such word no later needle is found ("fb render: not found in the PDF: a23.1, a23.2,
   h3, ..."); the docx render fails. Same in petition-for-review ("GR 37" inside "GR 37(i)").
6. **typst breaks between "¶¶".** amicus-brief p30 line 11 "673, 678 ¶" / "¶ 6-7": LaTeX and Word keep "¶¶" whole.
7. **A URL breaks at a different slash.** amicus-brief p25 (footnote 4): typst breaks "…/sol3/papers." /
   "cfm?abstract_id=…", Word "…/sol3/" / "papers.cfm?…".
8. **A footnote split over two pages.** amicus-brief: LaTeX moves the line that calls note 1 (p17 line 15,
   "equipment.¹") to the next page where typst splits the note; Word puts a continued note's line on the other page
   (p23 line 15). From there every page end differs (latex p18-p33). Also reply-brief latex p24 line 17 (note 7)
   and from p25 on, petition-for-writ latex p38-p41, petition-for-review latex p11, p18-19.
