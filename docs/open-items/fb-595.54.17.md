# fb-595.54.17: Washington Supreme Court on the shared document model

Folders: `courts/ninth-circuit/washington/state/supreme-court` and its nine doc types. The nine doc folders had one
identical typst template; each now has a `typst/layout.yaml` (RAP 18.17, RAP 10.3 and Form 5, cited in the court's
`rules.md` as WASH-*), its typst template on `lib/typst/xdoc.typ`, and generated `latex/` and `docx/`
(`fb engines gen`). The court root has no template (its `typst/` and `latex/` are empty): nothing to generate.

Changes to the samples (usage/INPUT-*.md, body only): pdftohtml left a space before `,` `;` `:` `)` and a closing
`.`, and after an opening quote or parenthesis ("Black’s , supra", "“ [a]nything"); the filed briefs have none, and
LibreOffice breaks at that space where typst does not, so the spaces are removed. Amicus brief: a quoted statute
paragraph opening "(a)" is escaped `\(a)` (pandoc read it as an ordered list; cmarker does not).

Changes to the layout against the old template (tier 2): the caption's column of ")" is gone (the model has no such
column; RAP Form 5 does not ask for it); the counsel block sits a fixed 64.4 pt below the caption instead of at the
foot of the page (no `v(1fr)` in the model); the `/s/` line is not underlined (see "LaTeX underline" below); the
signature block starts 2 in from the left margin.

## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **docx: a cover without tables is dropped.** `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` emits `spec.cover` only inside `if paged`
   (contents or authorities present); a layout with a cover and no tables loses its title page in Word (typst and
   LaTeX set it). Folders: motion-brief, petition-for-rehearing (worked around: the title page is `front` blocks
   ending in `{break: true}`, page numbers from 2, which DEF-COVER-001 accepts as a counted cover).
2. **LaTeX: underlined text cannot break.** `underline: true` sets the run in one box: a `/s/ name, WSBA no.` line
   longer than the column ran 25 pt into the right margin (petition-for-rehearing p4, motion-brief p14) where
   typst and Word wrap it. Folders: all nine (worked around: no underline).
3. **Word lines run about 0.1 pt shorter than typst's.** A typst line that overflows the measure by less than
   ~0.1 pt keeps its last word in Word: motion-brief docx p8 line 10, "Br. at 31-33." (typst: "at" at 454.25 pt,
   the word ends at 504.01 against a 504 pt margin; Word: "at" at 454.13, ends at 503.9). The longer the brief,
   the likelier such a line. Folders: motion-brief (docx), and see the per-folder table.
