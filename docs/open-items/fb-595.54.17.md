How the seven table folders were ported:

- **Pass-1 request 4 (a page list wider than the line) is still not in the model**, but keyed authorities with
  `passim` now are. Four of the nine Washington filers set an authority cited on many pages as *passim* (the amicus,
  answering, discretionary-review and writ exemplars), so the samples' authorities are now keyed
  (`[text](toa:key)` links in the body, generated from each entry's former `find`/`finds`: longest first,
  word-bounded, a find that fills an italic span takes the span into the link) and the layouts set
  `passim: 6`. The groups are the court's (Cases, Constitutional Provisions, Statutes, Rules, Regulations, Other
  Authorities); cases alphabetical (RAP 10.3(a)(2)), the others in the filer's order, by an explicit `sort` rank: the
  engines' default keys collate differently (typst put "N.Y. State …" and "D.C. v. Heller" before "Nielsen" and
  "Dabbs", LaTeX and Word after). A section cite inside a chapter cite ("RCW 10.77.086") now marks the section only.
- Pass-1 request 5 (nested finds lose every docx page) is gone with the links.
- The caption's designation is indented 0.5 in (was 1.4 in) so "Plaintiffs/Respondents," fits unbroken: LaTeX cannot
  break after its slash where typst and Word do.
- Samples (body only): pdftohtml's stray spaces removed (before `, ; : ) ] .` and after `“ ( ‘ [`, "Co .," and
  "Servs*.,"), double spaces collapsed, paragraphs split by a page break joined, merged headings split
  (reply-brief "II. ARGUMENT A. …", discretionary-review "A. … 1. Purpose …"), petition-for-review's subheadings
  and issues rebuilt from a list-plus-quotation split, "----" escaped so every engine prints the filer's four hyphens,
  "4.16.300- .310" as filed.

### Shared-model requests still open (for a second serial lane)

A. **Line-fit tolerance** (the open item "the line-fit tolerance at a line typst fills to within hundredths of a
   point"): five folders here, each one line. typst ends "awaiting" at 503.987 pt (petition-for-writ p38, latex drops
   it), "and" at 503.994 (brief-on-the-merits p74, latex drops it); typst breaks before a word that would end at
   504.02 to 504.04 (petition-for-review p11 "after", answering-brief p23 "City", brief-on-the-merits p32 "Thus,"),
   which LaTeX and Word keep. Measure 360 pt (left 144, right 504).
B. **A hyphen before a full stop.** "RCW 4.16.300-.310": typst never breaks between "-" and "." (Unicode LB13, no
   break before IS), LaTeX and Word break after the hyphen. petition-for-discretionary-review p7 line 13, p22 line 7.
   Needs the same rule in every engine (no break after a hyphen that a "." follows in LaTeX and Word, or the break in
   typst).
C. **docx: a needle that ends an italic span has no text after it.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes a mark's needle as the
   find plus `xd-after`, which is empty when the find closes an emphasis (`*Hand*, 192 …`): the needle is "hand" and
   docxpages.rs matches "healt**h and**" a page early (petition-for-writ: *State v. Hand* listed on p2 for p3).
   Worked around in the samples (the link takes the emphasis: `[*Hand*](toa:k)`); a find inside a longer italic span
   (`*State v. [Hand](toa:k)*`) still has an empty tail.
D. **typst: a find inside another entry's find word** (no longer used here, recorded for the `find` form): with
   "10.77" and "10.77.086" both entries, typst marked the "10.77" of "RCW 10.77.086(1)." (a paragraph's last word, at
   a page end) on the next page; LaTeX and Word on its own page (petition-for-writ, pass-2 draft).
E. **docx: a `{fill}` beside a `repeat` column overfills the page.** A cover with a caption column of ")"
   (`{width: 18, repeat: ")"}`) and counsel pushed to the foot by `{fill: 1}` runs to two pages in Word (the second
   counsel group alone on page 2); either alone fits, and typst and LaTeX set one page. So the Washington layouts drop
   the ")" column (RAP Form 5 does not require it).
F. **Keyed sort keys collate differently** without an explicit `sort` (see above): typst orders "n.y. state" before
   "nielsen", LaTeX and Word after (the documented key, lower-case letters, digits and spaces, gives the latter).

Tier 2, accepted: a cover with many counsel (reply-brief, brief-on-the-merits: eleven names) sets them two to a row and
wraps the longer bold names; one group under another would run the cover to two pages. The brief title wraps in the
caption's right column. A row whose text fills the line gets one leader dot.
