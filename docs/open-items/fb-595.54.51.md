## Shared-model requests

1. **Bare URLs and over-long words.** A long URL in a footnote breaks in a different place in each engine: typst
   after a `/` and, when a part still does not fit, inside the word (an emergency break: `https://mississi` /
   `ppitoday.org/...`); LaTeX only at a hyphen, never after a `/` and never inside a word, so the URL overflows the
   right margin; Word after a `/` before a letter (lib/docx/xdoc.lua adds a ZWSP there) and at hyphens. Needs one set
   of break opportunities inside a URL for all three engines (after `/` and the other URL separators) and the same
   emergency break of a word wider than the line (typst's), in lib/latex/xengine.lua, lib/docx/xdoc.lua and
   lib/typst/xengine.typ. Folders: Mississippi motion-brief (p5: footnotes 2-6, XENG-002/003 differ from p5 on),
   petition-for-writ (p7 l21 `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4M5D1BupC2U&t=38s`, p17 l18-20; latex p17 right edge
   584.68 pt > 576: DEF-FORM-002 FAIL; docx p1 l23: an e-mail address in a cover column of a third of the page),
   petition-for-rehearing (footnote 2, p3).
2. **A cover without tables.** `cover` with `page_number.front` sets the cover unnumbered and the body from 1 in typst,
   but lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits the cover only when the layout has `contents` or `authorities` (the `paged` branch: the
   docx render starts with the body), and lib/latex/xdoc.lua returns to arabic numbers only after the tables (the body
   is numbered ii, iii ..). Mississippi motion-brief therefore sets its cover as `front` blocks closed by
   `{break: true}` with `page_number.from: 2` (the cover counted as page 1, the body numbered from 2); the old
   template and the petition-for-writ exemplar number the body from 1 (tier 2, OPEN). With this feature the three
   Mississippi motions and petitions go back to `cover` + `front: lower-roman`. Folders: Mississippi motion-brief,
   petition-for-rehearing, petition-for-writ.
3. **A footnote split across pages.** typst splits a long footnote over two pages (Mississippi petition-for-rehearing
   p2, footnote 1, 19 lines); LaTeX keeps it whole and moves the line with its reference to the next page (21 pages
   to typst's 20, page ends differ from p2). Word splits as typst does there (its first difference is p14 line 23).
4. **Typographic quotes inside emphasis in a heading (LaTeX).** Mississippi petition-for-rehearing, heading
   `## III. *The Mississippi Constitution’s Ban on “Cruel or Unusual* Punishment”`: the LaTeX PDF reads `''Cruel`
   where the markdown has `“Cruel` (an opening double quote that starts inside the italic span set as two
   apostrophes): DEF-CONT-001 FAIL on latex (block 56). typst and docx are right.
5. **The Mississippi table of authorities.** The four Mississippi briefs (amicus-brief, answering-brief,
   brief-on-the-merits, reply-brief) do not use the shared schema (`authorities: [{heading, entries: [{cite,
   find}]}]`): their frontmatter keys each authority (`authorities: {aa: {kind: case|statute|rule|other, name,
   cite}}`), the body marks each citation with a link `[text](toa:aa)` (11, 43, 92 and 140 marks), the template
   groups by `kind` under CASES / STATUTES / RULES / OTHER AUTHORITIES, sorts each group by name, italicizes a case
   name and prints "passim" for seven or more pages. xdoc's authorities would fail on the keyed map, and the INPUTs
   cannot be rewritten into the shared schema here (no new fields). Needs either the keyed schema with `toa:` link
   marks, kind groups, sorting and "passim" in the model, or a decision to convert these four samples to the shared
   schema (a decision for David: the samples are transcriptions of the filed exemplars). The rest of these briefs
   fits the motion-brief layout (cover with contents and authorities numbered i, ii .., `front: lower-roman`), and
   they carry long URLs too (request 1).
6. **Arial (Liberation Sans) in LaTeX.** Fla. R. App. P. 9.045(b) allows only Arial 14-point or Bookman Old Style
   14-point; the seven Florida 6th DCA templates set Liberation Sans. `fb engines gen` refuses it
   (tools/fb/src/enginegen/gen.rs `latex_face`: "no LaTeX face for `Liberation Sans`"); Bookman (TeX Gyre Bonum,
   vendored) is not there either. Needs a LaTeX face for Arial with Arial's advances (helvet/uhvr carries Helvetica's,
   which are Arial's) and its ellipsis advance, and `list_width` (lib/docx/xdoc.lua) for that face.
7. **Headings numbered by the template** (already in "Not yet in the model"): the Florida templates number argument
   headings I., A., 1. themselves (`point-numbering`); the markdown carries no numerals.
8. **Space that fills the page** (already in "Not yet in the model"): the Florida covers set `v(1fr)` above and below
   the title so the counsel block sits at the foot of the cover.
