## Colorado shared-model blocker

All five Colorado brief templates require a fixed-size box with content anchored at its foot under C.A.R. 32(d),
plus a certificate-of-compliance position switch. The box is at least 2 1/2 inches wide and 1 3/4 inches tall; its
“COURT USE ONLY”, “Case Number:” and case number contents sit within a ruled box on the cover. Current model blocks
cannot express the fixed height, vertical rules, or bottom alignment. A shared model feature and tests are required
before porting; do not drop the mandated area to force engine generation.

## Remaining Kansas tier-2 notes

The prior review recorded these open convention differences and they remain: Kansas certificate of service is a
frontmatter closing block and is not listed in the TOC; the certificate repeats the full signature block; and cover
vertical-fill gaps were modeled as fixed 72 pt gaps. Shared-model pagination increased the opening brief from 31 to
34 pages and the reply brief from 9 to 10. These are conventions/style differences, not failed tier-1 checks.

Kansas exemplars are the one filed example per document type listed in each court-root `usage/SOURCE.md`; the current
folder-level sources identify the corresponding filer and filing URL. Colorado remains unchanged pending the model
feature.
