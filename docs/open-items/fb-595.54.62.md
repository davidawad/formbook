## Shared-model requests (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82)

1. **A ruled box of fixed size with content set at its foot** (already listed in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md "Not yet in the
   model"). Folders: all five Colorado Court of Appeals doc folders
   (`courts/tenth-circuit/colorado/state/court-of-appeals/*-brief`). Evidence: C.A.R. 32(d) (Rule Change 2005(6),
   cited in that court's rules.md) requires on the first page a "Court Use Only" area at least 2 1/2 in wide and
   1 3/4 in long; the current typst cover draws it as `box(width: 2.6in, height: 1.8in, stroke: 0.75pt)` beside the
   court, lower court and party column, with "COURT USE ONLY", "Case Number:" and the number bottom-aligned inside.
   The model's blocks (text, rule, columns, each, group, when, break) cannot draw the box's vertical sides or fix its
   height, and dropping the box breaks a tier-1 (mandated form) item. The Colorado templates are left as they are
   (typst only, no layout.yaml); the cover also uses `v(...)` gaps and a counsel grid that the model can express
   (`columns: {each: counsel, per_row: 2}`), so the port is mechanical once the box exists. The certificate of
   compliance's "front"/"body" position switch (`compliance.position`) is a second need there: a block placed before
   the tables or as the first body page by a frontmatter value (`when` covers presence, not a value).

2. **Line breaks inside a long word (bare URLs).** Folder:
   `courts/tenth-circuit/kansas/state/court-of-appeals/amicus-brief`. Evidence: the INPUT (a transcription) carries
   bare URLs in body paragraphs, e.g. `http://www.acog.org/~/media/Statements%20of%20Policy/Public/2013LegislativeInterference.pdf`.
   typst breaks it after `Statements%20` (p3 line 17), LibreOffice after `media/`, pdflatex not at all (the line runs
   into the right margin: DEF-FORM-002 fails, body right edge 585.62 pt > 540). XENG-002/003 fail on latex and docx
   from p3 line 17 (p15, p16 page starts follow). Wanted: one rule for break points inside a word longer than the
   line (after `/`, and inside `%20`?) applied by xengine in all three engines; LaTeX must break there too. Until
   then the folder is `cross_engine: report` and ships typst and docx only; the latex engine is BLOCKED on
   DEF-FORM-002 (its generated `latex/` is not committed, so `fb engines gen --check` reports it missing).
   Writing the URLs as `<...>` autolinks was tried: pdflatex fails (no `\url`), and DEF-CONT-001 then fails on the
   angle brackets.

3. **An authority mark whose `find` spans a line or page break.** Folder: kansas opening-brief (worked around).
   typst puts the mark after the last word of the find; lib/docx/xdoc.lua's needle lands at the find's start. With
   `find: "92 S. Ct. 1921"` broken over pp. 25/26, typst listed p. 26 and docx p. 25 (XENG-003, p4 line 3). The
   INPUT's find is now `Adams v. Williams` (on one line); the model should mark one place (the find's first word, the
   page a reader looks at) in every engine.

4. **pandoc reads paragraphs that start with `(1)` or `a.` as lists** (the body is read as `markdown+smart`, with
   fancy_lists), cmarker as paragraphs: the list label box then let LaTeX and Word fit a word more (opening brief p19,
   17.7 pt). Worked around in the Kansas INPUTs by escaping the marker (`\(1)`, `a\.`); the pandoc reader of the
   shared model should drop `fancy_lists` (CommonMark list markers only, as cmarker).

5. **A frontmatter hard break (`\` + newline, or two spaces + newline) in a value** reads as a line break in typst
   (cmarker) but is lost in the docx engine. Worked around in the Kansas INPUTs with the model's `<br>`; the shared
   inline reader could map a LineBreak to the same line split.
