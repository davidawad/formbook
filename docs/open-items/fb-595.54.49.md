# fb-595.54.49: open items (generated engines, NC Superior Court and SC Supreme Court)

Bead fb-595.54.49 moves the typst templates of the folders below onto the shared document model
(`typst/layout.yaml` + `lib/typst/xdoc.typ`) and generates their latex and docx engines with `fb engines gen`
(docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Generated engines"). This file lists what the batch could not do inside its own folders: the
shared-model features some folders need (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82) and the folders that cannot match yet.
A folder listed here keeps `params.cross_engine: report` in its `eval.yaml`.

## Court roots

`courts/fourth-circuit/north-carolina/state/superior-court` and `courts/fourth-circuit/south-carolina/state/supreme-court`
hold no template (their `typst/` and `latex/` are empty, `usage/` holds only FILED-EXAMPLEs and SOURCE.md): there is
nothing to move onto the model or to render, so nothing was changed there.

## Shared-model requests

### 1. Break points inside URLs, the same in every engine

- Feature: a bare URL in the body or a footnote must break at the same places in typst, LaTeX and Word. Today typst
  breaks after `//`, `/`, `&` (its own URL rules), LibreOffice after `?` and `/`, and LaTeX not at all (the whole URL is
  one word under xengine, so it runs into the right margin: DEF-FORM-002 fails on the latex engine).
- Folders: NC `opening-brief`, `amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `motion-brief` (and any sample with a long URL).
- Evidence (first differing line, typst vs latex/docx, `fb diff --engines`):
  - NC opening-brief p7 line 21: typst `... archived at https://appellate.nccourts.org/orders.php?t=P&court=l&id=444272&pdf=l&` /
    `a=0&docket=l&dev=l. The Supreme Court ...`; docx breaks after `orders.php?`, latex sets the URL on a line of its
    own and runs 7 pt into the margin (p21 of the latex render: 71 pt, `Final_Order_11_28_2016.pdf.`).
  - NC amicus-brief latex p34: `https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/HAVA%20Administrative%20Complaints/...` ends at
    x = 618.7 pt (margin 540).
  - NC answering-brief p5 line 18: typst ends the line after `archived at https://`; latex and docx carry the URL whole.
  - NC motion-brief p11 line 20: footnote 2, typst `... Available at https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nc/charlotte/politics/2021/11/04/redistricting-in-n-c`
    / `—new-maps-approved–favoring-gop`; latex and docx break elsewhere.
- Suggested fix: in xengine (all three engines) a URL gets explicit break opportunities (after `/`, `?`, `&`, `=`,
  `-` inside a word that starts with `http`), typst's own URL breaks switched off.

### 2. A footnote reference followed by punctuation

- Feature: typst keeps a footnote mark and the punctuation after it (`Voters")[^2];`) with the word before it; LaTeX
  and LibreOffice let the line break after the mark, before the `;`, so their line holds one word more.
- Folders: NC `amicus-brief`.
- Evidence: NC amicus-brief p7 line 8: typst ends the line after `"Impacted`, latex and docx after `Voters")` (mark 2,
  then `; and (2) ...` on the next line).

### 3. Smart quotes: cmarker and pandoc read an unbalanced straight quote differently

- Feature: a straight `"` nested inside a curly quotation (`"No department ... and thus "the harm ...`) is an opening
  quote to cmarker (typst) and a closing one to pandoc (latex, docx), which also takes the space after it, so the
  words differ and the line breaks move.
- Folders: NC `reply-brief`.
- Evidence: NC reply-brief p8 line 23: typst `and thus "the harm Plaintiffs would` (an opening quote), latex
  `and thus"the harm` (a closing quote, no space).

### 4. A frontmatter or body string that a markdown reader takes for a list

- Feature: cmarker (typst) and pandoc (latex, docx) disagree on what starts a list. pandoc's `fancy_lists` reads a
  paragraph that opens with `(d) ` or `(2008) ` as an ordered list (and xdoc.lua then stops: "a quotation holds
  paragraphs only"), cmarker as text; cmarker reads a frontmatter value that opens with `* ` as a bullet list and
  typst prints nothing for it, pandoc prints the text.
- Folders: SC `petition-for-rehearing` (`> (d) who are cohabiting ...`), SC `petition-for-writ` (`(2008) (quoting
  Sloan ...` and the counsel line `* pro hac vice applications pending`).
- Done here: those three strings are escaped in the INPUTs (`\(d)`, `\(2008)`, `"\\* pro hac ..."`); every engine prints
  the same text as before. Suggested fix: the pandoc reader without `fancy_lists` (CommonMark list starts), and
  `md()` in lib/typst/xdoc.typ reading a frontmatter value as inline markdown only.

### 5. `when` on a field set to `null`

- Feature: `when: certificate_of_service` with `certificate_of_service: null` is false in typst (none) and true in
  lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua, which then fails on the missing `certificate_of_service.text`.
- Folders: SC `motion-brief`. Done here: the `certificate_of_service: null` line is dropped from the INPUT (no
  certificate either way).

### 6. `upper` in the authorities `group` style

- Feature: typst sets the group headings of the table of authorities in capitals with `group: {upper: true}`; the
  latex and docx engines do not (SC amicus-brief p3 line 2: typst `CASES`, latex and docx `Cases`).
- Folders: SC `amicus-brief`. Done here: `upper` dropped from its group style.

### 7. "passim" in the table of authorities

- Feature: an authority cited on many pages lists them all; when the list is wider than the page column, the column
  grows to the list and the citation is set a word to a line (DEF-TOC-001: "page reference alone on a line"). The
  old SC template printed "passim" from seven pages.
- Folders: SC `brief-on-the-merits` (*Opternative*, p5 of the typst render: some 30 pages).

### 8. Characters pdflatex cannot set

- Feature: the latex engine (pdflatex, T1) stops on characters outside its encodings, e.g. U+2502 `│` in a firm
  name; typst and Word set them from the font.
- Folders: SC `reply-brief` (`SMITH│ROBINSON` in the counsel and signature blocks).

## Needs a decision: SC INPUTs transcribed one printed line per paragraph

`answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`, `reply-brief` and `petition-for-writ` of the SC Supreme Court were transcribed
with a blank line after every printed line of the filing (599, 769, 294 and 381 paragraphs of 79 to 97 characters on
average), so every line is a paragraph of its own. The old template hid it (a single-line paragraph is 1.1 em plus a
0.6 em paragraph gap, about 19 pt a line); on the model each line is a double-spaced paragraph with a first-line
indent, so the renders run long (answering 34 -> 51 pages, over its 50-page DEF-LEN-001 limit; merits 47 -> 71) and a
quotation or citation that spans two lines is split across paragraphs (the reply's docx loses its order, DEF-CONT-001).
Re-joining the lines is a re-transcription against each FILED-EXAMPLE (where each paragraph ends cannot be read from
the INPUT), not a layout change, so this batch left `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits` and `reply-brief` on their
old templates, unchanged. `petition-for-writ` is ported and gated (its typst, latex and docx agree and pass tier 1),
but its pages show the same line-per-paragraph indents. Question for David: re-transcribe these four INPUTs (paragraphs
re-joined from the filed PDFs) before they are ported?
