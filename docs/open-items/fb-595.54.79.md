## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

### 1. LaTeX: a line break after a slash inside a word

- Folders: `ninth-circuit/northern-mariana-islands/state/supreme-court/answering-brief`.
- Evidence: INPUT-answering-brief, page 21 line 28. Typst ends the line after "Bisom/" and puts "Sorensen." on the
  next line (UAX #14 allows a break after a solidus). lib/docx/xdoc.lua puts a ZWSP after a slash inside a word
  (`([^%s/])/(%a)`), so docx breaks there too. lib/latex/xengine.lua has no such rule, so pdfLaTeX keeps
  "Bisom/Sorensen." whole. 847/859 lines are identical, and page 22 starts one line later.
- Needed: the same rule in the LaTeX filter (an `\allowbreak{}` or empty discretionary after the slash), with a
  test. Once it lands, drop `cross_engine_latex: report` from the folder's `eval.yaml`.

### 2. The page number in the header (top right)

- Folders: `ninth-circuit/oregon/state/court-of-appeals/amicus-brief` (and every Oregon Court of Appeals doc type
  that follows ORAP 5.05(3)(c)).
- Rule: ORCTAPP-FORM-007 (ORAP 5.05(3)(c)): page numbers at the top, within 3/8 inch of the top edge. The current
  typst sets `header-ascent: 0.375in` and `header: align(right, counter(page).display())` from page 2 on.
- What the model lacks: `page_number` is a footer line box only. It has `align` and `from`, but no `position: top`
  or a distance from the edge.
- Needed: `page_number: {position: top, ...}` in all three engines (typst header, LaTeX head, Word header).

### 3. A bordered caption box, and contents on the caption page

- Folders: `second-circuit/new-york/state/supreme-court/complaint`, `.../motion-brief`.
- Rule: NYSUPCT-FORM-006, NYSUPCT-CONT-001 (22 NYCRR 202.5(a)): the index number to the right of the caption.
  Both templates draw the party box with a top, right and bottom rule (`grid(stroke: (x, y) => if x == 0 {(top:
  0.75pt, right: 0.75pt, bottom: 0.75pt)})`), with "-against-" between the sides and the index number and title in
  the right column.
- What the model lacks: `{rule}` is horizontal only, and `columns` has no cell borders. Also, the table of contents
  (`toc: true` in both INPUTs) follows the caption on page 1. In the model, `contents` always opens its own page
  before `front`.
- These folders already have a gated hand-written cross-engine docx (`docx/nysupct-*.lua`, fb-595.68.37,
  `.check-docx.txt` PASS). Moving them onto the model now would lose the box.

### 4. A word count computed by the template

- Folders: `second-circuit/new-york/state/supreme-court/motion-brief` (`count-words`, NYSUPCT-LEN-001/-002, 22
  NYCRR 202.8-b: the certification states the count of the body, without the caption, tables and signature block).
  The INPUT has no `word_count`. This is the same gap as fb-595.54.34 request 2.

### 5. A conditional style, and a running header

- Folders: `seventh-circuit/indiana/state/supreme-court/motion-brief`.
- Evidence: the cover's caption is a list of `caption_rows: [{left, indent?, right}]`. Each row is the party text,
  a ")" column and the docket text. A row with `indent: true` sets its party text 0.6 in in ("Appellant-Defendant,").
  `when` only tests whether a field is present, so the model cannot set one block for rows with `indent` and another
  for rows without it. A style cannot read its value from a field either. The template also prints `running_header`
  at the top of every page after the first (IND-FORM-007 convention). This INPUT has no running header, but the
  template's contract does.
- Needed: `unless: <field>` (or `when` with a value), or a style length read from a field. Also a page header from
  a frontmatter list, from page 2. The ")" column itself works as a three-column row while each row is one line.
  The model's "column of ')'" gap only matters when a row wraps.

### 6. Placeholders in the body, and front matter before the tables

- Folders: `tenth-circuit/oklahoma/state/court-of-civil-appeals/answering-brief`.
- Evidence: the markdown is split at a `{{contents}}` line. Before it is front matter on roman pages after the
  cover (the identity of parties and counsel, `{{identity}}`). After it come the contents, the index of authorities
  and then the body from page 1. `{{signature}}` and `{{signer}}` lines place signature blocks inside the body (each
  certificate is kept with its signer). The index reads `authorities: [{group, items: [{name, cite, find, italic}]}]`,
  not the model's `[{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`. Short paragraphs (under 50 characters) are set flush left.
- Needed: blocks placed by a body marker, and a numbered body part before the tables (as fb-595.54.34 request 3).
  Changing the INPUT to the model's authorities shape would change the folder's markdown contract.

### 7. Doubled section and paragraph signs ("¶¶ 15-20")

- Folders: `third-circuit/delaware/state/court-of-chancery/opening-brief` (INPUT-opening-brief-2 has many "Compl.
  ¶¶").
- Evidence: on the model, typst ends p10 line 16 with "(¶" and starts line 17 with "¶ 17-19)". xengine keeps only a
  single sign with its number, so typst can break between the two signs. LaTeX and Word keep "¶¶" whole and end the
  line after it: docx differs at p10 line 16, and 1566/1585 lines are identical. The NY Supreme Court complaint
  template works around this locally (`show regex("[§¶]{2} \\S+"): it => box(it)`).
- Needed: a doubled sign kept together, and with its number, in all three engines (lib/typst/xengine.typ,
  lib/pandoc/xengine.lua).

### 8. A list inside a quotation

- Evidence: `fb render --engine latex` and `--engine docx` stop with "xdoc: a quotation holds paragraphs only"

### 9. A footnote that moves a line to the next page (typst and LaTeX disagree)

- Folders: `third-circuit/delaware/state/court-of-chancery/opening-brief` (INPUT-opening-brief-2).
- Evidence: typst ends p22 one line early. "contacted during the first go-shop period, and received no expression of

## Model defects found while porting (not worked around)

- **An empty column in LaTeX.** `columns: [{width: 234, blocks: []}, {blocks: [...]}]` (a signature column at the
  right) sets the right column's text 234 pt too far left in the generated LaTeX: the left-edge diagnostic shows
  -234.00 pt on every line of the column. The breaks still match, so XENG passes and the defect goes unnoticed.
  Seen on the Ohio Court of Claims draft (reverted).
- **An empty text block.** `{text: "{role}"}` with `role: ""` gives an empty paragraph. Typst sets it with no height,
  while LibreOffice gives it a line, so docx moved the rest of page 1 down 41.37 pt (Ohio draft, the "v." party row).
  Either a value of "" should give no paragraph, as with a missing optional field, or every engine should set an
  empty line.

## Decisions (for David)

- **Ohio Court of Claims, motion-brief: the 15-page limit against true double spacing.** L.C.C.R. 4(A)(1)
  (OHIOCTCL-FORM-001) keeps the top 1½ inches of every page blank. EFILE § VII(D) (OHIOCTCL-FORM-002) asks for
  double spacing, and `eval.yaml` sets `page_limit: 15` (OHIOCTCL-LEN-001, counted over every page). The current
  template passes at 15 pages, but only because its body pitch is 22.7 pt (`leading: 1.2em` on typst's default
  edges), which is not double. The filed exemplar sets 27.6 pt (Word's double at 12 pt), with a 1-inch top on
  pages 2 and later, on 16 pages. On the model, at 27.6 pt and with 1½ inches on every page, the render is 18
  pages, which fails `page_limit`. Which should govern: (a) the 1½-inch top on page 1 only, as the exemplar does
  (the model can do this with a `front` block's `above`); (b) a page limit that leaves out the certificate of
  service; (c) a shorter or fictional INPUT? With (a) and the certificate excluded, the brief itself would still be
  about 16 pages, as filed. The folder is left unchanged until this is decided.
