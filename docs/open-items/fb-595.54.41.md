## Shared-model requests (for a second serial lane)

A. **A line may end after a slash inside a word in LaTeX** ("counsel/parties", "and/or").
   - Folders: `amicus-brief` (latex).
   - Evidence: the certificate of service's frontmatter paragraph "... served to all counsel/parties of record" ends a
     line after "counsel/" in typst and Word (page 19, line 3); pdfLaTeX has no break there and carries "counsel/parties"
     to the next line. lib/docx/xdoc.lua already writes a break after a slash inside a word before a letter
     ("CM/ECF"); lib/latex has no counterpart (an `\allowbreak` after such a slash).
B. **Word's line fit with TeX Gyre Bonum.** LibreOffice sets a Bonum line up to 1.16 pt wider than typst does (80 of
   383 lines over 0.5 pt in amicus-brief, 52 of 536 in reply-brief), so a line typst fills to within a few tenths of
   a point ends a word early in Word.
   - Folders: `amicus-brief` (page 8 line 13: typst ends at 539.60 of 540 pt), `reply-brief` (page 2 line 11, a
     contents row; page 19 line 11: typst ends at 539.88 pt).
   - Evidence: `fb diff --engines` width diagnostics; the same family as the open "line-fit tolerance" item of
     fb-595.54.82, here wider because the drift per glyph is larger for Bonum.
C. **typst sets a footnote mark in Liberation Sans as the face's superior figure.** `set super(size: 0.58em)` in
   lib/typst/xengine.typ leaves `typographic: true`, so typst sets Liberation Sans's own superscript glyphs (the
   footnote numbers are 1.5 pt wider than the 58 % digits LaTeX and Word set: width diagnostic -1.52 pt on every note
   line), and pdftotext joins the mark to a word that ends in a period ("227-242).4") where the other engines' mark is
   a word of its own.
   - Folders: `petition-for-review` (page 17 line 1, page 26 line 9); any Liberation Sans folder with a note after a
     period.
   - Evidence: with `typographic: false` (tried locally, not committed) the docx render of petition-for-review passes
     1293/1293 lines and every page end; LaTeX then differs only by request D.
D. (Already open in fb-595.54.82: "typst setting a note on another page than its mark".) petition-for-review page 26:
   typst keeps the line that anchors note 6 on page 26 and sets the note on page 27; LaTeX moves the line with its
   note, and pages 27 to 33 end a line apart.
E. **A page break and fills that a frontmatter field switches.** The old motion template set the caption, title and
   counsel as a page of their own unless `cover_page: false` (its INPUT sets false). `front` accepts fills only before
   a top-level `{break: true}` (xdoc.typ `check-fills`), so a `{when: cover_page, blocks: [fill, .., break]}` stops the
   render. The layout sets the `cover_page: false` form only.
   - Folders: `motion-brief`.

(Opening-brief's docx difference is Word's footnote area, already open in fb-595.54.82: LibreOffice keeps one body line
more above note 1 on page 21, and every later page starts a line early.)
