Folders: `courts/tenth-circuit/colorado/state/supreme-court` and its eight doc types (amicus-brief,
answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits, motion-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review, petition-for-review,

None of these folders was moved onto the shared model in this batch. Each keeps its own typst template and
`params.cross_engine` stays unset (report). No latex or docx engine was generated.

## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

### 1. A ruled box of fixed size: the "Court Use Only" area (tier 1)

- Folders: all eight doc types listed above. Their typst templates are one template, with only the cover's
  compliance statement and the authority row differing.
- Rule: C.A.R. 32(d), rules.md row COLO-CONT-001 (M). Page 1 must have, on the right, a "Court Use Only" area
  at least 2½ inches wide and 1¾ inches long, with the case number under it.
- Current typst: `box(width: 2.6in, height: 1.8in, stroke: 0.75pt, inset: 8pt, align(bottom)[...])` in a
  `grid(columns: (1fr, 2.6in))` beside the caption (`<doctype>/typst/colo-<doctype>.typ`, `cover`).
- Evidence: in `usage/FILED-EXAMPLE-answering-brief.pdf` page 1 (and every FILED-EXAMPLE in the court root's
  `usage/`), the whole caption is a ruled table. The left column has cells for the court, the lower court, the
  parties, counsel and the title. The right column has a tall cell with "▲COURT USE ONLY▲" at its foot and a
  cell with the case number.
- What the model lacks: `{rule}` only draws a horizontal line. A block has no vertical stroke and no fixed or
  minimum height. Columns have no cell borders. A cover written in the current vocabulary would either lose the
  box (a tier-1 failure of COLO-CONT-001) or fake its height with empty lines, which is a forced break.
- Needed: a bordered block of fixed (or minimum) width and height, with its content aligned to the top or
  bottom, in all three engines. In LaTeX that is a `\fbox`/minipage of fixed height. In Word it is a table cell
  with an exact row height and borders. Ideally `columns` would also take cell borders, so the whole ruled
  caption table of the exemplars can be expressed (tier 2).
- The docs already list this gap ("Not yet in the model": a ruled box of fixed size; "Recommended fan-out":
  Colorado and Utah need the ruled case-number box).

### 2. Smaller gaps in the same folders (to check once item 1 lands)

- The certificate of compliance goes right behind the cover (roman page) when `compliance.position: front`
  (petition-for-discretionary-review), and otherwise is the first body page. `when` tests only whether a
  field is present, not its value. This might be expressible with `front` blocks plus a frontmatter change,
  but the checklist forbids new fields.
- `compliance.statement` replaces the word-count sentence when given (amicus-brief, petition-for-writ,
  petition-for-discretionary-review). This is an either/or between two texts, and the model's `optional` covers
  only one of them.
