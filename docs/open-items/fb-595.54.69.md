## Shared-model requests

Open for a third serial lane: 2, 9 (narrowed), 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17 (third pass). 7 and 13 landed with lane 2;
1, 4, 5, 6 and 8 with fb-595.54.82; 3 in part (request 10 is what remains of it). The texts of 1 to 8 are as the first
pass recorded them.

1. **Widow and orphan control in a paragraph with a footnote mark.** lib/latex/xdoc.lua and lib/docx/xdoc.lua turn
   widow and orphan control off for such a paragraph (docs/EVAL-LOOP.md: typst may move its last line alone with the
   note); lib/typst/xdoc.typ keeps typst's default widow and orphan costs for it, so where the paragraph's first or
   last line would stand alone at a page end typst carries a line over and the other two engines do not.
   Folders: mississippi/.../opening-brief (typst p5, body page 1: the paragraph "On the afternoon of March 25" with
   footnote 1; typst moves its first line to page 2, LaTeX and Word keep it on page 1; every later page shifts:
   XENG-001 22 vs 21 pages), mississippi/.../amicus-brief (typst p6, body page 3: the paragraph "Bias is a term used"
   with footnotes 12 and 13; typst moves two lines to page 4, LaTeX and Word one; p7-p9 differ). Either engine side
   could change: typst without widow and orphan costs in a footnoted paragraph, or the other engines keeping them
   (with the footnote-widow case of xengine.typ `footnote-widows: false`).
2. **No break after an opening bracket followed by a space.** typst (UAX #14, OP SP*) never breaks a line after
   "( "; LaTeX does. Folder: mississippi/.../answering-brief, latex p7 line 18: the INPUT (a transcription) reads
   "evidence.. ( R. Vol. 2, pp. 28 - 29)"; typst ends the line after "evidence..", LaTeX after "(". Word matches
   typst (docx gates). A shared rule (an unbreakable space after an opening bracket in lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua) fixes it.
3. **A footnote split over a page end.** typst, LaTeX and LibreOffice split a long footnote at different places (or
   move its anchor line), and LibreOffice's PDF reads the continued note inside the body text.
   Folder: mississippi/.../reply-brief, p8 (body page 4): footnote 4 ("The regulations governing water quality
   certifications ..."): typst splits it after its third line, LaTeX moves the anchor line to page 9, Word splits it
   elsewhere; the docx render then fails DEF-CONT-001 (body blocks 24 and 25 read with the note's continuation inside
   them). The folder's docx engine is not delivered (docx/ and the docx OUTPUTs removed; `fb engines gen --check`
   reports the two docx files missing on purpose) until the model sets split notes alike.
4. **Two authorities that mark the same word.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes one needle per mark; when two entries' `find`
   strings match the same word ("49-17-29" and "49-17-29(3)(c)"), the needles are identical, the second is searched
   after the first and falls back to its short text further on, and every later needle is lost ("fb render: not
   found in the PDF: a24.1, ..."). typst and LaTeX list both. Worked around in mississippi/.../reply-brief by listing a
   section once (its subsections dropped from the table: § 49-17-29, R. 1.3.1(B)(2), R. 1.3.2, R. 1.3.4); docxpages.rs
   could search a needle again from the same position.
5. **A cover with no tables in the docx engine.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits `cover` only when the layout has `contents`
   or `authorities` (`paged`); without them the docx render has no cover page. Worked around in
   mississippi/.../motion-brief by setting the caption page as `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}`.
6. **URLs.** typst breaks a URL after "/" and after "www."; LaTeX does not break inside it (it moves the URL whole or
   overfills); LibreOffice breaks after "/" only. Folder: texas/.../motion-brief, p7 line 14 (footnote: "https://abc13.com/
   texas-mail-in-ballot-voting-coronavirus-during/6243587/": typst and Word break after "during/", LaTeX not) and p8
   line 2 (footnote: "https://www.census.gov/...": typst breaks after "www.", LaTeX and Word before "https"). The shared
   model needs one set of break points inside a URL in all three engines.
7. **A slash inside a word at a line end (LaTeX).** texas/.../amicus-brief, p14 line 25: typst and Word end the line
   after "plaintiffs/" ("plaintiffs/appellees’"), LaTeX carries the whole word over.
8. **"§§" in typst.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua binds doubled signs; typst breaks between the two signs of "§§".
   texas/.../motion-brief, docx p15 line 9 ("Tex. Elec. Code §" / "§ 84.012"), p18 line 15.
9. **A brief whose front matter precedes the tables, with body placeholders** (narrowed in the second pass). The
   Texas opening, answering and reply briefs (typst on lib/typst/xengine.typ, docx hand-ported and gated by
   fb-595.68.36). Now in the model: the split at a `{{contents}}` line (`body_before_tables: {marker}`), word counts
   in layout blocks (`{xd.words}`, `words`), `keep_with_closing`. Still missing, each used by the INPUTs as they are:
   body placeholders, a line of the body that is the placeholder alone: `{{identity}}` (label rows from the
   frontmatter's `identity: [{role, lines}]`, opening line 144, answering line 139), `{{signature}}` (the signature
   block, set where the line is, kept with the paragraph or list item before it: opening 267, answering 259, reply 107)
   and `{{signer}}` (the signer's /s/ line inside a certificate, which is a body section: opening 273 and 281,
   answering 268 and 274, reply 113 and 123); `{{word-count}}` inside a paragraph's text (answering line 265:
   "... because it contains {{word-count}} words"), not in a layout block; a two-column markdown table in the body
   set as label rows (answering lines 153-159, the statement of the case: "**Nature of the Case:** | ..."; the
   serial lane's "tables in the body"); short lines flush left and a paragraph ending in a colon kept with what
   follows (the lane's "paragraph rules"). Moving the certificates and the identity into the frontmatter would change
   the INPUT contract the gated docx engine (fb-595.68.36) reads; not done. The texas amicus brief now sets its
   identity before the contents with `body_before_tables: {count: 1}`.
10. **LibreOffice keeps a note's anchor line where typst moves it with the whole note.** typst and LaTeX set a note
   whole and move its anchor line to the next page when the note does not fit; LibreOffice keeps the line and sets
   (or continues) the note on the next page (the serial lane's open "Word's footnote area" item; the Pennsylvania
   amicus case). Folders: mississippi/.../reply-brief (docx p8-p9, note 4), texas/.../amicus-brief (docx p19-p20,
   note 19), texas/.../motion-brief (docx p10-p11, note 3; Word one page shorter, 22 vs 23).
11. **LibreOffice breaks after a period before an opening parenthesis inside a token.** mississippi/.../reply-brief,
   docx p19 line 5: "R. 1.1.3.(C)(1)(D)" (the INPUT as filed): Word ends the line after "1.1.3.", typst and LaTeX
   carry the whole token ("R." ends typst's line). A no-break (U+2060) between "." and "(" inside a word in
   lib/docx/xdoc.lua, as for the other in-word rules, would match typst.
12. **docx page lookup: a section needle that is a prefix of the next heading's.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes the
   tables' section start (`front`) with the needle "identityofpartiesandcounsel" and then the first heading `h1` with
   "identityofpartiesandcounselappellants" (the heading and the next heading); docxpages.rs searches `h1` from one
   past the `front` match (`cur`), misses the heading on page i and finds the contents row instead: Word's contents
   list the identity and its three subheadings at "iii" (typst and LaTeX: i, i, i, ii). Folder: texas/.../amicus-brief
   (`body_before_tables: {count: 1}`, a level-1 heading followed directly by a level-2 heading). The "equal needle
   after a section start" rule could take a needle that starts with the section's needle too.
13. **docx page lookup: one authority cited with the same words in notes on two pages.** The note needles (kind `N`)
   of an authority's marks in notes 21 (p14) and 22 (p15) are the same text; the second is searched from the start of
   the body's page and finds the first again: Word lists "American Psychiatric Association. Position Statement ..."
   at 12, 14 (typst and LaTeX 12, 14, 15). Folder: texas/.../amicus-brief. A same-authority note needle could be
   searched past the previous one's position, as body needles are.
14. **typst `finds: [..]` lists no page.** mississippi/.../reply-brief: the entry "Miss. Code Ann. § 49-17-29(3)(a)"

   with `find: "49-17-29(3)(a)"` alone typst lists 4, 6, 14 (the note's "(3)(A)" on p5 is then unlisted, as committed).
   Both forms occur, and the parent entry's find "49-17-29" marks the same words (lib/typst/xdoc.typ `with-marks`).
15. **docx: a fill right before a `columns: {each, per_row}` block does not settle.** texas/.../amicus-brief cover:
   `{fill: 1}` before the title and another before the counsel columns (the old template's two `v(1fr)`): typst and
   LaTeX render, `fb render --engine docx` stops ("the pages did not settle in 5 passes"); a single fill above the
   title settles and is what the layout uses (the title and counsel at the foot of the cover).
16. **gate0 (DEF-CONT-001) reads a note inside the table of authorities.** texas/.../amicus-brief: with the identity

## Changes to INPUTs (mississippi/state/court-of-appeals, all five doc types)

- `authorities` moved from the folder's own schema (`{key: {kind, name, cite}}` and `[text](toa:key)` links in the
  body) to the shared one (`[{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`); the links are plain text again. The old template
  listed only the kinds case, statute, rule and other and dropped the constitutional provisions and regulations the
  samples cite; the groups now include them.
- motion-brief: two unbalanced straight closing quotes written as ”: cmarker set them as opening quotes, pandoc as
  closing ones.
- reply-brief: "(d) a concise ..." inside a quotation escaped (`\(d)`): pandoc read it as an ordered list, cmarker as
  text.
- The old template's cover named the Supreme Court; the cover now names the Court of Appeals, as the filed briefs do.
