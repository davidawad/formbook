## Shared-model requests

1. **Widow and orphan control in a paragraph with a footnote mark.** lib/latex/xdoc.lua and lib/docx/xdoc.lua turn
   widow and orphan control off for such a paragraph (docs/EVAL-LOOP.md: typst may move its last line alone with the
   note); lib/typst/xdoc.typ keeps typst's default widow and orphan costs for it, so where the paragraph's first or
   last line would stand alone at a page end typst carries a line over and the other two engines do not.
   Folders: mississippi/.../opening-brief (typst p5, body page 1: the paragraph "On the afternoon of March 25" with
   footnote 1; typst moves its first line to page 2, LaTeX and Word keep it on page 1; every later page shifts:
   XENG-001 22 vs 21 pages), mississippi/.../amicus-brief (typst p6, body page 3: the paragraph "Bias is a term used"
   with footnotes 12 and 13; typst moves two lines to page 4, LaTeX and Word one; p7-p9 differ). Either engine side
   could change: typst without widow and orphan costs in a footnoted paragraph, or the other engines keeping them
   (with the footnote-widow case of xengine.typ `footnote-widows: false`).
2. **No break after an opening bracket followed by a space.** typst (UAX #14, OP SP*) never breaks a line after
   "( "; LaTeX does. Folder: mississippi/.../answering-brief, latex p7 line 18: the INPUT (a transcription) reads
   "evidence.. ( R. Vol. 2, pp. 28 - 29)"; typst ends the line after "evidence..", LaTeX after "(". Word matches
   typst (docx gates). A shared rule (an unbreakable space after an opening bracket in lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua) fixes it.
3. **A footnote split over a page end.** typst, LaTeX and LibreOffice split a long footnote at different places (or
   move its anchor line), and LibreOffice's PDF reads the continued note inside the body text.
   Folder: mississippi/.../reply-brief, p8 (body page 4): footnote 4 ("The regulations governing water quality
   certifications ..."): typst splits it after its third line, LaTeX moves the anchor line to page 9, Word splits it
   elsewhere; the docx render then fails DEF-CONT-001 (body blocks 24 and 25 read with the note's continuation inside
   them). The folder's docx engine is not delivered (docx/ and the docx OUTPUTs removed; `fb engines gen --check`
   reports the two docx files missing on purpose) until the model sets split notes alike.
4. **Two authorities that mark the same word.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes one needle per mark; when two entries' `find`
   strings match the same word ("49-17-29" and "49-17-29(3)(c)"), the needles are identical, the second is searched
   after the first and falls back to its short text further on, and every later needle is lost ("fb render: not
   found in the PDF: a24.1, ..."). typst and LaTeX list both. Worked around in mississippi/.../reply-brief by listing a
   section once (its subsections dropped from the table: § 49-17-29, R. 1.3.1(B)(2), R. 1.3.2, R. 1.3.4); docxpages.rs
   could search a needle again from the same position.
5. **A cover with no tables in the docx engine.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits `cover` only when the layout has `contents`
   or `authorities` (`paged`); without them the docx render has no cover page. Worked around in
   mississippi/.../motion-brief by setting the caption page as `front` blocks ending in `{break: true}`.
6. **URLs.** typst breaks a URL after "/" and after "www."; LaTeX does not break inside it (it moves the URL whole or
   overfills); LibreOffice breaks after "/" only. Folder: texas/.../motion-brief, p7 line 14 (footnote: "https://abc13.com/
   texas-mail-in-ballot-voting-coronavirus-during/6243587/": typst and Word break after "during/", LaTeX not) and p8
   line 2 (footnote: "https://www.census.gov/...": typst breaks after "www.", LaTeX and Word before "https"). The shared
   model needs one set of break points inside a URL in all three engines.
7. **A slash inside a word at a line end (LaTeX).** texas/.../amicus-brief, p14 line 25: typst and Word end the line
   after "plaintiffs/" ("plaintiffs/appellees’"), LaTeX carries the whole word over.
8. **"§§" in typst.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua binds doubled signs; typst breaks between the two signs of "§§".
   texas/.../motion-brief, docx p15 line 9 ("Tex. Elec. Code §" / "§ 84.012"), p18 line 15.
9. **A brief whose front matter precedes the tables, with a computed word count.** The Texas opening, answering and
   reply briefs (typst on lib/typst/xengine.typ, docx hand-ported and gated by fb-595.68.36) need what the model does
   not set: markdown front matter (the identity of parties and counsel, TRAP 38.1(a)) on lower-roman pages before
   the table of contents, split at a `{{contents}}` line of the INPUT; body placeholders `{{identity}}` (label rows
   from the frontmatter), `{{signature}}` and `{{signer}}` (the signature block kept with the paragraph or list item
   before it; a certificate kept with its signer); `{{word-count}}`, the TRAP 9.4(i)(1) count the template computes
   (sections excluded by their level-1 heading); two-column markdown tables set as label rows; short lines flush left;
   a paragraph ending in a colon kept with what follows. They are left as they are (cross_engine gate on docx, no
   latex). The same front-matter feature would put the identity of parties before the contents in texas/.../amicus-brief
   (TRAP 38.1(a), (b) order; its INPUT has the identity as the first body section, as the old template set it).

## Changes to INPUTs (mississippi/state/court-of-appeals, all five doc types)

- `authorities` moved from the folder's own schema (`{key: {kind, name, cite}}` and `[text](toa:key)` links in the
  body) to the shared one (`[{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]`); the links are plain text again. The old template
  listed only the kinds case, statute, rule and other and dropped the constitutional provisions and regulations the
  samples cite; the groups now include them.
- motion-brief: two unbalanced straight closing quotes written as ”: cmarker set them as opening quotes, pandoc as
  closing ones.
- reply-brief: "(d) a concise ..." inside a quotation escaped (`\(d)`): pandoc read it as an ordered list, cmarker as
  text.
- The old template's cover named the Supreme Court; the cover now names the Court of Appeals, as the filed briefs do.

## Changes to the Texas amicus and motion layouts

- The covers use fixed space where the old templates set `v(1fr)` (not in the model); the motion's caption is the
  parties beside the docket lines, without the old column of ")" (not in the model).
- Footnotes 12 pt single-spaced (TRAP 9.4(e) minimum, as the court's opening brief), tables on lower-roman pages, the
  certificates signed with the signers' /s/ lines (the model cannot pick the first signer of a list).
