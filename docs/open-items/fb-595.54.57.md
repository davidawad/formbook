# fb-595.54.57: open items (generated engines, Alaska Supreme Court and California Court of Appeal, Fifth District)

Batch of bead fb-595.54.57 (docs/EVAL-LOOP.md, "Generated engines"). Every folder below has its `typst/layout.yaml`
and its typst template on the shared model; what the model does not yet cover is listed here for the serial lane
fb-595.54.82. A folder held back by one of these stays `params.cross_engine: report` (its `eval.yaml`).

## Shared-model requests

### 1. Footnotes that run over a page end (one rule in all three engines)

- Feature: the same decision, in every engine, about a footnote that does not fit on the page of its reference.
  Today LaTeX never splits one (`lib/latex/xengine.sty`: `\interfootnotelinepenalty=10000`), so it moves the line
  that holds the reference to the next page; typst splits the note after as many lines as fit, and sometimes sets the
  referencing line on the next page while the note stays (typst's footnote migration); LibreOffice splits it after
  a different number of lines.
- Folders: every Alaska folder (each INPUT has 20 to 130 footnotes, many of several lines);
  `courts/ninth-circuit/alaska/state/supreme-court/{amicus-brief,answering-brief,brief-on-the-merits,motion-brief,reply-brief}`.
- Evidence (`fb diff --engines` against the typst render):
  - amicus-brief latex: first differing p14 line 19, footnote 15 split after 3 lines in typst, moved whole with its
    referencing line in LaTeX; docx splits it after 4 lines. 794/824 lines identical (latex), 803/824 (docx).
  - answering-brief latex: p22 line 1 (a note split over p21/p22); 1158/1343 lines identical.
  - brief-on-the-merits latex: p4 line 19 (footnote 2 split in typst, whole on p5 in LaTeX); 999/1124.
  - motion-brief latex: p6 line 1: typst sets the line that carries footnote 8's mark at the top of p6 while the note
    is printed on p5; LaTeX keeps both on p5. 829/971.
  - reply-brief latex: p9 line 9 (footnote 15 split); 429/480.

### 2. The width of a footnote mark in Word

- Feature: LibreOffice fits more on a line that carries a footnote reference mark than typst and LaTeX do (the mark's
  advance in `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` / the reference style is narrower than typst's `super` at 58 %).
- Folders: the Alaska folders with a docx engine (amicus-brief, answering-brief, brief-on-the-merits).
- Evidence: amicus-brief docx p8 line 8: typst ends the line '“reasonable persons could not differ in their judgment
  of the facts.”6 Since Superior' and Word sets 'Court' on it too; brief-on-the-merits docx p6 line 23 ('ours fits
  more', a line with a mark); motion-brief docx p6 line 1 ('crisis.8 Barring judicial constitutional review of
  legislation').

### 3. docx: a cover without tables is not written (lib/docx/xdoc.lua)

- Feature: `spec.cover` is emitted only inside `if paged` (contents or authorities), so a layout with a cover and no
  tables loses its cover in Word (typst and LaTeX set it).
- Folder: `alaska/state/supreme-court/motion-brief` (a motion has no tables). Worked around in the folder: the cover
  blocks are its `front`, ending in `{break: true}` (the cover is page 1 of the body in every engine, as before).

### 4. docx: a footnote in a heading is lost or moved

- Feature: a footnote reference inside a heading (`### A. ... Appeal[^83]`) is not set at the heading in Word; every
  later note is numbered one less (LaTeX and typst number it 83 at the heading).
- Folder: `alaska/state/supreme-court/motion-brief`, docx. `check.sh --engine docx` fails DEF-CONT-001 (23 blocks,
  the first `H block 50 "A. Youth Plaintiffs Are Likely to Succeed in This Appeal83"`). The docx engine is not
  delivered for this folder (no `docx/`) until the model sets notes in headings.

### 5. docx: page needles match a table of contents row

- Feature: the docx needles of the headings (lib/docx/xdoc.lua, docxpages.rs) are searched from the cover, so a
  heading followed directly by another heading ("ARGUMENTS" then "I. DOC has shown ...") is found in the contents
  rows, which hold the same text in the same order: every contents row reads page 2. The body search should start
  after the tables.
- Folder: `alaska/state/supreme-court/reply-brief`, docx (every contents row "2"; typst and LaTeX 4, 4, 5, 6, ..).
  Tier 1 passed, but the contents are wrong: the docx engine is not delivered for this folder (no `docx/`).

### 6. Pipe tables

- Feature: a markdown table in the body. `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` and `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` stop with `xdoc: unsupported
  block Table`.
- Folder: `alaska/state/supreme-court/petition-for-review` (two tables of house and senate districts, INPUT lines 52
  and 61). The typst template is on the model (typst sets the table); latex and docx are not generated for it.
