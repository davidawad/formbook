## Shared-model requests (for a second serial lane)

| feature | folders | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A footnote mark right after the token that follows a section sign is lost in LaTeX | `petition-for-review` (latex reported) | "O.C.G.A. § 21-2-385(d)(1)(B).[^three]": `lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua` `hold_sign_tokens` takes the Note into the token's pieces and `lib/latex/xdoc.lua` sets them in one `\mbox`, where LaTeX drops a `\footnote`. Footnote 3 is missing from `OUTPUT-petition-for-review.latex.pdf` page 6 (typst and docx set it); tier 1 does not see it. The token should end before a Note. |
| Word's text width for TeX Gyre Schola | `amicus-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`, `petition-for-discretionary-review` (docx reported); `answering-brief`, `reply-brief` (not moved) | LibreOffice sets Schola narrower than typst and fits a word more on a line typst fills to within about a point: amicus p14 line 4 and p25 line 20 (629/642 lines), merits p3 line 3 and p24 line 18 (665/680), petition p20 line 23 and p22 line 18 (729/735); answering-brief on the model: 234/251 lines, 12 pages against typst's 13 (p4 line 7, "Plaintiffs show"). The generated `docx/reference.xml` has no such allowance (gen.rs has a LaTeX bias for newtx, none for Word). |
| A line break after a slash inside a word in LaTeX | `opening-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits` (latex reported) | typst breaks after the slash of "redline/blue" (opening p10 lines 13-14) and "d/b/a" (merits cover, p1 lines 3-4); Word gets a zero-width space there (`lib/docx/xdoc.lua` `breaks`); pdfLaTeX has no break (`lib/latex/xeschola.lua` sets the slash as `\xeglyphscaled` "a line may end after it", but no break is written). |
| The width a narrower Schola glyph leaves, set before a dash's break (LaTeX) | `amicus-brief`, `petition-for-discretionary-review` (latex reported) | `\xeflush` writes the pending width of ")" (TeX Live 333 against 483/1000 em) before the `\allowbreak` of an em or en dash, a gap pdftotext reads as a space: "(2)—that" reads "(2) —" (amicus p6 line 16), "§ 2(1)–(2)" reads "(1) –(2)" (petition p12 line 5, and p18 line 14 before an em dash). |
| TeX Live's bold Schola in a bold heading (LaTeX) | `amicus-brief` (latex reported) | A level-4 bold heading breaks a word earlier in LaTeX ("of his child's cause of death", p22 lines 18-19; 639/642 lines). |
| The docx contents page of a heading whose text recurs in the body | `petition-for-discretionary-review` (docx reported) | The Word contents give CONCLUSION page 21 (typst 24): the needle (the heading and the words after it) is not found, and the fallback, the heading's text alone, finds "conclusion" in "permit only one conclusion: the" on page 21 (`tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs`, `find(&n.fallback, from)`). |
| Keyed authorities: an entry with a name and no citation; a case name in italics with its comma | `opening-brief` (worked around), `answering-brief`, `reply-brief` | `keyed-text` sets `e.name + ", " + e.cite`: a statute or constitution entry with only `name` stops the typst render (no `cite`) and prints "nil" in LaTeX and Word; a group's `italic` sets "*name* cite" without the comma Georgia citations carry ("*Bibbs v. Toyota Motor Corp.*, 304 Ga. 68"). The opening brief's INPUT now writes such an entry's text under `cite` and a case's name in italics as markdown (`*...*`, the group not italic); the sort keys spell hyphens as spaces ("5 6 34") so the statutes keep their order. A model that set a name alone, and an italic name with its comma, would read the frontmatter as written. |
| An Arabic count from 1 after an unnumbered cover, running through the tables and the body | `petition-for-review`, `petition-for-discretionary-review` | The earlier templates numbered the questions presented 1, the contents 2 and the body on from 3 (SCt R 21: counted pages in Arabic; the questions presented are counted, R 20(6) does not exclude them). The model numbers the tables apart (`front`, the body from 1 again) or counts the cover (`from: 2`); these folders now count the cover (questions presented page 2). |
