## Shared-model requests

1. **A cover with its own top margin.** Minn. R. Civ. App. P. 132.01 subd. 2(a) (MINNCTAPP-CONT-001, rules.md):
   the docket number is "placed one-half inch from the top center of the cover". The current templates set the cover
   page with `margin: (top: 0.5in)` and the rest at 1 in; the renders put "No." at y = 36.0 pt on page 1 (amicus 14
   pp., answering 75, opening 52, reply 30). The model has one page margin (`margin`), and a cover block's `above`
   only adds space below it, so the port would move the docket number to 1 in and break a mandated (tier-1) placement.
   Needed: e.g. `cover_margin: {top: 36}` (typst: a page of its own margin; LaTeX: `\newgeometry` for the cover page;
   Word: a section of its own with that top margin). Folders: minnctapp `amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`,
   `opening-brief`, `reply-brief`. (Both filed exemplars of other filers set the number at about 1 in, y = 70.5; the
   rule, not the exemplar, decides. If David reads the rule as "half an inch below the top margin", these folders
   need no new feature: that is the decision to ask for.)
2. **Counsel in two balanced flowing columns.** minnctapp `reply-brief` sets the cover's counsel groups as one flow
   in two columns of equal height (typst `columns(2)` in a box of half the flow's height), so a long group runs on
   into the second column; the template notes that a grid of one group per cell pushed the cover onto a second page
   (DEF-COVER-001). The model's `columns: {each, per_row}` sets one group per cell. Folder: minnctapp `reply-brief`.
3. **Headings numbered by the template.** Already listed under "Not yet in the model" in docs/EVAL-LOOP.md. All five
   pasuperct templates number levels 2 to 4 themselves (`I.`, `A.`, `1.`, the number then 0.5 in, the text hanging
   0.5 in; the table of contents shows the numbers); the INPUTs carry no numerals (e.g. opening-brief
   `## THE SENTENCE IS ILLEGAL ...` renders as "I.  THE SENTENCE IS ILLEGAL ..."). Writing numerals into the INPUTs
   would move the numbering from the template to the user and lose the tab to the hang: a decision, not a port.
   Folders: pasuperct `amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `motion-brief`, `opening-brief`, `reply-brief`.
4. **A break after a hyphen before a digit.** typst (xengine) breaks
   `https://www.mncourts.gov/.../High-Profile-Cases/27-CR-20-12951-TKL/...` after "27-CR-20-" (before the digit
   "1"); lib/latex/xengine.lua keeps a hyphen before a digit unbreakable ("as Unicode line breaking does") and
   LibreOffice does the same, so both break after "Cases/27-". First differing line: minnctapp `petition-for-writ`,
   latex and docx, p3 line 24 (footnote 3), 281/283 lines identical, every page start and end the same. The engines
   need one rule for HY before NU (typst's or the other two's). Folder: minnctapp `petition-for-writ`
   (`cross_engine: report` until then).
5. **Space that fills the page on a cover.** Already listed under "Not yet in the model" (typst `v(1fr)` on a
   cover). pasuperct `opening-brief` sets `v(1fr)` around the title, `answering-brief` and `reply-brief` once; fixed
   space would do for a port (amicus-brief and motion-brief already use fixed space), so this is not blocking on its
   own.
