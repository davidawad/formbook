Folders of the bead: the Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District, the Texas Supreme Court, the California Court
of Appeal, Third District, the San Diego County Superior Court and the Supreme Court of Guam, each court root and its
doc types. The five court roots hold no template of their own (`typst/.gitkeep`, `latex/.gitkeep`, only
FILED-EXAMPLEs): there is nothing to generate there.

## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

### 1. typst: the authority mark after a paragraph's last word is set on the next line (a page late)

- Folders: `courts/eighth-circuit/missouri/state/court-of-appeals-southern-district/answering-brief` (ungated,
  `cross_engine: report`).
- Evidence: `INPUT-answering-brief.md` line 238 ends its paragraph with "287.020.3(1)", the `find` of the
  authority "§287.020.3". The paragraph's last line is the last line of page 18. typst's table of authorities lists
  "14, 18, 19"; LaTeX and Word list "14, 18" (correct). XENG-003 fails on both engines at p3 line 19 (the table row).
- Repro: `lib/typst/xdoc.typ` `with-marks` sets `[#it#metadata(k)<xd-toa>]`. When `it` is the last word of a
  paragraph, `query(<xd-toa>)` reports the mark at x = the left margin, y = the top of the *next* line, on every
  page (a one-paragraph document with k short paragraphs before it: k = 26 gives page 1, y 678.15 pt; k = 27, where
  the paragraph's last line is the page's last, gives page 2, y 72 pt, while the word is on page 1). The marker
  is a zero-width inline after the paragraph's last word, and typst places it after the line break.
- Needed: a mark that stays on the line of its word at the end of a paragraph (for example a mark inside a `box`
  with the word, or the word's own location), in `with-marks`. The LaTeX and Word engines are already right.

### 2. LaTeX: a line that fills the measure to within 0.002 pt breaks one word early

- Folders: Missouri `answering-brief` (ungated).
- Evidence: page 18, the block quotation of § 287.020.3 (left and right indent 36 pt): typst ends the line
  "defined to be the primary factor, in relation to any other factor, causing both" at x = 503.9985 pt, the
  measure ends at 504 pt. LaTeX's words drift 0.057 pt over the line (newtx's advances, "causing" ends at 477.694
  against typst's 477.637), more than the 0.05 bp the generated text block allows (gen.rs `latex_bias`), so "both"
  moves to the next line. Word breaks as typst does.
- Needed: a bias that scales with the words of a line (or per-glyph widths matched to Liberation Serif) for
  Times New Roman in LaTeX.

### 3. URLs and "and/or": where a line may break inside a word with "/" or "-"

- Folders: Missouri `opening-brief` (ungated).
- Evidence: footnote 2 (page 20) holds a bare URL. typst breaks it after "mayoclinic.org/" and after "faq-";
  LaTeX after "effectively." and inside at "expert-"; Word after "faq-2005849" (the last digit alone on a line).
  Page 26, last line: typst and Word break "and/or" after the slash ("physical and/" | "or emotional"); LaTeX
  does not, so page 27 starts a line earlier (XENG-002 fails in LaTeX).
- Needed: one rule for break opportunities inside a word at "/" and "-" (URLs included), set alike in the three
  engines.

## INPUT changes (content unchanged)

- Paragraphs that open with "(1) ", "(A) ", "I. " or "II. " are escaped ("\(1) ", "II\. "): pandoc's markdown
  reads them as list markers (fancy_lists), CommonMark (typst's cmarker) does not. Both now read a paragraph that
  prints the same characters.
- Missouri opening brief: `5'8"` is written `5’8”`. typst's smart quotes set an opening double quote there, pandoc
  a closing one.
