## Decisions and notes

- dc `answering-brief`: DEF-LEN-001 counts the whole file. With Word's double spacing (32.2 pt, as the filed brief) the
  sample is 51 pages, of which the title page and tables (6 pages) do not count under Rule 32 (a) (5); `page_limit`
  is 56 with that reason in eval.yaml. A count that skips the excluded pages would need a check option.
- dc `reply-brief`: eval.yaml keeps `page_limit: 50`, but Rule 32 (a) (5) limits a reply brief to 20 pages
  (DC-LEN-001). The sample's body runs p6-p26 (21 pages including the signature) at Word's double spacing: needs a
  decision (the filed reply is 24 pages whole).
- dc body pitch moved from the old 1.15 em leading (25.8 pt) to Word's double (32.2 pt), as the filed exemplars set
  it; every dc render grew by 20-30 %.
- dc tier 2, open: the signature block may split over a page (merits p24/25), since a block of several signers can be
  longer than a page (petition-for-review); the certificate of service is no longer listed in the table of contents
  (it is a closing block, not a body heading); the "Table of Authorities" row of the old contents is gone.
- okla rules: Okla. Sup. Ct. R. 1.11 could not be fetched (justia 403, courtlistener 202, oscn.net timeout on
  2026-10-22); rules.md now cites it as a web search index quotes it (12 pt, 11 pt notes, double spacing, 1 1/4 in
  left margin, 30 pages), and every filed exemplar sets the 1 1/4 in left margin. Needs the court-posted text.
  No `page_limit` is set: whether the 30 pages exclude the cover and tables is not known without the rule text, and
  the answering brief is 32 pages whole (the filed one is 32 too).
- okla INPUTs: the authorities moved to the model's schema (`heading`, `entries`, `cite` = the case name in italics
  and the citation); `{{contents}}`, `{{signature}}` and `{{signer}}` are gone from the bodies (the model sets the
  tables and the closing); list-like quoted labels ("9.", "A.", "(A)") and the writ's relief items ("a.") are escaped
  so pandoc and cmarker read the same paragraphs; "___" signature lines and "* Admitted" are escaped as text.
- okla `petition-for-writ`: counsel are not on the caption page (the filed application sets none there; its long
  caption fills the page); the signature block names them. The old template printed no signature for the writ.
- okla covers use fixed spaces in place of `v(1fr)` (counsel 21.6 pt below the title); the motion's cover, with five
  firms, fits one page with counsel at 2.5 in from the margin.
- okla tier 2, open: the old template set lines under 50 characters flush left and kept a paragraph ending in a colon
  with what follows; the model does neither.
