## Shared-model requests

1. **A cover with its own top margin** (the same request as fb-595.54.60 request 1). Minn. R. Civ. App. P. 132.01
   subd. 2(a) (MINN-COVER-001, rules.md): the court name and docket number "one-half inch from the top center" of the
   cover. The six cover templates set the cover page with `margin: (top: 0.5in)` (the rest at 1 in, MINN-FORM-001);
   their renders put "NO." at y = 36.0 pt on page 1 (amicus 25 pp., answering 57, merits 57, review 11, writ 46,
   reply 18). The model has one page margin, so a port would move the number to 1 in and break a mandated (tier-1)
   placement. Needed: e.g. `cover_margin: {top: 36}` (typst: the cover a page of its own margin; LaTeX
   `\newgeometry`; Word a section of its own). Decision for David, as for fb-595.54.60: all six FILED-EXAMPLEs of
   this court (different filers) set the first line at about 1 in (y = 70.2, 70.6, 72.5, 72.5, 75.3, 79.4 pt); if
   the rule is read as "half an inch below the top margin", these folders need no new feature.
   Folders: `amicus-brief`, `answering-brief`, `brief-on-the-merits`, `petition-for-review`, `petition-for-writ`,
   `reply-brief`.
2. **Counsel in two balanced flowing columns** (fb-595.54.60 request 2). `brief-on-the-merits` and `reply-brief` set
   the cover's counsel groups as one flow in two columns of equal height (typst `columns(2)` in a box of half the
   flow's height); the template notes that one group per grid cell ran the cover onto a second page (DEF-COVER-001).
   The model's `columns: {each, per_row}` sets one group per cell.
3. **A cover without tables in Word.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua emits `spec.cover` only inside `if paged` (contents or
   authorities, lines 431-446); typst and LaTeX set it always. A folder with a cover page and no tables lost the
   whole cover in the docx (found on `motion-brief`: docx 7 pages, typst 8, page 1 missing). Worked around in the
   folder: the caption page is `front` ending in `{break: true}` (same pages in all three engines).
4. **A missing optional block keeps its space in typst.** lib/typst/xdoc.typ `blocks` sets `v(lead)` for the first
   block of a container before it expands the text, so an `optional` block whose field is missing still leaves its
   `above` there; lib/pandoc/xdoc.lua drops the block with its space. Found on `motion-brief` (the first caption
   party has no `joiner`: typst 7.8 pt lower on p1 than LaTeX, and the centered title broke on other lines).
   Worked around: the joiner has `below` only.
5. **"s/Name" e-signatures split in Word's PDF text.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua `breaks` puts a zero-width space after a
   slash inside a word before a letter ("CM/ECF"); "s/Charles N. Nauen" (as filed) is such a word, and LibreOffice's
   PDF then reads "s/" and "Charles" as two words (typst and LaTeX one). `motion-brief`, docx, p7 line 10; every line
   break, page and page end equal. Needed: treat a word-initial "s/" as "/s/" is treated (no space). The folder's
   docx is `cross_engine_docx: report` until then.
6. **An empty string in a list field.** A `""` item of a list a `{field}` text expands (a blank line between two
   counsel groups, between "Judgment Entered" and "Appealable Order Entered") gives an empty paragraph: typst sets it
   with no height, Word with a full line (motion-brief p8 +14.94 pt). The two INPUTs were changed instead
   (`motion-brief`: the third counsel group split in two `signature_more` items; `petition-for-discretionary-review`:
   the `""` of `right_lines` removed). The unported folders' `counsel` lists hold `""` too (amicus 2, answering 2,
   merits 4, review 2, reply 2, writ 1). Needed: one rule for `""` in every engine (a blank line of the block's pitch).
7. **Tables and lines after the signature** (`petition-for-writ`): the INPUT holds pipe tables (12 rows; the pandoc
   model stops on a Table: "unsupported block") and an `after_signature` block of declaration lines set double spaced
   after the signature, before the certificates; its cover's docket label comes from `docket_label`.
8. **A signed certificate after the tail.** `petition-for-discretionary-review`: the filed petition signs its
   certificate of document length again (date and signature block after it); the old template did the same. The
   model has no blocks after the tail, so the ported certificate is unsigned (the petition itself is signed after the
   conclusion, MINN-FORM-006). Tier 2, not blocking; a `tail_closing: [blocks]` would restore it.
