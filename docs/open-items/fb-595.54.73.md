## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

1. **Paper size.** `layout.yaml` has no page size: `lib/typst/xdoc.typ` sets `paper: "us-letter"`, `tools/fb/src/enginegen/tex.rs`
   writes `letterpaper`, `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` and `tools/fb/src/enginegen/word.rs` write `w:pgSz w:w="12240" w:h="15840"`.
   Needed: `paper: legal` (612 x 1008 pt; Word 12240 x 20160 twips). Folders: the four Puerto Rico doc folders. Evidence:
   TSPR Rule 40(b) (8 1/2 x 14 in legal paper, cited in the court's `rules.md`); each folder's `eval.yaml` checks
   `page_height_pt: 1008`, a tier-1 check a letter-size port would fail.
2. **Authorities from `toa:` links.** The samples key their authorities by id (`authorities: {key: {kind, name, cite,
   sort}}`) and mark each citation in the body as `[text](toa:key)`; the template groups them by `kind` under court
   titles (Puerto Rico JURISPRUDENCIA / LEYES Y ESTATUTOS / ...; New York Cases / Statutes / Regulations / ...; Vermont
   Cases / Statutes and Regulations / ...), sorts each group (by `sort`, else the name), italicizes a case name, and prints
   "passim" at 7 pages or more (NY: a key no link reaches stops the build). The model reads only `authorities:
   [{heading, entries: [{cite, find}]}]` and finds text. Folders: Puerto Rico amicus-brief, answering-brief,
   brief-on-the-merits; New York amicus, answering, opening, reply; Vermont amicus, brief-on-the-merits, reply. Evidence:
   the INPUTs carry 1 to 41 `](toa:` links each (`grep -c '](toa:'`); rewriting the frontmatter is "new fields" under the
   checklist.
3. **Headings numbered by the template** (already listed under "Not yet in the model"). New York numbers headings itself:
   in the Argument "POINT I" on a line of its own, the point centered below, then A., 1., a.; elsewhere A., 1., a., (1);
   the contents rows put the label in a box (1 in for POINT, 0.5 in otherwise). Folders: the four New York doc folders.
4. **Checks the template computes.** New York stops the build on more words than 22 NYCRR 1250.8(f)(2) allows (14,000
   for the opening brief), on a footnote (Part 1000), and on the 1250.8(b) sections missing or out of order. The model has
   no such checks. Folders: the four New York doc folders.
5. **Line breaks inside a long URL.** A URL longer than the room left on a line breaks at a different character in each
   engine: typst (a link) after "/" and after "%20", LaTeX not after "/" (the whole URL moves to the next line) and at a
   hyphen, LibreOffice after a period. Folder: Vermont answering-brief. Evidence (fb diff of the renders): latex
   XENG-003 721/740 lines, first differing p15 line 8 (body text after `https://www.cbp.gov/border-security/along-us-borders`,
   typst "https://www.cbp.gov/" | "border-security/...", LaTeX the URL whole on the next line); docx 733/740, first
   differing p25 line 23 (footnote URL `.../2020-Jan/U.S.%20Border%20Patrol...`: typst breaks before "Year%202019",
   Word after "U.S."), after which the pages drift by a line (typst 31 pages, latex and docx 30).
6. **A frontmatter value of underscores.** Frontmatter strings are read as markdown, so a signature line
   `"__________________________"` is a thematic break (typst: "block may not occur inside of a paragraph", a warning that
   fails the render). Vermont answering-brief escapes it in the INPUT (`'\_\_...'`, the same printed line); the model
   could take a block's text literally (a `literal: true` style) instead.

## Decisions and folders that cannot match

1. **Vermont rules unverified.** `courts/second-circuit/vermont/state/supreme-court` has no `rules.md`. The Vermont Rules
   of Appellate Procedure (V.R.A.P. 28, 32) are published on vtcourts.gov (formerly vermontjudiciary.org); both hosts
   answer 403 to this host (curl and WebFetch, 2026-10-22), and no other primary source was found. The Vermont
   answering-brief layout therefore keeps the old template's basis, the filed exemplar's convention (marked `VT-CONV` in
   its `layout.yaml`), and no rule is cited. Someone with access should write `rules.md` from V.R.A.P. 32(a) and check
   the layout's size, spacing, margins and footnote size against it.
