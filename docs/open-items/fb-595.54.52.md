## Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82)

### 1. A section or pilcrow sign repeated with a space: "§ § 49-22-08", "¶¶ 11" (XENG-003)

- Folders: nd/brief-on-the-merits (p19 line 11, and later "§ §" lines), nd/answering-brief (p15 line 20, which
  moves the authority pages of p3 and page 16's first word), nd/petition-for-rehearing (p7 line 12: "¶¶ 11").
- `lib/typst/xengine.typ` boxes `regex("[§¶] \\S+")`: the matches do not overlap, so in "§ § 49-22-08(5)" the first
  sign is boxed with the second and the number is free (typst breaks after "§ §"), and in "¶¶ 11" the second "¶" is
  boxed with the number and the first is free (typst breaks between the two signs). `lib/pandoc/xengine.lua`
  `bind_signs` chains every "§"/"¶" + space into a no-break space, so LaTeX and Word keep "§ § 49-22-08(5)" whole;
  `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` also binds "§§"/"¶¶" + space, LaTeX does not break inside "¶¶ 11" at all.
- Evidence: `fb diff --engines usage/OUTPUT-<id>.typst.pdf usage/OUTPUT-<id>.latex.pdf` in those folders, e.g.
  brief-on-the-merits p19: typst `under § §` / `49-22-08(5)`, latex and docx `under` / `§ § 49-22-08(5)`.
- Needed: one rule in all three engines, e.g. typst `regex("([§¶]+ )+\\S+")` boxed whole, and the docx and LaTeX
  filters binding a run of signs to the word after it (and no break between two signs). The INPUTs type the signs
  as filed; they were not changed.

### 2. A typst line that overfills the measure by less than 0.05 pt (XENG-003)

- Folder: nd/brief-on-the-merits, p16 line 13 ("... *Geffre v. N.D.* / *Dep't of Health*").
- typst ends the line before "Dep’t", whose right edge would be 540.04 pt on a 540 pt measure; LaTeX (newtx, the
  text block 0.05 bp wider by design, docs/EVAL-LOOP.md) sets it at 540.048, and Word fits it as well. Both
  generated engines carry the word, typst does not.
- Needed: the same fit tolerance in typst as in the other engines (or the LaTeX and Word measure without the 0.05
  slack for Times New Roman where Word itself fits the word); a folder cannot fix it without a fake margin.

### 3. Two authority marks on the same word break the docx page lookup (docx not delivered)

- Folder: nd/answering-brief. `fb render --engine docx` fails: `not found in the PDF: a2.1, a2.2, a27.4`.
- Authorities 27 (`find: "49-03-01"`) and 28 (`find: "49-03-01.1"`) both mark "49-03-01.1" in "§ § 49-03-01,
  49-03-01.1; *Application of Otter Tail Power Co.*" (body line 146). `tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs` looks each body
  needle up from the end of the one before; the second needle on the same word is then found at the next
  "49-03-01(1)" two lines on, past authority 2's mark, which is never found.
- Needed: needles at the same place found from the same start (search from the start of the previous match, or
  group marks of one word). Until then the folder has no `docx/` (removed so that `fb check-changed` checks only
  the engines delivered): docx BLOCKED, `fb engines gen --check` reports it missing.

### 4. Two documents in one filing: the motion for leave with its own cover, then the brief

- Folder: nd/amicus-brief. The filing is the Rule 29(a)(3) motion for leave (its own cover from `motion_title`, the
  body text before the first heading, `motion_date`, `motion_signature`) followed by the brief (a second cover from
  `brief_title`, contents, authorities, body, signature, certificates).
- The model has one `cover`, one body and one `closing`; body markdown cannot go before the cover or between two
  covers. Writing it as one document would drop the motion's cover or set the motion inside the brief.
- Needed: a leading part (`cover`, the markdown up to a split point, `closing`) before the main document, or a
  sequence of documents per sample. Not ported; the typst template and its render are unchanged.
