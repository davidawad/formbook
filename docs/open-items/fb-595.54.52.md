### Shared-model requests (for fb-595.54.82; items 3, 5-8 and 9 landed there)

#### 1. A section or pilcrow sign repeated with a space: "§ § 49-22-08", "¶¶ 11" (XENG-003)

- Folders: nd/brief-on-the-merits (p19 line 11, and later "§ §" lines), nd/answering-brief (p15 line 20, which
  moves the authority pages of p3 and page 16's first word), nd/petition-for-rehearing (p7 line 12: "¶¶ 11").
- `lib/typst/xengine.typ` boxes `regex("[§¶] \\S+")`: the matches do not overlap, so in "§ § 49-22-08(5)" the first
  sign is boxed with the second and the number is free (typst breaks after "§ §"), and in "¶¶ 11" the second "¶" is
  boxed with the number and the first is free (typst breaks between the two signs). `lib/pandoc/xengine.lua`
  `bind_signs` chains every "§"/"¶" + space into a no-break space, so LaTeX and Word keep "§ § 49-22-08(5)" whole;
  `lib/docx/xdoc.lua` also binds "§§"/"¶¶" + space, LaTeX does not break inside "¶¶ 11" at all.
- Evidence: `fb diff --engines usage/OUTPUT-<id>.typst.pdf usage/OUTPUT-<id>.latex.pdf` in those folders, e.g.
  brief-on-the-merits p19: typst `under § §` / `49-22-08(5)`, latex and docx `under` / `§ § 49-22-08(5)`.
- Needed: one rule in all three engines, e.g. typst `regex("([§¶]+ )+\\S+")` boxed whole, and the docx and LaTeX
  filters binding a run of signs to the word after it (and no break between two signs). The INPUTs type the signs
  as filed; they were not changed.

#### 2. A typst line that overfills the measure by less than 0.05 pt (XENG-003)

- Needed: the same fit tolerance in typst as in the other engines (or the LaTeX and Word measure without the 0.05
