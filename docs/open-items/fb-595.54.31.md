Batch lane of fb-595.54 (the "Generated engines" checklist of docs/EVAL-LOOP.md). Folders:
`courts/sixth-circuit/michigan/state/supreme-court` and its eight doc types (amicus-brief, answering-brief,
brief-on-the-merits, motion-brief, petition-for-discretionary-review, petition-for-rehearing, petition-for-writ,
reply-brief).

## Done in the folders

- Each doc type has a `typst/layout.yaml` written from its old typst template and `../rules.md` (MSC-*), and its
  template is the three-line `xdoc` call (function name `mich-brief` kept for `main.typ`). `fb engines gen` wrote
  `latex/` and `docx/`; every INPUT was rendered with typst, latex and docx and every page of the typst renders
  looked at.
- motion-brief: the filed motion (usage/SOURCE.md) has no cover: the caption heads page 1 and the text is numbered
  from 1. The layout now does the same (`front`, numbered from 1), counsel kept in the caption (MSC-FORM-005).
- petition-for-writ: the cover is page 1 of the complaint and the text starts on page 2 (as filed); set as `front`
  with a page break, which also keeps the cover in the docx engine (see 9 below).
- INPUTs (usage/): a paragraph that opens with "(1) ", "(a) ", "a. " or "I. " has its marker escaped (`\(1)`,
  `a\.`): the printed text is unchanged in both readers, and pandoc no longer reads it as a list (see 1). The amicus
  INPUT's straight quotes are now the curly quotes of the filed brief (unbalanced straight quotes were turned in
  opposite directions by the two readers).
- The court root (`supreme-court/`) has no typst template (its `typst/` and `latex/` are empty; it holds exemplars
  only): nothing to port.

## Shared-model requests (for the serial lane fb-595.54.82)

1. **One markdown reading.** fb render reads the sample with pandoc `markdown+smart` (fancy_lists on); typst reads
   it with cmarker (CommonMark). A paragraph opening "(1) ", "(a) ", "a. ", "I. " is a list to pandoc and text to
   cmarker; inside a quotation it stops both pandoc filters ("xdoc: a quotation holds paragraphs only",
   lib/latex/xdoc.lua:256, lib/docx/xdoc.lua:403). Unbalanced straight quotes are also curled differently. Folders:
   all eight here (worked around in the INPUTs). Request: read with `-fancy_lists` (or commonmark_x) so both agree.
2. **A kept paragraph never splits in LaTeX.** lib/latex/xdoc.lua `par()` adds `\nobreak` after a kept paragraph but
   lets its own lines break over a page; typst moves the heading whole, Word keeps its lines (`keepLines`). Evidence:
   answering-brief p11/12. Request: `\interlinepenalty=10000` inside a kept paragraph.
3. **A paragraph that anchors a note.** LaTeX (club and widow penalties 0) and Word (Body Text Note) let its first
   line end a page alone; typst does not. Evidence: reply-brief p8, amicus-brief p6.
4. **Spaced ellipsis.** typst never breaks before " ." (". . ." after a word, UAX 14 LB13); LaTeX and Word do.
   Evidence: brief-on-the-merits p11 ("such violation . . .”"). Request: a no-break space before each spaced period
   of an ellipsis in the latex and docx engines.
5. **Long URLs.** typst breaks a URL inside the word (after "www.", "/", "-"); LaTeX and Word break it elsewhere.
   Evidence: petition-for-writ p3, petition-for-discretionary-review p4 (authorities row).
6. **En dash in a table row.** LaTeX prints U+2013 in an authorities row as "--" (no ligatures under xengine).
   Evidence: petition-for-discretionary-review p3 ("Auto--Owners").
7. **An authority mark at the end of a page-ending paragraph.** typst counts the mark after the last word of a
   paragraph that ends a page ("400.111b(16)." on the last line of p6) on the next page; LaTeX and Word on the right
   one. Evidence: petition-for-discretionary-review ("MCL 400.111b": typst 2, 6, 7; latex, docx 2, 6; "MCL 600.1629"
   ... 21 against 20), brief-on-the-merits p3.
8. **docx page needles** (tools/fb/src/docxpages.rs `lookup`): (a) two finds where one is a prefix of the other
   ("R 432.652(1)" and "R 432.652(1)(c)(v)") mark the same word: both needles start at the same place, `cur` moves one
   past it after the first, and the second is not found (or found far on, losing every needle after it). Folders:
   answering-brief, amicus-brief, reply-brief. (b) a needle in a footnote that runs over a page end has no split
   fallback (body needles do). Folder: answering-brief (note 6, "Parise v Detroit Entertainment, LLC").
9. **A cover numbered apart without tables.** lib/docx/xdoc.lua writes the cover only when the folder has contents
   or authorities (`if paged`); lib/latex numbers the body roman when `page_number.front` is set and there are no
   tables. Folder: petition-for-rehearing (a caption of two unnumbered pages, the motion from 1, as filed).
10. **Footnote splits in LaTeX.** typst sets the first lines of a long note at a page foot and continues it; LaTeX
    moves the note whole to the next page. Evidence: motion-brief p3 (note 2).
11. **Empty lines in a list value** (`""` in the rehearing counsel names and signature lines, a blank line in the
    filed motion separates its counsel blocks with them): typst sets no line for an empty paragraph. Folder: petition-for-rehearing.
12. **Tier 2, accepted:** the old amicus, brief-on-the-merits, motion and writ templates set space between body
    paragraphs (the model has no paragraph gap); the old motion and application covers set counsel at the page foot
    (`v(1fr)`, not in the model: a fixed space now); the signature group can take a page alone (the model does not
    keep the last paragraph with the closing).

### Shared-model requests still open (for a second serial lane)

a. **A footnote after "§ n" is lost in LaTeX** (content loss, not caught by tier 1). lib/latex/xdoc.lua binds the
   token after "§" or "¶" in `\mbox{..}`; when the token carries a note (`MCL § 432.302.[^18]`) the `\footnote` is
   inside the box and LaTeX drops it: answering-brief's latex render has no note 18 ("Even assuming arguendo ...";
   typst and docx print it), and the anchor line stays on p24 (typst moves it to p25 with its note). Request: end the
   bound token before a note mark (or set the note outside the box). Folder: answering-brief.
b. **A slash inside a word.** typst breaks after the "/" of "he/she" (UAX 14), pdfTeX does not (the URL rule covers
   URLs only). Evidence: brief-on-the-merits p7 lines 6-7 ("... if he/" | "she has complaints").
   Request: one rule in every engine (a break after a slash between letters, or none, as for URLs).
c. **typst sets a note on the page after its anchor** (fb-595.54.82 "not implemented", 7 folders): brief-on-the-merits
   p22/23, the one-line quotation "261 Mich App at 546.[^9]" ends p22 in typst with note 9 on p23;
   LaTeX moves the line on with its note.
d. **"* * *" in LaTeX's PDF text.** pdftotext reads LaTeX's spaced asterisks (the same glyph positions as typst's,
   3 pt apart) as one word "***"; typst's PDF carries the spaces. Evidence: brief-on-the-merits p36,
   lines 3, 7, 10 (`> \* \* \*`). Request: real interword spaces (`\pdfinterwordspaceon` or an ActualText span)
   around a lone "*", or the diff joining them.
e. **Word's footnote placement** (fb-595.54.82 "not implemented", 8 folders): LibreOffice keeps an anchor line on its
   page and sets a note that does not fit there on the next page; typst and LaTeX move the line on. Evidence:
   motion-brief p3/4 (note 2), brief-on-the-merits p12/13 (notes 3, 4; every later page shifts).
f. **Word's line-fit tolerance** (fb-595.54.82 "not implemented", 10 folders): a line typst fills to within a
   fraction of a point. Evidence: answering-brief note 1 (p7: Word fits "Appeal." on the first line; the note grows a
   line and the pages after shift), petition-for-writ p2 line 28 (Word ends after "is", typst after "submitted").
g. **Orphan control in list items.** typst lets the first line of a bullet item end a page alone; LaTeX and Word
   (club penalty, widow control) move it on. Evidence: petition-for-rehearing p11 line 27 ("• What's more, Dr.
   Handley's prediction ..."). Request: the same orphan rule for list items in all three engines.
h. **Word's page lookup misses a find in a footnote**: "Pompey" in note 4 on p9 of reply-brief (printed 6) is listed
   at 4, 5, 6 by typst and LaTeX, at 4, 5 by Word; the page itself breaks alike (XENG-003, authorities row p3 line 16).
   Also (tier 2, docx only, not caught by the diff): a contents row whose label is shorter than its `hang` ("I." of
   reply-brief's headings, 18 pt hang) loses its leader in Word: the hanging indent is Word's first tab stop, so the
   tab stops there and the right tab's dot leader is never reached (`I.   <tab>1`). Request: a tab at the hang before
   the right tab, or no hang tab (Word `w:tab w:val="clear"`) in a contents row.
